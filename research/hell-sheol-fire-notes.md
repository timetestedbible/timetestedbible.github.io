# Hell, Sheol, and the Fire of God — Research Notes

Research session exploring what Scripture actually says about "hell," the fire of judgment, and the fate of the wicked. Connected to the "Are All Children Saved?" blog — the pagan concept of eternal conscious torment drives the emotional force behind the "all children are saved" doctrine.

---

## Core Framework

### God IS the Unquenchable Fire

- **Deut 4:24** — "For the LORD thy God is a consuming fire"
- **Heb 12:29** — "For our God is a consuming fire"
- "Unquenchable fire" (Mark 9:43) doesn't mean the fuel burns forever — it means the FIRE cannot be extinguished because the fire IS God. God is eternal. The fire is eternal. The things cast into it are consumed.
- The sun analogy: the sun doesn't need your body to keep burning. Throw something in — the sun continues, the object is consumed. The sun is unquenchable. The fuel is not.

### Fire = Undifferentiation / Un-creation

- **Genesis 1:2** — Before creation, everything was *tehom* (H8415, the deep) — formless, void, undifferentiated chaos.
- Creation was the act of DIFFERENTIATION: separating light from darkness, waters above from below, land from sea, kind from kind.
- Fire reverses creation — it undifferentiates. It breaks complex structures to basic components (ash, gas, energy). It returns the formed to the formless.
- H8415 *tehom* (the deep, Gen 1:2) = G12 *abyssos* (the abyss) in the Septuagint. The bottomless pit of Revelation IS the deep of Genesis.
- The second death is UN-CREATION — return to the undifferentiated state. "As though they had not been" (Obad 1:16).

### The Sun — God's Physical Image

- **Ps 84:11** — "For the LORD God is a sun and shield"
- **Mal 4:2** — "the Sun of righteousness shall arise"
- At the sun's surface, nothing maintains form — everything is plasma, stripped to elementary particles. Raw creative power that destroys all form.
- Yet at the right distance, with the right covering (atmosphere, magnetosphere), it gives life.
- The fire of judgment IS God's unshielded presence. Same fire that consumes also sustains. The difference is COVERING.

### Three Responses to the Same Fire

1. **Ashes** — no covering, consumed (Mal 4:1 — "burn them up... they shall be ashes")
2. **Burning bush** — God's chosen vessel, sustained in the fire (Ex 3:2 — "burned with fire, not consumed")
3. **Three in the furnace** — covered by the fourth man, walk out refined, bondage burned off (Dan 3:25-27)

The fire is the same in every case. It's God. What differs is the COVERING.

### Daniel's Seven-Fold Fire (Dan 3:19)

Nebuchadnezzar heated the furnace SEVEN times more than usual. Seven = completion, perfection. The perfected fire.

In the seven-fold (perfected) fire:
- The **soldiers** (uncovered) — killed by the heat before entering (Dan 3:22)
- The **ropes** (bondage) — burned off, consumed
- The **three** (covered by the fourth man) — walk freely, not a hair singed, no smell of smoke (Dan 3:27)

The perfect fire LIBERATES the covered — consumes the bondage but leaves the person untouched. This is the refiner's fire (Mal 3:2-3): dross consumed, silver purified.

## The Spirit Returns to God

**Eccl 12:7** — "Then shall the dust return to the earth as it was: and the spirit shall return unto God who gave it."

- Body → dust (undifferentiated earth)
- Spirit → God (undifferentiated source)
- At death, everything returns to its origin — becomes undifferentiated, part of the raw material of God's creative potential
- Resurrection = re-creation: God differentiates again, forming new body, breathing spirit back in, as He did with Adam from the dust
- For the righteous: re-created in glory at the resurrection
- For the wicked at the great white throne: temporarily re-differentiated for judgment, then second death = permanent return to the undifferentiated

## Hebrew Gospel Data — Critical Distinctions

The Hebrew text of Revelation uses THREE distinct terms where English flattens to "hell" or "lake of fire":

| Verse | Greek text | Hebrew gospel | Notes |
|-------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Rev 17:8, 11 | goes to destruction | goes to **Sheol** (H7585) | The beast goes to sheol |
| Rev 19:20 | thrown into the lake of fire | cast into the **Sheol** (H7585) that burns with sulfur | "Lake of fire" = fire of sheol |
| Rev 20:10 | tormented in lake of fire | cast into the **fire of Sheol** (H7585) | Devil/beast/false prophet — H6031 *anah* (afflicted), H5769+H5769 (olam va-olam) |
| Rev 20:13 | Death and Hades gave up dead | death and **Sheol** gave up the dead | Standard sheol usage |
| Rev 20:14 | Death and Hades thrown into lake of fire | death and **Gehinnom** cast into the fire | GEHINNOM here, not sheol — distinct term |
| Rev 20:15 | thrown into the lake of fire | cast into the **fire** | Just "fire" (H784) |
| Rev 21:8 | the lake that burns with fire | the **fire of Sheol** (H7585) which burns with fire and sulfur | "Second death" |

### Key Observations:
- Rev 20:10 (devil/beast/false prophet): "afflicted from everlasting to everlasting" — spiritual beings
- Rev 20:14-15 (humans): "cast into the fire" — called "second DEATH," NOT eternal torment. No mention of affliction forever for humans.
- Rev 20:14 uniquely uses GEHINNOM where elsewhere the text uses Sheol — the Valley of Hinnom is what's destroyed, not what continues
- The Hebrew distinguishes what English/Greek conflates

### Rich Man and Lazarus (Luke 16:22-26)

- Rich man is "in Hades" (Greek) / in Sheol — "in agony in this fire"
- Lazarus at "Abraham's side" — comfort
- Both in the SAME realm (sheol), different compartments, separated by a chasm
- Connects to Deut 32:22: "a fire is kindled in mine anger, and it burns to the depths of Sheol" — fire WITHIN sheol
- This is INTERIM state, not final state. Hades/sheol itself is later cast into the fire (Rev 20:14)

## Proof Texts for Eternal Conscious Torment — Examined

### Matthew 25:41, 46 — "eternal fire" / "eternal punishment"

- G166 *aionios* from G165 *aion* (age)
- Even taking as "eternal," it modifies the RESULT, not the process:
  - "Eternal redemption" (Heb 9:12) — Christ doesn't keep redeeming forever. Result is permanent.
  - "Eternal judgment" (Heb 6:2) — the act isn't eternal. The verdict is.
  - "Eternal punishment" — the result (death/destruction) is permanent.
- "Eternal fire" destroyed Sodom (Jude 1:7) — Sodom is not still burning.

### Mark 9:43-48 — "unquenchable fire" / "worm does not die"

- Quotes **Isaiah 66:24**: "they shall look upon the **carcases** of the men that have transgressed"
- CARCASSES — dead bodies, not living souls in torment
- The worm and fire consume dead flesh — that's what worms and fire do to corpses
- "Unquenchable" = cannot be PUT OUT (runs its course), not "burns forever"
  - Jer 17:27: fire kindled in Jerusalem's gates "shall not be quenched" — those gates are not still burning
- GOD is the unquenchable fire — the fire is eternal because HE is eternal, not because the fuel is indestructible

### Revelation 14:9-11 — "smoke goes up forever and ever"

- Compare: smoke of Babylon "rose up for ever and ever" (Rev 19:3) — Babylon is destroyed, not still burning
- Isaiah 34:10 about Edom: "smoke thereof shall go up for ever; from generation to generation it shall lie waste" — Edom is not still smoking
- Smoke = EVIDENCE of completed destruction, the permanent aftermath
- "No rest day or night" — specifically about beast-worshippers during a specific judgment period
- Hebrew text (Rev 14:10) uses H6064 *anash* (to punish/fine)

### Revelation 20:10 vs. 20:14-15 — Devil vs. Humans

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION the text makes:**
- **Rev 20:10** (devil, beast, false prophet): "afflicted day and night from everlasting to everlasting" — these are spiritual beings, not humans
- **Rev 20:14-15** (humans): "cast into the fire" — labeled "second DEATH." No mention of eternal affliction. Just death.
- The text does NOT apply "tormented day and night forever" to ordinary humans. Only to the devil and his agents.

### 2 Thessalonians 1:9 — "eternal destruction"

- G3639 *olethros* = destruction, ruin
- The destruction is eternal = PERMANENT. Permanently destroyed.
- "Away from the presence of the Lord" — separated from the source of life and form. Un-created.

### Daniel 12:2 — "everlasting contempt"

- H1860 *dera'on* — contempt, abhorrence
- Appears only TWICE in entire OT: here and Isaiah 66:24 (the carcasses passage!)
- The CONTEMPT is everlasting — others view them with permanent disgust
- The dead are viewed with contempt. The dead are not conscious.

### Weeping and Gnashing of Teeth

- Matt 8:12, 13:42, 13:50, 22:13, 24:51, 25:30, Luke 13:28
- **Gnashing of teeth in OT = anger/rage, not pain:**
  - Ps 35:16, 37:12, 112:10 — wicked gnash teeth AT the righteous
  - Lam 2:16 — enemies gnash in triumph
  - Acts 7:54 — Sanhedrin gnash teeth AT Stephen
- Context is always EXCLUSION from the kingdom, not burning:
  - Luke 13:28: "when ye shall see Abraham, Isaac, Jacob... and you yourselves thrust out"
  - Matt 22:13: "outer darkness" (not fire) — excluded from wedding feast
- Weeping = grief at exclusion. Gnashing = fury at being shut out.
- These describe the MOMENT of judgment verdict, not a permanent state.

### Note: Many Fire/Destruction Verses = Physical Judgment

Many verses about fire and destruction describe the METHOD OF DEATH (war, divine judgment on cities/nations) — not the afterlife. E.g.:
- Sodom's fire = physical destruction of a city
- Jerusalem's fire (Jer 17:27) = physical siege
- Mal 4:1 — "the day cometh that shall burn them up" — the DAY of judgment, physical
- Need to carefully distinguish "how they die" from "what happens after death"

## Hebrew Gospel Word Study — "Torment" Passages

### Greek "Torment" Words (NT)

| Strong's | Word | Root Meaning | KJV Translation |
|----------|------|-------------|-----------------|
| G931 *basanos* | touchstone | A black stone used to TEST the purity of gold/silver | torment |
| G928 *basanizo* | to test by touchstone | To test metals; extended to torture/questioning | torment, pain, vex, toss |
| G929 *basanismos* | the testing | The act/state of testing | torment |
| G2851 *kolasis* | pruning | To lop/prune trees; to curb, restrain; penal infliction | punishment, torment |
| G2849 *kolazo* | to curtail | To prune, curb, check, restrain, chastise | punish |

Note: *basanos* originally = a TOUCHSTONE for testing metal purity. Extended metaphorically to torture (extracting truth). The root concept is TESTING, not torture for its own sake.

Note: *kolasis* (Matt 25:46 "eternal punishment") originally = PRUNING. To cut back, restrain. Aristotle distinguished *kolasis* (corrective punishment for the offender's benefit) from *timoria* (retributive punishment for the punisher's satisfaction). Matthew uses *kolasis*, not *timoria*.

| Greek/KJV | Hebrew Gospel | Hebrew | Significance |

### Key Findings

1. The Hebrew words behind "torment" are NOT torture words:
   - H6064 *anash* = judicial penalty/fine
   - H6031 *anah* = humble, bring low, afflict
   - H4496 *menuchah* negated = denied rest/sabbath

2. The "worm" in Hebrew is not a worm — it's a venomous creature (serpent?) that doesn't die

3. "Unquenchable fire" in Hebrew = fire that does not REST (*nuach*) — God's fire has no sabbath, it never stops being what it is

4. The contrast in Mark 9 is Garden of Eden vs. Valley of Hinnom — two physical places, not "heaven vs. hell"

5. *Kolasis* (Matt 25:46 "eternal punishment") etymologically means PRUNING — corrective cutting, not retributive torture

## "Gain the World, Forfeit the Soul" — What Remains?

### Mark 8:36 in Hebrew Gospel
"What does it profit a man if he has all the world (H8398 *tevel*) and **loses** (H6 ***abad***) his **soul** (H5315 *nephesh*)?"

**H6 *abad*** = "properly, to **wander away**, i.e. lose oneself; by implication to perish (causative, destroy)"

The root meaning is to WANDER AWAY — to become lost, scattered, dissipated, undifferentiated. The nephesh wanders into formlessness. It perishes. This fits the un-creation framework: the soul isn't tortured forever, it **dissipates**.

**Critical point:** If the nephesh has been *abad*-ed (wandered away, perished), what conscious entity remains to experience eternal torment? You can't torture what has dissipated. The very word used for "lose" implies the soul ceases to maintain its differentiated form.

### Basanos (G931) = TOUCHSTONE TEST, Not Torture

From Abarim Publications' analysis:
- A touchstone is a stone you rub gold against to TEST if it's real
- The test DAMAGES the object — that's the semantic connection to "torment"
- The tester wants to EXTRACT or VERIFY something — investigative, not retributive
- "This word's element of trial indicates both intent and suspicion on behalf of the one doing the testing, or **the accuser** in whose name he tests"
- The "accuser" language connects to H7854 *satan* (the adversary/accuser)

The rich man in Luke 16:23 is in *basanos* — being TESTED for authenticity, not punished for eternity. The touchstone reveals what's real gold (genuine) and what's counterfeit (fake). The fire of God does the same: tests everything, reveals what can survive (gold/silver) and what can't (dross/stubble).

**Matt 18:34** — the only use of *basanistes* (tormentor/tester): a man handed to the "tormentor" until he paid his debts. Abarim notes: "the victim was probably not physically tortured, but rather financially — his tormenter was rather an **accountant** who dissected his victim's life in search of remaining properties." The basanistes is an auditor, not a torturer.

### Kolasis (G2851) = PRUNING

Matt 25:46 "eternal punishment" — *kolasis* from *kolazo* = "to lop or prune, as trees and wings; to curb, check, restrain."

- Root image: cutting back a branch. Pruning.
- Aristotle distinguished *kolasis* (corrective punishment, for the offender's benefit) from *timoria* (retributive punishment, for the punisher's satisfaction). Jesus uses *kolasis*, NOT *timoria*.
- Eternal *kolasis* = permanently pruned off. Cut away. Not eternally being pruned — permanently cut.
- Connects to John 15: the branch that doesn't bear fruit is CUT OFF and thrown into the fire.
- The pruning is the cutting. The fire finishes it.

### Convergence

| G928 *basanizo* | To test by touchstone | To torment forever | To put through refining/testing |

Every "torment" word, when traced to its root, describes TESTING, PRUNING, WANDERING AWAY, HUMBLING, or PENALIZING — none describe eternal conscious torture as a primary meaning. The ECT reading requires every word to be used in its most extreme extended sense simultaneously.

## Smoke, Vapor, and the Final Picture

### Smoke (H6227 *ashan*) vs. Vapor (H1892 *hevel*)

Different words, but conceptual parallel:
- **Smoke** (*ashan*) = what remains after fire consumes something. Rises, dissipates, gone. Evidence of destruction.
- **Vapor/vanity** (*hevel*) = a breath that appears and vanishes. Transitory, empty. Ecclesiastes' word. Also Abel's name.
- Both describe something SUBSTANTIAL becoming INSUBSTANTIAL — dissipating, losing form, returning to nothing.

### Rev 19:3 — "From Generation to Generation"

Hebrew gospel: "the smoke goes from **generation** (H1755 *dor*) to **generation** (H1755 *dor*)." NOT "forever and ever." The language of historical continuity, not infinite duration. The same phrase used for God's covenant faithfulness.

### Rev 14:11 — "From Age to Age"

Hebrew gospel: "the smoke of their punishment shall go upward from **age** (H5769 *olam*) to **age** (H5769 *olam*)." *Olam* = concealed time, age — CAN mean forever but often means "a long duration" or "an age."

The smoke is EVIDENCE that the fire did its work. It rises and dissipates — like *hevel*, like breath. The destruction is permanent (the thing burned is gone). The smoke is not permanent — it's the visible aftermath that fades.

### The Final Contrast: Life vs. Death (Not Bliss vs. Torture)

| View | What happens to the unsaved |
|---|---|
| Atheist | Cease to exist — everyone |
| Bible | Cease to exist — the wicked. "As though they had not been" (Obad 1:16) |
| Traditional Christianity | Eternal conscious torment in fire — forever |

The biblical picture is closer to the atheist's expectation than to traditional Christianity's. The difference: the atheist thinks cessation is universal; Scripture says the righteous are resurrected to eternal life.

**Romans 6:23 reads exactly as written:** "The wages of sin is **death**, but the gift of God is **eternal life**." Not "the wages of sin is eternal torment." DEATH. Not two parallel eternal conscious states (bliss vs. torture) — **existence vs. non-existence**. Life vs. death.

The real contrast in Scripture was never heaven vs. hell. It was always **life vs. death**. Being vs. not being. The resurrection vs. the second death. One side gets to exist. The other side doesn't.

For children: uncovered children don't face Dante's inferno. They face not being — not participating in the resurrection, not existing in the renewed earth. "As though they had not been." Still sobering enough to guard the doorpost. But a fundamentally different kind of sobering — one that doesn't require a pagan horror story to motivate it.

## Unresolved Arguments for ECT (Require Further Study)

### 1. Matt 25:46 — Parallel Structure
"Eternal punishment" vs. "eternal life" — same adjective *aionios* for both. If life is truly unending, punishment must be too. Need rigorous grammatical analysis. Note: *kolasis* (pruning/correction) may help — "eternal pruning/cutting" = permanently cut off, not eternally being cut.

### 2. "Forever and Ever" Used for God Too
*Aion ton aionon* describes both torment of the devil (Rev 20:10) AND God's reign (Rev 11:15). If we limit it for punishment, do we limit it for God? Need to examine whether the phrase functions differently in different contexts, or whether Hebrew *olam va-olam* has a range.

### 3. Consciousness in Sheol
Rich man and Lazarus (Luke 16) — conscious conversation in sheol. But this is clearly a parable (like the 10 virgins — imaginary framework to teach a point). Also: Isa 14:9-10 (shades speak), Ezek 32:21 (mighty speak from sheol), 1 Sam 28:15 (Samuel). These may be poetic/prophetic imagery or literal. Tension with Eccl 9:5 "dead know not anything."

### 4. *Apollumi* = "Lost" Not "Annihilated"?
G622 *apollumi* used for "lost sheep" (still exists), prodigal (still alive). Does "destroy soul and body in gehenna" mean "ruin" rather than "cease to exist"? Need full word study.

### 5. Degrees of Punishment
Luke 12:47-48 — few stripes vs. many stripes. Matt 11:22,24 — "more tolerable." If annihilation is binary, how are there degrees? Could refer to severity of the dying process, or temporal judgment before death.

### 6. "Second Death" — Cessation or Separation?
"Dead in trespasses" (Eph 2:1) = spiritually dead but conscious. Could "second death" = eternal spiritual death (conscious separation from God)? Need to test against Hebrew usage of H4194 *maveth*.

### 7. Rev 14:11 — "No Rest Day or Night"
Hebrew uses H4496 *menuchah* negated — no sabbath-rest. This could mean: they never enter the promised rest (Heb 3-4, the rest that remains for God's people). Exclusion from rest, not active torture. But "day or night" suggests ongoing experience. Need careful examination.

## The *Acher* / *Sheni* Distinction in Hebrew Revelation

The Hebrew Revelation uses TWO different words for "second" in "second death":
- **H312 *acher*** (another, different kind) — Rev 2:10, 20:6, 20:14
- **H8145 *sheni*** (second, ordinal) — Rev 21:8 only (the final summary)

*Acher maveth* = "another kind of death" — qualitatively different from ordinary mortality. Not just "death #2."

Greek collapses both into G1208 *deuteros*, losing the nuance.

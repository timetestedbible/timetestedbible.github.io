## Context Bag

**H8141 shaneh** = "a year (as a revolution of time)" from H8138 shanah (primitive root) = "fold, duplicate, transmute, change, return, repeat, do the second time." H8141 does NOT intrinsically specify the revolution's SIZE — only that a complete cycle returns to origin. Strong's: "a year (as a revolution of time)." G1763 eniautos = "a year" (NT).

**ROOT FAMILY — ALL FROM H8138 shanah.** H8141 shaneh (year) = שנה unpointed. H8145 sheni (second/again) = שני. H8147 shᵉnayim (two/twofold) = שנים. H8141 PLURAL shanim (years) = שנים. CRITICAL: H8147 "two" and H8141-plural "years" are **consonantally identical** (שנים) — only Masoretic vowels distinguish them. H4932 mishneh (repetition/double) also from H8138. H8132 shana' (alter/change) = same consonants. H8140 shᵉnah (Aramaic "year") = שנה = same as H8141. Core biliteral root שנ (shin-nun) = cyclical return across all derivatives.

**TIME-UNIT SCALING CHAIN.** Scripture scales time-units systematically:
- Day→year: Num 14:34, Ezek 4:6 "each day for a year"
- Day→1000yrs: Ps 90:4, 2Pet 3:8
- Sabbath(1day)→week: G4521 sabbaton = both "sabbath" AND "week" (Mat 28:1, Mk 16:2, Lk 24:1, Jn 20:1)
- Sabbath→7th year: Lev 25:4 sabbath-year
- Sabbath→7yr period: Lev 25:8 "sabbaths of years"
- Week(7days)→7yrs: Dan 9:24 H7620 shabuwa = "sevened" = 7-year period
- Sabbath→1000yrs: Rev 20:2-7 millennial reign = cosmic sabbath-day (1day=1000yrs per 2Pet 3:8)
- **Year→Jubilee cycle(49yrs):** follows same established pattern

**HIERARCHY.** Sabbath-day → sabbaton/week(7d) → year(seasons) → sabbath-year(7th yr) → sabbaths-of-years(7yr blocks) → Jubilee(7×7=49yr) → cosmic week(7×1000yr) → millennium(cosmic sabbath). Self-similar at every scale; 7th unit brings rest. Jubilee = highest sabbatical revolution. Year (shaneh=revolution) names the cycle at whatever scale context requires.

**LEV 25:8 ELASTICITY.** H8141 shaneh appears 5× in one verse — simultaneously naming individual years, sabbath-year periods, AND the 49-year total. Word describes both parts and whole. The 49-year period IS a compound shaneh.

**ISAIAH DAY/YEAR BINARY.** Three prophetic oracles pair "day of vengeance" with "year of [Jubilee term]":
- Isa 61:1-2: "acceptable YEAR (H8141) of the LORD" + "proclaim LIBERTY (H1865 derowr)" = EXACT Jubilee word from Lev 25:10. H7522 ratson = from H7521 ratsah = "satisfy a debt." Jesus reads this Lk 4:18-19, declares fulfilled.
- Isa 34:8: "YEAR (H8141) of recompences (H7966 shilluwm)" from H7999 shalam = restore/make whole. + "day of vengeance."
- Isa 63:4: "YEAR (H8141) of my redeemed (H1350 gaal)" = kinsman-redeemer = Jubilee redemption term Lev 25:25,48-49. + "day of vengeance."
- Ezek 46:17: "YEAR (H8141) of liberty (H1865 derowr)" = calls Jubilee simply "the year of liberty."
DAY = concentrated judgment. YEAR = Jubilee-cycle restoration. Binary only works if "year" carries Jubilee content.

**DAN 9 PATCHED READING.** Consonants שבעים appear 2× identically in v24 — Masoretic points distinguish "seventy" from "weeks." Patched: "seventy seventy (or jubilees)" → two alternative readings: 70×70=4,900yrs OR 70 Jubilee cycles=3,430yrs (dual fulfillment). v25-26: H8147 שנים ("two") = consonantally identical to H8141-plural שנים ("years"). "Threescore and two weeks" can read "sevens, sixty and years." Jubilee cycle implicitly encoded as measuring unit through sabbatical "sevens" (shabuwa) + embedded "years" (שנים). Definite article הַ on שבעים in v26 points back to Jubilee system of v24.

**GEN 6:3.** "His days shall be 120 years (H8141)." Literal 120 fails as lifespan cap (Abraham 175, Isaac 180, Jacob 147) and as flood countdown (~100yrs off). 120 Jubilee cycles × 49 = 5,880yrs from Adam → present era. Moses embodies both: died at exactly 120 literal years (Deut 34:7), birthday (Deut 31:2), body taken by God (Jude 9). Literal life enacts prophetic Jubilee-span.

**PATTERN SUMMARY:** shaneh (year) = revolution of time. Root H8138 = return/repeat/change. The Jubilee cycle IS a revolution — complete socio-economic reset returning to origin. Biblical time-scaling operates at every level (sabbath alone covers 5 scales: day, week, year, 7yr, 1000yr). Year scaling to 49-year Jubilee cycle is the natural next step. Isaiah's prophetic "year" always carries Jubilee vocabulary (derowr, gaal, shilluwm). Daniel 9 consonantal text encodes Jubilee counting in שבעים שבעים and שנים. Genesis 6:3 "120 years" → 120 Jubilees produces coherent timeline.

## Context Bag

DEFINITION (not import — Scripture's own equation): WINE = a spirit that fills/possesses the drinker. Same verbs govern wine + spirit: POURED OUT (Isa 29:10 spirit of deep sleep ‖ Zech 12:10 spirit of grace ‖ Rev 14:10 wine of wrath), DRINK (1Cor 12:13 "drink into one Spirit" ‖ Jer 25:15 drink the fury), MINGLED/MIXED (Ps 75:8 wine "full of mixture" ‖ Isa 19:14 "mingled a perverse spirit"). Clincher: Isa 29:9 "drunken, but not with wine… stagger" = v10 "poured… the spirit of deep sleep." Eph 5:18 "not drunk with wine… but filled with the Spirit" = rival fillings. Acts 2:13-17 Spirit-outpouring mistaken for new wine → Peter: "pour out my Spirit."

PRODUCTION CHAIN: VINE (person/Israel) → GRAPES = FRUIT = deeds → WINEPRESS (pressed/trial) → WINE = the spirit produced → drunk/possesses/offered. Fruit decides the wine: Deut 32:32-33 vine of Sodom → grapes of gall → wine = poison of dragons. Isa 5:2-7 vineyard=Israel, winepress built in it; looked for good grapes (judgment/righteousness) got wild → no good wine, judgment. Gal 6:7-8 sow flesh→reap corruption / sow Spirit→reap life. Matt 7:17 tree→fruit. ‖ OIL: same pressed FRUIT — oil = proven works (outward LIGHT), wine = the spirit (inward possession + worship).

HOLY vs UNHOLY (one wine, by the fruit):
- HOLY: gladness Ps 104:15; wedding wine Jn 2:10; covenant cup Mt 26:28; spirit of grace Zech 12:10; new-wine Spirit Acts 2. = the Holy Spirit.
- UNHOLY: spirit of whoredoms Hos 4:11-12 (‖ Babylon's wine of fornication Rev 17:2; 18:3; Jer 51:7 nations drunk→mad); spirit of deep sleep/stupor Isa 29:10; perverse spirit Isa 19:14; strong delusion 2Th 2:10-11 (reject the TRUTH → get the delusion-spirit); mocker/error Prov 20:1, Isa 28:7; cup of wrath/trembling Ps 75:8, Jer 25:15, Isa 51:17.

TWO MOTIONS: WINEPRESS = making (crush the fruit, press out the spirit/life — Isa 63:2-3; Rev 14:19-20). CUP = consuming (portion handed from a hand, drunk → possesses — Ps 16:5 "portion of my cup"). Blood stays literal: wine = "blood of grapes" (Gen 49:11; Deut 32:14); blood=life (Lev 17:11); life=spirit (Jn 6:63 "words are spirit and life"). So wine = the life/spirit in the blood, drunk in — never need BLOOD as a symbol.

BREAD + WINE = TRUTH + SPIRIT: BREAD=word/truth (Deut 8:3), WINE=spirit. Jn 4:23-24 Father SEEKS worship "in spirit and in truth" = both. Counterfeit worship = leavened bread (soured truth/false doctrine) + wine of fornication (unholy spirit) = neither. True = unleavened bread + the good wine (Spirit from righteous fruit). Abstention (Num 6:3 Nazarite; Lev 10:9 priest in service) = the wholly-consecrated take no rival possessing-spirit.

TWO PENTECOSTS (same feast — Shavuot/Sinai; firstfruits, Ex 34:22; loaves baked WITH leaven, Lev 23:17):
- SINAI: law given (truth on stone) → golden calf = false image (false truth) + "eat, drink, rose up to play" (unholy spirit/revelry, Ex 32:6) → ~3,000 fell by the sword (Ex 32:28). Letter kills (2Co 3:6).
- ACTS 2: word received + Holy Spirit poured = "firstfruits of the Spirit" (Rom 8:23), the new wine (Acts 2:13) → ~3,000 saved (Acts 2:41). Spirit gives life.
One feast, two vintages: 3,000 slain vs 3,000 saved.

SUMMARY CHAIN: fruit (deeds) →[winepress]→ wine (spirit) →[drunk]→ possesses → righteous fruit = Holy Spirit (joy/grace/life) ‖ corrupt fruit = unholy spirit (whoredom/delusion/wrath). + BREAD (truth) = worship in spirit and truth.

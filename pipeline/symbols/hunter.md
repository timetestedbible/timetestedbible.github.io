## Context Bag

**LEXICAL:** H6719 *tsayyad* = hunter/huntsman. Only 2× OT (Gen 25:27 Esau, Gen 10:9 Nimrod). H6679 *tsud* = to lie alongside (in wait), to catch an animal or men, to victual for a journey. ~18× OT. Root implies AMBUSH — "to lie alongside" prey. H6718 *tsayid* = the chase, game, provisions/venison. KJV renders as "hunter," "venison," "hunting," "provision," "victuals." The same word means both THE HUNT and THE FOOD OBTAINED — hunting = provision-gathering.

**FIRST OCCURRENCE — GEN 10:9 — NIMROD:** "He was a mighty HUNTER (H6718) before the LORD: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty hunter before the LORD." NIMROD: son of Cush, grandson of Ham. "Began to be a mighty one in the earth" (Gen 10:8). Built BABEL, Erech, Accad, Calneh (Gen 10:10) → founder of BABYLON. "Before the LORD" (H6440 *panim* + H3068) — not necessarily "in service to" but "in the face of" / "in opposition to." First hunter = first empire-builder = founder of the system that opposes covenant order. Hunting = CAPTURING PEOPLE to build kingdoms. Mic 5:6: "land of NIMROD" = Assyria. co: BABYLON.

**SECOND NAMED HUNTER — GEN 25:27 — ESAU:** "Esau was a cunning HUNTER (H6718), a man of the FIELD (H7704); and Jacob was a plain (H8535 *tam* = complete, whole, perfect) man, dwelling in TENTS." The HUNTER vs the PLAIN MAN. *Tam* is the same word used of the $dove (Song 5:2 "my dove, my undefiled/tam") and of Job (Job 1:1 "perfect/tam"). Esau = hunter = man of the field (exposed, outside). Jacob = tam = dweller in tents (sheltered, covenant). Esau SELLS his BIRTHRIGHT for food (Gen 25:34) — the hunter trades covenant standing for provision. Gen 27: Esau hunts venison (H6718) for Isaac's blessing, but Jacob (the plain man) obtains it. The hunter LOSES the covenant; the dweller-in-tents RECEIVES it.

**JER 16:16 — GOD SENDS HUNTERS (PHASE 2):** "After will I send for many HUNTERS (H6719), and they shall HUNT (H6679) them from every MOUNTAIN (H2022), and from every HILL (H1389), and out of the HOLES of the ROCKS (H5553 *sela*)." Phase 1 = FISHERS (open gathering from sea). Phase 2 = HUNTERS (targeted pursuit from land). The hunter finds what hides: in mountains ($mountain = kingdom), in hills, in rock-holes. The ROCK ($rock = covenant) has HOLES (H5357 *neqiyq* = cleft, fissure) — some hide within the covenant's structure. God's hunters go WHERE THE FISH CANNOT: onto dry land, into high places, into hidden crevices. Context (vv14-18): this is JUDGMENT-gathering — God bringing scattered Israel to account AND bringing them home. co: FISH, MOUNTAIN, ROCK.

**EZEK 13:18-20 — HUNTING SOULS:** "Woe to the women that... HUNT SOULS (H5315 *nephesh*)! Will ye HUNT the SOULS of my people, and will ye save the souls alive?" False prophetesses "hunt souls" — spiritual predation using deception (pillows on armholes, kerchiefs on heads = false comfort/covering). v20: "I am against your pillows, wherewith ye there HUNT the SOULS to make them fly, and I will let the SOULS go, even the SOULS that ye HUNT." God OPPOSES this kind of hunting — predatory soul-capture through false prophecy. The hunter's target here is explicitly SOULS — not bodies. co: DECEIVE, SNARE.

**GOD AS HUNTER:** Job 10:16 — "Thou HUNTEST me as a fierce lion." God pursues Job with relentless attention. This is not predatory but revelatory — God's pursuit forces encounter. Ps 140:11 — "evil shall HUNT the violent man to overthrow him." Evil itself hunts — the violent man's own violence becomes his pursuer. Lam 3:52 — "Mine enemies chased (H6679) me sore, like a BIRD." Lam 4:18 — "They HUNT our steps, that we cannot go in our streets." Enemies as hunters of God's people — but God allows it as part of covenant discipline.

**PROV 6:26 — THE HARLOT HUNTS:** "The adulteress will HUNT (H6679) for the precious life." The $harlot is a HUNTER — she pursues the soul to capture it. The harlot-as-hunter connects to Ezek 13:18 (false prophetesses hunting souls) and to Ecc 7:26 (woman whose heart = snares and nets = cherem). co: HARLOT, SNARE, NET.

**HUNTER vs SHEPHERD CONTRAST:** The two named hunters (Nimrod, Esau) are contrasted with shepherds throughout Scripture. Abel = shepherd (accepted). Nimrod = hunter (empire-builder). Jacob = tent-dweller who became the great shepherd of Israel. David = shepherd called from flocks. Jesus = good $shepherd. The shepherd LEADS and PROTECTS; the hunter PURSUES and CAPTURES. The shepherd gathers by voice; the hunter gathers by force. God uses BOTH methods: fishing (open call), hunting (targeted pursuit) — but the ideal is shepherding.

**MIC 7:2 — MUTUAL HUNTING:** "They hunt (H6679) every man his brother with a CHEREM (H2764 = net/ban)." Social breakdown: people hunting each other with nets. The $net study notes cherem = both net AND ban — mutual hunting = mutual destruction. co: NET, CHEREM.

**PROVISION CONNECTION:** H6718 *tsayid* = both "hunting" AND "venison/provision/victuals." Gen 27:3: "take me some venison (tsayid)." Josh 9:12: "bread... we took hot for our provision (tsud)." The hunt's purpose = obtaining food/provision. Esau hunts FOR Isaac, but loses the blessing TO Jacob. The hunter gets the provision but misses the covenant.

## Candidates Tested

1. **One who pursues and captures by targeted force — predatory or divine** — BEST. Nimrod (empire) ✓ | Esau (pursuer who loses covenant) ✓ | Jer 16:16 (God's phase-2 gathering) ✓ | Ezek 13:18 (soul-hunting) ✓ | Mic 7:2 (mutual predation) ✓ | Prov 6:26 (harlot hunts) ✓ | God hunts Job ✓
2. **Empire-builder** — works for Nimrod but too narrow for Jer 16:16, Ezek 13, Prov 6:26.
3. **Covenant rejecter** — works for Esau, Nimrod, but not for Jer 16:16 (God's own hunters).
4. **Provider** — from tsayid = provision. But misses the predatory/pursuit dimension.
5. **Deceiver** — partial: Ezek 13 hunting through deception. But Nimrod and Esau hunt openly.
6. **Judgment agent** — works for Jer 16:16, Lam passages. Too narrow for Nimrod, Esau.
7. **Tracker of the hidden** — nice for Jer 16:16 (mountains, hills, rock-holes). But too specific.

## Pattern Summary

HUNTER = targeted pursuer who captures by force. Two archetypes: NIMROD (predatory empire-builder who captures men to build kingdoms = $babylon) and GOD'S HUNTER (agent sent to find those who hide from gathering, Jer 16:16). Esau the hunter = the covenant rejecter who pursues provision but loses the blessing. The HUNTER contrasts with the FISHERMAN (Jer 16:16): fishermen gather openly from the $sea; hunters pursue specifically from the land. The HUNTER contrasts with the $shepherd: hunter captures by force; shepherd gathers by voice. False prophetesses HUNT SOULS (Ezek 13:18-20) — spiritual predation through deception. The $harlot hunts for the precious life (Prov 6:26). At the societal level, when everyone hunts their brother with a $net (Mic 7:2), the covenant order has collapsed completely.

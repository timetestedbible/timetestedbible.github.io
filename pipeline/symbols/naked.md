## Context Bag

### Strong's Data

H5903 `ʻêyrôm` — nudity, from H6191 `ʻâram` (crafty/subtle — same root as serpent in Gen 3:1). 10× OT.
H6172 `ʻervâh` — nakedness/shame/pudenda, from H6168. Concentrated 24× in Lev 18-20 ("uncover nakedness"). ~54× total.
H6168 `ʻârâh` — to make bare, empty, pour out, demolish. Verb root of H6172.
H6544 `pâraʻ` — to loosen, expose, cast off restraint. Used for "naked" in Ex 32:25 AND for "perish/cast off restraint" in Prov 29:18. Same word = nakedness IS unrestrained lawlessness.

### Definitional — Ezek 16:8 (the clincher)
"I spread my skirt over thee, and **covered thy nakedness**: yea, I sware unto thee, and **entered into a covenant** with thee." Cover nakedness = enter covenant. Three actions in synonymous parallelism: spread skirt + cover nakedness + enter covenant. Nakedness IS the state before/without covenant.

### First Occurrence Pattern — Gen 2-3
Gen 2:25 — naked, not ashamed. In perfect communion, no breach, no need for covering.
Gen 3:7 — after disobedience, KNEW they were naked. Covenant breach → awareness of exposure.
Gen 3:7 — sewed FIG LEAVES. Human attempt to self-cover (cf. $fig-tree).
Gen 3:21 — God makes coats of SKINS (requiring death/blood). God provides covenant covering.
Pattern: breach → awareness of nakedness → failed human covering → God provides covering through sacrifice.

### H5903/H6191 Root Connection
H5903 `ʻêyrôm` (naked) shares root H6191 `ʻâram` with Gen 3:1 "the serpent was more subtil (H6175 `ʻârûm`)." Wordplay: the serpent's craftiness leads to awareness of nakedness. Same consonant cluster ע-ר-ם.

### H6544 — Naked = Cast Off Restraint
Ex 32:25 — Aaron made people "naked" (H6544) = loosed from restraint → golden calf → shame.
Prov 29:18 — "Where there is no vision, the people perish (H6544)" = become naked/unrestrained.
Prov 13:18 — "Poverty and shame to him that refuseth (H6544) instruction."
Pattern: to be naked = to cast off the restraint of Torah/covenant.

### Leviticus 18-20 Concentration
"Uncover nakedness" (H1540+H6172) appears 24× in two chapters. Every prohibited act = violating a covenant boundary. "Uncovering nakedness" = breaching the protective covering of covenant relationship structure.

### Walking Naked — Prophetic Sign
Isa 20:2-4 — Isaiah walks naked and barefoot 3 years as sign: Egypt/Ethiopia led captive, "to the shame (H6172 `ʻervâh`) of Egypt." Walking naked = living without national/covenant covering.
Mic 1:8 — "I will go stripped and naked" — mourning that Samaria loses covering.
Rev 16:15 — "Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments, lest he walk naked." Walk (G4043 `peripateō`) = conduct of life. Walking naked = conducting life without covenant covering.

### NT Spiritual Nakedness

2 Cor 5:3 — "being clothed we shall not be found naked" — resurrection covering.
1 Cor 15:37 — "bare grain" → sown naked, raised clothed. Death strips; resurrection clothes.

### Charitable Covering

### Pattern Summary
Naked = without covenant covering. Clothing/covering = covenant. God covers nakedness through covenant (Ezek 16:8, Gen 3:21). Nakedness exposed = covenant stripped in judgment. Walking naked = conducting life outside covenant protection. The opposite of dwelling under God's $shadow. Same root connects nakedness to the serpent's subtlety (H6191) and to casting off restraint (H6544). "Clothe the naked" = extend covenant protection to the exposed.

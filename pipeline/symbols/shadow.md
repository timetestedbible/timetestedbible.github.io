## Context Bag

**H6738 tsel** = shade/shadow, from H6751 tsalal (to shade, as twilight). ~49× OT. KJV: "defence, shade(-ow)." **H6757 tsalmaveth** = tsel+maveth (shadow of death). 18× OT. **G4639 skia** = shadow. 7× NT. **G1982 episkiazo** = cast shadow upon / overshadow. 5× NT. **H4699 metsullah** = shade (Zech 1:8 "bottom"). Root family: H6751 tsalal → H6738 tsel, H6752 tselel, H6754 tselem (image/phantom — "from unused root meaning to shade"), H4699 metsullah.

**PHYSICAL LOGIC — THREE ELEMENTS:** (1) Burning SOURCE (judgment/exposure/heat). (2) BODY standing between you and source. (3) POSITION behind/under the body. Shadow is proportional to body's size. Cannot be manufactured — only positioned into. Shadow = evidence of the body's presence + orientation toward you. The shadow arrives before the body when the body approaches (sun behind → shadow cast forward).

**UNIFIED MEANING: Shadow = covenant protection provided by a body.** The same word (H6738 / G4639) carries the same meaning across OT and NT. The appointed times, tabernacle, and law ARE shadow — not "mere preview" but actual covenant protection cast by Christ's body. Isa 4:6 "tabernacle (H5521 sukkah) for a SHADOW from the heat" = Col 2:17 "which are a shadow... the body is of Christ." Same word, same function: the tabernacle/appointed times ARE the shadow = ARE covenant protection = ARE cast by Christ (the body).

**GOD'S SHADOW = COVENANT PROTECTION (12 verses):**
Ps 91:1 — "dwell in secret place... abide under SHADOW of Almighty." v.4: "under his WINGS (H3671=covenant) shalt thou trust." Shadow+wings = double covenant term. Ps 17:8, 36:7, 57:1, 63:7 — all combine SHADOW+WINGS. Ps 121:5 — "LORD is thy SHADE upon thy right hand." God himself = your shade. Isa 4:6 — "TABERNACLE for a SHADOW from heat." Booth+shadow = covenant shelter. Isa 25:4-5 — SHADOW from heat when blast of terrible ones. Isa 32:2 — "SHADOW of a great ROCK in a weary land." ROCK=covenant → shadow OF covenant. Double-locked.

**SHADOW OF HIS HAND = HIDDEN FOR PURPOSE (2 verses):**
Isa 49:2 — Servant "hid in SHADOW of his hand + made polished shaft + hid in quiver." Hidden ≠ abandoned. Hidden = prepared weapon. Isa 51:16 — "covered thee in SHADOW of mine hand, THAT I MAY plant heavens + lay foundations of earth + say to Zion: thou art my people." Concealment serves commission. Shadow = purposeful protection DURING preparation for new creation.

**MESSIANIC SHADOW = LIFE AMONG THE NATIONS (3 verses):**
Lam 4:20 — "the ANOINTED of the LORD... under his SHADOW we shall LIVE among the heathen." Messiah's shadow = survival in exile. co: MYRTLE (Esther = hidden faithful among nations). Hos 14:7 — "they that DWELL under his SHADOW shall RETURN; shall REVIVE as corn, GROW as vine." Shadow = the place where LIFE returns. Song 2:3 — "I sat under his SHADOW with great delight, his FRUIT was sweet." co: MARRIAGE, FRUIT. Intimacy under covenant covering.

**APPOINTED TIMES / TABERNACLE AS SHADOW (3 verses — SAME MEANING AS CLUSTER ABOVE):**
Col 2:17 — "which are a SHADOW of things to come; but the BODY is of Christ." Context: festivals, new moons, sabbaths (v.16). These appointed times ARE the shadow = ARE the covenant protection. Christ IS the body casting that shadow. You experience the body's protection BY positioning yourself in the shadow (observing the appointed times). The shadow doesn't become "obsolete" when the body arrives — the shadow is STRONGEST when the body is nearest. Heb 8:5 — tabernacle = "shadow of heavenly things." = Isa 4:6: "tabernacle for a shadow from the heat." Same word, same concept, same function: the tabernacle provides actual covenant shelter patterned on the heavenly reality. Heb 10:1 — "law having a SHADOW of good things to come, not the very IMAGE (G1504 eikon)." Shadow provides REAL but INCOMPLETE protection (cannot make perfect/complete). The body provides COMPLETE protection. Physical parallel: shadow shields from direct heat but not everything; the body/rock itself provides total shelter. The law's appointed times = real covenant protection; Christ himself = complete covenant protection. Not shadow-as-inferior but shadow-as-proportional.

**TREE/NATION'S SHADOW = POLITICAL COVERING (5 verses):**
Ezek 31:6 — "under his SHADOW dwelt all great NATIONS" (Assyria = great cedar). Ezek 31:12,17 — shadow gone when tree CUT DOWN. Ezek 17:23 — God's OWN planted cedar: "in SHADOW of branches shall they dwell." Ps 80:10 — vine (Israel) covered hills with shadow. Mk 4:32 — mustard tree: birds lodge under shadow. co: TREE → shadow of tree = protection nation provides. Every TREE (nation) casts a shadow (political umbrella).

**FALSE SHADOW = WRONG COVERING (4 verses):**
Isa 30:2-3 — "trust in SHADOW of EGYPT" → shame, confusion. Judg 9:15 — bramble (THORN) offers shadow → produces FIRE instead. Hos 4:13 — shade of idolatrous oaks = false worship. Pattern: every entity casts shadow, but only God's is reliable. False shadow = false covenant. Bramble's shadow = destructive counterfeit.

**TRANSIENCE (9 verses):**
1Chr 29:15, Job 7:2, 8:9, 14:2, 17:7, Ps 102:11, 109:23, 144:4, Eccl 6:12, 8:13. Human days = passing shadow. ≠ God's shadow which endures. Jas 1:17 — "no SHADOW of turning (G5157 trope)" in God. He doesn't rotate → His shadow is CONSTANT. His covering never shifts.

**SHADOW OF DEATH (H6757 tsalmaveth — 18 verses):**
Ps 23:4 — "valley of SHADOW OF DEATH, I fear no evil: thou art WITH me." Even death casts a shadow — but God's presence (rod+staff) overrules death's shadow. Isa 9:2 / Mat 4:16 / Lk 1:79 — those in "shadow of death" see great LIGHT. Light dispels death's shadow. Job (8×), Ps 44:19, 107:10,14, Jer 2:6, 13:16, Amos 5:8. Pattern: death's shadow = the covering/domain of death. God BREAKS that shadow with light/deliverance.

**NT OVERSHADOW (G1982 episkiazo = epi+skia):**
Lk 1:35 — "power of Highest shall OVERSHADOW thee" → Mary conceives. God's shadow = GENERATIVE, not just protective. Things come to life under His shadow. Mat 17:5 / Mk 9:7 / Lk 9:34 — cloud overshadowed at Transfiguration → voice speaks. Acts 5:15 — Peter's shadow might overshadow → healing. co: Hos 14:7 "dwell under his shadow → REVIVE."

**MYRTLE CONNECTION:** Zech 1:8 — myrtles in the H4699 metsullah (shade, from H6751 tsalal). Faithful remnant (MYRTLE) dwell IN God's shade. = Ps 91:1 enacted in vision. The myrtles' location IS the shadow. Sukkot booths (Neh 8:15 myrtle branches for booths) = Isa 4:6 tabernacle for shadow = Col 2:17 appointed times as shadow. The appointed time of Sukkot = dwelling in the shadow = dwelling in covenant protection = constituted by the faithful remnant.

**PATTERN:** (1) SHADOW = covenant protection by a body standing between you and destruction. Same meaning OT→NT. (2) SHADOW+WINGS = covenant covering (Ps 17:8, 36:7, 57:1, 63:7). (3) SHADOW+ROCK = rest in covenant (Isa 32:2). (4) SHADOW+HAND = hidden for purpose (Isa 49:2, 51:16). (5) APPOINTED TIMES = THE SHADOW, CHRIST = THE BODY (Col 2:17). Festivals/sabbaths are HOW you position yourself in the shadow. (6) TABERNACLE = SHADOW (Isa 4:6 = Heb 8:5). (7) LAW = REAL BUT INCOMPLETE shadow; Christ = COMPLETE protection (Heb 10:1). (8) MESSIANIC SHADOW = life among nations (Lam 4:20, Hos 14:7). (9) FALSE SHADOWS = Egypt, bramble, idols (Isa 30:2, Judg 9:15). (10) OVERSHADOW = GENERATIVE presence (Lk 1:35). (11) NO SHADOW OF TURNING = God doesn't rotate; His covering is constant (Jas 1:17). (12) MYRTLE IN THE SHADE = faithful remnant positioned in God's shadow (Zech 1:8). (13) SUKKOT = dwelling in shadow = Col 2:17 shadow = appointed time of living under His covering.

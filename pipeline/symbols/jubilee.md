## The 49+1 Pattern — Textual Foundation

**Lev 25:8** — "Thou shalt NUMBER{H5608 *saphar* = score with a mark, inscribe} SEVEN SABBATHS of years unto thee, SEVEN TIMES SEVEN years; and the space of the seven sabbaths of years shall be unto thee FORTY AND NINE years."
The text explicitly says the cycle = **49 years**. Not 50. Seven sevens.

**Lev 25:9** — "Then shalt thou cause the trumpet{H7782 *shophar*} of the jubile{H8643 *teruah* = shout/blast/alarm} to sound on the TENTH day of the SEVENTH month, in the DAY OF ATONEMENT."
Jubilee announced on **Yom Kippur** — 10th of Tishri. The reset begins at atonement.

**Lev 25:10** — "And ye shall hallow the FIFTIETH year, and proclaim LIBERTY{H1865 *deror* = freedom, spontaneous outflow, from unused root = to move rapidly} throughout ALL the land unto ALL the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a JUBILE{H3104 *yobel*} unto you; and ye shall RETURN every man unto his POSSESSION, and ye shall RETURN every man unto his FAMILY."
The 50th year is hallowed. But 49 years were counted (v.8). The 50th year = year 1 of the next cycle (49+1 overlap pattern, like the 8th day = 1st day).

**H3104 *yobel*** = from H2986 *yabal* = "to flow, bring forth." Jubilee = the flowing forth / bringing forth. Also: ram's horn, trumpet. The jubilee IS the instrument and the event.

**Evidence the 50th year = year 1 of the next cycle (not an extra inserted year):**
- Lev 25:8 counts 49 years explicitly.
- The 8th day pattern: 8th day = 1st day of new cycle (circumcision on 8th day, Tabernacles 8th day assembly). The 50th year follows the same logic.
- **Proven by historical calibration** (see below): a 50-year system with resetting 7-year cycles causes sabbatical years to drift out of alignment with known historical anchors. The 49-year continuous system (where 49 = 7x7, so sabbatical and jubilee cycles are permanently synchronized) matches all anchor points. The jubilee year IS year 1 of the next cycle — the 7-year count never breaks.

### Shavuot Counting — The 49+1 Pattern at Day-Level
The same pattern operates at the day/week scale, confirming the structure:
**Lev 23:15-16** — "Count... seven sabbaths shall be complete: even unto the morrow after the seventh sabbath shall ye number fifty days." The unit of counting = the WEEK (sabbath), not the day. Seven complete weeks = 49 days. The 50th day = first fruits, but ALSO day 1 of the next 7-week count.

Three successive 49+1 harvest cycles:
1. 49 days + 1 → First fruits of **wheat** (Shavuot/Pentecost)
2. 49 days + 1 → First fruits of **new wine** (the feast Aaron declared "to YHWH," Exod 32:5; LXX Exod 32:18 "the voice of those beginning with wine")
3. 49 days + 1 → First fruits of **oil**

The counting unit in both Shavuot and Jubilee is the **completed sabbath** (week or sabbatical year), not the individual unit (day or year). Seven completed sabbaths = 49. Then the reset/firstfruit. The jubilee year-cycle mirrors the Shavuot day-cycle at a larger scale.

For the full treatment of the New Wine Feast and the three-harvest pattern, see [extra/e05_FirstFruitsNewWine.md](extra/e05_FirstFruitsNewWine.md).

## What Happens at Jubilee — The Six Resets

1. **Debts cancelled** — Lev 25:28 "in the jubile it shall go out, and he shall return unto his possession"
2. **Slaves freed** — Lev 25:40,54 "shall serve thee unto the year of jubile... he shall go out"
3. **Land returned** — Lev 25:10,13 "return every man unto his possession"
4. **Family restored** — Lev 25:10 "return every man unto his family"
5. **Liberty proclaimed** — Lev 25:10 H1865 *deror* throughout all the land
6. **No sowing/reaping** — Lev 25:11 "ye shall not sow, neither reap... nor GATHER{H1219 *batsar*} the grapes"

Note: H1219 *batsar* in Lev 25:11 = same word as Isa 22:10 (gather grapes / fortify). In the jubilee year, you do NOT gather the grapes. The grape-harvest of wrath (Rev 14:19-20) and the jubilee operate in the same vocabulary.

## Anchor Points and Chronological Proof

### Counting Rules
- **Sabbatical years**: continuous 7-year count from 1406 BC (year 1). Sabbaticals at years 7, 14, 21, ... (every year divisible by 7). Count NEVER resets.
- **Jubilee years**: every 49 years from 1405 BC. Jubilee year = year 1 of the next cycle.
- **Year reckoning**: spring to spring (agricultural year). Observance of sabbatical begins the prior fall (no planting in fall for spring harvest). So Tishri/Day of Atonement of the 6th year = start of sabbatical observance.

### "When ye come into the land" — Lev 25:2
Counting begins at entry into the land. ~1406 BC.

### 7th year — land rested from war
Joshua 11:23 — "the land rested{H8252 *shaqat*} from war." After ~7 years of conquest = first sabbatical year.
Joshua 14:7,10 — Caleb: 40 at Kadesh-Barnea, 85 at land division. 45 years = 38 wilderness + 7 conquest.

### Historical Anchor Points — 49-year System Verified

**35 BC sabbatical (Josephus):** Year 1372 from 1406 BC. 1372/7 = 196. **Exact sabbatical.** Josephus records the sabbath year at Day of Atonement 36 BC = fall observance (no planting) for the spring 35 BC sabbatical year. ✓

**29 AD sabbatical (Yeshua):** Year 1435 from 1406 BC. 1435/7 = 205. **Exact sabbatical.** Luke 4:18-19: Yeshua declares "the acceptable year of the LORD." Compatible with John 2:20 "46 years building the temple" in spring 30 AD (Herod began ~16 BC: 16+30 = 46). ✓

**573 BC jubilee (Ezek 40:1):** "In the five and twentieth year of our captivity, in the beginning of the year, in the tenth day of the month, in the fourteenth year after that the city was smitten." Year 834 from 1406 BC. 834/49 = 17.02. ~17th jubilee. From 1405 BC jubilee start: (573-1405) = 832/49 = 16.98. Very close to 17th. ✓

**1535 AD (Suleiman rebuilds Jerusalem):** 2940 years from 1405 BC. 2940/49 = 60. **Exact 60th jubilee.** Historically documented: Ottoman Sultan Suleiman orders foreign workers to rebuild Jerusalem's walls 1535-1541 AD. Isa 60:10 "the sons of strangers shall build up thy walls." Dan 9:25 alternative reading: "jubilee sixty and the second [decree]." See [words/DANIEL-9.md](words/DANIEL-9.md). ✓

**2025 AD:** 3430 years from 1405 BC. 3430/49 = 70. **Exact 70th jubilee.** Jer 25:11-12 "when seventy years are accomplished." If year → jubilee prophetically: 70 jubilees from entry into the land. ✓

### 50-Year Alternative — Falsified

Tested: if jubilee = 50-year cycle with 7-year sabbatical count resetting after each jubilee (year 1 of new cycle = jubilee + 1):
- **35 BC**: NOT a sabbatical. Nearest: 39 BC and 32 BC (off by 2-3 years). ✗
- **29 AD**: NOT a sabbatical. Nearest: 24 AD and 31 AD (off by 2 years). ✗
- **60th jubilee**: 1595 AD (no historical event; misses Suleiman by 60 years). ✗

The 50-year system fails because each inserted jubilee year shifts the sabbatical count by +1 year. After 27 jubilees (1406 BC to 36 BC), the drift = 27 years, destroying alignment. The 49-year system (49 = 7x7) keeps sabbaticals permanently synchronized — no drift, ever. **The 7-year count never breaks.**

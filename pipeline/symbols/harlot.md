## Context Bag

**LEXICAL:** H2181 *zanah* = "to commit adultery; figuratively, to commit idolatry (Israel being regarded as the spouse of YHWH)" — 82× OT. G4204 *porne* = "strumpet; figuratively, an idolater" — 12× NT. Strong's itself notes figurative = idolatry. Derivatives: H2183 *zanun* (adultery/idolatry), H2184 *zenuth* (infidelity/idolatry), H8457 *taznuth* (harlotry/idolatry).

**DEFINING EQUATION — Isa 1:21:** "How is the FAITHFUL (H539 *aman*) city become a HARLOT (H2181)! It was full of judgment (H4941 *mishpat*); righteousness lodged in it; but now murderers." Explicit: FAITHFUL → HARLOT is the transition. The faithful city (JERUSALEM) was defined by justice and righteousness; the harlot city is defined by their absence. HARLOT = the state of having abandoned faithfulness. Opposite of H539 *aman* (to be firm, faithful, to support).

**DEFINING EQUATION — Deut 31:16:** "this people will rise up, and GO A WHORING (H2181) after the gods of the strangers... and will FORSAKE me, and BREAK my COVENANT (H1285)." Three-way equivalence: go a whoring = forsake God = break covenant. HARLOTRY = COVENANT BREAKING explicitly stated.

**CONCENTRATED PATTERN — "GO A WHORING AFTER":** Exod 34:15-16 (after their gods) || Lev 17:7 (after devils) || Lev 20:5 (after Molech) || Lev 20:6 (after wizards) || Deut 31:16 (after gods of strangers) || Judg 2:17 (after other gods) || Judg 8:27, 8:33 (after ephod, after Baalim) || 1Chr 5:25 (after gods of the people) || Ezek 6:9 (after their idols) || Ezek 20:30 (after abominations) || Hos 4:12 (from under their God) || Hos 9:1 (from thy God). Always: "whoring AFTER" other gods/masters OR "whoring FROM" the true God. The preposition defines the symbol: AWAY from covenant, TOWARD other allegiance.

**NUM 15:39 — EYES AND HEART CONNECTION:** "seek not after your own heart and your own eyes, after which ye go a whoring (H2181)." Located within tzitzit command (H3671 *kanaph* = WINGS = covenant reminder). Following own heart/eyes instead of commandments = harlotry. Direct link: WINGS remind you of commandments to PREVENT harlotry. HARLOT = one who has abandoned the commandments to follow own desires.

**MARRIAGE METAPHOR FRAME:** God = Husband, Israel = Wife, Covenant = MARRIAGE. HARLOT = the wife who has broken the marriage covenant. Eze 16:8 — "spread my skirt (H3671 *kanaph* = WINGS) over thee... entered into COVENANT" = marriage. Same word (WINGS) that in Num 15:39 prevents harlotry. The MARRIAGE (covenant) is entered under WINGS; the HARLOT is one who leaves from under WINGS.

**HOSEA — MARRIAGE TO THE HARLOT:** Hos 1:2 — "wife of whoredoms... for the land hath committed great whoredom, DEPARTING FROM the LORD." God commands Hosea to marry an unfaithful woman to enact Israel's covenant infidelity. Hos 2:5 — "I will go after my lovers, that give me my bread and water" (seeking provision from other nations/gods). Hos 3:3 — restoration condition: "thou shalt not play the harlot." The entire book: covenant wife → harlotry → punishment → restoration.

**EZEK 16 + 23 — JERUSALEM AND SAMARIA AS HARLOTS:** Ezek 16:15-35 — Jerusalem took covenant gifts and used them for harlotry. Ezek 16:33-34 — "thou givest thy gifts to all thy lovers" — REVERSED harlotry: usually harlot receives hire, but Jerusalem PAYS other nations. Ezek 23 — Aholah (Samaria) and Aholibah (Jerusalem) = two sisters who "committed whoredoms in Egypt" (v.3) and "played the harlot when she was mine" (v.5). The harlot was GOD'S WIFE — not a stranger. The betrayal is from within the covenant.

**NATIONS AS HARLOTS:** Isa 23:15-17 — Tyre "shall commit fornication with all kingdoms of the world." Nah 3:4 — Nineveh "the wellfavoured harlot, mistress of witchcrafts, that SELLETH NATIONS through her whoredoms." Rev 17:1-5 — Babylon "the great whore that sitteth upon many waters" + "MOTHER OF HARLOTS." Rev 17:15 — "waters where the whore sitteth = peoples, multitudes, nations, tongues." Rev 19:2 — "judged the great whore which did corrupt the earth with her fornication." Pattern: harlot cities/empires LEAD OTHERS into covenant breaking — they are not merely unfaithful themselves but produce unfaithfulness in others.

**RAHAB — HARLOT REVERSED:** Josh 2:1, 6:17, 6:22, 6:25 — Rahab the harlot, saved because she hid the spies. Heb 11:31 — "By FAITH (G4102) the harlot Rahab perished not." Jas 2:25 — "Rahab the harlot justified by WORKS." A covenant breaker who REVERSED course through faith and works — entered Israel's covenant. Title "harlot" REMAINS even after conversion → it marks WHAT SHE WAS, showing transformation is possible. She enters the lineage of Christ (Mat 1:5).

**MAT 21:31-32 — HARLOTS ENTER THE KINGDOM:** "publicans and harlots go into the kingdom of God before you." The harlots BELIEVED John; the chief priests did NOT. The covenant breakers repented; the covenant keepers (who thought they were faithful) did not. Pattern: the title "harlot" can be shed through repentance; the title "faithful" can be lost through self-righteousness.

**1CO 6:15-16 — BODY OF CHRIST vs BODY OF HARLOT:** "Shall I take the members of Christ, and make them members of a harlot?" Joined to harlot = "one body" (G1520 *hen* + G4983 *soma*). Joined to Lord = "one spirit" (G1520 *hen* + G4151 *pneuma*). Exclusive union: you are members of EITHER Christ (covenant) OR the harlot (covenant breaking). Cannot be both.

**DOVE CONNECTION:** Hos 7:11 — "$ephraim is like a silly DOVE without heart" — going to Egypt and Assyria. The dove (faithful covenant devotion) acting like a harlot (going to foreign powers). DOVE-capacity misdirected = harlot behavior. DOVE = faithful covenant devotion; HARLOT = its opposite. Same person can embody either quality.

**FOREHEAD — MARKING:** Jer 3:3 — "thou hadst a WHORE'S FOREHEAD (H4696 *metsach*), thou refusedst to be ashamed." Rev 17:5 — "upon her FOREHEAD was a name written, MYSTERY BABYLON THE GREAT, MOTHER OF HARLOTS." Compare Rev 14:1 — servants with Father's NAME in their FOREHEADS. The FOREHEAD = visible allegiance. Harlot's forehead = shameless display of false allegiance. Servant's forehead = MARK of covenant allegiance. co: MARK, NAME.

3. **Unfaithful wife** — Too narrow. Only works in marriage-metaphor contexts. Doesn't explain Rahab, Mat 21:31, Proverbs.
4. **Idolater** — Close but overlaps with IDOLATRY symbol. HARLOT = the PERSON; IDOLATRY = the underlying condition; FORNICATION = the ACT. HARLOT emphasizes BROKEN ALLEGIANCE more than mere worship of other gods.
5. **Foreign alliance maker** — Partial. Works for Tyre, Nahum, some Hosea. But doesn't explain Lev/Deut legal contexts, Rahab.
6. **Deceiver/seducer** — Partial: Nah 3:4 "mistress of witchcrafts." But harlotry is about allegiance, not deception.
7. **One who sells covenant for profit** — Interesting: "hire of a whore" language (Mic 1:7, Deut 23:18, Hos 9:1). But too narrow.

## Pattern Summary

HARLOT = covenant breaker — the person who has abandoned exclusive allegiance to God to serve other masters. Distinguished from: FORNICATION (= the ACT of covenant breaking / idolatry); IDOLATRY (= the underlying condition / self-rule); VIRGIN (= the faithful partner who has NOT broken covenant). The MARRIAGE metaphor frames everything: God = Husband, Israel = Wife, Covenant = Marriage. The harlot is the wife who has left. HARLOT applies to persons (Rahab, Mat 21:31), cities (Jerusalem Isa 1:21, Tyre Isa 23, Nineveh Nah 3:4), and systems (Babylon Rev 17). Cities/systems that are harlots PRODUCE harlotry in others — they are "mother of harlots." Harlotry is reversible: Rahab, the publicans and harlots of Mat 21:31, and Hosea's wife all demonstrate that the covenant breaker can return. The DOVE connection (Hos 7:11) shows that the same entity can embody dove-quality OR harlot-quality depending on the direction of their allegiance.

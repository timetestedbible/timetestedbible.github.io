## Context Bag

**H3290 Ya'aqob** = from H6117 aqab (seize by heel, supplant, circumvent, restrain). ~349× OT. G2384 Iakob = ~25× NT. **G2385 Iakobos = "James"** — English hides that "James" IS "Jacob." Two apostles bear this name. Every "James" in the NT is a Jacob.

**ROOT FAMILY — H6117 and derivatives:**
H6117 aqab — seize by heel, supplant, circumvent. 5× OT.
H6119 aqeb — heel (as protuberant); track, rear of army.
H6121 aqob — crooked, deceitful, fraudulent. 3× OT: **Jer 17:9, Isa 40:4, Hos 6:8.**
H6122 oqbah — trickery, subtilty. 1×: 2Ki 10:19.

**ROOT IN OTHER CONTEXTS — THE KEY FINDS:**

Jer 17:9 — "The heart is H6121 (aqob/deceitful) above all things." The human HEART is described with Jacob's root word. The Jacob-nature is not one man's condition — it's the universal human condition. co: HEART.

Isa 40:4 — "the H6121 (crooked) shall be made straight." Messianic preparation: the Jacob-nature (crooked) will be straightened. co: WAY. The Jacob-root word in the passage about preparing the LORD's way.

Jer 9:4 — "every brother will utterly H6117 (supplant)." The Jacob-action universalized. ALL brothers supplant — not just Jacob. The verb is doubled (infinitive absolute) for emphasis. co: DECEIVE.

2Ki 10:19 — Jehu acts in H6122 (subtilty/trickery) to destroy Baal worshippers. The Jacob-method used for God's purposes. Grasping/scheming CAN serve God — method is not inherently condemned.

Hos 12:3 — "took his brother by the H6117 (heel) in the womb." Hosea recounts the defining act. v4 adds: prevailed by strength AND wept/supplicated. Both together.

## NAME USAGE — WHAT THE TEXT ACTUALLY DOES

**INTERCHANGEABLE (same person, same moment, both names):**
Gen 35:22 — "when Israel dwelt... sons of Jacob were twelve." Mid-verse switch.
Gen 46:2 — "God spake unto Israel... said, Jacob, Jacob." God addresses Israel-person as Jacob.
Gen 46:5 — "sons of Israel carried Jacob their father." Both names, one sentence.
Gen 48:2 — "Israel strengthened himself" / told "Jacob." Both, same scene.
Gen 49:2 — "sons of Jacob... hearken unto Israel your father." Both, same speech.
Exo 19:3 — "say to house of Jacob, tell children of Israel." Both, same audience at Sinai.

These are NOT choosing one name for flesh and one for covenant. They use both names for the same person/people in the same moment without distinction.

**"GOD OF JACOB" — 22× (God's permanent self-identification):**
Exo 3:6,15 — God's eternal memorial name: "God of Abraham, Isaac, and JACOB." Not "God of Israel" — God of JACOB. co: NAME.
Ps 46:7,11 — "the God of Jacob is our REFUGE."
Ps 81:4 — "a statute for Israel, a law of the God of Jacob." Both names, same verse, legal context.
Ps 146:5 — "Happy is he that hath the God of Jacob for his help."
Isa 2:3 / Mic 4:2 — nations go to "the house of the God of JACOB." Eschatological. Nations drawn to the God identified by the "supplanter" name.
2Sam 23:1 — "the anointed of the God of Jacob, sweet psalmist of Israel." Both names in David's last words.
Acts 7:32,46 — Stephen uses "God of Jacob" in his defense.

If Jacob = flesh/weakness, God permanently identifies Himself by the flesh/weakness name. This breaks the framework.

**POSITIVE USES OF "JACOB" (not judgment/flesh contexts):**
Num 23:21 — Balaam: "He hath NOT beheld INIQUITY in Jacob." Jacob = no iniquity seen. Positive.
Num 23:23 — "no enchantment against Jacob." Protected.
Num 23:10 — "who can count the DUST of Jacob?" Abundance.
Gen 49:24 — "the mighty God of Jacob; from thence the shepherd, the stone of Israel." Jacob paired with God's might.
Ps 22:23 — "seed of Jacob, glorify him." Positive call to praise.
Isa 45:19 — "I said not unto the seed of Jacob, Seek ye me in vain." Positive promise.
Jer 33:26 — God will NOT cast away "the seed of Jacob." Permanence.
Mal 1:2 / Rom 9:13 — "Jacob have I loved." God's sovereign love directed at the supplanter-name.

**JUDGMENT USES OF "JACOB":**
Hos 12:2 — "LORD hath controversy with Judah, punish Jacob." Judgment.
Isa 43:22,28 — "thou hast not called upon me, O Jacob... I have given Jacob to the curse." Judgment.
Isa 48:1 — "house of Jacob, called by name of Israel, not in truth nor righteousness." Gap between name and character.
Jer 30:7 — "Jacob's trouble; but he shall be saved out of it." Judgment + deliverance.

**JUDGMENT USES OF "ISRAEL":**
Isa 43:28 — "given Jacob to the curse, ISRAEL to reproaches." BOTH names under judgment, same verse.
Hos 5:3 — "I know Ephraim, and ISRAEL is not hid from me: O Ephraim, thou hast committed whoredom, ISRAEL is defiled." Israel = defiled.
Amos 9:7 — God humbles Israel by comparing exodus to other nations' migrations.
Hos 9:1 — "Rejoice not, O ISRAEL, for joy... thou hast gone a whoring."

Both names appear in positive and negative contexts. The text does not consistently assign "Jacob = negative" and "Israel = positive."

**ISAIAH'S PARALLELISM:**
Isa 41:8 — "Israel my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen." 
Isa 44:1 — "Jacob my servant, Israel whom I have chosen."
Isa 45:4 — "Jacob my servant's sake, Israel mine elect."
NOTE: In 41:8, Israel = servant, Jacob = chosen. In 44:1, Jacob = servant, Israel = chosen. The assignments SWAP. This confirms synonymous parallelism — the names are interchangeable, not carrying distinct theological weight.

## PATTERNS

1. **UNIVERSAL HUMAN CONDITION.** The Jacob-root (H6117/H6121) describes all human hearts (Jer 17:9) and all brotherhood (Jer 9:4). Jacob is not one man's flaw — it's the species' condition. God chooses it, names Himself by it, works through it.

2. **GOD IDENTIFIES BY "JACOB."** 22× "God of Jacob" — His permanent memorial name (Exo 3:15). If Jacob = only flesh/weakness, God's self-identification makes no sense. The name carries BOTH the human condition AND God's choice of it.

3. **INTERCHANGEABLE IN PRACTICE.** Gen 35:22, 46:2, 46:5, 49:2, Exo 19:3 — the text switches between names mid-verse without apparent distinction. The parallelism in Isaiah swaps the assignments (41:8 vs 44:1). This is synonymous usage, not contrastive.

4. **BOTH NAMES IN POSITIVE AND NEGATIVE CONTEXTS.** "No iniquity in Jacob" (Num 23:21) vs "punish Jacob" (Hos 12:2). "Israel is defiled" (Hos 5:3) vs "Israel mine elect" (Isa 45:4). Neither name is consistently positive or negative. The text uses whichever name fits the poetic or narrative need.

5. **THE CROOKED MADE STRAIGHT.** Isa 40:4 — H6121 (Jacob-root) made straight (H4334 mishor). The messianic preparation = transforming the Jacob-nature. Not erasing it — straightening it. The crooked path becomes the level way. co: WAY.

6. **JACOB LOVED — SOVEREIGN CHOICE.** Mal 1:2, Rom 9:13. God chose the heel-catcher, the supplanter, the one named for grasping — over the one who let go (Esau). The choice is of the one who WANTED it (even by wrong methods).

7. **JACOB'S TROUBLE = DELIVERANCE THROUGH SUFFERING.** Jer 30:7 — trouble is Jacob's; salvation comes through it. Jer 30:10 — "fear not, my servant Jacob." Even in the trouble passage, Jacob = God's servant.

8. **"WORM JACOB" (Isa 41:14)** — lowest self-description paired with God's help. The weakness is real; the help is real. Both.

9. **JAMES = JACOB.** G2385 Iakobos. English hides this. Two of the twelve apostles are "Jacobs." The "supplanter" name persists into the NT as a name people bear — not erased, not shameful.

10. **JER 17:9 + GEN 32:27.** God asks "what is thy name?" and Jacob confesses: "Jacob" (= I am the supplanter/crooked one). Jer 17:9: the heart IS aqob (= Jacob-natured). The confession at the wrestling = acknowledging the universal human condition. Transformation begins with naming what you are.

## Context Bag

**ROOTS:** G3653 olynthos = "an unripe (because out of season) fig." Uncertain derivation. 1× NT (Rev 6:13). Winter fig that forms in autumn, clings through cold, never ripens, falls in spring. H6291 pag = "crude; an unripe fig" from unused root meaning "to be torpid/sluggish." 1× OT (Song 2:13). H5309 nephel = "something fallen, i.e. an abortion/untimely birth" from H5307 naphal = "to fall." 3× OT (Job 3:16, Ps 58:8, Ecc 6:3). H5034 nabel = "to wilt, fall away, fade, be foolish" — the verb in Isa 34:4 for the falling fig: "all their host shall nabel as a nabel-ing fig from the fig tree." Triple nabel root in one verse = alliterative emphasis. KEY: naphal (to fall) roots both nephel (untimely birth) and the falling-stars verb in Isa 34:4. The untimely birth IS "the fallen thing."

**AGRICULTURAL CYCLE — THE BACKBONE:**
Autumn (Sep-Oct): small figs form on current-year wood = future olynthos. Winter (Nov-Feb): olynthos cling to bare branches, never ripen. Early spring (Mar-Apr, Nisan): fig trees leaf out; old olynthos shaken loose by spring winds; new pag (green figs) begin forming on old wood. Late spring (May-Jun): bikkurah (firstripe) matures — breba crop. Summer (Jun-Sep): main crop (te'enah) = full harvest.

**BIOLOGICAL MECHANISM — WHY THEY FALL:** Auxin (growth hormone) flows from fruit through abscission zone, keeping it attached. When fruit stops developing (olynthos never ripen), auxin decreases. Reduced auxin makes abscission zone sensitive to ethylene (shedding hormone). In spring, tree redirects auxin to NEW buds/leaves → old fruit gets less auxin → attachment weakens → wind dislodges. The tree's own new growth causes the old fruit to fall. Life itself is the agent that sheds what never matured.

**REV 6:12-17 (SIXTH SEAL) — COLLAPSE OF GOVERNING ORDER:** Sun black as sackcloth, moon as blood, stars fall like olynthos shaken by great wind, heaven rolled as scroll, mountains/islands moved, kings-to-bondmen hide. "The great day of his wrath is come." The olynthos falls during total dismantling of power structures. Sun/moon darkening vocabulary = Isa 13:10 ("burden of BABYLON"), Ezek 32:7 (judgment of Pharaoh/Egypt), Amos 8:9 (same chapter as qayits/qets pun). Every OT cosmic-sign passage describes fall of a world empire. co: SUN_MOON_STARS, WIND, EARTHQUAKE.

**ISA 34:1-8 — JUDGMENT ON NATIONS:** God's indignation on all nations. Host of heaven H5034 nabel (wilt/fall) "as a nabel-ing fig from the fig tree." v.5: sword comes on Edom. v.8: "day of the LORD's vengeance, year of recompenses for the controversy of Zion." Hebrew Gospels data: Rev 6:13 preserves Isa 34:4 vocabulary — withered-fig (nabel) rather than Greek's shaken-olynthos. Hebrew emphasizes withering; Greek emphasizes violent shaking.

**SONG 2:8-17 — BRIDEGROOM CALLS BRIDE OUT OF WINTER:** "Rise up, my love, come away. The winter is past, the rain is over and gone." v.13: "The fig tree putteth forth her H6291 pag (green/unripe figs), and the vines with tender grape give a good smell. Arise, my love, come away." The pag = same developmental stage as olynthos but heading TOWARD ripeness. Season marker for the bridegroom's summons. co: VINE, MARRIAGE.

**MK 11:13 / MAT 21:19 — CURSED FIG TREE BEFORE PASSOVER:** Multi-day event on Nisan 10-11. Timeline: Nisan 9 arrival at Bethany (Jn 12:1). Nisan 10 triumphal entry / lamb selection day (Jn 12:12, Exo 12:3) — enters temple, "looked round about upon all things" (Mk 11:11). Nisan 11 fig tree cursed on way from Bethany (Mk 11:12-14). Nisan 12 found "dried up from the roots" (Mk 11:20) — withered overnight. Nisan 14 Passover. Tree has leaves, no fruit. Mark: "the G2540 kairos (appointed time) of figs was not yet." At this season a living leafy tree should have at minimum lingering olynthos. Finding nothing = not even old-season holdover fruit. Leaves promise what the tree cannot deliver. Tree found "dried up from the roots" (Mk 11:20). co: FAITH, MOUNTAIN, CURSE. Aaron's rod contrast: dead wood → buds, blossoms, almonds overnight (Num 17:8). Opposite outcome, same timeframe. ALMOND = life from dead wood; cursed fig = death from living wood.

**PS 58:1-9 — CORRUPT RULERS WHO DO NOT JUDGE UPRIGHTLY:** v.1: "Do ye judge uprightly, O ye sons of men?" v.8: "like the H5309 nephel (untimely birth) of a woman, that they may not see the sun." The untimely birth = authority that never reaches full expression. David prays for removal of corrupt judges. co: SERPENT (v.4), LION (v.6).

**JOB 3:11-19 — JOB WISHES HE HAD NEVER BEEN BORN:** v.16: "Or as an hidden H5309 nephel I had not been; as infants which never saw H216 light." The stillborn = preferable to life under oppression. v.17-19: "the wicked cease from troubling; the weary be at rest." The nephel never saw light = never participated in the visible world. co: LIGHT, DARKNESS, REST.

**ECC 6:1-6 — ABUNDANCE WITHOUT SATISFACTION:** Man with riches, 100 children, long life — "his soul be not filled with good, and also that he have no burial." v.3: "H5309 nephel is better than he." v.5: "hath not seen the sun, nor known any thing: this hath more rest." Potential that never produced meaning. Even 2000 years of abundance = futility without satisfaction.

**NAH 3:12 — FALL OF NINEVEH (ASSYRIAN EMPIRE):** "Thy strongholds like fig trees with H1063 bikkurah (firstripe figs): if shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater." Impressive exterior collapses at first pressure. World empire consumed instantly. The FIRSTRIPE that falls = promising start that cannot withstand shaking. co: WIND (implied shaking).

**HOS 9:10 — ISRAEL PRECIOUS AT FIRST, THEN CORRUPTED:** "I saw your fathers as the H1063 bikkurah in the fig tree at her first time: but they went to Baalpeor." First-fruit promise → immediate failure. v.7-9: "the days of visitation are come, Israel shall know it." co: VINE.

**AMOS 8:1-2,9 — SUMMER FRUIT = THE END:** God shows basket of H7019 qayits (summer fruit). "The H7093 qets (end) is come upon my people." Qayits/qets near-homophones = enacted pun. Summer = the end. v.9: "I will cause the sun to go down at noon" — sun-darkening in SAME chapter. co: SUN_MOON_STARS, HARVEST.

**MAT 24:32-34 — FIG TREE PARABLE IN OLIVET DISCOURSE:** After full sequence of signs (vv4-31 including sun/moon darkened v.29), the fig tree parable: "when branch tender, puts forth leaves, ye know G2330 theros (summer) is nigh." Theros shares root with G2325 therizo (to reap) = summer IS harvest-time. v.34: "this G1074 genea (generation) shall not pass till all be fulfilled." Genea from G1085 genos (kind/stock). Read in fig tree's terms: one season's crop, one window, one outcome. Pipeline study at contexts/Mat/24.032-051.md covers full context. G2540 kairos appears in both Mk 11:13 ("time of figs") and Mat 24:45 ("due season") — same word linking cursed fig to faithful servant's proper timing. Jer 8:20 = inverse: "harvest past, summer ended, NOT SAVED."

**WIND = SPIRIT CONNECTION:** Rev 6:13 uses G417 anemos (wind). In Hebrew, wind = H7307 ruach = spirit/breath/wind (same word). The $wind study establishes: wind IS spirit — identity, not metaphor. The "mighty wind" that shakes olynthos IS the Spirit. Same ruach does two things: shakes loose what never matured (Rev 6:13) AND gives life to the dead (Ezek 37:9-10 "Come from the four winds, O breath, breathe upon these slain"). The Spirit that resurrects = the Spirit that dislodges the unripe. Maps onto biological mechanism: tree's own life force (redirected to new growth) is what causes old fruit to detach.

**ALMOND OVERLAP:** H8247 shaqed (almond) from H8245 shaqad (to watch/wake). Blooms Jan-Feb on bare wood — earliest tree to wake. At same time, olynthos cling dark and unripe on bare fig branches. Same landscape, opposite trajectories: almond = dead wood → life (Num 17:8 Aaron's rod). Olynthos = potential → never ripened → falls. The Watcher-tree wakes and announces God is watching. The untimely fig is what the Watcher finds when He inspects.

**"NEVER AGAIN" CURSE — FIG TREE AND BABYLON:** Mat 21:19 "no fruit... henceforward for ever." Mk 11:14 "no man eat fruit... hereafter for ever." Rev 18:14 "the FRUITS... departed... find them no more at all." Rev 18:21-23 "no more at all" ×6: music, craft, millstone, light, bridegroom/bride. Jer 25:10 = OT source for Rev 18 list. Same formula: permanent cessation of productive activity. Both = systems with appearance of life but no substance, receiving "never again" sentence.

**NAPHAL / NEPHEL CONNECTION:** H5307 naphal = common verb "to fall" (~400× OT, general purpose). But H5309 nephel = rare derived noun "the fallen thing" (3× OT only: Job 3:16, Ps 58:8, Ecc 6:3). Isa 21:9 "Babylon is NAPHAL, is NAPHAL" — doubled = irrevocable/complete (Hebrew doubling intensifies). The common verb receives an uncommon sentence: Babylon declared twice the nephel — the fallen thing. Rev 14:8 + 18:2 quote Isa 21:9 directly. The etymological link is real (nephel from naphal, Babylon naphal-naphal) but naphal itself is too common to function as a specialized keyword. The connection is in the rare noun nephel applied to Babylon through the doubled verb.

**PATTERN:** (1) The untimely fig = potential that formed in the wrong season, clung but never matured, shed when new-season life arrives. (2) The biological mechanism IS the meaning: the tree's own new growth redirects life away from old fruit → attachment weakens → wind dislodges. Life itself judges what never matured. (3) Wind/Spirit = same ruach. The Spirit that brings new life is the same force that sheds the old. (4) The olynthos falls in the SAME event as sun/moon darkening — and every OT use of cosmic-sign language describes fall of a world empire (Isa 13 = Babylon). (5) Summer (qayits) = the end (qets). The fig tree's leaves signal that harvest/end is near. (6) The cursed fig tree at Passover season had not even the olynthos — total emptiness behind the display of leaves. (7) The almond wakes first, announcing God's watchfulness, while the olynthos still clings in the same landscape. (8) The untimely birth (H5309 nephel from naphal = to fall) appears in contexts of corrupt authority removed (Ps 58), life that never saw light (Job 3), and abundance without fruit (Ecc 6). (9) G1074 genea ("this generation") read in the fig tree's own terms = one crop season. One window to mature. One outcome.

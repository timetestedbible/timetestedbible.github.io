## Context Bag

**H3478** = H8280 sarah (prevail/prince) + H410 El (God). Strong's: "a SYMBOLICAL name of Jacob; also TYPICALLY of his posterity." G2474: "literally or FIGURATIVELY." Strong's own entry flags both literal and symbolic uses. ~2297× total.

Gen 32:24-30 — name given for character: persistence + contending + prevailing despite injury. co: NAME, COVENANT. NOTABLE: Gen 35:10 declares "no more Jacob" yet text keeps using both names (Gen 46:2 "Jacob, Jacob"). Both names persist — the declaration doesn't erase the old.

Hos 12:3-4 — adds weeping + supplication to wrestling. Text presents strength AND prayer together. Neither alone.

Exo 4:22 — national entity called God's firstborn son. co: FIRSTBORN. National and relational coexist — same entity, both dimensions.

Exo 12:38 — mixed multitude left Egypt WITH Israel. Non-descendants present from the founding event.

Exo 12:48-49 — stranger who keeps Passover + circumcision = "as one born in the land." ONE LAW for homeborn and stranger. co: MARK(circumcision), COVENANT. **The Torah builds the inclusion mechanism into the first Passover.** Not a later accommodation — foundational.

Num 15:15 — "as ye are, so shall the stranger be before the LORD." Repeated principle. Law makes no distinction between born-Israelite and joined-stranger.

Deut 7:7-8 — election grounded in two things: God's love + oath to the FATHERS. The oath is lineage-linked. Election flows through a specific family because of a promise made to that family. But the same Torah provides for others to enter (Exo 12:48).

Isa 49:3-6 — **KEY PARADOX:** servant IS Israel (v3) AND is sent TO Israel (v5-6). Israel = both the identity and the mission-target simultaneously. Multi-level: covenant identity + the individual who embodies it. v6 extends mission to Gentiles. co: SERVANT, LIGHT.

Isa 41:8, 43:1, 44:1-2, 44:21, 45:4 — Jacob ‖ Israel in Hebrew poetic parallelism. Standard parallelism = synonymous. Two names for the same entity. co: JACOB.

Rom 9:6-8 — states: flesh alone ≠ Israel; promise = what counts for seed. Examples (Isaac/Ishmael, Jacob/Esau) are WITHIN lineage — God choosing which descendants. Text says: flesh without promise = no. Silent on: promise without flesh = ?. Rom 11 grafting addresses from another angle.

Gal 6:16 — kai is grammatically ambiguous (epexegetic or additive). Text unresolved. Both readings possible.

Rom 2:28-29 — states: outward alone ≠ real; inward = real. Phil 3:3: "no confidence in flesh." OT already commands heart circumcision (Deut 10:16, 30:6, Jer 4:4) without revoking physical. Text says: outward without inward = no. NOTE: if the ekklesia = qahal (see symbols/church-ekklesia — same word, Acts 7:38 confirms), then the qahal's own admission process (Exo 12:48: circumcision + Passover) is the established mechanism. Paul is speaking within the qahal framework, stating what makes the identity real — not replacing the qahal with a new category. The qahal always needed heart (Deut 10:16); Paul intensifies what was already required.

Rom 11:17-24 — olive TREE is Israel's; Gentiles grafted IN to it; natural branches can be regrafted; root + fatness = theirs. co: TREE, CHURCH_EKKLESIA. Eph 2:12: Gentiles were aliens from the COMMONWEALTH — brought near, not given a separate structure. The tree is not replaced; it's joined. This is the same pattern as Exo 12:48 (stranger joins Israel) now described with the grafting metaphor. And since ekklesia = qahal (see symbols/church-ekklesia), the "church" Gentiles enter IS Israel's assembly. Rom 11:25-26: all Israel saved — in context of Gentile fullness + remnant returning.

Jn 1:47 — G230 alethos (truly) + G1388 dolos (guile/deceit). ANTI-JACOB marker: Jacob's pre-name-change trait was guile. "Israelite indeed" = character matches the name. Text praises character as genuineness-marker in a man who is also a physical Israelite. Silent on whether either dimension alone suffices.

Hos 1:9-10 — Lo-ammi judgment on NORTHERN TRIBES (context = Ephraim). Paul quotes in Rom 9:25-26. AMBIGUITY: could mean (a) Gentiles included, (b) scattered northern Israelites called back, (c) both — since scattered tribes became indistinguishable from nations. Text supports multiple readings.

1Ki 18:31, Mat 19:28, Rev 7:4, Rev 21:12 — 12-tribe structure persists: Elijah → Jesus → Revelation. Structural, not merely historical. co: NAME.

Jer 31:31-33 — new covenant explicitly WITH Israel and Judah. Law on hearts. co: COVENANT. Jer 31:36-37 ties permanence to NATIONAL entity ("cease from being a NATION").

Ezek 37:11, 21-22 — two nations → one. Division healed. co: EPHRAIM, JUDAH.

Amos 9:7-9 — compares Israel's exodus to other nations' migrations. v8-9: "will NOT utterly destroy... sift among ALL nations." Humbles but does not erase.

Ruth 1:16 — Moabitess declares "thy people my people, thy God my God." Enters Israel; becomes great-grandmother of David (Ruth 4:17). In Jesus' genealogy (Mat 1:5). co: NAME, COVENANT. Non-descendant in the messianic line.

Josh 14:14 — Caleb the KENIZZITE inherits Hebron in Israel. "Wholly followed the LORD God of Israel." Non-Jacobite given tribal inheritance for covenant faithfulness. co: FAITH.

Isa 56:3,6-7 — stranger who joins the LORD + keeps sabbath + takes hold of covenant → brought to holy mountain; given NAME better than sons/daughters; everlasting name not cut off. "House of prayer for ALL people." co: NAME, SABBATH, COVENANT, MOUNTAIN. Strangers receive name + inheritance through covenant, not lineage.

Ezek 47:22-23 — strangers who sojourn among Israel receive inheritance BY LOT among the TRIBES. "As born in the country among the children of Israel." co: TREE(inheritance), COVENANT. Eschatological land division includes non-descendants WITH tribal allotment.

Esth 8:17 — "many of the people of the land BECAME Jews." Narrative. Mass joining from outside.

Mat 10:6, 15:24 — lost sheep WITHIN Israel. Israel can be lost to itself. co: SHEPHERD.

Acts 7:38 — Stephen calls Israel at Sinai "the EKKLESIA (G1577) in the wilderness." The NT word for "church" IS Israel's assembly. Same G1577 used for all NT communities. The word spans Sinai to Revelation without the text marking a new entity. co: CHURCH_EKKLESIA, COVENANT, TRUMPET. See symbols/church-ekklesia for full study.

Acts 1:6 — disciples ask risen Jesus: "wilt thou at this time restore the kingdom to Israel?" Jesus does not correct the category — redirects to timing ("not for you to know the times"). The question assumes a future national restoration; Jesus doesn't deny it.

Lk 2:32 — "glory of thy people Israel" + "light to lighten the Gentiles." BOTH in same sentence. Israel's glory and Gentile illumination are not competing — they're simultaneous. co: LIGHT.

**PATTERNS:**
1. **INCLUSION FROM THE BEGINNING.** Exo 12:38,48-49 → Num 15:15 → Ruth → Caleb → Isa 56:3-7 → Ezek 47:22 → Esth 8:17 → Rom 11:17. Non-descendants joining Israel is not a NT innovation — the Torah provides the mechanism at the first Passover. The identity has ALWAYS been joinable through covenant. Rom 11's grafting is the same pattern, not a new one.
2. **MULTI-LEVEL IDENTITY.** Person → nation → covenant community → servant-figure. The text uses "Israel" for all four without resolving them into one. Isa 49:3-6 makes this explicit: Israel IS the servant AND the servant's target.
3. **BOTH NAMES PERSIST.** Gen 35:10 declares the change; text ignores the declaration. Jacob and Israel coexist — origin and calling carried together.
4. **ELECTION = OATH + LOVE + OPEN DOOR.** Deut 7:7-8 ties election to ancestors. But Exo 12:48 + Isa 56:6 provide entry for those outside the lineage. Election through a specific line does not mean exclusion of all others — the line is the trunk; others graft in.
5. **ONE LAW.** Exo 12:49, Num 15:15 — the Torah does not create two classes. Homeborn and stranger under ONE ordinance. Once joined, no distinction.
6. **NON-DESCENDANTS IN THE MESSIANIC LINE.** Ruth (Moabitess) and Rahab (Canaanite, Mat 1:5) are in Jesus' genealogy. The messianic line itself includes those who joined from outside.
7. **OUTWARD WITHOUT INWARD = INSUFFICIENT** (Rom 2:28-29, 9:6-8). Clearly stated. The qahal always required heart (Deut 10:16) alongside sign. Paul intensifies what was already there — not creating a new category. See symbols/church-ekklesia: the ekklesia IS the qahal, so Paul speaks within the existing assembly framework.
8. **TREE = ISRAEL'S = QAHAL = EKKLESIA.** Rom 11 (grafting) + Acts 7:38 (Israel at Sinai = ekklesia) + Exo 12:48 (stranger joins Israel). These are not three separate concepts — they describe the same reality with different metaphors. The tree is Israel's. The ekklesia is the qahal. The qahal includes strangers. Gentiles graft into Israel's tree because the "church" they enter IS Israel's assembly.
9. **12-TRIBE STRUCTURE = PERMANENT ARCHITECTURE.** Elijah's altar stones → New Jerusalem's gates. Ezek 47:22 puts strangers' inheritance WITHIN this tribal framework — the structure includes non-descendants by design.
10. **SCATTERED = NOT DESTROYED.** Amos 9:9 sifted among nations. Hos 1:9-10 "not my people" → "sons of living God." Ezek 37 dead bones raised. Identity survives dispersion and apparent death.
11. **ISA 49 PARADOX = UNRESOLVED KEY.** Servant is Israel and restores Israel. Identity operates at minimum two levels. Stated, not explained.
12. **EKKLESIA = QAHAL = ISRAEL'S ASSEMBLY.** See symbols/church-ekklesia. The NT word "church" (G1577 ekklesia) IS the LXX rendering of H6951 qahal. Acts 7:38 confirms: Israel at Sinai = the ekklesia. The word was not redefined in the NT — using an established word without redefining it means the established meaning holds. A change would require announcement; none exists. The "church" is not a new entity alongside or replacing Israel — it is Israel's assembly (the qahal), with the same inclusion mechanism (Exo 12:48), the same one-law principle (Num 15:15), the same trumpet-convocation (Num 10:7 → 1Th 4:16), and the same possessive ownership (Num 16:3 "qahal of the LORD" → Mat 16:18 "my ekklesia").

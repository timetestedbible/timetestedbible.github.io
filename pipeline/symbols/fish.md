## Context Bag

**LEXICAL:** H1709 *dag* = fish (collectively), from root suggesting "squirming/moving by vibratory action of tail"; also possibly from H1672 *da'ag* = timid/anxious. H1710 *dagah* = feminine form, same meaning. H1711 *dagah* = to spawn, become numerous (denominative from H1709). H1728 *davvag* = fisherman. H1771 *dayag* = fisherman. G2486 *ichthus* = fish. G231 *halieus* = fisherman (from G251 *hals* = salt/sea — a "salt-water man"). ~31 OT occurrences of dag/dagah; ~20 NT of ichthus.

**HAB 1:14 — EXPLICIT EQUATION:** "And makest MEN as the FISHES of the sea, as creeping things, that have NO RULER over them." God Himself equates men with fish. Critical detail: fish have NO RULER — they are leaderless, vulnerable, without covenant governance. This is the condition of men-as-fish: people scattered in the $sea (nations), without a covenant shepherd. Then v15: "They take up ALL of them with the angle, they catch them in their NET (H2764 cherem), and gather them in their drag (H4365 mikmereth)." Babylon catches the leaderless fish-people with nets. co: SEA, NET, BABYLON.

**GEN 1:26,28 — DOMINION OVER FISH:** "Let them have dominion over the FISH of the sea." Fish are the FIRST creatures named under human dominion — before fowl, cattle, or creeping things. Ps 8:8 echoes: "fish of the sea, whatsoever passeth through the paths of the seas." If SEA = nations, then "dominion over fish of the sea" = authority over peoples of the nations. Gen 9:2: after the flood, the fear of man falls on fish — same dominion restored.

**EZEK 29:4-5 — PHARAOH'S FISH = HIS PEOPLE:** "I will put hooks in thy jaws, and I will cause the FISH of thy rivers to STICK unto thy scales." Pharaoh = great dragon/crocodile; the fish = his people, clinging to him. God pulls Pharaoh out of his rivers AND the fish come with him: "all the fish of thy rivers shall stick unto thy scales." v5: "I will leave thee thrown into the wilderness, thee and all the FISH of thy rivers." Pharaoh's fish = Egypt's population. The ruler is extracted; the people come too. co: EGYPT, SEA, DRAGON.

**EZEK 47:10 — FISHERS AT THE RESTORATION RIVER:** "The FISHERS shall stand upon it from En-gedi even unto En-eglaim; they shall be a place to spread forth NETS (H2764 cherem); their FISH shall be according to their kinds, as the fish of the GREAT SEA, exceeding many." The living water river flowing from the temple (Ezek 47:1-9) produces fish — abundant life. Fishers gather them with cherem-nets. Fish "according to their kinds" = peoples organized by identity. This is the POSITIVE vision: fishing in the restoration = kingdom-gathering from living waters. co: NET, SEA, CHEREM.

**JER 16:16 — TWO-PHASE GATHERING:** "I will send for many FISHERS, and they shall FISH them; and after will I send for many HUNTERS (H6719), and they shall HUNT them from every MOUNTAIN, and from every HILL, and out of the holes of the ROCKS (H5553 sela)." PHASE 1: fishers — open gathering from the sea (broad, net-based). PHASE 2: hunters — targeted pursuit from land (mountains, hills, rock-holes). Fish are gathered openly; the hidden are tracked down. Context (v14-18): God is removing Israel from the land AND bringing them back. The two phases may represent different eras or methods of gathering. co: HUNTER, MOUNTAIN, ROCK.

**MAT 4:18-19 — FISHERS OF MEN:** "Follow me, and I will make you FISHERS of MEN." Jesus explicitly defines the symbolic equation: fisherman = one who gathers people. The disciples were literal fishermen (catching fish from the sea); Jesus transforms them into symbolic fishermen (gathering people from the nations). co: SEA, NET.

**MAT 13:47-48 — KINGDOM DRAGNET:** "Kingdom of heaven is like a NET (G4522 sagene) cast into the SEA, and gathered of every kind. When full, drew to shore, sat down, separated good into vessels, bad cast away." The kingdom gathers fish (people) of EVERY kind from the sea (nations). Separation happens AFTER catch, on shore, not in the water. Fish = gathered people, good and bad together until the judgment. co: NET, SEA, HARVEST.

**LK 5:4-6 vs JN 21:6-11 — TWO MIRACULOUS CATCHES:** Lk 5: Jesus directs the cast → net almost BREAKS from abundance → fishermen "astonished." Pre-resurrection: the gathering overwhelms the old structures. Jn 21: post-resurrection catch → 153 great fish → net is NOT broken. Post-resurrection: the restored net holds everything. 153 = disputed symbolism, but the contrast between breaking and not-breaking is structural.

**MAT 7:10 / LK 11:11 — FISH vs SERPENT:** "If he ask a fish, will he give him a serpent?" Fish = good provision (from God the Father). Serpent = harmful counterfeit. The contrast: what God gives (fish/people gathered in covenant) vs what the deceiver offers (serpent/deception). co: SERPENT, BREAD.

**ISA 19:8 — FISHERS MOURN:** "The fishers also shall mourn, and all they that cast angle into the brooks shall lament." Egypt's fishers lament = Egypt's gathering system collapses. When the waters fail (v5-7), the fish die and the fishers mourn. co: EGYPT.

**FISH = PROVISION:** Mat 14:17-19, 15:36, Lk 9:13, Jn 6:9-11 — loaves and FISHES feeding the multitude. Fish paired with $bread (covenant terms/Word). The people fed = the fish gathered. Mat 17:27 — fish with a coin in its mouth = provision from the sea.

## Candidates Tested

1. **People in the sea of nations — leaderless, waiting to be gathered** — BEST. Hab 1:14 explicit: men = fish with no ruler. Ezek 29:4 explicit: fish = Pharaoh's people. Mat 4:19 explicit: fish = men. Mat 13:47-48 confirms: fish caught from sea = people gathered into kingdom. Ezek 47:10: restoration fishing = abundant gathering. ✓ ALL
2. **Provision/sustenance** — partial. Explains loaves+fish, fish-mouth coin. But doesn't explain Hab 1:14, Ezek 29, Jer 16:16. Too narrow.
3. **Converts/believers** — too narrow. The net catches good AND bad (Mat 13:48). Fish ≠ believers only.
4. **Gentiles** — partial. Many fish are in the "great sea" (gentile nations), but Jer 16:16 is about gathering Israel. Not exclusively gentile.
5. **Souls** — too abstract. Fails secular-reader test overlap with generic "soul."
6. **The vulnerable** — partial. Hab 1:14 "no ruler" suggests vulnerability. But doesn't explain the provision/feeding contexts.
7. **Multiplying life** — H1711 dagah = "to spawn, become numerous." Fish = prolific multipliers. But this is the physical property, not the symbolic meaning.

## Pattern Summary

FISH = people living in the $sea (nations), particularly those without covenant governance ("no ruler" — Hab 1:14). FISHERMEN = agents sent by God to gather those people. The NET is the mechanism (indiscriminate gathering; separation comes later). Jesus makes the equation explicit: "fishers of MEN" (Mat 4:19). The kingdom dragnet catches fish of "every kind" from the sea (Mat 13:47-48). In restoration, the living-water river teems with fish and fishers spread nets (Ezek 47:10) — abundant gathering from abundant life. Jer 16:16 pairs FISHERS (open gathering from the sea) with HUNTERS (targeted pursuit from land) as two phases of God's gathering mission.

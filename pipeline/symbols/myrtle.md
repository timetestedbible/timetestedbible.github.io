## Context Bag

**H1918 hadas** = myrtle, from uncertain derivation. 6× OT. **H1919 Hadassah** = fem. of H1918, Esther's Hebrew name. 1× OT. Evergreen shrub, low-growing (2-5m vs. cedar), fragrant leaves release scent when CRUSHED. Thrives in lowlands/shade. First blooms white → dark berries.

**DEFINING TEXT (Isa 55:13):** "Instead of THORN (H5285) → fir. Instead of BRIER (H5636) → MYRTLE." Myrtle = explicit ANTI-THORN. THORNS = worldly people who choke word (Mat 13:22). ∴ MYRTLE = faithful who REPLACE cursed order. "Shall be to LORD for a NAME, for an EVERLASTING SIGN not cut off." Myrtle-people = God's permanent covenant sign.

**WILDERNESS PLANTING (Isa 41:19):** God plants cedar + shittah + MYRTLE + oil tree in wilderness; fir + pine + box in desert. co: TREE (multiple types = varied nations/peoples). God establishes His people-garden in desolate places → parallels Isa 51:3 "wilderness like Eden."

**ZECHARIAH VISION (Zech 1:8-11):** Man on red horse stands AMONG the myrtles in H4699 metsullah (shade, from H6751 tsalal = to shadow/darken). LOW POSITION — not on mountains (power) but in valley/shade. Agents walk TO-AND-FRO through earth → report: "all earth sits still, at rest." God's response: jealousy for Jerusalem, anger at nations at ease (v.14-15). Pattern: faithful remnant hidden in low place; world comfortable; God intervenes FOR the myrtles. LXX renders "myrtle trees" as "shady mountains" (ὄρη τὰ κατάσκια) — interpreting rather than translating, equating myrtles with nations (MOUNTAIN=kingdom).

**SUKKOT MATERIAL (Neh 8:15):** Myrtle branches + olive + palm + thick trees → build BOOTHS (H5521 sukkah). Post-exile restoration: "since days of Joshua son of Nun had not done so" (v.17). Result: "very great gladness." Myrtle-people = material of God's temporary dwelling. co: BOOTHS, JERUSALEM.

**HADASSAH / ESTHER (Esth 2:7):** Esther's Hebrew name = H1919 = myrtle-girl. Orphaned (no root), hidden identity (concealed among nations), beautiful (favor before king), faithful (risked death for people), in EXILE (dwelling in foreign empire). Hebrew name mentioned once → replaced by Persian name "Esther" — myrtle-identity hidden under foreign covering. ∴ Myrtle = faithful remnant hidden within the empires.

**PRODUCTION CHAIN:** Myrtle leaves → CRUSHING → fragrance released. Faithful people → trials/pressure → pleasing works/worship. Parallels olive→press→oil. Scent not spontaneous — requires pressing.

**PATTERN:** (1) ANTI-THORN: replaces cursed order (Isa 55:13). (2) LOW/HIDDEN: in shade, not on mountains (Zech 1:8). (3) FRAGRANT THROUGH CRUSHING: production chain = trials→worship. (4) EMBODIED BY ESTHER: orphan, hidden, faithful, in exile. (5) CONSTITUTES GOD'S DWELLING: material for Sukkot booths (Neh 8:15). (6) EVERLASTING: evergreen + "sign not cut off" (Isa 55:13). (7) GOD JEALOUS FOR THEM: Zech 1:14-15 — God angry at nations at ease while myrtles are in the shade. (8) REPLACES BARREN FIG'S FALSE SHADOW: when cursed fig tree withers (Mat 21:19 = leaves without fruit = false covering), the myrtle replaces the thorn/brier (Isa 55:13) and provides the TRUE shadow. The vine+fig formula (Mic 4:4 "under vine and fig, none afraid") = dwelling in covenant shadow; when fig fails, myrtle-people become the new shadow-material (Neh 8:15 booths = Isa 4:6 "tabernacle for a shadow"). Sukkot booths = appointed time = shadow (Col 2:17) = constituted by the faithful remnant.

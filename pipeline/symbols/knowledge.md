## Context Bag

**H1847 da'ath** = knowledge, from H3045 yada (to know). 93× OT. First occurrence: Gen 2:9 — the tree of da'ath of good and evil. Da'ath is introduced as DISCERNMENT BETWEEN GOOD AND EVIL — not information, not theology, not data. The ability to distinguish right from wrong.

**G1108 gnosis** = knowing (the act), knowledge. ~28× NT. **G1922 epignosis** = full recognition, complete discernment. ~20× NT. Intensified form.

**FIRST OCCURRENCE (Gen 2:9, 2:17):** Tree of da'ath of good and evil. The tree is in the garden, alongside the tree of life. Da'ath is something you EAT — you take it in, it becomes part of you. It is not abstract. It is consumed and internalized. co: TREE, GARDEN, GOOD, EVIL, LIFE, DEATH.

**PROV 1:7 — THE DEFINING VERSE:** "The fear of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 is the BEGINNING of da'ath: but fools despise wisdom and instruction." Da'ath STARTS with the fear of God. Without fear, no knowledge exists — only information. co: FEAR, FOOL, WISE.

**PROV 1:29:** "They HATED da'ath, and did NOT CHOOSE the fear of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄." Hating knowledge = not choosing fear of God. Parallel construction — same act.

**PROV 9:10:** "The da'ath of the holy is understanding." Knowledge OF the Holy One = understanding. Not knowledge ABOUT Him.

**PS 119:66:** "Teach me good judgment and da'ath: for I have believed thy COMMANDMENTS." Da'ath linked directly to commandments. You learn knowledge through the commands.

**HOS 4:1:** "No da'ath of God in the land." Followed by a list of violations: swearing, lying, killing, stealing, adultery (v.2) — the Ten Commandments broken. Lack of da'ath = breaking commandments.

**HOS 4:6 — THE KEY DIAGNOSTIC:** "My people are DESTROYED for lack of da'ath: because thou hast REJECTED da'ath, I will also REJECT thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast FORGOTTEN THE LAW of thy God, I will also forget thy children." Three-step chain: (1) rejected knowledge → (2) forgotten Torah → (3) destroyed. Da'ath IS Torah. Rejecting knowledge IS forgetting the law. They are equated in the same sentence.

**HOS 6:6:** "I desired mercy, and NOT sacrifice; and the da'ath of God more than burnt offerings." Da'ath of God > ritual. Jesus quotes this twice (Mat 9:13, 12:7). co: SACRIFICE, MERCY.

**JER 22:16:** "He judged the cause of the poor and needy; then it was well: was not this to KNOW me? saith 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄." Knowing God = doing justice for the poor. Not theology but action.

**ISA 5:13:** "My people are gone into captivity, because they have no da'ath." Same as Hos 4:6 — exile results from lack of knowledge.

**ISA 11:2:** The Spirit of da'ath rests on the Messiah. co: SPIRIT, WISDOM, COUNSEL, MIGHT, FEAR.

**ISA 11:9:** "The earth shall be full of the da'ath of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄, as the waters cover the sea." Eschatological promise — da'ath fills the earth in the kingdom. co: WATER, SEA.

**ISA 53:11:** "By his da'ath shall my righteous servant justify many." The Servant's knowledge — His intimate covenantal knowing — is the mechanism of justification.

**DAN 12:4:** "Many shall run to and fro, and da'ath shall be increased." End-time increase in knowledge. co: TO-AND-FRO, THE-END, SEAL.

**MAL 2:7:** "The priest's lips should keep da'ath, and they should seek the law at his mouth." The priest's job: preserve knowledge = preserve Torah. Same equation.

**2TIM 3:7:** "Ever learning, and never able to come to the da'ath of the truth." Information without da'ath. Accumulating facts but never arriving at discernment. co: TRUTH, DECEIVE.

**2PET 1:5-6:** "Add to your faith virtue; and to virtue da'ath; and to da'ath temperance." Knowledge sits between virtue and self-control in the growth chain. It is not the starting point — faith and virtue precede it.

**PATTERN:** (1) First occurrence = discernment between good and evil, associated with a TREE in the garden. (2) Begins with fear of God (Prov 1:7). (3) Equated with Torah (Hos 4:6, Mal 2:7). (4) Expressed through doing justice, not ritual (Jer 22:16, Hos 6:6). (5) Lack of it = exile and destruction (Isa 5:13, Hos 4:6). (6) Opposed to: accumulating information without transformation (2Tim 3:7). (7) Fills the earth in the kingdom (Isa 11:9). Da'ath is not what you know ABOUT God. It is what you know OF God through walking in His commands.

## Context Bag

### Strong's Data
H4325 *mayim* — "water; figuratively, juice" — dual of primitive noun, always plural. ~580× OT.
H2416 *chay* — "alive; hence raw (flesh); fresh (plant, water, year), strong" — from H2421 *chayah* (to live). KJV: "living, running, springing." When combined with H4325: "living waters" or "running water."
H4726 *maqor* — "something dug, a source (of water, even when naturally flowing; also of tears, blood; figuratively, of happiness, wisdom, progeny)" — fountain, wellspring.
H4599 *ma'yan* — "a fountain, spring" — from H5869 *ayin* (eye, spring). A spring-source.
H875 *be'er* — "a pit, a well" — from H874 *ba'ar* (to dig, declare). A dug access to underground living water.
H877 *bor* — "a cistern" — SAME ROOT as H875. Critical: well and cistern share a root, but differ functionally. Well accesses a living source; cistern stores collected dead water.
G5204 *hydor* — "water" — NT equivalent of H4325.
G4077 *pege* — "a fount, source, spring" — used in Rev 7:17, Rev 21:6, John 4:14.
G2198 *zao* — "to live" — used with G5204 for "living water" in John 4:10-11, 7:38.

### The Physical Distinction
Water exists in two states in the physical world:
1. SOURCED/FLOWING — springs, rivers, fountains. Continuously renewed from a source. Fresh. The Torah calls this *mayim chayim* ("living water").
2. COLLECTED/STILL — seas, cisterns, pools. Gathered and contained. Can go stagnant, bitter, salty. No source — only what was put in.

This physical distinction is THE key to reading every symbolic water passage. When Scripture says "water," the context always tells you which kind: sourced (living) or gathered (dead/sea).

### Ritual Law: Only Living Water Purifies
Lev 14:5-6, 50-52 — Leprosy purification requires "running water" (H2416 *mayim*).
Lev 15:13 — Cleansing from bodily issue requires "running water."
Num 19:17 — Red heifer purification: ashes mixed with "running water."
Lev 11:36 — "a fountain or pit, wherein there is plenty of water, shall be clean."

Pattern: Serious defilement (leprosy, death-contact, bodily issue) requires LIVING water — not stagnant. The purification power is in the source-connection, not the volume.

### God as the Fountain
Jer 2:13 — "They have forsaken ME the FOUNTAIN (H4726) of living waters, and hewed them out cisterns (H877), broken cisterns, that can hold no water." GOD = fountain of living waters. Cisterns = man-made alternatives that fail.
Jer 17:13 — "they have forsaken the LORD, the fountain (H4726) of living waters."
Ps 36:9 — "with thee is the fountain of life" (H4726 *maqor* + H2416 *chayim*).

### Water and Spirit Parallel
Isa 44:3 — "I will pour WATER upon him that is thirsty... I will pour my SPIRIT upon thy seed." Water ‖ Spirit in synonymous parallelism.
John 7:38-39 — "out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water. This spake he of the Spirit."
John 3:5 — "born of water and of the Spirit."
Eze 36:25-27 — "sprinkle clean water upon you" → "new spirit within you" → "I will put my Spirit within you." Sequential: water-cleansing precedes Spirit-indwelling.

### Water from the Rock/Throne
Exod 17:6 — water from the struck rock. Num 20:8 — water from the spoken-to rock.
Ps 114:8 — "turned the rock into a standing water, the flint into a fountain of waters."
Rev 22:1 — "a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God."
Eze 47:1-12 — water from the temple threshold. Ankle→knee→loin→swimming. Flows into the SEA and HEALS it (47:8). "Every thing shall live whither the river cometh" (47:9). Trees with unfading leaves and fruit for medicine (47:12).

### Living Water Heals the Sea
Eze 47:8-9 is the bridge between this study and the $sea study. The sea = chaotic nations. Living water from the sanctuary flows INTO the sea and heals it. The relationship: living water transforms the sea. God's life entering the mass of nations is how the sea is healed.
But 47:11 — "the miry places... shall not be healed; they shall be given to salt." Not all is healed. The marshes remain salt.

### Marah — Bitter Water Made Sweet
Exod 15:23-26 — Waters of Marah are bitter. A TREE cast into them makes them sweet. Immediately after: statute and ordinance established. "I am the LORD that healeth thee." Pattern: tree + bitter water = healing. The tree transforms death-water into life-water.

### Water + Word
Eph 5:26 — "sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word (G4487 *rhema*)."
Isa 55:10-11 — Rain comes down from heaven, waters the earth, makes it bring forth — "So shall my WORD be that goeth forth out of my mouth."
Prov 18:4 — "the wellspring (H4726) of wisdom as a flowing brook."

### Pattern Summary
1. Living water = sourced, flowing, fresh. Sea/cistern water = gathered, stagnant, contained.
2. God is THE fountain of living water (Jer 2:13). Forsaking Him = making cisterns.
3. Only living water purifies in Torah ritual — the source-connection is what cleanses.
4. Water and Spirit are parallel (Isa 44:3, John 7:38-39). Living water = God's Spirit flowing.
5. Living water proceeds from the rock/throne/temple (Exod 17:6, Rev 22:1, Eze 47:1).
6. Living water enters the sea and HEALS it (Eze 47:8-9) — God's life transforming nations.
7. The bitter can be made sweet by the tree (Exod 15:25).
8. 1 John 5:6-8: Three witnesses — Spirit, water, blood. Water is distinct from Spirit but connected.

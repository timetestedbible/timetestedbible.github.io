## Context Bag

### Greek words
G1080 gennaō — "to procreate; of the father = beget; of the mother = bear/be born; figuratively, to regenerate." Root of the "born of God" passages in John + 1 John. G509 anōthen — "from above; by analogy, from the first; by implication, anew." From G507 anō = upward. Every other Johannine use = "from above" (Joh3:31, 19:11, 19:23). G313 anagennáō — "to beget again." Only 2× NT: 1Pe1:3, 1Pe1:23. G3824 palingenesia — "rebirth, Messianic restoration." Only 2× NT: Mat19:28, Tit3:5.

### Critical: anōthen = "from above," not merely "again"
Joh3:3 — born G1080 + G509. Nicodemus heard "again" → imagined re-entering womb (3:4). Jesus corrected: not a second birth of the same kind but a first birth from a DIFFERENT SOURCE. Joh3:31 same word: "He that cometh from above (G509) is above all." Joh19:11: "power given thee from above (G509)." The birth is sourced from heaven, not from human will.

### Key Occurrences

**Definitional cluster — Joh3:3-8**: Born anōthen required to "see the kingdom" (3:3). Born of water+Spirit required to "enter the kingdom" (3:5). "Born of flesh = flesh; born of Spirit = spirit" (3:6) — two kinds, two sources. Wind/Spirit blows where it wills — you hear it but can't control it; "so is every one born of the Spirit" (3:8). Not human-initiated.

**Rebuke — Joh3:10**: "Art thou a master of Israel, and knowest not these things?" → Jesus expected a Jewish teacher to recognize this teaching. Points to Eze36:25-27: "I will sprinkle clean water upon you" (= born of water) + "a new spirit will I put within you" (= born of Spirit) + "I will cause you to walk in my statutes" (= behavioral result). Also Eze37:5,14 — breath/Spirit entering dead bones → they live. Nicodemus should have known Ezekiel.

**Source negation — Joh1:13**: "born not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God." Three negations: not ethnic lineage, not bodily desire, not human decision. Sourced entirely from God.

**Seed identified — 1Pe1:23**: "Born again (G313), not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God." The father's seed = God's word. Incorruptible = produces lasting nature. Jas1:18: "Of his own will begat he us with the word of truth." Same identification: word = seed = source of new nature.

**Resurrection anchored — 1Pe1:3-4**: "begotten us again (G313) unto a lively hope BY THE RESURRECTION of Jesus Christ from the dead, to an inheritance incorruptible." Born again → hope → inheritance. Resurrection is the mechanism. Luk20:36: "children of God, being the children of the resurrection." Being born of God = being children of resurrection.

**Behavioral evidence — 1 John cluster**:
- 1Jo2:29 — born of him → does righteousness
- 1Jo3:9 — born of God → does not commit sin; "his seed remaineth in him"
- 1Jo3:14 — "passed from death unto life, because we love the brethren"
- 1Jo4:7 — born of God → loves
- 1Jo5:1 — born of God → believes Jesus is the Christ
- 1Jo5:4 — born of God → overcomes the world
- 1Jo5:18 — born of God → sinneth not; wicked one toucheth him not
Pattern: every "born of God" statement in 1 John has a BEHAVIORAL marker. No passage identifies born of God by a prayer, feeling, or single moment. It is known by its fruit.

**Two lineages — Gal4:23-29**: Ishmael born "after the flesh" vs Isaac born "by promise" / "after the Spirit." Two covenants: Hagar = Sinai = bondage = present Jerusalem; Sarah = heavenly Jerusalem = freedom = "the mother of us all" (4:26). Born after the Spirit = children of promise, children of the free. The mother of the born-again is the Jerusalem above.

**Cosmic renewal — Mat19:28**: "in the regeneration (G3824) when the Son of man shall sit in the throne of his glory." Palingenesia = re-genesis = cosmic rebirth. Not just individual; the world itself is born again.

**Washing — Tit3:5**: "washing of regeneration (G3824), and renewing of the Holy Ghost." Same word as Mat19:28. Water+Spirit again (cf. Joh3:5, Eze36:25-27).

**Death→life — Rom6:4**: "buried with him by baptism into death… walk in newness of life." The old man dies; the new rises. Parallel: born of water (immersion/death) + Spirit (new life).

### Pattern Summary

BORN AGAIN = receiving God's nature from above through His word/Spirit, producing a new identity evidenced by behavior. Core structure: (1) **Source is God, not man** — Joh1:13 triple negation, Joh3:8 Spirit blows where it wills; (2) **Seed is the word** — 1Pe1:23 incorruptible seed = word of God, Jas1:18 begat with word of truth; (3) **Mechanism is water+Spirit** — Joh3:5, Eze36:25-27, Tit3:5; (4) **Evidence is behavioral** — 1 John's entire "born of God" cluster defines it by actions: righteousness, love, faith, overcoming, not sinning; (5) **Connected to resurrection** — 1Pe1:3 anchored in Christ's resurrection, Luk20:36 "children of the resurrection," Mat19:28 cosmic palingenesia; (6) **Produces heirs** — 1Pe1:3-4 born again → inheritance incorruptible, Rom8:17 children → heirs, Heb12:23 church of the FIRSTBORN.

The non-obvious finding: G509 anōthen means "from above" in every other Johannine use. Jesus was NOT teaching "born a second time" but "born from a different source." Nicodemus should have recognized Eze36:25-27. And 1 John defines born-of-God exclusively by behavior — never by a confession, a moment, or a feeling.

Substitution test: "born from above" → "receiving God's nature from above" ✓ | "born of water and Spirit" → "cleansed and given God's nature" (Eze36:25-27) ✓ | "born of God does not commit sin" → "one who has God's nature does not practice lawlessness" ✓ | "born of God overcomes the world" → "one with God's nature overcomes the world's systems" ✓ | "regeneration when Christ sits on throne" → "cosmic renewal when Christ reigns" ✓

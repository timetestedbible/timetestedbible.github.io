## Context Bag

**LEXICAL ROOT — THE WAYMARK:** H6726 *Tsiyyon* = "the same as H6725." H6725 *tsiyyun* = "a monumental or guiding pillar, waymark, sign, title" — from H6723 *tsiyah* "in the sense of conspicuousness." H6723 = desert, dry place, wilderness. ROOT CHAIN: desert (H6723) → conspicuous marker in the desert (H6725) → ZION (H6726). Zion IS the waymark — the conspicuous pillar visible from afar in desolate terrain that guides travelers to their destination. G4622 *Sion* = "a hill of Jerusalem; figuratively, the Church (militant or triumphant)." Even the Greek lexicon recognizes the figurative dimension.

**JER 31:21 — THE ROOT WORD USED AS COMMON NOUN:** "Set thee up WAYMARKS (H6725 *tsiyyun*), make thee high heaps: set thine heart toward the $highway (H4546 *mesillah*), the $way (H1870 *derek*) which thou wentest: turn again, O $virgin of Israel, turn again to these thy cities." The virgin of Israel is told to set up TSIYYUN — the SAME WORD as Zion — to mark her return path. The waymarks guide the return. co: HIGHWAY, WAY, VIRGIN. ZION is the ultimate tsiyyun — the permanent waymark that draws the scattered faithful home.

**HEB 12:22 — ZION DISTINGUISHED FROM JERUSALEM:** "Ye are come unto MOUNT SION (G4622), AND unto the city of the living God, the heavenly JERUSALEM (G2419)." Listed as TWO items, not one. Mount Sion = one reality. Heavenly Jerusalem = another. Both are present realities believers have "come to" (not future). ZION = the $mountain/throne. JERUSALEM = the city/community. The throne AND the community who gathers at it.

**PS 2:6 — THE KING SET ON ZION:** "Yet have I set my KING upon my holy hill (H2022 = $mountain) of Zion." God enthrones His King ON Zion. ZION = throne-mountain. $mountain = kingdom/ruling authority. Holy hill of Zion = God's specific kingdom-seat. Not "I set my king in Jerusalem" (the community) but "on Zion" (the throne).

**PS 125:1 — THE FAITHFUL ARE LIKE ZION:** "They that trust in the LORD shall be AS mount Zion, which CANNOT BE REMOVED, but ABIDETH FOR EVER." The faithful BECOME like Zion — immovable, permanent, unshakeable. Cf. $rock = covenant = cannot be moved (Ps 62:6). Zion shares the $rock quality: the throne-kingdom built on the covenant cannot be removed. co: ROCK, FAITH.

**ISA 28:16 / ROM 9:33 / 1PE 2:6 — THE FOUNDATION STONE LAID IN ZION:** "Behold, I lay IN ZION for a foundation a STONE (H68 *eben*), a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation: he that believeth shall not make haste." The $rock (covenant) is the FOUNDATION of Zion. God's kingdom is built ON the covenant. Rom 9:33 quotes this: "I lay in Sion a stumblingstone and $rock of offence." 1Pe 2:6: "I lay in Sion a chief corner stone, elect, precious." The $rock/$stone is IN Zion. Zion is WHERE the covenant foundation is established. co: ROCK, FAITH.

**THINGS THAT COME FROM ZION (source-function):** Salvation (Ps 14:7, 53:6), the law/Torah (Isa 2:3, Mic 4:2), strength/rod of power (Ps 110:2), beauty/perfection (Ps 50:2 "Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined"), blessing (Ps 128:5, 134:3), help (Ps 20:2), the Deliverer (Rom 11:26 "There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer"), comfort (Isa 51:3), deliverance (Joel 2:32, Obad 1:17). ZION = SOURCE POINT. Everything needed flows FROM Zion. Cf. $light on a $mountain (Mat 5:14) — the visible example set on a kingdom. Zion is the $mountain that shines with $light. co: LIGHT, MOUNTAIN, BREAD.

**THINGS BROUGHT TO ZION (destination-function):** Returning exiles (Isa 35:10, 51:11 — "ransomed of the LORD shall return and come to Zion with songs"), presents from nations (Isa 18:7 — "present brought... to the place of the name of the LORD of hosts, the mount Zion"), the backsliding (Jer 3:14 — "I will bring you to Zion"). Zion = DESTINATION. The waymark that draws the scattered. co: HIGHWAY, NAME.

**GOD'S DWELLING / CHOICE:** Ps 132:13-14 — "The LORD has CHOSEN Zion; he has desired it for his HABITATION. This is my REST for ever: here will I DWELL." God CHOSE Zion specifically. His rest. His dwelling. Ps 76:2 — "In Salem also is his tabernacle, and his dwelling place in Zion." Ps 9:11 — "the LORD, which dwelleth in Zion." Isa 8:18 — "the LORD of hosts, which dwelleth in mount Zion." co: SHADOW (dwelling/tabernacle), BOOTHS.

**REV 14:1 — THE LAMB ON MOUNT ZION:** "A Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's NAME written in their FOREHEADS." The $lamb (covenant offering) stands on the throne-mountain. The sealed remnant bears the Father's $name (covenant identity) on their foreheads (visible $mark of allegiance). This parallels Rev 17:5 — the $harlot has BABYLON written on her forehead. Two mountains, two marks: Zion with the Name vs Babylon with the Mystery. co: LAMB, NAME, MARK.

**OBAD 1:17-21 — ZION vs ESAU'S MOUNTAIN:** "Upon mount Zion shall be DELIVERANCE, and there shall be HOLINESS... saviours shall come up on mount ZION to JUDGE the mount of ESAU; and the KINGDOM shall be the LORD'S." Two mountains in direct opposition: Zion (God's kingdom, deliverance, holiness) vs Esau's mount (the hunter's kingdom — Gen 25:27 Esau the $hunter). The kingdom belongs to the LORD — not to the hunter-empire. co: HUNTER.

**ISA 4:5 — CLOUD AND FIRE ON ZION:** "The LORD will create upon every dwelling place of mount Zion, and upon her ASSEMBLIES, a CLOUD and SMOKE by day, and the shining of a FLAMING FIRE by night: for upon all the glory shall be a DEFENCE (H2646 *chuppah* = covering, canopy, bridal canopy)." The exodus pillar — cloud by day, fire by night — recreated over Zion. The *chuppah* (bridal canopy / $marriage covering) protects. Zion's assemblies = the visible gatherings of the kingdom, covered by the same presence that led Israel through the wilderness. co: SHADOW, FIRE, MARRIAGE.

**MOUNT ZION = MOUNTAIN + ZION:** Combining the two symbols: $mountain = kingdom/ruling authority (from pipeline) + ZION = waymark/conspicuous beacon (from root) = **God's conspicuous kingdom — the visible, immovable seat of His ruling authority that stands as a beacon drawing all nations.** Isa 2:2: "the $mountain of the LORD's house shall be ESTABLISHED in the TOP of the mountains, and shall be EXALTED above the hills; and ALL NATIONS shall FLOW unto it." God's $mountain (kingdom) rises above all other $mountains (kingdoms) — and nations flow to it like a river to the sea (reversed!). co: MOUNTAIN, SEA.

**ISA 51:16 — ZION = "MY PEOPLE":** "I have put my words in thy mouth, and I have covered thee in the $shadow of mine hand, that I may plant the heavens, and lay the foundations of the earth, and SAY UNTO ZION, THOU ART MY PEOPLE." God NAMES Zion "my people." Zion is both throne and people — the kingdom is its citizens. The waymark is not empty — it is the gathered faithful who BECOME the beacon. co: SHADOW, NAME.

**DAUGHTER OF ZION — PERSONIFIED:** 2Ki 19:21 — "the $virgin the daughter of Zion hath despised thee." Isa 1:8 — "the daughter of Zion is left as a cottage in a vineyard." Isa 52:2 — "captive daughter of Zion." Isa 62:11 — "Say ye to the daughter of Zion, Behold, thy salvation cometh." Mat 21:5, Jn 12:15 — "Tell ye the daughter of Sion, Behold, thy King cometh." Zec 9:9 — "Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion... thy King cometh." The "daughter" = the community as offspring of the throne-kingdom. co: VIRGIN, JERUSALEM.

**ISA 66:8 — ZION TRAVAILS AND BRINGS FORTH:** "As soon as Zion travailed, she brought forth her children." Zion GIVES BIRTH — like $jerusalem the mother (Gal 4:26). But the emphasis: Zion's travail is SUDDEN — "shall a nation be born at once?" The kingdom produces a nation in a moment. co: BIRTH PAINS, JERUSALEM.

7. **Permanent/eternal dwelling** — Ps 125:1, 132:14. Captures immovability but misses the beacon/conspicuousness dimension.

## Pattern Summary

ZION ≠ JERUSALEM. Heb 12:22 lists them as two items. ROOT: H6726 from H6725 *tsiyyun* = waymark, guiding pillar — the conspicuous beacon in the wilderness. ZION = God's kingdom-throne — the immovable, conspicuous seat of His ruling authority. MOUNTAIN (kingdom) + ZION (waymark) = God's visible kingdom that draws all nations. The $rock (covenant) is the FOUNDATION of Zion (Isa 28:16). The King is SET on Zion (Ps 2:6). The Lamb STANDS on Zion (Rev 14:1). The law goes forth FROM Zion (Isa 2:3). The faithful are LIKE Zion — immovable (Ps 125:1). JERUSALEM = the bride/community who gathers AT Zion. Zion is the throne; Jerusalem is the people on the throne. The daughter of Zion is the community born from the kingdom. Opposite: Mount Esau / Babylon's mountains = rival kingdoms. The waymark draws the scattered home (Jer 31:21 *tsiyyun*); the $highway leads to it; the scattered faithful follow $light toward the conspicuous beacon.

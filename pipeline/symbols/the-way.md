## REDIRECT → way

This symbol is fully covered by the WAY study. The WAY pipeline distinguishes two senses
of H1870/G3598: indefinite "a way" = general conduct, and definite "THE way" = Torah/God's
commandments. Both are treated in a single study since they share the same word, root, and
meaning spectrum.

Key "THE WAY" content in the way study:
- Gen 18:19: "keep the way of the LORD = to do justice and judgment" (explicit definition)
- Acts 9:2, 19:9, 24:14: early church called "the Way" — identity by conduct
- John 14:6: "I am THE way, THE truth, THE life" — Christ = Torah incarnate
- 2 Pet 2:21: "the way of righteousness" || "the holy commandment"

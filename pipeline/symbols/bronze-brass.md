## Context Bag

### Root family — the decisive connection
H5172 *nachash* (primitive root) = to hiss, whisper a spell, prognosticate. Produces:
H5175 *nachash* = SERPENT (from its hiss). H5178 *nechosheth* = brass/copper. H5153 *nachuwsh* = coppery, figuratively HARD. Same root → brass literally carries the serpent's name. Num 21:9 *nechosheth nachash* (brass serpent) is an intentional pun. 2Ki 18:4 names it Nehushtan = "the brass thing."

### H5178 translation range (KJV)
brasen, brass, copper, chain, fetter, **filthiness**, steel. Note: "filthiness" at Ezek 16:36 = H5178 nechosheth, same word as "brass." Not a different word — the translators chose "filthiness" because the context demanded it. This means nechosheth CARRIES the connotation of corruption/impurity inherently.

### Pattern clusters

**CORRUPTION / FILTHINESS:**
Ezek 16:36 — "thy nechosheth (filthiness!) was poured out through thy whoredoms." H5178 = filthiness.
Jer 6:28 — "they are brass and iron; they are all corrupters."
Ezek 22:18 — "Israel is to me become dross: all they are brass... the dross of silver."
Ezek 24:11 — brass pot heated to burn out filthiness/scum.
Jer 6:29 — "the founder melteth in vain: for the wicked are not plucked away."

**HARDNESS / OBSTINACY:**
Isa 48:4 — "thy neck is an iron sinew, and thy brow brass" = obstinate, unyielding.
H5153 def: "figuratively, HARD." Job 6:12 — "is my flesh of brass?" Job 40:18 — behemoth's bones "strong pieces of brass."

**BONDAGE / FETTERS:**
Judg 16:21 — Samson bound with fetters of brass (by Philistines).
2Ki 25:7 — Zedekiah bound with fetters of brass, carried to Babylon.
Dan 4:15,23 — stump bound with band of iron and brass.
H5178 def includes "fetter, chain."

**GATES / BARRIERS:**
Ps 107:16 — "gates of brass" (God breaks them). Isa 45:2 — same. 
Jer 1:18, 15:20 — brasen walls. 1Ki 4:13 — brasen bars.

**CURSE / CLOSED HEAVEN:**
Lev 26:19 — "heaven as iron, earth as brass." Deut 28:23 — "thy heaven shall be brass." Brass heaven = impenetrable, no blessing passes through.

**KINGDOMS / DEGRADATION:**
Dan 2:32,39 — belly/thighs of brass = third kingdom, "bear rule over all the earth." Gold→Silver→Brass→Iron = decreasing value, increasing hardness.
1Ki 14:27 / 2Chr 12:10 — Rehoboam replaced Solomon's GOLD shields with BRASS. Same form, debased substance.
Isa 60:17 — "For brass I will bring gold" = eschatological upgrade, brass→gold.

**DIVINE/ANGELIC APPEARANCE:**
Ezek 1:7 — living creatures sparkle like burnished brass. Ezek 40:3 — measuring man's appearance like brass. Dan 10:6 — arms/feet like polished brass. Rev 1:15 — Christ's feet like fine brass BURNED IN FURNACE. Rev 2:18 — same.
Key: in every divine case, the brass is POLISHED/BURNISHED/REFINED. Christ's brass feet = He who has PASSED THROUGH the fire and now TREADS on the serpent's authority (Gen 3:15 enacted). The serpent's own metal under His feet.

**TABERNACLE POSITION:**
Brass = outer court metal. Brasen altar (sacrifice/judgment), brasen laver (washing), sockets/foundations, pins. Gold = inner sanctum. Silver = hooks/connectors. Brass faces the fire, faces the public, bears the weight. It's where sin meets judgment — the boundary between holy and common.

**SERPENT LINK:**
Num 21:9 — brass serpent = look at it → live. Jn 3:14 — "as Moses lifted up the serpent... so must the Son of man be lifted up." The nachash cast in nechosheth = sin objectified in its own metal. Looking at judged sin → healing. 2Ki 18:4 — Israel worshiped it → Hezekiah destroyed it. Same dual-valence: God's instrument → idol.

### Candidates tested (7+)
1. Judgment — fails Isa 48:4 (obstinacy ≠ judgment), fails Ezek 16:36
2. Hardness — fails divine appearance, fails filthiness dimension
3. Inferior substitution / degraded glory — fails Christ's feet, fails obstinacy
4. Worldly strength / human authority — fails Ezek 16:36, fails Christ
5. Corruption / filthiness — fails tabernacle (God's altar ≠ corrupt), fails Christ
6. Endurance under fire — fails fetters, fails obstinacy, fails filthiness
7. Sin externalized / serpent nature made visible — fails Dan 2 kingdoms, partial elsewhere
8. Power that binds / constraining authority — too narrow, fails filthiness

No single-word substitution passed universally. The convergence points to a COMPOUND meaning rooted in the physical/etymological properties: **corrupt strength — hard authority sharing the serpent's root.** This works because:
- Nations of brass = hard, corrupt, serpent-ruled authority (Dan 2, Zech 6)
- Brow of brass = hardened in corruption (Isa 48:4)
- Filthiness = nechosheth itself (Ezek 16:36)
- Fetters = the serpent's binding power
- Gates = the serpent's barriers
- Tabernacle brass = where the serpent's corruption (sin) is dealt with by fire
- Christ's brass feet = He treads the serpent's authority, having passed through fire
- Brass serpent = the nachash cast in its own metal — sin objectified for judgment

### Production chain
Copper ore (stone) → smelted by fire → alloyed with tin → bronze/brass.
Stone → FIRE → alloy = raw material → TESTING → hardened product.
Bronze is HARDER than its components — the combination creates something tougher. The fire that forges it is the same fire that could purge it (Ezek 22, 24).

### Opposite confirmed
GOLD: divine nature, glory, inner sanctum, covenant fidelity.
Isa 60:17 "for brass I will bring gold" = corruption replaced by divine nature.
Dan 2 hierarchy: gold (head/divine authority) vs brass (belly/worldly dominion).
Rehoboam: gold→brass = glory→corruption.

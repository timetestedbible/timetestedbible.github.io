## Context Bag

**LEXICAL ROOT CHAIN — two derivations encode the symbol:**
H3123 yonah ← "probably from the same as H3196 yayin (WINE)" = effervescence/warmth. Wine = covenant blood (pipeline/symbols/wine). Dove lexically tied to covenant-blood warmth. H8449 towr ← H8447 towr = "succession, order, turn" from H8446 tuwr = "to spy out, explore, turn." Turtledove = bird-of-ORDER, the one who goes out and returns in sequence. Same root as the 12 SPIES (Num 13:2 tuwr). H3124 Yonah (Jonah) = same word as H3123 — the prophet IS the dove.

**CRITICAL WORDS USED WITH DOVE:**
G185 akeraios (Mat 10:16 "harmless") = a- + kerannumi (to MIX) = UNMIXED, unadulterated. NOT "harmless" — "unpolluted." Wine connection: unmixed wine vs diluted. H8535 tam (Song 5:2, 6:9 "undefiled") = complete, whole, perfect. From H8552 tamam = to complete. Dove = the COMPLETE/UNDIVIDED one. H6601 pathah (Hos 7:11 "silly") = to open, entice, seduce. A dove that has been ENTICED AWAY from its devotion — the faithful partner led astray.

**GEN 15:9-10 — COVENANT CEREMONY: BIRDS NOT DIVIDED.**
Abraham cuts heifer, goat, ram in half → lays pieces against each other. "But the BIRDS divided he NOT." The turtledove + young pigeon remain WHOLE in the covenant-cutting ceremony. H8535 tam (complete/whole) + Gen 15:10 (not divided) = the dove is the undivided covenant partner. Everything else is cut; the dove remains intact.

**GEN 8:8-12 — NOAH'S DOVE vs RAVEN (Gen 8:7).**
Raven: goes to-and-fro (H7751 shut = same as Satan Job 1:7), never returns, feeds on death. DOVE: sent out, finds no rest (H4494 manoach), returns to ark. Sent again → returns with OLIVE leaf (H2132 zayith + H5929 aleh). Olive = covenant tree (Rom 11) → leaf = sign of life/covenant surviving judgment. Third sending → does not return = freed into renewed world. Pattern: dove = faithful scout who returns until the world is ready.

**PS 74:19 — EXPLICIT EQUATION:**
"Deliver not the SOUL (H5315 nephesh) of thy TURTLEDOVE (H8449) unto the multitude; forget not the CONGREGATION (H2416 chay = living assembly) of thy POOR (H6041 ani) for ever." Turtledove SOUL ‖ congregation of the poor. Direct synonymous parallelism. Dove = God's humble faithful people.

**JER 8:7 — TURTLEDOVE KNOWS THE MO'EDIM:**
"The turtledove (H8449) and the crane and the swallow OBSERVE (H8104 shamar = guard/keep) the TIME (H6256 eth) of their COMING (H935 bo); but MY PEOPLE know NOT the JUDGMENT (H4941 mishpat) of the LORD." The turtledove keeps its appointed schedule. Israel does NOT. Also: "the stork in the heaven knoweth her APPOINTED TIMES (H4150 mo'edim)." Birds know the mo'edim; God's people do not. Dove = the one who faithfully observes covenant timing.

**SONG OF SOLOMON — DOVE AS COVENANT BELOVED:**
Song 1:15, 4:1 — "doves' eyes" = eyes of faithful devotion. Song 2:14 — "O my dove, in the clefts of the ROCK (H5553 sela), in the secret places" — dove dwells IN the ROCK (= covenant, pipeline/symbols/rock). Song 5:2 — "my dove, my UNDEFILED (H8535 tam = complete)" — the faithful one who is whole. Song 5:12 — HIS eyes also "as doves" — BOTH parties have dove-quality. Song 6:9 — "My dove, my undefiled is but ONE; she is the ONLY ONE of her mother, the CHOICE ONE." Singular, unique, undivided. The covenant partner is ONE — not many.

**HOS 7:11 — EPHRAIM THE SILLY DOVE:**
"Ephraim is like a silly (H6601 pathah = enticed/seduced) dove without heart (H3820 lev): they call to Egypt, they go to Assyria." The covenant partner (dove-identity) has been SEDUCED away — going to foreign powers instead of to God. A dove without heart = covenant loyalty without understanding. Dove-identity present; dove-behavior absent. co: EPHRAIM.

**HOS 11:11 — DOVE RETURNS FROM EXILE:**
"They shall tremble as a bird out of Egypt, and as a DOVE out of Assyria: I will place them in their HOUSES." The dove = the returning covenant people. co: EPHRAIM, ISRAEL.

**ISA 60:8 — DOVES TO THEIR WINDOWS:**
"Who are these that fly as a cloud, and as the DOVES to their WINDOWS (H699 arubbah = lattice/dovecot)?" Return flight. Doves heading home.

**MAT 3:16 / MK 1:10 / LK 3:22 / JN 1:32 — SPIRIT LIKE A DOVE:**
All four gospels: Spirit descends LIKE (G5616 hosei) a dove. Not IS a dove — LIKE one. Jn 1:32 adds: "it ABODE (G3306 meno = remain, dwell permanently) upon him." The Spirit comes and STAYS — the faithful presence that doesn't leave. The dove-quality = permanent dwelling, covenant abiding. Jesus = the one upon whom the faithful presence rests without departing.

**MAT 10:16 — WISE AS SERPENTS, UNMIXED AS DOVES:**
Serpent = cunning/shrewd (G5429 phronimos). Dove = unmixed/unadulterated (G185 akeraios). Dual instruction: use the deceiver's perception for good + maintain undivided covenant loyalty. SERPENT ≠ DOVE :: cunning speech ≠ unmixed devotion :: divided allegiance ≠ single-hearted faithfulness.

**SACRIFICIAL DOVE — POOR PERSON'S OFFERING:**
Lev 1:14, 5:7, 12:8, 14:22, 15:14, 15:29, Num 6:10. The dove is consistently the substitute when the worshipper CANNOT AFFORD a lamb. Not a lesser offering — an alternative path for the humble. Lk 2:24 — Mary + Joseph offer turtledoves for Jesus' dedication = they are among the poor. The dove-offering positions the worshipper among the humble faithful. co: LAMB.

**DOVE'S SOUND = MOURNING:**
Isa 38:14 — "mourn as a dove." Isa 59:11 — "mourn sore like doves: we look for judgment but there is none." Ezek 7:16 — "doves of the valleys, all mourning, every one for his iniquity." Nah 2:7 — "voice of doves, tabering upon their breasts." The dove's characteristic sound is mourning — specifically mourning for sin and separation from covenant wholeness.

**JONAH (H3124) = THE DOVE-PROPHET:**
Sent to nations (Nineveh). Fled (like Ephraim, Hos 7:11). Swallowed → 3 days in the deep → emerged (death/resurrection pattern). Preached repentance. The dove-messenger who resists but eventually fulfills the commission. Jonah sat in a BOOTH (H5521 sukkah) under a SHADOW (H6738 tsel) — connecting dove to booth + shadow chain. co: SHADOW, BOOTHS.

## Candidates Tested

1. **Faithful covenant devotion** — BEST. Works in ALL contexts: Gen 8 (returns), Gen 15 (not divided), Ps 74:19 (God's people), Song (beloved), Jer 8:7 (knows appointed times), Hos 7:11 (faithless = "silly dove"), Hos 11:11 (returns), Mat 3:16 (Spirit abides), Mat 10:16 (unmixed). ✓ ALL
2. Holy Spirit — fails Hos 7:11, Ps 74:19, sacrificial contexts, Gen 8, Song. Too narrow.
3. Innocence/Purity — dictionary synonym of G185. Fails secular-reader test.
4. Mourning/Grief — what the dove DOES, not what it REPRESENTS. Functional, not symbolic.
5. Sacrifice/Offering — doesn't explain Song, Hos, Mat 10:16, baptism.
6. The Bride specifically — too narrow; both parties have dove-eyes (Song 5:12).
7. The Humble Poor — partial; explains Ps 74:19 + sacrifice but not baptism, not Song fully.
8. Messenger/Herald — explains Jonah, Gen 8, but not Hos 7:11, not sacrifice, not Song.
9. Peace — REJECTED. Dictionary/cultural synonym. Secular reader would arrive here. No explicit scripture says "dove = peace."
10. Returning exile — partial; only fits some prophetic contexts.

## Pattern Summary

DOVE = faithful covenant devotion. Lexically from WINE (covenant blood). The one who belongs, returns, remains undivided (Gen 15:10), whole (H8535 tam), unmixed (G185 akeraios). Knows the appointed times (Jer 8:7 H4150). Dwells in the ROCK (Song 2:14 = covenant). Mourns when separated. Returns from exile. Gives itself as offering to the humble. When unfaithful = "silly/enticed dove" (Hos 7:11). Opposite = RAVEN (never returns, feeds on death, roams to-and-fro). Spirit descends LIKE a dove = the faithful covenant presence that ABIDES permanently.

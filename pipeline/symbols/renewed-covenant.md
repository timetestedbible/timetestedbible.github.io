## Context Bag

**Root chain**: H2319 *chadash* (adj "new") ← H2318 *chadash* (verb). H2318 Strong's def: "to be new; causatively, to rebuild." KJV translates H2318 as: "renew, repair." Root verb is explicitly renewal/restoration, not novelty.

**H2318 verb occurrences** (10x): 1Sa 11:14 renew kingdom; 2Ch 15:8 renewed altar; 2Ch 24:4,12 repair temple; Job 10:17 renewest witnesses; Ps 51:10 renew a right spirit; Ps 103:5 youth renewed; Ps 104:30 renewest face of earth; Isa 61:4 repair waste cities; Lam 5:21 renew our days as of old. Pattern: every verb use = restoration of something that already existed, never creating something that didn't exist before.

**Moon connection**: H2320 *chodesh* (new moon/month) ← same root H2318. The moon is not a different moon — it's the same moon renewed in its cycle. The "renewed moon" = the returning light of the same body.

**Greek split in NT**: Two different Greek words used for "new" in covenant passages:
- G2537 *kainos* = "new especially in freshness" (quality, not time). Used in: Heb 8:8, 8:13, 9:15, Mat 26:28, Mk 14:24, Lk 22:20, 1Co 11:25, 2Co 3:6. Compounds G340 *anakainizo* + G341 *anakainoo* both = "to restore, to renovate."
- G3501 *neos* = "new in time, youthful; figuratively regenerate." Used in: Heb 12:24 only. Note: "regenerate" = born again/renewed.
- G166 *aionios* = "everlasting." Used in Heb 13:20. No "new" — *everlasting* covenant.

**LXX**: Jer 38:31 (= MT 31:31) renders H2319 with G2537 *kainos* — διαθήκην καινήν. LXX translators chose quality-newness, not time-newness.

**Jer 31:31-33 critical details**: v.32 problem was "my covenant THEY broke" (H6565 *parar*) — fault was with the people, not the covenant terms. v.33 "I will put my law (H8451 *torah*) in their inward parts" — same law, same torah, internalized to new location. The content of the covenant is unchanged; the inscription surface changes from stone to heart.

**Everlasting pattern**: Gen 9:16 (Noah), Gen 17:7,13,19 (Abraham), Lev 24:8 (showbread), 2Sa 23:5 (David), 1Ch 16:17/Ps 105:10 (Jacob/Israel), Isa 24:5, 55:3, 61:8, Jer 32:40, Eze 16:60, 37:26, Heb 13:20. Every major covenant called "everlasting" (H5769 *olam* / G166 *aionios*). An everlasting covenant cannot be replaced — only renewed.

**Lam 3:23 + Deu 32:4 connection**: "They are new{H2319} every morning: great is thy faithfulness{H530}." Same H530 *emunah* that describes the Rock in Deu 32:4 ("God of truth{H530}"). The mercies are renewed (not replaced) daily — tied to the same faithfulness/firmness that defines the Rock/Covenant.

**Heb 8:13 challenge**: "In that he saith, A new{G2537} covenant, he hath made the first old." *kainos* here = renewed in quality/freshness. What "decays and waxes old" is the *administration* (priesthood, sacrificial system, Levitical mediation) — not the covenant terms themselves. Heb 8:6 says "better covenant established upon better promises" — better mediator, better blood, better access, same law now written on the heart.

**Pattern summary**: Every layer of evidence — Hebrew root, Hebrew verb usage, moon cognate, Greek word choice, LXX translation, Jeremiah's own explanation, and the "everlasting" designation — converges on renewal rather than replacement. The covenant is one continuous binding agreement between God and man, periodically renewed with better mediation and deeper internalization, but never replaced in substance.

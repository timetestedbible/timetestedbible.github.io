# His Coming at the Full Moon — A Word Study

## The Hidden Word

The Hebrew word for "full moon" is **H3677 כֶּסֶה (keseh)** — and the KJV hides it completely, translating it as "time appointed" in both of its only two occurrences. Multiple other translations (SLT, JPS, DRB, DBT) correctly render it "full moon."

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Strong's** | H3677 |
| **Hebrew** | כֶּסֶה / כֶּסֶא (keseh / kese') |
| **Root** | H3680 כָּסָה (kasah) — to fill up hollows, to cover, to make full |
| **Definition** | "Properly, **fulness** or the **full moon**, i.e. its festival" — Strong's |
| **Occurrences** | 2 verses: Psalm 81:3, Proverbs 7:20 |
| **KJV Translation** | "(time) appointed" — the word "moon" does not appear in KJV |

The root H3680 (kasah) means "to plump, fill up hollows, to cover." The full moon IS the filled-up moon — the moon at its maximum, its hollows filled with light, its fullness achieved.

### The Throne Connection

From the **same root** H3680 comes another word:

| Strong's | Hebrew | Meaning |
|----------|--------|---------|
| H3677 כֶּסֶה | keseh | **Full moon** — the moon's fulness |
| H3678 כִּסֵּא | kissé | **Throne** — "properly, covered" i.e. a canopied seat |

The FULL MOON and the THRONE share the same root. The full moon = the moon on its throne. The "time appointed" (keseh) of the full moon is the time the king takes his throne. This is not accidental — it is built into the language.

## The Two Occurrences

### 1. Psalm 81:3 — Blow the Shofar at the Full Moon

> "Blow up the trumpet (H7782 shofar) in the new moon (H2320 chodesh), in the **time appointed** (H3677 keseh = **full moon**), on our solemn feast (H2282 chag) day."

**What translations hide:** SLT reads "Strike ye the trumpet in the new, in the **full moon**, for the day of our festival." JPS: "Blow the horn at the new moon, at the **full moon** for our feast-day."

The command is to blow the shofar at TWO times: the new moon AND the full moon. The solemn feast at the full moon = the 15th of the month. In the biblical lunar calendar, the 15th is always the full moon. The major feasts that fall on the 15th:

- **Passover** (15th Nisan) — Unleavened Bread begins
- **Tabernacles/Sukkot** (15th Tishri) — the great harvest feast

**Connection to the Last Trump:** If the shofar is blown at the full moon, and the "last trump" (1 Cor 15:52, 1 Th 4:16) marks the return of Christ, then the return happens at a full moon. The TRUMPET (shofar) and the FULL MOON (keseh) are bound together in the same verse.

Psalm 81 continues (vv. 4-5): "For this is a statute for Israel, and a law of the God of Jacob. This he ordained in Joseph as a testimony (H5715 'eduth), when he went out through the land of Egypt."

The shofar at the full moon is a **statute**, a **law**, and a **testimony** — ordained since the Exodus. It is not optional or symbolic. It is enacted law pointing to the day of return.

### 2. Proverbs 7:20 — The Husband Returns at the Full Moon

> "He hath taken a bag of money (H3701 keseph = **silver**) with him, and will come home at the day **appointed** (H3677 keseh = **full moon**)."

**What translations reveal:** SLT: "he will come to his house the day of the **full moon**." JPS: "He will come home at the **full moon**." DRB: "he will return home the day of the **full moon**." DBT: "he will come home on the day of the **full moon**."

This is the **husband-returns** proverb. A man leaves on a journey with silver (money). He will RETURN HOME at the day of the full moon. In the context of Proverbs 7, this is the legitimate husband — as opposed to the adulteress who seduces while the husband is away.

**Rapture/return typology:** The bridegroom departs (ascension), takes a bag of silver (the price paid, the covenant sealed), and the **day of his return** is the day of the FULL MOON. This maps directly to:

- **Daniel 9:26 (alternative):** Messiah cuts a covenant and vanishes (אַיִן)
- **John 14:2-3:** "I go to prepare a place... I will come again and receive you unto myself"
- **Matthew 24:36:** "Of that day and hour knoweth no man" — but the MONTH is known: the full moon

The Hebrew marriage pattern: the bride knows the groom will return, but not the exact day or hour. Yet she knows it will be at the full moon. She watches. She is the "watchful city" (H5892 עיר from H5782 עור = to be awake).

## Celestial Phenomena Only Possible at Full Moon

### 3. Matthew 24:27 — Light from East Seen in the West

> "For as the **lightning** (G796 astrape) cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be."

**G796 ἀστραπή (astrape)** = "lightning; by analogy, **glare**" — Strong's. The word does not exclusively mean a lightning bolt. It means a **bright shining, a glare, a brilliance**. Luke 11:36 uses the same word for the "bright shining" of a lamp.

Actual lightning does not "come from the east and shine unto the west" — lightning strikes locally and randomly. But the **full moon** does exactly this: it rises in the EAST at sunset and its light is visible all the way to the WEST across the entire sky. It is the only celestial body whose light "comes from the east and shines to the west" throughout the night.

At full moon, the moon is exactly 180° opposite the sun. When the sun sets in the west, the full moon rises in the east. Its reflected light — a bright shining, a glare (astrape) — fills the sky from horizon to horizon. "So shall the coming of the Son of Man be" — visible everywhere, from east to west, at the full moon.

### 4. Joshua 10:12-13 — The Long Day Requires Full Moon

> "Sun, stand thou still upon Gibeon; and thou, **Moon**, in the valley of Ajalon. And the sun stood still, and the **moon stayed**."

For BOTH the sun AND the moon to be simultaneously visible on opposite sides of the sky — the sun over Gibeon (to the west/southwest) and the moon in the valley of Ajalon (to the east/southeast) — the moon must be in **opposition** to the sun. Opposition = full moon. This is the ONLY lunar phase where the moon and sun are on opposite horizons simultaneously.

Joshua commanded both luminaries because both were visible. At any other phase, the moon would either be invisible (new moon) or in the same part of the sky as the sun (crescent/quarter). Only the full moon stands opposite the sun.

### 5. Habakkuk 3:11 — Sun and Moon Stood Still (Again)

> "The sun and moon stood still in their habitation: at the light of thine arrows they went, and at the shining of thy glittering spear."

Habakkuk references the same event as Joshua. The sun AND moon both standing still = both visible = full moon. The "arrows" and "glittering spear" connect to the judgment vocabulary (see SWORD symbol study). This prophetic oracle recasts Joshua's long day as a template for the day of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄's judgment.

### 6. Zechariah 14:7 — At Evening Time It Shall Be Light

> "But it shall be one day which shall be known to 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄, not day, nor night: but it shall come to pass, that **at evening time it shall be light**."

Normally, evening brings darkness. What celestial phenomenon produces **light at evening**?

The full moon rises precisely as the sun sets. At evening, when darkness should come, the full moon appears on the eastern horizon and fills the sky with light. "Not day, nor night" — the twilight of sunset combined with the rising full moon creates a unique condition: it is neither fully day (the sun is gone) nor fully night (the moon provides light). This phenomenon is exclusive to the full moon.

### 7. Joel 2:31 / Acts 2:20 / Revelation 6:12 — Moon Turned to Blood

> "The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the **moon into blood**, before the great and terrible day of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 come." — Joel 2:31

> "The moon became as **blood**." — Revelation 6:12

Many assume "blood moon" = a total lunar eclipse. But eclipses are predictable, rare, brief, and occur only at full moon anyway. **Smoke and fire** in the atmosphere also turn the moon red/blood-colored — and this mechanism is far more consistent with the prophetic context:

- **Rev 18:9,18** — "the smoke of her burning" (Babylon's fire)
- **Joel 2:30** — "blood, and fire, and **pillars of smoke**"
- **Rev 9:2** — "smoke from the pit... the sun and air darkened by the smoke"
- **Isa 34:10** — "the smoke thereof shall go up forever"

The smoke from Babylon's burning darkens the sun ("sun became black as sackcloth") and turns the visible moon blood-red. But for the moon to appear blood-colored at all, it must be **visible** — which means it must be full or near-full. A new moon or thin crescent cannot appear "as blood" because there is virtually nothing to see. The blood moon requires a full moon seen through smoke.

**The sun-moon-stars symbol study already documents the smoke-as-mechanism chain:** sackcloth (H8242) = smoke = cloud (H6051) = the covering that darkens. The full moon seen through this smoke = the blood moon. This is not an eclipse — it is the judgment-smoke reddening the full moon.

## Three Hebrew Words for Moon

Scripture uses three different words for the moon, each emphasizing a different aspect:

| Strong's | Hebrew | Meaning | Emphasis |
|----------|--------|---------|----------|
| H3394 יָרֵחַ (yareach) | The moon | General term — the moon as a celestial body. ~26 OT occurrences. |
| H3842 לְבָנָה (levanah) | The white one | The moon at brightness — from H3835 laban "to be white." 3 occurrences. |
| H3677 כֶּסֶה (keseh) | The full one | The moon at fullness — from H3680 kasah "to fill." 2 occurrences. |

**H3842 levanah** — "the white" — appears in three verses:

- **Song 6:10** — "Who is she that looketh forth as the morning, **fair as the moon** (levanah), clear as the sun?" — The BRIDE is compared to the levanah-moon at its brightest. The bride = the church = fair as the full/white moon.
- **Isaiah 24:23** — "Then the **moon** (levanah) shall be confounded, and the sun ashamed, when 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 of hosts shall reign in mount Zion." — When 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 reigns, even the bright moon is outshone.
- **Isaiah 30:26** — "The light of the **moon** (levanah) shall be as the light of the sun, and the light of the sun sevenfold, as the light of seven days." — Eschatological brightening: the moon becomes as bright as the sun.

The bride (Song 6:10) is fair as the levanah (white/bright moon). The return of the king outshines even the levanah (Isa 24:23). In the age to come, the levanah shines as bright as the sun (Isa 30:26).

### Isaiah 30:26 — The Sevenfold Light Requires Full Moon

> "Moreover the light of the **moon** (H3842 levanah) shall be as the light of the **sun** (H2535 chammah), and the light of the **sun** shall be **sevenfold**, as the light of **seven days**, in the day that 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 bindeth up the breach of his people, and healeth the stroke of their wound."

The math only works at full moon. The full moon reflects approximately 1/7th of the sun's apparent brightness (the maximum the moon can reflect at full illumination). If the sun becomes 7x brighter, and the moon reflects 1/7th of that 7x-brighter sun, then:

- Moon's new brightness = 1/7 × 7 = **1** (the current sun's brightness)
- Sun's new brightness = **7** (seven days of light in one)

The moon becomes "as the light of the sun" (its current brightness) — exactly as Isaiah states. But this calculation ONLY works when the moon is full, reflecting at its maximum ratio. A half moon reflects roughly half the light; a crescent almost nothing. Only the full moon at 1/7th reflectivity × 7x brighter sun = current sun brightness. Isaiah's prophecy is a **mathematical description** of a full moon event.

Furthermore, "seven days" of light compressed into one day echoes the creation pattern (seven days), the Jericho pattern (seven days of marching before the fall), and the jubilee pattern (seven sevens). The sevenfold light is the fullness of all light — the ultimate keseh (fullness) of the ultimate day.

## The Jericho Connection

**H3405 יְרִיחוֹ (Yericho)** — Jericho. Strong's: "perhaps from H3394 (יָרֵחַ, the moon); its month."

Jericho = the **moon city**. The city that falls at the trumpet blast (Josh 6:20) = the city named after the moon. The shofar at the full moon (Ps 81:3) destroys the moon city.

This connects directly to the Jericho/Babylon typology (see `pipeline/symbols/jericho.md`):

- Jericho (moon city) falls at the shofar blast = Babylon the Great falls at the last trump
- The shofar is blown at the full moon (Ps 81:3) = the return happens at the full moon
- Rahab (the harlot-bride) is extracted before the fire = rapture before Babylon burns
- Joshua (Yeshua) appears as captain of the host BEFORE the 7-day battle = the return

The moon city falls at the full moon. The trumpet that destroys Babylon is the same trumpet that marks the full moon feast. The return of the king is at the keseh — the fullness, the throne-day.

## The Faithful Witness

> "It shall be established forever as the **moon** (H3394 yareach), and as a **faithful witness** (H5707 'ed + H539 'aman) in heaven." — Psalm 89:37

The moon is called a **faithful witness** in the sky. The word H539 'aman = faithful, established, trustworthy — the root of "Amen." The moon faithfully reflects the sun's light. At full moon, this witness is at maximum — the entire face reflects the sun. The bride (Song 6:10) who is "fair as the moon" is the faithful witness who reflects the Son's light.

> "He appointed the **moon** (H3394) for **seasons** (H4150 mo'edim = appointed times / festivals)." — Psalm 104:19

The moon exists to mark the mo'edim — the appointed times, the festival calendar. The full moon (15th) is when the great feasts occur: Passover, Tabernacles. The moon is not decoration — it is a covenant clock.

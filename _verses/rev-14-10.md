# Revelation 14:10-11

> *"He also will drink the wine of God's wrath... and he will be **tormented** with fire and sulfur... And the **smoke** of their **torment** goes up **forever and ever**, and they have **no rest**, day or night."* — NKJV

**Part of the [Nature of Hell](/research/symbols/hell/) study.**

---

## The Common Reading

The wicked are tormented with fire and sulfur, their smoke rising forever — proof of eternal conscious torment without relief, day or night.

---

## What the Passage Actually Says

### The Hebrew Gospel Text

> v.10: *"This one shall drink from the wine of the wrath of YHWH which is in the cup of fury, and shall be **punished** (H6064 **anash**) with $fire and sulfur before his holy messengers and the lamb."*

> v.11: *"And the **smoke** (H6227) of their **punishment** shall go upward from **age** (H5769 **olam**) to age (H5769), and they shall not have **rest** (H4496 **menuchah**) day or night — those who received the mark and who pray to the graven-image."*

### "Tormented" → H6064 *Anash* (Fined / Penalized)

The Greek G928 *basanizo* (touchstone testing) becomes H6064 *anash* in Hebrew — a word meaning to impose a fine, to penalize judicially. Its OT uses:

- Ex 21:22 — a man who causes a miscarriage "shall be surely punished (*anash*)" — he pays a FINE, assessed by judges
- Deut 22:19 — the false accuser "shall be fined (*anash*) a hundred shekels of silver"
- 2 Chr 36:3 — the king of Egypt "fined (*anash*) the land"

This is a judicial/financial penalty — not torture. The Hebrew describes a sentence being carried out, not sadistic torment.

### Smoke Rises "From Age to Age"

The Hebrew uses *olam* to *olam* — age to age. Compare Rev 19:3, where the Hebrew says Babylon's smoke rises "from generation (H1755 *dor*) to generation (*dor*)." Babylon is not still burning. The smoke is the EVIDENCE of completed destruction — the permanent aftermath, not ongoing fire.

Compare Isaiah 34:10 about Edom: "the smoke thereof shall go up for ever; from generation to generation it shall lie waste." Edom is not still smoking. The language describes permanent desolation — the result endures, not the burning.

### "No Rest" → H4496 *Menuchah* (Sabbath-Rest)

The Hebrew word for "rest" here is H4496 *menuchah* — the word for the rest of the Promised Land, the sabbath-rest. "They shall not have *menuchah*" means they do not enter the rest that remains for God's people (Heb 4:9). This is the language of EXCLUSION from rest — denied entry into the inheritance — not active, ongoing torture.

### Who Is This About?

The passage specifies: "those who received the mark and who pray to the graven-image." This is a specific group during a specific judgment — beast-worshippers — not a universal statement about all unsaved humans throughout history.

---

## Harmony

1. **H6064 *anash* means judicial penalty/fine** — not torture. Used for court-assessed fines in Exodus and Deuteronomy.
2. **Smoke = evidence of completed destruction** — Babylon's smoke "rises from generation to generation" (Rev 19:3), but Babylon is destroyed, not still burning. Same pattern.
3. **"No rest" = H4496 *menuchah*** — denied the sabbath-rest of the inheritance. Exclusion, not torture.
4. **H5769 *olam* = age, concealed time** — "from age to age," not necessarily "forever."
5. **This applies to beast-worshippers specifically** — not a universal statement about all unsaved people.

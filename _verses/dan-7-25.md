# Daniel 7 — The Foremost of Days

> *"He shall think to **change times and laws**."* — Dan 7:25

Everyone reads Daniel 7 as a prophecy about empires and persecution. It is. But the chapter's climactic conflict is not about political power — it is about **time**. The adversary's defining act is changing *times and laws*. And the resolution is not military victory — it is the *attiq yomin* taking His כסא, and "the **time** came that the saints possessed the kingdom" (7:22). Time corrupted, time restored.

This study reads Daniel 7 alongside [Isaiah 14:12–15](/research/verses/isa-14-13/), where the same conflict — the same word for throne, the same calendar agenda — appears in the mouth of the *helel ben shachar*.

---

## The Foremost of Days

> *"I beheld till the thrones were cast down, and the **Ancient of days** did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool: his **throne** was like the fiery flame."* — Dan 7:9

The KJV renders H6268 *attiq* as "Ancient." But its root H6275 *athaq* means **"to advance, to proceed forward, to remove to the next place."** In Genesis 12:8, Abraham *athaq* — he advanced forward. In Genesis 26:22, Isaac *athaq* — he moved on to the next place. The root is about proceeding, advancing, going first.

*Attiq yomin* is not merely "the old one of days." He is **the one who advances the days** — the foremost of days, the one from whom the calendar proceeds.

He takes His H3764 *korse* — the Aramaic cognate of H3678 *kisse* (Hebrew), the same consonantal root as H3677 *kese* (full moon). The Foremost of Days takes His throne — His כסא — clothed in white, blazing with light. This is the legitimate כסא: the seat from which time proceeds, the full moon clothed in light.

## The Son of Man Comes with Clouds

> *"One like the **Son of man** came with the **clouds** of heaven, and came to the **Ancient of days**."* — Dan 7:13

H1247 *bar enash* — son of man. He comes with H6050 *anan* — clouds. He comes **to** the Foremost of Days — not to replace him but to receive dominion from him.

The Son receives H7985 *sholtan* — dominion, authority — and H4437 *malku* — kingdom. Given to him by the one who sits on the כסא. The Father gives authority to the Son. The sun gives light to the moon. The Foremost of Days advances time through the faithful witness.

## The Adversary Changes Times

> *"He shall speak great words against the most High, and shall **wear out** the saints of the most High, and **think to change times and laws**: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time."* — Dan 7:25

Three actions define this figure:

**He speaks against the *illay*** (H5943, Most High). The same *elyon* (H5946) that appears in Isaiah 14:14 — the Uppermost. The adversary's words are directed against the highest authority.

**He wears out the saints.** H1080 *bela* — to wear out, to consume gradually. Not a sudden conquest but a slow erosion. The saints are ground down over time.

**He thinks to change H2166 *zeman* (times) and H1882 *dath* (laws/decrees).** This is the defining act — not the persecution, not the blasphemy, but the changing of **times**. *Zeman* is specifically calendar-time — appointed seasons, fixed dates. *Dath* is decree, law, edict. He changes when things happen and the rules that govern them.

Note H5452 *sebar* — "think." He does not necessarily succeed in changing the actual times — he *thinks* to do so. He changes the calendar as observed, not the celestial reality. The sun and moon continue their courses; the nations simply stop watching them correctly.

## The Restoration of Time

> *"Until the Ancient of days came, and judgment was given to the saints of the most High; and the **time** came that the saints possessed the kingdom."* — Dan 7:22

The resolution is stated in terms of **time**: *"the **time** came."* H2166 *zeman* — the same word used for the "times" the adversary changed. When the Foremost of Days takes His כסא, the *zeman* is restored. The saints receive the kingdom not through military victory but through the restoration of the correct calendar — the right *times* under the right authority.

> *"The judgment shall sit, and they shall take away his dominion."* — Dan 7:26

H1780 *din* — judgment — shall H3488 *yethib* — sit, take its seat. The same verb used for the Foremost of Days sitting on His throne (7:9). The judgment takes its seat, the usurper's dominion is removed, and the kingdom is given to the saints.

## The Isaiah 14 Connection

The parallels between Daniel 7 and [Isaiah 14:12–15](/research/verses/isa-14-13/) are precise:

**The כסא.** Isaiah 14:13 — the *helel ben shachar* raises his כסא above the stars. Daniel 7:9 — the Foremost of Days takes His כסא, blazing with light. Same word (Hebrew *kisse* / Aramaic *korse*, same root). One is the usurpation, the other is the restoration.

**The appointed times.** Isaiah 14:13 — the usurper establishes on the high place of the *moed* (appointed times). Daniel 7:25 — the adversary changes the *zeman* (times). Same calendar, same target.

**The Most High.** Isaiah 14:14 — "I will silence the *elyon*." Daniel 7:25 — "He shall speak against the *illay/elyon*." Same title, same assault.

**The darkness.** Isaiah 14:13 — the usurper operates from the farthest extent of *tsaphon* (darkness/concealment). Daniel 7:25 — the saints are "given into his hand" for "a time and times and the dividing of time" — a period of calendar darkness.

**The reversal.** Isaiah 14:15 — cast down to the farthest extent of the pit. Daniel 7:26 — his dominion consumed and destroyed. Both end the same way.

Isaiah 14 describes the ambition in the usurper's own words: five "I will" declarations. Daniel 7 describes the execution, the suffering, and the resolution. Together they form a complete narrative: the calendar was seized, the saints were worn out, and the Foremost of Days reclaimed His כסא and restored the times.

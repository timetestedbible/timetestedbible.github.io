# 1 Thessalonians 5:3

> *"For when **they** shall say, **Peace** and **safety**; then **$[sudden-destruction]** cometh upon **them**, as **$[birth-pains]** upon a woman with child; and **they shall not escape**."*

## The Symbolic Reading

> **The "they" who say peace and safety are covenant insiders — people who invoke God's name as their security while violating God's terms. Paul's Greek pronoun structure, his accusation earlier in the same letter, and every OT source passage for this verse converge on one identification: hardened Israel, the covenant community that rejected its Messiah and declared itself safe.**

---

## The Pronoun Structure

The Greek of 1 Thessalonians 5:1-9 sets up a deliberate binary. Paul shifts between persons with surgical precision:

| Verse | Person | Greek | Who |
|-------|--------|-------|-----|
| 5:1-2 | **you** | ὑμῖν, οἴδατε | brothers, believers |
| 5:3 | **they/them** | λέγωσιν, αὐτοῖς, ἐκφύγωσιν | *unidentified group* |
| 5:4-5 | **you** | Ὑμεῖς, ἀδελφοί | brothers, believers |
| 5:6 | **us** vs **the rest** | καθεύδωμεν ὡς οἱ λοιποί | we vs. *the others* |
| 5:7 | **those who** | οἱ καθεύδοντες, οἱ μεθυσκόμενοι | sleepers, drunkards |
| 5:8-9 | **we/us** | ἡμεῖς, ἡμᾶς | believers |

In verse 3, λέγωσιν is 3rd person plural present active subjunctive with no expressed subject noun. The subject is deliberately left implicit — but the rhetorical structure makes the contrast unmistakable: "whenever **they** say peace and safety... destruction on **them**" is immediately followed by "but **you**, brothers, are NOT in $[darkness]."

The critical phrase is in verse 6: **οἱ λοιποί** — "the rest" / "the others." Paul already used this exact phrase in 1 Thessalonians 4:13: "that ye sorrow not, even as **the rest** (οἱ λοιποί) which have no hope." The same "rest" who have no hope are the same "rest" who $[sleep] and get drunk in the $[night].

## The Same Letter Names "They"

The identification is not ambiguous when you read the entire epistle. In chapter 2, Paul has already established a specific "they":

> *"For ye also have suffered like things of your own countrymen, even as they have of **the Jews**: who both killed the Lord Jesus, and their own prophets, and have persecuted us; and they please not God, and are contrary to all men: forbidding us to speak to the Gentiles that they might be saved, to fill up their sins alway: for **the wrath has come upon them to the uttermost**."* — 1Th 2:14-16

Then in 5:9: *"God has **not** appointed **us** to wrath (ὀργήν)."*

The same letter. The same wrath (ὀργή). Two groups:

- **2:16** — wrath has come on **them** (unbelieving Jews who killed Jesus and the prophets)
- **5:9** — God has **not** appointed **us** to wrath

The "them" receiving wrath in chapter 2 and the "them" receiving $[sudden-destruction] in chapter 5 form an inclusio across the letter. Paul already told his audience who "they" are.

## Romans 11:7-9 — The Vocabulary Bridge

Paul uses the exact "rest" terminology in Romans to describe Israel-minus-the-believing-remnant:

> *"What Israel seeketh for, that he hath not obtained; but the election hath obtained it, and **the rest** (οἱ δὲ **λοιποὶ**) **were hardened**."* — Rom 11:7

> *"God hath given them the spirit of **slumber** (κατανύξεως), **eyes that they should not see**, and **ears that they should not hear**."* — Rom 11:8

> *"And David saith, Let their table be made a **$[snare]** (παγίδα), and a trap, and a stumblingblock."* — Rom 11:9

Three vocabulary links to 1 Thessalonians 5:

1. **οἱ λοιποί** ("the rest") — 1Th 5:6 = Rom 11:7 = Israel minus the believing remnant
2. **spirit of slumber** — 1Th 5:6-7 ($[sleep], drunkenness) = Rom 11:8 (Isa 29:10) = judicial $[sleep] imposed by God on hardened Israel
3. **$[snare]** (παγίς) — Lk 21:35 "as a $[snare] shall it come on all" (same event as 1Th 5:3, linked by G160 *aiphnidios*) = Rom 11:9 "let their table become a $[snare]" = judgment on hardened Israel

Paul's own vocabulary across his letters consistently links "the rest," "$[sleep]/stupor," and "$[snare]" to hardened Israel.

## Every OT Source Targets Covenant Israel

Paul's language in 1Th 5:3 is not original. It is drawn word-for-word from the prophets through the Septuagint. Every source passage targets the same entity:

| Passage | Target | False peace declared by | Instrument |
|---------|--------|-------------------------|------------|
| Jer 6:14 | **Jerusalem** | her prophets + priests | $babylon from the north |
| Jer 8:11 | **Jerusalem** | her prophets + priests | barren $[vine] + $[fig-tree] |
| Jer 23:17 | **Jerusalem** | prophets "as $[sodom]" | — |
| Ezek 13:10,16 | **Jerusalem** (from exile) | prophets of Israel | — |
| Mic 3:5,12 | **Zion / Jerusalem** | prophets who divine for money | Zion plowed, Jerusalem heaps |

Every "peace, peace; when there is no peace" is spoken **over Jerusalem, by her own prophets**, while covenant-breaking is underway. The false peace is not a pagan delusion. It is a **covenant-community phenomenon**. The prophets lean on God's name (Mic 3:11), invoke His temple, and assure the people that the covenant guarantees safety regardless of conduct:

> *"They build up **Zion** with blood, and **Jerusalem** with iniquity... yet will they lean upon the LORD, and say, **Is not the LORD among us?** none evil can come upon us."* — Mic 3:10-11

This is the posture of 1Th 5:3. Not pagan ignorance of God — but covenant-presumption: claiming God's protection while violating God's terms. You cannot declare *shalom* (covenant-wholeness) if you have no covenant. Only insiders can make this claim.

## The Harlot Connection — Daughter of Zion Rides the Beast

The "they" who say peace and safety are not merely a historical group. They are the ongoing spiritual entity Scripture calls the $[harlot]:

**The harlot was a wife first.** Scripture never calls a pagan nation a harlot simply for being pagan. The word is reserved for those who had a covenant and broke it (Deut 31:16: "go a whoring" = "forsake me" = "break my covenant").

> *"How is the faithful city become an **$[harlot]**! it was full of judgment; righteousness lodged in it; but now murderers."* — Isa 1:21

The faithful city is Jerusalem. The $[harlot] IS Jerusalem that broke covenant. And Revelation identifies this $[harlot] as the woman riding the beast — Babylon the Great.

Three witnesses make the same accusation against the same entity:

- **Jesus** (Mat 23:35,37): "Upon **you** may come **all the righteous blood** shed upon the earth... O **Jerusalem, Jerusalem**, thou that **killest the prophets**"
- **Paul** (1Th 2:15): "the **Jews**: who both **killed the Lord Jesus**, and **their own prophets**"
- **John** (Rev 18:24): "In her was found the **blood of prophets**, and of saints, and of **all that were slain upon the earth**"

The charge is identical. All righteous blood shed on earth — attributed to **Jerusalem** by Jesus, to **the Jews** by Paul, to **$babylon** by John. Three names for one entity.

Rev 11:8 confirms the identification: *"the **great city**, which spiritually is called $[sodom] and Egypt, **where also our Lord was crucified**."* The great city is Jerusalem. Rev 17:18 says the woman IS "that great city." The woman riding the beast — the $[harlot] — is daughter of Zion fallen.

## The Hardened Have Not Come Out

> *"**Come out of her, my people**, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues."* — Rev 18:4

This command is not addressed to pagans. It is addressed to **"my people"** — covenant insiders who are **inside** the $[babylon] system. The hardened of Rom 11:7 are God's covenant people still trapped inside — judicially $[sleep]ing, intoxicated by the $[perpetual-sleep] mechanism (Jer 51:39,57 → 1Th 5:6-7), saying "peace and safety" while the covenant is shattered beneath them.

They have not come out. They are "the rest" (οἱ λοιποί). They are "they" who say peace and safety. And $[sudden-destruction] is what closes the gap between their declared security and their actual standing.

## Genuine Safety vs. False Safety

The same Hebrew word — H983 *betach* — describes both genuine God-given safety and false self-declared safety. The diagnostic is not the vocabulary. It is **who declares it** and **whether righteousness precedes it**.

> *"The work of righteousness shall be **peace**; and the effect of righteousness **quietness** and **assurance** for ever."* — Isa 32:17

Righteousness → peace → safety. This order is non-negotiable. Reverse it — claim security without righteousness — and the same words signal judgment.

| | Genuine *betach* | False *betach* |
|---|---|---|
| **Who declares it** | God gives it (Ps 4:8, Lev 26:5-6) | "They" declare it (1Th 5:3) |
| **Covenant status** | Keeping covenant | Breaking covenant |
| **What precedes it** | Righteousness (Isa 32:17) | Nothing — claimed without basis |
| **Result** | God defends (Ezek 38 — Gog destroyed) | $[Sudden-destruction] on the declarers |

Ezekiel 38's Israel dwells in genuine *betach* — post-restoration, covenant-keeping, and God defends them when Gog attacks. The "they" of 1Th 5:3 claim false *betach* — self-declared peace without the righteousness that produces it.

## Who "They" Are

The evidence converges from five independent directions:

1. **Greek grammar** — Paul's they/you binary in 5:3-4 sets up an explicit contrast; "the rest" (οἱ λοιποί) in 5:6 matches "the rest" of Rom 11:7 = hardened Israel
2. **Same-letter context** — 1Th 2:14-16 names "the Jews" who killed Jesus and the prophets, on whom wrath has come; 5:9 says "us" are not appointed to wrath — inclusio across the letter
3. **OT source passages** — Every "peace, peace; no peace" targets Jerusalem, spoken by her own prophets (Jer 6:14, 8:11, 23:17; Ezek 13:10,16; Mic 3:5,12)
4. **Blood charge** — Mat 23:35 (Jesus to Jerusalem) = 1Th 2:15 (Paul: the Jews) = Rev 18:24 (Babylon the Great) — identical accusation, three witnesses
5. **Harlot identification** — The $[harlot] must be a former covenant partner (Deut 31:16); "the great city where our Lord was crucified" (Rev 11:8) = Jerusalem; the woman IS "that great city" (Rev 17:18)

"They" are not the pagan world ignorant of God. "They" are people who **know God's $[name]** and invoke it as their security while violating His terms — the hardened covenant community, daughter of Zion turned $[harlot], riding the beast of the world system, saying "Is not the LORD among us?" while the covenant lies shattered beneath them.

# Matthew 24:4-8

> *"Take heed that no man $[deceive] you. For many shall come in my $[name], saying, I am Christ; and shall $[deceive] many. And ye shall hear of $[wars-rumors] and rumours of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but $[the-end] is not yet. For $[nation-against-nation] shall rise against nation, and $[kingdom] against kingdom: and there shall be $[famine], and $[pestilence], and $[earthquake], in divers places. All these are the beginning of $[birth-pains]."*

## The Symbolic Reading

> **Jesus is not listing random end-times disasters. He is quoting the specific vocabulary of Jeremiah's Babylon oracle and the covenant-collapse formula of 2 Chronicles. The "signs" are the mechanism by which $babylon-the-great — the global system of human self-rule — destroys itself.**

---

## The Vocabulary Triangle

The key to this passage is recognizing WHERE Jesus's words come from. His list of signs is not original — it is drawn from two specific OT passages, and the combination identifies the subject.

### Source 1: Jeremiah 51:46

> *"And lest your heart faint, and ye fear for the **rumour** that shall be heard in the land; a **rumour** shall both come one year, and after that in another year shall come a **rumour**, and violence in the land, **ruler against ruler**."* — Jer 51:46

This is Jeremiah's Babylon oracle — instructions for God's people living inside Babylon when it collapses. Jesus uses the same vocabulary:

| Jeremiah 51:46 (Babylon) | Matthew 24:6-7 |
|---------------------------|----------------|
| Rumours in the land | Rumours of wars |
| Ruler against ruler | Kingdom against kingdom |
| Heart faint / fear | "See that ye be not troubled" |
| Violence in the land | Wars |

The $alarmed-fear study traces G2360 *throeo* ("be not troubled") — it appears only 3 times in the NT: Mat 24:6, Mk 13:7, and 2 Th 2:2. In all three, the context is "don't panic when you see these things — they are expected, not surprising."

### Source 2: 2 Chronicles 15:5-6

> *"And in those times there was no peace to him that went out, nor to him that came in, but great vexations were upon all the inhabitants of the countries. And **nation was destroyed of nation**, and **city of city**: for **God did vex them** with all adversity."* — 2 Chr 15:5-6

The Greek LXX of 2 Chr 15:6 uses the exact formula Jesus quotes: ἔθνος ἐπ᾽ ἔθνος — "$[nation-against-nation] against nation." Jesus is quoting the LXX verbatim.

And 2 Chronicles names the CAUSE: *"For Israel hath been without the **true God**, and without a **teaching priest**, and without **law**"* (2 Chr 15:3). Three simultaneous absences — true God, teaching priest, Torah — produce the fracture-sequence. This is the $famine of the word (Amos 8:11) that triggers the collapse.

### The Triangle Identifies the Subject

| Jer 51:46 | 2 Chr 15:5-6 | Mat 24:6-7 |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| Babylon's collapse | Israel without God/priest/Torah | Jesus's "signs" |
| Rumour + ruler vs ruler | Nation vs nation, city vs city | Wars, rumours, nation vs nation |
| God's people told to flee | God Himself is the agent ("God did vex") | "All these must come to pass" |

Jesus is not predicting random future events. He is mapping the $fall-of-babylon pattern onto his generation — and every generation until the pattern completes. The vocabulary identifies the subject: $babylon-the-great, the worldwide system of human self-rule that replaces God's authority with its own.

## The Sword-to-Earthquake Substitution

Jeremiah's standard judgment triad is $sword + $famine + $pestilence — appearing over 30 times in Jeremiah. But Jesus substitutes **$earthquake** for $sword:

| Jeremiah (30× triad) | Matthew 24:7 |
|-----------------------|-------------|
| $sword | $earthquake |
| $famine | $famine |
| $pestilence | $pestilence |

Why the substitution? The $earthquake study establishes that earthquake = God shaking any established order to remove what is unfaithful (Hag 2:6, Heb 12:26-27). The $sword is personal violence — individual against individual. The $earthquake is systemic — the entire authority structure convulsing.

Jesus is telling his disciples: first the SYSTEM shakes (earthquake — political, economic, social upheaval), THEN personal violence reaches individuals (which arrives in v9: "then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted, and shall kill you").

And G4578 *seismos* does not mean only "earthquake." The KJV translates the same word as "tempest" in Mat 8:24 (the storm on the sea). It means any violent commotion — political, social, economic, natural. "Seismoi in various places" = upheavals of every kind, everywhere.

## "The Beginning of Birth Pains" — Not "The Beginning of the End"

> *"All these are the **beginning** of $[birth-pains]."* — Mat 24:8

Two words demand attention:

**G746 *arche*** — "beginning." Not merely "first in time" but also "ruling principle, generative cause." The same word used for "in the beginning" (Jn 1:1) and "principalities" (Eph 6:12). These signs are not just chronologically first — they are the MECHANISM that generates what follows. Deception produces fracture → fracture produces war → war produces famine → famine produces death. Jesus is giving the causal chain, not a calendar.

**G5604 *odin*** — "$birth-pains." Not death-throes. Birth pains always produce something new. They precede DELIVERY — not destruction. Paul uses the identical word in 1 Th 5:3: *"then $[sudden-destruction] cometh upon them, as travail (G5604 *odin*) upon a woman with child."* The birth pains are real suffering, but they are producing something — the new creation that emerges when $babylon-the-great falls.

And Jesus says explicitly: *"$[the-end] is **not yet**"* (v6). He deflates premature expectation. These signs mark the beginning of labor, not the delivery.

## The Four Horsemen Alignment

The sequence of Mat 24:4-8 maps precisely onto the four horsemen of Rev 6:1-8:

| Mat 24 | Rev 6 | Color | Symbol |
|--------|-------|-------|--------|
| v5: False Christs / $deceive | 1st seal: white horse, bow, crown | White | Counterfeit authority |
| v6: $wars-rumors | 2nd seal: red horse, great $sword | Red | Covenant-fracture / violence |
| v7a: $famine | 3rd seal: black horse, scales | Black | Scarcity / economic collapse |
| v7b: $pestilence + $earthquake | 4th seal: pale horse, Death + $sheol | Pale | Death + the demanding place |

The $four-horsemen study identifies these as the four $wind — spirits dispatched from God's throne (Zech 6:5). They are not random plagues. They are sovereign agents executing the covenant-judgment sequence. And Rev 7:1-3 adds a critical detail: the four winds CANNOT blow until God's servants are sealed. The birth pains do not begin until the elect are marked.

## Testing Against Scripture

**Babylon 539 BC** — Jer 51:46 rumours in the land, ruler vs ruler, earth shakes. The Medo-Persian invasion follows the exact pattern. **Matches.**

**Jerusalem 586 BC** — False prophets ("peace, peace" — Jer 6:14), wars, siege-famine, Jer 14:12 triad. **Matches.**

**Jerusalem 70 AD** — Theudas and the Egyptian (false messiahs — Acts 5:36, 21:38), ethnic pogroms across Judea, famine in Acts 11:28, earthquakes at Pompeii (62 AD) and Laodicea (60 AD). **Matches.**

**Future/Global** — Rev 6 seals, Rev 16:18 greatest earthquake, 1 Th 5:3 birth pains WITH sudden destruction. **Anticipated.**

The pattern fulfills MULTIPLE TIMES across eras — not just once. Each fulfillment is a smaller enactment of the same mechanism, building toward the final and complete fall of $babylon-the-great.

## What Babylon the Great IS

The system Jesus describes collapsing is not historical Babylon the city. It is $babylon-the-great (Rev 17:5, 18:2) — the **worldwide system** of human self-rule that substitutes itself for God's covenant authority. The $babylon-the-great study traces how this system wears different faces across eras (Shinar, Egypt, Tyre, Sodom, Rome) but is one continuous structure.

And $babylon-the-great is not destroyed by an external invader. Rev 17:16-17: *"the ten horns… shall hate the $[harlot], and shall make her desolate, and shall eat her $[flesh], and burn her with $[fire]."* Her own political apparatus turns on her — because **God put it in their hearts**. Eze 28:18: *"$[fire] from the **midst** of thee."* The system consumes itself. The birth pains of Mat 24:4-8 are the early contractions of this self-destruction.

## The Survival Briefing

Jesus is not giving a doomsday forecast. He is giving a **survival briefing** for people who will live through Babylon's collapse — as Jeremiah gave instructions to the exiles living inside Babylon (Jer 51:6, 45: "come out of her, my people").

The practical instruction embedded in the symbols:
- **"Be not $[deceive]"** (v4) — don't follow counterfeit authority
- **"Be not troubled"** (v6) — these signs are expected, not alarming
- **"The end is not yet"** (v6) — don't panic prematurely
- **"Beginning of birth pains"** (v8) — this produces something; endure the labor

The fuller instruction comes in vv9-14: endure, testify, love doesn't grow cold, and *"this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and **then** shall the end come"* (v14). The testimony must be completed before the delivery.

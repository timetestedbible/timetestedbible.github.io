# Revelation 21:8

> *"But the cowardly, unbelieving, abominable, murderers, sexually immoral, sorcerers, idolaters, and all liars shall have their part in the **lake** which burns with **fire** and brimstone, which is the **second death**."* — NKJV

**Part of the [Nature of Hell](/research/symbols/hell/) study.**

---

## The Common Reading

The lake of fire is the final destination of the wicked — a place of eternal conscious torment, burning with fire and sulfur forever. This is "hell" in its ultimate form.

---

## What the Passage Actually Says

### The Hebrew Gospel Text

> *"But to those who do not believe, and to murderers, and to fornicators, and to sorcerers, and to liars, I will give their **wages** (H7939 **sakar**) from the **fire** (H784) of **Sheol** (H7585) which burns with fire and sulfur (H1614); and this is the **second** (H8145) **death** (H4194)."*

### "Lake of Fire" → "Fire of Sheol"

The Greek "lake (*limne*) of fire" becomes "fire of $sheol" in the Hebrew. There is no lake. There is $fire within the grave — the realm of the dead. This connects to the consistent OT picture:

- Deut 32:22 — "a fire is kindled in mine anger, and it burns to the depths of Sheol"
- Ps 11:6 — "upon the wicked He shall rain fire and brimstone"

The $fire is not a separate realm from the grave — it IS the fire within the realm of the dead. And the grave is itself destroyed (Rev 20:14).

### "Wages" — H7939 *Sakar*

The Hebrew does not say "their part" or "their portion." It says **wages** — H7939 *sakar*, the same word family as a hired worker's pay. Compare Rom 6:23: "the **wages** of sin is **death**." The parallel is exact — wages paid, and what is paid is death.

This is not a description of ongoing punishment. It is the settlement of an account. The wage is earned (by sin); the wage is paid (death). Transaction complete.

### "Second Death" — H4194 *Maveth*

The text names what this is: H4194 *maveth* — death. Not "second torment," not "eternal suffering." The first death was sheol (temporary — it delivers up its dead in Rev 20:13). The second death is permanent — the final wage.

---

## Harmony

1. **The "lake of fire" is the "fire of Sheol"** in Hebrew — $fire within the grave, not a separate realm.
2. **The word is "wages" (H7939 *sakar*)** — payment for what was earned, echoing Rom 6:23 exactly.
3. **"Second death" means death (H4194 *maveth*)** — permanent cessation, not conscious eternal torment.
4. **Sheol's fire connects to Deut 32:22** — God's anger burns to the depths of sheol. The $fire is His.
5. **This is the conclusion of the sequence**: sheol gives up its dead (v.13) → judged (v.13) → death and grave destroyed (v.14) → the unsaved receive wages: death (v.15, 21:8).

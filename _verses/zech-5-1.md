# Zechariah 5:1–11 — The Flying Curse

> *"This is the curse that goeth forth over the face of the whole earth."* — Zech 5:3

This study examines every word of Zechariah 5:1–11 from the **consonantal text** — the original Hebrew letters without the Masoretic vowel points added in the 6th–10th century AD. The vowel points represent one interpretive tradition. The consonants are the data.

What emerges is a vision of startling physical specificity: a flying cylinder containing fire sealed under lead, mass-produced with identical copies throughout the earth, with a pointed nose cone and thrust at its base, that enters buildings and consumes them — timber and stone together. No ancient weapon fits this description. One modern weapon fits every detail.

---

## Verse 1–2: The Flying Cylinder

> *"I lifted my eyes and looked, and behold: a **flying roll**. And he said to me, What do you see? And I said, I see a **flying roll**; the **length** thereof is **twenty cubits**, and the **breadth** thereof **ten cubits**."*

**מְגִלָּה** (H4039, *megillah*) — a scroll. A scroll is a rolled document. When rolled, it is a **cylinder**. This is geometry, not interpretation.

**עָפָה** (H5774) — flying. The cylinder is in flight.

**Twenty cubits long, ten cubits in breadth** — the exact size depends on which cubit is used. The Hebrew text does not specify, so the measurement spans a range:

- **Short cubit** (~17.5"): 20 cubits = **29.2 ft** long, diameter **4.6 ft**
- **Standard cubit** (~18"): 20 cubits = **30 ft** long, diameter **4.8 ft**
- **Royal cubit** (~20.7"): 20 cubits = **34.5 ft** long, diameter **5.5 ft**
- **Long cubit** (~22"): 20 cubits = **36.7 ft** long, diameter **5.8 ft**

The word for "breadth" (H7341 *rochab*) means "width." For a flat scroll, breadth is the width of the sheet. For a rolled cylinder, the relevant measurement is the **circumference** — the distance around the tube. Ten cubits of circumference yields the diameters shown above.

The full cubit range — **29–37 feet long, 4.6–5.8 feet in diameter** — lands directly in the size class of modern ballistic missiles. Since Zechariah describes the cylinder body and the stork-tip nose cone as **separate components** (v.1–2 vs. v.9), the 20 cubits likely measures the **cylindrical body only**. The comparison below separates body from nose cone where data is available:

- **Zechariah's cylinder**: **29–37 ft** body, **4.6–5.8 ft** diameter (+ nose cone)
- **RSM-56 Bulava** (Russian SLBM): **~33.1 ft** body + **~6.6 ft** nose cone = **39.7 ft** total, **6.6 ft** diameter
- **R-29RMU2 Sineva** (Russian SLBM): **~40.4 ft** body + **~8.2 ft** nose cone = **48.6 ft** total, **6.2 ft** diameter
- **JL-2** (Chinese SLBM): **42.7 ft** total, **6.6 ft** diameter
- **Trident II D5** (US/UK SLBM): **44.5 ft** total, **6.9 ft** diameter
- **Topol** (Russian ICBM): **~64.6 ft** body + **~9.8 ft** nose cone = **74.5 ft** total, **7.1 ft** diameter
- **Minuteman III** (US ICBM): **59.9 ft** total, **5.5 ft** diameter
- **Sarmat** (Russian heavy ICBM): **115.8 ft** total, **9.8 ft** diameter

The Bulava — Russia's current front-line submarine-launched missile deployed on Borei-class submarines — has a cylindrical body of approximately **33.1 feet**, landing squarely inside the Zechariah cubit range (29–37 ft). Its nose cone adds ~6.6 feet. The Minuteman III diameter (**5.5 ft**) matches the royal cubit calculation exactly.

The cubit range brackets the **body dimensions** of modern SLBMs — the weapons that would be launched from submarines in a first strike. The nose cone, described separately as "the tip of a stork," accounts for the remaining length.

## Verse 3: The Curse Over the Whole Earth

> *"This is the **curse** that goeth forth over the face of the **whole earth**: for every one on this side shall be purged **according to its likeness**, and every one on that side shall be purged **according to its likeness**."*

**הָאָלָה** (H423) — the curse. This object IS the curse itself.

**כָּמוֹהָ** — "according to its likeness." This word appears twice — purging on "this side" and "that side" by things that look alike. **Copies.** Identical units operating in every direction.

## Verse 4: Consumes Timber and Stone

> *"I have sent it forth, declares the LORD of Hosts. It shall **enter** the house and shall **remain** in its midst — and shall **consume** it, **with the timber thereof and the stones thereof**."*

The curse enters a structure. It lodges inside. And it **consumes** (H3615 *kalah* — to finish, to complete, to utterly destroy) the building — **both its wood and its stone**.

Ordinary fire burns timber. It does not consume stone. Stone survives fire — that is why civilizations build with it. Whatever this is, it generates heat intense enough to **destroy stone**. This detail alone eliminates every pre-modern weapon.

## Verse 5–6: Their Resemblance Throughout the Earth

> *"This is the **ephah** going forth. This is **their resemblance** throughout the whole earth."*

**הָאֵיפָה** (H374) — an ephah. A dry measure of approximately **22 liters / 6 gallons**. A small container — not a building, not a chariot. A payload the size of a bucket.

**עֵינָם בְּכָל־הָאָרֶץ** — "their resemblance in all the earth." The word עַיִן (H5869) here means **appearance, resemblance, semblance**. Things that look alike. The vision is not showing a unique object — it is showing **one example of many identical copies** deployed across the whole earth. Mass-produced. Standardized. Found everywhere.

## Verse 7: Fire Sealed Under Lead

> *"And behold, a **disc of lead** was lifted — and this is a **fire** contained in the midst of the ephah."*

**כִּכַּר עֹפֶרֶת** (H3603 + H5777) — a talent / disc of lead. Lead is the densest common metal in the ancient world. It is also the element used for **radiation shielding** — and the **decay product** of uranium. Every uranium atom, after its chain of fission and decay, ultimately becomes lead. The payload begins as fire and ends as lead.

**אשה** — the consonants א-ש-ה. Without vowel points, this is equally:
- H802 *ishshah* — woman
- H801 *ishshah* — fire, fire offering

The Masoretic pointing selects "woman." The consonantal text does not. In a vision where the object consumes timber and stone (v.4), is sealed under lead (v.7), and is called "wickedness" (v.8), **fire** is the reading that fits every contextual detail. "Woman" requires a symbolic interpretation layered on top of the physical vision. The consonants say fire. The context says fire. The Masoretes said woman — but the Masoretes are one interpretive tradition, not the text itself.

## Verse 8: The Wickedness

> *"And he said, This is **wickedness**. And he cast it into the midst of the ephah, and he cast the **stone of lead** upon its mouth."*

**הָרִשְׁעָה** (H7564) — the wickedness. The fire inside the lead-sealed container is given a name: **wickedness**. Not merely dangerous — morally abhorrent. The most wicked fire ever created.

**אֶבֶן הָעֹפֶרֶת** — the stone / weight of lead. Cast upon the **mouth** (H6310 *peh*) — the opening. The container is **sealed** with lead over its opening. Shielded. Contained. Until it is opened.

Isaiah describes what this wickedness does once released: *"**Wickedness burneth as the fire**: it shall devour the briers and thorns, and shall kindle in the thickets of the forest, and they shall **mount up like the lifting up of smoke**. The **land is darkened**, and the people shall be as the **fuel of the fire**"* (Isaiah 9:18–19). Self-propagating. A chain reaction. The people become fuel. The land goes dark.

## Verse 9: The Second Fire — Thrust and Nose Cone

> *"And I looked, and behold: a **second fire** came forth — and **blast** at its **end** — and it had **two ends** like the **ends of a stork**. And they lifted the ephah between the earth and the sky."*

This is the most physically detailed verse. Every word:

**שתים** (sh-t-y-m) — from root H8138 שנה (*shanah*): "to fold, duplicate, repeat, do again." The derived form H8145 שני (*sheni*) means: "second, another, again." The consonants do not require "two [separate entities]." They equally support **"a second / another."**

There is already one fire inside the ephah (v.7). Now **a second fire** comes forth — the propulsion system, distinct from the payload.

**נשים** (n-sh-y-m) — plural of אשה. If אשה = fire (as established in v.7), then נשים = **fires**. The second fire joins the first.

**יוצאות** (yots'ot) — coming forth, emerging. The propulsion fire emerges.

**רוח** (r-w-ch, H7307) — wind, breath, blast, driving force. In context of something that lifts a payload between earth and sky: **blast** or **driving force**. Rocket exhaust.

**בכנפיהם** (b'khanfeihem) — "at their edge/end/extremity." H3671 כנף (*kanaph*) is defined by Strong's as: **"an edge or extremity"** — then lists specialized applications: wing (of a bird), flap (of a garment), quarter (of the earth), pinnacle (of a building). The **primary meaning is edge/end**. "Wing" is one application. In context: blast at the **end** of the cylinder — the exhaust end.

**כנפים** (kh'nafayim) — the **DUAL** form of כנף. Dual is grammatical, not dependent on pointing. It means **exactly two** edges/extremities/ends. The cylinder has **two ends**.

**ככנפי החסידה** (k'khanfei hachasidah) — "like the ends of the stork."

H2624 חסידה (*chasidah*) — the stork. Of all birds mentioned in Scripture, the stork has the most distinctive extremities:

- **Front end**: the longest, most sharply pointed, tapered beak of any common bird — a **cone**
- **Back end**: straight, swept-back tail and wing edges

"Two ends like the ends of a stork" — a pointed nose cone at the front, swept edges at the back. The stork's full body profile — elongated cylinder, pointed at the front, swept at the back — is a **missile silhouette**. No other biblical bird (eagle, hawk, dove, raven, owl, sparrow) has this shape.

**ותשאנה את האיפה בין הארץ ובין השמים** — "and they lifted the payload between the earth and the sky." The two fires — the warhead and the rocket — together carry the ephah through the atmosphere.

## Verse 10–11: Guided to Named Location

> *"Where are they carrying the ephah?"*
>
> *"To make its home in the land of Babylon — readied, and guided to named location from its fixed base."*

**לבנות לה בית** — "to build for it a house." The weapon's "house" is the wasteland it creates — its permanent dwelling. *"Thou shalt be **desolate forever**"* (Jer 51:26). *"It shall **never be inhabited**"* (Isa 13:20). The weapon arrives and never leaves. The contamination IS its home.

**בארץ שנער** — "in the land of Shinar" = Babylon. The destination.

**והוכן** — from כון (H3559): "readied, prepared, made ready." The weapon is **armed**.

**והנחה** (h-n-ch-h) — the consonants support two roots:
- H5117 נוח (*nuach*) — to rest, to set down (Masoretic reading)
- H5148 נחה (*nachah*) — to guide, to lead, to direct

In a vision where the object is already airborne (v.9), **guided** fits the motion. "Set to rest" fits a static scene. The consonants do not prefer one over the other.

**שם** (sh-m) — the consonants support:
- שָׁם (*sham*) — there (a location)
- שֵׁם (*shem*) — name, designation

"Guided to a **named location**" — a designated target. Every modern guided weapon is programmed with its target coordinates — a named destination.

**על** — upon (static) or from (motion). With verbs of movement, על carries the "from upon" sense — departure from a surface.

**מכנתה** — from כון (H3559): "its fixed / engineered / established base." The same root (כון) used for the bronze laver platforms in Solomon's temple (1 Kings 7:27–43) — purpose-built, permanent, engineered platforms. The root appears **twice** in this verse: once for the weapon being readied (והוכן), once for the base it launches from (מכנתה). Same root, two applications: the weapon is armed; the platform is engineered.

## What the Plain Text Demands

Even without any consonantal alternatives, the **standard pointed text** contains details no pre-modern weapon can explain:

- A **flying** object (not thrown, not launched by catapult — in sustained flight)
- Specific **dimensions** (~38 × 5 ft)
- **Consumes stone** (no ancient fire does this)
- Sealed under **lead** (the radiation shield element; the decay product of uranium)
- **Mass-produced** with identical copies throughout the earth
- **Lifted between earth and sky** (atmospheric flight, not ground travel)

The consonantal alternatives (fire for woman, second for two, ends for wings, guided for rested, named for there) sharpen the picture. But the picture is already there without them. The standard text describes something that did not exist until 1945 — and now exists in the thousands, deployed on engineered platforms across the globe, each assigned to a named target.

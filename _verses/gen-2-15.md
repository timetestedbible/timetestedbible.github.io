# Genesis 2:15

> *"And the LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to **dress** it and to **keep** it."*

## The Symbolic Reading

> **God stationed man inside His defended sanctuary as its priest — to serve and to guard the holy place.**

---

## The Priestly Commission

### "Dress" → "Serve / Minister"

H5647 *abad* — Strong's: "to work; by implication, **to serve**, till, (causatively) enslave." KJV also translates it as "worshipper." This is not a gardening word — it is the standard term for priestly and Levitical service before God:

> *"They shall **keep** his charge... to **do the service** (H5647 *abad*) of the tabernacle."* — Num 3:7

> *"The Levites... they shall come near to me to minister unto me... they shall **minister** (H8334) unto me."* — Ezek 44:15

### "Keep" → "Guard the Sanctuary"

H8104 *shamar* — the same word from the Gen 3:24 study: "to **hedge about (as with $[thorns])**, guard, protect, preserve." This is the Levitical guarding mandate:

> *"They shall **keep** (H8104 *shamar*) his charge, and the charge of the whole congregation before the tabernacle of the congregation."* — Num 3:7

> *"And the LORD said unto Aaron... thou and thy sons with thee shall **keep** (H8104) the charge of the sanctuary."* — Num 18:5

The word-pair *abad* + *shamar* is not coincidental. It is the exact priestly job description: serve the sanctuary and guard its boundaries. Adam is the first priest.

When he fails to guard, God stations the $fire-$sword to do the *shamar* instead (Gen 3:24). The mandate Adam dropped is picked up by the $fire-word at the gate.

## The Garden Is a Temple

### Defended Enclosure

H1588 *gan* — from H1598 *ganan*: "to **hedge about, protect, defend**." A *gan* is defined by its boundary, not its vegetation. It is a defended enclosure — the same function as the tabernacle (curtained enclosure) and temple (walled sanctuary). See the Gen 3:24 study for the full analysis.

### On a Mountain

> *"A river went out of Eden to water the $[garden]; and from thence it was parted, and became into four heads."* — Gen 2:10

Rivers do not flow uphill. A single source that divides into four rivers flowing outward means Eden sits at the **high point**. The $garden is on a $mountain — the sanctuary at the summit, with life-giving $water flowing downhill to the world.

Ezekiel 28 confirms this directly, describing the figure who was "in Eden, the $[garden] of God":

> *"Thou wast upon the holy **$[mountain]** of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of $[fire]."* — Ezek 28:14

Eden IS the holy $mountain. The $garden IS the temple on the $mountain. And the $mountain study establishes: mountain = kingdom, a seat of ruling authority. Eden is God's throne-seat — His kingdom on earth — with a priest stationed to serve and guard it.

### Cherubim as Temple Furniture

When Adam is driven out, God stations H3742 *keruvim* (cherubim) at the entrance (Gen 3:24). The cherubim are not unique to Eden — they are recurring temple fixtures:

- **The mercy seat** — Two cherubim on the ark, wings covering the place where God speaks (Exod 25:18–22)
- **The Holy of Holies** — Two great cherubim overlaid with $gold, wings spanning the inner sanctuary (1 Ki 6:23–28)
- **Ezekiel's throne** — Four living creatures bearing God's throne, each with four faces (Ezek 1, 10)
- **Revelation's throne** — Four living creatures around the throne, saying "Holy, holy, holy" (Rev 4:6–8)

Their presence at Eden's boundary marks it as a sanctuary entrance. The same guardians who overshadow the ark of the covenant overshadow the gate of Eden. The garden is the original Holy of Holies.

### Precious Stones and Gold

> *"The $[gold] of that land is good: there is bdellium and the onyx stone."* — Gen 2:12

Eden is associated with precious stones and $gold. Ezekiel 28:13 describes the covering cherub's adornment in Eden:

> *"Every precious stone was thy covering, the sardius, topaz, and the diamond, the beryl, the onyx, and the jasper, the sapphire, the emerald, and the carbuncle, and $[gold]."* — Ezek 28:13

These are the same stones on the high priest's breastplate (Exod 28:17–20). The temple, the priest, and Eden share the same gemstone inventory. The sanctuary of Eden, the tabernacle of Moses, and the temple of Solomon are variations of the same design — marked by the same materials, guarded by the same cherubim, served by the same priestly mandate.

## The River Pattern

The architecture of Eden — sanctuary on a height, river flowing out, $trees bearing $fruit along its banks — repeats in every sanctuary Scripture describes.

### Eden (Gen 2:10)

A river flows **out of Eden**, divides into four heads, waters the world. The sanctuary is the source. Life-giving $water flows downhill from the temple to the nations.

### Ezekiel's Temple (Ezek 47:1, 12)

> *"Waters issued out from under the threshold of the house eastward... and the waters came down."* — Ezek 47:1

The temple-river deepens as it flows — ankle-deep, knee-deep, waist-deep, then too deep to cross (Ezek 47:3–5). And on its banks:

> *"All $[trees] for meat, whose leaf shall not fade, neither shall the $[fruit] thereof be consumed... the $[fruit] thereof shall be for meat, and the leaf thereof for medicine."* — Ezek 47:12

$trees with unfading leaves and perpetual $fruit, fed by sanctuary $water. The leaf for "medicine" (H8644 *teruphah*) anticipates what Revelation will call "healing."

### Psalm 46:4

> *"There is a river, the streams whereof shall make glad the city of God, the holy place of the tabernacles of the most High."*

### Zechariah 14:8

> *"Living $[water]s shall go out from $[jerusalem]; half of them toward the former $[sea], and half of them toward the hinder $[sea]."*

### New Jerusalem (Rev 22:1–2)

> *"A pure river of $[water] of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the $[throne] of God and of the $[lamb]. In the midst of the street of it, and on either side of the river, was there the $[tree] of life, which bare twelve manner of $[fruit]s... and the leaves of the $[tree] were for the healing of the nations."*

Same architecture every time: sanctuary on a height → river flowing out → $trees bearing $fruit → life and healing for the nations. Eden is the original. Ezekiel's temple is the prophetic vision. Revelation 22 is the restored reality. The beginning and the end are the same place.

## The Serpent in the Temple

If the $garden is a temple and the $serpent is in the garden, then the deceiver is **inside the sanctuary**. Not attacking from outside — operating from within the holy place.

### The Covering Cherub

Ezekiel 28:14–16 describes a guardian figure stationed in Eden who fell:

> *"Thou art the anointed cherub that covereth... thou wast upon the holy $[mountain] of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of $[fire]. Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee."* — Ezek 28:14–15

A temple guardian — a "covering cherub" — with access to the stones of $fire on the holy $mountain, who became corrupt from within. He was not an invader. He was part of the sanctuary's own staff. His judgment is expulsion: *"I will cast thee as profane **out of** the $[mountain] of God"* (Ezek 28:16).

### The Adversary in the Court

The $serpent's access to Eden matches a pattern that runs through Scripture — the adversary operating from **within** the divine assembly:

- **Job 1:6** — *"The sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and **Satan came also among them**."* He has standing in the court.
- **Zech 3:1** — Satan stands at the high priest's right hand, **inside** the divine council, accusing.
- **1 Ki 22:19–22** — A lying spirit stands before the LORD's $[throne], among the host of heaven, and volunteers to $[deceive].
- **Rev 12:10** — The accuser accused the brethren **"before our God day and night"** until cast down.

The threat to the sanctuary is never the army at the gate. It is the deceiver already inside.

### Serpents in the Temple — The Pattern Continues

- **Mat 23:33** — Jesus calls the religious leaders *"ye $[serpent]s, ye generation of vipers"* — $serpent-figures operating **inside** the temple system, twisting God's word from their position of authority.
- **2 Thess 2:4** — The man of sin *"sitteth **in the temple** of God, showing himself that he is God"* — a deceiver claiming divine authority from within the sanctuary. The same act as the $serpent in Eden: "ye shall be as gods."
- **Ezek 44:7–8** — *"Ye have brought into my sanctuary strangers... to pollute it... ye have **not kept the charge** of mine holy things."* The same priestly failure as Adam: the mixed element enters because the guard did not *shamar*.

## The Word on the Gate

The $fire-$sword at Eden's gate is stationed to *shamar* (guard/preserve) the *derek* ($way). The $sword (*chereb*) shares its root with Horeb, where Torah was given in $fire. The $fire-word stands at the entrance to the sanctuary.

Moses commands the same pattern for every Israelite household:

> *"And thou shalt **write them** upon the posts of thy house, and on thy **gates**."* — Deut 6:9

"Them" = "these words, which I command thee this day" (Deut 6:6) — Torah. God's covenant terms written on the gates. The $fire-word at Eden's gate and the commandments on Israel's gates are the same word at the same place.

The pattern: God's covenant stands at every entrance — the gate of the sanctuary, the gate of the city, the gate of the home. The word is on the gate.

## The Covenant Is Cut at the Gate

The $sword (*chereb*) is a cutting instrument. The covenant is "cut" — H3772 *karath*: "to cut; specifically, to **covenant** (originally by cutting flesh and passing between the pieces)." Cutting IS covenant-making.

In Gen 15:9–10, God instructs Abram to take clean animals — heifer, she goat, ram, turtledove, pigeon — and divide them. The halves are laid facing each other. Then:

> *"A smoking furnace, and a burning lamp that **passed between** those pieces. In the same day the LORD **made** (H3772 *karath* = **cut**) a covenant with Abram."* — Gen 15:17–18

$fire passes between the divided halves. The covenant is cut. And what follows immediately is the promise of the **land** — defined by its rivers (river of Egypt to the Euphrates), occupied by ten nations.

The sword at Eden's gate and the covenant ceremony share the same elements: a cutting instrument, $fire, separation, and a way into the promised land.

## The Living Temple

Eden is a temple — but the temple was never meant to remain a place. The destination of the whole pattern is **people**.

> *"Know ye not that **ye are the temple** of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?"* — 1 Cor 3:16

> *"Ye are the temple of the **living** God; as God hath said, I will **dwell in them**, and **walk in them**."* — 2 Cor 6:16

"Dwell in them and walk in them" — the same language as Eden: *"They heard the voice of the LORD God **walking** in the garden"* (Gen 3:8). The $garden where God walks IS the people He dwells in.

Peter draws every thread into one passage:

> *"Ye also, as **lively stones**, are built up a **spiritual house**, an **holy priesthood**, to offer up spiritual sacrifices."* — 1 Pet 2:5

> *"But ye are a chosen generation, a **royal priesthood**, an **holy nation**, a peculiar people."* — 1 Pet 2:9

Three things in one breath: **stones** in a temple, **priests** in the sanctuary, a **nation** — every element from Eden. The precious stones of Gen 2:12 and the breastplate of Exod 28 find their fulfillment in living people built into a living house.

And Paul adds the growth:

> *"Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone; in whom all the building fitly framed together **groweth** unto an holy temple in the Lord."* — Eph 2:20–21

The temple *groweth* — like a $tree, like a living thing. A living temple made of living stones. The *ets chayim* (tree of life = living nation) and the temple of lively stones describe the same reality: a covenant community that IS the sanctuary where God dwells.

## The Priestly Garment

> *"Unto Adam also and to his wife did the LORD God make coats (H3801 *kethoneth*) of skins, and clothed them."* — Gen 3:21

The H3801 *kethoneth* is the same word used for the priestly tunic — the garment of service:

> *"And for Aaron's sons thou shalt make **coats** (H3801 *kethoneth*)."* — Exod 28:40

God does not merely cover their $nakedness. He re-clothes a priest. The garment requires the death of a $lamb — the first sacrifice. The priest who failed to guard the sanctuary is given a new garment of service, provided through sacrifice. The priesthood is not revoked — it is restored through blood.

## Testing Against Scripture

**Num 3:7–8 — Levites abad and shamar the tabernacle.** The identical word-pair from Gen 2:15 applied to Levitical service. Adam's mandate and the Levitical mandate are the same commission. **Supports.**

**Exod 25:18–22 — Cherubim on the mercy seat.** The same H3742 *keruvim* stationed at Eden's gate overshadow the ark in the Holy of Holies. The garden and the tabernacle share the same guardians. **Supports.**

**Ezek 28:13–14 — Eden, the garden of God, on the holy mountain.** Ezekiel explicitly places Eden on God's holy $mountain with stones of $fire and a covering cherub. The garden IS a mountaintop sanctuary. **Supports.**

**Ezek 47:1, 12 — Temple river with trees bearing fruit and leaves for medicine.** The identical architecture to Gen 2:10 (river from the sanctuary) and Rev 22:2 (tree of life with leaves for healing). The pattern spans from the first temple to the last. **Supports.**

**Ezek 44:7–8 — "Ye have not kept the charge."** The same *shamar* failure as Adam, same consequences: the mixed element pollutes the sanctuary because the priest did not guard. **Supports.**

**Rev 22:1–3 — The restored Eden.** River of life from the $throne, $tree of life with $fruit and healing leaves, "no more $[curse]," "his servants shall serve (G3000 *latreuo*) him." The end-state restores the original: sanctuary, river, tree, priestly service. The architecture of Eden is the architecture of eternity. **Supports.**

No passage tested contradicts the reading. Six confirm it independently.

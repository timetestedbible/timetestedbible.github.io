# Meat

**Doing the will of the Father — the will *done* is the soul's true food. (Milk is the word *heard*; meat is the will *done*.)**

> *"Jesus saith unto them, My **meat** is to do the will of him that sent me, and to finish his work."* — John 4:34

This is the rare case where the symbol is **defined out loud**. Jesus doesn't say his meat is *like* doing the will — he says it **is** to do it. So meat is not a topic or a flavor of doctrine; it is **the Father's will, carried out.**

**Symbolizes:** doing — and finishing — the will of the Father
**Opposite:** $milk — the word merely *heard / received* (a babe is fed; he hasn't yet *done*)
**Connected:** $milk, $bread, $blood

## "My flesh is meat indeed" — the will done, then given

Jesus came for one purpose: *"not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me"* (Jn 6:38). When that will was carried all the way out, he said *"It is finished"* (Jn 19:30) — and the finished will, his life laid down, becomes food:

> *"the bread that I will give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world."* — Jn 6:51
> *"For my flesh is **meat** indeed, and my blood is drink indeed."* — Jn 6:55
> *"he that eateth me, even he shall live by me."* — Jn 6:57

Watch the one Greek root tie it together — *tel-* ("complete / finish"):
- Jn 4:34 — meat is to **finish** (G5048 *teleióō*) the work
- Jn 19:30 — **"It is finished"** (G5055 *teléō*) — the will fully done
- Heb 5:14 — strong meat belongs to **"full age"** (G5046 *téleios*)

So the chain runs: *do the Father's will → finish it → the finished will (Christ given) is "meat indeed" → and it belongs to the finished/mature.* To eat him (Jn 6:57) is to feed on a will already done.

## Milk is the word *heard*; meat is the will *done*

This reframes the milk/meat pair. Milk is *"the sincere milk of the word"* (1 Pet 2:2) — the word **received**. Meat is that word **acted on**:

> *"strong meat belongeth to them… who by reason of **use** have their senses exercised to discern."* — Heb 5:14
> *"be ye **doers** of the word, and not hearers only."* — Jas 1:22
> *"…he that **doeth the will of my Father**."* — Mt 7:21

A babe (G3516 *nḗpios*) is *"unskilful in the word of righteousness"* (Heb 5:13) — he has heard but not yet practiced. Maturity is the move from hearing to **doing**, which is exactly why meat is for the "full age" — and why Paul's Corinthians, still *"carnal,"* could take only milk (1 Cor 3:1-2). The bottleneck was never the difficulty of the ideas; it was the unwillingness to *do.*

## Meat in due season — the steward serves the Father's will

A faithful steward doesn't withhold meat; he serves it **at the right time**:

> *"…a faithful and wise servant… to give them **meat in due season**?"* — Mt 24:45 (cf. Lk 12:42, *"their portion of meat in due season"*)
> *"thou givest them their **meat in due season**."* — Ps 145:15 (cf. Ps 104:27)

God himself gives meat *in due season* (G2540 *kairós* — the appointed moment) — and the under-steward is trusted to do the same: feed the household the Father's will, rightly portioned, when they are ready for it (the same readiness Heb 5 measures).

## Labour for the meat that endures

> *"Labour not for the **meat** which perisheth, but for that **meat** which endureth unto everlasting life, which the Son of man shall give… this is the work of God, that ye believe."* — Jn 6:27-29

The enduring meat is again *the will of God done* — and 6:29 names that "work": believing on the One the Father sent. Even Job ranked it above food: *"I have esteemed the words of his mouth more than my necessary food"* (Job 23:12); man lives *"by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God"* (Mt 4:4).

## The recursion (terminates concretely)

`meat → "to do the will of him that sent me" (Jn 4:34) → Christ came to do the Father's will (6:38) → finished it (19:30) → his flesh given = "meat indeed" (6:55) → eaten / lived-by (6:57) → fed on by the mature through doing (Heb 5:14; Jas 1:22)`

**Terminus: the will of the Father, done.**

## Patterns

1. **Explicitly defined** (rare) — *"My meat is to do the will…"* (Jn 4:34). Not a synonym for "food."
2. **The will *done* is food** — Christ's whole sustenance, and his finished will *is* the "meat indeed" he gives.
3. **Milk = heard, meat = done** — the maturity divide (Heb 5; Jas 1:22; Mt 7:21) is hearing → doing, not basic → advanced topics.
4. **Served in due season** — the faithful steward (and God) give meat at the appointed time, to those ready.
5. **The *tel-* thread** — *finish* (4:34) → *finished* (19:30) → *full age* (Heb 5:14): the meat and the mature share the root of completion.

## Connections

- **$milk** — the opposite / pair: the word *received*; first food, meant to give way to *doing*.
- **$bread** — Jesus the *living bread* (Jn 6:51); the bread he gives *is his flesh* — bread and meat converge in him.
- **$blood** — *"my blood is drink indeed"* (Jn 6:55), the companion of the flesh-as-meat.

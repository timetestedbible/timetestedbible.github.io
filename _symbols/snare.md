# Snare

**A concealed mechanism of sudden seizure — what you cannot see captures you.**

> *"For as a **snare** shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth. Watch ye therefore, and pray always, that ye may be accounted worthy to escape."* — Lk 21:35–36

---

## The Key Insight

A snare only works if the prey does not see it.

> *"Surely in vain the **net** is spread in the sight of any bird."* — Prov 1:17

This single proverb unlocks the entire symbol. A trap set in full view catches nothing. The bird sees it and flies away. Visibility nullifies the mechanism. Which means: **the snare's power is concealment, and its antidote is sight.**

Every snare passage in Scripture turns on this axis. The wicked lay snares *secretly* (Ps 64:5, Ps 142:3). Fear of man is a snare because it operates on what you *don't* examine (Prov 29:25). The Day of the Lord comes as a snare because people are asleep in false security (Lk 21:34–35). The devil's snare catches the unaware (2 Tim 2:26). And the escape — every time — involves watching, waking, seeing.

Two Hebrew nouns carry the symbol, and each describes a different trap mechanism — but both share the quality of hiddenness:

- **H4170 *moqesh*** — from H3369 *yaqosh* (to ensnare): a **noose** or hook that tightens around the prey.
- **H6341 *pach*** — from H6351 *pachach* (to spread flat): a **spring-net** hammered thin as a metal sheet, laid flat on the ground — invisible until it snaps shut.

The noose tightens. The spring-net collapses. Both are concealed, triggered, and irreversible.

The snare is the inverse of watchfulness. Where watchfulness sees what is coming, the snare catches what cannot see. And Scripture consistently presents these as the only two options: you are either watching or being ensnared.

**Symbolizes:** Concealed mechanism of sudden seizure — the invisible trap that captures the unaware

**Opposite:** Watchfulness / Escape (H4422 *malat* — Ps 124:7; G1127 *gregoreuo* — Lk 21:36)

**Defining verses:** Lk 21:35, Ps 91:3, Ps 124:7, Isa 24:17–18, Prov 29:25

**The surprise:** The trap has an agent. Every snare presupposes a *fowler* — and Scripture identifies him. The devil is the ultimate fowler (1 Tim 3:7, 2 Tim 2:26). His deployed instruments are false prophets (Hos 9:8), and his method is $deceive — concealment. The snare is not impersonal fate; it is the work of a trapper who hides his device in your path.

**Connected:** $fear, $net, $darkness, $idolatry, $babylon, $false-messiah, $deceive, $sudden-destruction, $perpetual-sleep, $pestilence

## Snare Across Scripture

### The Fowler and His Device

Every snare presupposes someone who set it. Scripture calls this agent the **fowler** (H3352/H3353 *yaqosh/yaqush* — from the same root as H4170 *moqesh*). The snare is the mechanism; the fowler is the hand.

> *"Surely he shall deliver thee from the **snare** of the **fowler**, and from the noisome $[pestilence]."* — Ps 91:3

The pairing is telling. The fowler's snare and $pestilence — covenant death-consequence — stand in parallel as the two threats from which the Most High delivers. One is a trap set by a personal agent; the other is direct divine judgment. The fowler's snare is the *hidden* danger; pestilence is the *open* one. God delivers from both.

Who is the fowler? The New Testament identifies him:

> *"Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without; lest he fall into reproach and the **snare** of the devil."* — 1 Tim 3:7

> *"And that they may recover themselves out of the **snare** of the devil, who are taken captive by him at his will."* — 2 Tim 2:26

The word "taken captive" here is G2221 *zogreo* — literally, *captured alive*. The devil's snare does not kill immediately; it captures men alive for his purposes. This is the fowler's trade: not slaughter but seizure. And the instrument he deploys?

> *"The prophet is a **snare** of a **fowler** in all his ways."* — Hos 9:8

The false prophet is not merely someone who falls into a snare. He *is* the snare — the fowler's deployed mechanism. The prophet should be a watchman; instead he has become the trap itself. Compare Jer 5:26:

> *"For among my people are found wicked men: they lay wait, as he that setteth **snares**; they set a trap, they catch men."* — Jer 5:26

"They catch *men*" — not birds. The fowler's prey is human. The wicked among God's people function as the fowler's agents, and they catch men. The chain runs: the devil (ultimate fowler, 1 Tim 3:7) → operates through $false-messiah figures — counterfeit covenant authorities who redirect worship and allegiance (Hos 9:8, the prophet-as-snare) → who are wicked men embedded among God's people (Jer 5:26) → who set snares through $deceive — misrepresenting God's commands to lead people away from the covenant (Mat 24:4–5) → targeting the sleeping and unaware (Lk 21:34–35).

The mechanism that connects them all? Concealment. The fowler hides his trap. The false prophet hides his allegiance. The deception hides the departure from truth. And Prov 1:17 stands as the antidote: make the net *visible*, and no bird is caught.

### Idolatry — The Primary Trigger

The Torah uses the snare for one situation above all others: contact with idolatrous nations.

> *"If thou shalt serve their gods, it will surely be a **snare** unto thee."* — Deut 7:16

> *"Take heed to thyself, lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land whither thou goest, lest it be for a **snare** in the midst of thee."* — Exod 34:12

> *"But if ye do in any wise go back, and cleave unto the remnant of these nations… know for a certainty that the LORD your God will no more drive out any of these nations from before you; but they shall be **snares** and traps unto you."* — Josh 23:12–13

Three Torah warnings, all using H4170 *moqesh*, all pointing to the same truth: $idolatry — self-rule that rejects God's Word — is the trigger mechanism of the snare. The nations are not the snare; their *gods* are. The trap is not the people but the allegiance-shift: serving another master while dwelling in covenant land.

The warnings proved accurate. Judges records the fulfillment:

> *"And Gideon made an ephod thereof, and put it in his city… and all Israel went thither a whoring after it: which thing became a **snare** unto Gideon, and to his house."* — Judg 8:27

> *"They served their idols: which were a **snare** unto them."* — Ps 106:36

The ephod, the idols — these are the *moqesh*. And notice: the snare-quality of idolatry operates through concealment just as the literal trap does. No one sets out to be ensnared by a false god. The approach is gradual, the mechanism hidden: cohabitation leads to familiarity, familiarity to participation, participation to worship. By the time the noose tightens, the prey is already caught.

### Fear — The Trap You Set for Yourself

> *"The fear of man bringeth a **snare**: but whoso putteth his trust in the LORD shall be safe."* — Prov 29:25

Here the snare is not laid by an external fowler. *Fear itself* is the trap mechanism. The man ensnared by fear of man has set his own trap — he has made another person's opinion the concealed spring that controls his steps. The parallel line reveals the alternative: trusting the LORD. The choice is between two invisible forces shaping behavior: fear (the snare) or trust (the safety).

This self-entrapment pattern recurs:

> *"The wicked is **snared** by the transgression of his lips."* — Prov 12:13

> *"A fool's mouth is his destruction, and his lips are the **snare** of his soul."* — Prov 18:7

> *"In the transgression of an evil man there is a **snare**."* — Prov 29:6

> *"It is a **snare** to the man who devoureth that which is holy, and after vows to make enquiry."* — Prov 20:25

Sin becomes its own trap. Rash words, broken vows, transgression — these are snares the sinner sets for himself. The Psalms confirm the principle at a national scale:

> *"The heathen are sunk down in the pit that they made: in the **net** which they hid is their own foot taken."* — Ps 9:15

> *"Let their table become a **snare** before them: and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap."* — Ps 69:22

This last verse Paul quotes directly in Romans 11:9, applying it to Israel: "Let their table be made a **snare**, and a trap, and a stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them." The blessing-table — the very provision of God — becomes the trap when the recipients use it for their own ends rather than recognizing the Giver. The mechanism is the same: what should have been for welfare becomes a snare precisely because the shift in allegiance is invisible to the one making it.

### Fear, Pit, Snare — The Inescapable Cascade

> *"**Fear** (H6343 *pachad*), and the **pit** (H6354 *pachath*), and the **snare** (H6341 *pach*), are upon thee, O inhabitant of the earth. And it shall come to pass, that he who fleeth from the noise of the **fear** shall fall into the **pit**; and he that cometh up out of the midst of the **pit** shall be taken in the **snare**."* — Isa 24:17–18

This is one of the most architecturally precise judgment passages in Scripture. Three words, linked by assonance in Hebrew (*pachad*, *pachath*, *pach*) — fear, pit, snare — form a three-stage cascade from which there is no exit. You flee fear; you fall into the pit. You climb out of the pit; the snare takes you.

Jeremiah repeats the identical structure for Moab (Jer 48:43–44), confirming this as a *pattern* — not a one-time oracle but the universal mechanism of inescapable judgment. The cascade eliminates every escape route. It doesn't matter which threat you flee; the next one is waiting. The system is designed to close.

Notice the progression: fear is psychological (internal), the pit is circumstantial (external), the snare is hidden (invisible). Each operates on a different level. Together they are total. This is the anatomy of $sudden-destruction — covenant judgment falling without warning on those who rejected covenant terms. Every exit leads to another entrance.

### The Evil Time — Snare and Sudden Destruction

> *"For man also knoweth not his time: as the fishes that are taken in an evil **net**, so are the sons of men **snared** in an evil time, when it falleth suddenly upon them."* — Ecc 9:12

Three parallels: fish in a $net, birds in a snare, men in an evil time. All three share: (1) the prey does not know the moment, (2) capture is sudden (H6597 *pith'om*), and (3) the mechanism was concealed until it triggered.

The word *pith'om* ("suddenly") connects the snare to $sudden-destruction — instantaneous accountability for those who rejected covenant terms. The snare *is* the delivery mechanism of sudden destruction. The two concepts are not merely associated; they are structurally identical. The snare is what makes destruction sudden — it was hidden, and then it wasn't.

This convergence reaches its climax in Lk 21:34–35:

> *"And take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting, and drunkenness, and cares of this life, and so that day come upon you unawares. For as a **snare** shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth."* — Lk 21:34–35

Jesus identifies the Day of the Lord as a *pagis* (G3803) — a trap fastened by a noose. Global. On *all* who dwell on the earth. And the mechanism is precisely the concealment principle: hearts overcharged with surfeiting, drunkenness, cares — the anesthetics that produce $perpetual-sleep, irrevocable judgment-death through intoxication and false security. The surfeiting produces the drowsiness; the drowsiness produces the unawareness; the unawareness allows the snare to spring.

Compare this with the Babylon-pattern in Jer 51:39 — rulers made drunk at their own feast, sleeping a perpetual sleep and not waking. The mechanism is the same: intoxication → unconsciousness → irreversible capture. $babylon's entire system functions as the fowler's apparatus at civilizational scale: it intoxicates nations (Rev 17:2), lulls them into false security ("peace and safety," 1 Th 5:3), and delivers them to the snare that closes on the Day.

### God Snares the Snarer

The trap always returns to its setter.

> *"I have laid a **snare** for thee, and thou art also taken, O **$[babylon]**, and thou wast not aware: thou art found, and also caught, because thou hast striven against the LORD."* — Jer 50:24

The phrasing is devastating: "thou wast *not aware*." The master of concealment is caught by concealment. God uses the fowler's own method against the fowler's own city. $babylon, the system that ensnares nations, is herself ensnared — and by the very mechanism she perfected. She was not watching. She was not aware. The snare took her.

> *"Upon the wicked he shall rain **snares** (H6341 *pach*), fire and brimstone, and an horrible tempest: this shall be the portion of their cup."* — Ps 11:6

God *rains* snares. The imagery inverts every expectation. Normally the snare is laid low, on the ground, flat and hidden. Here God hurls them from above — inescapable, falling on the wicked from the sky. The trap-mechanism turned on the trappers.

> *"The heathen are sunk down in the pit that they made: in the **net** which they hid is their own foot taken."* — Ps 9:15

> *"Let destruction come upon him at unawares; and let his **net** that he hath hid catch himself: into that very destruction let him fall."* — Ps 35:8

The reversal is certain. The fowler's snare catches the fowler. $babylon is ensnared by God using $babylon's own tools. The self-entrapment principle — visible from Proverbs through the Prophets — finds its ultimate expression in the destruction of the world system that set the trap.

### Escape Exists

The snare is not fate. The trap can be broken.

> *"Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the **snare** of the fowlers: the **snare** is broken, and we are escaped."* — Ps 124:7

The word "broken" is H7665 *shabar* — to shatter, to dash in pieces. Not merely evaded but *destroyed*. The snare is not dodged; it is demolished. And the soul escapes *like a bird* — the very creature the snare was designed to catch.

> *"The law of the wise is a fountain of life, to depart from the **snares** of death."* — Prov 13:14

> *"The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, to depart from the **snares** of death."* — Prov 14:27

Two fountains, one result: departure from the snares of death. Wisdom and the fear of the LORD — these are the mechanisms that make the trap visible. If concealment is the snare's power, then revelation is its undoing. Wisdom *sees*. Reverence *watches*. And the one who sees and watches departs from the snare.

This is why Jesus gives the command that completes the snare-prophecy:

> *"Watch ye therefore, and pray always, that ye may be accounted worthy to **escape** all these things that shall come to pass."* — Lk 21:36

The snare comes on all the earth (v. 35). But escape exists (v. 36). The condition is *watching*: the opposite of the surfeiting, drunkenness, and cares that overcharge the heart in v. 34. The snare catches the sleeping. The watcher escapes.

And recovery is possible even after capture:

> *"And that they may recover themselves out of the **snare** of the devil, who are taken captive by him at his will."* — 2 Tim 2:26

The Greek *ananepho* ("recover themselves") means to *return to soberness* — to wake up from intoxication. The captive in the devil's snare is not beyond rescue. Sobriety — waking — breaks the trap. The $perpetual-sleep that the fowler relies on can be interrupted by repentance.

### God Himself as Snare — The Stumbling Stone

One passage inverts the entire pattern:

> *"And he shall be for a sanctuary; but for a **stone** of stumbling and for a **rock** of offence to both the houses of Israel, for a gin and for a **snare** to the inhabitants of Jerusalem."* — Isa 8:14

The same God who delivers from the fowler's snare (Ps 91:3) *becomes* the snare for those who reject Him. He is sanctuary to the faithful and *pach* and *moqesh* to the rebellious — the same two trap-words used throughout Scripture, now applied to God Himself. The very Word of God that should have been the fountain of life (Prov 13:14) becomes the mechanism of capture for those who stumble over it.

Isaiah 28:13 extends the pattern: "the word of the LORD was unto them precept upon precept… that they might go, and fall backward, and be broken, and **snared**, and taken." The Torah itself — designed for life — becomes the snare when rejected. The commandment that should have guided their steps instead triggers the trap underfoot.

This is the ultimate expression of the self-entrapment principle. The snare is not some device foreign to God's order. It is what God's own provision becomes when it is despised. The table becomes a trap (Ps 69:22, Rom 11:9). The sanctuary becomes a snare (Isa 8:14). The Word becomes the net (Isa 28:13). What was given for life captures unto death — not because the gift changed, but because the recipient did.

## Patterns

1. **Concealment is the essence.** Every snare is hidden. Ps 64:5 — "they commune of laying snares privily." Ps 142:3 — "in the way wherein I walked have they privily laid a snare for me." Prov 1:17 — visibility nullifies the trap. The snare's power *is* its invisibility.

2. **Idolatry is the primary trigger.** Exod 23:33 → Judg 8:27 → Ps 106:36. The Torah's dominant use of *moqesh* is for idol-worship — $idolatry, self-rule that rejects God's Word. The shift in allegiance is the concealed spring.

3. **Fear, pit, snare — the inescapable cascade.** Isa 24:17–18 = Jer 48:43–44. Three stages, three levels (psychological, circumstantial, invisible), no exit. The anatomy of inescapable judgment.

4. **Self-entrapment.** Ps 9:15, Ps 35:8, Prov 12:13, Prov 29:6. Sin sets its own trap. The wicked fall into their own net. The snare always returns to its setter.

5. **The snare is the mechanism of $sudden-destruction.** Ecc 9:12 — snared in evil time falling *suddenly*. Lk 21:35 — the Day as *pagis* on all earth. Concealment + suddenness = sudden-destruction's delivery system.

6. **The fowler is the devil; the snare is the false prophet.** 1 Tim 3:7, 2 Tim 2:26 — the devil's snare. Hos 9:8 — the prophet *is* the snare. The trap is not impersonal. It has an agent, an instrument, and a target.

7. **Sleep is the fowler's prerequisite.** Lk 21:34 — surfeiting, drunkenness, cares overcharge the heart. The snare catches the sleeping. $perpetual-sleep — the intoxicated stupor of false security — is the condition the fowler depends on.

8. **Escape is always possible.** Ps 124:7 — snare broken. 2 Tim 2:26 — recovery from the devil's snare. Lk 21:36 — watch and pray to escape. Prov 13:14, 14:27 — wisdom and fear of the LORD are the departure from the snares of death.

9. **God snares the snarer.** Jer 50:24 — $babylon snared by God using her own concealment. Ps 11:6 — God rains snares on the wicked. Ps 9:15 — their own foot taken. The reversal is certain.

10. **God Himself becomes a snare to the rebellious.** Isa 8:14 — sanctuary to the faithful, snare to the disobedient. The provision that should have been life becomes the trap when rejected. The gift doesn't change; the recipient does.

## Connections

- **$sudden-destruction** — Covenant judgment falling without warning. The snare is its delivery mechanism: concealment produces the "suddenness" — the gap between perceived safety and actual guilt. Ecc 9:12 and Lk 21:35 fuse the two: the evil time falls suddenly because the snare was hidden.

- **$darkness** — Hidden, concealed disobedience. $darkness and the snare share the same operative principle: what is concealed controls the outcome. The fowler works in $darkness (Ps 64:5 — "privily"); the snare catches those walking in $darkness who cannot see the trap in their path.

- **$net** — The snare's companion at a different scale. The snare (H4170/H6341) is an individual trap; the $net (H2764/H7568) is a system-level dragnet. Ps 140:5 shows both together. $babylon operates at $net-scale (Hab 1:15–17); false prophets operate at snare-scale (Hos 9:8). Both are deployed by the same fowler.

- **$idolatry** — Self-rule and rejection of God's Word — the primary trigger mechanism of the snare in Torah. Serving other gods is described as *moqesh* (Exod 23:33, Deut 7:16, Judg 8:27, Ps 106:36). The shift from God's authority to self-rule is the concealed spring that the prey does not notice until it closes.

- **$deceive** — The fowler's method. To $deceive is to cause covenant-breaking by misrepresenting God's commands. Deception *is* the concealment that makes the snare effective. Mat 24:4 — "take heed no man deceive you" — is the prophetic warning that makes the trap visible (Prov 1:17).

- **$false-messiah** — Counterfeit covenant authority. Hos 9:8 — the false prophet *is* the snare of the fowler. Not an agent who falls into a trap, but the trap itself deployed by the devil. The $false-messiah redirects worship and allegiance — the snare's concealed function at the level of prophetic office.

- **$perpetual-sleep** — Irrevocable judgment-death through intoxication and false security. The snare catches the sleeping. Lk 21:34 links surfeiting and drunkenness directly to the snare of v. 35. Jer 51:39 shows $babylon's rulers drunk at feast, sleeping perpetually — the terminal stage of the fowler's work.

- **$babylon** — Man's substitute system for God's covenant rule. $babylon is the fowler's apparatus at civilizational scale — intoxicating nations (Rev 17:2), lulling them into false security, delivering them to the snare. And God snares $babylon with her own method: "thou wast not aware" (Jer 50:24). The system that ensnares nations is itself ensnared.

- **$pestilence** — Covenant death-consequence. Paired with the snare in Ps 91:3 — "the snare of the fowler" parallel to "the noisome pestilence." Both are mechanisms of covenant judgment; the snare is hidden and set by an agent, while $pestilence is direct and sent by God. Deliverance from both belongs to the one who dwells in the secret place of the Most High.

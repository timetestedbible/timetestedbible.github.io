# Mark

**Observable practice — the visible evidence of whose covenant you keep, expressed in what you do and what you believe.**

> *"And it shall be for a **sign** unto thee upon thine hand, and for a memorial between thine eyes, that the LORD's law may be in thy mouth."* — Exo 13:9

---

## The Key Insight

The KJV translates one Hebrew word — H226 *'owth* — as "mark," "sign," and "token" in different passages. This conceals a stunning unity. Watch what Scripture calls by that single word:

- The mark on Cain (Gen 4:15) — H226 *'owth*
- The rainbow after the flood (Gen 9:12) — H226 *'owth*
- Circumcision (Gen 17:11) — H226 *'owth*
- The blood on the doorposts (Exo 12:13) — H226 *'owth*
- The Sabbath (Exo 31:13) — H226 *'owth*
- The commandments on hand and forehead (Deut 6:8) — H226 *'owth*

These look like unrelated things — a brand, a weather phenomenon, a surgical rite, blood smeared on wood, a day of rest, phylacteries. But the word is the same every time. What do they share?

Every one is an **observable practice that identifies covenant membership**. The rainbow marks God's commitment to His covenant with the earth. Circumcision marks Abraham's household. Doorpost blood marks those who keep Passover. The Sabbath marks those who keep God's time. The commandments on hand and forehead mark those who practice God's $truth.

The mark is not the covenant itself — that is the $name. The mark is the **visible evidence** of which covenant you observe.

**Symbolizes:** Observable practice — the visible evidence identifying whose authority you serve

**Opposite:** Unmarked / unidentified — no visible allegiance, no distinguishing practice

**Defining verses:** Deut 6:6–8, Exo 31:13, Exo 13:9, Rev 13:16, Rev 14:1, Ezek 9:4

**The surprise:** "Sign," "token," and "mark" are the same word in Hebrew (H226). When Scripture says the Sabbath is a *sign* between God and Israel, and then Revelation warns against taking the beast's *mark*, it is using the same concept: whose practices do you observe?

**Connected:** $name, $truth, $faith, $bread, $fornication, $idolatry, $babylon

## Mark Across Scripture

### What the Hand and Forehead Actually Mean

> *"And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart: And thou shalt bind them for a **sign** upon thine hand, and they shall be as frontlets between thine eyes."* — Deut 6:6–8

This is the passage that decodes the symbol. The pronoun "them" in verse 8 refers back to "these words which I command thee" in verse 6. The sign on the hand and the frontlets between the eyes are not arbitrary labels — they *are* the commandments.

The hand represents what you **do** — your actions, your practices. The forehead represents what you **think** — your beliefs, your allegiance of mind. When God's commandments are the sign upon your hand and between your eyes, it means your actions and your thinking both align with His $truth.

Exodus 13:9 makes the equation explicit: the sign on the hand exists "that the LORD's law may be in thy mouth." The observable practice reveals the internal reality. The mark is not a tattoo or a chip or a physical stamp. It is the answer to a question: *whose instructions are you following?*

This is not a metaphor pasted onto a physical object. It works the other direction: the physical practices *are* the mark. Keeping the Passover is a sign. Keeping the Sabbath is a sign. Practicing God's commandments is a sign. The practice itself is the identifying mark.

### The Sabbath — God's Defining Mark

> *"Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep: for it is a **sign** between me and you throughout your generations; that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you."* — Exo 31:13

> *"It is a **sign** between me and the children of Israel for ever: for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh day he rested, and was refreshed."* — Exo 31:17

The Sabbath is singled out as the *sign* (H226) of the covenant between God and Israel — the observable practice that distinguishes His people. Not the only commandment, but the identifying one: the practice that makes visible who you belong to. Notice the purpose: "that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you." The mark reveals the master. The Sabbath identifies whose calendar you keep, whose rhythm orders your life, whose authority structures your week.

This establishes a principle that runs all the way to Revelation: the mark is a matter of **whose schedule you observe**. The turtledove knows her appointed times (Jer 8:7). The mark identifies who keeps them.

### The First Mark — Cain

> *"And the LORD set a **mark** upon Cain, lest any finding him should kill him."* — Gen 4:15

The first appearance of H226 *'owth* translated "mark" comes after the first murder. Cain has rejected God's prescribed offering (his practice was wrong) and killed the brother whose practice was right. Now God puts a mark *on* Cain — a visible identifier.

The mark here is protective, but the context is telling: Cain's problem was a matter of **practice**. God told him: "If thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted?" (Gen 4:7). Cain's offering — his observable practice — did not conform to God's standard. The mark placed on him afterward identifies him visibly. From the very first use, the mark is connected to what you do and whose instructions you follow.

### Ezekiel's Tav — Marked by Grief Over Abominations

> *"And the LORD said unto him, Go through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and set a **mark** upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations that be done in the midst thereof."* — Ezek 9:4

> *"Slay utterly old and young… but come not near any man upon whom is the **mark**."* — Ezek 9:6

The word here is different — H8420 *tav*, the name of the last letter of the Hebrew alphabet (which in paleo-Hebrew was shaped like an X or a cross). But the function is the same: a mark on the forehead that identifies those whose hearts and practices are aligned with God.

Who receives this mark? Those who "sigh and cry for all the abominations" — those whose internal grief (forehead) over covenant-breaking is visible. Those without the mark are destroyed. The mark separates those who grieve over $idolatry from those who practice it.

This passage bridges directly to Revelation 7:3 — "Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads." Same location (forehead), same function (protection during judgment), same criterion (allegiance to God expressed in practice).

### Two Masters, Two Marks

The contrast in Revelation becomes clear once the symbol is understood:

> *"And I looked, and, lo, a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father's **$[name]** written in their foreheads."* — Rev 14:1

> *"And he causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a **mark** in their right hand, or in their foreheads."* — Rev 13:16

The Father's $name (covenant) in the forehead versus the beast's mark (G5480 *charagma* — a stamp, an imprint) in the hand or forehead. Both are in the same locations. Both involve allegiance. Both are expressed in practice.

Revelation 14:12 tells us what distinguishes the two groups: "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that **keep the commandments of God**, and the $[faith] of Jesus." The identifying practice of God's people is keeping His commandments — exactly what Deut 6:8 and Exo 31:13 described as the *sign*. Those who worship the beast follow the beast's ordinances instead.

The mark of the beast is not a technology. It is the same kind of thing as the mark of God — an observable set of practices, in hand (what you do) and forehead (what you believe), that identifies which authority you serve. God's mark = His commandments kept. The beast's mark = the beast's system observed. The question is never *whether* you bear a mark, but *whose* mark your practices display.

### The Blood on the Doorpost

> *"And the blood shall be to you for a **token** upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you."* — Exo 12:13

The Passover blood is also H226 *'owth* — a visible sign of covenant observance. The blood on the doorpost is not merely decorative; it is the evidence that the household *kept the Passover*. They slaughtered the lamb at the appointed time, applied the blood as instructed, ate the meal with unleavened $bread as commanded.

The blood-sign marks those who practiced what God commanded. The destroying angel passes over them — the same logic as Ezek 9:4–6, where the marked are spared and the unmarked are destroyed. The pattern is consistent: **the mark is the evidence of observance, and the mark protects**.

### Circumcision — The Covenant Token

> *"This is my covenant, which ye shall keep, between me and you and thy seed after thee; Every man child among you shall be circumcised… and it shall be a **token** of the covenant betwixt me and you."* — Gen 17:10–11

Again, H226 *'owth* — translated "token." Circumcision is the *visible evidence* of belonging to Abraham's covenant. Paul later clarifies that the circumcision that matters is "of the heart, in the spirit" (Rom 2:29) — but the principle remains: the mark is the practiced evidence of covenant relationship.

The $name is the covenant itself. The mark is the cut that shows you've entered it.

### The Rainbow — God's Own Mark

> *"I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a **token** of a covenant between me and the earth."* — Gen 9:13

Even God takes a mark. The rainbow is His own H226 *'owth* — the visible, recurring sign of His covenant with creation. The mark is not only for human beings. It is the principle that covenant is made visible through observable evidence. God Himself practices this principle.

## The Chain: Name, Truth, Faith, Mark

The co-occurring symbols form a logical chain:

The $name is the covenant — the relationship itself. $truth is God's commandment — the standard that defines the covenant's terms. $faith is trusting obedience — the response of practicing that standard. The mark is the **observable result** — the visible evidence that identifies whose $truth you follow and whose $name you bear.

Or from the negative side: $idolatry is self-rule, rejecting God's $bread (word/covenant terms). $fornication is the act of turning to other masters. And the beast's mark is the observable practice of that false allegiance — the hand and forehead given to $babylon's system instead of to God's.

The mark sits at the end of the chain, where internal reality becomes external identification. You cannot have the mark without the practice. You cannot hide the practice from the one who reads the mark.

## Patterns

1. **One word, three translations.** H226 *'owth* appears as "mark" (Gen 4:15), "sign" (Exo 31:13), and "token" (Gen 9:12, Gen 17:11) in KJV — obscuring the unity. Every instance involves visible evidence of covenant membership.

2. **Hand = practice; forehead = belief.** Deut 6:8, Exo 13:9, Rev 13:16, Rev 14:1, Ezek 9:4. The mark always involves the same two locations because allegiance is always expressed in two dimensions: what you do and what you think.

3. **The mark protects.** Gen 4:15 (Cain's mark prevents killing), Exo 12:13 (blood-mark prevents the plague), Ezek 9:4–6 (marked are spared from destruction), Rev 7:3 (sealed before judgment). Those who bear God's identifying mark are preserved through judgment.

4. **Two marks, same locations, opposite masters.** Rev 14:1 (Father's $name in forehead) versus Rev 13:16 (beast's mark in hand/forehead). The question is not whether you are marked, but whose system your practices reflect.

5. **The mark is the commandments.** Deut 6:6–8 makes this explicit: the "sign upon thine hand" = "these words which I command thee." The Sabbath is a "sign" (Exo 31:13). The mark is not a physical object but the observance of God's $truth.

6. **Mark ≠ Name.** The $name is the covenant relationship. The mark is the evidence of it. You can invoke the $name and still practice lawlessness (Mat 7:22–23) — using the covenant's name without bearing the covenant's mark. The mark tests whether the $name is real.

## Connections

- **$name** — The $name is the covenant itself; the mark is its visible evidence. You bear the $name (enter the covenant relationship) and display the mark (practice the covenant's terms). Rev 14:1 combines them: the Father's $name written in the forehead. The mark without the $name is dead religion; the $name without the mark is empty profession (Mat 7:22–23).

- **$truth** — $truth is God's commandment — the standard itself. The mark is what happens when that standard is practiced. Deut 6:6–8 places God's commands (His $truth) on the hand and forehead as the identifying sign. Those who "do the truth" (Jn 3:21) are those who bear God's mark.

- **$faith** — $faith is trusting obedience — the response of acting on God's word. The mark is the visible result of that obedience. $faith without works is dead (Jas 2:17); a mark without observable practice is no mark at all. The mark is $faith made visible.

- **$bread** — $bread is the word / covenant terms — what God speaks and what His people consume. The mark is the evidence that the $bread has been received and acted upon. Shewbread was set out every Sabbath (1 Chr 9:32) — God's word renewed on the day that is itself the identifying sign (Exo 31:13).

- **$fornication** — $fornication is the act of turning to other masters — idolatry in practice. It is the anti-mark: the visible evidence of serving the wrong authority. "Go a whoring after other gods" (Deut 31:16) is the inverse of keeping God's sign on hand and forehead.

- **$idolatry** — $idolatry is self-rule, rejecting God's word. The beast's mark in Revelation is the culmination of this: a system of practice that replaces God's commandments with a counterfeit authority's ordinances. $idolatry is the root; the beast's mark is its visible expression.

- **$babylon** — $babylon is man's substitute system for God's covenant rule. Its mark is the set of practices that identify participation in that system — its calendar, its commerce, its worship. "Come out of her, my people" (Rev 18:4) is a call to stop bearing $babylon's mark and resume bearing God's.

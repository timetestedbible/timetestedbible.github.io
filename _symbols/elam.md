# Elam

**Modern Iran — the place where Scripture first enacts the fall of Babylon and where it says the bow will be broken in the latter days.**

> *"Thus saith the LORD of hosts; Behold, I will **break the bow** of Elam, the chief of their might."* — Jer 49:35

---

## The Key Insight

Elam appears in three major passages. Each one tells the same story from a different angle: *a coalition falls, captives are freed, and God's authority replaces the old order.*

Genesis 14 walks it out as an enacted parable. Isaiah 22 fills it with Messianic detail. Jeremiah 49 anchors it to the latter days. The word "Elam" quietly threads through Scripture as the place where $babylon-the-great begins to crack.

Ancient Elam occupied southwestern Iran (Khuzestan province). Its capital was Shushan — the same Susa where Daniel received his vision of successive empires (Dan 8:2). H5867 *Eylam* derives from H5956 *alam* — "to veil from sight, conceal."

**Connected:** $babylon-the-great, $fall-of-babylon, $four-horsemen, $pit-abyss, $throne, $jubilee

---

## The First Enacted Parable — Genesis 14

The very first military coalition in Scripture is led by Elam.

Chedorlaomer king of Elam and Amraphel king of Shinar (= Babel) lead a four-king alliance. They take Lot captive from Sodom. Abraham — with 318 dedicated servants born in his own house — defeats the coalition by night, rescues Lot, and brings back everything.

The details repay close attention.

### The Battlefield Is Made of Babel

The vale of Siddim was "full of slimepits" (Gen 14:10). The word is H2564 *chemar* — the same word used in Gen 11:3 for the mortar at the Tower of Babel: "slime had they for mortar." The battleground where the Elam-Babylon coalition operates is literally paved with Babel-building material.

### 318 = Eliezer

Abraham's 318 servants are described with a word that appears nowhere else in the Old Testament: H2593 *chaniyk* — "dedicated, initiated," from the root *chanak* (the same root as Hanukkah, "dedication"). These are not hired soldiers. They are consecrated members of his household.

And the number 318 is the gematria value of H461 *Eliezer* — "God is my help" — Abraham's chief servant (Gen 15:2). The fighting force is numerically identified with the servant whose name means divine help. Abraham even pursues the coalition "unto Hobah, which is on the left hand of Damascus" — Eliezer's home territory (Gen 15:2, "Eliezer of Damascus").

### Covenant Lords vs. Self-Covenant

Abraham's allies are called *ba'alei berith* (H1167 + H1285) — "covenant-lords" (Gen 14:13). A real covenant alliance stands against the self-covenant of the Babel system, whose founding act was "let us make us a name" (Gen 11:4) — let us make our own covenant.

### What Follows the Victory

After the coalition falls, two encounters happen in sequence:

**Melchizedek appears** — king of Salem, priest of the Most High God — with bread and wine (Gen 14:18). Hebrews 7:1 explicitly identifies this as a Yeshua-type: "who met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings, and blessed him." The King-Priest appears *after* the Elam-Babylon coalition is defeated. Not before.

**Abraham refuses Sodom's goods** — "I will not take from a thread even to a shoelatchet, and that I will not take any thing that is thine, lest thou shouldest say, I have made Abram rich" (Gen 14:23). Complete separation from the system's wealth.

The pattern: captives taken → defeated by God's help → righteous extracted → King-Priest appears with covenant meal → system's goods refused. This is the template. The rest of the Elam passages fill it in.

## The Bow — Weapon and Covenant Fused

The word H7198 *qesheth* carries multiple dimensions simultaneously.

It is a **weapon** — the primary military instrument of the ancient world, projecting power at distance. "Elam bare the quiver" (Isa 22:6). "The bow of Elam, the chief of their might" (Jer 49:35).

It is a **covenant sign** — the very same word appears in Gen 9:13: "I do set my bow{H7198} in the cloud, and it shall be for a token{H226} of a covenant{H1285}." God's rainbow is His *qesheth* — His war-bow set in the sky, a covenant sign.

And it is a **binding obligation** — in Isa 22:3, rulers are "bound{H631 *asar*} by the bow{H7198}." The KJV renders this "archers," but the word is *qesheth*. H631 *asar* yields H632 *essar* — "an obligation or vow." The rulers are yoked by their own bow/covenant.

These dimensions are not competing meanings to choose between. Military power, covenant commitment, and binding obligation are fused in the same word. The self-covenant of the Babel system ("make us a name") manifests as military power and binds its rulers even as they flee.

When Jer 49:35 says "I will break the bow of Elam, the *reshiyth* of their might" — H7225 *reshiyth* means "beginning, chief, firstfruit" — it describes the shattering of the foundation from which the power flows. The covenant is not a byproduct of the military; the military is an expression of the covenant.

Compare Jer 51:56: "upon Babylon... every one of their bows is broken." Same object, same word, same judgment. The Elam prophecy and the Babylon prophecy use the same vocabulary because they describe the same event from different angles.

## The Prophecy — Jeremiah 49:34-39

This is the passage that anchors Elam to the latter days. It sits as the last prophecy before the massive Babylon prophecy of Jeremiah 50-51 — a literary prologue to the fall.

### "In the Latter Days"

The prophecy is dated to "the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah" (v.34), but it concludes with an unmistakable eschatological marker: "it shall come to pass **in the latter days**, that I will bring again the captivity of Elam, saith the LORD" (v.39). Whatever the historical shadow, the primary fulfillment is future.

### The Sequence

**v.35** — The bow broken. The foundation of the system's power shattered (see above).

**v.36** — "Upon Elam will I bring the four winds from the four quarters of heaven." The $four-horsemen study identifies the four winds as God's executing spirits (Zech 6:5, "These are the four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth"). God's judgment brought upon the system through real-world events — armies, geopolitics, the kind of instruments God has always used (compare Isa 45:1, where Cyrus is called God's "anointed" without knowing it).

Then the most striking phrase: "there shall be no nation whither **the outcasts** of Elam shall not come."

The word "outcasts" is H5080 *niddach* — "to push off, banish, drive away, cast out." The *niddachim* are the driven-out ones, the banished. If Elam represents $babylon-the-great in this eschatological context, then "the outcasts OF Elam" are not Iranians being scattered — they are God's people who were **banished by** the Babylonian system, already scattered among every nation. "There shall be no nation whither they shall not come" describes where they already ARE. They **come** from every nation. This is a gathering, not a scattering.

Compare Mat 24:31: "He shall gather together his elect from the four winds." Compare Rev 18:4: "Come out of her, my people." Compare Isa 11:11: remnant recovered from Elam and Shinar.

**v.37** — Dismayed before enemies. The sword consumes.

**v.38** — "I will set my throne in Elam, and will destroy from thence the king and the princes." God's $throne replaces worldly authority. The same pattern as Gen 14 (Melchizedek appears after the coalition falls), Isa 22:20-23 (Eliakim installed after Shebna removed), and Rev 11:15 ("The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord").

**v.39** — Latter-day restoration.

## The Valley of Vision — Isaiah 22

Isaiah 22 is often read as a straightforward siege narrative, but it contains Elam (v.6), the bow/covenant (v.3), and one of the most detailed Messianic typologies in the prophets (v.20-25) — all in one chapter.

### Rulers Bound by the Bow

"All thy rulers fled together, they are bound{H631} by the bow{H7198}" (v.3). The same rulers who held power through the bow/covenant discover it holds *them*. They cannot escape their own system even in flight.

### Elam Bears the Quiver

"Elam bare the quiver with chariots of men and horsemen" (v.6). The military dimension of the bow/covenant is present — Elam carries the system's striking power.

### Breaches, Gathering, Inscribing, Grape-Harvest

Verses 9-10 contain words with striking dual meanings:

"Ye have seen the **breaches**{H1233} of the city of David, that they are many; and ye **gathered together**{H6908} the **waters**{H4325} of the **lower pool**{H1295}." The breaches are many. The people are gathered from the lowest cistern — the place of containment and suppression ($pit-abyss: Zech 9:11 "prisoners out of the pit wherein no water" = covenant liberation).

"Ye have **numbered**{H5608 *saphar* = to score with a mark, inscribe} the **houses**{H1004 = clans} of Jerusalem, and the houses have ye broken down to **fortify**{H1219 *batsar*} the wall." H1219 *batsar* carries a dual meaning directly from the lexicon: (1) to gather grapes, (2) to fortify. The same word. The clans are inscribed; the grape-harvest proceeds.

### Feasting Before Judgment

"Let us eat and drink; for to morrow we shall die" (v.13). The spirit of Belshazzar's feast (Dan 5) — denial and indulgence while the system collapses around them. Compare Rev 18:7: "I sit a queen, and shall see no sorrow."

### "God Raises Up, Son of Yah's Inheritance"

After the corrupt steward Shebna is removed (v.15-19), God installs His servant:

"I will call **my servant** Eliakim the son of Hilkiah" (v.20).

The names decode: H471 *Eliakim* = H410 *El* (God) + H6965 *qum* (to rise, raise up) = **"God raises up."** H2518 *Hilkiah* = H2506 *cheleq* (portion, inheritance) + H3050 *Yah* = **"Yah's inheritance."** "My servant" (*ebed*) is a Messianic title (Isa 42:1, 52:13, 53:11).

He is clothed with the robe{H3801 *kethoneth* — same word as God's coats for Adam and Eve in Gen 3:21, and Joseph's coat in Gen 37:3} and strengthened with the priestly girdle{H73 *abnet*, Exod 28:4}. He receives government. He becomes "a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem" (compare Isa 9:6, "Everlasting Father").

"The **key** of the house of David will I lay upon his **shoulder**; so he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open" (v.22). Revelation 3:7 quotes this directly about Yeshua. And Isa 9:6 places "the government upon his **shoulder**" — the same body part, the same authority.

"I will fasten him as a **nail**{H3489 *yathed*} in a **sure**{H539 *aman* — root of "Amen"} place; and he shall be for a **glorious throne**{H3678 *kisse*} to his father's house" (v.23). The same *kisse* appears in Jer 49:38: "I will set my throne in Elam."

"They shall **hang**{H8518 *talah*} upon him all the glory of his father's house" (v.24). H8518 *talah* is the word used for crucifixion — Deut 21:22-23, "hang him on a tree," quoted in Gal 3:13. All the father's glory *hung* on the one who is nailed.

Then v.25: the nail is removed, cut down, falls. The burden "cut off" — H3772 *karath*, the same word as "cut a covenant" (*karath berith*). The nail, the hanging, and the covenant-cutting in one sequence.

The full arc of Isaiah 22: rulers bound by the bow/covenant → Elam's military force → sins of God's people, people gathered from the pit, clans inscribed, grape-harvest → feasting before judgment → corrupt authority removed → "God-raises-up, son of Yah's-inheritance" installed with key of David, nailed, hung with glory, covenant cut.

## The Prologue to Babylon's Fall

Jeremiah 49:34-39 occupies a precise structural position: it is the **last** prophecy in the sequence of nation-prophecies (Jer 46-49) and sits immediately before the massive Babylon prophecy of Jeremiah 50-51. It shares Babylon's own judgment vocabulary — bow broken, dismayed, sword, scattering. The Elam prophecy introduces the fall of Babylon.

Jeremiah 25:25-26 provides the cup-of-wrath sequence: kings of Elam drink **before** Babylon. "The king of Sheshach (= Babylon) shall drink after them." The Elam conflict comes first; Babylon's full fall follows.

The Jer 50-51 Babylon prophecy itself contains nine $jubilee terms — trumpet, Redeemer, captives freed, debts cancelled, land returned, rest, perpetual covenant, "come out." The fall of Babylon IS the cosmic Jubilee. And the Elam prophecy is its prologue.

## The Gathering

At Pentecost, "Elamites" appear among those gathered in Jerusalem, hearing in their own tongues (Acts 2:9). They are listed alongside "Parthians, Medes, and... dwellers in Mesopotamia" — peoples from the Babylon-system geography, now gathered into covenant.

Compare Jer 49:36: the banished ones from every nation *come*. Compare Jer 49:39: "in the latter days, I will bring again the captivity of Elam." Pentecost may be the initial fulfillment of this restoration — the driven-out ones gathered and hearing God's word.

And in Ezra 10:2, it is the sons of Elam who initiate repentance: "We have trespassed against our God... yet now there is **hope**{H4723} in Israel concerning this thing." The Elam-clan declares hope.

## For Further Study

- **$fall-of-babylon** — The full Babylon prophecy that Elam introduces. Nine jubilee terms. The "come out" command. The cosmic Jubilee.
- **$jubilee** — The 49+1 cycle, the 70th jubilee from entry into the land, and the chronological proof connecting Jer 25:11-12 to the latter days. The bow-breaking of Jer 49:35 uses the same H7665 *shabar* as Isa 61:1 "brokenhearted" — the jubilee release from the system's bondage.
- **$four-horsemen** — The four winds of Jer 49:36 = the four spirits/horsemen of Zech 6:5. The bow of the white horse rider (Rev 6:2, G5115 *toxon* — only NT occurrence) and its connection to the bow of Elam.
- **$pit-abyss** — Ezek 32:24, where Elam goes down to the pit with all the other empire-systems. Enforced containment that removes influence — the destination shared by every self-exalting power.

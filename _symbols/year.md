# Year

## Summary

**A turning — shanah, written on the root to change, repeat, return: one complete cycle of time, at whatever scale the count is running — Jeremiah’s seventy “years” close only as seventy jubilees, and a thousand years pass as a [day](/research/symbols/day/).**
> *"To proclaim the acceptable **year** of the LORD, and the $[day] of vengeance of our God; to comfort all that mourn"* — Isa 61:2

---

## Definitions

### Symbolic Definition

**A turning — shanah, written on the root to change, repeat, return: one complete cycle of time, at whatever scale the count is running — Jeremiah’s seventy “years” close only as seventy jubilees, and a thousand years pass as a [day](/research/symbols/day/).**

### Literal Biblical Definition

*Not yet stated separately. This is a research gap: the literal sense must be derived from ordinary biblical uses, not copied from Webster.*

### Webster's Definition

Webster's 1913 entry for **year** begins: “The time of the apparent revolution of the sun trough the ecliptic; the period occupied by the earth in making its revolution around the sun, called the astronomical year; also, a period more or less nearly agreeing with this, adopted by various nations as a measure of time, and called the civil year; as, the common lunar year of 354 days, still in use among the Mohammedans; the year of 360 days, etc.”

These definitions may agree, but they are independent evidence. A biblical passage may use the term literally, symbolically, or both at the same time; the literal properties remain part of the logic when Scripture uses the object as a symbol.

## The Key Insight

The Hebrew word for "year" — H8141 *shaneh* — does not mean "365 days." It means **a revolution of time**. Strong's defines it: *"a year (as a revolution of time)."* And it derives from H8138 *shanah*, a primitive root meaning "to fold, duplicate, change, **return**, **repeat**, do the second time."

The word encodes a cycle that comes back to where it started. But it never specifies how large the cycle is. What constitutes one "revolution" depends on what is revolving.

For a farmer watching seasons, one *shaneh* is the earth's orbit — a single calendar year. But for Israel's covenant calendar, the fundamental revolution is the Jubilee cycle: seven sabbaths of years (7 × 7 = 49 years), after which every debt is cancelled, every slave freed, every inheritance returned to its original owner (Lev 25:8-10). The Jubilee IS a *shaneh* — the complete turn of the socio-economic wheel back to its origin. The grandest revolution in the system.

When Isaiah writes "the acceptable **year** of the LORD," he is not marking a 12-month window. He is invoking the entire Jubilee institution — the appointed revolution when all things return.

**Symbolizes:** A revolution of time — the appointed cycle of return, from a single calendar year to a complete Jubilee revolution

**Opposite:** $day — concentrated judgment. Where "year" is the comprehensive restoration-cycle, "day" is the singular moment of divine action. Isaiah consistently pairs them: "the **day** of vengeance" with "the **year** of [Jubilee concept]." The day destroys the bondage-system; the year establishes the liberty.

**Defining verses:** Isa 61:1-2, Isa 34:8, Isa 63:4, Lev 25:8-10, Ezek 46:17, Gen 6:3

**The surprise:** H8147 *shᵉnayim* ("two/twofold") and the plural of H8141 *shanim* ("years") are **consonantally identical** — both written שנים in the unpointed Hebrew text. Both derive from the same root H8138 *shanah*. In Daniel 9:25-26, the word the KJV translates "two" could equally be read "years" — the Jubilee counting system is embedded in the consonantal text itself.

**Connected:** $trumpet, $day-as-1000-years, $fall-of-babylon, $return-of-christ, $rapture-gathering, $harvest

## The Root Family: Everything Returns

H8138 *shanah* is a primitive root. Strong's: *"to fold, i.e. duplicate; by implication, to transmute... change, repeat, return, do the second time."* From this single root, Hebrew derives an entire family of words — and every one of them encodes the concept of cyclical return:

| Strong's | Unpointed | Meaning | Derivation |
|---|---|---|---|
| H8138 *shanah* | שנה | to return, repeat, change, revolve | Primitive root |
| H8141 *shaneh* | שנה | year (revolution of time) | From H8138 |
| H8141 plural *shanim* | שנים | years | Plural of H8141 |
| H8145 *sheni* | שני | second; adv: **again** | From H8138 |
| H8147 *shᵉnayim* | שנים | two; **twofold** | Dual of H8145 |
| H4932 *mishneh* | משנה | repetition, duplicate, double | From H8138 |

The root verb "return/repeat" (שנה), the noun "year" (שנה), and the number "two/years" (שנים) share the same consonants. The core biliteral root שנ (shin-nun) carries one meaning across every derivative: **cyclical return to origin**.

The Jubilee is exactly this concept enacted in law. Leviticus 25:10: "ye shall **return** (H7725 *shub*) every man unto his possession, and ye shall **return** every man unto his family." The Jubilee IS the *shanah* — the revolution that brings everything back.

## Scripture Scales Time-Units at Every Level

The claim that "year" can mean a Jubilee cycle is not a one-off interpretive stretch. It follows a systematic pattern that Scripture builds from the smallest unit to the largest. Every time-word in Israel's calendar operates at multiple scales:

### The Sabbath Alone Covers Five Scales

The word "sabbath" (H7676 *shabbath* / G4521 *sabbaton*) demonstrates the principle most dramatically:

1. **The 7th day** — its primary meaning (Exo 20:10)
2. **An entire week (7 days)** — G4521 *sabbaton* = "week" in Mat 28:1, Mk 16:2, Lk 24:1, Jn 20:1, Acts 20:7, 1 Cor 16:2. The KJV translates the same Greek word as "sabbath" in one verse and "week" in the next.
3. **The 7th year** — Lev 25:4: "in the seventh **year** shall be a **sabbath** of rest unto the land"
4. **A 7-year period** — Lev 25:8: "seven **sabbaths** of years... forty and nine years." Here "sabbath" means an entire 7-year block, and seven of them compose one Jubilee cycle.
5. **The millennial reign (1,000 years)** — Rev 20:2-7, where the saints reign for one "day" measured at the scale of 2 Pet 3:8 and Ps 90:4: "one day is with the Lord as a thousand years." The millennium is the cosmic sabbath — the 7th "day" of the 7,000-year creation week.

One word operating at five scales — from 24 hours to a millennium. The context determines the size of the cycle.

### The Full Scaling Chain

| Smaller Unit | Scales To | Passage |
|---|---|---|
| **Day** → year | 1 day = 1 year | Num 14:34, Ezek 4:6 |
| **Day** → 1,000 years | 1 day = 1,000 years | Ps 90:4, 2 Pet 3:8 |
| **Sabbath** → week | sabbath-day = 7-day cycle | G4521 *sabbaton* = "week" |
| **Sabbath** → 7th year | sabbath-day = sabbath-year | Lev 25:4 |
| **Sabbath** → 7-year block | "sabbaths of years" | Lev 25:8 |
| **Week** → 7 years | 1 week = 7 years | Dan 9:24, H7620 *shabuwa* |
| **Sabbath** → 1,000 years | millennial reign = cosmic sabbath | Rev 20, 2 Pet 3:8 |
| **Year** → Jubilee cycle | 1 revolution = 49 years | The pattern's next step |

### The Nested Hierarchy

```
Sabbath-day (7th day)
 └─ Sabbaton/Week: 7 days, governed by the sabbath
     └─ Year (shaneh): cycle of seasons returns to origin
         └─ Sabbath-year: 7th year, land rests (Lev 25:4)
             └─ "Sabbaths of years": 7 sabbath-years = 49 years (Lev 25:8)
                 └─ Jubilee: the 50th year — total reset, liberty proclaimed
                     └─ Cosmic Week: 7 "days" of 1,000 years = 7,000 years
                         └─ Millennial Reign: the 7th "day" — cosmic Sabbath (Rev 20)
```

At every level, the 7th unit brings rest and the cycle resets. The self-similar pattern runs unbroken from a literal 24-hour day to a 1,000-year cosmic sabbath. A "year" meaning a 49-year Jubilee cycle is not a stretch — it is the most natural reading within a system that Scripture itself builds from top to bottom.

## Leviticus 25:8 — The Word That Describes Both Parts and Whole

> *"And thou shalt number seven sabbaths of **years** unto thee, seven times seven **years**; and the space of the seven sabbaths of **years** shall be unto thee forty and nine **years**."* — Lev 25:8

The word H8141 *shaneh* appears **five times** in this single verse. It simultaneously describes:

- The individual calendar years being counted
- The qualifying unit for each sabbath-period ("sabbaths of **years**")
- The sum total of the cycle ("forty and nine **years**")

The word is elastic enough to name both the parts and the whole in the same sentence. The 49-year period IS a compound *shaneh* — one complete revolution of the Jubilee system. Just as we say "seven days make a week" and then treat the "week" as a single unit, Leviticus says "seven sabbaths of years make a Jubilee cycle" — and that cycle functions as a single revolution.

## Isaiah's "Year" = Jubilee, Not Calendar

Isaiah uses "year" (H8141) three times in prophetic oracles, and each time loads it with unmistakable Jubilee vocabulary. The word is never a calendar duration in these passages — it invokes the entire Jubilee institution:

### Isaiah 61:1-2 — "The Acceptable Year of the LORD"

> *"The Spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek; he hath sent me... to proclaim **liberty** (H1865 *derowr*) to the captives... to proclaim the acceptable **year** of the LORD"*

H1865 *derowr* ("liberty") is the **exact word** from the Jubilee legislation — Lev 25:10: "proclaim **liberty** (H1865 *derowr*) throughout all the land." Same verb "proclaim" (H7121 *qara*) in both passages. H7522 *ratson* ("acceptable") comes from H7521 *ratsah*, which Strong's defines as: *"to be pleased with; specifically, to satisfy a debt."* Debt satisfaction IS the Jubilee function.

Jesus reads this passage in Luke 4:18-19 and declares: "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears." He was not inaugurating a 12-month period. He was proclaiming the Jubilee.

### Isaiah 34:8 — "The Year of Recompences"

> *"For it is the $[day] of the LORD's vengeance, and the **year** of recompences for the controversy of Zion."*

H7966 *shilluwm* ("recompences") derives from H7999 *shalam* — "to make complete, to make whole, to **restore**." The root of H7965 *shalom*. The Jubilee IS the institution of *shalam*: making whole what was broken, restoring what was lost.

### Isaiah 63:4 — "The Year of My Redeemed"

> *"For the $[day] of vengeance is in mine heart, and the **year** of my redeemed is come."*

H1350 *gaal* = the kinsman-redeemer — the **technical Jubilee term**. Lev 25:25: "If thy brother be waxen poor... any of his kin come to **redeem** (H1350 *gaal*) it." Lev 25:48-49 repeats *gaal* for redemption of persons. When Isaiah says "the year of my *gaal*," he is saying: the Jubilee-revolution of my kinsman-redeemed ones.

### Ezekiel 46:17 — "The Year of Liberty"

> *"Then it shall be his to the **year** of **liberty** (H1865 *derowr*); after it shall return to the prince"*

Ezekiel does not even need the word "jubilee." He calls it simply "the year of liberty." The word "year" with the right qualifier already MEANS the Jubilee cycle.

### The Day/Year Binary

Isaiah's consistent pairing reveals the structural relationship:

| Passage | "Day" = concentrated judgment | "Year" = Jubilee restoration |
|---|---|---|
| Isa 34:8 | day of the LORD's vengeance | year of recompences (restore/make whole) |
| Isa 61:2 | day of vengeance of our God | acceptable year (debt satisfaction + liberty) |
| Isa 63:4 | day of vengeance in mine heart | year of my redeemed (kinsman-redemption) |

The **day** destroys the bondage-system. The **year** (Jubilee-revolution) establishes the restoration. This binary only works if "year" is carrying Jubilee content, not merely marking a duration.

## Daniel 9 — Jubilee Counting in the Consonantal Text

### The Identical Consonants of Daniel 9:24

The consonants שבעים appear **twice identically** in Dan 9:24. Only the Masoretic vowel points — added over a millennium after the text was written — distinguish "seventy" (שִׁבְעִים H7657) from "weeks" (שָׁבֻעִים H7620). The unpointed consonantal text reads: **שבעים שבעים**.

The project's translation patch (`data/translation-patches.json`) reads both as "seventy": **"Seventy seventy (or jubilees)."** This admits two alternative readings, potentially a dual fulfillment:

- **70 × 70 = 4,900 years** (seventy multiplied by seventy)
- **70 Jubilee cycles = 70 × 49 = 3,430 years** (seventy Jubilees)

Different numbers, but the blog post traces both timelines to converging endpoints from different starting points and different eras of history.

### The שנים Connection in Daniel 9:25-26

The standard reading: "threescore and **two** (H8147 *shᵉnayim*) weeks." But H8147 שְׁנַיִם and the plural of H8141 שָׁנִים ("years") are **consonantally identical** in the unpointed text — both written שנים. Only Masoretic vowels distinguish "two/twofold" from "years."

The phrase could therefore be read: **"sevens (H7620 *shabuwa*), sixty (H8346) and years (שנים)"** — the Jubilee unit + the count + the word "years" itself, woven into the consonantal fabric of the prophecy.

Both words derive from the same primitive root H8138 *shanah* (return, repeat, revolve). The Jubilee system, the counting units, and the word "years" all trace back to the same Hebrew root — and in the unpointed text of Daniel 9, they are written with the same letters.

### What the Prophecy Accomplishes

When Daniel's timetable completes, the result IS the cosmic Jubilee: "to finish the transgression, and to make an end of sins, and to make reconciliation for iniquity, and to bring in **everlasting righteousness**, and to seal up the vision and prophecy, and to anoint the most Holy" (Dan 9:24). Permanent restoration. Permanent liberty. Permanent atonement. The ultimate *shaneh* — the revolution from which there is no further cycle.

## Genesis 6:3 — 120 Years as 120 Jubilees

> *"And the LORD said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty **years**."* — Gen 6:3

The traditional reading takes "120 years" as 120 calendar years. But:

- **Not a lifespan cap** — Abraham lived to 175, Isaac to 180, Jacob to 147. People lived far beyond 120 after the flood.
- **Not a precise flood countdown** — Noah was 500 when his sons were born, 600 at the flood. The 120 doesn't fit cleanly.
- **But 120 Jubilee cycles × 49 years = 5,880 years** — a span that, from the traditional reckoning of Adam, terminates in the present era.

Moses embodies both readings simultaneously: he died at exactly 120 calendar years (Deut 34:7), on his birthday (Deut 31:2), his body taken by God (Jude 9). The literal 120-year life enacts the prophetic 120-Jubilee span. One man walks out in a single life what the species walks out across history — the smaller revolution (one life) as an enacted parable of the larger revolution (the full span measured in Jubilee-years).

## Patterns

1. **The root encodes the meaning.** H8138 *shanah* = return, repeat, change. H8141 *shaneh* = year (revolution). The Jubilee = the ultimate return. The word for "year" and the function of Jubilee are the same concept: cyclical return to origin.

2. **Time-units scale at every level.** Day → year (Num 14:34). Day → 1,000 years (2 Pet 3:8). Sabbath → week, year, 7-year block, millennium. Week → 7 years (Dan 9). Year → Jubilee cycle follows the identical pattern.

3. **Isaiah's "year" is always a Jubilee.** Three prophetic "years" (Isa 34:8, 61:2, 63:4) each carry exclusive Jubilee vocabulary: *derowr* (liberty), *gaal* (kinsman-redeemer), *shilluwm* (restoration). Never a generic calendar reference.

4. **The day/year binary structures judgment and restoration.** Day = the moment of breaking. Year = the cycle of restoring. The $trumpet sounds on the day; the Jubilee unfolds across the year.

5. **Consonantal ambiguity is structural, not accidental.** שבעים שבעים in Dan 9:24 (seventy/weeks) and שנים in Dan 9:25-26 (two/years) hold dual readings because the concepts are related at the root level. The text encodes both the counting system and its Jubilee purpose in the same consonants.

6. **The 7,000-year framework is the capstone.** If 1 day = 1,000 years, the creation week = 7,000 years: six "days" of labor, one "day" of sabbath rest (the millennial reign). The fractal pattern repeats from hours to millennia. A "year" meaning a Jubilee cycle is one more level in a structure Scripture builds from top to bottom.

## Relationship to Other Symbols
- **$trumpet** — The Jubilee trumpet (Lev 25:9) proclaims the *shaneh* — the year of liberty. The $trumpet initiates the revolution. Five OT trumpet traditions converge at the seventh trumpet (Rev 11:15), which IS the cosmic Jubilee: kingdom transferred, debts cancelled, captives freed. The trumpet sounds on the "day"; the Jubilee-year is what unfolds.

- **$day-as-1000-years** — The companion scaling principle. If a "day" can mean 1,000 years, then a "year" meaning 49 years is the modest version of the same principle. Together they establish that biblical time-words operate at whatever scale the covenant context requires. The 7,000-year creation week and the Jubilee cycle are two applications of the same fractal system.

- **$fall-of-babylon** — Nine Jubilee terms appear in Jeremiah 50-51 (trumpet, redeemer, captives freed, debts cancelled, land returned, rest, return, perpetual covenant, "come out"). The fall of Babylon IS the cosmic Jubilee — the ultimate *shaneh*, the revolution that resets everything. Isaiah 34:8 pairs "the year of recompences" with the destruction of God's enemies. The year of restoration requires the day of judgment.

- **$return-of-christ** — The Covenant King's arrival is the hinge between the "day" and the "year." The day of vengeance (Isa 63:4) and the year of the redeemed are the same event from two angles: the day destroys the old order, the year inaugurates the new. The $return-of-christ is when the trumpet sounds and the Jubilee-revolution begins.

- **$rapture-gathering** — The gathering of the $elect at the $trumpet (Mat 24:31) is the Jubilee ingathering — the year when all return to their inheritance. Isaiah 27:13: "the great trumpet shall be blown, and they shall come which were ready to perish... and shall worship the LORD in the holy mount at Jerusalem."

- **$harvest** — The Jubilee year is also a sabbath year — no sowing, no reaping (Lev 25:11). The land rests. But the year AFTER the Jubilee, the harvest resumes. The cosmic Jubilee ($fall-of-babylon, $return-of-christ) is the moment between the final $harvest and the new planting — the great reset before the millennial age.

## Corpus and Method

This study checks the glossary's defining citations first, then compares them with every KJV verse that prints *year*, *years*. The English-form sweep found **738 verses**. It is exhaustive for those printed forms, but it is not a substitute for a Strong's-number sweep where several Hebrew or Greek words share one English translation.

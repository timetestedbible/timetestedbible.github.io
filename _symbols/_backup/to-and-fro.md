# To-and-Fro

**Restless traversal without covenant $rest — the lash-stroke of an agent who has no settled place.**

> *"And the LORD said unto Satan, Whence comest thou? Then Satan answered the LORD, and said, From going **to and fro** in the $[earth], and from walking up and down in it."* — Job 1:7

---

## The Key Insight

The Hebrew verb behind "to and fro" (H7751 *shuwt*) means "to push forth" — to lash, to sweep, to traverse. Its derivative noun (H7752 *showt*) is a **scourge** — a whip. The back-and-forth motion of the whip *is* the motion of the verb. To go to-and-fro is to move like a lash: repetitive, sweeping, restless, never arriving.

This connection — verb and noun sharing a single root — reveals that the to-and-fro motion is not neutral travel. It is the signature movement of something that has no covenant resting place. The whip strikes and returns, strikes and returns. The agent traverses and comes back, traverses and comes back. There is no destination. There is no home.

Scripture's first to-and-fro agent is the $raven, sent from the ark in Gen 8:7. It goes "to and fro" over the judgment waters and never returns. The $dove goes out, finds no rest, and *comes back* to the ark. The raven is content in the world of death; the dove needs a home. Noah's two birds establish the pattern: to-and-fro belongs to the creature without covenant rest.

Then in Job 1:7, Satan uses the same language. He has been going "to and fro" (H7751 *shuwt*) in the $earth. The same word. The same motion. The raven over the flood and Satan roaming the earth share not just a concept but a verb — and a posture: traversal without arrival, searching without finding rest.

**Symbolizes:** Restless agent-traversal — the motion of one who has no covenant home, whether adversary seeking prey, divine eyes seeking the faithful, or desperate humans seeking truth in a time of famine

**Opposite:** $rest (H5117 *nuach*, H7673 *shabath*) — settled in one's covenant place, the Sabbath-stillness of arriving where you belong

**Defining verses:** Job 1:7, 2 Chr 16:9, Dan 12:4, Amos 8:12, Zech 4:10

**The surprise:** The same verb (H7751) describes Satan's patrol *and* God's watchful eyes sweeping the earth. Same motion — opposite purpose. The to-and-fro is not inherently evil; it is inherently *unsettled*. What defines it is the absence of rest.

**Connected:** $raven, $earth, $sea, $wind, $locust, $tyre, $babylon

## To-and-Fro Across Scripture

### The Raven's Motion, the Adversary's Motion

The raven in Gen 8:7 goes "forth to and fro" using a different Hebrew construction (H3318 *yatsa* + H7725 *shub* — "going out and returning") — but the concept is identical, and when Satan describes his own activity in Job 1:7 and 2:2, the verbal echo is unmistakable:

> *"And he sent forth a **raven**, which went forth to and fro, until the waters were dried up from off the earth."* — Gen 8:7

> *"From going **to and fro** in the $[earth], and from walking up and down in it."* — Job 1:7

Two paired verbs define Satan's motion: H7751 *shuwt* (to lash/traverse) and H1980 *halak* (to walk). He is not going somewhere. He is covering ground — sweeping back and forth like the stroke of a whip, patrolling a territory that is not his, looking for what he can find. Peter captures the same image with different words: "your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour" (1 Pet 5:8).

The $raven never returns to the ark. Satan has no covenant dwelling. Both are at home in the to-and-fro precisely because they have no home at all.

### God's Eyes — Same Motion, Opposite Purpose

> *"For the **eyes** of the LORD run **to and fro** throughout the whole $[earth], to shew himself strong in the behalf of them whose heart is perfect toward him."* — 2 Chr 16:9

> *"For who hath despised the day of small things? for they shall rejoice, and shall see the plummet in the hand of Zerubbabel with those seven; they are the **eyes** of the LORD, which run **to and fro** through the whole $[earth]."* — Zech 4:10

The same verb — H7751 *shuwt* — now with a different subject. God's eyes perform the identical sweeping traversal that Satan does. The whole $earth is covered. The motion is the same restless, comprehensive scanning.

But the purpose inverts. Satan goes to-and-fro seeking whom he may devour. God's eyes go to-and-fro seeking whom they may *strengthen*. The adversary's patrol looks for vulnerability. The divine patrol looks for faithfulness. Both find what they are looking for.

This pairing suggests that the to-and-fro motion itself is not the problem — it is the condition of the one who performs it and the aim they carry. The motion belongs to the agent who must traverse because the object of their search is *hidden* somewhere in the earth. If everyone were faithful, God's eyes would not need to sweep. If no one were vulnerable, Satan would have no prey. The to-and-fro exists because the $earth is unsettled — and the agents who cross it reflect that unsettlement.

Zechariah expands the picture further. In Zech 1:10–11 and 6:7, angelic patrols "walk to and fro" (H1980 *halak*) through the earth and report back: "all the earth sitteth still, and is at rest." The patrol agents themselves are in motion so that they can confirm — or deny — whether the earth has found its rest. Their movement serves the question: *has the restlessness ended yet?*

### The End-Times Search — Two Famines

> *"But thou, O Daniel, shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run **to and fro**, and knowledge shall be increased."* — Dan 12:4

> *"And they shall wander from sea to sea, and from the north even to the east, they shall run **to and fro** to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it."* — Amos 8:12

Two end-times prophecies use the same verb (H7751 *shuwt*). Both describe frantic, widespread searching. But the outcomes diverge completely:

In Daniel, the to-and-fro *produces* something — knowledge increases. The sealed book is opened. The searching bears fruit, at least in terms of human understanding.

In Amos, the to-and-fro finds *nothing*. The context is devastating: "Behold, the days come, saith the Lord GOD, that I will send a famine in the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the LORD" (Amos 8:11). The searching is desperate — "from $[sea] to $[sea], from the north even to the east" — but the word of the LORD is gone. The to-and-fro motion without the word to anchor it becomes pure restlessness. They traverse and traverse and never arrive.

Set side by side, these two passages suggest a terrible possibility: that human knowledge and divine word can move in opposite directions. One can increase while the other disappears. The to-and-fro intensifies in both cases, but only one search finds what it seeks.

### The Desperate City — Searching for Righteousness

> *"Run ye **to and fro** through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in the broad places thereof, if ye can find a man, if there be any that executeth judgment, that seeketh the truth; and I will pardon it."* — Jer 5:1

God himself commands the to-and-fro — a sweeping search through Jerusalem's streets for even one righteous person. The echo of Abraham's negotiation over Sodom (Gen 18:23–32) is unmistakable, but here the count has been reduced to *one*. The search is H7751 *shuwt*: lash through the streets, sweep every corner.

The implied answer is silence. The to-and-fro finds no one who executes judgment or seeks truth.

> *"Run **to and fro** by the hedges."* — Jer 49:3

In this second Jeremiah passage, the daughters of Rabbah (Ammon) are in flight from judgment. The to-and-fro is now panic — not searching but scattering, running along the boundaries, looking for any escape route. The motion of the lash has become the motion of the lashed.

### The Rowers of Tyre — Commerce as Restless Traversal

> *"The inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad were thy mariners (H7751): thy wise men, O $[tyre], that were in thee, were thy pilots."* — Ezek 27:8

> *"Thy rowers (H7751) have brought thee into great $[waters]: the east $[wind] hath broken thee in the midst of the $[seas]."* — Ezek 27:26

In Ezekiel's oracle against $tyre, the men who drive the great merchant-ship are literally "to-and-fro-ers" — H7751. The mariners who row, who sweep oars back and forth through the $sea, are performing the lash-stroke of the root. $tyre's entire commercial enterprise is propelled by the to-and-fro motion — agents traversing the $sea of nations, sweeping from port to port, never arriving because the next trade is always somewhere else.

And it is the east $wind — the judgment-wind of God — that breaks the ship. The restless traversal of commerce meets the irresistible force of divine judgment, and the rowers who drove $tyre into the great waters are the very ones who brought it to the place of its destruction. The to-and-fro motion carried the system to its ruin.

### The Whip — The Noun That Reveals the Verb

The derivative noun H7752 *showt* — a scourge or whip — appears in contexts that illuminate the verb's symbolic weight:

> *"My father hath chastised you with **whips** (H7752), but I will chastise you with scorpions."* — 1 Ki 12:11

Rehoboam's boast turns the to-and-fro motion into an instrument of oppressive rule. The whip-stroke that should have been correction becomes tyranny.

> *"When the **scourge** (H7752) shall pass through… for the overflowing **scourge** (H7752) shall pass through, then ye shall be trodden down by it."* — Isa 28:18

Isaiah describes a "covenant with death" (Isa 28:15) that promises protection from the sweeping scourge. But the covenant is annulled — and the scourge *passes through*. The same back-and-forth stroke now traverses the land unchecked. Those who thought they had rest from the to-and-fro discover they are directly in its path.

> *"And the LORD of hosts shall stir up a **scourge** (H7752) for him according to the slaughter of Midian at the rock of Oreb."* — Isa 10:26

God himself wields the whip-stroke against Assyria. Just as the verb H7751 describes both Satan's patrol and God's searching eyes, the noun H7752 can be the adversary's oppression *or* God's judgment instrument. The motion belongs to whoever has the power to sweep.

## Patterns

1. **Same motion, three agents.** Satan patrols to devour (Job 1:7). God's eyes sweep to strengthen (2 Chr 16:9). Desperate humans search for truth (Jer 5:1, Amos 8:12). The to-and-fro does not belong to one moral category — it belongs to the *condition* of unsettlement. The restless motion reveals that something is unfound.

2. **The verb IS the whip.** H7751 *shuwt* (traverse) and H7752 *showt* (scourge) share a root. The to-and-fro motion is the back-and-forth stroke of a lash. Agents who traverse restlessly are themselves whips — instruments of striking. Those without rest become instruments of scourging.

3. **The raven is the prototype.** Gen 8:7 establishes the first to-and-fro pattern — going out and never returning, content among judgment waters. Job 1:7 echoes it. The $raven and Satan share the motion of one who has no ark to return to.

4. **Commerce is powered by the lash-stroke.** $tyre's mariners and rowers are H7751 — the same word. The restless traversal of the $sea that drives $babylon's trade system is literally the to-and-fro. When the east $wind breaks the ship (Ezek 27:26), the lash-stroke motion that built the empire is what delivered it to destruction.

5. **End-times intensification.** Dan 12:4 and Amos 8:12 both describe an increase in to-and-fro activity at the time of the end — but one finds knowledge while the other cannot find the word of the LORD. The to-and-fro accelerates; whether it bears fruit depends on what is being sought.

6. **The opposite is Sabbath.** If to-and-fro = restless traversal without arrival, its opposite is $rest — the settled stillness of one who has found their covenant place. The Sabbath (H7673 *shabath*) and Noah's rest (H5117 *nuach* — the very word embedded in Noah's name, Gen 5:29) represent the cessation of the lash-stroke. The dove found rest; the raven never did.

7. **The to-and-fro resolves in Zechariah's report.** The angelic patrols walk to-and-fro through the earth and report: "all the earth sitteth still, and is at rest" (Zech 1:11). The patrol continues only until the restlessness ends. When the earth enters its rest, the to-and-fro has no further purpose.

## Connections

- **$raven** — The to-and-fro's first agent. The raven goes forth and never returns to the ark (Gen 8:7), establishing the pattern that Satan repeats in Job 1:7. Both are at home in the restless sweep because they have no covenant dwelling.

- **$earth** — The territory traversed. Satan goes to-and-fro "in the earth" (Job 1:7). God's eyes run to-and-fro "throughout the whole earth" (2 Chr 16:9). The $earth is the field being swept — the place where rest has not yet been established.

- **$sea** — $tyre's rowers (H7751) traverse the $sea (Ezek 27:8, 26). The $sea = nations. To-and-fro through the sea is restless commercial traversal among the peoples. Amos 8:12 — wandering "from sea to sea" seeking the word of the LORD.

- **$wind** — The east $wind breaks $tyre's ship driven by its rowers/to-and-fro agents (Ezek 27:26). Eph 4:14 — "tossed to and fro… by every wind of doctrine." $wind = invisible spiritual power that drives or destabilizes the motion.

- **$tyre** — $babylon's commercial face, propelled by H7751 agents. The mariners and rowers of Ezek 27 are literally to-and-fro-ers — restless commercial agents whose sweeping traversal drives the merchant empire.

- **$babylon** — The system whose restless commercial activity is the to-and-fro in its economic form. $tyre's rowers feed $babylon's trade. Satan's patrol (Job 1:7) mirrors the spirit of the world-system that never rests, never arrives, never ceases consuming.

- **$locust** — Joel 2:9 describes the locust-army running "to and fro" (H8264 *shaqaq*) in the city. Isa 33:4 — "running to and fro of locusts." The $locust swarm's consuming sweep is a collective form of the to-and-fro motion — many agents traversing in consuming waves.

## For Further Study

- **$raven** — The to-and-fro's prototype agent. What does it mean that the creature who never returns to the ark is the one God commands to feed Elijah during famine (1 Ki 17:4–6)?

- **$rest** — The direct opposite of the to-and-fro. How does Sabbath-rest function as the resolution of restless traversal — and what does it mean that the dove's defining quality is seeking rest while the raven's is not needing it?

- **$tyre** — The commercial system propelled by H7751 rowers. How does Ezekiel's ship-of-Tyre oracle illuminate the restless traversal at the heart of $babylon's trade network?

- **$sea** — The territory the rowers traverse. If the $sea = nations, then to-and-fro through the sea is commercial and spiritual restlessness among the peoples — the condition that ends only when "there was no more sea" (Rev 21:1).

- **$locust** — The collective consuming swarm whose motion through the city (Joel 2:9) is a corporate version of the to-and-fro. How does the swarm relate to the individual agents — Satan, God's eyes, desperate seekers — who traverse alone?

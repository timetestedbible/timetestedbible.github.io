# Hunter

**One who pursues and captures by targeted force — either as a predatory empire-builder or as God's agent of unavoidable pursuit. The hunter finds what the $fisherman's $net cannot reach.**

> *"Behold, I will send for many fishers, saith the LORD, and they shall fish them; and after will I send for many **hunters**, and they shall **hunt** them from every $[mountain], and from every hill, and out of the holes of the $[rock]s."* — Jer 16:16

---

## The Key Insight

Scripture names exactly two hunters. The first is Nimrod, who built $babylon. The second is Esau, who lost the covenant.

Every other use of hunting in the Bible — whether God sends the hunters or the wicked set the traps — orbits these two archetypes. The hunter is defined by the method: targeted pursuit and capture. What distinguishes one hunter from another is *who is being pursued, and why*.

Nimrod hunted men to build an empire. Esau hunted game but lost the birthright. God sends hunters to find those who hide from His gathering. False prophetesses hunt souls through deception. The $harlot hunts for the precious life. In every case, the hunter's action is the same: deliberate, targeted pursuit of a specific quarry — unlike the $fisherman, who casts a $net broadly into the $sea and gathers whatever swims into it.

This distinction — the $net sweeps indiscriminately; the hunter tracks individually — is the key that unlocks Jer 16:16 and connects every hunting passage in Scripture.

**Symbolizes:** Targeted pursuit and capture by force — predatory or divinely commissioned

**Opposite:** The $shepherd, who gathers by leading and voice rather than by pursuit and force

**Defining verses:** Gen 10:9, Gen 25:27, Jer 16:16, Ezek 13:18–20, Prov 6:26

**The surprise:** The Hebrew root H6679 *tsud* means "to lie alongside" — the hunter's method is *ambush*, not open chase. And H6718 *tsayid* means both "the hunt" and "the provision obtained" — the same word covers the pursuit and its fruit. Esau hunts venison (*tsayid*) for his father's blessing, but Jacob — the *tam* (complete, whole, undivided) man — obtains the blessing instead.

**Connected:** $fish, $net, $snare, $mountain, $rock, $babylon, $serpent, $shepherd

## Hunter Across Scripture

### The First Hunter Built the First Empire

> *"He was a mighty **hunter** before the LORD: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty **hunter** before the LORD."* — Gen 10:9

> *"And the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar."* — Gen 10:10

Nimrod is the first named hunter (H6718 *tsayid*) in Scripture. He is also the first empire-builder. The text connects these identities without separation — he "began to be a mighty one in the earth" (Gen 10:8), he was a "mighty hunter before the LORD" (Gen 10:9), and his kingdom was Babel (Gen 10:10).

"Before the LORD" (H6440 *panim* + H3068) can mean "in the face of" — not necessarily in service to, but in the presence of, or even in opposition to. The first hunter builds the first $babylon — the system that gathers people by force to construct a substitute kingdom. Hunting, at its origin, is *capturing people to build empires*. $babylon is man's substitute system for God's covenant rule, and the hunter is its founding method.

Micah confirms the link centuries later: "the land of Nimrod" is used as a synonym for Assyria (Mic 5:6) — the great hunting empire that pursued and captured nations.

### The Second Hunter Lost the Covenant

> *"And the boys grew: and Esau was a cunning **hunter** (H6718), a man of the field; and Jacob was a plain (H8535 *tam*) man, dwelling in tents."* — Gen 25:27

The contrast is precise and loaded. Esau = hunter, man of the $field (H7704 — the open, exposed, unprotected space). Jacob = *tam*, dweller in tents.

The word *tam* (H8535) is the same word used for the $dove in Song 5:2 — "my dove, my *undefiled*" — and for Job in Job 1:1 — "*perfect* and upright." It means complete, whole, undivided. The hunter is set against the one who is whole; the man of the exposed field against the man sheltered under covenant covering.

What follows is devastating for the hunter's archetype. Esau hunts venison (*tsayid*) to obtain his father's blessing, but Jacob — the complete man — receives it instead (Gen 27:33). Before that, Esau sells his birthright for a bowl of stew (Gen 25:34). The hunter obtains *provision* but loses *covenant*. The very word for what he pursues (*tsayid* = game/provision) is what distracts him from what matters.

This pattern echoes through Scripture: the hunter gets the food but misses the blessing.

### Two Phases of God's Gathering

> *"Behold, I will send for many fishers, saith the LORD, and they shall fish them; and after will I send for many **hunters**, and they shall **hunt** them from every $[mountain], and from every hill, and out of the holes of the $[rock]s."* — Jer 16:16

This is the defining verse for understanding the hunter's role in God's purposes, and it depends on knowing what the $fisherman does by contrast.

**Phase 1 — Fishers.** $fish are people living in the $sea of nations, leaderless and vulnerable (Hab 1:14). The fisherman casts the $net — an instrument of indiscriminate enclosure — into the $sea, gathering everyone within the sweep. The $net does not select; separation happens after the catch (Mat 13:47–48). Fishing is *open, broad, net-based gathering from the nations*.

**Phase 2 — Hunters.** The hunter goes where the $net cannot reach: onto dry land, up $mountains (kingdoms/ruling authorities), into hills, and into the holes of $rocks (clefts within the covenant structure itself). Hunters pursue *specifically*. They track the individual quarry that escaped the broad sweep. There is no hiding place the hunter cannot penetrate — not even the fissures of the $rock.

The $mountain represents kingdom and ruling authority (Rev 17:9, Dan 2:35). The $rock represents the covenant (1 Cor 10:4). The hunter pursues those who hide within *kingdoms* and within *covenant structures* — those who have avoided the open gathering and taken refuge in elevated or protected places. God's hunter finds what the fisherman's net missed.

The context of Jer 16:14–18 makes clear this is both judgment and restoration — God bringing scattered $israel to account AND gathering them home. The hunter, when commissioned by God, serves the covenant purpose even though the method is force rather than invitation.

### Hunting Souls — The Predatory Counterfeit

> *"Woe to the women that sew pillows to all armholes, and make kerchiefs upon the head of every stature to **hunt** souls! Will ye **hunt** the souls of my people, and will ye save the souls alive that come unto you?"* — Ezek 13:18

> *"Wherefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I am against your pillows, wherewith ye there **hunt** the souls to make them fly, and I will tear them from your arms, and will let the souls go, even the souls that ye **hunt** to make them fly."* — Ezek 13:20

False prophetesses "hunt souls" (H5315 *nephesh*) — spiritual predation using deception. The method is false comfort (pillows on armholes) and false covering (kerchiefs on heads), suggesting a counterfeit of covenant protection. The target is not bodies but *souls*. And the verb is the same root (H6679 *tsud*) used for Nimrod's and Esau's hunting.

God directly opposes this hunting: "I am against your pillows... I will let the souls go." The souls caught by false prophecy are released when God tears away the deceptive apparatus. This is the hunter as $snare-setter — operating through concealment and false promise, the soul-level equivalent of $babylon's empire-level capture.

### The Harlot Hunts

> *"For by means of a whorish woman a man is brought to a piece of bread: and the adulteress will **hunt** (H6679) for the precious life."* — Prov 6:26

The $harlot is a hunter. She pursues the soul to capture it — the same verb, the same targeted pursuit, the same ambush method. This connects to Ecc 7:26, where the woman whose heart is "$snares and $nets" (H4685 *matsod* + H2764 *cherem*) represents the entrapment system.

The hunter-harlot-$babylon connection emerges: $babylon is the world system that captures by force and enticement; the harlot represents its seductive face; and hunting is the method by which individual souls are targeted and taken. The $snare is the concealed mechanism; the hunter is the agent who deploys it.

### When Everyone Hunts Their Brother

> *"The good man is perished out of the earth: and there is none upright among men: they all lie in wait for blood; they **hunt** every man his brother with a net (H2764 *cherem*)."* — Mic 7:2

When the covenant order collapses entirely, everyone becomes a hunter. The word for "net" here is *cherem* — the same word that means both $net and ban. Mutual hunting with *cherem*-$nets is the final expression of societal breakdown: predation has become universal. No one leads; everyone pursues. The $shepherd is absent, and the hunter's method fills the void.

This is the anti-covenant: instead of brothers bound together by covenant loyalty, brothers *hunt* each other with instruments of enclosure and destruction.

### God as Hunter

> *"Thou huntest me as a fierce lion: and again thou shewest thyself marvellous upon me."* — Job 10:16

> *"Mine enemies chased me sore, like a bird, without cause."* — Lam 3:52

> *"They hunt our steps, that we cannot go in our streets: our end is near, our days are fulfilled; for our end is come."* — Lam 4:18

God Himself hunts. When Job experiences relentless divine attention, he describes it as being hunted by a lion. This is not predation — it is *unavoidable pursuit* that forces encounter. God's hunting serves revelation, not destruction.

But in Lamentations, the enemies who hunt Jerusalem's inhabitants function as instruments of God's covenant discipline. "They hunt our steps" — the targeted, unavoidable pursuit of those who broke covenant. The hunter finds what is hidden; nothing escapes.

Even evil itself hunts: "Evil shall **hunt** the violent man to overthrow him" (Ps 140:11). The violent man's own violence becomes his pursuer — the hunter hunted by the consequences of his own predation.

## The Hunter and the Shepherd

The two archetypes that most oppose each other in Scripture's leadership imagery are not king and peasant, or priest and layman. They are hunter and $shepherd.

| Hunter | $shepherd |
|---|---|
| Pursues by force | Gathers by voice |
| Captures the quarry | Leads the willing |
| Takes life from the flock | Gives life for the flock |
| Ambushes (H6679 *tsud* = lie alongside) | Goes before (Jn 10:4) |
| Builds empires (Nimrod → $babylon) | Tends covenant communities (David → $jerusalem) |
| Loses the covenant (Esau) | Receives the covenant (Jacob the *tam* man) |

The $shepherd is the covenant caretaker — one who walks with the flock, feeds them, seeks the lost, binds the broken, and gives his life for the $sheep (Jn 10:11). The shepherd's authority IS provision. The hunter's method is pursuit and capture — the quarry has no voice in the matter.

Abel was a $shepherd; his offering was accepted. Nimrod was a hunter; he built $babylon. Jacob was the *tam* man in tents who became $israel's great shepherd-patriarch. Esau was the cunning hunter who lost the birthright. David, the $shepherd-king, was the man after God's heart. The pattern is consistent: the $shepherd inherits the covenant; the hunter loses it or serves as its instrument of discipline.

Yet God *uses* hunters (Jer 16:16) — because some will not come to the $shepherd's voice. When the open invitation fails, when the $fisherman's $net has swept through and still some hide, the hunter is sent. The method is force; the purpose is still gathering. God's hunter serves the covenant even through coercion — but the ideal remains the $shepherd who gathers by leading.

## Patterns

1. **The root means ambush.** H6679 *tsud* = "to lie alongside" — the hunter's defining method is not open pursuit but waiting, positioning, and striking from concealment. This connects hunting directly to the $snare (concealed mechanism of sudden seizure).

2. **Hunting = provision at the cost of covenant.** H6718 *tsayid* means both "hunting" and "provision/venison." Esau hunts *tsayid* for Isaac's blessing but loses the birthright. The hunter gets the food; the *tam* man gets the covenant.

3. **Two named hunters, one trajectory.** Nimrod → builds $babylon. Esau → loses the covenant. The hunter archetype moves *away* from God's order. The $shepherd archetype moves *toward* it.

4. **Fisher then hunter.** Jer 16:16 establishes a sequence: first the broad $net, then the targeted pursuit. $fish are gathered from the $sea (nations); the hunted are tracked from $mountains (kingdoms), hills, and $rock-holes (covenant structures). The hunter is phase two of God's gathering.

5. **Soul-hunting is the spiritual counterfeit.** Ezek 13:18–20, Prov 6:26 — false prophets and the $harlot hunt *souls*, not bodies. The method shifts from physical capture to spiritual deception, but the verb remains the same (H6679).

6. **Mutual hunting = covenant collapse.** Mic 7:2 — when everyone hunts their brother with a $net (*cherem*), the good man has perished. Universal predation is the sign that the $shepherd is absent and the covenant order has dissolved.

7. **The hunter gets hunted.** Ps 140:11 — evil hunts the violent man. Jer 50:24 — God lays a $snare for $babylon, and she is caught unaware. The fowler's own mechanism returns to him. The empire that hunted nations is hunted by God using the same method.

## Connections

- **$fish** — $fish are people in the $sea of nations, leaderless and vulnerable (Hab 1:14). The fisherman gathers them with a broad $net. The hunter finds those the $net could not reach — those who fled to dry land, to $mountains, to the clefts of the $rock (Jer 16:16). Fisher and hunter are two phases of the same divine gathering.

- **$net** — The $net is an instrument of indiscriminate enclosure; the hunter's pursuit is targeted and specific. Yet Mic 7:2 shows them converging: men hunt their brothers *with a cherem-net*. The $net also connects to $babylon's imperial dragnet — Hab 1:15–17 describes Babylon worshipping its own $net, the empire-scale equivalent of Nimrod's hunting.

- **$snare** — The $snare is a concealed mechanism of sudden seizure, and the hunter's root (H6679 *tsud* = to lie alongside) encodes the same ambush method. The $snare is set by the fowler (the Devil — 2 Tim 2:26); the hunter is the human agent who deploys it. Ezek 13:18–20 shows hunting and $snare-craft merging in the work of false prophetesses.

- **$mountain** — $mountains represent kingdoms and ruling authority (Rev 17:9, Dan 2:35). God's hunters pursue quarry "from every mountain" (Jer 16:16) — tracking those who hide within the structures of earthly power. The hunter invades the kingdom to extract the hidden.

- **$rock** — The $rock represents the covenant (1 Cor 10:4). God's hunters pursue even "out of the holes of the rocks" (Jer 16:16) — the fissures and hiding places within the covenant structure itself. Some hide in the clefts of the $rock where the $dove properly dwells (Song 2:14); the hunter finds them there too.

- **$babylon** — $babylon is man's substitute system for God's covenant rule, and its founder was the first hunter. Nimrod built Babel (Gen 10:10); hunting = capturing people to build kingdoms. The hunter's method scales up to become the imperial dragnet. $babylon hunts nations — and is eventually hunted by God (Jer 50:24).

- **$serpent** — The $serpent is the deceiver working through cunning speech. The hunter-$serpent connection appears in the method: both operate through concealment and ambush. Mat 10:16 pairs the $serpent's shrewdness with the $dove's unmixed loyalty — the opposite of the hunter's predatory cunning. Dan (Gen 49:17) is called a $serpent that bites the horse's heel; the tribe associated with $snares and ambush.

- **$shepherd** — The hunter's defining opposite. The $shepherd is the covenant caretaker who gathers by voice, leads from the front, and gives his life for the flock. The hunter captures by force, ambushes from concealment, and takes life from the quarry. God's ideal is the $shepherd; the hunter is employed when the $shepherd's voice goes unheeded.

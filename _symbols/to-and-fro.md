# To-and-Fro

**Restless traversal without covenant rest — the lash-stroke of an agent who has no home.**

> *"And the LORD said unto Satan, Whence comest thou? Then Satan answered the LORD, and said, From going **to and fro** in the $[earth], and from walking up and down in it."* — Job 1:7

---

## The Key Insight

The Hebrew verb behind "to and fro" (H7751 *shuwt*) means "to push forth" — and its derivative noun (H7752 *showt*) means **a whip**. The same root gives both the motion and the instrument.

This is not incidental. The to-and-fro IS a lash-stroke. Back and forth. Repetitive. Sweeping. Never arriving. The word encodes the restless, striking motion of a scourge — the kind of movement that belongs to someone who has no resting place, no covenant territory, no home to return to.

When Satan describes his activity in Job 1:7, he uses this verb. When God's eyes search the earth in 2Chr 16:9, they use the same verb. When Tyre's rowers drive their merchant ship across the $sea in Ezek 27:26, it is this same word. And when desperate seekers run through the streets of Jerusalem looking for one righteous man (Jer 5:1) or through the whole land searching for the word of the LORD (Amos 8:12), it is still H7751.

The verb never describes someone going home. It describes the motion of someone who *has* no home — or who is searching for something that isn't where it should be.

**Symbolizes:** Restless agent-traversal — the back-and-forth motion of one without covenant rest, whether searching, scourging, or roaming

**Opposite:** $rest (H5117 *nuach*, H7673 *shabath*) — settled, ceased, dwelling in the covenant place. The dove finds no *manoach* (rest) and returns to the ark (Gen 8:9). The to-and-fro agent finds no rest and keeps moving.

**Defining verses:** Job 1:7, 2Chr 16:9, Amos 8:12, Dan 12:4, Ezek 27:26

**The surprise:** The same verb describes Satan's patrol AND God's watchful eyes. The motion is identical — the difference is the agent and the purpose. One roams to devour; the other searches to strengthen.

**Connected:** $raven, $earth, $sea, $wind, $locust, $tyre, $babylon

## To-and-Fro Across Scripture

### The Whip Hidden in the Word

Before tracing H7751 through its appearances, consider what the root family reveals. The primitive root *shuwt* means "to push forth." From it comes:

- **H7751** *shuwt* — to go/push to and fro, to traverse, to row
- **H7752** *showt* — a lash, a scourge, a whip

The noun is the key to the verb. A whip moves in exactly one pattern: back and forth, back and forth. It never lands and stays. It sweeps, strikes, returns, sweeps again. The to-and-fro motion is kinetically identical to the lash-stroke — and Scripture draws on this connection explicitly:

> *"My father hath chastised you with **whips** (H7752), but I will chastise you with scorpions."* — 1 Ki 12:11

> *"And the LORD of hosts shall stir up a **scourge** (H7752) for him according to the slaughter of Midian at the rock of Oreb."* — Isa 10:26

> *"When the overflowing **scourge** (H7752) shall pass through, then ye shall be trodden down by it."* — Isa 28:18

The scourge *passes through* — it sweeps across, back and forth. Those who made a "covenant with death" (Isa 28:15) thought they were safe from this overflowing lash. They were not. The scourge's motion is the to-and-fro's motion. The to-and-fro does not arrive. It passes through, again and again.

### Satan's Patrol — The First Agent

> *"And the LORD said unto Satan, Whence comest thou? Then Satan answered the LORD, and said, From going **to and fro** (H7751) in the **$[earth]**, and from walking up and down in it."* — Job 1:7

> *"And the LORD said unto Satan, From whence comest thou? And Satan answered the LORD, and said, From going **to and fro** (H7751) in the **$[earth]**, and from walking up and down in it."* — Job 2:2

Two verbs describe Satan's activity: H7751 (*shuwt* — lashing/traversing) and H1980 (*halak* — walking). He does not dwell. He patrols. He has no covenant territory, no home in the $earth — he roams it, sweeping back and forth like a whip over its surface.

This is the $raven's motion. In Gen 8:7, the raven goes forth "to and fro" (using H3318 *yatsa* + H7725 *shub*, not H7751, but the identical concept — going out and returning without ever coming home). The $raven is the creature of darkness and desolation that thrives where covenant order collapses. It never returns to the ark. It feeds on death in the judgment waters. Satan's to-and-fro patrol and the raven's restless circling are the same motion performed by the same kind of agent: one who is at home in desolation, not in the covenant.

Peter makes the connection explicit: *"Your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour"* (1 Pet 5:8). Walking about. Seeking. Never resting. The to-and-fro of one who belongs nowhere and consumes everywhere.

### God's Eyes — The Same Motion, Opposite Purpose

> *"For the **$[eyes]** of the LORD **run to and fro** (H7751) throughout the whole **$[earth]**, to shew himself strong in the behalf of them whose heart is perfect toward him."* — 2 Chr 16:9

> *"For who hath despised the day of small things? for they shall rejoice, and shall see the plummet in the hand of Zerubbabel with those seven; they are the **$[eyes]** of the LORD, which **run to and fro** (H7751) through the whole **$[earth]**."* — Zech 4:10

The same verb. The same territory. But a completely different agent and intent.

Satan traverses the earth to accuse and devour. God's eyes traverse the earth *to show himself strong* on behalf of the faithful. The angelic patrols of Zech 1:10–11 and 6:7 (using H1980 *halak* — walking to and fro) report back to the LORD after surveying the earth: "all the earth sitteth still, and is at rest" (Zech 1:11). God's agents go out, survey, and *return with a report*. Satan goes out and never reports back — he is *asked* where he has been.

The to-and-fro motion itself is neutral. What distinguishes the agents is whether they have a commission and a home base. God's eyes run to and fro *from* the throne, *for* a purpose, and the seven eyes of Zechariah's vision are identified with the Spirit's active presence in the world. Satan's to-and-fro is purposeless in the sense that it has no covenant home to return to — it is the restless motion of one who patrols what he does not own.

### The End-Times Search — Two Kinds of Running

> *"But thou, O Daniel, shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall **run to and fro** (H7751), and knowledge shall be increased."* — Dan 12:4

> *"Behold, the days come, saith the Lord GOD, that I will send a famine in the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the LORD: And they shall wander from sea to sea, and from the north even to the east, they shall **run to and fro** (H7751) to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it."* — Amos 8:11–12

These two prophecies sit in devastating tension. Daniel sees an end-times explosion of human traversal — running to and fro — accompanied by an increase in knowledge. Amos sees the same frantic motion but for a different object: people running to and fro searching for the word of the LORD and *not finding it*.

Both are H7751. Both describe the same restless motion. But the outcomes are inverted:

- In Daniel, the to-and-fro *produces* something — knowledge increases.
- In Amos, the to-and-fro *produces nothing* — the word of the LORD is absent.

The pattern suggests that human knowledge and divine revelation move in opposite directions at the end. The very era that multiplies information starves the hearer of God's word. The to-and-fro intensifies in both directions — more traversal, more searching — but the covenant word becomes the one thing the restless motion cannot locate.

Amos specifies the geographic scope: "from sea to sea, from the north even to the east." They cover every direction — the same sweeping, whip-like traversal — and come back empty. The famine is not of bread but of *hearing*. The word may still exist; the capacity to receive it does not. This is the ultimate expression of to-and-fro without rest: endless searching that never finds what it needs.

### The Desperate Urban Search

> *"**Run** ye **to and fro** (H7751) through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in the broad places thereof, if ye can find a man, if there be any that executeth judgment, that seeketh the truth; and I will pardon it."* — Jer 5:1

God issues an invitation that sounds like a challenge: traverse the city. Search every street, every broad place. Find *one* person who does justice and seeks truth. If you can find one, God will pardon the whole city. The echo of Abraham's intercession for Sodom is unmistakable (Gen 18:23–32) — but here the search is given to the people themselves, and the implication is that the to-and-fro will come up empty.

> *"**Run to and fro** (H7751) by the hedges"* — Jer 49:3

The daughters of Rabbah in Ammon run to and fro by the hedges — not searching but *fleeing*. Judgment has fallen. The to-and-fro here is the panicked motion of those with nowhere to hide. Same verb, same restlessness, but now it is the scattering motion of the defeated.

Both uses share the quality of purposeful urgency without resolution. The seeker in Jer 5:1 searches and cannot find. The refugees in Jer 49:3 flee and cannot escape. The to-and-fro offers no arrival.

### Tyre's Rowers — Commerce on the Sea

> *"The inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad were thy **mariners** (H7751): thy wise men, O **$[tyre]**, that were in thee, were thy pilots."* — Ezek 27:8

> *"Thy **rowers** (H7751) have brought thee into great **$[waters]**: the east **$[wind]** hath broken thee in the midst of the **$[seas]**."* — Ezek 27:26

The connection between to-and-fro and $tyre — $babylon's commercial face, the trade-network that corrupts worship into traffick — is embedded in the vocabulary. Tyre's rowers are literally those who perform the H7751 motion: pushing oars back and forth, driving the merchant ship across the $sea of chaotic nations. The to-and-fro is the engine of commerce.

And the result? The east $wind — the judgment-wind of God — breaks the ship in the midst of the $sea. Tyre's restless commercial traversal, her endless back-and-forth across the nations for profit, ends in catastrophic shipwreck. The rowers' to-and-fro motion is the very thing that brought the vessel into the deep waters where it could be destroyed.

The pattern recurs in Rev 18, where $babylon's merchants stand afar off weeping as the great commercial system burns — consuming itself with fire from its own midst (Ezek 28:18). The to-and-fro of trade, the restless rowing across nations, was never arrival. It was the back-and-forth of a whip, and in the end, the lash turns on itself.

### Gathering and Numbering — The Neutral Uses

Not every use of H7751 is dark. Two passages use it for legitimate traversal:

> *"And the people went about (H7751), and gathered [the manna]."* — Num 11:8

> *"Go now through (H7751) all the tribes of Israel, from Dan even to Beersheba, and number ye the people."* — 2 Sam 24:2

The people traverse the camp gathering manna. Joab traverses the land numbering Israel. In both cases the to-and-fro serves a purpose — but note that in 2 Sam 24, the census itself is David's sin, and the traversal leads to judgment (the plague of 2 Sam 24:15). Even the "neutral" use carries the shadow of restlessness: nine months and twenty days of going through the land (2 Sam 24:8) for a count that should never have been taken.

### The Larger Vocabulary of Restlessness

While H7751 is the primary Hebrew root, Scripture uses a constellation of terms to describe to-and-fro motion, each adding texture to the pattern:

**The $raven's prototype** — Gen 8:7 uses H3318 *yatsa* (go forth) + H7725 *shub* (return): "going forth and returning, going forth and returning." Different words, identical pattern. The $raven — the creature of darkness and desolation — establishes the to-and-fro as the motion of one who *has no rest in the covenant*.

**The earth reeling** — *"The **$[earth]** shall reel **to and fro** (H5128 *nua*) like a drunkard"* (Isa 24:20). The very ground staggers under judgment. Creation itself becomes restless.

**Sailors staggering** — *"They reel **to and fro** (H2287 *chagag*), and stagger like a drunken man"* (Ps 107:27). Maritime judgment produces the same unsteady, directionless motion.

**The $locust army** — *"They shall run **to and fro** (H8264 *shaqaq*) in the city; they shall run upon the wall"* (Joel 2:9). The $locust — God-commissioned agents of consumption that strip what has been produced — courses through the city with the same restless sweep. Isaiah confirms: *"the running **to and fro** of $[locust]s"* (Isa 33:4). The devouring swarm IS a to-and-fro force.

**Driven by doctrine** — *"Tossed **to and fro** (G2831 *kludonizomai* — surged like waves), and carried about with every **$[wind]** of doctrine"* (Eph 4:14). Paul transposes the pattern into spiritual terms. The $wind — invisible power, spirit — drives the unstable believer back and forth like a $sea wave. Those without doctrinal anchor are subject to the same restless motion as the raven, the scourge, and the frantic seeker.

**Tossing on the bed** — *"I am full of tossings **to and fro** (H5076 *nadud*) unto the dawning of the day"* (Job 7:4). Even in sleep, the suffering man finds no rest. The to-and-fro invades the body itself.

## Patterns

1. **Three agent-types, one motion.** The adversary (Satan, the $raven), the divine watchman (God's eyes, angelic patrols), and the desperate human (Jer 5:1, Amos 8:12) all perform the same to-and-fro. The motion itself is neutral — the agent determines its character.

2. **The noun reveals the verb.** H7752 *showt* (whip/scourge) is the noun-form of H7751 *shuwt* (to go to and fro). The to-and-fro motion IS a lash-stroke. Those without covenant $rest become the very instrument of scourging.

3. **To-and-fro is the opposite of Sabbath.** The motion never arrives, never ceases, never dwells. It is the anti-$rest: *shuwt* undoes *shabath*. God's $rest is the telos of creation (Gen 2:2); the to-and-fro is the condition of those excluded from it.

4. **End-times intensification.** Dan 12:4 and Amos 8:12 describe simultaneous accelerations of the same motion — one producing human knowledge, the other failing to find God's word. The to-and-fro increases as the age concludes.

5. **Commerce as to-and-fro.** Tyre's rowers (Ezek 27:8, 26) perform H7751 on the $sea. The global trade network of $babylon is powered by restless traversal — and the east $wind breaks the ship in the deep waters it was never meant to enter.

6. **The $raven is the to-and-fro creature.** Gen 8:7 is the first to-and-fro agent in Scripture. The raven never returns to the ark. It defines the motion: purposeless patrol, at home in death, with no covenant rest to return to.

7. **The dove is the answer.** Where the $raven goes to and fro and never returns, the dove goes out and *comes back*. The dove finds no rest (H4494 *manoach*) — and so it returns to the ark. The to-and-fro agent accepts restlessness; the dove refuses it.

## Connections

- **$raven** — The prototype to-and-fro creature: goes forth and returns, goes forth and returns over the judgment waters (Gen 8:7), never coming back to the ark. The $raven is the creature of darkness and desolation that thrives where covenant order collapses — and its restless circling is the first picture of to-and-fro in Scripture. Satan's patrol in Job 1:7 repeats the raven's motion exactly.

- **$earth** — The territory traversed. Satan goes to and fro "in the $earth" (Job 1:7); God's eyes run to and fro "throughout the whole $earth" (2 Chr 16:9). The earth is the field of restless motion — the domain that both adversary and divine watchman sweep across.

- **$sea** — The chaotic nations where Tyre's rowers perform H7751 (Ezek 27:8, 26). The $sea represents the turbulent mass of peoples, and the to-and-fro on the sea is $babylon's commercial engine — restless traversal across nations for profit.

- **$wind** — The invisible power that drives to-and-fro motion. The east $wind breaks Tyre's ship (Ezek 27:26). Believers are "tossed to and fro" by every wind of doctrine (Eph 4:14). Wind acts on the to-and-fro agent as either divine judgment ($wind from God) or spiritual instability (false teaching).

- **$tyre** — $babylon's commercial face, the trade-network that corrupts covering into traffick. Tyre's rowers and mariners (Ezek 27:8, 26) are literally H7751 agents — to-and-fro people who drive the merchant system across the $sea. The to-and-fro is the economic engine of $babylon.

- **$babylon** — Man's substitute system for God's covenant rule. Babylon's commercial network is propelled by restless to-and-fro (Ezek 27 → Rev 18). The overflowing scourge (H7752) that passes through (Isa 28:18) is the whip-motion of $babylon's judgment — and $babylon itself is scourged by its own restless energy, fire from its own midst (Ezek 28:18).

- **$locust** — God-commissioned consuming agents that run to and fro in the city (Joel 2:9, Isa 33:4). The $locust swarm is the to-and-fro motion made collective — a sweeping, stripping force that traverses and devours what has been produced. Their running to and fro IS the scouring.

## For Further Study

- **$raven** — The to-and-fro creature. How does the raven's restless scavenging define what it means to be without covenant rest — and what does it mean that God commands ravens to feed Elijah?
- **$rest** — The to-and-fro's opposite. What does Sabbath rest represent, and how does entering God's rest (Heb 4:1–11) answer the restless motion of Satan, the frantic seeker, and the commercial rower?
- **$tyre** — The to-and-fro economy. How does Tyre's merchant system — driven by rowers performing H7751 across the $sea — become the template for Revelation 18's fallen $babylon?
- **$locust** — The to-and-fro swarm. How do God's consuming agents run to and fro through the land, and what does the Rev 9 inversion (locusts that don't eat grass) reveal about end-times judgment?
- **$sea** — The field of restless motion. If the $sea represents chaotic nations, what does it mean that the to-and-fro of Tyre's commerce takes place on its surface — and that in the new creation, there is "no more sea" (Rev 21:1)?

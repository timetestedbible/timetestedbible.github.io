# Knowledge

**Discernment between good and evil — rooted in the fear of God, equated with Torah, and expressed through obedience to His commands.**

> *"My people are **destroyed** for lack of **knowledge**: because thou hast **rejected knowledge**, I will also reject thee… seeing thou hast **forgotten the law** of thy God, I will also forget thy children."* — Hos 4:6

---

## The First Occurrence — A Tree, Not a Textbook

H1847 *da'ath* appears for the first time in Genesis 2:9: "the $[tree] of **knowledge** of good and evil." Knowledge is introduced not as a concept to study but as something that grows on a $[tree] — something you eat, take into yourself, and are changed by. It sits in the garden alongside the tree of $[life]. The two trees define the choice: $[life] or *da'ath* apart from God.

The content of *da'ath* is not information. It is **discernment between good and evil** — the ability to distinguish right from wrong. Gen 3:5: "your eyes shall be opened, and ye shall be as gods, **knowing good and evil**." Gen 3:22: "the man is become as one of us, to **know good and evil**."

**Symbolizes:** The ability to discern good from evil through God's standard

**Opposite:** $fool — "fools despise wisdom and instruction" (Prov 1:7)

**Connected:** $tree, $light, $darkness, $wise, $truth, $the-way

---

## Where Knowledge Begins

> *"The fear of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄 is the **beginning** of knowledge: but $[fool]s despise wisdom and instruction."* — Prov 1:7

*Da'ath* does not start with study. It starts with the **fear of God**. Without fear, you can accumulate information — but it is not knowledge. Proverbs 1:29 makes the equation explicit: "They **hated** knowledge, and did **not choose** the fear of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄." Hating knowledge and not choosing to fear God are the same act, stated in parallel.

Prov 9:10: "The knowledge of the holy is understanding." Not knowledge *about* the Holy One — knowledge *of* Him. The distinction is the difference between reading a biography and knowing the person.

---

## Knowledge Is Torah

The most important single verse for understanding *da'ath* is Hosea 4:6, because it **equates** knowledge with the law of God in a single sentence:

> *"My people are **destroyed** for lack of knowledge: because thou hast **rejected knowledge**, I will also reject thee… seeing thou hast **forgotten the law of thy God**, I will also forget thy children."*

Three steps: rejected knowledge → forgotten the law → destroyed. *Da'ath* and Torah are not merely connected. They are identified as the same thing. To reject knowledge IS to forget the law. To forget the law IS to lack knowledge. The prophet does not treat these as separate problems.

Psalm 119:66 confirms: "Teach me good judgment and **knowledge**: for I have believed thy **commandments**." Knowledge and commandments are a pair — you learn one through the other.

Malachi 2:7: "The priest's lips should keep **knowledge**, and they should seek **the law** at his mouth." The priest's job is to preserve *da'ath*. What does he preserve? Torah. Same thing.

Hosea 4:1 opens the indictment: "There is no *da'ath* of God in the land." What follows in v.2? A list of commandment violations — swearing, lying, killing, stealing, adultery. No knowledge of God = the Ten Commandments broken. The diagnostic is behavioral, not intellectual.

---

## Knowledge vs Information

> *"Ever learning, and never able to come to the knowledge of the $[truth]."* — 2 Tim 3:7

This verse describes the opposite of *da'ath*: people who accumulate facts but never arrive at discernment. They study endlessly. They have opinions. They can quote sources. But they cannot tell good from evil — because they have disconnected learning from the fear of God and the commands that define right and wrong.

Hosea 6:6: "I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the **knowledge of God** more than burnt offerings." Yeshua quotes this twice (Mat 9:13, Mat 12:7). *Da'ath* of God is not ritual performance. It is knowing Him through doing what He said — justice, mercy, walking humbly (Mic 6:8).

Jeremiah 22:16: "He judged the cause of the poor and needy; then it was well: **was not this to know me?** saith 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄." Knowing God = doing justice. Not theology but action.

---

## The Consequence of Lacking Knowledge

Every passage that describes the *absence* of *da'ath* describes catastrophe:

- **Hos 4:6** — "destroyed for lack of knowledge"
- **Isa 5:13** — "My people are gone into captivity, because they have no knowledge"
- **Job 36:12** — "they shall perish by the $[sword], and they shall die without knowledge"

The pattern is uniform: no *da'ath* → exile, destruction, death. And in every case, the lack is not ignorance in the modern sense (they never had access to the information). It is **rejection** — "thou hast rejected knowledge" (Hos 4:6). They had the Torah. They chose to forget it.

---

## The Promise — Knowledge Fills the Earth

> *"The earth shall be full of the knowledge of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄, as the $[water]s cover the $[sea]."* — Isa 11:9

In the kingdom, *da'ath* is not scarce. It fills the earth — as pervasive as water in the ocean. No corner untouched. This is the reversal of Hosea 4:1 ("no knowledge of God in the land"). What was absent will be everywhere. And the agent is the Messiah Himself: "the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, the Spirit of counsel and might, the Spirit of **knowledge** and of the fear of 𐤉𐤄𐤅𐤄" (Isa 11:2).

Isaiah 53:11: "By his **knowledge** shall my righteous servant justify many." The Servant's *da'ath* — His intimate, lived, covenantal knowing of the Father — is the mechanism by which many are justified.

---

## Patterns

1. **First occurrence** = discernment between good and evil, growing on a $[tree] in the garden
2. **Begins with** the fear of God (Prov 1:7) — without fear, information is not knowledge
3. **Equated with** Torah (Hos 4:6, Mal 2:7, Ps 119:66) — knowing the commands IS knowledge
4. **Expressed through** action, not ritual (Jer 22:16, Hos 6:6) — doing justice = knowing God
5. **Absence** = exile and destruction (Isa 5:13, Hos 4:6)
6. **Counterfeit** = "ever learning, never arriving" (2 Tim 3:7) — information without transformation
7. **Fulfillment** = the earth filled with *da'ath* in the kingdom (Isa 11:9)

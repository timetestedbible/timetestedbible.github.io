# Locust

**God-commissioned agents of consumption — a multiplying swarm sent to strip what has been produced.**

> *"That which the **palmerworm** hath left hath the **locust** eaten; and that which the **locust** hath left hath the **cankerworm** eaten; and that which the **cankerworm** hath left hath the **caterpiller** eaten."* — Joel 1:4

---

## The Key Insight

Every Hebrew word for a locust-type creature is named for what it *does*: H697 *arbeh* from H7235 *rabah* — "to multiply." H1501 *gazam* — "to devour." H3218 *yeleq* — "to lick up." H2625 *chasil* — "to strip off." The lexical field itself is the definition: these are agents of consumption at escalating stages.

But the detail that transforms the symbol from mere catastrophe into theological statement comes in Joel 2:25:

> *"And I will restore to you the years that the **locust** hath eaten, the **cankerworm**, and the **caterpiller**, and the **palmerworm**, **my great army which I sent among you**."*

*My* army. *I sent.* God claims the swarm. Not a rogue disaster. Not a foreign invasion that slipped past divine sovereignty. The locusts belong to the One who dispatched them.

This is confirmed in every prophetic context. "If **I command** the locust to devour the land" (2 Chr 7:13). "He spake, and the **locusts** came" (Ps 105:34). "The LORD shall bring a nation against thee… thy fruit shall the **locust** consume" (Deut 28:38, 42). The swarm arrives by divine speech. It strips by divine commission. And when it has finished its work, God promises to restore what was taken — because the consuming was never random. It was measured.

**Symbolizes:** God-commissioned agents of consumption that strip what has been produced — collective instruments of devouring judgment

**Opposite:** $rain / $dew — provision that causes growth, versus agents that strip what has grown

**Defining verses:** Joel 1:4, Joel 2:25, Rev 9:3–10, Exod 10:4–15, 2 Chr 7:13

**The surprise:** In Revelation 9, the locust-swarm from the $pit-abyss is *forbidden* to eat $grass or $tree — the very things real locusts devour. Instead, these locusts torment only those without God's $seal. The natural symbol is inverted: the consuming agents bypass vegetation (people under covenant protection) and consume the wellbeing of the unprotected.

**Connected:** $grass, $tree, $fire, $smoke, $pit-abyss, $wind, $darkness, $scorpion, $lion, $crown, $seal

## Locust Across Scripture

### Four Stages, One Stripping

> *"That which the **palmerworm** hath left hath the **locust** eaten; and that which the **locust** hath left hath the **cankerworm** eaten; and that which the **cankerworm** hath left hath the **caterpiller** eaten."* — Joel 1:4

Joel's four words — H1501 *gazam* (devourer), H697 *arbeh* (multiplier), H3218 *yeleq* (licker), H2625 *chasil* (stripper) — are not random synonyms. Each names a stage of consumption. What the first leaves, the second takes. What the second spares, the third licks clean. What the third overlooks, the fourth strips bare.

Whether these represent four successive waves or four developmental stages of the same insect is debated. But the symbolic effect is unmistakable: the stripping is *total*. Nothing produced survives the full sequence. Every harvest, every orchard, every vine — consumed stage by stage until nothing remains:

> *"He hath laid my vine waste, and barked my fig $[tree]: he hath made it clean bare, and cast it away; the branches thereof are made white."* — Joel 1:7

$tree — nations and people groups — are stripped to white wood. Even the bark is gone. The consuming agents leave no outer covering, no fruit, no life.

### "My Great Army Which I Sent"

The critical theological claim appears when Joel 2 reveals who is behind the swarm:

> *"And I will restore to you the years that the **locust** hath eaten, the **cankerworm**, and the **caterpiller**, and the **palmerworm**, **my great army which I sent among you**."* — Joel 2:25

This is not a consolation prize. It is a confession of ownership. God calls the swarm *my great army*. He says *I sent*. The locust is not a foreign threat that overwhelmed divine protection — it is the instrument *of* divine action.

This is consistent throughout Scripture:

- **2 Chr 7:13** — "If I shut up heaven that there be no rain, or if **I command the locusts** to devour the land"
- **Ps 105:34** — "**He spake**, and the **locusts** came, and caterpillers, and that without number"
- **Ps 78:46** — "He **gave** also their increase unto the caterpiller, and their labour unto the **locust**"
- **Amos 4:9** — "the **palmerworm** devoured them: yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the LORD"
- **Amos 7:1** — "the Lord GOD shewed me; and, behold, **he formed grasshoppers** in the beginning of the shooting up"

In Amos 7, God is not merely permitting the swarm. He is *forming* it. The locusts are crafted instruments. And in Amos 4, the purpose is made explicit: the stripping is meant to provoke return. "Yet have ye not returned unto me" — repeated five times. The consuming agents serve the goal of repentance.

### The Eighth Plague — Stripping What the Hail Left

> *"Else, if thou refuse to let my people go, behold, to morrow will I bring the **locusts** into thy coast: And they shall cover the face of the earth, that one cannot be able to see the earth: and they shall eat the residue of that which is escaped, which remaineth unto you from the $[hail]"* — Exod 10:4–5

The plague sequence matters. The seventh plague — hail — shattered the crops and trees. The eighth — locusts — consumed *what the hail left*. Two stages of judgment on the same production: first broken, then stripped. The pattern mirrors Joel 1:4's escalating consumption.

> *"And the **locusts** went up over all the land of Egypt, and rested in all the coasts of Egypt: very grievous were they; before them there were no such **locusts** as they, neither after them shall be such. For they covered the face of the whole earth, so that the land was darkened; and they did eat every herb of the land, and all the fruit of the $[tree]s which the hail had left: and there remained not any green thing in the $[tree]s, or in the herbs of the field, through all the land of Egypt."* — Exod 10:14–15

The land was *darkened* — $darkness, concealed disobedience made visible through judgment. Not one green thing remained. The east $wind brought them; the west $wind swept them into the Red Sea (Exod 10:13, 19). The same agents that stripped Egypt were themselves swept away when their commission ended.

### The Day of the LORD — Locust as Army

Joel 2 takes the locust imagery and escalates it into something beyond any natural swarm:

> *"A day of $[darkness] and of gloominess, a day of clouds and of thick $[darkness], as the morning spread upon the mountains: a great people and a strong; there hath not been ever the like, neither shall be any more after it…"* — Joel 2:2

> *"The appearance of them is as the appearance of $[horse]s; and as horsemen, so shall they run. Like the noise of chariots on the tops of mountains shall they leap…"* — Joel 2:4–5

> *"…a strong people set in battle array. Before them the people shall be much pained… They shall run like mighty men; they shall climb the wall like men of war…"* — Joel 2:5–7

> *"The earth shall quake before them; the heavens shall tremble: the $[sun] and the $[moon] shall be dark, and the stars shall withdraw their shining: And the LORD shall utter his voice before his army: for his camp is very great: for he is strong that executeth his word."* — Joel 2:10–11

Notice: the LORD utters His voice *before* this army. It is *His* camp. These are not merely insects or merely soldiers — they are the judgment of the Day of the LORD described in locust terms: consuming, collective, irresistible, darkening the sky, shaking the earth.

The composite description — horses, chariots, wall-climbers, $fire before and behind — reappears almost exactly in Revelation 9, suggesting Joel 2 and Revelation 9 describe the same reality from different vantage points.

### The Inversion — Revelation 9

> *"And there came out of the $[smoke] **locusts** upon the earth: and unto them was given power, as the $[scorpion]s of the earth have power. And it was commanded them that they should **not hurt the $[grass] of the earth, neither any green thing, neither any $[tree]**; but only those men which have not the $[seal] of God in their foreheads."* — Rev 9:3–4

Here the symbol turns inside out. Natural locusts consume vegetation — $grass (mortal people) and $tree (nations). These locusts are *forbidden* to touch them. Instead, they torment only those without God's $seal — authority's binding claim that marks and protects.

The inversion is theologically precise. Under the old pattern, God sent consuming agents against His covenant people's *production* — their harvests, their vines, their fig trees — to provoke repentance. Under this apocalyptic pattern, the consuming agents bypass the protected and consume the *wellbeing* of those who refused the seal. The stripping is redirected from agriculture to persons.

The origin is also significant: these locusts emerge from $smoke rising out of the opened $pit-abyss — enforced containment now released. The chain reads: $pit-abyss opened → $smoke ascends → $darkness covers sun and air → locusts emerge from $smoke. They come from containment, through the ascending evidence of $fire's application, through obscured order, into the world.

### The Composite Creature

> *"And the shapes of the **locusts** were like unto $[horse]s prepared unto battle; and on their heads were as it were $[crown]s like gold, and their faces were as the faces of men, And they had hair as the hair of women, and their teeth were as the teeth of $[lion]s, And they had breastplates, as it were breastplates of iron, and the sound of their $[wings] was as the sound of chariots of many $[horse]s running to battle. And they had tails like unto $[scorpion]s, and there were stings in their tails: and their power was to hurt men five months."* — Rev 9:7–10

Every component carries weight:

- **$horse** — Joel 2:4 already described locusts with the appearance of horses. War-readiness.
- **$crown "like gold"** — not real crowns (G5613 *hos* — "as it were"), not real gold (G3664 *homoios* — "like"). Double qualifier. Counterfeit authority. Compare Prov 30:27: natural locusts "have no king." These have a king (Rev 9:11) — and their crowns are imitations.
- **Faces of men** — human intelligence behind the consuming.
- **Hair of women** — perhaps the Nazirite connection (long hair = consecration), or perhaps deception/enticement. The image resists easy resolution.
- **Teeth of $lion** — devouring power, sovereign dominion exercised. Joel 1:6 already called the swarm "a nation… whose teeth are the teeth of a lion."
- **Breastplates of iron** — armored, impervious to counterattack. The consumed cannot fight back.
- **Wings as chariots** — Joel 2:5 again: "like the noise of chariots." The sound precedes them.
- **$scorpion tails with stings** — the trailing consequence. Scorpion power is non-lethal, incapacitating torment (Rev 9:5 explicitly: "not that they should kill them, but that they should be tormented"). The sting comes from behind — from what follows the initial encounter.

> *"And they had a king over them, which is the angel of the bottomless $[pit-abyss], whose name in the Hebrew tongue is Abaddon, but in the Greek tongue hath his name Apollyon."* — Rev 9:11

"Abaddon" (H3 *Abaddown*) = "destruction." "Apollyon" (G623) = "destroyer." The king of the consuming agents is Destruction personified. Note: this is distinct from Satan, who is *cast into* the abyss (Rev 20:1–3), while this angel *rules* it.

### The No-King Paradox

> *"The **locusts** have no king, yet go they forth all of them by bands"* — Prov 30:27

Natural locusts self-organize without a visible ruler. They move in coordinated devastation with no apparent command structure. This is one of four "little things exceedingly wise" in Proverbs 30:24–28 — creatures whose collective behavior transcends their individual smallness.

But in Revelation 9:11, the apocalyptic locusts *do* have a king: Abaddon. The natural order (no king, self-organizing) is superseded by the supernatural reality (a king named Destruction). What was instinctive becomes directed. What was natural becomes orchestrated from the $pit-abyss.

The tension is itself the insight: in the ordinary world, God's consuming judgments appear leaderless — famines, plagues, economic collapses — moving through natural mechanisms. In the apocalyptic unveiling, the curtain is pulled back: there is a directing agent, and his name is Destroyer.

### The Covenant Curse Sequence

The locust appears in the formal covenant curses of Deuteronomy:

> *"Thou shalt carry much seed out into the field, and shalt gather but little in; for the **locust** shall consume it."* — Deut 28:38

> *"All thy $[tree]s and fruit of thy land shall the **locust** consume."* — Deut 28:42

The pattern is specific: you produce, and the consuming agent strips what you produced. Seed goes out; harvest does not come back. The work is not prevented — it is *devoured after the fact*. This is the locust's signature: it does not prevent planting. It consumes the fruit.

Solomon understood this when dedicating the temple:

> *"If there be in the land famine, if there be pestilence, blasting, mildew, **locust**, or if there be caterpiller; if their enemy besiege them in the land of their cities; whatsoever plague, whatsoever sickness there be…"* — 1 Kgs 8:37

And God's response confirms the commission:

> *"If I shut up heaven that there be no $[rain], or if **I command the locusts** to devour the land, or if I send pestilence among my people; If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray…"* — 2 Chr 7:13–14

The locust is listed alongside drought and pestilence as a divine instrument. All three serve the same purpose: stripping the fruit of the land to provoke humbling, prayer, and return.

### Merchants as Locusts — Nahum's Equation

> *"Thou hast multiplied thy merchants above the stars of heaven: the **cankerworm** spoileth, and flieth away. Thy crowned are as the **locusts**, and thy captains as the great **grasshoppers**, which camp in the hedges in the cold day, but when the sun ariseth they flee away, and their place is not known where they are."* — Nah 3:16–17

Nahum names Nineveh's merchants as cankerworms and its rulers as locusts. The identification is explicit and devastating. They consume, multiply, camp temporarily — and when conditions change (when the sun rises, when $darkness is exposed), they *flee*. Their place is unknown. They were never committed to the city they consumed.

This passage reveals a property of the locust often overlooked: **transience**. The swarm arrives, strips, and departs. It has no stake in what it consumed. The merchants who profited from Nineveh vanish when Nineveh falls. The consuming agents have no permanent home in what they devour.

### The Locust as Food — What the Prophet Ate

> *"And the same John had his raiment of camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins; and his meat was **locusts** and wild honey."* — Matt 3:4

John the Baptist fed on locusts in the wilderness. This is no accident of diet. The Levitical law declares four types of locust *clean* — fit for eating:

> *"Even these of them ye may eat; the **locust** after his kind, and the **bald locust** after his kind, and the **beetle** after his kind, and the **grasshopper** after his kind."* — Lev 11:22

God's consuming agents are clean food. They can be internalized. And John — the prophet whose entire message *was* consuming judgment ("the axe is laid unto the root of the $[tree]s" — Matt 3:10) — literally lived on them. The wilderness prophet was sustained by the very instruments of stripping. His body was built from them. His voice *was* the locust: "Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand" (Matt 3:2).

### Small Before God

Not every appearance of the locust emphasizes judgment. Several use the grasshopper to express human insignificance before the divine:

> *"It is he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as **grasshoppers**"* — Isa 40:22

> *"And there we saw the giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants: and we were in our own sight as **grasshoppers**, and so we were in their sight."* — Num 13:33

> *"I am tossed up and down as the **locust**."* — Ps 109:23

The locust is small, wind-driven, transient. Before God, humanity is grasshopper-scale. Before the giants, the spies felt grasshopper-small. In affliction, the psalmist is tossed like a locust in the wind. These uses capture a different facet of the same creature: not the swarm's collective power, but the individual insect's *vulnerability*. Tiny, windblown, displaced.

Even this connects to the core meaning. The consuming agents are themselves consumed by the wind. The swarm that strips the earth is itself swept into the sea (Exod 10:19). The instruments of judgment are not exempt from being judged.

## Patterns

1. **God commissions them.** Joel 2:25 ("my army which I sent"), 2 Chr 7:13 ("I command"), Ps 105:34 ("He spake"), Amos 7:1 ("he formed"). In every prophetic context, the locust swarm is explicitly God's instrument, not a rogue force.

2. **They consume what was produced, not what is being planted.** Deut 28:38 — seed goes out, harvest is consumed. The locust strips *fruit*, not *roots*. Production continues; the benefit is devoured.

3. **They arrive in stages.** Joel 1:4 — four named stages of escalating consumption: devour, multiply, lick, strip. What one leaves, the next takes. The judgment is progressive and total.

4. **They are collective, not individual.** No individual locust is ever named or singled out. The symbol is always *swarm* — bands, armies, clouds. The consuming force is corporate, operating as one body with many members.

5. **They are transient.** Nah 3:17 — camp temporarily, flee when exposed. Exod 10:19 — swept away by wind when the commission ends. The consuming agent does not remain in what it consumed.

6. **The Revelation 9 inversion.** Natural locusts eat $grass (mortal people) and $tree (nations). The apocalyptic locusts are forbidden to touch these — they torment only the unsealed. God's consuming agents are *redirected* from covenant-people's production to the wicked's wellbeing.

7. **Joel 2 = Revelation 9.** Horses (Joel 2:4 / Rev 9:7), chariots (Joel 2:5 / Rev 9:9), lion's teeth (Joel 1:6 / Rev 9:8), $fire before and behind (Joel 2:3 / Rev 9:17), $darkness of sun (Joel 2:10 / Rev 9:2), God's voice before His army (Joel 2:11). The parallels suggest the same event described prophetically and apocalyptically.

8. **The locust is clean food.** Lev 11:22. God's judgments can be internalized, processed, lived on. John the Baptist built his body and his ministry on them.

## Connections

- **$grass** — Mortal people. Real locusts devour grass; the Rev 9 locusts are forbidden to touch it. The inversion marks the distinction between covenant judgment (consuming production to provoke repentance) and apocalyptic judgment (bypassing the protected to torment the exposed).

- **$tree** — Nations / people groups. Joel 1:7, 12 — the locust strips the vine and fig tree bare, lays waste every tree of the field. Deut 28:42 — fruit and trees consumed. Rev 9:4 — trees explicitly protected from the abyss-locusts. What the covenant curse stripped from Israel's national life, the apocalyptic swarm leaves untouched.

- **$fire** — God's holy standard in active contact. Joel 1:19–20 — fire has devoured the pastures alongside the locust. Joel 2:3 — "a fire devoureth before them, and behind them a flame burneth." Fire and locust work the same field: fire consumes what violates the standard; the locust strips what was produced. Both are God's.

- **$smoke** — The ascending evidence of fire's application. Rev 9:2–3 — locusts emerge *from* smoke. The smoke of the abyss is the visible byproduct of fire-judgment; the locust swarm is what the smoke carries into the world. Chain: $pit-abyss opened → $fire produces $smoke → $smoke darkens → locusts emerge from $smoke.

- **$pit-abyss** — Enforced containment that removes influence. Rev 9:1–2 — the abyss is opened and the locusts pour out. They were *contained*; now they are released. Their king rules the abyss (Rev 9:11). The consuming agents come from the place of restrained destruction.

- **$wind** — Spirit / invisible power. Exod 10:13 — east wind brings the locusts; west wind removes them. The locusts arrive and depart by invisible force. Ps 109:23 — "tossed up and down as the locust" — windblown, driven. Even the consuming agents are themselves at the mercy of the $wind.

- **$darkness** — Concealed disobedience. Exod 10:15 — the locust-covered land "was darkened." Joel 2:2 — the locust-army is "a day of darkness and of gloominess." Rev 9:2 — smoke from the abyss darkens sun and air before the locusts emerge. The consuming agents arrive in $darkness and through $darkness — judgment and concealment are intertwined.

- **$scorpion** — Concealed agent of piercing torment that incapacitates without killing. Rev 9:3, 5, 10 — the locusts receive scorpion-power in their tails. The consuming swarm acquires a trailing sting. The locust's function (consume) merges with the scorpion's function (torment without killing). The result: agents that strip and leave lingering, incapacitating pain.

- **$lion** — Sovereign dominion, the power to devour. Joel 1:6 — "a nation is come up upon my land, strong, and without number, whose teeth are the teeth of a **lion**." Rev 9:8 — locust-creatures with lion teeth. The swarm carries the devouring power of sovereign authority — whether rightful or predatory.

- **$crown** — Visible marker of authority-source. Rev 9:7 — "on their heads were as it were crowns like gold" — double qualifier (*as it were*, *like*). Not actual crowns, not actual gold. Counterfeit authority. Compare natural locusts who "have no king" (Prov 30:27). The abyss-locusts *appear* crowned but their authority is imitation.

- **$seal** — Authority's binding claim. Rev 9:4 — the locusts cannot touch those with God's seal. The seal is the boundary the consuming agents cannot cross. What is claimed by divine authority is exempt from the swarm's commission.

## For Further Study

- **$fire** — The locust and fire work the same terrain in Joel 1–2. Fire is God's holy standard consuming what violates covenant; the locust strips what was produced under covenant. How do these two instruments divide the labor of judgment?

- **$pit-abyss** — The origin of the Revelation 9 locusts. What does it mean that consuming agents were *contained* in enforced holding — and what does their release signal?

- **$scorpion** — The locust acquires scorpion-power in Revelation 9. The scorpion stings without killing. How does the merger of consumption (locust) and incapacitating torment (scorpion) reshape the nature of this judgment?

- **$seal** — The one barrier the locust-swarm cannot cross. What does God's seal protect, and what does it mean that authority's binding claim is the only defense against commissioned consumption?

- **$grass** — Mortal people. The locust's natural food becomes the one thing the apocalyptic locust cannot touch. How does this inversion illuminate the shift from old covenant judgment to new covenant judgment?

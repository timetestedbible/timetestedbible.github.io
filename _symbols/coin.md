# Coin

**The stamped image — a person as the owner's minted image-bearer, and the price set on a soul.**

> *"Whose is this **image** and **superscription**? … Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's."* — Matt 22:20-21

A coin is the $seal made money: metal dug from a field, refined in fire, struck with an image and a name. From that moment it belongs to whoever's face and superscription it bears. Jesus' tribute-penny question answers itself twice: the coin bears Caesar's image — render it to Caesar; *you* bear God's image (Gen 1:27) — render yourself to God (Rom 12:1).

**Symbolizes:** a person as God's minted image-bearer; the ransom-price of a soul
**Counterfeit:** the mark of the beast — Babylon's census-coin (name, number, buy-and-sell) where God's census takes the Name, the count, and the atonement money
**Connected:** $seal, $name, $fish, $widow, $field, $lamp, $remnant

## Scripture states it

- **The lost coin is the lost sinner, decoded in-text** — the woman lights a lamp (the commandment, Prov 6:23), sweeps, seeks, and *"there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner that repenteth"* (Luke 15:8-10).
- **The law prices persons in silver** — *"the persons shall be for the LORD by thy estimation"* (Lev 27:2-8), and every census exacts *"a ransom for his soul... The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less"* (Ex 30:12-16). The soul-price is flat, and the money is *"a memorial"* — the frontlets' own word.
- **The sanctuary stood on the ransom** — the census silver was cast into the sockets of the tabernacle, *"a talent for a socket"* (Ex 38:25-27). God's house rests on the melted ransom-coins of His counted people.
- **The unransomed census brings the plague** — Satan provoked David's numbering (1 Chr 21:1), seventy thousand died, and the plague stopped where a price was paid — the threshingfloor that became the temple mount (2 Sam 24:24; 2 Chr 3:1).
- **The fish pays it from the mouth** — the temple didrachma *is* the Ex 30 ransom; the first fish up carries a stater (exactly two half-shekels, *"for me and thee"*) in its **mouth** — where the seal-texts put the word (Ex 13:9; Deut 30:14; Rom 10:9). A man wears the seal on his forehead; a fish carries it in its mouth — on a fish they are the same place. The Hebrew Matthew gives the coin *be'ad* ("on behalf of") — the atonement's own preposition (Lev 16:6).
- **The widow's two mites** — the covenant-widow casts in *"all her living"* (holon ton bion, her whole life): the coin-self rendered entire, weighed above all abundance (Mark 12:41-44).
- **Man's mint priced the Shepherd** — thirty pieces (Zech 11:12-13), the law's damages for a gored slave (Ex 21:32); the treasury refused the coin and it bought the potter's field for strangers (Matt 27:6-7). The true price: *"not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold... but with the precious blood"* (1 Pet 1:18-19); *"ye are bought with a price"* (1 Cor 6:20).

## The two censuses

Revelation holds two enrollments. God counts and seals: *"I heard the **number** of them which were sealed"* (Rev 7:4). The beast counts and marks: name for the Name, *"the number of his name"* for the count, buy-and-sell for the atonement money (Rev 13:16-18) — with both registers facing each other in the same chapter (the Lamb's book of life, 13:8; cf. Ps 87:6). The plague-clause inverts with the rest: the ransomed take no plague when numbered (Ex 30:12; Rev 9:4); the first vial falls precisely on the marked (Rev 16:2). And 666 stands in the Old Testament at the counterfeit's two poles: Solomon's annual gold (1 Kgs 10:14) and a census entry (Ezra 2:13) — money and count.

Every man ends in one census or the other: counted and ransomed, or counted and marked.

# Ancient of Days

**The starting point from which the days advance. The Aramaic root behind "Ancient" means "to advance, to proceed forward" — not merely "the old one." He is the Beginning of Days: the personified calendar origin, seated on His $throne at Nisan 1.**

> *"I watched until the **thrones were set in place**, and the **Starting of Days** took His seat; His clothing was white as snow, and the hair of His head like pure wool; His **throne** was like a fiery flame."* — Dan 7:9

---

## The Root: To Advance, To Go First

The KJV title "Ancient of Days" translates H6268 **עַתִּיק** (*attiq*, Aramaic), from the root H6275 **עָתַק** (*athaq*) — a word that means **"to remove, to advance, to proceed forward to the next place."**

Strong's defines H6275 as: *"to remove (intransitive or transitive); figuratively, to grow old; specifically, to transcribe."*

The root appears 20 times across the Old Testament. Its primary sense is **motion forward** — advancing to the next position. "To grow old" is a figurative extension: one who has *advanced* through many days. But the concrete, physical meaning is first.

---

## Every Occurrence of the Root

The full range of H6275 and its derivatives reveals a consistent picture:

### H6275 — The Verb: "to advance, remove, proceed"

**Genesis 12:8** — *"And he **removed** from thence unto a mountain."* Abraham *athaq* — he **advanced forward** to his next camp. This is the word's first biblical use, and it sets the tone: forward motion, proceeding to the next place.

**Genesis 26:22** — *"And he **removed** from thence."* Isaac *athaq* — he **moved on** to the next location. Same word, same idea: departure to a new position.

**Job 9:5** — *"Which **removeth** the mountains, and they know not."* God is the one who **displaces** mountains — the ultimate mover. Not aging, but power to reposition.

**Job 14:18** — *"And the rock is **removed** out of his place."* Even solid rock is **advanced** from its position.

**Job 18:4** — *"Shall the earth be **forsaken** for thee? and shall the rock be **removed** out of his place?"* The same sense: shall immovable things be **displaced** because of you?

**Job 21:7** — *"Wherefore do the wicked live, become **old**, yea, are mighty in power?"* Here the figurative sense appears: they have **advanced** through many days — grown old. But the root image is still "proceeding forward through time."

**Job 32:15** — *"They were amazed, they answered no more: they **left off** speaking."* They **moved on** — ceased, departed from their position.

**Psalms 6:7** — *"Mine eye is consumed because of grief; it **waxeth old** because of all mine enemies."* The eye has **advanced** (deteriorated) — again the figurative "moved forward through time."

**Proverbs 25:1** — *"These are also proverbs of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah king of Judah **copied out**."* They **transcribed** — literally "removed [the text] to a new place." The same root, applied to transferring words from one scroll to another.

### H6266 — "durable, splendid" (from the root)

**Isaiah 23:18** — *"for **durable** clothing"* — that which **endures**, having advanced through time without decay.

**Proverbs 8:18** — *"Riches and honour are with me; yea, **durable** riches and righteousness."* Wisdom's wealth is that which has **advanced** through all time — enduring, not perishable.

### H6267 — "ancient, removed, weaned"

**1 Chronicles 4:22** — *"and these are **ancient** things"* — things that have **advanced** far from the present — removed in time.

**Isaiah 28:9** — *"them that are **weaned** from the milk"* — those **removed** from the breast. Same root: physically moved away from a prior position.

### H6268 — The Aramaic Form (Daniel only)

**Daniel 7:9, 13, 22** — The three occurrences of *attiq yomin* — the "Ancient of Days." The Aramaic cognate of H6267, used exclusively as a title in this single vision.

### H6277 — "arrogant, impudent" (from the root)

**1 Samuel 2:3** — *"Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not **arrogancy** come out of your mouth."* The one who **pushes forward** presumptuously — advancing beyond his proper boundary.

**Psalms 31:18, 75:5, 94:4** — In each case, *athaq* describes **bold, presumptuous speech** — words that advance beyond their station. The arrogant one oversteps, pushes past limits.

## The Pattern

Across all 20 occurrences, the root carries one consistent image: **forward motion from one position to the next.**

- **Physical**: Abraham advances to his next camp. Rock is removed from its place. Text is copied (moved) to a new scroll.
- **Temporal**: one who has advanced through days = old. Things advanced far from the present = ancient.
- **Moral**: one who advances presumptuously beyond his place = arrogant.

"Ancient" is a **derived sense** — the one who has traveled far forward through time. The primary sense is the **starting point, the one who goes first, the origin from which the advancing begins.**

---

## Not Just "Old" — The Starting Point

When applied to God as a title, "the one who has advanced through many days" and "the starting point from which days advance" are two sides of the same coin. But the calendar reading of Daniel 7 leans into the second sense — and the context supports it.

In Daniel 7, this figure:

- **Takes His seat** when the thrones (covered moons) are set in place (7:9)
- **Opens the books** and begins judgment (7:10)
- **Receives the Son of Man** who comes to the appointed time (7:13)
- **Gives the kingdom** to the saints when "the **time** came" (7:22)

Everything is anchored to **time**: thrones set up, books opened, time comes, kingdom transferred. The title itself — the one from whom the days advance — names the calendar's origin point. The physical tabernacle (tent of the appointed time) was erected on **Nisan 1** (Exodus 40:2) — the first day of the first month. The Starting of Days takes His seat at the same moment the heavenly tent is established.

English personifies time boundaries the same way:

- "**New Year's Day is almost here.**" The boundary **comes** like a person.
- "**When New Year's Day comes, everything changes.**" The day **comes** and **changes** things.
- "**Judgment Day is coming.**" The day **comes** as an approaching agent.

Daniel speaks of the **Starting of Days** as the One who sits when the thrones are set in place — a calendrical origin point spoken of as a character.

## Symbolizes

**The origin and authority of God's calendar** — the starting point from which all days, months, and appointed times proceed. Not merely the oldest being, but the **first mover of time**: the one who sets the thrones in place, opens the books, and restores the times that the adversary corrupted.

**Opposite:** The adversary of Daniel 7:25, who *"thinks to change **times** and laws"* — attempting to displace the Starting of Days from His seat and substitute a counterfeit calendar.

**Connected:** $throne, $full-moon, $new-moon, $north

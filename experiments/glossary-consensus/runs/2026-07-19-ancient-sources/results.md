# Ancient-sources audit — all 40 badged entries (2026-07-19)

Trigger: the author's Hilary of Poitiers find (ship = type of the Church)
exposed that the blind-grading pipeline measured only the MODERN consensus.
Five researchers swept all 25 DIVERGENT + 15 NOVEL entries against pre-1500
interpretation (fathers, rabbinic/targumic corpus, Philo, Qumran, medieval
compilations) under a strict no-fabrication brief; many claims were verified
in-run against Sefaria/New Advent/CCEL (marked V below; unmarked = stated
from model knowledge at the confidence given, to be primary-verified before
book use). The glossary preamble was re-scoped the same night: badges
measure the broad modern consensus, not a census of every interpreter.

## TIER 1 — Ancients AGREE with the book against the modern reading

The gold class: the book's "divergent" reading is the older one.

- **Moon (DIVERGENT)** — Origen (Hom. Gen. 1.5-7), Ambrose (Hexaemeron
  IV.8.32: the moon "a figure of the Church... shines not with her own
  light but with the light of Christ"), Augustine (En. Ps. 10.3): the moon
  = the Church reflecting the Sun's light. HIGH ×3. Also Sanhedrin 42a
  (Israel "destined to be renewed like her"), Gen. Rabbah 84:11 (moon = the
  mother/wife of the relational pair). NEEDS primary verification pass.
- **Oil, lamp oil (DIVERGENT)** — Chrysostom (Hom. Matt. 78.1: oil = mercy,
  alms, succor), Hilary (via Catena Aurea Matt 25: "the oil is the fruit of
  good works," vessels the bodies storing conscience's treasure), Gregory
  the Great (Hom. Ev. 12), Augustine (Serm. 93). NO pre-1500 source found
  reading the virgins' oil as the Holy Spirit. The book's stored-works
  ruling is the patristic mainstream. NEEDS primary verification pass
  (batch was knowledge-heavy).
- **Coin (NOVEL)** — Tertullian (De idol. 15: "the image of Caesar on the
  coin to Caesar, the image of God in man to God; money to Caesar, yourself
  to God"), Gregory the Great (Hom. Ev. 34, the lost drachma bearing the
  image), Gregory of Nyssa (De virg. 12), Augustine. V (Tertullian,
  Gregory). Broad ancient consensus for image-stamped-person.
- **Egypt (DIVERGENT)** — Victorinus of Pettau (c. 260, the EARLIEST
  surviving Apocalypse commentary), on Rev 11:8: the great city spiritually
  called Egypt IS Jerusalem. V (quote). Andrew of Caesarea concurs
  (MEDIUM). The modern world-city reading descends from Tyconius/Bede.
- **Great Fish (DIVERGENT)** — Targum Jonathan Isa 27:1 (the sea-monsters
  decoded as KINGS — Pharaoh, Sennacherib, "the king strong as the dragon
  in the sea"), Exodus Rabbah 9:4 (the rod became a tannin "because Pharaoh
  was likened to a serpent," citing Ezek 29:3). Both V. Hippolytus/Jerome:
  sea-beasts = kingdoms (Dan 7 self-decode).
- **Sabbath (DIVERGENT)** — LXX Lev 23:15 renders shabbatot "hepta
  hebdomadas" (weeks); Menachot 65a-66a argues the identification outright;
  Didache 8.1 "second and fifth of the sabbath"; Josephus Ant. 3.250-252;
  Barnabas 15 scales the unit. V (multiple). Joins the same-day blind-grade
  (3/3 DIVERGENT vs moderns) — ancients on the book's side.
- **The Way (DIVERGENT)** — Didache 1-6 (the Two Ways: the way of life
  defined entirely as commanded conduct), Barnabas 18-20 (independent Two
  Ways witness), Bava Metzia 30b (Exod 18:20 "the way" parsed as covenant
  deeds). All V or HIGH.
- **Truth (DIVERGENT)** — Berakhot 5b: "'Truth' — this is Torah" (Prov
  23:23). V. (Chrysostom maps oppositely: type by Moses, Truth by Christ.)
- **West (NOVEL)** — Sifre Devarim 357 (adopted by Rashi on Deut 34:2):
  "do not read the last SEA (yam ha-acharon) but the last DAY (yom
  ha-acharon) — He showed him the world from creation until the dead
  live" — the exact acharon/hinder word-family move the entry makes. V
  (both). MAJOR: tannaitic precedent for a NOVEL entry's method and sense.
- **Sign (DIVERGENT)** — Midrash Tanchuma (Lech Lecha 9) via Ramban on Gen
  12:6: ma'aseh avot siman le-vanim — the fathers' deeds enacted in advance
  as signs whose fulfillment comes on the sons. Augustine's sign-definition
  (De Doctr. 2.1.1) kindred. HIGH.
- **Righteousness (DIVERGENT)** — Chrysostom (Hom. Matt. 15: righteousness
  = the whole of virtue / lived right-doing), LXX Deut 6:25 (tsedaqah →
  eleemosyne, the commanded deed), Bava Batra 9a-10a. The imputed-standing
  reading the book diverges from is post-1500. V (several).
- **Gospel (DIVERGENT, linkage)** — Justin (1 Apol. 39), Irenaeus (AH
  4.34.4), Tertullian (Adv. Jud. 3), Eusebius (Dem. Ev. 1.4) all weld
  gospel to Isa 2:3-4 "the law goes forth from Zion" — the structural
  linkage the book asserts (the ancients read a new law already gone forth;
  the book reads Torah in the coming kingdom).
- **Ancient of Days (DIVERGENT, leaning)** — Hippolytus on Dan 7:9 ("who
  maketh the days old, and yet becometh not old Himself by times and
  days"), Ps-Dionysius (Div. Names 10: God as Author of age, time, days).
  V. Jerome and Hagigah 14a carry the aged-Judge reading (the modern one).

## TIER 2 — Kindred ancient precedent (context, possible footnotes)

- Issachar (NOVEL): Targum Ps-Jonathan Gen 49:14-15 (donkey = Torah-yoke;
  bowing; tribute); Gen. Rabbah 72:5 — "understanding of the times" =
  fixing the CALENDAR (intercalation): ancient support for the entry's
  "discerning the appointed times" clause. Testament of Issachar: the
  field-laborer archetype.
- Butter (NOVEL): Berakhot 63b "the butter of Torah," churned from milk by
  toil (V); Gregory Moralia XIX on Job 29:6, butter washing the preachers'
  feet (V) — the entry's own two moves, ancient.
- Island (NOVEL): Augustine En. Ps. 97: "the isles = all the churches...
  the waves of all temptations roar around them" (V).
- Gnashing of teeth (DIVERGENT): Augustine En. Ps. 112 equates Ps 112:10
  (the wicked sees the righteous honored, gnashes, melts) with the Gospel
  phrase — rage at vindication, not torment-pain (V).
- Life (DIVERGENT): Berakhot 61b (R. Akiva's fish: Torah is the
  life-element; V), Philo Leg. All. 1.105-108 (life = the manner of living;
  death = vice).
- Living creature (NOVEL): Hippolytus + Lev. Rabbah 13:5 — animal = kingdom
  structurally; the fathers' four-evangelists reading is the DIFFERENT
  mainstream.
- Heaven (NOVEL): Augustine (De Serm. Dom. 2.5-6) and Origen (De Orat.
  23, 26) read "in heaven" non-spatially (the saints; where God's will is
  done); Mishnah's "yoke of the kingdom of Heaven."
- Name / New name (DIVERGENT/NOVEL): Philo (Conf. 146: the Logos as "the
  Name of God"), Hermas (Sim. 9.14: the Name sustains the world; the
  faithful BEAR it), Justin (Dial. 75: "my name is in him" = the Son),
  Sanhedrin 38b (Metatron; delegated Name). New name: Bede (sonship),
  Jerome/Alcuin ("Christian"), Rosh Hashanah 16b (name-change tears the
  decree).
- Valley (DIVERGENT): Gregory the Great (Hom. Ev. 20, via Catena on Luke
  3:5): valleys = the humble, filled and exalted (V); Augustine En. Ps. 84
  (valley of weeping = humility from which one ascends; V).
- Year (NOVEL): Barnabas 15.4, Irenaeus AH 5.28.3, Justin Dial. 81 +
  Jubilees 4:30 (Adam's 930 inside the thousand-year "day"), Sanhedrin 97a
  (the world's millennial shemittah; V), Rashi on Lev 26:35 (the seventy
  exile years = the seventy unkept sabbatical/jubilee years; V),
  11QMelchizedek (history run in jubilees to the tenth). Rich kindred chain
  for cycle-scaling; no ancient states shanah = turning as a figure.
- Elam (NOVEL): Gen. Rabbah 42:4 — Gen 14's allied kings as the four
  world-kingdoms (imperial confederacy typology). Philo allegorizes
  differently.
- Ship (DIVERGENT): the fathers uniformly Church (Tertullian De Bapt. 12;
  Hippolytus De Antichr. 59 — Christ the Pilot, V; Augustine Serm. 75, V);
  Augustine CD 15.26 calls the ark "a figure of the CITY of God sojourning
  in this world" — civitas, the closest ancient step toward the book's
  kingdom/city/state. Already cited in-chapter via Hilary.
- Storm (DIVERGENT): persecutions/temptations beating the vessel
  (Tertullian, Chrysostom Hom. Matt. 28; V) — kindred agitation-of-the-
  world; Augustine internalizes (different).
- Disciple (DIVERGENT): Ignatius (discipleship perfected through full
  conformity, at cost), Augustine/Chrysostom on John 8:31 (continuance).
- Judgment / Justice (DIVERGENT): Augustine's discretio/damnatio
  distinction; din as governance (Gen. R. 12:15); tsedaqah → alms in LXX,
  Vulgate Dan 4:27, Bava Batra 9a-10b.
- Shadow of Death (NOVEL): Jerome and Chrysostom on Matt 4:16 — the living
  sinner's state patterned on death, short of it — kindred; no ancient
  carries the book's full structure.
- Garden (DIVERGENT): Cyprian (Ep. to Jubaianus: the Church as paradise of
  fruit-trees watered by the four Gospels; V), Lev. Rabbah 32:5 / Targum
  Song 4:12 (the locked garden = Israel), Jerome on Ezek 31 (the garden's
  trees = kings/nations, following the oracle).
- Firmament (NOVEL): Augustine Conf. 13.15-17 (the divider over the bitter
  sea of mankind; the firmament = Scripture), Origen Hom. Gen. 1.2, Basil
  (reporting the allegorists), Gen. Rabbah 4:6.
- Heart of the earth (DIVERGENT): the fathers read Matt 12:40 as
  Hades/tomb (Tertullian De Anima 55, Irenaeus AH 5.31; V) — DIFFERENT —
  but the Jerusalem-as-navel premise is itself ancient on other texts:
  Jubilees 8:19 ("Zion, the centre of the navel of the earth"), Tanchuma
  Kedoshim 10, Cyril of Jerusalem Cat. 13.28 (Golgotha "the very centre of
  the earth," citing Ps 74:12).

## TIER 3 — Honest negatives (ancients read otherwise, or nothing found)

- **New moon**: the located ancient testimony sides AGAINST the book's
  full-moon reckoning — Philo Spec. Leg. 2.140-144 (light returning after
  conjunction), Rosh Hashanah 8a (keseh = "covered" = the hidden moon at
  Rosh Hashanah — the opposite reading of the book's Ps 81:3 anchor),
  Origen Hom. Num. 23 (conjunction figure). These are the very traditions
  TTT contests on evidential grounds; recorded plainly.
- **Pearl**: ancients = Christ / the kingdom / the mysteries (Origen,
  Ephrem's Pearl hymns, Clement, Chrysostom, Gregory); no moon/month link.
- **Sapphire**: ancients = the sky-blue of the Throne of Glory (Menachot
  43b via Ex 24:10 + Ezek 1:26), heavenly minds (Gregory), hope-raised
  souls (Bede); no bridal-moon identification. (The throne-glory component
  is at least adjacent to the entry's tohar clause.)
- **Palm of the hand (kaph)**: the spread-hands image is identified — as
  the CROSS (Barnabas 12, Justin Dial. 90-91, Tertullian De Orat. 14, Odes
  of Solomon 27) — but no ancient isolates kaph from yad as a
  covering/offering symbol. NOVEL holds cleanly.
- **Harlot** (as Rev 17 = Jerusalem): no pre-1500 witness found; the
  ancient field is Rome (Tertullian Adv. Marc. 3.13, Victorinus) or a
  generalized end-city (Andrew). Origen's Ezek 16 homilies carry the
  general harlot = unfaithful covenant people equation (kindred) without
  extending it to Rev 17.
- **Fatherless**: Gregory Moralia XIX reads the orphan INVERSELY (the
  convert whose devil-father died); Augustine on John 14:18 kindred but
  explicating Jesus' own metaphor.

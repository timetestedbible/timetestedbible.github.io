# Blind grading run — Sabbath (2026-07-19)

Trigger: the Sabbath chapter rework (MEAT ch. 31 / TTT ch. 12) and the
author's glossary question. Pipeline: 3 blinded consensus agents (no repo
access) + 3 skeptical graders (citation audit against repo texts,
adversarial rubric). Entry graded: `[[sym-sabbath]]` — "A complete seven
crowned by rest…"

## Verdict

**DIVERGENT** (graders 3/3; blind panel unanimous on the traditional
identification).
Badge applied; preamble census now 40 novel = 25 DIVERGENT + 15 NOVEL, 152
symbols, 168 entries.

## Blind consensus (3 agents, no repo access)

Unanimous: tradition — Jewish, Catholic, Protestant, Adventist — identifies
the sabbath as the seventh DAY (Friday sunset to Saturday sunset), extended
analogically to the shemittah year; NO mainstream tradition teaches the word
names the whole seven-day unit. The lexicons (BDAG, Thayer, BDB) list
"week" as a second gloss of sabbaton/shabbat in exactly the counted texts
(Luke 18:12; mia sabbatōn; Lev 23:15) but file it as idiom/metonymy — "the
week named after the day that bounds it" — never as the word's identity.
One blind agent noted the rabbinic reading of Lev 23:15 treats shabbat
there as denoting week-cycles (Menachot 65b territory) while the Sadducee/
Karaite minority insists on the literal rest-day — either way the taught
IDENTITY of "the sabbath" remains the day.

## Grader audits (both VERDICT: Divergent)

Every load-bearing citation verified against repo texts: Lev 23:15
shabbatot temimot (H7676+H8549, WLC checked); Deut 16:9 shavuot (H7620)
same count same festival; Lev 25:2-8 sabbaths of years; 2 Chr 36:21; Luke
18:12 dis tou sabbatou; Matt 28:1 double sabbatōn (+ Mark 16:2, Luke 24:1,
John 20:1, Acts 20:7, 1 Cor 16:2); Heb 4:9 sabbatismos; Acts 17:2 sabbata
tria; Mark 15:42 prosabbaton; Luke 4:16/Acts 13:14/16:13 hē hēmera tōn
sabbatōn; LXX psalm titles (Pss 23/47/92/93 LXX numbering, Brenton in
bibles/lxx.txt — day-of-week assignments confirmed; Greek wording sourced
outside the repo, flagged); Ezek 45:21 H7620 with WLC consonants שבעות;
HG Matt 28:1 mehashavua (H7620). No citation failed.

Grader rationale for Divergent (not Refined): the lexicons concede the
datum but invert its rank — "week" filed as secondary metonymy while the
taught identity stays the rest day. The book relocates the referent from
the day to the unit (rest-day = one-seventh, the completing member); no
tradition teaches that, uses temimot as span-evidence, or reads yom
ha-shabbat as disambiguating genitive. Different referent → Divergent.

## Counter-texts weighed (both graders)

1. **Lev 16:31 / 23:32 — Atonement is shabbat shabbaton on the 10th**: a
   doubled rest-name with no seven-unit under it. Sharpest residue. Fatal
   only to an exclusive "sabbath = always the unit" claim; absorbed by the
   book's rest-as-completing-member wording — and now handled structurally
   by the author's entry split (2026-07-19): "Sabbath" carries the unit,
   "Sabbath day" carries the doubled resting-member sense *wherever the
   rest falls* (seventh day, Atonement, land-year).
2. **Ex 20:10 predicates bare shabbat of the day with no yom-prefix** — the
   word's root sense (cessation) applies to the day directly; the genitive
   rule covers Ex 20:8, not 20:10. Noted in the entry's own framing.
3. Day-sense-only texts (Matt 24:20; Acts 1:12; Num 15:32; Neh 13; John
   5:18): consistent with day-as-member; none breaks the definition.
4. Frequency honesty: most of ~150 occurrences are day-sense in context;
   unit-sense explicit in the counting/duration texts. Matches the research
   file's coverage note.

## Third-grader flags, actioned

1. **Acts 17:2 downgraded** — "three sabbath days" reads equally well as
   three Saturdays; not probative for the unit-sense. Removed from the
   counting list in BOTH chapters (MEAT 31 / TTT 12) and the audio twin;
   research file annotated.
2. **2 Chr 36:21 softened, then CUT (author's ruling)** — what the land
   collects is seventy rest-YEARS (resting members), not seventy units;
   the softened clause "lacks all relevance," so the passage was removed
   entirely from both chapters, the audio twin, and the MEAT seeref.
3. **LXX psalm-title numbering** — the chapters cite MT numbers (24/48/94/93);
   the repo's bibles/lxx.txt uses LXX numbering (23/47/93/92), so in-repo
   verification must look one psalm lower. Both numberings recorded in the
   research file; a checker who greps MT numbers in lxx.txt will get a false
   miss.
4. Also noted: bare shabbat can predicate the day directly (Ex 16:25; 31:14;
   20:10 without the yom-prefix) — covered by the counting-vs-conduct rule
   and the entries' rest-as-member framing, kept as a range caution; Ezek
   46:1 flipped from objection to supporting evidence (yom-genitive present
   in WLC); Luke 18:12 held as the week-sense witness the day-reading cannot
   absorb.

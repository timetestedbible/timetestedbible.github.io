# Blog image style

Blog feature images use a consistent **old-master oil-painting** style:

- Wide 16:9 composition suitable for both the blog card and article hero.
- Historically grounded people, clothing, architecture, landscape, and objects.
- Rembrandt-inspired chiaroscuro: warm directional light, deep but readable shadows,
  restrained earth colors, visible oil texture, and natural human expressions.
- One strong narrative moment rather than a collage of symbols.
- Symbolic structure should be expressed through the physical scene, light, placement,
  and scale—not through floating icons or fantasy effects.
- No typography, captions, borders, watermarks, modern objects, glossy digital rendering,
  or theatrical religious kitsch.

Each post keeps its final generation prompt in this directory under the post slug.

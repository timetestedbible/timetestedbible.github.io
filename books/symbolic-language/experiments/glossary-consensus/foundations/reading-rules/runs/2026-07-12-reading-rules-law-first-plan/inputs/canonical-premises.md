# Canonical premises v1 — proposed for human approval

These are premises of the experiment, not conclusions the participating AI is
asked to reconsider.

## 1. Canon

The Old and New Testaments are the inspired, truthful, internally coherent word
of God. The experiment does not ask whether either Testament is authoritative.

## 2. Direction of interpretation

Revelation is cumulative. The Torah was given before the Prophets, and the
Torah and Prophets were given before the New Testament. Therefore later
Scripture is interpreted through definitions, covenant categories, symbols,
and tests established in earlier Scripture:

**Torah -> Prophets -> New Testament**

The New Testament may identify a fulfillment, disclose a referent, or confirm
a meaning contained in earlier Scripture. It may not be used to project a
meaning backward that contradicts, abolishes, or redefines the Torah and
Prophets.

## 3. The written law is the prior witness

Deuteronomy 31:9-13 orders the written law to be read publicly and repeatedly.
Deuteronomy 31:24-26 places the completed book of the law beside the ark "for a
witness." The written law is therefore the standing prior testimony against
which later claims are heard.

## 4. Later claims are tested by the prior word

Deuteronomy 13:1-4 refuses even a fulfilled sign when its messenger teaches
departure from God's prior commandments. Deuteronomy 18:21-22 also requires a
claimed prophetic word to come to pass. Fulfillment is necessary where a
prediction is made, but fulfillment never licenses contradiction of the law.
Isaiah 8:20 states the same order: "To the law and to the testimony."

Accordingly, Jesus, an apostle, a prophet, a translation, or an interpretation
is not correctly understood as teaching against the prior written word. An
apparent conflict requires renewed interpretation; it does not authorize
nullification of the earlier witness.

## 5. Old Testament definitions govern New Testament symbols

The Old Testament supplies the primary vocabulary for New Testament symbolic
language. A New Testament symbol is first derived from the Torah, histories,
wisdom writings, and Prophets. New Testament use may confirm or complete that
derivation, but it does not serve as a free-standing replacement dictionary.

## 6. Human traditions remain subordinate

Jewish, Christian, denominational, scholarly, translational, and scribal
traditions are human witnesses. Their data and arguments may be valuable, but
their authority, antiquity, institutional status, or popularity cannot override
the canonical text.

The Masoretic vowel points and accents are evaluated as a later reading
tradition applied to the consonantal text. This premise does not decide how
much historical evidence that tradition supplies; determining a fair and
objective weight is one purpose of the rules experiment. The points are not
permitted to become binding Scripture merely by being printed with the text.

## 7. Language and manuscript witnesses

The experiment does not presume that the surviving Greek form of a New
Testament writing is necessarily the original-language form merely because it
is Greek, early, numerous, or the source text printed in modern critical
editions. A Greek manuscript is a witness to the original text. It does not
automatically override Hebrew, Aramaic, Latin, or other ancient witnesses by
language, institutional acceptance, or numerical survival.

The same restriction applies in every direction. A Hebrew or Aramaic witness
does not become original merely because its language fits the biblical setting,
and a Latin witness does not become independent merely because it preserves a
different reading. For each locus, evaluate:

- manuscript date and provenance;
- independence or dependence of textual lineages;
- whether copies repeat one exemplar or preserve independent information;
- translation technique and plausible direction of translation;
- Semitic or Greek syntax, idiom, wordplay, and other signs of composition or
  translation;
- agreement with the prior-scripture vocabulary and context;
- whether one reading gives a documented explanation for the rise of the
  others.

Count independent lineages and discriminating evidence, not the raw number of
surviving copies. Numerical majority establishes prevalence in the surviving
tradition; it does not by itself establish originality. The original language
and earliest recoverable reading remain conclusions to be demonstrated rather
than presumptions granted to Greek, Hebrew, Aramaic, or Latin.

## 8. Role of the New Testament in a Hebrew-reading dispute

When two vocalizations of the same Old Testament consonants are linguistically
admissible:

1. derive each candidate from the Hebrew or Aramaic consonants, grammar,
   lexicon, immediate context, and earlier-scripture usage;
2. compare each candidate with the Torah's covenant framework and the
   Prophets' established vocabulary and symbols;
3. then use the New Testament as canonical confirmation, fulfillment evidence,
   or a check against misreading the earlier text.

A candidate does not win merely because a Christian tradition calls it
Christological. It must be derivable from the Old Testament. Conversely, a
later tradition's rejection of Jesus cannot veto an Old-Testament-derived
reading that the New Testament then confirms.

## 9. Experiment objective

The experiment asks for an objective, reproducible, non-ad-hoc procedure for
deciding which of two admissible readings best explains the unpointed text
within this law-first canonical order. It must distinguish textual and
linguistic facts from inference, expose burdens and evidence weights, avoid
logical fallacies, and permit `UNDERDETERMINED` when the evidence does not
select a winner.

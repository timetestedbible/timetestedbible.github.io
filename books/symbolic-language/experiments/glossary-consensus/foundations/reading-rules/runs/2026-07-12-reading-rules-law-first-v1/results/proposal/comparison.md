# Law-first Phase 1 — independent proposal comparison

All providers received the same frozen canonical premises and no author rules,
rationale, chapter, disputed word, symbol, or desired interpretation.

## Change from the secular-neutral control

| Provider | Earlier control | Law-first run |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAI | `REBUTTABLE_PRESUMPTION` | `EVIDENCE_ONLY` |
| Anthropic | `REBUTTABLE_PRESUMPTION` | `REBUTTABLE_PRESUMPTION` |
| Gemini | `REBUTTABLE_PRESUMPTION` | `EVIDENCE_ONLY` |
| xAI | `REBUTTABLE_PRESUMPTION` | `EVIDENCE_ONLY` |

The omitted canonical scope was therefore material. Three providers changed
their proposed treatment of the pointing when the law-first order and the
subordination of all human traditions were fixed in advance.

## Unanimous results

1. The consonantal datum must be fixed before vocalizations are compared.
2. Each candidate must pass the same consonantal, lexical, morphological,
   grammatical, and contextual admission gate.
3. Old Testament derivation precedes New Testament confirmation.
4. Torah covenant categories and prior-scripture vocabulary constrain later
   symbolic or typological readings.
5. The New Testament may confirm a reading only after it is derivable from the
   Old Testament; it may not generate the reading by backward projection.
6. Masoretic pointing is not binding Scripture.
7. Manuscript and translation evidence must be counted by independent lineage,
   not raw surviving copy count.
8. Greek, Hebrew, Aramaic, Latin, and other witnesses must be assessed for
   dependence, translation technique, and discriminating information rather
   than granted automatic language privilege.
9. Source identity or communal label alone neither proves nor discredits a
   reading.
10. `UNDERDETERMINED` is a required terminal outcome when the evidence does not
    discriminate.

## Material choices still requiring human decision

### Status of the received pointing

- OpenAI, Gemini, and xAI: one important historical witness with no automatic
  evidentiary presumption.
- Anthropic: a rebuttable practical presumption, defeated only when the rival
  wins at least two independent evidence axes and the received reading wins
  none. Anthropic also scores the Masoretic tradition once as evidence, so the
  proposed burden and the evidence score must be checked for double-counting.

### Burden after admission

- OpenAI and Gemini: symmetrical positive burden.
- Anthropic: the challenger bears a defined additional burden.
- xAI: formally equal after the gate, but the reading using rarer senses,
  strained grammar, Torah tension, or later-tradition dependence bears the
  practical burden created by those facts.

### Canonical contradiction

All providers give Torah coherence high or controlling weight, but the final
rules must decide whether an apparent contradiction is:

- a hard disqualifier after every coherent construal is exhausted; or
- comparative evidence that strongly disfavors the candidate.

### One meaning or deliberate dual reading

OpenAI proposed a separate `DUAL_READING` outcome where context positively
activates two linguistically available senses. The other providers retained
winner-or-underdetermined outcomes. This matters for deliberate biblical
wordplay and must be decided prospectively.

### Form of the decision threshold

- Anthropic proposed a fixed two-independent-axes threshold.
- OpenAI proposed ordinal dominance plus sensitivity testing.
- Gemini proposed a shorter qualitative comparison.
- xAI proposed ranked qualitative comparison, with a quantitative sheet only
  if pre-agreed for auditability.

### Consonantal variants

All providers requested a decision whether the experiment freezes one
consonantal base or runs a separate preliminary textual-criticism stage. None
recommended silently mixing consonantal emendation with vocalization analysis.

No author rule has been drafted or submitted from these results.

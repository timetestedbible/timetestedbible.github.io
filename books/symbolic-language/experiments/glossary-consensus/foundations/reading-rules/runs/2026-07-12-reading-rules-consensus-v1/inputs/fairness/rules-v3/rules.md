# Proposed baseline rules v3

## 1. Fixed question, scope, and outcomes

The one target question is: **Which admissible vocalization or word division is
the best-supported contextual reading of the same agreed unpointed Hebrew or
Aramaic consonantal text?** A claim about a different consonant, the earliest
recoverable vocalization, or one community's historical reading is a different
question requiring a separate comparison.

Accent- or maqqef-dependent syntactic readings with identical consonants and
vowels are in scope: the competing syntax is evaluated as immediate linguistic
evidence, while the accentual tradition is a later reading-tradition witness.

Before collecting evidence, freeze and disclose the base edition, agreed
consonants, witness set, chronological and dialectal windows, genre and corpus
boundaries, editions and databases, search terms, exclusions, negative-search
policy, candidate definitions, and control-selection procedure. Each candidate
is fixed by division, vocalization, root, lexeme, morphology, syntax,
contextual sense, and potential falsifiers.

Outcomes are `A_BETTER_SUPPORTED`, `B_BETTER_SUPPORTED`, `UNDERDETERMINED`,
`INADMISSIBLE_A`, `INADMISSIBLE_B`, and `INADMISSIBLE_BOTH`. If exactly one
candidate fails the gate, the survivor is admissible but is not thereby proved
original. If more than two candidates survive, run disclosed pairwise
comparisons; a candidate is `BETTER_SUPPORTED` only if it satisfies the
decision rule against every rival.

## 2. Symmetrical admission gate

Each candidate must independently:

1. account for every consonant, mater lectionis, and relevant early word
   divider or spacing feature in the agreed text;
2. use an attested or regularly derived lexeme and sense;
3. use a morphologically possible form;
4. use a construction supported either by a genuinely comparable cited
   parallel in the relevant language variety or by a cited productive
   grammatical rule established from the frozen corpus;
5. produce a coherent proposition in the immediate clause;
6. cite a frozen period-and-corpus parallel for any claimed non-default
   orthography, rare morphology, redivision, or exceptional construction;
7. avoid dependence on a form, sense, or convention incompatible with every
   frozen plausible chronological window.

A parallel need attest the same construction type or morphological pattern;
exact lexical identity is required only for a sense otherwise unique to the
disputed locus. If the matching corpus is too small, use the nearest broader
corpus frozen in Rule 1, disclose the distance, and treat both candidates
identically. Ordinary period-normal plene/defective spelling is allowed only
where compatible with the actual agreed consonants. Uncertain dating is not a
gate failure unless every frozen plausible window excludes the candidate.

Dictionary inclusion establishes admissibility, not probability. A gloss
derived only from the disputed locus cannot validate itself. A mechanically
encoded fact establishing orthographic, morphological, or grammatical
impossibility produces `INADMISSIBLE`, not a comparative victory.

## 3. Received pointing and divided traditions

The consonants are the written datum. Masoretic vowels and accents record a
later historical interpretation: neither binding nor valueless.

The received pointing is one historically important reading-tradition witness.
Dependent manuscripts, accents, translations, lexicons, and commentaries do
not multiply it. When the comparison is underdetermined it may remain the
practical citation default, but that default has zero comparative weight,
never breaks a tie, and must be labeled "traditional reading; comparison
underdetermined."

Both candidates bear the same burden of positive evidence; departure from the
received pointing creates no additional burden. Anyone alleging alteration,
bias, exceptional grammar, or a transmission mechanism bears the burden for
that additional claim.

Where Ketiv/Qere, pointings, or accents materially divide the tradition, each
alternative enters the gate and the citation default is suspended at that
locus. Ketiv records a written form; Qere records a reading tradition and has
no automatic priority.

## 4. Strict evidence hierarchy

After the gate, assign every datum to exactly one primary numbered class, with
1 highest and 7 lowest. Other attributes may be recorded, but the datum and its
dependent descendants cannot be counted again.

1. **Immediate linguistic evidence:** morphology, syntax, discourse,
   collocation, reference, and poetic parallelism in the immediate unit.
2. **Same-book evidence:** repeated unambiguous usage in the same book or a
   demonstrably coherent authorial stratum.
3. **Broader biblical evidence:** progressively broader linguistic usage and
   genuinely diagnostic canonical or formulaic parallels. Thematic resemblance
   alone is not a linguistic parallel.
4. **Independent manuscript evidence:** witnesses that explicitly encode or
   constrain a division or vocalization through pointing, partial pointing,
   transliteration, annotation, or disambiguating orthography. A witness with
   only the agreed unpointed consonants is neutral.
5. **Ancient versions:** translations that distinguish the candidates after
   retroversion, target-language ambiguity, and translator technique are
   documented.
6. **Later reading traditions:** Masoretic pointing and accents and other later
   interpretations, counted by independent lineage rather than copy count.
7. **Remote evidence:** cognates, analogies, and modern judgments, used on
   their merits rather than authority or head count.

A lower class may corroborate but cannot overturn a directional higher-class
result without a specific independently supported explanation admissible under
these rules.

## 5. Materiality, independence, mechanisms, and scoring

A datum is material only if an expressly stated inference could change a gate
result, a class score, or a decision condition. State that criterion before
scoring; list excluded data and reasons. Record favorable, neutral, ambiguous,
and contrary evidence.

An evidentiary family is data sharing a common origin or dependence lineage.
Families are independent only when the disclosed map shows no shared exemplar,
translation source, database, observation, or reading tradition. Count
lineages, not copies. State the identity and number of independent families.

For each candidate, apply the same predeclared catalogue of possible scribal,
vocalization, harmonization, euphemism, translation, and normalization
mechanisms. Record whether a documented mechanism explains the rise of the
rival. Score it in class 4 when manuscript-level, class 5 when translational,
or class 6 when a later reading-tradition mechanism. A merely possible motive
has no weight.

After all data in a class are recorded, give that class one comparative score:

- `STRONGLY_FAVORS_A/B`: independently checkable evidence sharply
  discriminates for that candidate, would be unexpected under the rival, and
  has no comparably qualified unresolved counterevidence, but does not render
  the rival inadmissible.
- `FAVORS_A/B`: material evidence makes that candidate more natural or probable
  while leaving the rival viable.
- `NEUTRAL`: the class has no net directional force.
- `AMBIGUOUS`: material evidence conflicts or its direction cannot be resolved
  under the frozen criteria.

Unresolved material conflict within a class is `AMBIGUOUS`. Evidence adverse
to one candidate favors the rival only when the inference is logically
comparative. A class with qualified evidence in both directions is not
directional unless a predeclared quality criterion resolves the conflict.
Record the score and reason before proceeding to the next class.

## 6. Objective-fact and logical-fallacy audit

Separate observable textual facts, cited lexical or grammatical facts,
independently established historical facts, inferences, theological premises,
and unresolved assumptions. All facts must be checkable and all material
contrary evidence disclosed. A scholar's data or argument may be used; status,
institution, denomination, and popularity carry no weight.

Reject appeals to tradition, authority, popularity, novelty, or incredulity;
circular lexicon or translation claims; genetic dismissal; guilt by
association; unsupported motive claims; special pleading; moving burdens;
post-result reweighting; equivocation between possible, probable, traditional,
original, and inspired; dependent-vote inflation; and treating criticism of
one candidate as proof of its rival.

Silence has weight only when independent evidence shows the missing evidence
would probably exist if the candidate were correct. Any claim that earlier
recognition should not be expected—including delayed disclosure or sealing—
must be established from explicit evidence independent of the disputed
vocalization and must identify the covered words and time. Apply this
identically to either candidate. It removes only an invalid expectation of
attestation and supplies no positive evidence.

## 7. Universal source-risk rule

Identity, affiliation, belief, commitment, interest, or topic does not itself
reduce a source's weight. Adjustment requires evidence independent of the
disputed locus demonstrating a relevant, testable pattern of textual, scribal,
translational, or enforced interpretive behavior for the precise class of
readings. A documented institutional policy governing permissible readings or
confessions counts as behavior.

Apply the identical test to every Jewish, Christian, sectarian, traditional,
ancient, or modern source and either candidate. Freeze the comparanda,
behavior, affected locus class, and one qualitative consequence before scoring:

- `NO_ADJUSTMENT`
- `DOWNWEIGHT_WITHIN_CLASS`
- `REQUIRE_INDEPENDENT_CORROBORATION`

Downweighting prevents that source alone from setting a directional class
score. Required corroboration prevents it from satisfying the independent-
support condition in Rule 9 without a separate family. An adjustment changes
only source weight and never proves the rival. No Christological, calendrical,
prophetic, or other topic receives special treatment unless it satisfies this
universal behavior test.

The evaluator also discloses prior published commitments or advocacy on the
locus; disclosure is not itself a weight adjustment.

## 8. Predeclared context adjustments

Apply identically to both candidates:

- Poetry permits documented ellipsis, inversion, archaic vocabulary, and rare
  morphology; clear parallelism gains weight and raw frequency loses weight.
- Proper names and technical terms use period-appropriate onomastic and
  epigraphic comparanda.
- Legal and formulaic prose uses genuine parallels but forbids forced
  harmonization.
- Biblical Aramaic, late Hebrew, code-switching, and loanword contexts use the
  matching variety.
- Corruption or consonantal emendation is a separate analysis invoked only
  after admissible unemended readings fail.

Genre, date, dialect, speaker, register, and adjustment membership are frozen
independently of the desired result.

## 9. Decision rule

The highest class that materially distinguishes the candidates is the
highest-numbered-priority class—that is, the lowest class number—with a
`FAVORS_*` or `STRONGLY_FAVORS_*` score. `NEUTRAL` and `AMBIGUOUS` do not
distinguish.

A candidate is `BETTER_SUPPORTED` only when:

1. the highest distinguishing class favors it;
2. no unresolved directional result at an equal or higher class favors the
   rival; and
3. it has positive corroboration either from a different numbered class or
   from a second evidentiary family within the highest distinguishing class,
   independent under Rule 5.

Contrary evidence is resolved only by a specific independently supported
dependence, corruption, translation-technique, or source-behavior explanation
admissible under Rules 4–7 and entered before the verdict. If any decision
condition fails, the result is `UNDERDETERMINED`.

## 10. Reproducibility, reversal, and calibration

The report shows the same ledger for both candidates, exclusions, dependence
map, source adjustments, possible falsifiers, outcome-changing evidence, every
material rule, and an ablation of disputed assumptions. Repeat the
classification with A/B labels exchanged.

Before target scoring, freeze suitability criteria and a search procedure for
control homographs in the same book whose readings are independently fixed by
encoded evidence. Report every located control satisfying the criteria. A
control failure occurs only when the independently fixed reading is not
recovered under the same gate and decision rule; failure bars a
`BETTER_SUPPORTED` verdict. Disputed suitability is an unresolved assumption;
absence of a suitable control is disclosed but is not evidence.

General rules and adjustment criteria are predeclared. Their case-specific
application is frozen before evidence scoring. No rule, exception, weight,
source-risk criterion, context category, or control may be added after evidence
collection or scoring begins. A missing rule requires `UNDERDETERMINED` for
that case and prospective revision tested only on future cases.

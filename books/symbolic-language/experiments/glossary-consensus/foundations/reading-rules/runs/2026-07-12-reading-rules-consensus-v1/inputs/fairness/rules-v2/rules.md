# Proposed baseline rules v2

## 1. Scope and outcomes

These rules compare two proposed vocalizations or word divisions of the same
agreed unpointed Hebrew or Aramaic consonantal text. A proposal requiring a
different consonant is a textual-variant claim evaluated separately.

Permitted outcomes are:

- `READING_A_BETTER_SUPPORTED`
- `READING_B_BETTER_SUPPORTED`
- `UNDERDETERMINED`
- `INADMISSIBLE_A`
- `INADMISSIBLE_B`
- `INADMISSIBLE_BOTH`

If exactly one candidate fails the gate, report that result. The surviving
candidate is admissible but is not thereby proved original. The method must not
force a winner.

## 2. Freeze the case before collecting evidence

Before comparison, freeze and disclose:

- the target question: best contextual reading, earliest recoverable reading,
  or the reading of a specified historical community;
- base textual edition and agreed consonants;
- manuscript and versional witness set;
- chronological, dialectal, genre, and corpus boundaries;
- editions, databases, search terms, exclusions, and treatment of negative
  searches;
- both candidates' consonantal division, vocalization, root, lexeme,
  morphology, syntax, contextual sense, and potential falsifiers.

Neither candidate nor the evidence protocol may change after comparison begins
without restarting the comparison and disclosing the change.

## 3. Symmetrical admission gate

Each candidate must independently:

1. account for every consonant and mater lectionis in the agreed text;
2. use an attested or regularly derived Hebrew or Aramaic lexeme and sense;
3. use a morphologically possible form;
4. use a construction supported by at least one cited independent parallel in
   the matching language stratum, period, and genre;
5. produce a coherent proposition in the immediate clause;
6. cite a period-and-corpus parallel for any non-default orthography, rare
   morphology, redivision, or exceptional grammatical claim;
7. avoid dependence on a form, sense, or orthographic convention demonstrably
   later than every plausible composition-and-transmission window.

Where early witnesses contain relevant word dividers or spacing, a redivision
must account for them. Dictionary inclusion establishes admissibility, not
probability. A gloss derived only from the disputed locus cannot independently
validate itself. A candidate that fails any gate is inadmissible, not merely
weak.

## 4. Status of the received pointing

The consonants are the written datum. Masoretic vowels and accents record a
later historical interpretation of that datum. They are neither binding nor
without value.

The received pointing counts as one historically important reading-tradition
witness. Its repetition in dependent manuscripts, accents, translations,
lexicons, or commentaries creates no additional independent witnesses. It is
the conventional citation default when the evidence is underdetermined, but
that practical default has zero comparative weight and cannot break a tie.

No candidate bears an increased burden merely because it departs from the
received pointing. Both candidates bear the same burden of positive evidence.
Anyone alleging alteration, corruption, bias, exceptional grammar, or a
special transmission mechanism bears the burden of establishing that claim.

Where the received tradition is internally divided—including Ketiv/Qere or
material pointing or accent disagreement—each attested alternative enters the
gate separately and the citation default is suspended at that locus. Ketiv
records the written form; Qere records a reading tradition and wins no
automatic priority.

## 5. Fixed and nonoverlapping evidence classes

Assign each datum to one primary class. Record other attributes, but never
count the datum or its dependent descendants again in another class.

1. **Direct encoded constraint:** a feature of the agreed consonants or
   immediate co-text that logically excludes one candidate, such as a mater,
   relevant word divider, forced concord, or another unambiguous encoded
   constraint. A witness preserving only the agreed unpointed consonants is
   neutral. Later pointing, translations, and external traditions never enter
   this class.
2. **Immediate linguistic context:** morphology, syntax, discourse,
   collocation, referential coherence, and poetic parallelism in the immediate
   unit.
3. **Same-book usage:** repeated, unambiguous usage in the same book or a
   demonstrably coherent authorial stratum.
4. **Broader biblical usage:** progressively broader usage and genuinely
   diagnostic canonical or formulaic parallels. Thematic resemblance is not a
   linguistic parallel.
5. **Independent manuscript evidence:** early or otherwise independent
   witnesses not already used in class 1 that explicitly encode or strongly
   constrain a division or vocalization through pointing, partial pointing,
   transliteration, annotation, or disambiguating orthography.
6. **Ancient versions:** translations whose wording distinguishes the
   candidates after documented consideration of retroversion, target-language
   ambiguity, and the translator's technique.
7. **Later reading traditions:** Masoretic pointing and accents and other later
   interpretations, counted by independent lineage rather than number of
   copies.
8. **Remote evidence:** cognates, analogies, and modern judgments, used on
   their merits and never by authority or head count.

A lower class may corroborate a higher class but does not overturn clear
higher-class evidence without a specific independently supported transmission
or corruption explanation.

## 6. Fixed scoring ledger

Before scoring begins, state what makes a datum material. List excluded data
and the reason for exclusion. Record all material favorable, neutral,
ambiguous, and contrary evidence.

Score each class for each candidate as:

- `STRONGLY_FAVORS`
- `FAVORS`
- `NEUTRAL`
- `AMBIGUOUS`

Record the fact, inference, evidentiary family, uncertainty, and reason for the
score before proceeding to the next class. Evaluate quality through Rule 5;
never use a raw tally of data or witnesses. Frequency alone does not decide,
and rare but attested language remains possible when context supports it.

## 7. Independence and double-counting

Count evidentiary lineages, not copies or authorities. Collapse manuscripts,
translations, lexicons, and commentaries dependent on the same exemplar,
translation, database, or reading tradition. An ancient version counts only
when a defensible retroversion distinguishes the readings. State uncertainty
about dependence rather than converting uncertainty into an extra vote. The
final report states the number and identity of independent lineages supporting
each candidate.

## 8. Objective-fact ledger

Every conclusion must distinguish:

- directly observable textual facts;
- lexical and grammatical facts supported by cited examples;
- historical facts established independently of the disputed reading;
- inferences from those facts;
- theological premises;
- unresolved assumptions.

A claim stated as fact must be independently checkable. A scholar's data or
argument may be used, but not the scholar's status, institution, denomination,
or popularity as evidentiary weight.

## 9. Logical-fallacy and silence audit

Reject:

- appeals to tradition, authority, popularity, novelty, or incredulity;
- circular use of a translation, lexicon, or commentary whose conclusion
  derives from the disputed pointing;
- genetic dismissal based solely on a source being Jewish, Christian,
  sectarian, traditional, or modern;
- guilt by association or motive claims unsupported by relevant documented
  conduct;
- special pleading, moving burdens, or changing weights after seeing which
  candidate benefits;
- equivocation between possible, probable, traditional, original, and
  inspired;
- converting criticism of one reading or witness into proof of its rival;
- aggregation of dependent weak indicators to outweigh a direct independent
  stronger indicator.

Silence has weight only when it is independently established that the missing
evidence would probably exist if the candidate were correct. A claim that
earlier recognition should not be expected—including a claim of deliberately
delayed disclosure or sealing—must be established from explicit evidence
independent of the disputed vocalization and must identify the words and time
covered. This rule applies identically whichever candidate benefits. Such a
claim removes only an invalid expectation of attestation; it supplies no
positive evidence for a reading.

## 10. Universal source-risk rule

A source's identity, affiliation, belief, commitment, or perceived interest
does not by itself reduce its weight.

Weight may be adjusted, or independent corroboration required, only when
evidence independent of the disputed reading demonstrates a relevant,
testable pattern of textual, scribal, translational, or enforced interpretive
behavior for the precise class of readings. An explicit institutional policy
governing which readings may be confessed counts as behavior when documented
independently of the disputed locus.

Apply the identical test to every Jewish, Christian, sectarian, traditional,
ancient, or modern source and to either candidate. Document the undisputed
comparanda, the precise behavior, the class of loci it affects, and the amount
of adjustment before scoring the disputed locus. The adjustment changes only
that source's weight and never supplies positive evidence for its rival.

No theological, Christological, calendrical, prophetic, or other subject
receives special treatment merely by its topic. Any heightened scrutiny must
satisfy this universal behavior-based rule.

## 11. Predeclared genre and textual-context adjustments

The following adjustments apply to either candidate:

- Poetry permits documented ellipsis, inversion, archaic vocabulary, and rare
  morphology; clear parallelism gains weight, while raw frequency loses weight.
- Proper names and technical terms use period-appropriate onomastic and
  epigraphic comparanda rather than ordinary vocabulary frequency.
- Legal and formulaic prose uses genuinely parallel formulations but forbids
  forced harmonization.
- Biblical Aramaic, late Hebrew, code-switching, and loanword contexts use the
  grammar and lexicon of the matching variety.
- Suspected corruption or emendation is a separate analysis invoked only after
  admissible unemended readings fail.

Genre, date, dialect, speaker, register, and class membership must be fixed
independently of the desired result.

## 12. Decision threshold

Class 1 is controlling only when its encoded constraint logically excludes one
candidate. Otherwise, a candidate is `BETTER_SUPPORTED` only when:

1. it is scored at least `FAVORS` at the highest class that materially
   distinguishes the candidates;
2. the rival has no `FAVORS` or `STRONGLY_FAVORS` evidence of equal or higher
   class left unresolved; and
3. at least one additional class contains positive, materially distinguishing
   support from an evidentiary family independent of the family establishing
   the highest-class advantage.

If any condition fails, the result is `UNDERDETERMINED`. The received pointing
may be reported afterward as the practical citation default, labeled
"traditional reading; comparison underdetermined." It never enters the ledger
again as a tiebreaker.

## 13. Reproducibility, reversal, and calibration

The final report must:

- apply the rules in the stated order;
- show the same ledger for both candidates;
- list excluded data and every dependence or source-weight adjustment;
- state which evidence would reverse the result;
- repeat the classification with labels A and B exchanged;
- report whether the result survives removal of each disputed assumption;
- disclose every rule that materially affected the outcome.

Where a suitable control homograph in the same book has a reading fixed by
independent encoded evidence, apply the procedure to that control and report
the result. Failure on a suitable control bars a `BETTER_SUPPORTED` verdict;
absence of a suitable control is disclosed but is not itself evidence.

No rule, exception, weight, source-risk adjustment, or context category may be
added after the target is known. A genuinely missing rule requires
`UNDERDETERMINED` for that case and a prospective revision tested only on
future cases.

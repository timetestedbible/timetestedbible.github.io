## Unanimous methodological core

1. Fix the consonantal text before comparing vocalizations.
2. Apply the same consonantal, morphological, grammatical, lexical, and
   minimally contextual admission gate to both candidates.
3. Do not confuse admissibility with equal probability.
4. Compare immediate syntax and discourse, attested lexical/collocational use,
   same-book usage, genuinely diagnostic parallels, manuscript evidence, and
   ancient versions.
5. Audit ancient versions for translation technique and witnesses for
   dependence; repetitions of one tradition are not independent votes.
6. Treat lexicons and grammars as evidence syntheses, not independent votes;
   do not use an entry circularly when it derives its gloss from the disputed
   locus.
7. Apply any bias or special-context adjustment through a rule fixed before
   seeing the target and apply it symmetrically.
8. Preserve `UNDERDETERMINED` as a terminal result.
9. Freeze the procedure before identifying the disputed reading and record
   evidence for both candidates in an auditable ledger.

## Unresolved issue exposed by phase 1

Every provider assigned the received pointing a `REBUTTABLE_PRESUMPTION`, but
they proposed materially different and partly undefined amounts of weight:

- **Anthropic:** the challenger must win at least two independent evidence
  classes with no strong evidence for the received reading.
- **Gemini:** the challenger needs "preponderant and compelling" evidence,
  sometimes "substantially superior" or "overwhelming" evidence.
- **OpenAI:** the challenger needs a material advantage at the highest
  discriminating evidentiary level.
- **xAI:** the challenger needs a clear cumulative advantage on higher-tier
  grounds.

Thus phase 1 produced agreement on the label but not on a common quantum,
calibration, or historical basis for the presumption. The author-rules round
must resolve this rather than inherit the ambiguous label.

## Other differences to resolve in the author draft

- Whether the received pointing is an evidentiary advantage, a practical
  citation default, or both.
- Whether a single controlling item can decide a case or two independent
  evidence classes are always required.
- The relative order of internal context, early Hebrew manuscripts, ancient
  versions, and Masoretic pointing.
- The exact threshold for `BETTER_SUPPORTED` versus `UNDERDETERMINED`.
- The conditions under which a demonstrated source tendency changes the
  source's weight at a particular locus.

No provider independently proposed a special rule concerning Messianic
readings, appointed-time/calendar readings, or passages expressly sealed until
the time of the end. If the author draft includes those triggers, they will be
newly visible rules rather than hidden premises, and the providers must judge
their procedural fairness before receiving the argument for them.

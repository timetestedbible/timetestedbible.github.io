Apply the book's evidentiary method rather than silently substituting a
dictionary-definition test. An explicit “X means Y” verse is decisive when one
exists, but do not treat the absence of that sentence as decisive by itself.
Test whether independent passages establish a recurring relationship, whether
the relevant uses remain consistent, whether counter-texts break the pattern,
and whether the pattern distinguishes this conclusion from its strongest
alternative. The method evidence shows that this kind of inference can work;
it does not prove this particular conclusion.

This is not a vote on whether the conclusion is traditional or comfortable.
It is a judgment of the supplied scriptural case.

## Works (good works, evil works) — FULL

Source run: `2026-07-12-works-symbol-links-final`

Model: `grok-4.5`

Book entry: Deeds — not a synonym for obedience. A work becomes obedience when it executes a legitimate command; it becomes disobedience when the command or deed conflicts with God's command. Romans 4 uses the laborer's work offered for wages and debt; James 2 uses Abraham's work as faith made visible. Complete obedience still earns no wage: the servant has only done his duty.

Adjudicated core identification:
- Works are deeds/actions; a work becomes obedience only when it executes a legitimate command and becomes disobedience when it conflicts with God's command; works are therefore not a synonym for obedience.

Adjudicated core identification:

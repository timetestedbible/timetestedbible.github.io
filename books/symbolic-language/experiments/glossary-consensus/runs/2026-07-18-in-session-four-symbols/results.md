## Verdict rationales (one line each)

- **Ancient of Days**: consensus defines the title (eternal God enthroned as Judge; a
  name, not a decoded symbol; explicitly no calendar teaching). The entry keeps the
  etymology but its load-bearing clause — seated at the head of the cycle, "the day
  from which the month, year, and age advance" — changes what the title is about.
  Departure from an occupied seat → Divergent.
- **Sapphire**: the seat is occupied by the text's own gloss (Ex 24:10 "the body of
  heaven"; R. Meir's tekhelet chain) — the stone as the sky's blue. The book installs
  a white luminary within the sky instead: same verses, displaced referent → Divergent.
  All citations verified; no verse holds sapphire and moon together — the
  identification rides the book's four bridges.
- **Issachar**: the tribal portrait (hireling, donkey, tents, appointed times — even
  the rabbinic calendar reading and the Numbers Rabbah sapphire/sun-moon banner) is
  established; but the definitional payload (the hireling who owns the field; Virgo by
  camp-adjacency; sapphire-as-full-moon) is book-system synthesis. _symbols/issachar.md
  already records verdict: novel → Novel.
- **Shadow of Death**: the entry absorbs everything tradition holds (sin, darkness,
  bondage, deliverance; the substance-casts-shadow homiletic; Origen's two-shadows
  frame) and its irreducible content — the genus "the Way and pattern of the world"
  and the Col 2:17 appointed-times opposite — answers to nothing in the consensus,
  which never defined the term (idiom only) → Novel.

## Grader corrections and open items for the author

1. **Shadow of Death counter-texts (OPEN — definition may need rebalancing).**
   Ps 44:17-19: the explicitly faithful, not declined from God's way, are "covered
   with the shadow of death" — unreadable under "the Way and pattern of the world:
   sin, darkness, bondage." Job's corpus (10:21-22; 38:17 "doors of the shadow of
   death") places the phrase at death's gates (the realm), where the entry makes it
   the road. Ch. 17's own passage teaches the outline-that-cannot-kill comfort
   reading; the two-poles assembly exists only at the glossary/_symbols layer. Also:
   Phil 2:5-9 and Rom 6:23 carry no shadow language (system glue, not lexical
   support), and ch. 16 (cited in the seeref) never contains the phrase. AWAITING
   AUTHOR'S RULING on whether to rebalance the definition toward the condition/realm
   range (e.g., the death-shadowed condition — darkness and bondage, the covering
   only Christ's dawn lifts) while keeping the two-shadows opposition.
2. **kisse/keseh (RESOLVED in the book's favor, keep anchored).** One grader objected
   that kisse ends in aleph and keseh in he; but the Pearl chapter hangs the identity
   on Prov 7:20 specifically, whose Masoretic spelling is with aleph (verified by the
   sapphire grader) — consonantally identical to throne there. The claim must stay
   anchored to Prov 7:20 (Ps 81:3 spells it with he), which the chapter already does.
3. **Sapphire sweep gap**: Ezek 28:13 (sapphire among the covering cherub's stones)
   is nowhere weighed in the derivation. Minor; add to the sapphire research sweep.
4. **Ancient of Days, Isa 43:13**: KJV "before the day was I am he" leans on
   translator supply (Hebrew mi-yom, "from the day"); keep the citation but do not
   make it load-bearing in prose.
5. **Issachar minor caveats**: Judg 5:15's "he was sent on foot into the valley" has
   a pronoun ambiguity (KJV's "he" reads most naturally as Barak; modern renderings
   support the tribal reading); 1 Kgs 4:7,17 shows parity among the twelve districts
   ("rich enough to feed a king" is rescued by 1 Chr 12:40). Virgo's attested rivals
   (Cancer — whose donkey-stars fit Gen 49:14 —; Taurus in the month schemes) are
   engaged nowhere in the book; consider a footnote acknowledging the internal-system
   status the research file already concedes.
6. **Lexical rebar confirmed** (usable in prose): tohar (H2892, 4 occurrences) binds
   Ex 24:10's sapphire "clearness" to Ps 89:44's throne-glory; shachaq (H7834) binds
   Job 37:18's molten-mirror sky to Ps 89:37's "faithful witness in heaven." The
   tohar link is now in the glossary entry per the author's ruling.

Source run: `2026-07-12-love-v11-revised`

Model: `claude-fable-5`

Book entry: Self-giving covenant allegiance embodied, for those under God's authority, in commandment-keeping. Obedience without love can be empty sacrifice, but claimed love that persistently refuses His commands is false — “this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments”.

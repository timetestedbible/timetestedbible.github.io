## Evaluate: blood as a dual-meaning symbol (death / covenant)

Blood has been treated as a plain meaning (not a symbol), but evaluate it as
dual-valued:

- **Death** — Rev 16:3 ties the equation directly: the sea "became as the
  blood **of a dead man**: and every living soul **died** in the sea."
  Waters→blood = death upon the peoples (first plague; Rev 16:6 "they have
  shed the blood of saints... thou hast given them blood to drink").
- **Covenant** — "the blood of the covenant" (Ex 24:8; Zech 9:11; Matt 26:28;
  Heb 13:20); "the life of the flesh is in the blood" (Lev 17:11).
- Question: one symbol with two poles (life-given vs life-taken), or two
  distinct uses distinguished by context (shed blood vs sprinkled blood)?
  Test across every occurrence before promoting to the glossary.

## Add directional symbols: North, South, East, West

Candidates for a chapter and/or glossary entries. Working notes:

- **North** = darkness; the quarter of the invader. "Waters rise up out of
  the **north**" (Jer 47:2); "out of the **north** an evil shall break forth"
  (Jer 1:14); Babylon's own destroyer is "an assembly of great nations from
  the **north country**" (Jer 50:9, 41; 51:48) — the North Country is the
  invading army that takes down Babylon and the Whore that rides her
  (spiritual Egypt). Also Lucifer's boast: "in the sides of the **north**"
  (Isa 14:13); God's true seat: "on the sides of the north, the city of the
  great King" (Ps 48:2).
- **East** = beginning and front. Hebrew *qedem* is both "east" and
  "ancient/before/in front" — Eden planted "eastward" (Gen 2:8); the glory
  enters from the east (Ezek 43:2); the wise men and the kings of the east.
- **West** = behind, hindermost. Hebrew *yam* (the sea!) is also the word for
  "west" — the sea at Israel's back; *achar* (behind/after) names the
  "hinder sea" = the western sea (Zech 14:8; Joel 2:20). "The **hindermost**
  of the nations" (Jer 50:12) = the westernmost nation. Facing east (qedem),
  west is what lies behind.
- **South** = ? (negev / teman / darom — gather the uses; Dan 11 king of the
  south; "out of the south cometh the whirlwind," Job 37:9).

## Chapter: the Fall of Babylon (the sinking stone)

"Cast into the sea / sank as a stone" is a **phrase-symbol** (glossary
`sym-cast-into-sea`, introduced in ch. 3): Jer 51:63-64 defines it (scroll
bound to a stone, "Thus shall Babylon sink, and shall not rise"), Rev 18:21
repeats it (the millstone), Ex 15:4-5 sings it first. Needs its own chapter
to establish fully — gather every stone/mountain/millstone-into-water text
(Matt 18:6; 21:21; Mark 9:42; 11:23; Luke 17:2; Neh 9:11; Zech 9:4) and the
"shall not rise" pattern.

**Thesis for the chapter: the Exodus is the TYPOLOGY of the Fall of
Babylon.** The links already established in ch. 3: (1) the plagues —
first plague waters→blood replayed in the vials, wielded by the two
witnesses (Rev 11:6); (2) the call out — "Come out of her, my people…
that ye receive not of her plagues" (Rev 18:4) ∥ "My people, go ye out of
the midst of her" (Jer 51:45) ∥ "let my people go"; (3) the sinking of the
pursuer — "sank into the bottom as a stone" (Ex 15:5) ∥ "Thus shall Babylon
sink, and shall not rise" (Jer 51:64) ∥ the millstone (Rev 18:21); (4) the
song — the victors over the beast stand on the sea of glass and sing "the
song of Moses… and the song of the Lamb" (Rev 15:2-3). Also: the Lord's
host as the flood turned around (Dan 9:26 patch; Jude 1:14; Rev 19:14),
chariots of yeshuah (Hab 3:8).

**The verdict is the not-rising, not the going down** (ch. 5 states it):
Babylon cast into the sea "shall not rise" (Jer 51:64); Jonah cast into the
same sea rises; Messiah "not possible that he should be holden" (Acts 2:24);
baptism enacts the split — the convert comes up (Rom 6:4) while his sins
stay down like Babylon (Mic 7:19). Righteous vs lawless in the same waters.

## The signs of Moses as spoken messages

Ex 4:8 calls signs VOICES ("the voice of the latter sign") — signs speak.
Read each of Moses' signs as a message (ch. 3 now carries the first, in the
two-witnesses passage; ch. 5 keeps only the sign=oracle definition):

- **First — the staff/serpent** (Ex 4:2-4): *matteh* = branch/tribe/staff
  (H4294, from natah "extend" — a branch of the family tree). The tribe cast
  down becomes a serpent = Israel's corruption; the Pharisees are the
  "generation of vipers" (Matt 3:7; 12:34 — five verses before they demand
  a sign; 23:33). Seized by the tail → a staff again = restoration.
  Ch. 3 also now carries the court replay: the magicians' staffs = the
  nations ruled/ridden/consumed by the whore, corrupted into serpents, and
  Aaron's rod swallowing them all = Dan 2:44 (the kingdom consuming all
  kingdoms). Still to gather: Aaron's dead rod buds (Num 17:8 — the tribe
  resurrected); the serpent lifted up (Num 21:8-9; John 3:14 — He was made

- **Second — the leprous hand** (Ex 4:6-7): hand in the bosom becomes
  leprous, restored in the bosom — gather the uses (hand = works? bosom =
  covenant intimacy?).
- **Third — water to blood** (Ex 4:9): already unpacked as the blood arc
  (ch. 3): the peoples' bloodshed shown for what it is.

## Virgin → the woman in travail (Isa 7:14 ↔ Rev 12)

The virgin sign is a HYBRID — an oracle whose own fulfillment is a miracle —

## "That great city" — one title, one city

Nineveh is "that great city" three times (Jonah 1:2; 3:2; 4:11) — the exact
title of the harlot ("the woman... is that great city," Rev 17:18; "that

crucified, Rev 11:8). Ch. 5 now notes it. Follow the thread: the world-city
of the nations under one title across Scripture; Nineveh the great city that
REPENTS vs Babylon the great city that FALLS — two endings for the same city.

# Research: Foreskin / Uncircumcised (author-commissioned side study, 2026-07-05)

Author: "what symbol does foreskins mean, if we are to take away the
foreskins of your heart... it is clearly a symbol that the reader is
supposed to know." All verses verified against kjv.json.

## Scripture's own decode

**Col 2:11** — "circumcised with the circumcision made without hands, in
putting off THE BODY OF THE SINS OF THE FLESH by the circumcision of
Christ." What the heart-circumcision removes is named: the sin-body of
the flesh. That is the foreskin's meaning, stated out loud.

## The pattern: a covenant organ, blocked by flesh

- HEART: Deut 10:16 (foreskin of heart ∥ stiffnecked); Lev 26:41
  (uncircumcised heart ∥ unhumbled); Jer 4:4 (foreskins of the heart,
  beside the fallow ground and thorns of 4:3); Deut 30:6 — God himself
  cuts it, and the freed function is LOVE "with all thine heart."
- EAR: Jer 6:10 — "their ear is uncircumcised, and THEY CANNOT HEARKEN:
  behold, the word of the LORD is unto them a reproach" — the flesh-layer
  blocks the word from the hear-and-obey organ.
- LIPS: Ex 6:12, 30 — Moses "of uncircumcised lips": speech blocked.
- FRUIT: Lev 19:23-25 — a young tree's fruit is counted "uncircumcised"
  (orlah, the same word) three years; the fourth year it is "holy to
  praise the LORD." The foreskin attaches even to fruit: produce blocked
  from acceptable use until the covenant time.
- ALL TOGETHER: Acts 7:51 — "stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and
  ears, ye do always RESIST the Holy Ghost."

## Meaning

The foreskin is the blocking flesh — "the body of the sins of the flesh"
— covering a covenant member and stopping its work: the heart from
loving and receiving, the ear from hearkening, the lips from speaking,
the tree from yielding. Circumcision is the cutting away of the blockage
— done finally by God (Deut 30:6), "without hands" (Col 2:11). Refuse
the cut and be cut off (Gen 17:14 — cut the flesh or be cut from the
people, the covenant's own wordplay).

## Observations (marked, not derivations)

- The literal sign sits on the organ of generation — where SEED passes.
  The covenant mark removes flesh from the seed-line's member; symbolically
  the seed is the word. Observation from the sign's placement, not a verse.
- Sower tie (derivation, already in ch. 2): Jer 4:3-4 parallels "break up
  your fallow ground" with "take away the foreskins of your heart" —
  plowing and circumcising are one operation in two figures: remove the
  barrier so the seed enters the heart.

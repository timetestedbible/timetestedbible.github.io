## Sign 1 — the staff/serpent (Ex 4:2-4) — SPOKEN FOR (ch. 3 carries it)

*matteh* = staff AND tribe (branch of the family tree). Cast down, the
tribe becomes a serpent — Israel corrupted (the Pharisees "generation
of vipers," Matt 3:7; 12:34 — five verses before they demand a sign);
seized "by the tail" — restored to a staff in the hand. Court replay:
the magicians' staffs = nations, swallowed by Aaron's rod = Dan 2:44
(ch. 3). STILL TO ADD (verified):
- Num 17:8 — Aaron's dead rod "budded, and brought forth buds, and
  bloomed blossoms, and yielded almonds" — the tribe resurrected: the
  staff-sign's third act (down-serpent, up-staff, dead-wood blooming).
- Num 21:8-9 + John 3:14 — the serpent LIFTED UP on the pole; "as
  Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son
  of man be lifted up" — He took the serpent's place on the standard
  (2 Cor 5:21 "made him to be sin for us" is the doctrinal name of the
  same move; verify wording before use).

Solomon spent a whole book trying every road under the sun, and ended it in one sentence:

[quote.scripture, Ecclesiastes 12:13]
____
Let us hear the *conclusion of the whole matter*: *Fear God, and keep his commandments*: for this is the *whole duty of man*.
____

This is the last symbol of Part Two because it is the root of all the rest — and, like the rest, it has been hollowed out.
The fear of the LORD is taken either as raw terror or as a soft “reverence” that asks nothing of anyone.
Scripture means neither.
It defines the fear of God by what it does.

[discrete]
=== Not Terror, but Obedience

Moses asks the question outright, and answers it:

[quote.scripture, Deuteronomy 10:12-13]
____
…what doth the LORD thy God *require* of thee, but to *fear* the LORD thy God… to *keep the commandments* of the LORD…
____

To fear God is to keep His commandments — the two are set down as one requirement; and again, __“the LORD commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God”__ (Deuteronomy 6:24).
It shows itself as a turning from evil: __“the fear of the LORD is to hate evil”__ (Proverbs 8:13); __“by the fear of the LORD men depart from evil”__ (Proverbs 16:6); __“the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding”__ (Job 28:28).
Fear is not a feeling about God; it is obedience to God — the same as love, the same as faith.
The last chapter watched Jesus make the same equation from the other side: quoting __“thou shalt *fear* the LORD thy God”__ (Deuteronomy 6:13), He rendered it __“thou shalt *worship* the Lord thy God”__ (Matthew 4:10) — the fear of the LORD is the thing sym:sym-worship[worship] acts out.

[discrete]
=== The Beginning of Wisdom

It is also where wisdom starts:

[quote.scripture, Proverbs 9:10]
____
The *fear of the LORD* is the *beginning of wisdom*: and the knowledge of the holy is understanding.
____

— and the wise, we saw, are those who __“do his commandments”__ (Psalms 111:10).
So the sym:sym-fool[fool], who has said __“There is no God,”__ has no fear, and therefore no wisdom, however clever he may be; he is back on the sym:sym-sand[sand].
Every wise man in Scripture begins here.

[discrete]
=== Fear Him

And the fear is owed to One only — not to men, not to the powers that can reach the body:

[quote.scripture, Matthew 10:28]
____
…fear not them which kill the body… but rather *fear him* which is able to *destroy both soul and body in hell*.
____

__“Sanctify the LORD of hosts himself; and let him be your fear”__ (Isaiah 8:13).
The world fears a thousand things and not God; those who fear God need fear nothing else.
And this fear is no burden — it is __“clean, enduring for ever”__ (Psalms 19:9), a __“treasure”__ (Isaiah 33:6), __“a fountain of life”__ (Proverbs 14:27).
It is also the doorway of everything this book has been doing: __“The *secret* of the LORD is with them that *fear him*; and he will *shew them his covenant*”__ (Psalm 25:14) — fear Him, and the covenant opens.
And the renewed covenant writes this very fear where the law is written: __“I will make an *everlasting covenant* with them… I will put *my fear in their hearts*, that they shall *not depart* from me”__ (Jeremiah 32:40).

[discrete]
=== The Fear of Man

There is a rival fear, and it wants the same seat.
Fear is the throne of obedience — the fear a man holds is the voice he obeys — so the fear of man is the exact counterfeit of the fear of God, and Scripture sets the two against each other in a single line:

[quote.scripture, Proverbs 29:25]
____
The *fear of man* bringeth a *snare*: but whoso putteth his *trust in the LORD* shall be safe.
____

One is a snare, the other safety; and the snare springs by the same law that makes the fear of God obedience.
Where the fear of God keeps the commandments, the fear of man breaks them.
Saul lost the kingdom to it — __“I have sinned… because I feared the people, and obeyed their voice”__ (1 Samuel 15:24): he feared them, so he obeyed them, and disobeyed God.
The rulers who believed __“did not confess him… for they loved the praise of men more than the praise of God”__ (John 12:42-43).
And Paul names it a fork with no middle ground: __“if I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ”__ (Galatians 1:10).
No man serves both.

Turn it the other way and the same law delivers.
The midwives __“feared God, and did not as the king of Egypt commanded them”__ (Exodus 1:17) — the greater fear overruled the lesser, and they kept the higher command.
Abraham names the vacuum from the other side: where __“the fear of God is not in this place,”__ men __“will slay”__ (Genesis 20:11) — take out the one fear and the other floods in.
So it is the fear of God that casts the fear of man out: __“The LORD is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me”__ (Hebrews 13:6; Psalm 118:6).

And weigh what the feared man actually is.
He is __“a man that shall die… which shall be made as sym:sym-grass[grass]”__ (Isaiah 51:12) — and __“all flesh is grass… the grass withereth… but the word of our God shall stand for ever”__ (Isaiah 40:6-8).
To fear man is to fear grass: to break the law that stands for ever for the favour of a face that withers by evening.
The fear of God and the fear of man are one throne with two claimants, and every man seats one of them.

[discrete]
=== The Whole Duty

It is fitting that the last word of these doctrine studies should be Scripture’s first demand.
When the everlasting gospel is carried to every nation at the end, it is this:

[quote.scripture, Revelation 14:7]
____
…*Fear God, and give glory to him*; for the hour of his judgment is come…
____

— and four verses on, the ones who belong to God are __“they that keep the commandments of God, and the sym:sym-faith[faith] of Jesus”__ (Revelation 14:12).
Fear and obedience, first to last.

So the symbols come home.
The kingdom is a sym:sym-mountain[mountain] and the peoples a sym:sym-sea[sea]; the sym:sym-gospel[gospel] is the renewed covenant, the law going forth from Zion; the sym:sym-way[way], the sym:sym-truth[truth], and the sym:sym-life[life] are that law walked; the sym:sym-name[name] is the covenant owned, the sym:sym-garment[garment] the righteousness worn, the sym:sym-light[light] the covenant made visible; the wise are those who do it, and the fools those who do not.
Strip every figure back to the thing it guards, and one command stands under them all: __“Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.”__
And that duty keeps a calendar: the lights of the fourth day were hung __“for signs, and for seasons”__ (Genesis 1:14) — the sym:sym-sun[sun], the sym:sym-moon[moon], and the sym:sym-stars[stars], whose offices are next.

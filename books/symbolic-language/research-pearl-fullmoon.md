## What the corpus CONFIRMS

1. **The OT has pearls, hidden under "rubies."** H6443 peninim — Strong's:
   "probably A PEARL (as round)"; KJV renders "ruby" everywhere (Prov 3:15;
   8:11; 20:15; 31:10; Job 28:18b). Wisdom is "more precious than pearls";
   the virtuous woman's "price is far above pearls." (KJV's one explicit
   "pearls," Job 28:18a, is H1378 gabish = crystal.) SOLID.
2. **keseh = full moon; kisse' = throne; same root, interchangeable
   spellings.** H3677 keseh (כֶּסֶא or כֶּסֶה) "properly, fulness or the FULL
   MOON, i.e. its festival" (KJV "time appointed": Ps 81:3; Prov 7:20);
   H3678 kisse' (כִּסֵּא or כִּסֵּה) "covered, i.e. a throne (as canopied)" —
   BOTH from H3680 kasah, to cover/fill. Strong's itself lists each word
   with both spellings; in the unpointed text they are letter-identical.
   Job 26:9 (tagged H3678, spelled כסה): "He holdeth back the face of his
   throne, and spreadeth his cloud upon it" — read consonantally as the
   full moon veiled by cloud, the verse is astronomical and coherent.
   SOLID as a consonantal reading (per the book's Job 22:30 precedent).
3. **The moon governs the appointed times.** Ps 104:19 "He appointed the
   MOON for seasons (moedim)"; Gen 1:14-16. SOLID.
4. **The sky is a tabernacle whose east gate opens on the new-moon day.**
   Ps 19:4-6 (tabernacle for the sun; chamber/going-forth in the east);
   Ezek 46:1 — the east gate "shut the six working days," OPENED on the
   sabbath and "in the day of the new moon." The full moon is the phase
   that rises in the east at sunset (opposite the sun). SOLID texts; the
   full-moon-through-the-east-gate application is the TTT calendar reading.
5. **Twelve gates, each of ONE pearl.** Rev 21:21 Greek: ἀνὰ εἷς ἕκαστος
   τῶν πυλώνων ἦν ἐξ ἑνὸς μαργαρίτου — "each ONE of the gates was of ONE
   pearl"; Hebrew Revelation (Cochin) doubles echad the same way: "every
   single (H259) gate was of ONE (H259) pearl." The ONE-complete emphasis
   is in both witnesses. Job 38:32 Mazzaroth (H4216 "some noted
   constellation… only in plural") — TTT reads the 12 constellations as 12
   sky-gates; suggestive, not text-explicit.
6. **A pearl is the one gem that is a complete white sphere** — the only
   moon phase that is complete is the full; keseh's own root meaning is
   "fulness" (Strong's). The visual identification full moon ↔ pearl costs
   nothing textually and the fulness-word carries it.

### ADDENDUM 2026-07-04 — the covering runs toward LIGHT (author's question answered)

Ps 89:36-37 settles "covered in darkness vs covered in light" for keseh, and
upgrades throne↔moon from consonantal wordplay to EXPLICIT SIMILE: "his
THRONE as the SUN before me. It shall be established for ever AS THE MOON,
and as a faithful witness in heaven" — the throne likened to both great
lights in the faithful-witness psalm itself. Corroborated: Dan 7:9 "his
throne was like the fiery flame"; Ezek 1:26-28 fire + rainbow brightness
round about the throne; Ps 104:2 "coverest thyself with LIGHT as with a
garment" (verb atah, wrap — same theology, different verb; note honestly).
The darkness texts put the dark OUTSIDE as a veil: Ps 97:2 "clouds and
darkness are ROUND ABOUT him"; 1 Ki 8:12 dwelling in thick darkness (the
veil); Job 26:9 the cloud spread UPON the face. Integrated into ch. 27
"The Fulness in the Night Sky."

## The assembled theory (author's chain)

Pearl (round, white, complete, of great price) = the full moon (keseh,
"fulness") = the governor of the appointed times (Ps 104:19) = the opened
east gate of the sky-tabernacle (Ezek 46:1 + Ps 19) = the gates of the New
Jerusalem, each one complete pearl (Rev 21:21). The merchant who sells all
for the ONE pearl buys the appointed times — entry through the gate. Isa
33:6 already welds the two words of the twin parable: "wisdom and knowledge
shall be the stability of thy TIMES… the fear of the LORD is his TREASURE";
Prov 25:2 — God conceals, kings search out.

## What remains INFERENCE (flag for author)

- No verse says "pearl" of the moon directly; the identification rides on
  (a) keseh = fulness/full moon, (b) the one-complete-pearl gates, (c) the
  east-gate/new-moon opening. Each link is textual; the chain is a reading.
- Mazzaroth = "gates" is TTT's framing; Job 38:32 says only "constellations
  in his season."
- The wisdom-derivation of the pearl (Prov 3:15; 8:11; 31:10 peninim) is
  independently solid and does not require the moon layer; the chapter
  presents wisdom-as-pearl as the graded derivation and the full-moon gate
  as the deeper identification under test.

# Research: Justice and Judgment — the corpus behind ch. 24

Done 2026-07-02 for the chapter. KJV "judgment*" falls in 408 verses,
"justice" in only 28. Tagged: *mishpat* H4941 421×; *tsedaqah* H6666
157×; *tsedeq* H6664 116×; *shaphat* (judge) H8199 203×; Greek *krisis*
G2920 48×, *krima* G2917 28×, *dikaiosynē* G1343 92×.

## The compression

Modern usage: "judgment" ≈ condemnation (the gavel, the Day, "don't
judge me"); "justice" ≈ a courtroom/social abstraction. Scripture:
both are LAW-words commanded to be *done* — "to DO justice and
judgment" (Gen 18:19; Prov 21:3), "EXECUTE judgment" (Jer 22:3),
"KEEP judgment" (Isa 56:1; Ps 106:3; Hos 12:6).

## Justice = righteousness (one Hebrew word, tagged proof)

KJV renders *tsedaqah* H6666 both ways **in the identical pair with
mishpat**:
- Gen 18:19 "to do JUSTICE{H6666} and judgment{H4941}"
- Jer 22:3 "Execute ye judgment{H4941} and RIGHTEOUSNESS{H6666}"
- Jer 23:5 the Branch "shall execute judgment{H4941} and
  JUSTICE{H6666}"; 2 Sam 8:15 David "executed judgment and
  justice{H6666}"; Isa 56:1 "do justice{H6666}: for… my
  righteousness{H6666} to be revealed" — both renderings in ONE verse.
- Psalter parallel columns: Ps 89:14 "JUSTICE{H6664} and judgment are
  the habitation of thy throne" = Ps 97:2 "RIGHTEOUSNESS and judgment
  are the habitation of his throne" — the same sentence, two renderings.
- Amos 5:24 "let judgment run down as waters, and RIGHTEOUSNESS as a
  mighty stream" (commonly misquoted as "justice" — the KJV pair is
  judgment/righteousness; same words either way).
So "justice" is sym-righteousness under a second English name — already
defined: the doing of God's commandments (Deut 6:25; Ps 119:172).

## Judgment (mishpat) = the law's ruling, to be kept and done

- "Statutes and JUDGMENTS, which I teach you, for to DO them"
  (Deut 4:1); "statutes and judgments so righteous as all this LAW"
  (Deut 4:8).
- Ps 119 uses "thy judgments" as a name for the word itself: "all the
  judgments of thy MOUTH" (119:13); "every one of thy righteous
  judgments endureth FOR EVER" (119:160); kept by oath (119:106).
- Ezek 18:5-9 defines "just… lawful{H4941} and right{H6666}" by
  inventory: restores the pledge, robs none, gives bread to the hungry,
  covers the naked, no usury, "executed true judgment between man and
  man, hath walked in my statutes, and hath kept my judgments… he is
  JUST, he shall surely live."
- Jesus: judgment is one of "the WEIGHTIER MATTERS OF THE LAW —
  judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have DONE" (Matt 23:23;
  Luke 11:42 "pass over judgment and the love of God").
- Isa 42:1-4 the Servant "shall bring forth JUDGMENT to the Gentiles…
  till he have set JUDGMENT in the earth: and the isles shall wait for
  his LAW" — judgment ∥ law, in parallel columns (quoted of Jesus,
  Matt 12:18-20).
- Hab 1:4 "the LAW is slacked, and JUDGMENT doth never go forth" —
  judgment goes forth from the law.

## Judgment = the due of the weak (ties to ch. 16's household)

- Deut 10:18 "He doth execute the JUDGMENT of the fatherless and widow."
- Isa 1:17 "seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, JUDGE the fatherless,
  PLEAD for the widow"; Isa 1:23 the princes "judge NOT the fatherless."
- Jer 5:28 "the RIGHT{mishpat} of the needy do they not judge."
- Zech 7:9-10 "Execute TRUE JUDGMENT… and oppress not the widow, nor
  the fatherless, the stranger, nor the poor" — mishpat commanded, then
  the four names of ch. 16.
- Jer 22:15-16 Josiah "did judgment and justice… He JUDGED THE CAUSE OF
  THE POOR AND NEEDY; then it was well with him: WAS NOT THIS TO KNOW
  ME? saith the LORD" — doing mishpat for the poor = knowing God
  (sym-knowing).
- Job 29:12-16 judgment worn "as a ROBE and a diadem" (sym-garment):
  delivered the poor, father to the poor, eyes to the blind.

## Judgment = ruling authority (author's recall, tested and CONFIRMED)

To judge (shaphat H8199) is to RULE/lead/deliver, not merely to
condemn — English semantic drift:
- The judges of Judges are deliverer-rulers: "raised up judges, which
  DELIVERED them" (Judg 2:16); Othniel "judged Israel, and WENT OUT TO
  WAR" (3:10); Nehemiah calls them "SAVIOURS, who saved them"
  (Neh 9:27); "saviours shall come up on mount Zion to JUDGE… and the
  KINGDOM shall be the LORD's" (Obad 21).
- Israel defines the word demanding a king: "a king to JUDGE us… and GO
  OUT BEFORE US, AND FIGHT OUR BATTLES" (1 Sam 8:5, 20).
- Isa 33:22 stacks the offices: "the LORD is our JUDGE{H8199}, our
  LAWGIVER, our KING; he will SAVE us."
- Judging = teaching/guidance: Moses "I judge between one and another,
  and I do MAKE THEM KNOW THE STATUTES of God" (Ex 18:16).
- The wilderness case (the author's memory — text says wilderness, not
  Sinai proper): Ezek 20:33-38 — "with a mighty hand… will I RULE OVER
  you… there will I PLEAD{H8199} with you face to face. Like as I
  PLEADED{H8199} with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of
  Egypt… I will cause you to pass under the rod, and I will bring you
  into the BOND OF THE COVENANT." KJV "plead" = shaphat (niphal, "enter
  into judgment with"): God entering into judgment = becoming King and
  binding into covenant; condemnation only for the refusers ("I will
  purge out from among you the REBELS," 20:38).
- Therefore judgment can be PRAYED FOR ("JUDGE me, O God… O DELIVER
  me," Ps 43:1; 26:1; 35:24), REJOICED AT (Ps 96:11-13 the trees
  rejoice "for he cometh to judge the earth"; 98:9), and INHERITED
  ("judgment was GIVEN to the saints… the saints POSSESSED the
  KINGDOM," Dan 7:22; Rev 20:4 thrones + judgment + reigned;
  Matt 19:28/Luke 22:29-30; 1 Cor 6:2).
- mishpat as "MANNER" (the prescribed order): "the manner{H4941} of the
  God of the land" (2 Kgs 17:26); Ps 72:1-4 the king judges the poor
  AND "shall SAVE the children of the needy."
- Nuance kept honest: the adversarial sense exists — "enter NOT into
  judgment with thy servant" (Ps 143:2); Isa 3:13-14. Judgment = the
  exercise of rule by the law; it vindicates or condemns per the case.

## The Judge, the throne, the Day

- Gen 18:25 "Shall not the JUDGE of all the earth DO RIGHT
  {mishpat}?" — the Judge Himself does judgment (verify tag when
  quoting).
- Thrones stand on it: Ps 89:14/97:2; Isa 9:7 the Son's throne ordered
  "with judgment and with justice… for ever"; 1 Kgs 10:9 Solomon made
  king "to DO judgment and justice."
- Eccl 12:13-14 "keep his commandments… For God shall bring every work
  into JUDGMENT" — the Day applies the same rulings men were commanded
  to keep; "we shall be judged by the law of liberty" (Jas 2:12).
- Mal 2:17 "Where is the God of judgment?"; Isa 59:8-15 judgment/justice
  ∥ way/truth collapse together ("the way of peace they know not…
  no judgment in their goings… truth is fallen in the street").

## First "way of the LORD" (ties to ch. 7)

Gen 18:19 — the first "way of the LORD" in Scripture, defined in the
same breath: "they shall KEEP THE WAY OF THE LORD, TO DO JUSTICE AND
JUDGMENT." The way = justice and judgment done.

## Weightier than sacrifice; joined to the covenant

- Prov 21:3 "To DO justice and judgment is MORE ACCEPTABLE to the LORD
  than sacrifice" (cf. 1 Sam 15:22; Mic 6:8 "do justly, love mercy,
  walk humbly").
- Isa 56:1-2 "Keep ye judgment, and do justice: for my salvation is
  near" — followed immediately by "keepeth the SABBATH" (law-positive).
- Isa 61:8 "For I the LORD LOVE JUDGMENT… and I will make an
  EVERLASTING COVENANT with them" — inside Isaiah's jubilee/liberty
  chapter (hand-off to ch. 25 Liberty).
- Jer 9:24 God's own résumé: "I am the LORD which exercise
  lovingkindness, JUDGMENT, and RIGHTEOUSNESS, in the earth: for in
  these things I DELIGHT."

## Meaning

Judgment (mishpat) = the law's ruling: what God's law declares due —
kept, done, and executed, above all toward the widow, the fatherless,
the stranger, and the poor; the Day of judgment is that same rule
applied by the Judge. Justice = righteousness under a second English
name: one Hebrew word, the commandments done. "Do justice and
judgment" = live the law's verdicts — the very definition Scripture
gives of keeping the way of the LORD.

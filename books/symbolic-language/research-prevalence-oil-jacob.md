## Ch. 18 — "How the oil is usually read" (rank 1 = Holy Spirit, 65%)

Rank-1 CONFIRMED as the dominant teaching:
- GotQuestions ("Parable of the Ten Virgins"): oil widely taken as the
  Holy Spirit / genuine saving relationship.
- Derek Prince, "The Oil in the Lamps… Explained" (sermons.love): oil =
  the Holy Spirit.
- Alabaster Strings, "The Oil…: Symbol of the Holy Spirit": "the oil…
  represents the Spirit of God"; "the Holy Spirit's indwelling is
  non-transferable."
- LDS Ensign (Mar 2009, churchofjesuschrist.org): oil = spiritual
  preparation/testimony (readiness family — table's rank 4).
- Feeding the Flock (2026): "the living presence of the Holy Spirit."
- eBible Q&A: mixed answers — Spirit, faith, relationship (ranks 2/5).
The spread matches the table's shape (Spirit >> faith/grace/works/
word). Exact 65/12/10/8/5 remain estimates — keep "Est. %" label or
soften to "most / some / a few"; sources above can be footnoted.

## Ch. 20 — "How Jacob→Israel is usually read" (rank 1 = old man/new man, 50%)

Rank-1 CONFIRMED as a standard reading:
- GotQuestions ("Why is Jacob called Jacob and Israel alternately?"):
  "some have suggested that the name Jacob represents his old nature
  and Israel his new — 'Jacob' when functioning in his carnal old
  nature… 'Israel' when acting out of his new nature" — stated, then
  qualified (the very qualification the chapter argues).
- Ligonier devotional ("From Jacob to Israel"): spiritual-maturity
  reading (table's rank 3 family).
- Logos sermon (Gen 32:27-28): significance-of-renaming (rank 4).
- TheTorah.com: "Why does the name Jacob remain?" — the plain-renaming
  scholarly view (rank 2).
All four table rows attested; percentages remain estimates — same
treatment as above.

# Sabbath — the whole seven, not the seventh alone (2026-07-08)

Author's proposal, tested: **the symbolic meaning of Sabbath is the full 6+1 unit
— the complete seven — keyed by the fact that a sabbath can be *complete*
(Lev 23:15), which requires that one can be incomplete; a day cannot be partially
complete, only a span can. Counting sabbaths is counting sevens; the day of rest
is one-seventh of the sabbath.** Calendar mechanics and the full derivation are in
_Time Tested Tradition_ — this file is the corpus test. Verses verified against
kjv_strongs.txt, wlc.txt, greek_nt.txt.

---

## The verdict

**Confirmed, with the decode stated in-text — twice in the law and once in the
Gospel's own grammar.**

- **The in-text equation (Tier 1):** the omer count is commanded in both books of
  the law, once in each vocabulary — "**seven sabbaths** shall be complete"
  (Lev 23:15) and "**seven weeks** shalt thou number" (Deut 16:9). Same command,
  same count, same festival: *sabbath = week* by parallel statute.
- **The completeness key (the author's own):** Lev 23:15's sabbaths are
  *temimot* — complete, whole, unblemished (the sacrifice-word). A seventh *day*
  is or is not; only the seven-day *unit* can be complete or broken. The word
  itself testifies that what is being counted is spans.
- **The unit scales (Tier 1):** "thou shalt number **seven sabbaths of years**
  unto thee, seven times seven years" (Lev 25:8) — the sabbath-unit at
  year-scale, forty-nine years to the jubilee; the land's seventh-year rest is
  "a **sabbath** unto the land" (Lev 25:2-4); and the exile's arithmetic runs on
  the unit: "until the land had **enjoyed her sabbaths**... to fulfil threescore
  and ten years" (2 Chr 36:21). *Sabbath* names the recurring seven, whatever the
  tick — day, year, or year-of-years.
- **The Greek NT calls the week "the sabbath" (Tier 1):** "I fast twice **τοῦ
  σαββάτου**" (Luke 18:12 — twice *in the sabbath*: twice a week; no man fasts
  twice in a day). Every "first day of the week" is literally "**one of the
  sabbaths**" (μία σαββάτων — Matt 28:1; Mark 16:2; Luke 24:1; John 20:1; Acts
  20:7; 1 Cor 16:2). And Matt 28:1 holds both senses in a single verse: "in the
  end of the **sabbaths** [the day], as it began to dawn toward **one of the
  sabbaths** [the week]." The language itself knows the word means the seven.

So the seventh day is to the sabbath what the tassel is to the garment: the
marked edge of a whole. The rest-day is one-seventh of the unit it crowns —
6 + 1, labor and rest as one fabric — and to keep the seventh is to confess the
ownership of all seven: "Six days shalt thou labour... but the seventh day is
the sabbath of the LORD thy God" (Ex 20:9-10) — both clauses inside one
commandment, the whole week under one signature (which is why the Seal chapter
found the full signet — name, office, domain — written in this command alone).

## Where it reaches

- **Day-as-a-thousand-years** (the book's day symbol; 2 Pet 3:8): scale the
  sabbath-unit by the prophetic day and the great week appears — six millennia of
  labor and the seventh of rest; "there remaineth therefore a **rest**
  [sabbatismos] to the people of God" (Heb 4:9). The millennial reign is the
  seventh-part of a sabbath whose whole is seven thousand years.
- **The counting commands** ("count unto you... seven sabbaths," Lev 23:15;
  "number seven sabbaths of years," Lev 25:8): what Israel counts toward
  Pentecost and toward jubilee is not rest-days but complete sevens — the count
  is the symbol in motion, and where the count begins ("the morrow after the
  sabbath") is the calendar question _Time Tested Tradition_ argues in full;
  cite TTT for the mechanics rather than re-deriving here.
- **2 Chr 36:21 / Lev 26:34-35**: the land repaid in sabbath-units — seventy
  years for seventy broken sevens: the unit is real enough to be owed and
  collected.

## Glossary candidate

> **Sabbath:: The complete seven — six-and-one as a single whole, at every scale
> the law counts it: the week whose seventh day is the rest, the seven years
> whose seventh rests the land, the seven-sevens that ring in jubilee. Scripture
> counts "seven sabbaths complete" and calls the week itself the sabbath — the
> rest-day is the crown of the unit, one-seventh of what the word names. +
> (Lev 23:15 with Deut 16:9; Lev 25:2-8; 2 Chr 36:21; Luke 18:12; Matt 28:1;
> Heb 4:9)**

The printed binding is not the whole shelf.
This book's digital edition — free at https://TimeTested.Bible[TimeTested.Bible] — carries three further chapters, and the full-length studies behind the ones compressed for print:

*Behold the Hand, Behold the Nail.*
Before Israel wrote in Babylon's square letters, every letter was a picture, and the letter names survive as Bible words: _yod_ the hand, _he_ the word _behold_, _vav_ the tabernacle's nail.
What the oldest pictures spell in the Name of God — and how the prophets say the same sentence in words: __“they shall look upon me whom they have pierced.”__

*Clouds.*
A cloud in Scripture is scarcely ever weather; it is a vehicle — __“who maketh the clouds his chariot”__ — and its cargo is defined: __“my doctrine shall drop as the rain.”__
Whom the clouds carry, from the pillar over the camp to the Son of man coming with them, and why the caught-up saints meet the Lord in the air.

*The Moment.*
Babylon falls __“in a moment, in one day”__ — and the Hebrew behind that word is the wink of an eye.
It is the word of Korah's swallowing, of the last trumpet, and of the twinkling in which the dead are raised: judgment does not creep; it blinks.

*The Remnant, in full.*
The count printed in this book compresses a fifteen-line study — the olive gleanings, the field's geometry, the gold assay, the lion's census, Job's household, the land's measured allotments — published complete as “Last Call Before the Fall.”

Every chapter of this book also stands there in full, each verse linked to Scripture and each symbol to its glossary entry.

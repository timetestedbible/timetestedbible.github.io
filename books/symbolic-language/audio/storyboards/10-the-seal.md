# Storyboard — Chapter 9: The Seal

Timed scene list for the video edition (audio: out/09-the-seal.mp3, 540.6s).
Windows are seconds into the chapter audio, anchored on whisper word times
(out/09-the-seal.align.json) at each beat's opening phrase; scene switches
crossfade at the window start.

Images are generated per scene (local-only, assets-video/09-the-seal/ is
gitignored); this file is the source of truth for regenerating them.
Recipe: Codex CLI (native ~1672x941) -> Upscayl 4x (out/upscaled/) ->
lanczos to 1920x1080 (see video-prompts.md for the exact commands).

House style suffix appended to every prompt:

> Style: 19th-century steel engraving in the manner of Gustave Doré — dense
> fine crosshatch linework, near-monochrome warm sepia ink, dramatic
> single-source chiaroscuro with a lighter, warm exposure,
> painterly-photographic detail. The noted red accent is the ONLY color in
> the frame. 16:9 widescreen, highest resolution available.

Approved style trial (author, 2026-07-10): assets-video/09-the-seal-bed.png —
right tone, red seal focus. Scene s01 reuses it unchanged; every other scene
carries one selective red accent where the seal/mark itself appears (warm
gold gleam instead where the "seal" is metal: the priest's plate, the coin).

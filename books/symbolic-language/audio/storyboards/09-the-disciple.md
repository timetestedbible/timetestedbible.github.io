# Storyboard — Chapter 9: The Disciple

audio-of: 09-the-disciple.adoc

Policy: every prompt carries the style suffix; red accents are selective (only where a seal/mark/blood element is itself the subject); no legible text in any image; the face of Jesus is never shown directly (hands, feet, silhouettes, from-behind compositions only).

01. [Opening] — "bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples" :: IMAGE: Hands binding a scroll of the law with cord and pressing a wax seal down upon the knot, SELECTIVE RED ACCENT on the sealing wax only; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

02. [Opening] — the taught ones: the scroll sealed in a living person :: IMAGE: A young disciple carrying a bound scroll clasped tight against his chest as he steps through a doorway into morning light; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

03. [The Gates] — "whosoever doth not bear his cross... cannot be my disciple" :: IMAGE: A man shouldering a rough wooden cross-beam out through his own gate, house, goods, and family left gathered behind him; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

04. [The Gates] — "that ye bear much fruit; so shall ye be my disciples" :: IMAGE: A vinedresser's hands lifting heavy grape clusters on a well-kept vine, pruning hook hanging at his belt; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

05. [Baptized into the Name] — "buried with him by baptism... walk in newness of life" :: IMAGE: A convert raised dripping from a river's water by the baptizer's arms, sunrise breaking flat across the surface; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

06. [Baptized into the Name] — "put on Christ": the covenant worn as a garment :: IMAGE: A clean white garment being drawn down over the shoulders of a newly washed man standing at the river's edge; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

07. [As His Master] — "so to walk, even as he walked" :: IMAGE: A disciple's bare feet stepping into the fresh footprints of his master on a dusty road, the master's robed figure walking on ahead seen only from behind; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

08. [The Name on the Forehead] — the Lamb on mount Sion; "his Father's name written in their foreheads" :: IMAGE: A lamb standing on a mountain summit before a great gathered company whose foreheads each bear a small written mark of fine strokes that are not legible, SELECTIVE RED ACCENT on the forehead marks only; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

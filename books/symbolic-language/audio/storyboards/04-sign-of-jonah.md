# Storyboard — Chapter 4: The Sign of Jonah

audio-of: 04-sign-of-jonah.adoc

Policy: every prompt carries the style suffix; red accents are selective (only where a seal/mark/blood element is itself the subject); no legible text in any image; the face of Jesus is never shown directly (hands, feet, silhouettes, from-behind compositions only).

01. [Opening] — "the LORD had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah" :: IMAGE: A great fish rising open-jawed beneath a man sinking through storm-churned depths, the dark hull of a ship pitching far above; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

02. [The Name] — Yonah the dove, ben Amittai, sent out over the sea of the nations :: IMAGE: A single dove flying low over an endless gray-lit open sea toward a far dark shoreline; monochrome-sepia photographic-painterly, lighter exposure, warm tone, 16:9.

## Treasures the draft missed (now added)

1. **Hab 3:9 — the cross-text definition**: "Thy BOW was made quite
   naked, according to the OATHS of the tribes, even thy WORD" — bow ∥
   sworn oaths ∥ word in one verse; beside the "chariots of SALVATION"
   (3:8, the sea chapter's yeshuah chariots).
2. **Jonathan's bow — the symbol staged**: covenant cut (1 Sam 18:3),
   sealed by handing over robe, sword, BOW, girdle (18:4); David's
   lament taught to Judah as "the BOW" (2 Sam 1:18, book of Jasher);
   "the bow of Jonathan turned not back" (1:22).
3. **The deceitful bow = the covenant-breaker**: "dealt UNFAITHFULLY…
   turned aside like a DECEITFUL BOW" (Ps 78:57); "they return, but not
   to the most High: they are like a deceitful bow" (Hos 7:16) — looks
   strung, betrays at full draw.
4. **God breaks Israel's own bow too**: "I will break the bow of
   ISRAEL in the valley of Jezreel" (Hos 1:5) — the breaking is not
   reserved for heathen kingdoms. And Hos 2:18's bow-breaking sits
   inside the re-betrothal scene (2:19 — ch. 9's wedding).
5. **The abiding bow around the throne**: Ezek 1:28 the glory ringed
   "as the appearance of the BOW that is in the cloud"; Rev 4:3
   "a RAINBOW round about the throne"; Rev 10:1 the angel crowned with
   one. Judgment is rendered from inside the everlasting covenant.
   Gen 49:24 names the secret of the unbroken bow: Joseph's "bow ABODE
   in strength… by the hands of the mighty God of Jacob."
6. **God strings His people as His bow**: "I have BENT JUDAH for me,
   FILLED THE BOW WITH EPHRAIM" (Zech 9:13), beside "the battle bow
   shall be CUT OFF: and he shall speak PEACE unto the heathen" (9:10).
   Also 1 Sam 2:4 "the bows of the mighty men are broken" (Hannah,
   beside 2:8 used in ch. 16).

## Summary

Replace Vineyard summary “furniture” with “remaining symbols.”

## Current

```adoc
Isaiah decoded the vineyard himself — __“the vineyard of the LORD of hosts is the house of Israel”__ — and the rest of the furniture is defined too: the wall is __“Salvation,”__ the tower is what a Name builds, the husbandmen are __“the builders,”__ and the owner's repeated verb, __“sent,”__ is the very name of Siloam, whose tower fell on the generation that refused the Sent One.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Isaiah decoded the vineyard himself — __“the vineyard of the LORD of hosts is the house of Israel”__ — and the remaining symbols are defined too: the wall is __“Salvation,”__ the tower is what a Name builds, the husbandmen are __“the builders,”__ and the owner's repeated verb, __“sent,”__ is the very name of Siloam, whose tower fell on the generation that refused the Sent One.
```

## Rationale

This is a compact chapter teaser, so the noun should do clean work. “Remaining symbols” matches the book’s method and avoids the awkward parable-furniture metaphor.

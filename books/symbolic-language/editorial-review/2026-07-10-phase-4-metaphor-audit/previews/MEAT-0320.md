## Summary

Replace “soils wear one anatomy” with “share one condition.”

## Current

```adoc
The sower's soils wear one anatomy — unplowed, unbroken, choked — and the cure was published with the command: let God circumcise the heart, __“that thou mayest live.”__
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The sower's soils share one condition — unplowed, unbroken, choked — and the cure was published with the command: let God circumcise the heart, __“that thou mayest live.”__
```

## Rationale

The sentence is about a shared spiritual condition. The anatomy metaphor is clever but not needed.

## Summary

Remove “map/furniture” wording from Vineyard page description.

## Current

```adoc
description: "Jesus answered the temple authorities with a vineyard every hearer could map — Isaiah had handed over the key: 'the vineyard of the LORD of hosts is the house of Israel.' The rest of the furniture is defined too: the wall is salvation — 'thou shalt call thy walls Salvation' — and the tower is what a Name builds; the vineyard's tower is the house built 'for my name.' The winepress names the estate's purpose — he looked for grapes with the verb of 'he looked for judgment' — and it takes 2 vintages: presses bursting with new wine, or the vine of the earth trodden and blood to the bridles. The owner's drumbeat verb is sent — apostello, the name of Siloam, 'which is by interpretation, Sent' — and the tower of Sent fell on the generation that refused the Sent One. The husbandmen are 'the builders' — the priests perceived He spake of them — and their own masonry, the conceit-wall, the whitewashed wall, the covenant with death, falls to one flood; the stone they rejected heads a corner of living stones, and the new city's wall carries twelve names."
```

## Proposed

```adoc
description: "Jesus answered the temple authorities with a vineyard every hearer knew — Isaiah had handed over the key: 'the vineyard of the LORD of hosts is the house of Israel.' The remaining symbols are defined too: the wall is salvation — 'thou shalt call thy walls Salvation' — and the tower is what a Name builds; the vineyard's tower is the house built 'for my name.' The winepress names the estate's purpose — he looked for grapes with the verb of 'he looked for judgment' — and it takes 2 vintages: presses bursting with new wine, or the vine of the earth trodden and blood to the bridles. The owner's drumbeat verb is sent — apostello, the name of Siloam, 'which is by interpretation, Sent' — and the tower of Sent fell on the generation that refused the Sent One. The husbandmen are 'the builders' — the priests perceived He spake of them — and their own masonry, the conceit-wall, the whitewashed wall, the covenant with death, falls to one flood; the stone they rejected heads a corner of living stones, and the new city's wall carries twelve names."
```

## Rationale

The metadata repeats the parable-furniture metaphor before the reader reaches the chapter. “Every hearer knew” and “remaining symbols” say the same thing more directly.

## Summary

## Current

```adoc
He is not dragging furniture into heaven; he is raising *his moon* — and remember whose product he is: a moon __“covered”__ in the wrong direction, clothed in darkness instead of light, the one phase that reflects nothing — over the heads of God's people.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
He is not claiming a literal seat in heaven; he is raising *his moon* — and remember whose product he is: a moon __“covered”__ in the wrong direction, clothed in darkness instead of light, the one phase that reflects nothing — over the heads of God's people.
```

## Rationale

The argument is about whether “throne” should be read as a literal seat or as a moon word. The replacement states that contrast directly.

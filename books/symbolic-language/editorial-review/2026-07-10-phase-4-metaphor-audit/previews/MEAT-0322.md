## Summary

Replace “bends the prop” in the Ezekiel sign-act discussion.

## Current

```adoc
A prophet cast in a similitude of defiled food pleads his clean diet as his whole credential — and God bends the prop, not the man: __“I have given thee cow's dung for man's dung”__ (Ezekiel 4:15).
```

## Proposed

```adoc
A prophet cast in a similitude of defiled food pleads his clean diet as his whole credential — and God changes the sign, not the man: __“I have given thee cow's dung for man's dung”__ (Ezekiel 4:15).
```

## Rationale

The sentence is about an acted sign, not a stage object. “Changes the sign” is plainer and keeps the focus on God preserving the prophet’s clean credential.

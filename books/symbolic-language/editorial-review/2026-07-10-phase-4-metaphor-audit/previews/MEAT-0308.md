## Summary

Replace “rest of the furniture” in the Vineyard method paragraph.

## Current

```adoc
The rest of the furniture is decoded too — the wall, the tower, the winepress, the tenants, and the owner's strange, repeated verb — each piece defined elsewhere in Scripture, waiting to be carried in.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The remaining symbols are decoded too — the wall, the tower, the winepress, the tenants, and the owner's strange, repeated verb — each one defined elsewhere in Scripture, waiting to be carried in.
```

## Rationale

The sentence is about how symbols are decoded. Naming them as symbols keeps the prose aligned with the book’s method and avoids a distracting object metaphor.

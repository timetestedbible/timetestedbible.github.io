## Summary

Replace “apparatus of death and judgment” with plainer “order.”

## Current

```adoc
Un-created — returned to the nothing they were called out of — while __“God shall wipe away all tears… and there shall be *no more death*, neither sorrow, nor crying”__ (Revelation 21:4), because the whole apparatus of death and judgment has itself been burned.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Un-created — returned to the nothing they were called out of — while __“God shall wipe away all tears… and there shall be *no more death*, neither sorrow, nor crying”__ (Revelation 21:4), because the whole order of death and judgment has itself been burned.
```

## Rationale

“Apparatus” adds a machine metaphor to an already weighty sentence. “Order” carries the same theological sense without extra machinery.

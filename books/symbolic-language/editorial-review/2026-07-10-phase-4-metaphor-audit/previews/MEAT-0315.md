## Summary

Replace “same anatomy” with “same reversal” in the trumpet armies paragraph.

## Current

```adoc
The trumpet armies carry the same anatomy — __“their power is in their mouth, and in their *tails*: for their tails were like unto *serpents*, and had heads”__ (Revelation 9:19) — tails grown heads, which is the covenant curse in one clause: __“he shall be the head, and thou shalt be the *tail*”__ (Deuteronomy 28:44), the inversion of the blessing that made obedient Israel __“the head, and not the tail”__ (Deuteronomy 28:13).
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The trumpet armies carry the same reversal — __“their power is in their mouth, and in their *tails*: for their tails were like unto *serpents*, and had heads”__ (Revelation 9:19) — tails grown heads, which is the covenant curse in one clause: __“he shall be the head, and thou shalt be the *tail*”__ (Deuteronomy 28:44), the inversion of the blessing that made obedient Israel __“the head, and not the tail”__ (Deuteronomy 28:13).
```

## Rationale

The point is covenant inversion, not anatomy. “Reversal” prepares the reader for head/tail language without adding another metaphor domain.

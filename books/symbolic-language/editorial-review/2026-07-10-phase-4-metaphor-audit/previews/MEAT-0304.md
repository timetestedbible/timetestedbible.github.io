## Summary

Replace “furniture of the covenant” with “covenant patterns.”

## Current

```adoc
This is the writing Paul learned to read — the _typos_ that __“happened unto them”__ — and it runs beyond lives into the very furniture of the covenant: the law is __“a sym:sym-shadow[shadow] of *good things* to come”__ (Hebrews 10:1), the tabernacle built __“according to the pattern”__ of heavenly things (Hebrews 8:5).
```

## Proposed

```adoc
This is the writing Paul learned to read — the _typos_ that __“happened unto them”__ — and it runs beyond lives into the covenant's own patterns: the law is __“a sym:sym-shadow[shadow] of *good things* to come”__ (Hebrews 10:1), the tabernacle built __“according to the pattern”__ of heavenly things (Hebrews 8:5).
```

## Rationale

This is not as jarring as the parable-furniture phrasing, but “patterns” is more precise here and echoes the Hebrews 8:5 quotation in the same sentence.

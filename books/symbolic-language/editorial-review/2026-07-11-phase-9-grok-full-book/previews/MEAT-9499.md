## Summary

Add comma after “design” and split the overloaded sentence at the colon.

## Current

```adoc
He spent fourteen years building systems that encourage integrity by design against the moral hazard and corruption of our financial and political lives: he invented Delegated Proof of Stake, built the first blockchain-based decentralized exchange, the first stablecoins, and the first social-media platform on a blockchain — three of the most-used chains of their day, BitShares, Steem, and EOS, entrusted with billions of dollars — and holds patents for provably secure voting systems, among other inventions.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
He spent fourteen years building systems that encourage integrity by design, against the moral hazard and corruption of our financial and political lives.
He invented Delegated Proof of Stake, built the first blockchain-based decentralized exchange, the first stablecoins, and the first social-media platform on a blockchain — three of the most-used chains of their day, BitShares, Steem, and EOS, entrusted with billions of dollars — and holds patents for provably secure voting systems, among other inventions.
```

## Rationale

Without the comma, “by design against” garden-paths as one phrase; the colon-bound line also runs too long with a nested appositive. A period restores a clean evidence seam and keeps every claim intact.

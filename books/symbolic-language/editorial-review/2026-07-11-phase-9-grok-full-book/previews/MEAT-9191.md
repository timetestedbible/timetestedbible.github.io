## Summary

Repair incomplete phrase “roles' endings not yet dealt”.

## Current

```adoc
It is the tableau of Luke 13:28 rehearsed in history with the roles' endings not yet dealt — and at the end of the age the same scene stands final, with the sight given to the ones outside.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
It is the tableau of Luke 13:28 rehearsed in history before the roles receive their final endings — and at the end of the age the same scene stands final, with the sight given to the ones outside.
```

## Rationale

“Not yet dealt” is unfinished English that obscures the historical-versus-final contrast. The revision states the same idea cleanly without adding doctrine.

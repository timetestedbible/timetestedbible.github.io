## Summary

Replace marriage idiom with direct join.

## Current

```adoc
Jesus had already tied the knot for Nicodemus: __“The *wind* bloweth where it listeth… so is every one that is *born of the Spirit*”__ (John 3:8) — one word for wind and Spirit, in Hebrew and in Greek alike.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Jesus had already joined wind and Spirit for Nicodemus: __“The *wind* bloweth where it listeth… so is every one that is *born of the Spirit*”__ (John 3:8) — one word for wind and Spirit, in Hebrew and in Greek alike.
```

## Rationale

The modern “tied the knot” cliché jars against the biblical register and confuses the intended identification of wind with Spirit.

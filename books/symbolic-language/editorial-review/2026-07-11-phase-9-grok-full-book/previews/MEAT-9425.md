## Proposed

```adoc
The symbol adds nothing — __“in the gardens, after one”__ is among the nations, following one — and it reads with the context, for the next verses show the LORD gathering __“all nations and tongues”__ and sending the escaped __“unto the nations”__ (Isaiah 66:18-19).
```

## Rationale

Supplies nothing is opaque beside the translators’ supplied tree; adds nothing is plain. Verses are the LORD gathering is ungrammatical; show restores agreement while keeping the nations context.

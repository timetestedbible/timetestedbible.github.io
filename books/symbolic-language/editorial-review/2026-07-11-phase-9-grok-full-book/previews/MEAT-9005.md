## Summary

Remove sentence-opening And in author prose.

## Current

```adoc
And the test is not for Paul only — your understanding of any verse must be tested against every other verse, for __“at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established”__ (Deuteronomy 19:15): one verse cannot make a doctrine.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The test is not for Paul only — your understanding of any verse must be tested against every other verse, for __“at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established”__ (Deuteronomy 19:15): one verse cannot make a doctrine.
```

## Rationale

House style forbids author-prose sentences that begin with And. Dropping the conjunction keeps the claim intact and assertive.

## Summary

Un-invert the final clause so the subject precedes its object.

## Current

```adoc
[[sym-new-moon]]New moon:: The *renewed* moon — _chodesh_, from _chadash_, to renew: the phase of the moon that serves as the sign of the start of the month, and so the name of the month itself. A sign must be watched for: the east gate opens on it for worship, commerce resents it, and Paul files it among the shadows of things to come; which phase renews the month, the sym:sym-pearl[Pearl] chapter weighs from the letters. +
```

## Proposed

```adoc
A sign must be watched for: the east gate opens on it for worship, commerce resents it, and Paul files it among the shadows of things to come; the sym:sym-pearl[Pearl] chapter weighs from the letters which phase renews the month.
```

## Rationale

The original places the relative clause first, producing a Yoda-like hang. Reordering yields a clear English sentence with identical meaning and markup.

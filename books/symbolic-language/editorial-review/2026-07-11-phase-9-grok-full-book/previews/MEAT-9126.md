## Summary

Repair the broken relative clause on psēphos.

## Current

```adoc
The Greek verb is _psēphizō_ — to tally with a _psēphos_, the pebble ancient counting and voting were done with — and its only other use in Scripture is a cost-tally: __“which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and *counteth the cost*?”__ (Luke 14:28).
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The Greek verb is _psēphizō_ — to tally with a _psēphos_, the pebble with which ancient counting and voting were done — and its only other use in Scripture is a cost-tally: __“which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and *counteth the cost*?”__ (Luke 14:28).
```

## Rationale

“the pebble ancient counting and voting were done with” lacks the relative pronoun and is ungrammatical; “with which” restores the clause without changing meaning.

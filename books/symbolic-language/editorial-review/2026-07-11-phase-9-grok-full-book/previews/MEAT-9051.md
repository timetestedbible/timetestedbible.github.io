## Proposed

```adoc
He is __“brought up… from corruption”__ — the precise word Peter presses at Pentecost: __“thou wilt not… suffer thine Holy One to see corruption”__ (Psalm 16:10; Acts 2:27).
```

## Rationale

House style forbids sentences in author prose starting with And. Dropping the conjunction keeps the claim intact and denser.

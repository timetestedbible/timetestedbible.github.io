## Proposed

```adoc
Defined by the wicked against the just, staged at Stephen's trial, patterned in the psalm: he shall see, be grieved, gnash, and perish.
```

## Rationale

“Defined on” is unclear English for the intended grounding of the definition. “Templated” is software register; “patterned” keeps the same meaning in biblical prose.

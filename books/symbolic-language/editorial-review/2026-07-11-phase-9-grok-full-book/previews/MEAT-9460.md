## Summary

Remove false claim that Genesis 35:10 occurs the night the name is given.

## Current

```adoc
The very night the name is given, God says __“thy name shall not be called any more Jacob”__ (Genesis 35:10) — and then keeps right on calling him Jacob, and names Himself to the end __“the God of Jacob.”__
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Even after the name is given, God says __“thy name shall not be called any more Jacob”__ (Genesis 35:10) — and then keeps right on calling him Jacob, and names Himself to the end __“the God of Jacob.”__
```

## Rationale

The name is first given at the ford (Genesis 32:28); Genesis 35:10 is the later Bethel reaffirmation. The dual-naming point stands without the erroneous “very night” timing.

## Summary

Replace the physically wrong verb “churn” for milk-to-meat.

## Current

```adoc
The Bible provides two symbolic words, sym:sym-meat[meat] and sym:sym-milk[milk], to describe its teachings, and if you do not do the work to churn the milk into meat, you will remain immature at best — and at worst turn away entirely, like the crowds in Jesus’ day.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
and if you do not do the work to turn milk into meat, you will remain immature at best — and at worst turn away entirely, like the crowds in Jesus’ day.
```

## Rationale

Milk is churned into butter, not meat. “Turn” keeps the biblical milk/meat contrast without fighting the image.

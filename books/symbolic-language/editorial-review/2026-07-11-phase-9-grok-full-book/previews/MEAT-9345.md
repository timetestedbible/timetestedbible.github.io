## Summary

Replace modern landscaping metaphor.

## Current

```adoc
A mountain is a kingdom and a tree is a nation; what faith commands into the sea is not landscaping — it is Babylon's sentence, spoken by the household of faith.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
A mountain is a kingdom and a tree is a nation; what faith commands into the sea is not about dirt — it is Babylon's sentence, spoken by the household of faith.
```

## Rationale

Landscaping is a modern image that fights the biblical mountain-into-sea judgment the chapter is proving. Keep the contrast with popular misreading without the stock modernism.

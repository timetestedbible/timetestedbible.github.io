## Summary

Insert “as” so the jubilee model is grammatical.

## Current

```adoc
[[sym-week]]Week:: A seven — _shavua_, a unit of sevens, and not only of days: Laban decodes it in the text — “Fulfil her *week*… yet seven other *years*” — and Jacob serves seven years for the week named; the jubilee is counted as “seven sabbaths of years,” and Daniel’s seventy weeks are sevens of years — unpointed, seventy sevens that open into seventy jubilees. The Feast of Weeks runs the same count in days — seven sevens to the fiftieth — the jubilee modeled a day for a sym:sym-year[year]. +
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The Feast of Weeks runs the same count in days — seven sevens to the fiftieth — the jubilee modeled as a day for a sym:sym-year[year].
```

## Rationale

“The jubilee modeled a day” lacks the required preposition; “modeled as” restores the intended day-for-year correspondence.

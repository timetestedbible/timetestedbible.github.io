## Summary

Change noun “decode” to “decoding.”

## Current

```adoc
[[sym-tail]]Tail:: The prophet that teacheth lies — Isaiah's own decode, with head and tail already defined by the covenant: obedience makes Israel “the head, and not the tail,” and the curse inverts it. The dragon fells a third of the sym:sym-stars[stars] with its tail — the led destroyed by the misleading — and the trumpet armies' serpent-tails “had heads”: the liar enthroned. Moses is told to seize the serpent exactly there. +
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The prophet that teacheth lies — Isaiah's own decoding, with head and tail already defined by the covenant: obedience makes Israel “the head, and not the tail,” and the curse inverts it.
```

## Rationale

“Decode” as a bare noun is a modern register slip; “decoding” matches the book’s denser, more formal voice while preserving the claim.

## Summary

Replace sentence-initial “That” with “This”.

## Current

```adoc
That sheaf is Christ: __“now is Christ risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of them that slept”__ (1 Corinthians 15:20), raised in the very season of the wave sheaf, the first of a harvest that rises after Him.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
This sheaf is Christ: __“now is Christ risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of them that slept”__ (1 Corinthians 15:20), raised in the very season of the wave sheaf, the first of a harvest that rises after Him.
```

## Rationale

Author prose must not open with That. “This sheaf” still points cleanly to the wave sheaf just named.

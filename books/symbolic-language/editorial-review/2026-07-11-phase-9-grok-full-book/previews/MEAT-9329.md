## Summary

Repair awkward “puts a Monday crucifixion” phrasing.

## Current

```adoc
On the known dates, none of the known weekly sabbaths align with Saturday; the year of the cross puts a *Monday* crucifixion — an impossibility for rising on the third day after three days and three nights.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
On the known dates, none of the known weekly sabbaths align with Saturday; the year of the cross puts the crucifixion on a *Monday* — an impossibility for rising on the third day after three days and three nights.
```

## Rationale

The original is compressed past natural English and briefly obscures the claim. The rewrite states the same fact in ordinary word order without adding doctrine.

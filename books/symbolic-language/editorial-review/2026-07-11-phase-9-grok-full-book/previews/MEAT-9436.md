## Summary

Restore missing “are” in the parallel list.

## Current

```adoc
A hearer who knew that flesh is grass, that the harvest is the day of the LORD, that the wicked are chaff and tares and the firstfruits the LORD's own, needed no interpreter — the meaning was written already in the Law and the Prophets.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
A hearer who knew that flesh is grass, that the harvest is the day of the LORD, that the wicked are chaff and tares and the firstfruits are the LORD's own, needed no interpreter — the meaning was written already in the Law and the Prophets.
```

## Rationale

The third “that” clause currently reads as if the wicked are also the firstfruits. Adding “are” restores the intended parallel without changing doctrine.

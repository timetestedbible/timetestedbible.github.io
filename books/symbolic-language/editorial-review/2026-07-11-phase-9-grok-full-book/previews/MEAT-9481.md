## Summary

Remove second-person director cue that recaps prior viewing.

## Current

```adoc
You have now watched the beast from every side — at the vine, under the pearls, among the tombs, at the trough, in the mire, and inside the gates claiming the name.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The beast has been shown from every side — at the vine, under the pearls, among the tombs, at the trough, in the mire, and inside the gates claiming the name.
```

## Rationale

House style forbids presenter staging such as “Now watch”; “You have now watched” is the same register. A direct assertion preserves the summary without the cue.

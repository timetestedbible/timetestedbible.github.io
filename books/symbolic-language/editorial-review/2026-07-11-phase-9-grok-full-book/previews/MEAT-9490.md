## Summary

Repair the ungrammatical apposition at the end of the Meat definition.

## Current

```adoc
[[sym-meat]]Meat:: The word obeyed to completeness — “My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, and to finish his work”; and the will is the word: where Matthew hears “do the will of my Father,” Luke records the same saying as “hear the word of God, and do it.” sym:sym-milk[Milk] and meat are one substance in two states — the word received, and the word done: chewed and tasted as the ear tries words, served in due season, its fruit senses exercised to discern. +
```

## Proposed

```adoc
sym:sym-milk[Milk] and meat are one substance in two states — the word received, and the word done: chewed and tasted as the ear tries words, served in due season, its fruit the senses exercised to discern.
```

## Rationale

“its fruit senses exercised” is missing a determiner and reads as a fragment. Adding “the” restores the intended parallel to Heb 5:14 while keeping every other word and the book’s central claim intact.

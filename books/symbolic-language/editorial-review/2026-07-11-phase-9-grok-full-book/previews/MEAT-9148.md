## Summary

## Proposed

```adoc
The pattern was set at Sinai: the cherubim stretch their wings __“covering the mercy seat”__ (Exodus 25:20), the testimony — the tablets of the law — goes _inside_ the ark beneath (Exodus 25:21), and God tells Moses where He will speak from:
```

## Rationale

House style bans And-starts in author sentences. Dropping the conjunction preserves the claim and density.

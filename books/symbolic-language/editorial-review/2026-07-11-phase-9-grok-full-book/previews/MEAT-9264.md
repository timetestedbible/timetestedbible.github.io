## Proposed

```adoc
The verb __“removed”__ is singular; read Noah as the righteous — a company — and the singular verb goes looking for a singular subject: *God took up* the righteous, __“caught up together… to meet the Lord”__ (1 Thessalonians 4:17).
```

## Rationale

Make Noah the righteous is unclear and slightly director-like; read Noah as matches the letter-reading practice used throughout the chapter.

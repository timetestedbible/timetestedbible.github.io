## Summary

Remove “mark” staging and sentence-open “And”.

## Current

```adoc
Taste what is set before you, and mark whether it was churned — or merely smoothed.
And the meat on the same table has its counterfeit too — the flesh that presents clean and is not — and it is the next chapter.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Taste what is set before you, whether it was churned — or merely smoothed.
The meat on the same table has its counterfeit too — the flesh that presents clean and is not — and it is the next chapter.
```

## Rationale

Both “mark whether” and the following “And” violate house style (director cues and sentence-open rules). The revision keeps the food-theme imperative, the true/counterfeit contrast, and the chapter transition intact.

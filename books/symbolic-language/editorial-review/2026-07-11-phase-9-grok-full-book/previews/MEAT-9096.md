## Summary

Complete the incomplete idiom 'made to sound.'

## Current

```adoc
The last line, then, is not the slammed door it is so often made to sound: __“no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.”__
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The last line, then, is not the slammed door it is so often made to sound like: __“no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.”__
```

## Rationale

English requires 'like' (or equivalent) after 'made to sound' when the following noun is the comparison. The fix restores grammar without changing force or metaphor.

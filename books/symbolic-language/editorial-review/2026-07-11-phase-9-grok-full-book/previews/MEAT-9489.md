```adoc
[[sym-north]]North, the north country:: The hidden quarter — _tsaphon_, from the root _to hide, to lurk_: the dark side of the sky, the door the ambush comes by. In the north country the spoilers of sym:sym-babel[Babylon] gather — “an assembly of great nations,” the ten allied kings who turn on the seven the sym:sym-harlot[whore] rules — and in its uttermost recesses, the “sides of the north,” the enemy set his seat. +
```

## Proposed

```adoc
In the north country the spoilers of sym:sym-babel[Babylon] gather — “an assembly of great nations,” the ten allied kings who turn on the seven that the sym:sym-harlot[whore] rules — and in its uttermost recesses, the “sides of the north,” the enemy set his seat.
```

## Rationale

“the seven the whore rules” lacks the required “that/whom,” leaving a dangling object. One-word insertion restores grammar without altering the claim.

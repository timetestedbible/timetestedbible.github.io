That is the signal to carry through Scripture: the kanaph is where the *reminder* of the commandments is bound — the fringe is a memory-device, not the law itself — so the kanaph in a verse is a flag that the verse may be speaking of remembering — or forgetting — the commands.

## Proposed

```adoc
This is the signal to carry through Scripture: the kanaph is where the *reminder* of the commandments is bound — the fringe is a memory-device, not the law itself — so the kanaph in a verse is a flag that the verse may be speaking of remembering — or forgetting — the commands.
```

## Rationale

House style bans sentences in author prose that start with That/And/But. “This” keeps the claim intact.

## Summary

Restore natural English order for the appositive claim.

## Current

```adoc
He is the Torah embodied — the standard walking.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
He is the Torah embodied — the walking standard.
```

## Rationale

'The standard walking' inverts expected order and can read as adjective + noun. 'The walking standard' states the same identification cleanly and keeps the density.

## Summary

Remove banned sentence-open But and clean the appositive dash.

## Current

```adoc
But He has named _three_ things, not one, each a word Scripture has already filled with meaning; and the small word fastened to each is _the_ — not _a_ way, _a_ truth, _a_ life, but _the_, the particular one God defines.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
He has named _three_ things, not one, each a word Scripture has already filled with meaning; and the small word fastened to each is _the_ — not _a_ way, _a_ truth, _a_ life, but _the_ — the particular one God defines.
```

## Rationale

Author prose may not open with But. The second em-dash also tightens the appositive after _the_ without changing meaning.

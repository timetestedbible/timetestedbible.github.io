## Summary

## Current

## Proposed

```adoc
The New Testament has already passed through one layer of translation by the time it reaches English, and the symbols still hold.
```

## Rationale

NT Greek is the composition language for most of the corpus; the translation layer is Greek-to-English. The reword keeps the survival-of-symbols claim without the factual slip.

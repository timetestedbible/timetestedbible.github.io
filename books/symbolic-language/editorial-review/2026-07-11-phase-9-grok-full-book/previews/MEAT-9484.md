## Summary

Fix broken parallel between passive and active verbs.

## Current

```adoc
[[sym-foreskin]]Foreskin (uncircumcised):: The blocking flesh — “the body of the sins of the flesh,” Scripture's own decode — covering a covenant member and stopping its work: the uncircumcised heart cannot be humbled or love, the uncircumcised ear “cannot hearken,” uncircumcised lips cannot be heard, and a young tree's fruit is “uncircumcised” until the covenant year. Cut it away, or be cut off. +
```

## Proposed

```adoc
the uncircumcised heart cannot be humbled and cannot love, the uncircumcised ear “cannot hearken,” uncircumcised lips cannot be heard,
```

## Rationale

“Cannot be humbled or love” mixes a passive complement with an active verb under one auxiliary. Splitting the negation restores parallel grammar without changing the claim.

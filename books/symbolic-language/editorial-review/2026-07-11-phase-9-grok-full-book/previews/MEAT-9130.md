Remove forbidden And-open in author prose.

## Current

```adoc
And Proverbs names the bond by its Owner — the adulterous woman is one __“which forsaketh the guide of her youth, and forgetteth *the covenant of her God*”__ (Proverbs 2:17).
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Proverbs names the bond by its Owner — the adulterous woman is one __“which forsaketh the guide of her youth, and forgetteth *the covenant of her God*”__ (Proverbs 2:17).
```

## Rationale

House style bars sentences in author prose from starting with And. Dropping the conjunction keeps the claim and density intact.

## Summary

## Current

```adoc
[[sym-sheol]]Sheol (the grave, “hell”):: The demanding place — from _sha'al_, to demand: mortality's claim on all flesh, swallowing righteous and wicked alike, personified with a mouth, gates, and bars. Its dead sleep in silence until it is made to give them back; then it is itself destroyed — “O grave, I will be thy destruction.” Distinct from Gehenna, the judgment. +
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Its dead sleep in silence until it is made to give them back; then it is itself destroyed — “O grave, I will be thy destruction.” It is distinct from Gehenna, the judgment.
```

## Rationale

“Distinct from Gehenna…” is a sentence fragment after a full stop. Prefixing “It is” supplies the missing subject and verb without changing the distinction drawn.

## Proposed

```adoc
The womb the law watched opens again in Revelation, where the travailing woman __“brought forth a *man child*… and her child was *caught up unto God*, and to his throne”__ (Revelation 12:5). The dragon stands before her to devour him as Egypt's king stood before the firstborn — every redeemed firstborn a rehearsal of __“the *firstborn* of every creature”__ (Colossians 1:15).
```

## Rationale

The trailing absolute (“the dragon standing…”) overloads one sentence and obscures the claim. Two short sentences preserve every link and improve assembly.

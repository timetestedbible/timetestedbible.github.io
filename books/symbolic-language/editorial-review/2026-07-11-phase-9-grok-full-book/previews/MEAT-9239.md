## Summary

Add comma to prevent misreading the prepositional phrase.

## Current

```adoc
To fear man is to fear grass: to break the law that stands for ever for the favour of a face that withers by evening.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
To fear man is to fear grass: to break the law that stands for ever, for the favour of a face that withers by evening.
```

## Rationale

Without the comma, for the favour can attach to stands for ever instead of to the breaking of the law.

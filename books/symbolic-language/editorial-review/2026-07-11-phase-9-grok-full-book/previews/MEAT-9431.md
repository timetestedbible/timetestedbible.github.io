## Current

```adoc
They rejected the tried stone because it did not fit the building they had underway — a high wall of conceit, whitewashed, chartered by a covenant with death — and the plumbline hung against their wall read the verdict the storm executed.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
and the plumbline that hung against their wall read the verdict the storm executed.
```

## Rationale

The bare sequence 'hung ... read' can parse as two finite verbs missing a conjunction; 'that' locks the intended participial reading while preserving the personification.

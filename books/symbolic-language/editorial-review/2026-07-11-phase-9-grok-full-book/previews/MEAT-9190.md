## Summary

Author prose must not open with And.

## Current

```adoc
And Matthew 24:51 closes a loop the psalms opened: the evil servant's portion is __“with the hypocrites”__ — and Psalm 35:16's gnashers are the __“*hypocritical* mockers.”__
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Matthew 24:51 closes a loop the psalms opened: the evil servant's portion is __“with the hypocrites”__ — and Psalm 35:16's gnashers are the __“*hypocritical* mockers.”__
```

## Rationale

House style forbids sentences in author prose that begin with And/But/That. Dropping the initial And leaves the claim and the scripture links intact.

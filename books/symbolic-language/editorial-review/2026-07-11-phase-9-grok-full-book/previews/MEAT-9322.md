## Summary

Corrects mismatched “neither does” after an affirmative verb phrase.

## Current

```adoc
The rule of the link:/books/symbolic-language/what-is-the-point/[What Is the Point?] chapter cuts both ways: the pointing gets a vote, not a veto — and neither does the re-reading, until the letters and the witnesses agree.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The rule of the link:/books/symbolic-language/what-is-the-point/[What Is the Point?] chapter cuts both ways: the pointing gets a vote, not a veto — and so does the re-reading, until the letters and the witnesses agree.
```

## Rationale

“Neither does” requires a negative antecedent; “gets a vote, not a veto” is affirmative, so the parallel must be “and so does.”

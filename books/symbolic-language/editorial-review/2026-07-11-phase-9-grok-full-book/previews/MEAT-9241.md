## Summary

Eliminate sentence-initial 'That' in author prose.

## Current

```adoc
That claim does not plead the blood; it counts the blood a common thing.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The claim does not plead the blood; it counts the blood a common thing.
```

## Rationale

House style forbids author sentences that open with That; 'The claim' preserves reference and force without hedging.

## Context

```text

> 193: That claim does not plead the blood; it counts the blood a common thing.
```

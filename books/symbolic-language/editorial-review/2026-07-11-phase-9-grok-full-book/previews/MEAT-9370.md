## Proposed

```adoc
He takes Jesus __“up into an *exceeding high mountain*”__ (Matthew 4:8) and shows Him __“all the kingdoms of the world *in a moment of time*”__ (Luke 4:5) — and by now every term of the offer is plain.
```

## Rationale

“Every term of the offer reads” lacks a complement and stalls the claim; “is plain” keeps the density and finishes the sense.

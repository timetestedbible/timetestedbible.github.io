The LORD Himself is source and sum: __“The LORD is my *light* and my *salvation*”__ (Psalm 27:1) — light and _yeshuah_ in one breath.

House style forbids author-prose sentences that begin with And. Removing the conjunction leaves the same identity claim and the Psalm quotation unchanged.

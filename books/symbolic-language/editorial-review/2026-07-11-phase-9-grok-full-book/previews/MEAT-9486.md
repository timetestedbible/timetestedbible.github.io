## Summary

Replace compressed “is” with “means” for the equation.

## Current

```adoc
[[sym-hate]]Hate verdict:word[]:: To un-choose — the repudiation of a covenant partner or claim, never a mere feeling: the un-favored wife, the ground of divorce, the King refused. Scripture glosses it by its enactments — sent away, driven out of the house, __“did not *choose*,”__ __“I will *love them no more*”__ — and keeps the grudge in other words (_satam_, _natar_); Jesus' parallel saying translates the hard text: “hate not” father and mother is __“he that loveth father or mother *more than me*.”__ +
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Jesus' parallel saying translates the hard text: “hate not” father and mother means __“he that loveth father or mother *more than me*.”__
```

## Rationale

The telegraphic “is” forces the reader to supply the equivalence. “Means” states the translation relation directly and matches the surrounding density.

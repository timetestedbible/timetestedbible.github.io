# Mountain

Strong case-study chapter: Scripture itself defines mountain as kingdom, then the same sense is pressed through Daniel, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Revelation, and the Gospel sayings with useful prevalence tables. The stone/rock/covenant chain is dense and on-voice. Main defects are local: two author sentences open with And; several presenter openers (See, Consider, Now consider, Now we have); one confused claim that the “final kingdom of the statue” is consumed after the prior sentence already named God’s kingdom as the final kingdom; and one overloaded multi-dash sentence on the fig-tree context. Surgical fixes restore house style without touching doctrine or quoted text.

This close of the mountain chapter is strong: two clean scripture cases, a dense related-symbol inventory, and a sharp method-versus-AI contrast that fits the book’s law-positive voice. Residual presenter staging still appears in a few lead-ins (“Now that you know… we should ask ourselves,” “Here we see,” “By now you should see the pattern”). One missing article and light hedging on the Babylon identification are the only other defects. Quotes and doctrine are clean; surgical cuts restore density without rewriting claims.

# The Sign of Jonah

This chunk delivers dense scene-by-scene typology of Jonah against the Passion, with clear internal symbol work on heart of the earth/seas and the great fish as kingdom. Prevalence tables and the refusal of the three-days-only reading fit the book’s method. Residual house-style defects cluster in multiple And/But sentence opens and director cues (listen, mark, Notice, hold). One high-severity grammar break appears in the Rome/Jerusalem mount sentence, where the subject collapses after the final em-dash. Surgical fixes restore the And/But ban and the broken clause without touching doctrine or quoted scripture.

The chunk’s strength is its dense, law-positive chaining of Jonah to the Passion and the nations, capped by a clean scene-by-scene table and a sharp correction of Augustine’s two-frame compression. The recovery claim is assertive and the symbol work (belly/Sheol, reeds/suph, yeshuah, great city) stays tight. Residual problems are concentrated in presenter staging: multiple “Now/Listen/See/Step back/mark here” director cues, three author-prose sentence opens with And/But/That, and second-person “you have just watched/hold/read” framing that softens the assertiveness. A few register slips (“reward the dig,” “no skeptic would script”) also surface. Surgical cuts restore the author’s voice without touching doctrine or quoted text.

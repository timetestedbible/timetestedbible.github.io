# The Moment

This chapter is one of the manuscript’s tightest symbol studies: rega and pithom are traced cleanly from Exodus through Revelation, and the Babylon–kingdom transfer close is dense and law-positive without padding. Residual presenter staging remains in “Watch them,” “And watch,” and “Take the phrase as a marker,” which undercut the assertive voice. Four author-prose sentences open with And, violating house style. One clause (“every term of the offer reads”) is incomplete and stalls the claim. Surgical fixes to those leave a strong, print-ready chapter.

# Grass

The chapter is dense, law-positive, and well-chained: grass to grain to harvest to chaff/tares to firstfruits to leaven, with OT definitions driving NT parables. Scripture density and assertive voice are strong; the closing reaping image lands cleanly. Residual house-style defects remain: one director cue (“Mark the mercy”), three author-prose sentences opening with “That,” and a compressed parallel that drops “are” before “the LORD’s own.” No doctrine, quote, or modernism problems need reopening.

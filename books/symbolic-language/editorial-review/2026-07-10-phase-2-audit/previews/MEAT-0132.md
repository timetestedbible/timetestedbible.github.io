## Current

```adoc
Evidence is a thing produced and examined — faith is the *seen* member of the pair, and the unseen is proved by it.
Scripture keeps the pattern consistently: hope's object is unseen by definition — __“hope that is *seen* is not hope… but if we hope for that we see not, then do we with *patience* wait for it”__ (Romans 8:24-25) — and the patience is faithfulness holding while the thing stays out of sight.
The unseen things are the eternal ones (2 Corinthians 4:18), and __“we *walk* by faith, not by sight”__ (2 Corinthians 5:7): conduct stands in where sight is denied.
Hebrews 11 itself is a catalog of visible acts — Noah, __“warned of God of things *not seen as yet*… prepared an ark”__ (Hebrews 11:7): unseen warning, visible obedience.
What is hoped for, Scripture also names: the resurrection (Acts 24:15), eternal life __“*promised* before the world began”__ (Titus 1:2), the city and better country Abraham looked for (Hebrews 11:10, 16), His appearing (Titus 2:13).
Faith is what obedience looks like while the promise is still out of sight — works that can be seen (Matthew 5:16), held steady for things that cannot.
```

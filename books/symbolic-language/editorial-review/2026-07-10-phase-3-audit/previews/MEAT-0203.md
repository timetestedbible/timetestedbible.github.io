## Summary

Tame the “out-devours the devourers” metaphor.

## Current

```adoc
The priesthood gone serpentine does not merely join the dragons of the nations; it *swallows* them — it out-devours the devourers and rides what remains.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The priesthood, gone serpentine, does not merely join the dragons of the nations; it *swallows* them and rides what remains.
```

## Rationale

The swallowed/ride image already carries the point. “Out-devours the devourers” adds sound and heat but not much clarity.


## Proposed

```adoc
Throughout Scripture, worship is a thing men __“go up”__ to do at appointed times: Elkanah __“went up out of his city *yearly* to worship and to sacrifice unto the LORD of hosts in Shiloh”__ (1 Samuel 1:3); the firstfruits are set down before the altar, __“and worship”__ (Deuteronomy 26:10); Greeks __“came up to *worship at the feast*”__ (John 12:20); and the Ethiopian eunuch __“had come to Jerusalem for to worship”__ (Acts 8:27). Paul then says he __“went up to Jerusalem for to worship”__ (Acts 24:11), and defines the act for the governor: __“so *worship* I the God of my fathers, sym:sym-belief[believing] all things which are written in the *law* and in the prophets”__ (Acts 24:14).
```

## Rationale

The list is useful, but Paul’s definition is the point. Splitting before Paul gives his testimony more weight and lowers the parse load.

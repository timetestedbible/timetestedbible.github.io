## Summary

Replace clunky “also known as” sentence after John 5:14.

## Current

```adoc
Notice that the penalty for sinning, also known as breaking the law after being healed, is worse than if we had never been healed or saved in the first place.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
Here sinning means breaking the law after being made well, and Jesus says that return leaves a man worse than before.
```

## Rationale

The original sounds like a note to self and forces the reader through a parenthetical definition. The revision states the definition and warning in one clean sentence.

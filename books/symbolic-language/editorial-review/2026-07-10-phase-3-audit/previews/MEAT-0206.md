## Summary

Make the Wings chapter handoff more concrete.

## Current

```adoc
From here on, when your English Bible says _skirt_, _border_, _corner_, _quarter_, or _the ends of the earth_, lift the word and look: if the wing is underneath, the commandments are near — remembered, refused, or waiting to be done.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
From here on, when your English Bible says _skirt_, _border_, _corner_, _quarter_, or _the ends of the earth_, check the word underneath: if the wing is there, the commandments are near — remembered, refused, or waiting to be done.
```

## Rationale

“Lift the word and look” is poetic but less direct than the action the reader should take. The revision keeps the tool-like handoff.

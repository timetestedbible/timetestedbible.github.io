## Summary

Replace “this parable’s twin” with explicit paired-parable wording in Coin.

## Current

```adoc
The woman sweeps *the house* — and the lost sheep of this parable's twin are __“the lost sheep of the *house of Israel*”__ (Matthew 10:6); her ten coins are the house's ten tribes.
```

## Proposed

```adoc
The woman sweeps *the house* — and in the paired lost-sheep parable, the sheep are __“the lost sheep of the *house of Israel*”__ (Matthew 10:6); her ten coins are the house's ten tribes.
```

## Rationale

“Twin” hides the relationship and makes the reader infer which parable is meant. “Paired lost-sheep parable” names the link directly.

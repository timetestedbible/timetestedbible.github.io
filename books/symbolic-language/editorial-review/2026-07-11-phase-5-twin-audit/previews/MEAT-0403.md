## Summary

The verse this Part opened with binds the good tidings to the feasts (Nahum 1:15) — the twin of the verse Paul quotes for the preaching of the gospel (Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15) — and the gospel chapter of this book already heard what the hearers are told to do about the news.

## Proposed

```adoc
The verse this Part opened with binds the good tidings to the feasts (Nahum 1:15) — a companion passage to the verse Paul quotes for the preaching of the gospel (Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15) — and the gospel chapter of this book already heard what the hearers are told to do about the news.
```

## Rationale

The point is textual companionship, not sameness. “Companion passage” tells the reader how to hold the verses together.

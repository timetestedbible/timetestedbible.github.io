# Research: Good (tov) — symbol candidate evaluation (TODO item)

## The corpus shape

- God's self-definition: "there is none GOOD but one, that is, God:
  but if thou wilt enter into life, KEEP THE COMMANDMENTS" (Matt
  19:17) — the exchange defines good twice over: God is the standard,
  and the commandments are where the standard is met.
- The law is good: "the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and
  just, and GOOD" (Rom 7:12); "GOOD statutes and commandments"
  (Neh 9:13 — Sinai's own inventory); "thou art GOOD, and DOEST good;
  TEACH ME THY STATUTES" (Ps 119:68 — being good, doing good, and the
  statutes in one breath).
- Set before Israel as a choice: "I have set before thee this day LIFE
  and GOOD, and death and evil" (Deut 30:15) — good ∥ life, the pair
  of the way (ch. 7).
- "He hath shewed thee, O man, what is GOOD… to do justly, and to love
  mercy" (Mic 6:8 — good = justice/judgment done, ch. 29).
- Gen 1's "God saw that it was good" — creation conforming to His
  intent; "it is not good that the man should be alone" — the one
  not-good.
- "How beautiful… that bringeth GOOD TIDINGS of GOOD" (Isa 52:7 —
  ch. 6 flagged this: the gospel is tidings OF GOOD).

## Verdict proposed

By the ch. 1 symbol test (meaning ≠ dictionary meaning), GOOD is a
MEANING-word, not a symbol — but a defined one: good = conformity to
God's own character as expressed in His commandments. The payoff is
apologetic: "why do you call the law a burden when Scripture calls it
GOOD?" Recommend: no chapter; add a one-paragraph beat to ch. 6
(gospel of good) or ch. 29 (Mic 6:8 already there), plus a glossary
entry marked as meaning-definition:

[[sym-good]]Good:: God's own character as expressed in His
commandments — "there is none good but one… keep the commandments";
the law is "holy, and just, and good," and to do good is to do it. +
(Matt 19:17; Rom 7:12; Ps 119:68; Deut 30:15) — placement for review.

This is the whole vocabulary in one place — 167 entries: 151 symbols and 16 recovered *words*, each with its meaning, a defining verse, and where Scripture establishes it: a chapter of this book, or — for a few marked so — a symbol study in the digital edition.
It is also the tip of the iceberg.
The Bible's symbolic language is larger than any one book can prove, and the method does not retire when the glossary ends: test the key words of every verse you come across.
What once took a lifetime of concordance work now takes minutes — pull every reference by key word, by the Hebrew or Greek word beneath the English, and by concept where the translators chose different words for the same thing; let Scripture's first use and plainest definition fix the meaning; and hold every hard verse to it.

Entries carrying a small DIVERGENT or NOVEL badge identify readings no tradition teaches — 39 novel meanings in all: 24 DIVERGENT, where tradition identifies the symbol with some other meaning (stated beside the entry, so the two can be weighed side by side), and 15 NOVEL, terms never before recognized as symbols at all. The rest of the 151 symbols agree with the familiar reading or sharpen it.
A WORD badge marks a recovered word rather than a symbol: no image stands for anything — the word's own scriptural meaning is the correction, and a materially different common reading is stated beside it where needed.
Where two glossary entries form a direct reciprocal contrast, each carries the same separate *Opposite* line linking to the other. Complementary figures and stages in a process are not labeled opposites merely because they differ.

[[sym-works]]Works (good works, evil works) verdict:word[]:: A work is a deed, not a synonym for obedience. It becomes obedience when it fulfills a legitimate command and disobedience when it conflicts with God's command; context separately identifies wages, duty, or faith made visible. +
[.commonview]__Commonly taught: human obedience and effort set against faith in Paul's salvation texts, with good works following salvation as evidence.__ +
[.seeref]__(Ex 18:20; Ps 111:7; Mic 6:16; Luke 17:7-10; Rom 4:4-5; Jas 2:21-22) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Love, Knowing, Faith & Belief][.chnum]#, ch. 6#, for faith and works, and link:/books/symbolic-language/the-end-of-the-law/[The End of the Law] for Paul's works, wages, and duty vocabulary__

[[sym-works-of-law]]Works of the law verdict:word[]:: Deeds performed under an obligation imposed by law or decree. Context identifies whose law and which duty; fulfilling it may be obedience, but it does not put the lawgiver in debt or earn any benefit. +
[.commonview]__Commonly taught: deeds required by the Mosaic law, generally treated as human obedience set against faith.__ +
[.seeref]__(Rom 3:20, 28; 9:32 KJV/TR; Gal 2:16; 3:2, 5, 10) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-end-of-the-law/[The End of the Law]__

[[sym-worship]]Worship:: To bow down and serve — homage in the body, allegiance in the life; the sym:sym-fear[fear of the LORD] made visible, kept at His appointed times, on His terms. Not the song service: Scripture separates worship from singing in single scenes. +

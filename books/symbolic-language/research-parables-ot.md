# Research: OT-only derivations for the seven parables (ch. 33 rewrite)

Author's directive 2026-07-03: the chapter "failed the test" — it decoded the
parables by quoting Jesus' own NT answers (Luke 8:11; Matt 13:19; 13:38-39),
"looking at the answer, not deriving the answer from the old testament
scriptures, all the disciples should have had." Rewrite so every symbol is
derived from the OT alone, then GRADED against Jesus' answer key — showing He
was reasonable to expect the disciples to understand (Mark 4:13).

All verses below verified against kjv.json / akjv_strongs / strongs dicts /
greek_nt / lxx / hg-chapters, 2026-07-03. Full pull in /tmp/parable-verses.txt.

## The exam is fair — the OT expects parables to be decoded

- Ps 78:2-3 (quoted at Matt 13:35): "I will open my mouth in a parable: I
  will utter dark sayings OF OLD: which we have heard and known, and OUR
  FATHERS HAVE TOLD US." The parable genre's content is old, inherited.
- The OT decodes its own parables in-text, and expects hearers to grade
  themselves: Nathan's ewe lamb → David decodes instantly, "Thou art the
  man" (2 Sam 12:7); Isaiah's vineyard → "the vineyard of the LORD of hosts
  IS the house of Israel" (Isa 5:7); Ezekiel's two eagles → "Know ye not
  what these things mean?… the king of Babylon" (Ezek 17:2, 12) — the exact
  question Jesus asks in Mark 4:13; Jotham's trees (Judg 9:8-15); the
  boiling pot (Ezek 24:3). Ezekiel's audience complained "Doth he not speak
  parables?" (Ezek 20:49).
- So Mark 4:13 ("Know ye not this parable? and how then will ye know all
  parables?") is a fair rebuke only if the vocabulary was already published.
  It was.

## 1. The Sower — derivation

- Seed = the word: Isa 55:10-11 — rain → "seed to the sower, bread to the
  eater… SO SHALL MY WORD BE." Word:seed :: word:bread, one verse.
- Soil = the heart: Jer 4:3-4 — "Break up your fallow ground, and sow not
  among THORNS" followed immediately by "take away the foreskins of your
  HEART"; Hos 10:12 — "sow… break up your fallow ground: for it is time to
  seek the LORD." The thorny ground is already there.
- Stony ground = the stony heart: Ezek 36:26 "I will take away the STONY
  heart out of your flesh."
- Fowls that devour = the enemy's agents: Gen 15:11 — fowls come down on
  Abram's covenant sacrifice; Abram drives them away. Gen 40:17-19 — birds
  eat the baker's bread from the basket; Joseph decodes the birds as doom.
  Hos 8:7 — sown wind, "the strangers shall swallow it up."
- GRADE vs Jesus' key: seed = word (Luke 8:11) ✓; ground = hearers'
  hearts (Matt 13:19 "heart") ✓; fowls = the wicked one (Matt 13:19) ✓.

## 2. Wheat and Tares — derivation

- Two seeds in one world since Eden: Gen 3:15 — "enmity… between THY SEED
  and HER SEED." Children of each sower.
- Weeds among grain: Job 31:40 "thistles instead of wheat, and COCKLE
  (H890 bo'shah, stink-weed) instead of barley"; Isa 5:2,4 wild grapes in
  the vineyard.
- Harvest = the day of judgment: Joel 3:13 "Put ye in the sickle, for the
  harvest is ripe"; Hos 6:11; Jer 51:33 (Babylon's harvest).
- The burning: Mal 4:1 — the proud and wicked "shall be STUBBLE… the day
  that cometh shall burn them up"; Mal 3:18 — that day one shall "discern
  between the righteous and the wicked"; Ps 1:4 chaff.
- GRADE vs Matt 13:37-43: enemy = the devil (the serpent whose seed,
  Gen 3:15) ✓; good seed = children of the kingdom ✓; tares = children of
  the wicked one ✓; harvest = end of the age ✓ (Joel 3:13); furnace ✓
  (Mal 4:1). Item for item.

## 3. Mustard Seed — derivation

- Least of all seeds ↔ Israel defined as least of nations: Deut 7:7 "ye
  were the FEWEST of all people" (same chapter as the remnant line, ch. 20).
- The planted twig that becomes the shelter-tree: Ezek 17:22-24 — God crops
  "a tender one" from the top twigs, plants it on the mountain height of
  Israel, it becomes "a goodly cedar: and under it shall DWELL (H7931
  shakan, the tabernacle root) all fowl of every wing"; v. 24 "I… have
  EXALTED THE LOW TREE." Dan 4:12, 21-22 — tree = a kingdom/king ("It is
  thou, O king"), "the fowls of the heaven… had their habitation (Aramaic
  H7932 shekan) in the branches" = the peoples under its dominion.
- The Branch: Isa 11:1 (netser H5342), Jer 23:5 (tsemach H6780), Zech 3:8;
  6:12 "the man whose name is The BRANCH… he shall build the temple." The
  fowls LODGE — rest — in the branches: Matt 13:32's Greek is kataskēnoō,
  "pitch tent/tabernacle down," the LXX's word family for shakan; and Isa
  11:10 — "a root of Jesse… to it shall the GENTILES seek: and his REST
  shall be glorious." Nations resting in the Branch.
- GRADE: Jesus left this one undecoded — but Ezekiel had already decoded
  the same picture in-text (Ezek 17:2, 12: "put forth a riddle… Know ye not
  what these things mean?"). Derivation stands on God's own prior parable.
- Both edges kept: fowls devour seed (parable 1) AND shelter in the
  kingdom-tree (Ezek 31:6, 13 — Assyria) — what lodges is what the nations
  bring (author's earlier caution retained).

## 4. Leaven — derivation (existing reading confirmed, now OT-first)

- Ex 12:15 purge leaven; Lev 2:11 "ye shall burn NO leaven… in any offering
  of the LORD made by fire"; Amos 4:5 (sarcastic leavened thanksgiving —
  "this liketh you"); Hos 7:4 — adulterous Ephraim "as an oven heated by
  the baker… until it be leavened."
- Grade: Jesus names leaven = doctrine of the Pharisees (Matt 16:12) —
  matches the OT's soured-word direction. Woman + three measures (Gen 18:6)
  stay on the candidate list.

## 5. Treasure hid in the field — derivation

- The treasure is the people: Ex 19:5 "a PECULIAR TREASURE (segullah) unto
  me above all people"; Deut 7:6; Ps 135:4 "Israel for his peculiar
  treasure."
- Hidden: Ps 83:3 — "consulted against thy HIDDEN ONES" — God's people are
  His hidden ones in the world.
- The buyer who sells all: no son of Adam ever did (Matt 19:22); the
  Redeemer did. Gold-ore reading (Hebrew Matthew) + Zech 13:9/Job 23:10
  refining kept from previous draft.
- Isa 33:6 binds treasure to TIMES in one verse: "wisdom and knowledge
  shall be the stability of thy TIMES… the fear of the LORD is his
  TREASURE" — with Prov 25:2 ("the glory of God to CONCEAL a thing: but the
  honour of kings is to search out a matter") this feeds the author's
  appointed-times layer — see research-pearl-fullmoon.md.

## 6. Pearl of great price — derivation (see research-pearl-fullmoon.md)

- KJV hid the OT's pearls: H6443 peninim, rendered "rubies," is per
  Strong's "probably A PEARL (as round)." So: Prov 3:15 wisdom "more
  precious than PEARLS"; Prov 8:11; Job 28:18 "the price of wisdom is above
  PEARLS"; Prov 20:15 "the lips of knowledge are a precious jewel"; Prov
  31:10 "who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above PEARLS"
  — the bride priced above pearls. (KJV's one explicit "pearls," Job 28:18,
  is actually H1378 gabish, crystal — report honestly.)
- The merchant seeking goodly pearls finds ONE of great price = the seeker
  of wisdom finds the one bride/wisdom above all pearls (Prov 2:4-5:
  search "as for hid treasures; THEN shalt thou… find the knowledge of
  God").
- Full-moon/gate layer: tested separately — research-pearl-fullmoon.md.

## 7. The Net — derivation

- Sea = nations (ch. 3, OT-established); men as fish: Hab 1:14-15 "makest
  men as the fishes of the sea… they catch them in their net"; Ezek 32:3
  (net over Pharaoh "with a company of many people"); Eccl 9:12.
- The fishers sent: Jer 16:16 "I will send for many fishers… and they
  shall fish them."
- Fish "of every kind" sorted: Ezek 47:10 — the healed waters' fish
  "according to their KINDS, as the fish of the great sea, exceeding many";
  and Torah itself taught Israel to sort fish — Lev 11:9-12 clean/unclean,
  fins-and-scales — the disciples were fishermen raised sorting catches by
  Leviticus.
- GRADE vs Matt 13:49: "sever the wicked from among the just" ✓ — the
  Lev 11 sort at the end of the age; same verdict as the harvest.

## Feeding of the multitude

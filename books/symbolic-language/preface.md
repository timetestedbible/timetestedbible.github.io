This book was years in the making and represents the cullimnation of studies that produced hundreds of
blog posts and YouTube videos about every subject imagineable. Over that time I became intimately,
familiar with how the Bible consitantly and reliably shares symbols, metaphores, and typology
from Genisis to Revelation. Each study would reveal more symbolic words, more connections, and
more understanding until I realized I no longer read scripture the same way as everyone else.
People would sing songs that quote scriptural words and symbols and have no idea the much deeper 
meaning in what they were saying. 

Every word has a deep and rich meaning that brings every parable, every story, and
every prophecy to life. Many people know in principle that the bible is fully of
symbology, but that is like someone saying that in principle the ocean is full of water.
This book documents over 140 words and symbols from the bible, most of which are used
in ways that are far richer than is commonly taught; however, a large number of words
the Church uses have entirely divergent or opposite meanings when studied by comparing 
scripture to scripture. 

I developed a process for identifying, studying, and applying
the symbolic meaning and that process is what this book hopes to share with you. Using this
process have identified dozens of deep and profound mysteries that will blow your mind
once you see them. These are mysteries that I have not seen anyone else talk about inspite
of using every resource I can think of to look for it. And the most amazing thing is
that these new unique discoceries are occuring on a regular basis. 

The challenge I had when writing this book is sort of like the problem of the chicken and the egg,
    which symbol should I introduce first? Everything is interconnected and therefore I am forced
    to present some symbols up front with minimal evidence and then ask you to trust me until
    later chapters when the full case can be made. 

There are many symbols which are commonly recognized in Christian circles, even if people
don't know the full depth. Symbols like the Fish, the Lamb and maybe even the Sea or a
Mountain. There are many books and resources that explain these things, so this book aims
to address the most interesting and ground breaking discoveries up front. These are the
discoveries that prove the method and reveal the rich reward of seeking to know His word.

The book is divided into parts giving you the method by demonstration right up front, then
systematically applying it to different areas of interest. I start with words we take for
granted, the things we know "that just ain't so". Then apply the same method to other
areas like understanding God's calendar and the prophetic passages. After that I have a
number of chapters that really explore the depth of common symbols like Mountain, Tree,
       and the Sea even though we use them throughout. Lastly the glossery. 

       The Glossery isn't
       an afterthought, it is a powerful tool to help you learn The Bible's Symbolic Language
       and something worth reading and becoming familiar with so that when you encounter any 
       of those words you can recognize them and dig deeper.

Enough about this book, lets get to the meat!

Satan's plan to corrupt the signs of heaven stands documented, because of something no conspirator can prevent: the plan was never announced — it was __“said in thine heart”__ — and God, __“he that… declareth unto man what is his thought”__ (Amos 4:13), read it there and entered it into the public record, a proverb His people are commanded to take up against the king of Babylon (Isaiah 14:4).
The enemy kept his counsel; the Judge published it.

[quote.scripture, Isaiah 14:12-15]
____
How art thou fallen from heaven, O *Lucifer, son of the morning*! how art thou cut down to the ground, which didst *weaken the nations*!
For thou hast said in thine heart, I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my *throne* above the sym:sym-stars[*stars of God*]: I will sit also upon the sym:sym-mountain[mount] *of the congregation*, in the *sides of the* sym:sym-north[north]:
I will ascend above the *heights of the clouds*; I will *be like the most High*.
Yet thou shalt be brought down to hell, to the *sides of the pit*.
____

Read as tradition reads it, the five declarations are five ways of saying the same thing — _I want to be God_ — and half the phrases in them go unexplained.
What is a __“mount of the congregation”__?
Why would a being already __“fallen from heaven”__ care about a compass direction like __“the north”__?
Why does the passage keep pointing at the sky — stars, heights, clouds — if the ambition is a seat in an abstract throne-room?

This book has learned what to do with a passage the translation has flattened: go under the vowel points, which are the tradition of men and not the original text, and let the consonants and the context speak.
Do that here, and the five declarations stop being repetition.
They become five sequential steps of a single act — and the act is performed with a sym:sym-moon[moon].
Here is the whole of it, read on the consonants, in the sky where the passage lives:

[quote.scripture, Isaiah 14:12-14 — on the consonants]
____
How art thou fallen from heaven, *shining one, producer of darkness*! how art thou cut down to the ground, which didst *weaken the nations*!
For thou hast said in thine heart: I will rise into the sky; I will raise my [darkened] *moon* above the sym:sym-stars[*stars of God*]; I will seat myself on the *mount [of authority] over the appointed times*, at the *farthest recess of the darkness*; I will rise at the height of the *thick dark covering*, and I will *silence the greatest [light]*.
____

The bracketed words are supplied — implied by the passage, not written in it — and every other word is the consonants' own.
Not five boasts — five steps: a shining one who produces darkness lifts a lightless moon over God's people, seizes the seat that governs the appointed times, works it from the invisible phase, and crowns the theft by eclipsing the sun itself.
Now every word of that reading must earn its place.

[discrete]
=== The Name: Son of the Dark

Start with the name the KJV renders __“Lucifer, son of the morning.”__
The first word, _helel_, is from the root of brightness — a shining one — and he is introduced as __“fallen.”__
The second phrase is _ben shachar_, and neither word is what it seems.
_Ben_ — __“son”__ — in Hebrew idiom means the product of a thing, what is built from it and characterized by it: the __“sons of Belial”__ are men characterized by worthlessness; the __“children of light”__ (1 Thessalonians 5:5) are people characterized by sym:sym-light[light].
And _shachar_, __“morning,”__ is written with the same consonants as the verb Strong's defines __“to be dim or dark (in color)… be black”__ — the dictionary itself derives the dawn-word __“through the idea of the *duskiness* of early dawn.”__
The hour called _shachar_ is the dark before sunrise — Mary came to the tomb __“early, when it was yet dark”__ (John 20:1) — so the fallen shining one is _ben shachar_ in the second sense: the product of the darkness before dawn, a light whose output is sym:sym-darkness[darkness].

Paul draws the same portrait from the other side: __“Satan himself is transformed into an *angel of light*”__ (2 Corinthians 11:14) — and the Greek verb is _metaschēmatizetai_, from _schēma_, the outward form: Strong's renders it __“to transfigure or *disguise*.”__
The light is the costume, not the substance; what he wears is shine, and what he produces is dark — Isaiah reading the substance, Paul the disguise, one portrait between them.
Hold that identity, for every line of the plan runs on it.

[discrete]
=== A Throne That Is a Moon

__“I will exalt my *throne* above the sym:sym-stars[stars] of God.”__
He is not dragging furniture into heaven; he is raising *his moon* — and remember whose product he is: a moon __“covered”__ in the wrong direction, clothed in darkness instead of light, the one phase that reflects nothing — over the heads of God's people.
The context votes for the sky: the boast is spoken among __“the stars of God,”__ above __“the heights of the clouds”__ — the vocabulary of the night, not of a courtroom — and the sym:sym-stars[stars], the last chapter proved, are the righteous.

The letters carry the reading.
The word under __“throne”__ is _kisse'_ — and the last chapter opened this door: the Hebrew word for the *full moon*, _keseh_, is spelled with the very same consonants; not a resemblance, but letter-identity in the text itself.
The link:/books/symbolic-language/the-pearl/[Pearls of Wisdom] chapter weighs the identity in full, verse by verse.

[discrete]
=== The Mount of the Congregation

__“I will sit also upon the *mount of the congregation*.”__
No English reader can do anything with that phrase, which should itself raise suspicion.
The Hebrew is _har moed_.
_Moed_ is one of the most load-bearing words in the Torah: the *appointed times* — __“the feasts (_moedim_) of the LORD, which ye shall proclaim to be holy convocations”__ (Leviticus 23:2); the lights of the fourth day were hung __“for signs, and for seasons (_moedim_)”__ (Genesis 1:14); the moon appointed for the seasons (Psalm 104:19); the tabernacle itself is the _ohel moed_, the tent of *meeting* — of the appointed time.
And _har_, the mountain, this book has already defined from one verse: a sym:sym-mountain[mountain] is a seat of ruling authority, a kingdom — not a geological feature (the full case study waits in Part Six).

Put the phrase back together and it is not scenery: _har moed_ is *the seat of authority over the appointed times* — the office the fourth day of creation assigned to the faithful lights of heaven.
That is the chair he announces he will sit in.
The being who produces darkness intends to govern the calendar.

[discrete]
=== The Sides of the North

__“…in the *sides of the north*.”__
Again the English is opaque, and again the Hebrew is not.
__“Sides”__ is _yarketh_ — not a side but the *farthest recess* of a thing: the innermost hold of Jonah's ship (Jonah 1:5), the deepest __“sides of the cave”__ where David hid (1 Samuel 24:3).
And __“north”__ is _tsaphon_, whose root is _to hide_ — Strong's: __“properly, *hidden*, i.e. dark; used only of the north as a quarter (gloomy and unknown)”__ — the meaning the glossary's sym:sym-north[north] entry carries.
A compass direction is a strange address for a being already in the heavens; __“the uttermost recesses of the hidden dark”__ is not strange at all — for a moon, it is a *phase*.
The farthest extent of the moon's darkness is the dark conjunction: the one point of the month when the moon stands invisible beside the sun and cannot be seen from the whole face of the earth.

Assemble the two lines: he seats himself on the authority over the appointed times, *operating from the far recess of the darkness* — he takes the full moon's office and works it from the opposite phase.
A calendar begun at the invisible moon instead of the full: the counterfeit is that specific.

[discrete]
=== Silencing the Greatest Light

__“I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will be like the most High.”__
Two flattenings hide the summit of the plan.
The word for __“clouds,”__ _av_, is not the white drift of an afternoon sky; Strong's defines it __“properly, an *envelope*, i.e. *darkness*… specifically a (scud) cloud”__ — a thick covering.
And __“I will be like”__ — _eddammeh_ — is written with the consonants of two verbs the dictionary keeps side by side: _damah_, __“to compare, to resemble,”__ and _damah_, __“to be dumb or *silent*; hence, to fail or perish; transitively, *to destroy*.”__
The translators chose __“be like,”__ and once __“the most High”__ is read as a title of God, no other choice survives — who can silence God?
But _elyon_ is simply __“the highest, the uppermost”__ before it is a title; and the psalmist has already told us what the highest light is: __“the LORD God is a sym:sym-sun[sun] and shield”__ (Psalm 84:11) — the greater light that rules the day, the Father of lights (Genesis 1:16; James 1:17).

Now read the line inside the plan.
What, in the whole sky, can rise at the height of a thick dark covering and *silence the greatest light*?
One thing only: the fully darkened moon, at the height of a total eclipse of the sun — the single phase from which the moon can blot the sun out.
The heavens __“declare the glory of God… day unto day uttereth *speech*”__ (Psalm 19:1-2); the summit of the counterfeit is to stop that speech — the day God threatens in the same vocabulary: __“I will cause the sym:sym-sun[sun] *to go down at noon*, and I will darken the earth in the clear day”__ (Amos 8:9).

Every word has now earned its place, and the passage stops explaining itself in circles; it becomes what testimony read out of a heart should be: specific, sequential, and checkable.

[discrete]
=== To Change Times and Laws

Checkable — because Daniel names the same agenda without a figure: the enemy of the saints __“shall think to *change times and laws*”__ (Daniel 7:25).
And Isaiah names the result in his opening line: the fallen one __“didst *weaken the nations*.”__
How does a counterfeit calendar weaken nations?
Not by force — by deception, the only weapon the passage requires.
The sym:sym-stars[stars] are the righteous, and stars keep time; if the righteous can be brought to reckon the appointed times from the moon's darkness instead of its fulness, then the people of God themselves keep the feasts of the LORD on days the LORD never appointed — sincere, diligent, and elsewhere.
The faithful witness's office (Psalm 89:37) is usurped; the Father's voice is eclipsed at the summit; and the stars still shine — but they follow the wrong moon.
Revelation shows where that ends: at the sixth seal __“the sym:sym-stars[stars] of heaven fell unto the earth, even as a fig tree casteth her *untimely figs*, when she is shaken of a *mighty* sym:sym-wind[wind]”__ (Revelation 6:13).
An untimely fig is fruit set out of season — borne on the wrong _moed_ — and the wind that shakes the tree is God's executing agent; the link:/books/symbolic-language/the-four-winds/[Four Winds] chapter will name it.
Stars that keep the counterfeit calendar are fruit grown at the counterfeit time, and when the tree is shaken, it is the untimely that drop.

Scripture does not treat that as a technicality.
The hallowed times are the *sign* of belonging — __“hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sym:sym-sign[sign] between me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God”__ (Ezekiel 20:20) — and their corruption carries national consequences written in fire (Jeremiah 17:27).

There is one more line in the record, and it answers him.
He chose for his address __“the recesses (_yarketh_) of the hidden dark”__ — and the sentence handed down answers him in his own word: __“thou shalt be brought down to hell, to the *recesses* (_yarketh_) *of the pit*”__ (Isaiah 14:15).
He asked for the uttermost dark, and the Judge granted the request.
The last chapter met that prison in the book Jude quotes by name — blackness reserved for stars that *did not come forth at their appointed times* — and here stands their prince, the first light to break its station, sentenced in his own word.

The counterfeit is precise, and its precision is the encouragement: no one forges a worthless document.
A plan this careful — the office of the faithful witness, the seat over the _moedim_, the one phase that can silence the sun — testifies to what the original is worth.
The lights still keep their appointments (Genesis 1:14), the witness in the sky is still faithful (Psalm 89:37), and the question the plan forces on every reader is the one this book keeps arriving at from every direction: whose calendar do your feet keep — the appointed times of the LORD, or the appointed times of the one who works from the dark?

And that question is not bolted onto the sym:sym-gospel[gospel] from outside; it lives inside it — feet and all.
The verse this Part opened with binds the good tidings to the feasts (Nahum 1:15) — the twin of the verse Paul quotes for the preaching of the gospel (Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15) — and the gospel chapter of this book already heard what the hearers are told to do about the news.
The good tidings announce the covenant, and the covenant keeps His feasts — which is why the plan God read out of the enemy's heart aims exactly there.

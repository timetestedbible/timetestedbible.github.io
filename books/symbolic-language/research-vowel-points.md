## The stakes (why this is not academic)

- **Obedience:** חלב sits inside a standing command — "ye eat neither FAT
  nor blood" (Lev 3:17). Which food a verse names decides what a servant
  may eat. Pointing decides readings; readings decide practice.
- **Doctrine:** the book's own findings ride on unpointed readings —
  keseh/kisse (throne = full moon), helel (bringer of darkness), shachar
  (the dark before dawn), Job 22:30 ("island of the innocent"),
  chalav/cheleb (milk/fat, Ezek 34:3), ishah/esh (woman/fire). If points
  are canon, several chapters lose their second witness.

## The argument skeleton

1. **The points are late.** The consonantal text is ancient; the pointing
   system is Masoretic, added roughly the 7th-10th centuries AD — a
   thousand years and more after the prophets. What the prophets wrote,
   and what the scribes counted jot by jot, was letters.
2. **The oldest witnesses have no points.** The Dead Sea Scrolls carry
   bare consonants; the Septuagint (3rd-1st c. BC) is a WITNESS TO HOW
   THE LETTERS WERE READ a millennium before the Masoretes — where LXX
   and MT pointing diverge (Ezek 34:3 milk/fat), the LXX testifies to an
   older reading tradition.
3. **The apostles ratify the older witness.** The New Testament quotes
   the LXX as Scripture — the writer of Hebrews above all (his milk/meat
   vocabulary, his Psalm citations). The apostolic practice treats the
   pre-Masoretic reading as the word of God.
4. **Jesus' own canon marker is consonantal.** "One jot or one tittle
   shall in no wise pass from the law" (Matt 5:18) — the jot (yod) and
   tittle (letter-stroke) are parts of LETTERS. The promise of
   preservation is pledged to the consonants; the points are not in it.
5. **The points are a commentary, not the text** — often right, worth
   weighing (the Masoretes were careful men), but a tradition of men in
   exactly the category the book already handles: like translation, like
   the seminary dictionary — an interpretive layer to be tested against
   the letters, never the judge of them.
6. **The method already has the rule** (memory: consonantal readings
   valid): a consonant-permitted reading may stand even against the
   pointing; wordplay is a second dimension of meaning the points
   flatten.

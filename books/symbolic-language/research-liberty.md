# Research: Liberty — the corpus behind ch. 25

Done 2026-07-02 for the chapter. The Hebrew liberty-word is tiny and
perfectly focused; the Greek side pairs freedom against slavery.

## The corpus

- ***deror*** H1865 — **7 occurrences, all jubilee-family**: Lev 25:10
  ("proclaim liberty throughout all the land" — the jubilee, and the
  Liberty Bell's inscription); Isa 61:1 ("proclaim liberty to the
  captives" — the verse Jesus claimed at Nazareth, Luke 4:18-21); Jer
  34:8, 15, 17 (2×) — the betrayed proclamation; Ezek 46:17 ("the year of
  liberty"); Ex 30:23 (*mor-deror*, "pure/flowing myrrh" — Strong's:
  "freedom; hence spontaneity of outflow, and so clear").
- ***deror*** H1866 — Strong's: "**the same as H1865, applied to a
  bird**" — the SWALLOW: Ps 84:3 ("the swallow a nest for herself…
  **even thine altars**, O LORD of hosts"); Prov 26:2 ("as the swallow by
  flying"). The liberty-word is the free bird — and the free bird nests
  at the altar.
- *chophshi* H2670 (freed, of slaves) 17×; Greek *eleutheria* G1657 10×,
  *eleutheros* G1658 23×.

## The shape of the symbol

1. **Liberty is the jubilee release** — proclaimed on a set day, by the
   King's decree: debts cancelled, slaves loosed, every man returned to
   his inheritance (Lev 25:10; Ezek 46:17). Jesus opened His ministry by
   claiming exactly this proclamation (Isa 61:1-2 / Luke 4:18-21) — the
   gospel is a liberty-proclamation (ch. 6).
2. **The slaver is sin** — "Whosoever committeth sin is the servant of
   sin" (John 8:34); "the truth shall make you free" (8:32); "if the Son
   therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed" (8:36).
3. **Freedom is a change of masters, not an absence of one** — "being
   then made FREE from sin, ye became the SERVANTS of righteousness"
   (Rom 6:18; also 6:22 "servants to God"); "as free… but as the servants
   of God" (1 Pet 2:16); "by love serve one another" (Gal 5:13).
4. **The law is liberty's constitution** — "the perfect LAW OF LIBERTY"
   (James 1:25; judged by it, 2:12); "where the Spirit of the Lord is,
   there is liberty" (2 Cor 3:17); "the glorious liberty of the children
   of God" (Rom 8:21); "stand fast therefore in the liberty" (Gal 5:1).
   Liberty is release UNDER God's rule — the opposite of autonomy.
5. **The counterfeit** — "While they promise them LIBERTY, they
   themselves are the servants of corruption" (2 Pet 2:19); liberty "for
   an occasion to the flesh" (Gal 5:13); "for a cloke of maliciousness"
   (1 Pet 2:16).
6. **The betrayed proclamation** — Jer 34:8-17: Zedekiah proclaims
   liberty (deror), the princes re-enslave their brothers, and God
   answers in kind: "ye have not hearkened unto me, in proclaiming
   liberty… behold, I proclaim a liberty for you… to the sword, to the
   pestilence, and to the famine" (34:17). Deny the release, and the
   release is granted to your destroyers.

## Meaning

Liberty = the King's jubilee release: out of the slaver's house (sin,
Egypt, Babylon) into the household and law of God. Its home is the altar
(the deror's nest); its constitution is the law of liberty; its
counterfeit is autonomy, which Scripture calls a return to bondage.

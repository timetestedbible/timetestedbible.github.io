# Research: The Land Itself Is a Census (ch. 20 spatial line)

Source: blog posts "The Population of the Renewed Earth" (2026-02-20) and
"Last Call Before the Fall" (2026-02-23) — the chart's "Vine and Fig Tree
(Mic 4:4)" bar. All scripture verified against kjv.json 2026-07-03.

## Scripture (verified)

- Gen 15:18 — "Unto thy seed have I given this land, from the river of
  Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates." Borders drawn.
- Ezek 47–48 — the same territory surveyed into measured tribal allotments.
- Rev 20:4, 6 — first resurrection; "reigned with Christ a thousand years";
  "priests of God and of Christ… shall reign with him a thousand years."
- Ps 37:11 / Matt 5:5 — "the meek shall inherit the earth"; Hebrew erets
  (H776) = earth AND land; Psalm 37's context is the covenant land
  (inheritance promised 5× in the psalm: vv. 9, 11, 22, 29, 34).
- 1 Ki 4:25 — "Judah and Israel dwelt safely, every man under his vine and
  under his fig tree… all the days of Solomon" — FIRST occurrence of the
  phrase; a dated, real population. (1 Ki 4:20: "many, as the sand which is
  by the sea… eating and drinking.")
- Mic 4:4 — "they shall sit every man under his vine and under his fig
  tree" — the kingdom promise quotes Solomon's census-density.
- Ex 23:29-30 — "I will not drive them out from before thee in one year;
  lest the land become desolate… By little and little… until thou be
  increased, and inherit the land." The land has a capacity; God fills it
  gradually.
- Isa 65:20-23 — millennial mortal nations still build, plant, bear
  children (population grows into the land over the 1,000 years).

## Numbers (arithmetic checked)

- Territory: traditional Promised Land ≈ 300,000 sq mi (192M acres);
  Nile-to-Euphrates "Greater Israel" ≈ 900,000 sq mi (576M acres).
  (Blog's estimates; order-of-magnitude, clearly labeled "roughly/some".)
- Solomon benchmark: ~5.5M people on ~15,000 sq mi settled territory
  = 9.6M acres / 5.5M ≈ 1.7 acres/person (blog: 1.6–1.75 depending on
  population estimate 5–6M).
- Capacity at 1.7 ac/person: 192M/1.7 ≈ 113M (~110M); 576M/1.7 ≈ 339M
  (~330M).
- Everyone born since Christ ≈ 43 billion (covenant-open era; the blog's
  derivation from historical birth-rate data; PRB's 117B all-history figure
  assumes a 200k-year timeline): 192M ac/43B = 195 sq ft; 576M/43B =
  583 sq ft → table "190-580 sq ft".
- Professing Christians alive ≈ 2 billion: 192M/2B = 0.096 ac;
  576M/2B = 0.288 ac → "0.1-0.3 acre".
- Remnant at ~0.2%: of living 8B → 16M; of 43B post-Christ births → 86M.
  192M/86M = 2.2 ac; 576M/16M = 36 ac → "2-36 acres".
- Convergence-table row: "fits at ~0.2%; breaks past ~0.8%" — hard cap =
  capacity/births: 339M/43B ≈ 0.79%; the chart bar spans ~0.18-0.24%.

# Research: Gospel — the full corpus and its patterns

Substantiation for ch. 6 (Gospel), reworked 2026-07-02. Method: every use of
the word, patterns from context (the author's framing: test the common
definition against the corpus).

## The corpus

- KJV **"gospel": 98 verses**; **"good/glad tidings": 15 more**.
- Greek: *euangelion* (the noun) G2098 **74×**; *euangelizō* (the verb,
  "preach the gospel / bring good tidings") G2097 **52×**.
- Hebrew: *basar* H1319 ("bring tidings") **21×**; *besorah* H1309 ("the
  tidings") **6×**.

## The exhibit (the common definition)

Ask an AI for the gospel in a sentence — the answer below is Grok's (author-supplied, 2026-07-02, used
as the chapter's opening): "The Gospel is the good news that Jesus Christ,
the Son of God, died for our sins according to the Scriptures, was buried,
and rose again on the third day, so that through repentance and faith in
Him alone we can be forgiven, reconciled to God, and given eternal life."
Claimed basis: 1 Cor 15:1-4. The chapter tests this against the corpus.

## Pattern 1 — the titles the gospel actually wears

"gospel **of the kingdom**" 4× (Matt 4:23; 9:35; 24:14; Mark 1:14 "of the
kingdom of God"); "gospel of God" 7×; "gospel of Christ" 11×; "gospel of
peace" 2×; "gospel of your salvation" 1×; "everlasting gospel" 1× (Rev
14:6); "my gospel" (Paul) 3×. **Never once**: "gospel of the death and
resurrection," "gospel of the cross," or "gospel of forgiveness." The one
CONTENT-title it carries is the kingdom — and the last occurrence in the
Olivet discourse is still "this gospel of the kingdom… then shall the end
come" (Matt 24:14).

## Pattern 2 — the Hebrew root: victory-news that must be published

*Basar/besorah* in narrative = battle/deliverance news: the runner from the
field (2 Sam 18:19-31); the lepers at the lifted siege — "this day is a day
of *good tidings*, and we hold our peace: if we tarry till the morning
light, some mischief will come upon us" (**2 Kings 7:9** — the siege of
Samaria broken, the enemy fled, food free; the news is criminal to sit on);
"The Lord gave the word: great was the company of those that *published*
it" (Ps 68:11). The gospel-word is a herald's word before it is a doctrinal
one.

## Pattern 3 — the content stated by the prophets and by Jesus

- **Isa 52:7** — "him that bringeth good tidings… that saith unto Zion,
  *Thy God reigneth*" (LXX renders with *euangelizō*; Paul quotes it as
  gospel-preaching, Rom 10:15). The announcement upon the MOUNTAINS.
- **Nahum 1:15** — the twin verse, and it commands: "O Judah, **keep thy
  solemn feasts, perform thy vows**" — the herald's cry includes keeping
  the appointed feasts (law-positive, inside the gospel-verse).
- **Isa 61:1-2 / Luke 4:18-21** — Jesus' own commission text at Nazareth:
  "anointed me to preach good tidings/the gospel to the poor… to proclaim
  the **acceptable year of the LORD**" — the jubilee proclamation
  (liberty, release, land-rest; ties to Time Tested Tradition's jubilee
  reckoning). "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears."
- **Rev 14:6-7** — the everlasting gospel's stated content: "Fear God, and
  give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come: and worship him
  that made heaven, and earth, and the sea" — no cross clause; Creator,
  judgment, worship.

## Pattern 4 — the gospel is OBEYED

"They have not all **obeyed** the gospel" (Rom 10:16); vengeance on "them
that **obey not** the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ" (2 Thess 1:8); "what
shall the end be of them that **obey not** the gospel of God?" (1 Pet
4:17). Information is believed; a king's proclamation with terms is obeyed.
This alone breaks the info-only definition.

## Pattern 5 — the gospel is older than the cross

- Jesus preached it for years before Calvary (Matt 4:17, 23; Mark 1:14-15).
- Preached at Sinai: Heb 4:2 ("unto us was the gospel preached, as well as
  unto them") + Ex 19:6 (the kingdom proclaimed).
- Pre-preached to Abraham: Gal 3:8 (*proeuangelizomai*).
- "Everlasting" (Rev 14:6) — cannot be everlasting and begin at AD 30.

## Pattern 6 — covenant renewal, not replacement (retained from prior draft)

*Chadash* = renew/repair (Ps 51:10; 2 Chr 24:4); Jer 31:31-33 ("my covenant
THEY brake"; same law, new location — the heart); Matt 5:17 *plēroō* = fill
full vs *kataluō* destroy; Rom 3:31 "we establish the law"; Isa 2:3/Mic 4:2
the law from Zion.

## Pattern 7 — the cross's true place

John 19:30 (the ratification); 1 Cor 15:3-4 read whole ("according to the
scriptures" 2×; the letter defends the resurrection, not redefining the
gospel's whole); Rom 1:1-4 (promised afore; seed of David; resurrection =
enthronement); Gal 1:8 one gospel only — the same one Abraham heard.

## Conclusion for the chapter

The AI-average sentence names the engine (the atonement) and forgets the
kingdom it drives; on the corpus, the gospel is the herald's cry that God
reigns — the kingdom announced, its covenant renewed onto hearts, its terms
obeyed — of which the cross and resurrection are the securing and the
enthronement. Test result: the theory fails as a WHOLE-definition, stands
as a part.

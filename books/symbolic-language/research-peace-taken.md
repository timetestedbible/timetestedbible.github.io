# The red horse tested — peace taken from the earth, and the four winds that gather (2026-07-08)

The theory as posed: in Rev 6:4, **PEACE = REST = NOAH = the covenant people, taken
from the earth by a great SPIRIT (the sword of the Spirit)** — a second hearing
alongside the literal (real war, a mighty weapon), per the book's own Spoken Once,
Heard Twice doctrine; and Rev 6:8's "beasts of the earth" are the beast-*kingdoms*;
and Matt 24:31's gathering of the elect "from the four winds" is the same event the
four-winds chapter describes — the commissioned spirits, released after the sealing,
gathering the covenant people out. Verses verified against kjv_strongs.txt,
greek_nt.txt, hg.txt, wlc.txt.

---

## The verdict

**Confirmed at both hearings, with the strongest links stated in-text.** The
peace = rest equation is made by Scripture in a naming (1 Chr 22:9); the
taken-away-into-peace-and-rest structure is one oracle (Isa 57:1-2); Noah is bound
to the covenant of peace by name (Isa 54:9-10); the sword = word is decoded
(Eph 6:17), and Hebrews sets the rest-people and the dividing sword three verses
apart (Heb 4:9, 12). The Hebrew Gospels supply two unasked-for witnesses: Matt
24:31 gathers the chosen from the four **ruchot** (spirits — Zechariah's own word
for the four winds), and Mark 13:27 gathers them from the four **kanphot** of the
earth — the garment-corner word: the gathering takes the earth by its hem. And the
Greek of Rev 6:8 marks the beasts apart from the other three killers: sword,
famine, death take ἐν (*with*, instruments); the beasts take ὑπό (*by*, agents) —
and the word is θηρίον, the very word of Revelation's Beast. The fourth killer is
the beast-empires.

The glossary already holds every piece separately: Peace ("the covenant made
whole"), Beast ("a nation or kingdom"), Sword ("war **and** the word of judgment" —
the double hearing is already in the entry), Wind ("Spirit — ruach"). This test
joins them.

## Tier 1 — the stated equations

- **1 Chr 22:9** — peace = rest, stated in a naming: "a son... shall be a **man of
  rest**; and I will give him **rest** from all his enemies... for his name shall
  be **Solomon** [shalom], and I will give **peace** and quietness unto Israel."
  The man of rest is named Peace. One office, two words.
- **Isa 57:1-2** — the theory's whole structure in one oracle: "the righteous is
  **taken away from the evil to come**. He shall enter into **peace**: they shall
  **rest** in their beds." Taken away → peace → rest. The removal of the righteous
  *is* their entry into peace; the earth that remains has lost its peace in losing
  them.
- **Gen 5:29; 8:4** — Noah (*noach*) named for rest/comfort; the ark **rested**
  (*vatanach*) in the seventh month. The rest-man rides out the judgment and rests.
- **Isa 54:9-10** — Noah bound to the covenant of peace by name: "this is as the
  **waters of Noah** unto me... neither shall the **covenant of my peace be
  removed**." Note the inversion against Rev 6:4: peace is taken *from the earth*;
  the covenant of peace is *never removed from the covenant people*. They do not
  lose the peace — the earth loses them.
- **Eph 6:17** — "the **sword of the Spirit**, which is the **word of God**" —
  decoded in-text. With Isa 11:4 ("with the **breath** [ruach] of his lips shall he
  slay the wicked") and 2 Thess 2:8 ("consume with the **spirit of his mouth**"):
  sword, word, breath, wind — one instrument.
- **Heb 4:9 + 4:12** — "There remaineth therefore a **rest** to the people of God"
  — and three verses later, "the word of God is... sharper than any twoedged
  **sword**, piercing even to the **dividing asunder**." The rest-people and the
  sword that divides them out, side by side in one argument.
- **Matt 10:34** — the red rider's two clauses in the Lord's own logion: "I came
  not to send **peace**, but a **sword**" — and the sword's work in context
  (10:35-39) is division of households: the word separating person from person.
  The Hebrew Matthew: "I have not come to put anything in the earth **but a
  sword**."

## Rev 6:8 — killed *by* the beasts of the earth

The pale rider's kill-list is Ezekiel's formula — "my **four sore judgments**...
the sword, and the famine, and the noisome beast, and the pestilence" (Ezek 14:21)
— a covenant-judgment set, not a zoology. And the Greek sharpens it: ἀποκτεῖναι
**ἐν** ῥομφαίᾳ καὶ **ἐν** λιμῷ καὶ **ἐν** θανάτῳ καὶ **ὑπὸ** τῶν **θηρίων** τῆς
γῆς — sword, famine, death are instruments (*with*); the beasts are agents (*by*).
θηρίον is the word Revelation gives the Beast itself (13:1ff), and Daniel decodes
it in-text: "These great beasts, which are four, are **four kings**" (Dan 7:17).
The fourth killer is the beast-kingdoms — and Daniel even gives the winds their
role in raising them: "the **four winds** of the heaven strove upon the sea. And
four great **beasts** came up" (Dan 7:2-3). The winds stir; the kingdoms rise; the
kingdoms kill. The horsemen's downstream instruments include the empires
themselves.

## The alignment — Matt 24 against the sixth seal

| Matt 24 | Rev 6-7 |
|---|---|
| "the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall" (24:29) | sixth seal: "the sun became black... the moon became as blood... the stars of heaven fell" (6:12-13) |
| "for the elect's sake those days shall be shortened" (24:22) | "Hurt not the earth... till we have sealed the servants of our God" (7:3) |
| "gather together his elect from the four winds" (24:31) | the four winds held at the four corners until the sealing (7:1-3) |

Same juncture, seen from both sides: Revelation shows the winds *held* until the
servants are sealed; Matthew shows the elect *gathered* at the four winds
immediately after the same cosmic signs. The chapter's corrected sequence (seals
commission; the sealing releases) is the hinge both texts turn on.

**The Hebrew Gospels' two witnesses:**

- **Matt 24:31 (hg.txt)** — "gather his chosen from the four **winds** (ruchot
  H7307) of the heavens": the four *spirits*, the very word of Zech 6:5. The Greek
  ἐκ τῶν ἀνέμων reads spatially ("from the four quarters"); the Hebrew witness
  restores the agents.
- **Mark 13:27 (hg.txt)** — "gather his chosen from the four **wings** (kanaph
  H3671) **of the earth**": the garment-corner word (Deut 22:12; Isa 11:12; Ezek
  7:2 — see research-river.md, the kanphot note). The earth wears a four-winged
  garment, and the gathering takes it by the hem — the covenant edge, the Wings
  chapter's own symbol.
- Supporting agency texts: the scattering was already wind-work — "I have spread
  you abroad **as the four winds**" (Zech 2:6); the regathering reverses it (Deut
  30:4); and the resurrection-breath *comes from the four winds* (Ezek 37:9) — the
  gathering of Matt 24:31 includes the dead, and the winds are how breath reaches
  them. The Four Winds chapter already holds the office: "scatter and gather
  peoples, raise the dead."

## The double hearing (Spoken Once, Heard Twice)

**First hearing — literal, and explicit in the verse:** war. "That they should
**kill one another**" (Rev 6:4b) is the rider's own clause; the μάχαιρα μεγάλη is
a real and mighty weapon, and the age has built weapons worthy of the adjective.
This hearing is not displaced by the second; the book's doctrine is that both
stand.

**Second hearing — the removal that explains the slaughter.** Read the Greek
precisely: ἐδόθη αὐτῷ λαβεῖν **τὴν εἰρήνην ἐκ** τῆς γῆς — "to take **the** peace
**out of** the earth" — definite article, extraction preposition. The Peace is a
definite thing resident in the earth, and it is *withdrawn*. The glossary already
defines it: peace is the covenant made whole — and Isa 54:10 fixes where it lives:
the covenant of peace rests on the covenant people. Take the rest-people out
(Isa 57:1-2) and the earth's peace leaves with them — *and then* they kill one
another, because the restraint is gone. That consequence-structure is Scripture's
own pattern: "I **cannot do any thing** till thou be come thither" (Gen 19:22 —
judgment waits on Lot's removal); the restrainer "taken out of the way," *and
then* the Wicked revealed (2 Thess 2:7-8); Noah enters, *then* the flood; the salt
removed, *then* the trampling (Matt 5:13). The days-of-Noah pattern the book
already carries — the covenant person secured, then the judgment loosed — is the
red rider's second hearing.

And the instrument fits both hearings at once: the great sword is war's weapon
*and* "the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God" — the divider (Heb 4:12)
that separates the rest-people out — wielded by a wind, which is a spirit, at the
juncture when the four ruchot gather the chosen. One rider, one sword, heard
twice: the earth hears war; the sealed hear the trumpet.

## Cautions (the range, honestly)

- **Peace/rest is structural, not lexical**: *shalom* and *noach* are different
  roots; the equation stands on 1 Chr 22:9's naming and Isa 57:2's parallelism —
  a thematic identity Scripture states, not a shared word. Say it that way.
- **The Greek of Matt 24:31 alone** reads "from the four winds" spatially; the
  agency reading rests on the Hebrew witness (ruchot/kanphot), Zech 2:6, and
  Ezek 37:9 together. Present it as the second witness restoring what the Greek
  flattened — the same argument-shape as the book's other Hebrew-Gospel readings.
- **One rider, two effects** is not a strain: the Four Winds chapter already gives
  the winds a dual office (deal death and breathe life, scatter and gather). The
  same release that gathers the sealed looses the nations on each other.
- **Isa 26:20-21** belongs to this cluster ("enter thou into thy chambers... until
  the indignation be overpast; for, behold, the LORD cometh out of his place to
  punish") — check the Shadow and Noah chapters before citing, both already work
  this ground.

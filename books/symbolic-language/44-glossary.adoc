This is the whole vocabulary in one place — a hundred and thirty-seven symbols, each with its meaning, a defining verse, and the chapter where Scripture establishes it; a few, marked _not yet proven_, are met in passing and still await their own study.
And it is the tip of the iceberg.
The Bible's symbolic language is larger than any one book can prove, and the method does not retire when the glossary ends: test the key words of every verse you come across.
What once took a lifetime of concordance work now takes minutes — pull every reference by key word, by the Hebrew or Greek word beneath the English, and by concept where the translators chose different words for the same thing; let Scripture's first use and plainest definition fix the meaning; and hold every hard verse to it.

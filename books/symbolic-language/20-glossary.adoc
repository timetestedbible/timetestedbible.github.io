This is the whole vocabulary in one place: every symbol the book uses, each with its meaning, a defining verse, and the chapter where Scripture establishes it. Entries marked _(not yet proven)_ are referenced in the text but have not yet been given their own demonstration; they are the working list of what is left to expand.

Axe:: The judgment that fells a fruitless nation — “the axe is laid unto the root of the trees” (Luke 3:9). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Babel / Babylon:: The self-made kingdom that builds a name against heaven — the covenant counterfeit. _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow (ch. 8)].

Beast:: A kingdom, the same power that rises from the sea (Daniel 7:17, 23). Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters (ch. 3)].

Belief, to believe:: To obey — a trusting, obedient response, not bare assent; the opposite of “believe” is _apeitheō_, to disobey (John 3:36). link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief (ch. 14)].

Blood:: The covenant-covering that cleanses — a life given to cover and wash (Revelation 7:14). link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments (ch. 11)].

Bow:: The covenant that binds a people together (Genesis 9:13). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow (ch. 8)].

Branches:: The members of a nation — broken off, or grafted in, while the tree itself stands (Romans 11:17). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Bread:: The word of God (Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4). link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless (ch. 13)].

Cedar:: A great Gentile empire (Ezekiel 31:3). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Crown:: The conqueror's rule, given to the rider who wins the nations (Revelation 6:2). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow (ch. 8)].

Darkness:: Concealed disobedience — deeds hidden from the light (John 3:19-20). link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness (ch. 16)].

Day:: The time of light — exposure and accountability, when deeds are seen and tested; the season to walk, work, and watch. The children of light are the children of the day (Genesis 1:5; John 9:4; 1 Thessalonians 5:5). link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness (ch. 16)].

Door, gate:: The destination of the way — the entrance to the house of God, marked on the posts. link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life (ch. 6)].

Dragon:: A devouring kingdom (Pharaoh, Ezekiel 29:3; Babylon, Jeremiah 51:34). link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah (ch. 4)].

Eating:: Taking in and partaking — of the word, or of the nations' defilement (John 6:51, 63). link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless (ch. 13)].

Elam:: Babylon under another name — the same self-bound kingdom God breaks (Jeremiah 49:35). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow (ch. 8)].

Ephraim:: The scattered firstborn whose seed becomes the fulness of the nations (Genesis 48:19). link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim (ch. 17)].

Faith:: Faithfulness — trusting obedience that acts; “faith without works is dead” (Habakkuk 2:4; James 2:17). link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief (ch. 14)].

Fatherless, orphan:: God's covenant people left without their Father — the remnant (Lamentations 5:3). link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless (ch. 13)].

Fear of the LORD:: Reverent awe expressed as keeping the commandments and departing from evil (Ecclesiastes 12:13). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fear-of-the-lord/[The Fear of the Lord (ch. 18)].

Feet:: The walk — a man's conduct, his _way_; washed clean while the body stays bathed (John 13:10). link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments (ch. 11)].

Fig leaves:: Self-made righteousness, cut to one's own measure — what is “right in your own eyes” (Genesis 3:7; Proverbs 14:12). link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments (ch. 11)].

Fish (great fish, sea-monster):: A devouring kingdom that swallows and is forced to give back (Jeremiah 51:34; Ezekiel 32:2). link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah (ch. 4)].

Fish (two fishes):: Witnesses sent two by two, the fishers of men (Mark 6:7; Matthew 4:19). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless (ch. 13)].

Flood:: An invading army (Daniel 9:26; Jeremiah 47:2). link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters (ch. 3)].

Fool:: One who hears God's word and will not do it — the lawless, not the unintelligent (Psalm 14:1; Matthew 7:26). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise (ch. 15)].

Four winds:: The four spirits of heaven — God's executing agents, shown as the four horsemen (Zechariah 6:5). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-four-winds/[The Four Winds (ch. 9)].

Fruit:: A nation's works, by which it is judged — “every good tree bringeth forth good fruit” (Matthew 7:17). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Garden:: A planted stand of trees — a grouping of nations (Ezekiel 31:9). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Garment:: Righteousness — the righteous acts of the saints (Revelation 19:8, ASV). link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments (ch. 11)].

Gospel:: The gospel of the kingdom — the renewed covenant, the law going forth from Zion (Isaiah 52:7). link:/books/symbolic-language/gospel/[Gospel (ch. 5)].

Grafting (grafted in):: The wild branch — the stranger, the Gentile — received into the covenant tree (Romans 11:17). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Grass:: The individual mortal — the single person (Isaiah 40:6-7). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)] and link:/books/symbolic-language/wings/[Wings (ch. 12)].

Harlot:: The covenant-breaker who wears the form of covenant without its substance (Revelation 17). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah (ch. 4)].

Harvest:: The end of the age, when the field is reaped (Matthew 13:39). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim (ch. 17)].

Heart of the earth:: Jerusalem — the navel and heart of the land (Ezekiel 38:12). link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah (ch. 4)].

Heaven:: The dwelling and government of God; the hope is heaven coming down, not man going up (Revelation 21:2-3). _(Not yet proven.)_ Promised a chapter in link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life (ch. 6)].

Highway:: The way of holiness — the prepared road home (Isaiah 35:8). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life (ch. 6)].

Horse, white horse:: The mount of an executing spirit; the conqueror who wins the nations by the bow (Revelation 6:2). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/the-four-winds/[The Four Winds (ch. 9)].

Image of metals (gold, silver, brass, clay):: The succession of gentile world-kingdoms, shattered by the stone-kingdom of God (Daniel 2:32-45). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain (ch. 2)].

Instruction:: The discipline of the law, received and kept — what only fools despise (Proverbs 1:7; 23:23). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise (ch. 15)].

Iron:: The fourth kingdom — Rome (Daniel 2:40). Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain (ch. 2)].

Island, isle:: A set-apart, sanctified people — the covenant-rock risen out of the sea of nations (a mountain breaking the surface), waiting for the law that sanctifies it; "the island of the innocent," delivered to safety while the kingdoms fall (Genesis 10:5; Job 22:30; Isaiah 42:4; Revelation 16:20). link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters (ch. 3)].

Israel:: The new name of the one who prevails with God — covenant and character, not blood (Genesis 32:28; Romans 9:6). link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim (ch. 17)].

Jacob:: The crooked, supplanting human heart that God transforms (Genesis 27:36; Jeremiah 17:9). link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim (ch. 17)].

Knowing, to know:: Covenant relationship proven by obedience — the intimate “knew” of marriage; a stranger is one not known (Genesis 4:1; Matthew 7:23; 1 John 2:3-4). link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief (ch. 14)].

Lamb:: Christ, the offering whose blood covers the guilty (John 1:29; Genesis 3:21). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments (ch. 11)].

Lamp:: The commandment (Proverbs 6:23). link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness (ch. 16)].

Life (the life):: Covenant existence — the life the law produces (John 14:6; Matthew 7:14). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life (ch. 6)].

Light:: The law lived visibly — good works others can see (Proverbs 6:23; Matthew 5:16). link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness (ch. 16)].

Living creature:: A nation — as the beasts of Daniel's sea are kingdoms and the living creatures of Revelation peoples. _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow (ch. 8)].

Living water:: The Spirit and word of God going out to give life (Jeremiah 2:13; John 7:38). _(Not yet proven.)_ Promised a chapter in link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters (ch. 3)].

Lost sheep:: The scattered house of Israel, sought and regathered (Matthew 15:24). link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim (ch. 17)].

Love:: Keeping the commandments — “this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments” (John 14:15; 1 John 5:3). link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief (ch. 14)].

Marriage, bride:: The covenant union of God and His people (Ephesians 5:23-32; Revelation 19:7). link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments (ch. 11)].

Meat:: Doing the will of God — the solid, full teaching (John 4:34). link:/books/symbolic-language/introduction/[Introduction (ch. 1)].

Milk:: The simplified first message, for babes (Isaiah 28:9; Hebrews 5:12-13). link:/books/symbolic-language/introduction/[Introduction (ch. 1)].

Mountain:: A kingdom — the seat of ruling authority (Jeremiah 51:25; Daniel 2:35). link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain (ch. 2)].

Naked, nakedness:: Without covenant covering — exposed in one's breach (Ezekiel 16:8; Genesis 3:7). link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments (ch. 11)].

Name:: The covenant itself (2 Samuel 7:13; Exodus 20:7). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-name/[The Name (ch. 7)].

Net:: The gospel's gathering of all kinds out of the sea of peoples (Matthew 13:47-48). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters (ch. 3)].

New name:: The renewed covenant, known — kept — only by the one who receives it (Revelation 2:17). link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief (ch. 14)].

Night:: The time of darkness — hiddenness and sleep, when opportunity closes; the children of the night sleep and are drunken, doing their deeds in the dark (Genesis 1:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:5-7). link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness (ch. 16)].

Nineveh:: A great city of the sea — gentile peoples who repent and rise in judgment (Matthew 12:41). link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah (ch. 4)].

Oil:: The pressed, tested fruit of the olive — proven works that make light; not the Spirit (Exodus 27:20; Matthew 25:3-9). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise (ch. 15)].

Olive, vine, fig:: Israel, the covenant people (Jeremiah 11:16; Hosea 9:10). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Passover:: The blood-marked deliverance, kept even by the stranger who joins (Exodus 12:48). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless (ch. 13)].

Remnant:: The faithful few who glean the edge — widow, fatherless, stranger, as Ruth did (Ruth 2). link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless (ch. 13)].

Rock:: The covenant — the Rock that followed Israel “was Christ,” the covenant in person; the stone the builders refused, made the cornerstone (1 Corinthians 10:4; Psalm 118:22). link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain (ch. 2)].

Root:: A nation's foundation and origin — the patriarchs, the covenant that makes it holy (Romans 11:16-17). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Sackcloth:: The garment of mourning and repentance (Jonah 3:5). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah (ch. 4)].

Sand:: The unstable foundation, and the countless multitude (Matthew 7:26; Genesis 22:17). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise (ch. 15)].

Scarlet:: Covenant blood made wearable — and counterfeited by those who wear the form without the substance (Isaiah 1:18; Revelation 17:4). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments (ch. 11)].

Sea, waters:: Peoples and nations — the restless, unrooted mass (Revelation 17:15). link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters (ch. 3)].

Seal (mark in the forehead):: The mark of covenant ownership set on God's servants before judgment (Revelation 7:3; 14:1). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/the-four-winds/[The Four Winds (ch. 9)].

Serpent:: The deceiver; the one who strips the covenant covering (Genesis 3:1). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters (ch. 3)].

Sheep:: The covenant people, who hear the Shepherd's voice and follow (John 10:27). link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief (ch. 14)].

Stone (cut without hands):: A covenant cut by God — for a covenant is _cut_ (Hebrew _karat_) — grown into the kingdom that breaks the world-empires and fills the earth (Daniel 2:45; 2:35). link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain (ch. 2)].

Storm, tempest:: The nations in turmoil — the raging sea, stilled at Christ's word, “Peace, be still” (Mark 4:39). link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters (ch. 3)].

Stranger:: A non-citizen of God's commonwealth — one outside the covenant, drawn in, or, once in, made a stranger to the world (Ephesians 2:12, 19). link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless (ch. 13)].

Sword:: War and the word of judgment — the great sword that takes peace from the earth (Revelation 6:4); the flaming sword that guards the way (Genesis 3:24). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-four-winds/[The Four Winds (ch. 9)].

Tree:: A nation or people (Daniel 4:22; Romans 11:17). link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees (ch. 10)].

Truth (the truth):: God's law — the standard (Psalm 119:142). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life (ch. 6)].

Tzitzit:: The tasselled corners of the garment that carry the commandments (Numbers 15:38-39). link:/books/symbolic-language/wings/[Wings (ch. 12)].

Understanding:: Discernment of good from evil, gained by departing from evil — “a good understanding have all they that do his commandments” (Job 28:28; Psalm 111:10). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise (ch. 15)].

Valley:: The low and humbled — the opposite of the mountain-kingdom; “every valley shall be exalted” (Isaiah 40:4). _(Not yet proven.)_ Referenced in link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain (ch. 2)].

Virgin:: The chaste bride — Greek _parthenos_, espoused to one husband; the opposite of the harlot who goes after many (2 Corinthians 11:2; Matthew 25:1). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise (ch. 15)].

Voice:: The words of the one who speaks; the Shepherd's voice is His words (John 10:27). link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief (ch. 14)].

Way (the way):: Covenant conduct — the walk (Jeremiah 6:16; Matthew 7:14). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life (ch. 6)].

Widow:: God's covenant people cut off from their Husband (Lamentations 1:1; Isaiah 54:5). link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless (ch. 13)].

Wind:: Spirit — _ruach_ (Zechariah 6:5). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-four-winds/[The Four Winds (ch. 9)].

Wings (kanaph):: The covenant edge — wing, skirt, border, corner — where the law is bound and refuge is found (Ezekiel 16:8). link:/books/symbolic-language/wings/[Wings (ch. 12)].

Wisdom:: The fear of the LORD lived out — the doing of the commandments; “the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom” (Proverbs 9:10; Psalm 111:10; Deuteronomy 4:6). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise (ch. 15)].

Wise:: One who hears God's word and does it, who fears the LORD (Matthew 7:24; Psalm 111:10). link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise (ch. 15)].

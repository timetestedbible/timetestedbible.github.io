# Research: The Ten Virgins — espoused companions, not the bride

Commissioned 2026-07-06 after the author flagged the contradiction in the
prior reading: if chastity = faithfulness and faithfulness = obedience,
then "ten faithful brides, five shut out" is self-defeating. Directives:
look closer at VIRGIN, weigh ten = house of Israel, consult Hebrew
Matthew, find the parable's OT original.

## Hebrew Matthew (Shem Tov / Even Bochan), Matt 25:1-13

Source: George Howard, *The Gospel of Matthew according to a Primitive
Hebrew Text* (1987), pp. 124-127 — read visually from the archive.org
scan (our Cochin Hebrew NT covers Mark/Luke/John/James/Jude/Revelation,
no Matthew). Independent agreement: *The Hebrew Gospels from Sepharad —
Matthew* v2.2 (Shem Tov family), same readings at every point checked.

Verified letter-level readings:

- **v1** ויצאו לקראת חתן וכלה — the virgins go out "to meet a
  bridegroom **and a bride**." The virgins are NOT the bride. The same
  reading stands in Codex Bezae (the oldest Greek–Latin codex), the Old
  Latin, and the Syriac tradition of Matt 25:1.
- **v2** חמש מהן היו עצלות כסילות וחמש מהן זריזות וחכמות — "five of
  them were **lazy fools** and five **diligent and wise**." The
  lazy/diligent axis states the fool = hearer-not-doer doctrine
  outright.
- **v3-4** lamps = נרות (the *ner* of Prov 6:23 "the commandment is a
  lamp"), oil = שמן, vessels = כלים.
- **v5** ויתמהמה החתן — the bridegroom "**tarried**": Habakkuk 2:3's
  exact verb, where the vision is "for the **moed**… though it tarry,
  wait for it." (Unused in ch 16 — calendar-adjacent; candidate for
  Part Four use.)
- **v6** ויהי בחצי הלילה — the midnight cry opens with Exodus 12:29's
  Passover-stroke formula verbatim ("And it came to pass at midnight…").
  Passover night divides lamps burning from darkness (cf. Ex 10:23).
  (Unused — same reason.)
- **v7** והטיבו נרותיהן — they "made **good** (hetivu, root tov) their
  lamps" (KJV "trimmed").
- **v8** שנרותינו נדעכו — "our lamps are **put out**" — *nid'akhu*,
  root דעך: the verb Scripture reserves for the lamp of the WICKED
  (Prov 13:9; 20:20 uses kavah; 24:20 yid'akh; Job 18:5-6). The foolish
  pronounce the wicked's sentence on themselves. (Scan resolution note:
  dalet/resh ambiguous in the type, but דעך is the only Hebrew root the
  consonants permit.)
- **v10** והמוכנות באו עמו לחופה ונסגר השער — "the ready went in with
  him **into the chuppah** and the **gate** (sha'ar, not delet) was
  shut." The chuppah is Ps 19:5's — the sun-bridegroom's canopy in the
  sky-tabernacle of ch 24; the shut gate is a sky-gate word. (Unused in
  ch 16 — ch 24 owns gates; candidate tie-in.)
- **v11** the foolish "came and called **at the gate**."
- **v13** שיבא החתן — "you do not know the day nor the hour **when the
  bridegroom will come**." No "wherein the Son of man cometh" (the KJV
  clause is a late addition; the oldest Greek also lacks it). Watching
  here is watching for a WEDDING procession. (Candidate tie to the
  observed new moon / Yom Teruah "day and hour" argument — TTT
  territory; not used in ch 16.)

## The OT original and anchors (all verified in kjv.json / wlc.txt)

- **Psalm 45:14-15** — the royal wedding psalm IS the parable's scene:
  "the **virgins her companions** that follow her… **shall enter into
  the king's palace**." Virgins-plural = the bride's espoused company,
  distinct from the queen. Same cast as Song 6:8-9 (queens, concubines,
  "virgins without number" vs "my **dove** is but **one**" — ch 26's
  block).
- **Song 1:3** — לריח שמניך טובים שמן תורק שמך — "**oil** poured out is
  thy **name** — therefore do the virgins (alamot) love thee." Shem
  inside shemen: to carry oil = to carry the Name's character/works in
  one's vessel (ch 08 tie). Unused — candidate for the oil section if
  the author wants the wordplay.
- **Virgin = espoused standing, not attainment:** Joel 1:8 (a virgin
  lamenting "the **husband of her youth**"); Deut 22:23-24 (the law
  calls a betrothed virgin "his neighbour's **wife**"); 2 Cor 11:2
  ("espoused… as a chaste virgin").
- **Ten = the house of Israel:** 1 Kings 11:30-31, 35 — Ahijah's torn
  garment, "Take thee **ten** pieces… **ten** tribes"; the northern
  house = "the **virgin of Israel**" (Amos 5:2; Jer 31:4, 21) — and Jer
  31 is the renewed-covenant chapter (31:31), with Hosea 2:19-20 the
  re-betrothal and ch 09 (Husband's death frees the divorced wife) the
  legal mechanism. "The kingdom of heaven is likened to TEN virgins" —
  the kingdom torn into ten, re-espoused, awaiting the wedding.
- **The wise virgin's portrait = Prov 31:18** — טעמה כי־טוב סחרה
  לא־יכבה בלילה נרה — "she perceiveth that her **merchandise** is good:
  her **candle goeth not out by night**." Buying + the lamp through the
  night watch — the parable's two marks in one verse, and the verse
  carries ki-tov-sachrah, ch 26's three-word lock. The foolish are sent
  "to them that **sell**, and **buy**" (Matt 25:9; cf. Prov 23:23; Rev
  3:18; Isa 55:1).
- **Lamp put out = the wicked's marker:** Prov 13:9; 24:20; Job 18:5-6;
  against Prov 31:18. Luke 13:25 (shut door, "Lord, Lord… I know you
  not") is the same scene in Luke.

## Resolution applied (ch 16 + glossary, 2026-07-06)

The ten are the espoused company of the wedding — covenant standing
alike, all pure, none a harlot — going out to meet the bridegroom AND
the bride. What divides them is the store of oil: proven works
(glossary sym-oil), the doing that turns the commandment-lamp into
light. Chastity is status; oil is doing; the contradiction dissolves —
and Hebrew Matthew's "lazy fools / diligent wise" says exactly this.
Glossary sym-virgin rewritten to espoused-standing + companions +
virgin-of-Israel; ch 16 Ten Virgins section rebuilt on Ps 45:14-15,
Joel 1:8/Deut 22:23-24, 1 Kgs 11:31, Jer 31; nid'akhu verdict added;
Prov 31:18 portrait added with forward link to Pearls of Wisdom.

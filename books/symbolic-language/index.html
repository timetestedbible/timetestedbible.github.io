Everything Jesus spoke publicly was a parable, and the prophets before him spoke in similitudes. This book gives you the key that unlocks the Bible's symbolic language — an objective, self-correcting method for letting Scripture define its own symbols, so the deep meaning survives translation and the drift of words over time.

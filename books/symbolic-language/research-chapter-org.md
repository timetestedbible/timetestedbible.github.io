This book has the following Goals:

0. Demonstrate that symbology is the rule rather than the exception
     - speaking in parables similtudes and analogies is what 
       scripture tells us it does. And it tells us the reason for
       doing so... so that those with hard hearts.. will hear what
       they want to hear, but those who hunger and thirst can seek out
       the true meaning. 
1. Convince People that the Method of Interpretation is correct
     - This can be achieved by using parables with scripture provided
       interpretation. The Parable's of the Kingdom is a good case
       study, at least for the subset that Jesus defines and expects
       the diciples to already understand.
     - there can be other places
     - address the common concerns with symbolic readings..
         a. people bring their own definitions to the symbols rather
         than letting scripture define the symbols shifting the authority
         to the interpreter rather than the scripture... how do we
         control for this?   The answer is that the symbolic readings
         render a consistent interp with other literal doctrines and
         thus reinforce.
         b. when the same symbol meanign applies across many contexts
         and isn't contradicted by any we are in a good place, but
         when people read oil of the virgins as holy spirit... they
         have contradictions... 
         c. they claim it de-historicallizes the bibles presentation of
            real events, and we claim that real events are lived 
            parables as does scripture, thus all creation and much 
            recorded biblical history is a parable, all scripture
            is suitable for teaching and reproof... 

3. Major Doctrines Knowing the Symbolic Language Overturns:
       a. the definition of the gospel and salvation
       b. that the law has been done away with
       c. that the church and israel are separate 
       d. the calendar 
              - full moon 
              - sign of jonah 
              - pearl 
       e. taking his name in vain 
       f. end times interpretation 
            - who is the whore 
            - who is babylon 
            - what is the flaiming mountain
            - the rapture 
       g. the remenant 
       h. grounds for divorce and nature of marriage 


4. Case Studies 
     - apply the method to various symbols, if we apply it to 
     a symbol we should identify the hidden meaning that aligns 
     with the rest of scripture.

# Research: Signs & Similitudes — corpus, precedents, and common teaching

Comprehensive research behind ch. 4 (Signs and Similitudes), done 2026-07-02
after the author asked for it (the chapter was first drafted from seed ideas).
Three parts: (1) the full word-corpus, (2) the index of Scripture-RATIFIED
types ("proven" precedents), (3) survey of what is commonly taught.

## 1. The corpus

KJV verse counts: **sign(s) 119**, **token(s) 21** (same Hebrew word),
similitude(s) 12, figure(s) 10, ensample(s) 6, example(s) 9, pattern(s) 14,
shadow(s) 74, wonder(s) 70. Tagged AKJV-Strong's counts: *oth* H226 ("sign/
token") 77; *mopheth* H4159 ("wonder/portent" — the word in Ezek 24:24!) 35;
*sēmeion* G4592 69. Full sign/token dump was tiered; the taxonomy:

**A. Covenant-marker signs** (oth as seal of a standing covenant — no
fulfillment awaited; the sign marks a bond): the BOW (Gen 9:12, 13, 17 — ch.
9's symbol); circumcision (Gen 17:11; Rom 4:11 "the SIGN of circumcision, a
SEAL of the righteousness of the faith"); the Passover blood on the houses
(Ex 12:13); the word bound on hand/eyes (Ex 13:9, 16; Deut 6:8; 11:18 — the
LAW itself worn as the sign); the SABBATH — "a sign between me and you
throughout your generations… that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth
sanctify you… a sign… FOR EVER" (Ex 31:13, 17; Ezek 20:12, 20); Isa 55:13
"an everlasting sign that shall not be cut off."

**B. Oracle-signs** (a prophecy given; fulfillment testifies): Isa 7:11, 14
(the virgin); 1 Sam 2:34 (Eli's sons); 1 Sam 10:2-9 ("all those signs came
to pass that day" — foretold ordinary scenes, now ch. 4's lead example);
1 Kgs 13:3, 5 (the altar rent); Jer 44:29; Ex 3:12 (sign confirmed AFTER
the fulfillment: "ye shall serve God upon this mountain").
CORRECTION (author, 2026-07-02): 2 Kgs 19:29/Isa 37:30 is NOT a mundane
agricultural note — the two consecutive no-sow years are the sabbatical +
JUBILEE pattern (Lev 25:20-22): Hezekiah's sign is a jubilee marker, tied
to Time Tested Tradition's jubilee reckoning. Removed as ch. 4's lead (also
not a lived/person sign); do not cite it as "nothing miraculous."
**Hezekiah's third-day promise** (2 Kgs 20:5-11): "I will heal thee: on the
third day thou shalt go up unto the house of the LORD." NOTE (author,
2026-07-02): removed from ch. 4 — the SIGN here is the shadow-wonder
performed that day (a wonder-sign, contrary to the oracle-sign thesis);
the third-day rising is the PROMISE the sign confirms, not the sign itself.
Candidate for third-day material elsewhere (e.g. ch. 5), at the author's
discretion.

**C. Prophet/person/nation as sign** (the lived story): Isa 8:18 (Isaiah +
message-named children); Isa 20:3 (three years barefoot); Ezek 4:3 (the
tile); Ezek 12:6, 11 ("I am your sign: like as I have done, so shall it be
done unto them"); Ezek 24:24, 27 (mopheth); Ezek 14:8 ("a sign and a
proverb"); Num 26:10 (Korah's company "became a sign"); Deut 28:46 (the
NATION itself: "they shall be upon thee for a sign and for a wonder, and
upon thy seed for ever"); Luke 2:34 (the child "a sign spoken against");
Luke 11:30 (Jonah).
**Sign-durations carry God's own conversion table**: "each day for a year"
(Num 14:34; Ezek 4:6 "I have appointed thee each day for a year"); "one day
… as a thousand years" (Ps 90:4; 2 Pet 3:8).

**D. Memorial signs** (looking back): Josh 4:6 (the stones — "when your
children ask"); Ex 13:9; Num 16:38 (the censers); Num 17:10 (Aaron's rod
kept "for a token").

**E. Heavenly signs**: Gen 1:14 ("let them be for signs"); Jer 10:2; Matt
24:30 ("the sign of the Son of man in heaven"); Luke 21:11, 25; Acts 2:19;
and Revelation TITLES its visions signs — Rev 12:1 (the woman = "great
wonder," Gk. *sēmeion mega*), 12:3 (the dragon), 15:1.

**F. Sign-seeking, counterfeit signs, and MOSES' TWO TESTS**: Matt 12:38-39;
16:1-4 ("discern the signs of the times"); Mark 8:11-12; John 4:48; 1 Cor
1:22 ("the Jews require a sign"); Matt 24:24; 2 Thess 2:9 (lying wonders).
The validation is two-sided: **Deut 18:21-22 — the definition of a valid
sign/word: it comes to pass ("if the thing follow not… the LORD hath not
spoken it"); and Deut 13:1-3 — the law-positive override: a sign COMING
TRUE does not validate the prophet; if he teaches rebellion against God's
commandments, the fulfilled sign is itself a TEST** "to know whether ye
love the LORD." Both tests in ch. 4.

**G. Dark inversion**: Judas's kiss is called a sign/token (Matt 26:48; Mark
14:44) — the betrayer hands the authorities a sign.

## 2. The index of Scripture-ratified types (the "proven" precedents)

The charter: **Luke 24:27** — "beginning at Moses and ALL the prophets, he
expounded unto them in ALL the scriptures the things concerning himself"
(also 24:44); **John 5:46** "Moses… wrote of me"; John 5:39.

Ratified instance by instance:
- Jonah → the Son of man (Matt 12:39-40; 16:4; Luke 11:29-30) — by Jesus
- Serpent lifted up → the Son lifted up (John 3:14) — by Jesus
- Days of Noah → the coming (Matt 24:37-39; Luke 17:26) — by Jesus
- Days of Lot → the day the Son is revealed (Luke 17:28-30) — by Jesus
- Manna → the true bread from heaven (John 6:32-35, 48-51) — by Jesus
- The temple → His body (John 2:19-21)
- Elijah → John the Baptist (Mal 4:5; Matt 11:14 "this is Elias, which was
  for to come"; 17:12) — a past MAN prophesying a future man
- The prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22; 7:37) — Moses a type by
  Moses' own word
- Israel's wilderness history → types (1 Cor 10:1-11, *typoi*; the crossing
  = baptism; the Rock = Christ, 1 Cor 10:4)
- Adam → "the figure (typos) of him that was to come" (Rom 5:14)
- Hagar/Sarah → the two covenants ("which things are an allegory," Gal
  4:22-31)
- The Passover lamb → "Christ our passover" (1 Cor 5:7); John 19:36 cites
  the passover-lamb RULE (Ex 12:46; Num 9:12) as fulfilled prophecy
- The flood → baptism — Peter's word is literally *antitypon*, "the like
  figure" (1 Pet 3:20-21): type/antitype is Scripture's own vocabulary
- Isaac received from the dead "in a figure" — *en parabolē*, in a parable
  (Heb 11:17-19)
- Melchizedek → the priest for ever (Ps 110:4; Heb 7)
- Tabernacle/priesthood/law → "example and shadow of heavenly things," built
  "according to the pattern" (Heb 8:5; 9:23-24 "figures of the true"; 10:1)
- David's life/psalms → "being a prophet… spake of the resurrection of
  Christ" (Acts 2:25-31, of Ps 16:10; Acts 2:34 of Ps 110)
- Joseph/Moses as the rejected-then-exalted deliverer — Stephen's whole
  sermon (Acts 7, esp. 7:9-13 "at the second time," 7:35-37, 51-52)
- God's stated method: "I have… used similitudes, by the ministry of the
  prophets" (Hos 12:10); prophet-enactments beyond Ezekiel: Hosea's marriage
  (Hos 1-3), Jeremiah's girdle/potter/yoke (Jer 13; 18-19; 27-28), Ahijah's
  rent garment (1 Kgs 11:30-31), Agabus (Acts 21:11)

## 3. What is commonly taught

- **Popular/evangelical baseline** (GotQuestions, Crosswalk, BibleRef on
  Eccl 1:9): "nothing new under the sun" = the cyclic futility of life
  "under the sun," i.e., an earth-bound life apart from God; surface
  pessimism contrasted with faith. NOT taught as a warrant for composed,
  repeating history. https://www.gotquestions.org/nothing-new-under-the-sun.html
- **Typology hermeneutics — the documented split** (Theopedia; Precept
  Austin; bible-researcher.com/Schmeling): the RESTRICTIVE school (Bishop
  Herbert Marsh; Andrew Jukes) — "only those types which are so designated
  in Scripture are valid types"; the MODERATE school (Patrick Fairbairn,
  Milton Terry) — "innate" (NT-declared) plus "inferred" types justified by
  the NT's own practice. https://theopedia.com/biblical-typology
- Even the moderate school treats types mostly as ILLUSTRATIONS of doctrine;
  the chapter's stronger claim — that the events were COMPOSED as prophecy
  and that Jesus treats them as things that "must be fulfilled" — is the
  position Luke 24:27/44 and 1 Cor 10:11 ("happened unto them for types")
  state outright but few systematize.
- Prevalence-table candidates (rule: research before numbers): how Eccl 1:9
  is usually read; how Heb 11:19 "in a figure / en parabolē" is rendered.

## 4. Conclusions applied to ch. 4

Added to the chapter from this research: (a) the covenant-marker family of
signs (bow / circumcision / blood / the word on the hand / the SABBATH as
perpetual sign); (b) the Deut 13:1-3 filter — a fulfilled sign does not
excuse lawbreaking; (c) Hezekiah's third-day sign; (d) Ezekiel's day-for-a-
year conversion (sign-durations translate by God's stated scales); (e) Rev
12:1 — Revelation titles its visions signs; (f) the ratified roll-call with
the Luke 24:27 charter and 1 Pet 3:21 *antitypon*; (g) the two-schools note.
Not yet used (future material): Judas's kiss as the dark sign; Deut 28:46
Israel-as-sign; memorial signs as a class; Num 26:10; Isa 55:13.

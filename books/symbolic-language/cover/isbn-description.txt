DESCRIPTION (~310 words):

The sign of Jonah is the only sign Jesus gave this generation — and read
in Scripture's own symbols, the whole book of Jonah opens as a
scene-by-scene prophecy of His betrayal, arrest, interrogation, death,
resurrection, and the gospel's turn to the nations, with "the heart of
the earth" naming a place you were never taught. Daniel's book was
sealed to the time of the end — and the seal was never wax: it is the
reading tradition men laid over the prophet's own letters, and when it
comes off, the unpointed text counts seventy jubilees to a date.
Lucifer's plan to corrupt the signs of heaven was never announced — but
God, who declares to man his thought, put it on the record, and this
book reads the document. And "the name of the LORD" is not a sound to
pronounce or a formula that makes prayers work: the name is the covenant
itself, owned the way a bride takes her husband's.

You have not read these in seminary notes, because they are not there.
They come from a method the book teaches openly: let Scripture define
its own symbols — each meaning stated in one passage, used consistently
in the rest, contradicted by none — and read the letters the prophets
wrote rather than the vowel-tradition added a thousand years later.
Applied symbol by symbol, the method fills a working glossary of more
than 140 terms, carried by over 2,500 citations from 64 books of the
Bible.

Every definition was then stress-tested blind against mainstream
teaching. Where the church has read carefully, the method agrees — proof
it is no private invention. Thirty-five entries say what has never been
taught, each badged in the glossary with the common reading printed
beside it, so you can weigh both.

Heaven and hell, the seal of God and its counterfeit, the remnant,
Babylon and its fall, the path to salvation — read from the vocabulary
Scripture supplies. Take no man's word — verify.

MEAT GLOSSARY STANDARDIZATION REVIEW
=====================================

Purpose
-------
This is an editing worksheet, not book source. It compares the current glossary
definition with a proposed standardized definition for every entry not already
approved during the present review. Nothing in this file changes the book until
the edited proposals are deliberately integrated into 49-glossary.adoc.

How to review
-------------
1. Edit the NEW text directly.
2. Edit COMMON NEW where present.
3. Use REVIEWER NOTE for questions, objections, or citation requests.
4. OLD is a frozen comparison and should not be edited.

House style used for proposals
------------------------------
- Definition first: symbolic meaning before proof or commentary.
- Prefer one short lead sentence and, only when needed, one clarifying sentence.
- Put competing popular readings in COMMON, not inside the definition.
- Leave the proof chain and extended argument in the cited chapter.
- KEEP means the wording already fits. REVISE means the meaning is retained but
  expressed more cleanly. CHECK flags a claim whose scope or precision deserves
  special attention before integration.

# Wall — symbol study (2026-07-10, bonus track)

Hebrew chomah (H2346, city wall); gader (H1447, vineyard wall); Greek teichos.

## Candidate definition
**A wall is salvation — the covenant's enclosure: God's protection around His
people, and the boundary that divides inside from outside.** Counterfeit: the
self-built wall — wealth imagined as a wall, and the whitewashed wall of false
peace.

## Defining verses
- **Isa 60:18 — "thou shalt call thy walls SALVATION (yeshuah), and thy gates
  Praise."** Scripture names the wall outright.
- **Isa 26:1 — "salvation will God appoint for walls and bulwarks."**
- **Zech 2:4-5 — Jerusalem inhabited without walls, "for I, saith the LORD,
  will be unto her a WALL OF FIRE round about."** God Himself the enclosure.

## The counterfeit
- **Prov 18:11 — the verse AFTER the Name-tower (18:10): "The rich man's
  wealth is his strong city, and as an high wall IN HIS OWN CONCEIT."**
  Deliberate adjacent pair: true tower vs imagined wall.
- **Ezek 13:10-15 — the whitewashed wall**: prophets crying "Peace, and there
  was no peace" build a wall daubed with untempered mortar; God's storm breaks
  it. A false covenant is a whitewashed wall. LEXEME NOTE (grader find):
  Ezekiel's false wall is chayits (H2434, hapax) and qir (H7023) — Scripture
  WITHHOLDS chomah, the true wall-word, from the counterfeit. The storm verb
  shataph (H7857, "overflow") bridges Ezek 13:11-13, Isa 28:2/15/17, and
  Isa 8:8 — one flood vocabulary across all three counterfeit-judgment texts.
- **Isa 28:15-17 — the builders' own words**: "we have made a covenant with
  death… lies our refuge" — answered by the tried CORNERSTONE laid in Zion,
  judgment to the line, righteousness to the plummet, hail sweeping the refuge
  of lies, waters overflowing (the flood = armies, Isa 8:7-8). Amos 7:7-8: the
  LORD on a wall with a plumbline.

## Facets
- Vineyard wall (Isa 5:5): protection withdrawn = wall broken = trampled.
- Breach & intercessor: Ezek 22:30 (stand in the gap); Isa 58:12 (repairer of
  the breach — sabbath context v13); Prov 25:28 (no self-rule = city without
  walls); Ps 106:23 (Moses in the breach).
- The bride: Song 8:9-10 "I am a wall" — fidelity unbreached.
- The partition: Eph 2:14 middle wall between stranger and citizen, broken
  down by the blood — joins the Stranger/commonwealth symbol.
- New Jerusalem: Rev 21:12-14 — the wall's foundations and gates carry NAMES
  (apostles, tribes). The wall, like the tower, is built of names.

## The vineyard synthesis (centerpiece)
Matt 21:33 with the vocabulary: vineyard = house of Israel (Isa 5:7); hedge/
wall = salvation-enclosure; tower = the Name's sanctuary; husbandmen = "the
BUILDERS" (Jesus' own word, 21:42); the repeatedly SENT servants and Son
(Shiloah = Sent, John 9:7) — Son cast OUT of the vineyard (without the gate,
Heb 13:12). The husbandmen are identified with the rejected stone's "builders" by
inference — Jesus quotes Ps 118:22 and "they perceived that he spake of them"
(Matt 21:45) — association the chief priests themselves ratified. GRADER
WELDS: Matt 21:33 "hedged" is phragmos (G5418), the identical word in Eph
2:14's "middle wall of partition"; and the owner's repeated "SENT" is
apostellō (G649), the verb of Siloam's own interpretation (John 9:7) — the
Sent-theme is inside the parable's Greek. Prov 18:10-11 share sagab (H7682,
"safe"/"high") — the tower/conceit-wall adjacency is lexically deliberate. Sentence: wall broken,
vineyard trodden (Isa 5:5; AD 70), kingdom to a fruit-bearing nation (Matt
21:43) — and the rejected cornerstone founds the new structure of living
stones (1 Pet 2:4-7; Eph 2:20-22, six verses after the middle wall falls).

## Counter-texts
Literal fortifications abound (Neh 3's wall-building is literal AND enacts
covenant restoration — dual; Babylon's walls; Jericho's fell with Rahab's
cord-marked house on it (house upon the wall, Josh 2:15; the scarlet cord —
tiqvah, hope's own word — Josh 2:18, 21; wall fell, house stood, Josh 6:20-23).
Literal walls don't dissolve the symbol; the freight rides the self-declared
figurative uses (Isa 60:18; Zech 2:5; Prov 18:11; Ezek 13).

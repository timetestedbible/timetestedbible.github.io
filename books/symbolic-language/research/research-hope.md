# Hope — tiqvah, the scarlet cord (2026-07-08)

Gap found: faith is defined in relation to hope (Heb 11:1 "the substance
of things hoped for") but HOPE has no glossary entry. Corpus: H8615
tiqvah, 33 occurrences, verified in kjv_strongs.txt.

## The finding

**Tiqvah's first use is not "hope" — it is Rahab's cord.** "Thou shalt
bind this LINE (tiqvah) of scarlet thread in the window" (Josh 2:18, 21).
Everywhere after, the same word is rendered hope/expectation. Scripture's
first-use definition: hope is a CORD — bound to a fixed point, held until
salvation comes. Not optimism; rigging.

- Heb 6:18-19 keeps the image in Greek: "lay HOLD upon the hope set
  before us: which hope we have as an ANCHOR of the soul" — the anchor
  LINE. What Rahab tied, the epistle ties to the Forerunner within the
  veil.
- Jer 29:11 "an expected end" = acharit v'tiqvah — an end AND a cord.
- Hos 2:15 "the valley of Achor for a door of hope" — Achor (trouble) as
  the doorway the cord passes through; Zech 9:12 "prisoners of hope" —
  bound BY the cord; Job repeatedly: the hypocrite's tiqvah perishes
  (Job 8:13-14 — his trust "a spider's web": the counterfeit cord!).
- Rahab: the cord bound in the window saved her HOUSE through the fall of
  a city — already one of the Remnant chapter's fifteen lines. The harlot
  saved by the scarlet line while Jericho's wall fell: hope is what you
  bind and keep bound through the siege.

## Relation to faith (Heb 11:1)

"Faith is the substance (hypostasis — what stands under) of things hoped
for" — faith is the standing-under of what the cord is tied to. Hope
names the tie to the promised end; faith is the reliable holding (emunah,
steady hands). The pair is rigging: hope the line, faith the grip,
love the keeping (the work the rigging exists for), per the four-offices
paragraph in ch06. 1 Cor 13:13's triad keeps all three distinct.

## Glossary candidate (run delta grading; likely NOVEL or DIVERGENT —
commonly taught: confident expectation/optimistic assurance, no cord)

> Hope:: The scarlet cord — tiqvah, whose first use is the line Rahab
> bound in her window: expectation TIED to a fixed promise and held
> until salvation comes; the anchor-line of the soul. Its counterfeit
> is the spider's web. +
> (Josh 2:18, 21; Heb 6:18-19; Jer 29:11; Hos 2:15; Zech 9:12; Job 8:13-14)

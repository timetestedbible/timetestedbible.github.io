# Research: Poor and Rich — the corpus behind the Poor-and-Rich section of ch. 17 (Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless)

Done 2026-07-02 for the chapter. Corpus: KJV "poor" ~200 verses, "rich"
~80. Tagged: *ani* H6041 (poor/afflicted) 77×; *anav* H6035 (meek) 24×;
*evyon* H34 (needy) 58×; *dal* H1800 (weak/poor) 46×; Greek *ptōchos*
G4434 (poor) 34×; *plousios* G4145 (rich) 28×.

## The discovery: poor = meek, one Hebrew family

Strong's H6041 (*ani*, poor): "practically the same as H6035 (*anav*,
meek), **although the margin constantly disputes this**" — the kethib/qere
interchange the scribes themselves recorded. Both from *anah*, to be bowed
down. So:
- Ps 37:11 "the MEEK (anavim) shall inherit the earth" = Matt 5:5.
- Isa 61:1 "good tidings unto the MEEK (anavim)" = Luke 4:18 "the gospel
  to the POOR (ptōchois)" — the same people under both renderings.
- Matt 5:3 "poor IN SPIRIT: for theirs is the kingdom" — names the state
  explicitly (bowed before God), beside Luke 6:20 "blessed be ye poor:
  for yours is the kingdom of God."
The poor of the promises are the bowed-humble — which is why the gospel
belongs to them.

## Who the poor are (God's own statements)

- Isa 66:2 — "to this man will I look, even to him that is POOR (ani) and
  of a contrite spirit, and trembleth at my word."
- Zeph 3:12 — "an afflicted and POOR people, and they shall trust in the
  name of the LORD" (the remnant).
- 1 Sam 2:8 — "He raiseth up the poor out of the dust… to make them
  inherit the throne of glory" (Hannah's song; cf. Ps 113:7-8).
- James 2:5 — "chosen the POOR of this world RICH in faith, and heirs of
  the KINGDOM."
- Matt 11:5 / Luke 7:22 — "the POOR have the gospel preached to them" —
  the credential Jesus sends to John the Baptist.

## The pair cross-defined (Scripture's own columns)

- Smyrna: "I know thy POVERTY, (but thou art RICH)" (Rev 2:9) vs Laodicea:
  "thou sayest, I am RICH… and knowest not that thou art… POOR" (Rev
  3:17), counseled to "buy of me gold tried in the fire, that thou mayest
  be rich; and white raiment" (3:18).
- Prov 13:7 — "There is that maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing: there
  is that maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches."
- Luke 12:21 — "so is he that layeth up treasure for himself, and is not
  RICH TOWARD GOD."
- 1 Tim 6:17-18 — the rich charged to be "RICH IN GOOD WORKS."
- 2 Cor 8:9 — "though he was rich… he became poor, that ye through his
  poverty might be rich."
- Luke 6:24 — "woe unto you that are rich! for ye have received your
  consolation"; Matt 19:23-24 (camel/needle) with 19:26.

## Meaning

Poor = the bowed-humble before God (ani/anav): empty-handed toward Him,
the gospel-receivers, heirs of the kingdom. Rich = the self-sufficient who
"have need of nothing" (Laodicea): full of self, therefore unable to
receive. The true wealth is being "rich toward God" — faith and good works.

## Caution (law-positive)

The symbol does not dissolve the literal poor: the law defends them by
name (gleaning, Lev 19:9-10; the release, Deut 15:7-11; wages, Deut
24:14-15), and the prophets prosecute their oppressors. The symbol deepens
the command; it never replaces it.

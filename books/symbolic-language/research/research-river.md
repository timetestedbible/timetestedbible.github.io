# The river theory tested — living water, the Spirit, and Eden's four heads (2026-07-07)

The theory as posed: **river = Spirit (living water), as wind = spirit (ruach); and
the river out of Eden parting into four heads is the water-form of the four winds
going out to the four corners.** This file tests it against the book's standing
definitions and the counter-texts.

What the book already holds: Sea & Waters (ch. 36) states the two-waters rule — the
gathered, salt, restless waters are the peoples, but living water, "water that
springs and flows from God," is "the Spirit and word of God going out to give life"
(Jer 2:13; John 7:38), with Ezek 47's river healing the sea; the glossary carries
Wind:: Spirit — *ruach* (Zech 6:5), and living water (sym-living-2); The Four Winds
(ch. 29) establishes the four winds as the four spirits that "go forth from standing
before the Lord" (Zech 6:5) and carry breath to the dead (Ezek 37:9); the flood that
is an army in motion is its own glossary symbol. Verses below verified against
kjv_strongs.txt and wlc.txt. This was a targeted test of the definitional and
structural verses, not a full *nahar* concordance sweep — run the full sweep before
a chapter is drafted.

---

## The verdict

**Confirmed, with the discriminator kept.** "River" is not univocally spirit — the
Euphrates is an empire and the dragon pours a river of his own — but the book's
existing rule already sorts every case: **the source decides.** Water going out from
the presence of God is the Spirit and the word; water gathered, foreign, or poured
from another mouth is the masses, the army, the counterfeit. Under that rule the
Eden river passes at full strength, and the structural claim — one source, fourfold
distribution, matching the winds' commission — holds and gains two witnesses the
theory did not ask for: the four corners of the earth are *kanphot* (wings/hems),
and Eden's second head bears the name of Jerusalem's own spring.

And the two equations were never parallel accidents: they meet in the Bible's second
verse — "the **Spirit** of God moved upon the face of the **waters**" (Gen 1:2) —
and in one conversation at night: "born of **water** and of the **Spirit**...
The **wind** bloweth where it listeth... so is every one that is born of the
**Spirit**" (John 3:5, 8). Wind above, water below: two carriers of one *ruach*.

## Tier 1 — the stated equations

- **John 7:38-39** — decoded in-text: "out of his belly shall flow rivers of living
  water. (But this spake he **of the Spirit**...)" The strongest kind of evidence
  the book uses: Scripture defining its own symbol out loud.
- **Isa 44:3** — the parallelism states it: "I will pour **water** upon him that is
  thirsty... I will pour **my spirit** upon thy seed." Water // spirit, one verse.
- **Eph 5:26** — "the washing of **water by the word**": the water named as the word
  in the act of cleansing.
- **Jer 2:13** — "they have forsaken me, **the fountain of living waters**" — the
  LORD Himself is the spring; all living water is His outflow. (And the alternative
  is man-made containment: "broken cisterns, that can hold no water.")
- **Prov 18:4** — "the **wellspring of wisdom** as a flowing brook"; **Prov 13:14** —
  "the **law** of the wise is a **fountain of life**"; **14:27** — "the **fear of
  the LORD** is a fountain of life"; **10:11** — "the **mouth of a righteous man**
  is a well of life." The fountain cluster: law, fear, wisdom, righteous speech —
  the word in its forms, always the life-spring.
- **Amos 5:24** — "let **judgment** run down **as waters**, and **righteousness as
  a mighty stream**."

### The law's own term — mayim chayim

The KJV's "running water" flattens the Hebrew: the law says **living water** (מים
חיים). The leper is cleansed over living water (Lev 14:5-6, 50-52); the water of
separation requires living water on the heifer's ashes (Num 19:17); Isaac's servants
dig and find "a well of **springing** [living] water" (Gen 26:19). The cleansing
agent prescribed by the law is the Spirit-kind of water — the symbol was in the
statute all along. And Ezekiel gives the sequence in full: "I will sprinkle **clean
water** upon you... a **new spirit** will I put within you... and cause you to
**walk in my statutes**" (Ezek 36:25-27) — water, spirit, walk: the Spirit's stated
effect is statute-keeping.

## The structure test — Eden's four heads and the four winds

**Gen 2:10** — "a river went out of Eden to water the garden; and from thence it was
parted, and became into four heads." One river, sourced at the presence, waters the
garden first, then divides fourfold to compass the lands (2:11-14).

**Zech 6:5** — the four winds/spirits "go forth from **standing before the Lord** of
all the earth." **Rev 7:1** — four angels at the four corners hold the four winds.

Same map, two elements: one source at the presence → fourfold going-out → the whole
earth. The winds carry the Spirit above; the river carries it below (Gen 1:2 — the
Spirit on the face of the waters, from the beginning).

**The return direction completes it.** The going-out is fourfold, and so is the
homecoming: "Come from the **four winds**, O breath, and breathe upon these slain"
(Ezek 37:9 — the resurrection breath arrives *from* the four); "they shall gather
together his elect **from the four winds**" (Matt 24:31); "gather together the
dispersed of Judah **from the four corners of the earth**" (Isa 11:12). Out from
Eden to the four quarters; home from the four quarters at the harvest.

**The garment witness (unasked-for).** The "corners" of Isa 11:12 and Ezek 7:2 are
*kanphot* — wings, hems (H3671, the Wings chapter's word). The earth has four
kanphot; and the law dresses the covenant man the same way: "make thee fringes upon
the **four kanphot of thy vesture**" (Deut 22:12) — the commandment-tassels hang at
exactly the four corners. The earth wears a four-winged garment, and the gathering
from the four winds is God taking the earth by its hem.

**The end-state inverts the beginning.** In Eden the one river *parts* to reach the
quarters; in the New Jerusalem the river stays one — "a pure river of water of life,
clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne" — and the *nations come to it*: the
tree's "leaves... for the healing of the nations" (Rev 22:1-2), no more sea, nothing
left to reach. Outbound distribution at creation, inbound gathering at the end — the
same two directions the winds run. Zech 14:8 is the interim form: "living waters
shall go out from Jerusalem; half toward the former sea, and half toward the hinder
sea" — the throne-water already flowing, two ways, summer and winter.

### The four heads themselves (proposal — hold loosely)

Pishon compasses Havilah — gold, bdellium, onyx: sanctuary materials (the onyx on
the ephod's shoulders, Ex 28:9; the manna "as bdellium," Num 11:7). Gihon compasses
Cush. Hiddekel runs toward Assyria; the fourth is the Euphrates — Babylon's. The
empires sit on Eden's heads: Babylon "dwellest upon many waters" (Jer 51:13). When
the presence-water is refused, the head-water floods back as the empire — that is
Isa 8:6-7 exactly (below); and at the end the empire's head is dried up (Rev 16:12,
the Euphrates) while the throne's river flows. The life-water reached the quarters
first; the beasts built on the channels.

## The Gihon witness — an Eden head flowing at Jerusalem

Eden's second head is named **Gihon** (Gen 2:13) — and Gihon is the name of
Jerusalem's spring. At Gihon, Solomon rides David's mule and is anointed king with
the oil of the tabernacle (1 Kgs 1:38-39). Hezekiah "stopped the upper watercourse
of **Gihon**, and brought it straight down to the west side of the city of David"
(2 Chr 32:30) — the conduit whose waters are the "waters of **Shiloah** that go
softly" that Israel *refused* (Isa 8:6), and whose pool is **Siloam, "which is by
interpretation, Sent"** — where the man born blind washes and sees (John 9:7). The
promise runs on the same water: "**living waters** shall go out from Jerusalem"
(Zech 14:8); "a fountain shall come forth of the house of the LORD" (Joel 3:18);
and the throne-river of Rev 22 seats it at the source. Isa 12:3 crowns the line in
the consonants: "with joy shall ye draw water out of the **wells of salvation**" —
ממעיני **הישועה**, the wells of *ha-yeshua*, the Name (ch. 8) — and it is Yeshua who
sits on Jacob's well and offers "a well of water springing up into everlasting life"
(John 4:14). This is a name-witness (the second dimension), not a claim that the
geographic Gihon drains Eden — the name is the link, and Scripture hung a great deal
on it.

## The counter-texts — rivers that are not the Spirit

The sweep, resolved by the standing discriminator (source and motion):

- **Isa 8:6-7 — two waters in one oracle.** "This people refuseth the waters of
  Shiloah **that go softly**" → "therefore the Lord bringeth up upon them the waters
  of the river, **strong and many, even the king of Assyria**... he shall come up
  over all his channels, and go over all his banks." The refused water is the soft,
  Sent, presence-water; the punishment-water is the empire in flood — decoded
  in-text ("even the king of Assyria"), and already the book's flood-symbol (an army
  in motion). Refuse the river of the Spirit, receive the river of the empire.
- **Rev 12:15-16 — the dragon's river.** "The serpent cast **out of his mouth**
  water as a flood after the woman." Out of the believer's belly, rivers of living
  water (John 7:38); out of the serpent's mouth, a flood. Water from a mouth is
  words; whose mouth tells you which water. The earth swallows the counterfeit.
- **Jer 2:13, 18 — drinking the wrong river.** The chapter that names the LORD "the
  fountain of living waters" asks in the same breath: "what hast thou to do... to
  **drink the waters of Sihor** [the Nile]? or... to **drink the waters of the
  river** [the Euphrates]?" Drinking is incorporation (see research-swine.md, the
  eating section — one mouth, one rule): the river you drink is the life you take
  in. Egypt's water or Assyria's water in place of the fountain is the swine's meal
  in liquid form.
- **Ps 46:3-4 — two waters in one psalm.** The waters that "roar and be troubled"
  (v. 3) against "a river, the streams whereof shall make glad the city of God"
  (v. 4). The masses rage; the presence-river gladdens.
- **Dan 7:2 — the winds churn the sea.** The same four winds that carry breath to
  the slain strive upon the great sea and the beasts rise: the winds are agents on
  the masses as well as carriers of life (ch. 29 already holds this office-view).
  The parallel holds for water too — the living river poured *into* the sea heals
  it (Ezek 47:8-9); and what refuses to flow is not healed: "the **miry places**
  thereof... shall not be healed; they shall be given to salt" (Ezek 47:11) — the
  standing wallow again (research-swine.md), the one place the living water passes
  by.

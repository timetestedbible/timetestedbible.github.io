# Research: Salt (melach) — author-commissioned (2026-07-23)

Full occurrence sweep verified against kjv_strongs.txt; second witness from
the Hebrew Gospels (Matthew word-level; Mark word-level; Luke word-level).
No prior salt entry exists in the glossary, _symbols/, or the chapters
(only ch. 40's salt-sea vs living-water contrast).

## Occurrence sweep (exhaustive)

- **H4417 melach (salt, noun)** — 26×. Nine are geography (Salt Sea ×8,
  Valley of Salt ×5 counting duplicates in Sam/Kgs/Chr, city of Salt);
  the rest split cleanly into the two poles below.
- **H4414 malach (verb)** — 4×: Lev 2:13 "season"; Ezek 16:4 "salted at
  all" (newborn rite); Ex 30:35 "tempered together" (the incense is
  SALTED — KJV hides it); Isa 51:6 "the heavens shall VANISH AWAY"
  (nimlachu — salted into dissolution; see wordplay).
- **H4415/H4416 (Aramaic)** — Ezra 4:14 "we have maintenance from the
  king's palace" is literally *"we are salted with the salt of the
  palace"* — eating a king's salt = bound in loyalty to him. Ezra 6:9,
  7:22: salt supplied for the temple offerings, and alone among the
  provisions it is given "without prescribing how much."
- **H4420 melechah (saltness)** — 3×: Job 39:6 "barren land"; Ps 107:34
  "a fruitful land into barrenness [saltness], for the wickedness of them
  that dwell therein"; Jer 17:6 "a salt land and not inhabited."
- **NT**: G217 halas (Matt 5:13; Mark 9:50; Luke 14:34; Col 4:6);
  G233 halizo "salted" (Matt 5:13; Mark 9:49); G252 halukos (James 3:12);
  G358 analos "lost his saltness" (Mark 9:50).
- **G3471 mōrainō** — the verb behind "lost his savour" (Matt 5:13;
  Luke 14:34) is elsewhere "became FOOLS" (Rom 1:22) and "made FOOLISH"
  (1 Cor 1:20). Savorless = foolish, in the Greek's own usage.

## The two poles — and both are covenant texts

**Covenant salt (preservation):**
- Lev 2:13 — "the SALT OF THE COVENANT of thy God": commanded on every
  minchah, and by the verse's own extension "with all thine offerings."
  Scripture's definitional statement: salt IS covenant.
- Num 18:19 — the priests' portion is "a COVENANT OF SALT for ever."
- 2 Chr 13:5 — the kingdom given to David "by a COVENANT OF SALT."
- Ezra 4:14 — salted with the palace's salt = sworn loyalty/maintenance.
- Ezek 43:24 — priests cast salt on the burnt offering.
- Ezek 16:4 — the newborn "not salted at all": salting was the covenant
  rite at birth; unsalted Jerusalem = born outside covenant care.
- Ex 30:35 — the incense "salted, pure and holy."

**Desolation salt (the curse preserved):**
- Deut 29:23 — "brimstone, and SALT, and burning… like the overthrow of
  Sodom" — this is the covenant-curse chapter itself (v. 21: "according
  to all the curses of the covenant"). The salted land is the covenant's
  curse executed and kept visible.
- Judg 9:45 — Abimelech beats down Shechem "and sowed it with salt."
- Ps 107:34 — fruitful land turned to saltness "for the wickedness of
  them that dwell therein" (and vv. 33-35: God turns it both ways).
- Jer 17:6 — the man who trusts man "shall inhabit… a salt land."
- Zeph 2:9 — Moab becomes "saltpits, and a perpetual desolation."
- Gen 19:26 — Lot's wife, a PILLAR of salt: judgment made a standing
  monument (Luke 17:32 "Remember Lot's wife").
- Ezek 47:11 — the river heals the Salt Sea, but the marshes that will
  not be healed "shall be GIVEN TO SALT."

**The unity is not "barrenness" and not "flavor" — it is PERMANENCE OF A
COVENANT STATE. What salt touches, keeps.** The offering keeps its
acceptance; the loyal man keeps his bond; the cursed land keeps its
curse. One substance, two poles, exactly as the covenant itself carries
blessing and curse (Deut 30:19).

## Taste = judgment: the ta'am bridge

Job 6:6 — "Can that which is unsavoury be eaten WITHOUT SALT? or is
there any TASTE (ta'am) in the white of an egg?" Salt is what gives food
its ta'am — and H2940 ta'am is Scripture's own word for **judgment**:
"teach me good JUDGMENT and knowledge" (Ps 119:66), Abigail's "ADVICE"
(1 Sam 25:33), the aged men's "UNDERSTANDING" (Job 12:20), a woman's
"DISCRETION" (Prov 11:22), rendering a "REASON" (Prov 26:16), the king's
"DECREE" (Jonah 3:7). Salt gives the word its judgment — the thing
tasted becomes the thing tested (Job 34:3; the book's taste=test spine).

Jer 48:11 closes the loop from the other side: Moab "settled on his
lees… NOT been emptied from vessel to vessel… his TASTE (ta'am) remained
in him" — the untested nation whose taste never changed — and Zeph 2:9
sentences that same Moab to become SALTPITS. The never-tested ends as
salt-of-desolation.

## The opposite word: tafel (unsalted = foolish = untempered)

One Hebrew word, H8602, spans the whole negative:
- Job 6:6 — "UNSAVOURY" (the thing salt would fix)
- Job 1:22 — "charged God FOOLISHLY"; Job 24:12 — "FOLLY"
- Jer 23:13 — "I have seen FOLLY in the PROPHETS of Samaria"
- Lam 2:14 — "thy prophets have seen VAIN AND FOOLISH things… they have
  not discovered thine iniquity"
- Ezek 13:10-15; 22:28 — the false prophets who cry "Peace, and there
  was no peace" daub the wall with "UNTEMPERED morter" (tafel), and the
  wall falls.

So the unsalted word is Scripture's picture of the false prophet:
foolish, untempered whitewash that hides iniquity instead of exposing
it. And Matt 5:13's Greek carries the same double sense — salt that
"mōranthē," becomes foolish. Jesus' warning to disciples is precisely:
do not become tafel-prophets. The fate matches: cast out and "trodden
under foot" — the Hebrew Gospels' note flags mirmas, Isaiah's
judgment-word for the vineyard "trodden down" (Isa 5:5; 28:18).

## Mark 9:49-50 — the priestly frame

"Every one shall be SALTED WITH FIRE, and every SACRIFICE shall be
salted with salt" — Lev 2:13 applied to persons: the disciples are the
offerings, and the salting is fire, the refiner's testing (Mal 3:2-3,
purifying the sons of Levi; 1 Cor 3:13, the fire that tries every man's
work). Then: "Have salt IN YOURSELVES, and have PEACE one with another"
— the Hebrew Gospels pair melach with shalom, and the two priestly
perpetuities stand side by side in Torah: the covenant of SALT (Num
18:19) and the covenant of PEACE (Num 25:12-13). Salt in yourselves =
the covenant's judgment carried within = peace between you.

Col 4:6 gives the speech application: "Let your speech be alway with
grace, SEASONED WITH SALT, that ye may know how ye ought to ANSWER every
man" — the salted word is the tested word, the opposite of tafel
whitewash. James 3:12 keeps the registers apart: no fountain yields both
salt water and fresh (salt sea = the peoples, ch. 40; living water = the
Spirit/word — the two waters never mix in one source).

## Second witness — Hebrew Gospels

- Matt 5:13: "You are salt (melach) in the WORLD (olam)… if the salt is
  ANNULLED (from batel, to annul/void — covenant-legal language) as to
  its TASTE (ta'am), with what will it be salted (yumlach)?"
- Mark 9:50: melach/shalom pairing explicit; "with what shall we SEASON"
  uses nevashel (bashal, to cook) — seasoning as cooking, the working of
  food.
- Mark 9:49 is textually difficult in the Hebrew ("refined by fire" /
  yit'alem) — treat the Greek as primary here; the HG rendering still
  lands on refining.
- Luke 14:34: salt become "EMPTY" (reqanit, Mishnaic 'void') — the empty
  word.

## Wordplay (second dimension — flagged, never the definition)

- Isa 51:6 — "the heavens shall VANISH AWAY (nimlachu) like smoke": the
  same consonants as salt carry "dissolve/disperse." The un-covenanted
  creation is salted away; cf. "they that dwell therein shall die in
  like manner."
- mallach (H4419), the MARINER — the salt-sea man (Jonah 1:5; Ezek
  27:9, 27-29): those who make their living on the sea of the peoples.
- NOT melech (king): final consonant differs (cheth vs kaph). No claim.

## Counter-texts weighed

- **2 Kings 2:20-21** — Elisha heals Jericho's deadly waters WITH salt
  ("there shall not be from thence any more death or BARREN land").
  This kills any "salt = barrenness" definition: here salt ENDS
  barrenness. It fits the permanence reading: covenant salt, from a NEW
  cruse, spoken with "Thus saith the LORD," seals the waters whole — the
  same both-ways turning as Ps 107:33-35. The definition must be the
  preserved covenant state, not the desolation pole alone.
- **Geography (9×)** — Salt Sea/Valley of Salt are literal places; but
  the Salt Sea is the sea of death the living river heals (Ezek 47), so
  the register is consistent, not symbolic per occurrence.
- **Ezek 16:4** — unsalted newborn is care/covenant omitted, not
  judgment; consistent with the covenant-rite reading.

## Candidate definition (pending author approval)

**Salt** — the covenant made permanent: the tested judgment (ta'am) that
keeps whatever it touches in its state — the offering in its savor, the
loyal in their bond, the cursed land in its curse. Commanded on every
offering as "the salt of the covenant" (Lev 2:13); a "covenant of salt"
is a covenant forever (Num 18:19; 2 Chr 13:5). The disciples are the
salt of the earth: those whose senses are exercised to discern (Heb
5:14), preserving the earth as an acceptable offering; salt gone foolish
(tafel — unsavoury, untempered) is the false prophet's whitewash, cast
out and trodden down (mirmas).

Opposite (reciprocal, if an entry is made): tafel — the untempered/
unsalted word. Related, not opposite: Milk/Meat/Butter (the taste=test
spine); Fire (the salting agent of persons, Mark 9:49).

## The sower circuit (author's assembly, 2026-07-23)

Land/ground = heart is Luke's own equation (Luke 8:15 "that on the good
GROUND… in an honest and good HEART"; Matt 13:19 "sown IN HIS HEART";
see research-earth.md). Assembled with the salt findings:

1. **Salt of the earth** = the faithful preserving the field of hearts
   as an acceptable offering (every sacrifice salted, Mark 9:49).
2. **Annulled salt = annulled covenant → trodden.** Isa 28:18 carries
   the exact pair behind Matt 5:13 (HG: salt "ANNULLED as to its
   taste"… trodden): "your covenant with death shall be DISANNULLED
   (kaphar — the atonement verb)… then ye shall be TRODDEN DOWN
   (mirmas)." Same verbs, same order; mirmas is the vineyard's judgment
   word (Isa 5:5).
3. **Trodden salt becomes the wayside.** Luke 8:5 "it was TRODDEN DOWN,
   and the fowls devoured it" — the wayside is a heart packed hard by
   feet (Luke 8:12). The savorless disciple, cast onto the ground and
   trodden under foot of men, becomes the unsowable path-heart.
4. **Luke 14:35** — savorless salt "neither fit for the LAND (ge), nor
   the dunghill": useless to the soil even as fertilizer.
5. **Heb 6:7-8** — two paragraphs after the milk/meat rebuke, the
   earth-heart bearing thorns is "nigh unto CURSING; whose end is to be
   BURNED" = Deut 29:23's salt-curse pattern (brimstone, salt, BURNING,
   nothing sown) applied to a heart.

Circuit: the faithful are salt keeping the earth of hearts fit for
seed; salt whose covenant is annulled is trodden into the very ground
it failed to keep (becoming the wayside), and the unkept field goes to
the salt-curse burning.

## The altar recipe (Lev 2 read with the approved symbols)

The bread-offering chapter legislates the offered word end to end:
unleavened cakes mingled with oil (2:4); no leaven, no honey by fire
(2:11); salt never lacking, "with ALL thine offerings" (2:13). With the
glossary's approved definitions (bread = the word; leaven = corrupt
teaching spreading unseen; oil = proven works) plus the salt candidate:
the word offered to God must be free of corrupting doctrine, mingled
with works, and carried in covenant faithfulness — faithfulness the one
ingredient that may never be lacking. Words ARE offerings by Scripture's
own equation (Heb 13:15 fruit of lips; Hos 14:2 calves of our lips), and
Col 4:6 restates Lev 2:13 for disciples (speech seasoned with salt).

- **Amos 4:5** — the inversion done proudly: "offer a sacrifice of
  thanksgiving WITH LEAVEN… for this liketh you" — leavened worship,
  loved because it pleases.
- **Lev 2:12 / Lev 23:17** — leaven and honey may come as FIRSTFRUITS
  (the two Shavuot wave loaves are "baken with leaven"), just never onto
  the fire: the firstfruits congregation is presented still carrying
  leaven; what enters the testing fire must be unleavened and salted.
- Honey's symbol is not established in the book — its altar ban is
  recorded here as an open question, not decoded.

## Verdict

Pattern coherent across both testaments and both poles; the unity
(covenant permanence + tested judgment) is carried by Scripture's own
definitional texts (Lev 2:13; Num 18:19; Job 6:6 + ta'am usage; H8602
range), not fitted. Strong enough to draft as a chapter section or
glossary entry on the author's word.

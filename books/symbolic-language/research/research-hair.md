# Research: Hair (se'ar / nezer / pera) — author-commissioned (2026-07-23)

Follows research-vine-vow-nazarite.md. No prior entry in glossary,
_symbols/, or research/. All citations verified against kjv_strongs.txt.

Words: H8181 se'ar (hair), H8185 sa'arah (a single hair), H6545 pera
(locks, the Nazarite growth), H5145 nezer (consecration — and, in Jer
7:29, the HAIR itself), H6285 pe'ah (corner — of head, beard, and FIELD),
H1548 galach (shave), H1494 gazaz (shear/poll), H7144 qorchah (baldness);
NT G2359 thrix, G2864 komē, G2751 keirō (shear), G3587 xuraō (shave).

## Scripture's own decodes

1. **Ezek 5:1-5 — hair = the people of the head.** The prophet shaves
   head and beard with a sword-for-razor, WEIGHS the hair, divides it in
   thirds — burned in the city, smitten with the knife, scattered to the
   wind — "also take thereof A FEW in number, and BIND THEM IN THY
   SKIRTS" (v3; even of those, some go back into the fire, v4). Then the
   key: "Thus saith the Lord GOD; THIS IS JERUSALEM" (v5). The hairs are
   her inhabitants; the shaving is the siege; the few bound in the hem
   are the remnant kept in the skirt (kanaph — the wing/hem of covering).
2. **Micah 1:16 — hair = children.** "Make thee BALD, and poll thee FOR
   THY DELICATE CHILDREN… for they are gone into captivity from thee."
   Baldness is the exile of the offspring who grew from the head.
3. **Jer 7:29 — hair = nezer.** "Cut off thine HAIR (nizrekh, H5145 —
   thy CONSECRATION), O Jerusalem, and cast it away… for the LORD hath
   rejected and forsaken the generation of his wrath." The city's hair
   is her consecrated status; its cutting is her rejection. This verse
   welds the personal (Nazarite) and corporate (Ezek 5) registers with
   one word.
4. **Isa 7:20 — the razor is a hired empire.** "The Lord [shall] shave
   with a RAZOR THAT IS HIRED… by the king of Assyria, the head, and the
   hair of the feet: and it shall also consume the beard" — the whole
   population, crown to sole, removed by the empire God rents for the
   job.

## The meaning

**Hair is what grows out of the head with time — the head's living
record and living offspring.** Two registers, one image:

- **On a person:** the accumulated, visible record of consecration and
  glory. The Nazarite lets the locks (pera) grow "all the days of his
  separation" (Num 6:5) — the vow made visible, time made substance —
  and "the CONSECRATION (nezer) of his God is upon his head" (6:7): the
  hair IS the crown. A woman's long hair "is a GLORY to her: for her
  hair is given her FOR A COVERING" (1 Cor 11:15) — glory that is given,
  not achieved, worn under a head (11:3, 10). The Ancient of Days wears
  the register perfected: "the hair of his head like the pure wool"
  (Dan 7:9; Rev 1:14) — the glory of unmeasured ages, white.
- **On a corporate head (city, nation):** the people who grew from her —
  Ezek 5's inhabitants, Micah 1:16's children, Isa 7:20's population,
  Ezek 16:7's "thine hair is grown" (Jerusalem's coming-of-age). The
  bride's hair "as a flock of goats, that appear from mount Gilead"
  (Song 4:1) — her multitude seen as flocks; "the king is HELD in the
  galleries [locks]" (Song 7:5) — the head captive in his people's love.

**The numbered hairs.** If hairs are the Head's people, the idiom "there
shall not one hair of his head fall to the ground" (1 Sam 14:45; 2 Sam
14:11; 1 Kgs 1:52) and its NT heirs read at full depth: "the very hairs
of your head are ALL NUMBERED" (Matt 10:30); "there shall not an hair of
your head perish" (Luke 21:18) — spoken TWO VERSES after "some of you
shall they cause to be put to death" (21:16). The paradox is the symbol:
bodies fall, but no part of the record — no member grown from the Head —
is lost (John 17:12; 18:9; Dan 3:27, not a hair singed in the furnace;
Acts 27:34).

## Implications of cutting — who holds the razor decides the meaning

1. **The vower, at the sanctuary — completion.** The Nazarite shaves at
   the tabernacle door and puts the hair "in the fire which is under the
   sacrifice of the peace offerings" (Num 6:18): the accumulated
   consecration-time is itself OFFERED — the record burned up INTO the
   offering. Paul keeps the practice: "having SHORN his head in
   Cenchrea: for he had a vow" (Acts 18:18; cf. 21:24). Cut-as-offering.
2. **God's razor / the enemy's — judgment and exile.** Isa 7:20's hired
   razor; Ezek 5's siege-shaving; Micah 1:16's baldness of captivity;
   Jer 7:29's severed nezer. Cut-as-cutting-off: the people removed from
   the head, the consecration rejected.
3. **Cleansing — the old record erased.** The healed leper shaves ALL
   his hair, twice, eyebrows included (Lev 14:8-9) — re-entering the camp
   hairless as a newborn, the old life's growth gone. The Levites shave
   all their flesh at ordination (Num 8:7); the captive bride shaves her
   head before joining Israel (Deut 21:12). Cut-as-new-birth: history
   removed so a new record can grow.
4. **Betrayal from within — consecration lost.** Samson's seven locks:
   "there hath not come a razor upon mine head; for I have been a
   Nazarite unto God from my mother's womb: if I be shaven, then my
   strength will go from me" (Judg 16:17) — shaved in Delilah's lap,
   "the LORD was departed from him" (16:20). The strength was never in
   the protein; it was the consecration the hair recorded. And the
   gospel in one verse: "Howbeit the hair of his head BEGAN TO GROW
   AGAIN after he was shaven" (16:22) — consecration can regrow.
5. **The forbidden cut — the corner is not yours.** "Ye shall not round
   the CORNERS (pe'ah) of your heads, neither shalt thou mar the corners
   of thy beard" (Lev 19:27) — the SAME word and the SAME chapter as the
   field law: "thou shalt not wholly reap the CORNERS (pe'ah) of thy
   field… thou shalt leave them for the poor and stranger" (19:9-10).
   The edge of your growth — field or face — belongs to God and is not
   self-harvested; the pagan cut styles the record for the dead (Deut
   14:1; Lev 21:5). Priests hold the middle: "neither shave… nor suffer
   their locks to grow long; they shall ONLY POLL" (Ezek 44:20) —
   service keeps the record trimmed, neither erased nor gloried in.
6. **Counterfeit glory — self-grown, self-weighed.** Absalom polls
   yearly and WEIGHS the harvest "at 200 shekels after the king's
   weight" (2 Sam 14:26) — glory as commodity — and his head's glory
   catches him in the oak and hangs him between heaven and earth (18:9).
   Rev 9:8's locusts wear "hair as the hair of women" — borrowed
   covering-glory on destroyers.

## Loose witnesses

- Mourning baldness (Job 1:20; Isa 22:12; Amos 8:10; Ezra 9:3) — the
  living cut their record when their people are cut.
- The woman who wipes Jesus' feet WITH HER HAIR (Luke 7:38, 44; John
  12:3) — her glory-covering laid on his feet; glory spent as service.
- 2 Kgs 2:23 — "Go up, thou bald head": Elisha mocked as shorn just
  after Elijah went up; the taunt reads his master's departure as
  Elisha's lost covering.
- Ezek 16:7 "thine hair is grown" — growth over time as maturity.

## Candidate definition (pending author approval)

**Hair** — what grows from the head over time: the head's living record
and offspring. On a person, the visible accumulation of consecration and
given glory (the Nazarite's nezer-locks; the woman's covering); on a
city or nation, her people and children (Ezek 5:5 "this is Jerusalem";
Micah 1:16). The meaning of a cutting is decided by whose hand holds the
razor: offered at the sanctuary it completes a vow; shaved in cleansing
it erases the old record for new growth; taken by God's hired razor it
is judgment and exile; taken by betrayal it is consecration lost — yet
hair grows again (Judg 16:22). The corners (pe'ah) are never
self-harvested. "Not an hair of your head shall perish": nothing grown
from the Head is ever lost.

Related: Head (authority), Kanaph/skirt (the remnant bound in the hem,
Ezek 5:3), Crown/nezer, Bramble-thorns (the mock crown).

## The rules of the gate (author's assembly, 2026-07-23)

**The one-door law cuts one way or the other (Lev 17:4, 9):** every
offering must come "unto the DOOR of the tabernacle of the
congregation"; offered anywhere else, "that man shall be CUT OFF
(karet) from among his people." At the gate the consecrated growth is
cut and offered; away from the gate, the man is cut off. Someone falls
at the gate either way.

**Completion protocol, exact order (Num 6:13-20):**
1. "He shall be BROUGHT unto the door" (v13) — presented, not strolling.
2. Brings burnt (he-lamb), sin (ewe-lamb), peace (ram), unleavened
   cakes/wafers with oil, minchah AND drink offerings — wine (vv14-15).
3. Sin first, then burnt (v16): atonement precedes everything.
4. Peace offering + bread + minchah + DRINK OFFERING (v17): the wine is
   POURED OUT to God before the Nazarite drinks any — wine poured
   before wine drunk (cf. 2 Tim 4:6, spendomai).
5. THEN the shave, at the door itself (v18): the hair into the fire
   UNDER the peace offering — the grown record of consecrated time
   becomes fuel beneath the fellowship sacrifice, ascending with it.
6. Boiled shoulder + cake + wafer placed ON THE NAZARITE'S HANDS and
   waved (v19) — the priestly ORDINATION gesture (Ex 29:24, the filling
   of the hands): the lay vower ends the vow in ordination posture.
7. "And AFTER THAT the Nazarite may drink wine" (v20).

**Broken vow (6:9-12):** defilement → shave day 7 (that hair is NOT
offered — defiled growth never reaches the fire), doves at the door day
8, atonement + trespass lamb, re-consecrate — and "the days that were
before SHALL BE LOST": the prior record falls void; restart from zero.

**The 144,000 as the numbered hairs of the Head.** Hair = the
consecrated, peculiar-priesthood company (Ex 19:5-6 → 1 Pet 2:9),
numbered on the Head (Matt 10:30) — and Revelation numbers the sealed:
144,000 (Rev 7:4), sealed IN THEIR FOREHEADS (7:3; 14:1), "redeemed
from among men, the FIRSTFRUITS" (14:4) — cut from the head at the
Door, which is Christ (John 10:9); record offered into the fire under
the peace offering; hands filled as priests (Rev 5:10; 20:6); after
that, the new wine (see research-vine-vow-nazarite.md). Real-life
anchor (anchor, not proof): a typical human scalp carries roughly
100,000-150,000 hairs — 144,000 is the hair-count of a head.

## Verdict

Two registers, one structure, each anchored by an explicit in-text
decode (Ezek 5:5; Micah 1:16; Jer 7:29's nezer; 1 Cor 11:15's "for a
covering"). The cutting taxonomy is carried by the razor-holder pattern
across all cases with no forced texts. Ready for the author's placement
call; pairs naturally with the vine-vow/Nazarite study — see that
notebook's 'Nezer joins hair and vine' section: Lev 25:5, 11 calls the
unpruned sabbath-year vine a NAZIR (H5139), making hair and vine one
symbol (growth left uncut in holy time), with John 15:1-2 assigning the
pruning knife to the Father exactly as the razor-holder pattern holds.

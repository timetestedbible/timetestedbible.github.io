# Research: Bramble / Thorns / Thistles / Briers (author-commissioned symbol)

Author 2026-07-04: "bramble (thorns, thistles)… is a symbol, those consumed
by the cares of this world and are burnt by fire… capture this symbol, do a
full study using our process… link it everywhere brambles and thorns are
quoted." All verses verified against kjv.json 2026-07-04.

## The Teacher's own key (graded symbol)

- Matt 13:22 — "he that received seed among the THORNS is he that heareth
  the word; and the CARE OF THIS WORLD, and the DECEITFULNESS OF RICHES,
  choke the word, and he becometh unfruitful." Luke 8:14 adds "and
  PLEASURES of this life." Jesus decoded this one Himself — thorns = the
  cares/riches/pleasures that consume, and the man consumed by them.

## Derivation the disciples already had

- Gen 3:17-18 — the curse-ground's own crop: "cursed is the ground…
  THORNS also and THISTLES shall it bring forth to thee." Thorns are what
  the cursed earth produces where food should grow.
- Jotham's parable (Judg 9:8-15) — the BRAMBLE offered kingship over the
  trees: "put your trust in MY SHADOW: and if not, let FIRE come out of
  the bramble" — the worthless ruler's shade is a false shadow, and the
  bramble's gift is fire (the shadow chapter's counterfeit).
- 2 Sam 23:6-7 — "the sons of Belial shall be all of them as THORNS
  thrust away… UTTERLY BURNED with fire" (David's last words).
- The burn-verdict is fixed: Heb 6:8 "that which beareth thorns and briers
  is rejected… whose END IS TO BE BURNED"; Isa 33:12 "as thorns cut up
  shall they be BURNED in the fire"; Nah 1:10 "folden together as thorns…
  devoured as stubble fully dry"; Isa 9:18 "wickedness burneth as the
  fire: it shall devour the briers and thorns"; Isa 10:17 "burn and devour
  his thorns and his briers IN ONE DAY."
- Fruitlessness: Matt 7:16 "Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of
  thistles?"; Prov 24:30-31 the slothful man's field "all grown over with
  thorns"; Job 31:40 "thistles instead of wheat"; Isa 5:6 the judged
  vineyard grows "briers and thorns"; Mic 7:4 "the best of them is as a
  brier."
- Way-markers: Prov 22:5 "thorns and snares are in the way of the
  froward"; Jer 4:3 "sow not among thorns" (the heart's old crop);
  Hos 2:6 the hedge of thorns (barrier use — note, not the person-sense).
- Reversal at restoration: Isa 55:13 "instead of the thorn shall come up
  the fir tree."
- The crown of thorns (Matt 27:29) — the curse-ground's crop platted and
  set on His head: He wore the curse (Gen 3:18) the day He bore it.

## Meaning (glossary)

Bramble, thorns, thistles: the curse-ground's crop — those consumed by
the cares of this world, the deceitfulness of riches, and the pleasures of
this life; fruitless, choking the word, offering a false shadow, and ending
in fire.

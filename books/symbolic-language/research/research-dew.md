# Research: Dew (tal) — author's hypothesis: dew = manna from heaven = word (2026-07-25)

Hypothesis to test (author): dew = manna from heaven = the word.
Full occurrence sweep verified against kjv_strongs.txt: tal H2919, 30
occurrences; Aramaic tal H2920, 5 (Daniel 4-5). No NT occurrence of dew;
the NT carries the chain through manna (John 6; Rev 2:17).

## Verdict up front: TESTS TRUE, with an explicit self-decode

**Deut 32:2 is Scripture defining the symbol out loud** — the opening of
Moses' song: "My DOCTRINE shall drop as the rain, my SPEECH shall
distil as the DEW, as the small rain upon the tender herb." Doctrine ::
rain, speech :: dew — the word descending as water from heaven, in two
grades. Seconded by Job 29:22-23 ("my SPEECH DROPPED upon them; and
they waited for me as for the rain… opened their mouth wide as for the
latter rain") and Prov 3:20 ("by his KNOWLEDGE… the clouds drop down
the dew" — dew is dropped by knowledge).

## The manna weld (the hypothesis's middle term)

- **Num 11:9** — "when the DEW fell upon the camp IN THE NIGHT, the
  MANNA fell UPON IT." The word-bread rides the dew down.
- **Ex 16:13-14** — "in the morning the DEW lay round about the host.
  And when the DEW that lay WAS GONE UP, behold… a small round thing" —
  the dew lifts and the bread stands revealed: the descent uncovers
  food.
- **Deut 8:3** — the manna's own stated lesson: "fed thee with manna…
  that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only,
  but by EVERY WORD that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD."
- **Ex 16:4** — and its stated test: "I will rain bread from heaven…
  that I may PROVE them, whether they will walk in MY LAW, or no."
  The manna is explicitly a Torah-walking test (daily gathering, double
  on the sixth, none on the sabbath).
- **Rev 2:17** — the chain's NT terminus: "to him that overcometh will
  I give to eat of the HIDDEN MANNA."

So: dew = the word's nightly, silent descent; manna = the word found as
bread when the dew lifts in the morning; bread = the word (the book's
approved symbol). The hypothesis holds as a three-link chain with each
link stated by Scripture itself.

## The properties carry the meaning

Dew descends at night, without rain, silently, on everything; it is
found early; it is gathered before the heat; **it melts when the sun
waxes hot (Ex 16:21 — the manna does exactly this)**. Scripture uses
every property:

- **Given by the word / withheld by the word:** "there shall not be DEW
  nor rain these years, BUT ACCORDING TO MY WORD" (1 Kgs 17:1 — Elijah
  binds dew to the prophetic word verbatim); withheld as curse (2 Sam
  1:21 Gilboa; Hag 1:10); restored in blessing (Zech 8:12; Gen 27:28;
  Deut 33:13, 28). The word-famine of Amos 8:11 ("not a famine of
  bread… but of HEARING THE WORDS OF THE LORD") is this figure's
  drought.
- **The transient pole — same symbol, failure mode:** "your goodness is
  as a morning cloud, and as the EARLY DEW IT GOETH AWAY" (Hos 6:4;
  13:3). Dew received but not rooted evaporates under the sun — exactly
  the manna melting (Ex 16:21) and exactly the stony-ground hearer:
  "when the SUN was up, it was scorched… no root… when affliction
  ariseth FOR THE WORD'S SAKE" (Mark 4:6, 17). Not a counter-text; the
  symbol's own warning label. The rooted contrast is Job 29:19: "my
  ROOT was spread out by the waters, and the DEW lay all night upon my
  branch" — root below, dew above, the flourishing life.
- **What the dew gives:** the king's FAVOUR (Prov 19:12); the commanded
  blessing, "even LIFE for evermore" (Ps 133:3 — Hermon's dew
  descending on Zion); growth (Hos 14:5); RESURRECTION — "Thy dead men
  shall live… awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust: for THY DEW is as
  the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast out the dead" (Isa 26:19).
  The word falling on dust-dwellers wakes them (cf. the word-begotten:
  "begat he us with the WORD of truth," Jas 1:18; "born again… by the
  WORD of God," 1 Pet 1:23).

## Who is dew

- **God himself, to restored Israel:** "I will be AS THE DEW unto
  Israel: he shall grow as the lily" (Hos 14:5).
- **The Messiah's company, morning-born:** "from the womb of the
  MORNING: thou hast the DEW of thy youth" (Ps 110:3) — the willing
  ones of the day of power as countless morning drops (with Job 38:28's
  "who hath BEGOTTEN the drops of dew?" — begotten drops).
- **The remnant among the nations:** "the REMNANT of Jacob shall be in
  the midst of many people AS A DEW FROM THE LORD… that TARRIETH NOT
  FOR MAN" (Mic 5:7) — the word-begotten become word-carriers, falling
  on the peoples on heaven's schedule, not man's. Dew begets dew.
- **The Bridegroom at the night door:** "it is the voice of my beloved
  that KNOCKETH… my head is filled with DEW, and my locks with the
  drops of the night" (Song 5:2) — the Word stands in the night
  dew-laden, knocking (cf. Luke 12:36; Rev 3:20).

## Gideon's fleece (Judg 6:36-40)

The one narrative where dew falls selectively: on the fleece while all
the ground is dry, then dry on the fleece while dew covers all the
ground — the word/favor resting on Israel alone, then passing to all
the earth while Israel lies dry (Rom 11 shape). Recorded as the sign's
shape; the fleece itself not decoded here.

## Aramaic sister (H2920)

Dan 4:15, 23, 25, 33; 5:21 — Nebuchadnezzar "wet with the DEW of
heaven" seven times "TILL THOU KNOW that the most High ruleth." The
beast-king schooled nightly by heaven's distillation until knowledge
comes — the dew as heaven's persistent teacher even on the humbled.

## Counter-texts weighed

- 2 Sam 17:12 (Hushai: "we will light upon him as the dew falleth") —
  property-use (silent totality), not the symbol; no strain.
- Isa 18:4 ("like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest") — the LORD's
  quiet watching; property-use.
- Dan 4's dew-as-humbling fits the teaching pole (see above).
- No occurrence requires a meaning outside "the word/favor of heaven
  descending" or its transience-property.

## Candidate definition (pending author approval)

**Dew** — the word descending from heaven: silent, nightly, without
rain, found in the morning — "my SPEECH shall distil as the DEW" (Deut
32:2). It carries the bread: the manna falls upon the dew (Num 11:9),
and when the dew lifts, the word lies on the ground as daily food (Ex
16:14; Deut 8:3). Given and withheld by the word of the prophet (1 Kgs
17:1); it brings favor (Prov 19:12), commanded life (Ps 133:3), growth
(Hos 14:5), and resurrection to those who dwell in dust (Isa 26:19).
Received without root it evaporates when the sun is hot (Hos 6:4; Ex
16:21; Mark 4:6, 17). The word-begotten become dew themselves — the
remnant among the peoples, morning-born (Mic 5:7; Ps 110:3).

Related: Bread (the word as food), Milk/Meat (the word worked), Rain
(doctrine, Deut 32:2's parallel line), Sea/Waters (the gathered
opposite: peoples below vs word descending from above).

# Research: Genesis 8:13 — the date-phrase hearings (barishon be'echad la-chodesh)

Question (author, 2026-07-16): the chapter's hearings reread the verse's
body; what of the date? "I have reason to believe it might not be 'in the
first month.'" Is "the One" a way of naming the full moon? If we hear
"on the Whole[-moon] day" inside the Masoretic account, what date falls
out? All claims below tag-verified against kjv_strongs.txt / wlc.txt /
data/hg-chapters/.

## 1. The words and their proofs

**בָּרִאשׁוֹן (barishon)** — the account's date formula elides "month"
after the ordinal (8:5's twin: בָּעֲשִׂירִי בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ — "in the
tenth, on the one, of the month"); the text says only "in the rishon."
- Log: "in the first [month]."
- Sense: "in the former" — Hag 2:9 renders the same word "the FORMER
  [house]."
- Sense: "in the First [One]" — the absolute divine title: "I am the
  FIRST (rishon), and I am the last; and beside me there is no God"
  (Isa 44:6; 48:12; 41:4). No repointing needed.
- Sense — the load-bearing one: "at the HEAD" — rishon's root is rosh,
  and Scripture welds them over this very month at the exodus:
  **Ex 12:2 — "This chodesh is to you the ROSH of chodashim (the head
  of renewals); RISHON it is to you of the renewals of the year"**
  (WLC: החדש הזה לכם ראש חדשים ראשון הוא). "In the rishon" = "at the
  head [renewal]" is Ex 12:2's own naming, instituted at the
  deliverance.
- Wordplay flag only: the letters open with ברא (create) — 8:13 replays
  Gen 1:9 (waters gathered, dry appearing): re-creation echo.

**בְּאֶחָד (be'echad)** — "on the one [day]"; "day" also supplied.
- Log: "on the first [day]" — Gen 1:5 yom echad, KJV's own "the first
  day."
- Sense: echad = the whole/united — Strong's H259 "properly, united";
  Ezra 2:64 "the WHOLE congregation TOGETHER (ke'echad)."
- Sense — the Day: **Zech 14:7 "it shall be ONE DAY (yom echad) which
  shall be known to the LORD, not day, nor night: but… at evening time
  it shall be LIGHT."** A day whose evening is light is the full moon's
  signature and no other's (she rises as the sun sets — the Return on
  the Full Moon geometry). The One Day's own description carries the
  full-moon fingerprint. Zech 14:9 doubles the word as title: "one
  LORD, his name one."
- Echo (wordplay grade): Song 6:9 — "my DOVE, my undefiled is but ONE
  (achat)" — the beloved called achat, spoken of a dove, while Noah's
  dove fills 8:8-12. Pairs with the verse's opening בְּאַחַת: achat…
  echad, one-and-one in a single date.

**לַחֹדֶשׁ (la-chodesh)** — "of the month."
- Sense, KJV's own rendering: "the new moon / the renewal" (chodesh
  rendered "new moon," 1 Sam 20:5; Ps 81:3; root chadash = new).
- Repointing, exact-form proof: **לְחַדֵּשׁ (l'chadesh), "to renew" —
  2 Chr 24:4, the identical letters: "Joash was minded TO REPAIR
  (לְחַדֵּשׁ) the house of the LORD."** Lam 5:21 gives the prayer:
  "RENEW our days as of old."
- Structural: Ps 81:3 pairs chodesh and keseh in one verse ("blow the
  trumpet at the chodesh… at the keseh, our feast day") — the same
  pair Gen 8:13's rehearing carries (la-chodesh… mi-keseh).

## 2. "The One" and the full moon — the verdict

As an attested ancient TITLE, "the One" = full moon is not verifiable
and is not claimed. (Cross-cultural color only: mankind names her by
wholeness — Greek panselēnos "all-moon," Sanskrit pūrṇimā "the complete
one.") Scripture builds the identity itself, every link citable:
1. echad = the whole (Strong's H259; Ezra 2:64);
2. Scripture's own name for the moon at full is "the WHOLE moon" —
   Rev 6:12, Greek critical text (Sinaiticus, Alexandrinus): ἡ σελήνη
   ὅλη — "the whole moon became as blood" — in the SIXTH SEAL, the
   scene the chapter's second hearing identifies;
3. Zech 14:7's One Day is lit at evening — the full moon's signature;
4. the book's calendar (month-head = keseh, Ps 81:3) puts "the one of
   the month" on the full moon by the log-reading too.
So "on the One [day]" = the Whole-moon day stands at citation grade —
argued from Scripture's definitions, not from an unverifiable title.
Bonus: Hebrew Revelation 6:12 reads "the moon was RED (אָדֹם, adom) as
blood" — the Admah/adumah red-root family, the same family the
chapter's sword-close carries (His sword upon the face of the Admah).

## 3. The Masoretic substitution — what date falls out

Substituting "on the Whole[-moon] day of the month" into the received
frame (months headed at the dark moon): the Whole-day is the 15th, so
Gen 8:13 dates the uncovering to the 15TH OF THE FIRST MONTH — and
Scripture names that day: **Num 33:3 — "in the first month, on the
fifteenth day of the first month; on the morrow after the passover the
children of Israel went out with an high hand"** — the departure day,
Ex 12:51's "selfsame day," head of the author's established sequence
(depart the 15th; firstborn claimed / firstfruits / resurrection the
16th). Under the book's calendar (month-head = full moon) the Whole-day
IS day one: hearing and log coincide. THE TWO CALENDARS DISAGREE ABOUT
THE NUMBER AND AGREE ABOUT THE DAY: either way the uncovering sits on
the year's first full moon. The hearing fixes the phase even where the
numbering is disputed — the Return on the Full Moon chapter's
97%-elimination move, written into the flood log.

## 4. The narrative clock frees the word

If barishon is not read "in the first month," the account's own
sequence still delivers the date. Tops seen 10/1 (8:5); "at the end of
forty days" the window opens (8:6 — anchor ambiguous):
- counted from the 10/1 sighting: window ~11/10; doves (7+7+7, with
  8:10's "yet OTHER seven days" implying a first wait) run to ~12/1;
- counted from the tenth month's end: window ~12/10; doves 12/17,
  12/24, and the THIRD DOVE — the one that never returns — flies on
  the next month-head itself, 8:13 following with no silent day.
Under either count the sequence exhausts months 11-12 and the next
"one of the month" the narrative can reach is the year's turn. The
chronology delivers the date on its own; barishon carries no load as a
month-number and is free for its senses (head / former / the First
One). Note the slot-work: the formula appears once with an ordinal
that has no second sense (ba'asiri, the tenth — proving the formula is
just a date), then fills the identical slot with the one ordinal that
is also the head-word and the divine title.

## 5. The assembled date-hearing

**"At the Head renewal — on the Whole-day — of the renewed moon."**
(Ex 12:2 · H259/Rev 6:12/Zech 14:7 · 2 Chr 24:4)
The year's first keseh, named three ways in three words, with the
narrative clock independently walking the reader to that day. It also
answers the singular verb four words later: vayyasar went looking for
a singular subject, and the date had already named Him (the First, the
One).

## 6. The Day of the LORD as the head renewal — the two witnesses split

Author's synthesis: "head renewal / start of renewal is exactly what
the Day of the LORD is in an abstract sense." The NT says it in one
word — **Matt 19:28, "in the REGENERATION (palingenesia, the
renewal-birth) when the Son of man shall sit in the throne of his
glory"** — with Acts 3:21 as apostolic gloss ("the times of RESTITUTION
of all things"). And the Hebrew Matthew renders the same clause **"on
the DAY OF THE JUDGMENT (b'yom ha-din), when the Man sits on the
KISSE (כסא) of his glory."** The two witnesses split the day's two
faces — Renewal (Greek) and Judgment-and-Throne (Hebrew) — and Gen
8:13's rehearing carries both faces in the same letters: the renewal
in לחדש, the sword of judgment in חרבו, and between them מכסה whose
other pointings are the keseh and the kisse. The day of the renewal is
the day of the throne.

## 7. The formula family — one key, four locks

The "redundant" double formula (…on the one, OF THE MONTH) is the
family signature, not 8:13's oddity — Ex 40:2 doubles it harder ("in
the day of the first month, on the one of the month"). The family is
exactly the chapter's Nisan-1 freight list, and the hearing opens
every member as one tableau:
- **Ezra 7:9** — "upon the first day of the first month began he to GO
  UP FROM BABYLON" → the going-up out of Babylon begins at the head
  renewal (Rev 18:4 "Come out of her, my people").
- **Ezek 45:18** — "in the first month, in the first day of the
  month… CLEANSE THE SANCTUARY" → the sanctuary cleansed at the head
  renewal.
- **Ex 40:2** — "on the first day of the first month… SET UP THE
  TABERNACLE" → the dwelling of God raised on the Whole-day of the
  renewal — Rev 21:1-3's sequence: new heaven, new adamah (Heb Rev),
  "the tabernacle of God is with men."
- 2 Chr 29:17 — Hezekiah's temple sanctification begun 1/1.
A key that opens one verse is a coincidence; a key that opens the
verse's family is a definition.

## 8. Status and honesty rails

- The log construal ("in the first month, day one") remains the plain
  first reading — 8:5's twin formula anchors it; every hearing rides
  as second voice, never unseating (the chapter's own law).
- The seeing-events pattern (8:5 and 8:13 — the account's only two
  dated SEEINGS — fall on the month's brightest night under the
  full-moon head, its darkest under the received head; the catastrophe
  of 7:11 falls two days past the dark under the full-moon head) is a
  soft supporting observation, not proof.
- Nothing in the Masoretic arithmetic breaks under any hearing (the
  150-day count concerns month lengths, not heads).

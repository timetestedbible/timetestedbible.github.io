# Winepress (gath / yeqev / purah / lenos) — symbol study (2026-07-10, bonus track)

Three Hebrew lexemes, one installation, disclosed up front (structural, not
lexical): **gath** H1660 (5×: Judg 6:11; Neh 13:15; Isa 63:2 "winefat";
Lam 1:15; Joel 3:13 "press"); **yeqev** H3342 (16×: Num 18:27, 30;
Deut 15:14; 16:13; Judg 7:25; 2 Kgs 6:27; Job 24:11; Prov 3:10; Isa 5:2;
16:10; Jer 48:33; Hos 9:2; Joel 2:24; 3:13 "fats"; Hag 2:16; Zech 14:10);
**purah** H6333 (2×: Isa 63:3; Hag 2:16). The tidy anatomy (gath = treading
trough, yeqev = collecting vat, purah = press-trough) blurs in usage —
yeqev is itself trodden in Job 24:11 and Isa 16:10 — so no freight rides
the trough/vat division; the words name one installation. Joel 3:13 holds
gath AND yeqev in one verse; Hag 2:16 holds yeqev AND purah — the KJV
shuffles "winepress/press/fat/winefat" across all three. Greek: **lenos**
G3025 (Matt 21:33; Rev 14:19, 20 twice; 19:15) and hypolenion G5276, the
under-vat (Mark 12:1). All occurrences pulled from kjv_strongs.txt.

**HG second witness (Matt 21:33):** the Hebrew Matthew reads יקב (yeqev)
and carries Isa 5:2's clause in Isaiah's own consonants — ויבן מגדל בתוכו
וגם יקב חצב בו, letter for letter (data/hg-chapters/Matthew-21.json; the
chapter note flags it: Greek translates the clause, Hebrew Matthew quotes
it, so no hearer could miss whose vineyard this is). CAVEAT (grader find,
per the JSON's own textual notes): Matt 21:33-46 is absent from the base
London ms and supplied from ms D (Howard prints the passage in
parentheses) — for this parable the second witness rests on one
manuscript.

**KJV flattening disclosed:** Deut 16:13 "after that thou hast gathered in
thy corn and thy wine" is literally "from thy threshingfloor and from thy
winepress" (goren H1637 + yeqev H3342) — Tabernacles is dated from the two
installations, and the next verse is "thou shalt REJOICE in thy feast"
(16:14).

## Candidate definition

**The winepress is the vineyard's reckoning — where the fruit a people has
borne is pressed out and rendered to the Owner: works turned into what is
poured. Good grapes burst the press with new wine and the vintage-shout of
joy; wild grapes put the growers themselves under the treading, and what
flows out is blood — "the great winepress of the wrath of God." One
installation, two outflows, and Isa 5:2's fork (grapes or wild grapes)
decides which.** Consistent with the wine draft's frame (draft-wine.adoc):
the wine is pressed from the fruit — deeds become the cup they fill.

## The estate is built FOR the press

- Isa 5:2 — fenced, planted, tower in the midst, "and also *hewed out* a
  winepress therein: and he *looked* that it should bring forth grapes, and
  it brought forth wild grapes" (ASV — quoted from ASV because KJV flattens
  chatsav H2672, "hew," to "made"; the press is cut into rock, dug once and
  for keeps). The press is the purpose clause of the whole property; every
  installation waits on the vintage.
- **The qavah repetition (author, added post-grade; tag-checkable):**
  Isa 5:2 "he LOOKED (qavah H6960, piel) that it should bring forth grapes"
  and Isa 5:7 "he LOOKED (qavah H6960, piel) for judgment… for
  righteousness" — the same verb, five verses apart. Isaiah names the crop
  by repeating the verb: the fruit the press was hewed to catch is judgment
  and righteousness — a nation's works (the book's Fruit symbol), rendered
  at the press.
- Matt 21:33-34 keeps the inventory (lenos; HG yeqev) and dates the plot by
  it: "when the time of the FRUIT drew near, he sent… that they might
  RECEIVE the fruits" — the rent falls due at the press.
- Num 18:27, 30 — the tithe "shall be RECKONED (chashav H2803) unto you…
  as the fulness of the winepress," "COUNTED (chashav) unto the Levites…
  as the increase of the winepress" (grader find: the definition's word
  "reckoning" is the Torah verse's own verb). The press is where increase
  is measured and rendered. Deut 15:14 — the freed slave is furnished "out
  of thy floor, and out of thy winepress." The floor/press pair is
  Scripture's standing figure for the whole yield (Num 18:27; Deut 15:14;
  16:13; 2 Kgs 6:27; Hos 9:2; Joel 2:24).

## The blessing pole

- **Prov 3:9-10** — honour the LORD with the firstfruits "so shall thy barns
  be filled with plenty, and thy presses (yeqev) shall BURST OUT with new
  wine."
- **Joel 2:24** — "the floors shall be full of wheat, and the fats (yeqev)
  shall OVERFLOW (shuq H7783) with wine and oil."
- **Amos 9:13** — restoration: "the treader of grapes [overtakes] him that
  soweth seed; and the mountains shall drop sweet wine."
- **Gen 49:11 (grader find)** — Judah's blessing, the wrath-scene's mirror:
  "he washed his GARMENTS in WINE, and his clothes in the BLOOD of grapes."
  On the blessing pole the garments are washed in the press's wine; in
  Isa 63 they are stained with the press's blood — one image, two vintages,
  and the phrase "blood of grapes" is the fork stated in three words.
- **Judg 9:27 (grader find)** — "[they] trode the grapes, and made merry":
  treading = festival joy, the joy-pole in narrative.
- The press's proper voice is the **heydad** (H1959), the treaders' shout
  (full set: Isa 16:9, 10; Jer 25:30; 48:33 ×3; 51:14): when God judges
  Moab the punishment is a SILENCED press — "the shouting for thy summer
  fruits… is fallen" (Isa 16:9); "the treaders shall tread out no wine in
  their presses; I have made their vintage shouting to cease" (Isa 16:10;
  same figure Jer 48:33, heydad three times in the clause). Judgment on a
  nation = its press stops singing; famine = "out of the barnfloor, or out
  of the winepress?" (2 Kgs 6:27; Hos 9:2; Hag 2:16 — 50 vessels expected
  from the purah, 20 drawn; Job 24:11 — the oppressed tread presses and
  thirst; thematic, no press lexeme: Micah 6:15, treading with darak only).

## The wrath pole

- **Joel 3:13** — "Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe… the press
  (gath) is full, the fats (yeqev) OVERFLOW (shuq H7783); for their
  wickedness is great." **The centerpiece weld: Joel writes the identical
  clause twice** — yeqev + shuq at 2:24 overflowing with wine and oil, yeqev
  + shuq at 3:13 overflowing "for their wickedness is great." One book, one
  press, two vintages; what fills it decides what runs out. Grader
  strengthening: shuq occurs only 3× in Scripture (Ps 65:9 "waterest"), so
  the two overflow-uses of a rare verb ARE the pair — both hiphil (H8689).
- **Jer 25:30** — "he shall give a SHOUT (heydad), as they that TREAD the
  grapes, against all the inhabitants of the earth." The vintage-shout and
  the war-shout are the same word, and Jeremiah says so in terms; Jer 51:14
  raises the heydad over Babylon. Same chapter, fifteen verses earlier: "Take
  the WINE CUP of this fury… cause all the nations… to drink it" (25:15) —
  the press fills the cup. Rev keeps the sequence: the "wine of the wrath of
  God… poured out" (14:10) before the winepress that makes it (14:19), and
  19:15 names it whole — "the winepress of the WINE of the fierceness and
  wrath of Almighty God" (lenos tou oinou). Isa 63:6: "make them drunk in
  my fury."
- **Lam 1:15** — the vineyard put through its own press: "the Lord hath
  trodden (darak) the VIRGIN, the daughter of Judah, as in a winepress
  (gath)." Isa 5:5 had pronounced it — the wall broken down, "and it shall
  be TRODDEN DOWN (mirmas)": the growers become the vintage.
- **Isa 63:1-6** — the Treader from Edom. Two press-words across the
  exchange: "garments like him that treadeth in the WINEFAT (gath)?" (v2);
  "I have trodden the WINEPRESS (purah) alone… their blood shall be
  sprinkled upon my garments, and I will stain all my raiment" (v3). Joins
  the Garments symbol: the vesture reads the verdict. The "blood" here is
  netsach H5332 — grape-juice gore, tagged nowhere else (63:3, 6): people
  pressed, and the word for what splashes is the vintage word. DISCLOSED:
  the only-here claim rests on the Strong's lemma split — the consonants
  נצח are those of netsach H5331 "victory/forever" (42×); by the book's
  consonantal standard that identity is a second dimension, flagged as
  wordplay only (the splash-word of the press spelled like the
  victory-word). The sprinkling verb is nazah H5137, the verb of the
  Servant — "so shall he SPRINKLE many nations" (52:15) — same lexeme,
  different stems (52:15 hiphil, 63:3 qal): the two sprinklings, mercy and
  press, association-grade only. Narrative echo (grader find): Jezebel's
  blood "SPRINKLED (nazah)" on the wall and horses, "and he TRODE her
  under foot (ramas H7429)" (2 Kgs 9:33) — Isa 63:3's exact word-pair
  enacted in a judgment scene. "The day of vengeance… and the year of my
  redeemed" (63:4): one treading, two outcomes.
- **Rev 14:17-20; 19:15** — Joel 3:13 split into two acts: grain sickle
  first (14:14-16, "the harvest of the earth is ripe"), then the vintage —
  "gather the clusters of the vine of the earth; for her grapes are FULLY
  RIPE" (14:18; the press waits for the yield to fill — cf. Gen 15:16,
  association-grade), "the great winepress of the wrath of God" (14:19),
  "trodden WITHOUT THE CITY, and blood came out of the winepress, even unto
  the horse bridles" (14:20). The vintage order issues from the ALTAR-angel
  "which had power over fire" (14:18). Rev 19:13-15 mounts Isa 63 whole:
  vesture dipped in blood, "and he treadeth the winepress."
- **The treading verb weld (grader-checked):** pateo G3961 is the verb of
  Rev 14:20 ("was trodden") and 19:15 ("he treadeth") — and of Luke 21:24,
  "Jerusalem shall be TRODDEN DOWN (pateo) of the Gentiles," and Rev 11:2,
  "the holy city shall they TREAD UNDER FOOT (pateo)." The sentence on the
  vineyard-city is spoken in the winepress's own verb. Full NT set
  disclosed: the verb's one other occurrence is Luke 10:19, "power to
  TREAD on serpents" — treading = subjugation there too; 4 of 5 uses are
  press/city.
- **Without the city:** the press stands outside the enclosure (Rev 14:20);
  the Son was cast "out of the vineyard" and slain (Matt 21:39), suffering
  "without the gate" (Heb 13:12). Grader upgrade: the echo is lexical, not
  only thematic — exō G1854 is the tagged word in all three texts. Still
  filed association-grade — the blood runs outside the wall, both times.
- NOT the passion: Isa 63's blood is the trodden's, not the Treader's —
  "of the people there was none WITH me" (63:3) answers 59:16's "no
  intercessor," and the garments are stained with THEIR blood (63:3). The
  devotional reading that makes Bozrah the cross is declined; Rev 19 confirms
  the direction (the vesture is dipped before the battle He rides to win).

## Literal uses and narrative echoes (counter-text sweep)

The bulk of yeqev's uses are plain agriculture — and even the literal set
holds the shape: the press is the accounting-point of the yield (tithe
reckoned there, Num 18:27; the slave released from there, Deut 15:14; famine
measured there, 2 Kgs 6:27; Hag 2:16). None counter the definition; no text
makes treading-people a blessing, and none makes the full press anything but
yield rendered. Remaining cases:

- **Judg 6:11** — Gideon threshed wheat "by the winepress" (Hebrew ba-gath,
  IN the press) "to hide it from the Midianites": oppression inverts the
  installations — floor-work hidden in the press, the land's yield exacted
  by the wrong lord. Narrative echo, flagged as echo — with its answer at
  **Judg 7:25**: the Midianite prince Zeeb slain "at the winepress of Zeeb."
- **Neh 13:15** — treading presses ON THE SABBATH: the exaction run on the
  day of rest, testified against. Literal command; no symbolic freight
  claimed beyond it.
- **Zech 14:10** — restored Jerusalem measured "from the TOWER of Hananeel
  unto the king's WINEPRESSES": the two vineyard installations bracket the
  healed city, the press now the King's. Association-grade.
- Place-name/wordplay dimension only, no freight claimed: the Philistine
  city Gath (H1661) is "winepress"; the Gittith psalm-titles (Ps 8; 81; 84)
  ride the same root.

## Weighed and dropped

- **Gethsemane:** the gat-shemanim ("oil-press") etymology is Greek/Aramaic-
  side; the Hebrew Matthew reads גיא שמנים, "Gei-Shemanim, the Valley of
  Oils" (HG Matt 26:36 + note) — the second witness counter-witnesses the
  press name, so no Gethsemane-press claim is made.
- **Rabbinic winepress = altar:** Targum Jonathan renders Isa 5:2's tower as
  the sanctuary and its yeqev as the altar ("I gave them my altar to atone").
  Extrabiblical background only — noted because it independently corroborates
  the book's tower = sanctuary finding, and because Rev 14:18's vintage order
  does issue from the altar; the identification itself is not claimed.

## Glossary entry

Glossary entry [[sym-winepress]] (Refined — no badge): "The vineyard's
reckoning — where the fruit a people has borne is pressed out and rendered
to the Owner: presses bursting with new wine when the grapes are good; the
growers themselves trodden, and blood for wine, when the vintage is
wickedness." Seerefs: Isa 5:2, 7; Prov 3:10; Joel 2:24 with 3:13;
Lam 1:15; Isa 63:3; Rev 14:19-20.

# Every "tail" in Scripture — the lying prophet at the serpent's end (2026-07-08)

Working reference behind the Fall of Babylon's rod-serpent section. Coverage: the
complete tail families — Hebrew *zanav* H2180 (9 verses) and Greek *oura* G3769
(3 verses, all in Revelation) — verified against kjv_strongs.txt. Twelve
occurrences in all; nothing dropped.

---

## The finding

The tail is **defined twice and used consistently**: by covenant rank (Deut 28)
and by office (Isa 9:15). Head and tail are the covenant's own vocabulary for
ruler and ruled — "the LORD shall make thee the **head**, and not the **tail**...
if thou hearken unto the commandments" (Deut 28:13), inverted in the curse: "he
shall be the head, and thou shalt be the tail" (28:44). And Isaiah decodes which
member of the body politic the tail is: "the ancient and honourable, he is the
head; and **the prophet that teacheth lies, he is the tail**" (Isa 9:15) — with
the mechanism in the next verse: "the leaders of this people cause them to err;
and **they that are led of them are destroyed**" (9:16).

Run every occurrence through that definition and the serpent-texts open:

- **Ex 4:4** — Moses is commanded to seize the serpent **by the tail** — at its
  lying-prophet end — and the *chazaq* grip turns it back into the staff of God.
  The liar's instrument taken in hand is mastered.
- **Rev 12:4** — "his **tail** drew the third part of the **stars** of heaven,
  and did cast them to the earth." Stars are the saints (the book's stars
  symbol), and Daniel supplies the twin with the trampling: the little horn
  "cast down some of the host and of the **stars** to the ground, and **stamped**
  upon them" (Dan 8:10 — the trample-chain again; cf. Dan 8:24, "shall destroy
  the mighty and the **holy people**"). The dragon fells a third of heaven's
  host not with claws but with its tail — by false teaching — exactly Isa 9:16's
  mechanism: the led destroyed by the misleading.
- **Rev 9:10, 19** — the trumpet armies' "power was to hurt men" **in their
  tails** (9:10), and at the sixth trumpet: "their power is in their **mouth**,
  and in their **tails**: for their tails were like unto **serpents, and had
  heads**" (9:19). Tails grown heads is Deut 28:44 executed — the lying prophet
  promoted to rulership, the covenant curse in one clause of anatomy. Power in
  mouth *and* tail: the word corrupted at both ends.
- **Isa 7:4** — Rezin and Pekah are "the two **tails** of these smoking
  firebrands" — two kings dismissed as burnt-out tail-ends: rulers who are, by
  Isaiah's own lexicon, lying-prophet stubs about to gutter out.
- **Isa 19:15** — Egypt left with no work "which the head or tail, branch or
  rush, may do" — the whole rank-ladder idled, top to bottom.

**Edge uses (literal, carried for completeness):** Judg 15:4 — Samson binds the
foxes "tail to tail" with a firebrand between: the vineyard-spoilers (Song 2:15)
joined at the liar's end with fire, loosed into standing grain — a weaponized
similitude worth its own look if the Judges material ever gets a chapter. Job
40:17 — behemoth "moveth his tail like a cedar": the beast's tail with the
stature of the towering-power tree.

## The connection this strengthens

In the Fall of Babylon's rod-serpent similitude, the grasp-by-the-tail is not
stage direction; it is the countermove. The dragon's tail is its lying-prophet
corps — the member that swept a third of the stars down — and the restoration of
the rod begins by seizing precisely that member. Aaron's rod is recovered at the
liar's end; Rev 20:2's laying-hold binds the same old serpent. And the woman of
Rev 17 who rides the beast wears her lie on her forehead while her power runs on
the tail: the mother of harlots is served by the prophets that teach lies (cf.
"thy prophets have seen vain and foolish things for thee," Lam 2:14).

**Glossary candidate — Tail:** the lying prophet; covenant rank inverted (the
led-astray end of the body politic). Defined Deut 28:13, 44; decoded Isa 9:15-16;
wielded Rev 12:4; enthroned Rev 9:19. (Run the delta grading before adding.)

# Grace, Mercy, and the Gift of God — word recovery study (2026-07-10)

Candidate chapter. Method: 3 blind proxies (no repo context) stated the traditional
definitions; concordance study run against kjv_strongs; author's 7-point framework
tested below. These are WORD RECOVERIES (translation/tradition flattening), not symbols.

## The traditional definitions (3 blind proxies, unanimous)

- **Grace** = "unmerited favor," "getting what you don't deserve"; acrostic G.R.A.C.E. =
  "God's Riches At Christ's Expense." Texts: Eph 2:8-9; Rom 3:24; 2 Cor 12:9.
- **Mercy** = "NOT getting the punishment you DO deserve." Universal paired formula:
  grace gives the undeserved good, mercy withholds the deserved bad. Courtroom
  illustration: mercy cancels the sentence, grace adopts the criminal. Texts:
  Lam 3:22-23; Eph 2:4-5; Titus 3:5.
- **Gift of God** = salvation/eternal life, free, "can only be received, never earned."
  Texts: Rom 6:23; Eph 2:8; John 4:10. (Secondary: the Spirit, Acts 2:38.)

## The words

- **Grace**: chen (H2580, from chanan H2603 "to favor/be gracious"); first use Gen 6:8
  "Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD." Greek charis (G5485).
- **Mercy**: chesed (H2617) — rendered "mercy" ~138x in KJV — covenant loyalty,
  steadfast love; the Deut 7:9 hendiadys "keepeth COVENANT AND MERCY." Also
  racham/rachamim (H7355/H7356) — same consonants as rechem, WOMB (H7358): mercy as
  womb-love, mother-compassion (Isa 49:15; 1 Kgs 3:26 "her bowels yearned" — rachamim).
  Wordplay dimension, flag as such. Greek eleos (G1656).
- **Gift**: charisma (Rom 6:23 — grace-gift), dorea (John 4:10; Eph)..

## Defining verses — grace

1. **Ex 34:6-7** — God's self-proclamation (the NAME): "merciful (rachum) and gracious
   (chanun)… abundant in goodness (chesed) and TRUTH (emet), keeping mercy (chesed) for
   thousands, forgiving iniquity… and that will BY NO MEANS CLEAR THE GUILTY." Both
   poles inside the Name itself.
2. **John 1:17** — "grace and truth came by Jesus Christ" = the Ex 34:6 pair (chesed
   ve'emet) walking; NOT law-versus-grace — grace arrives WITH the truth, and truth =
   the law (Ps 119:142). The verse tradition uses to retire the law actually quotes
   the law-giving scene's own vocabulary.
3. **Rom 5:8 (author's #6)** — "while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us" — the
   verse says LOVE commended; grace named at 5:15-21. Grace moves FIRST, toward the
   undeserving — the traditional "unmerited" is correct here.
4. **Titus 2:11-12** — grace's job description: "the grace of God that bringeth
   salvation… TEACHING us that, denying ungodliness… we should live soberly,
   righteously, and godly." Grace is a tutor toward obedience, not an exemption from it.
5. **Jude 4** — the counterfeit named: "turning the grace of our God into
   lasciviousness." Rom 6:1 guards the same door: "shall we continue in sin that grace
   may abound? God forbid."
6. 2 Cor 12:9 — grace as sustaining power ("my grace is sufficient… strength made
   perfect in weakness").

**Recovered: grace = God's favor that moves first — toward the undeserving, before any
deserving (Rom 5:8; Gen 6:8) — and that, having arrived, TEACHES the receiver to live
righteously (Titus 2:11-12). "Unmerited favor" is true but half: tradition stops before
grace's job description, and the counterfeit (license) is named inside the canon.**

## Defining verses — mercy

1. **Deut 7:9** — "the faithful God, which keepeth COVENANT AND MERCY (chesed) with them
   that LOVE him and KEEP HIS COMMANDMENTS" (so 1 Kgs 8:23; Ps 103:17-18 "his mercy…
   upon them that fear him… to such as KEEP HIS COVENANT"). Chesed lives inside the
   covenant.
2. **Prov 28:13** — the mechanics: "whoso CONFESSETH AND FORSAKETH them shall have
   mercy" (vs. "he that covereth his sins shall not prosper"). Isa 55:7: forsake way
   AND thoughts, return — "he will have mercy… abundantly pardon."
3. **Ezek 33:14-16** — the cancellation clause: turn from sin, do that which is lawful
   and right, RESTORE the pledge, give again what he robbed — "NONE of his sins that he
   hath committed shall be MENTIONED unto him." The ledger wiped on repentance.
4. **Matt 18:23-35 (author's #7)** — mercy enacted as DEBT-cancellation: "moved with
   compassion… FORGAVE HIM THE DEBT" (10,000 talents; granted "because thou desiredst
   me" — asked for). And the boundary: reinstated for the unmerciful — "delivered him to
   the tormentors… so likewise shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from
   your hearts forgive not." Col 2:14 gives the instrument: "blotting out the
   HANDWRITING (cheirographon, the debt-note) that was against us… nailing it to his
   cross."
5. **The reciprocity circuit** (sharpest divergence from tradition): Matt 5:7 ("blessed
   are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy"); Matt 6:14-15; James 2:13 ("judgment
   without mercy, that hath shewed no mercy"); Micah 6:8 (LOVE mercy — something you DO);
   Matt 23:23 — mercy is one of "the WEIGHTIER MATTERS OF THE LAW." Tradition defines
   mercy purely as received; Scripture wires it as covenant practice, and receiving is
   conditioned on practicing.
6. Hos 6:6 "I desired mercy (chesed), and not sacrifice" (Jesus quotes twice, Matt 9:13;
   12:7) — joins the Samuel obey-over-offering line (1 Sam 15:22).

**Recovered: mercy = chesed, covenant loyalty — the debt canceled for the one who
confesses, forsakes, and restores (Prov 28:13; Ezek 33:14-16; Matt 18:27; Col 2:14),
kept "with them that love him and keep his commandments" (Deut 7:9), required of the
pardoned (Matt 18:35; Jas 2:13), and womb-deep in its feeling (racham/rechem).
"Not getting what you deserve" describes the transaction's surface; Scripture gives its
conditions, its instrument (the canceled debt-note), and its obligation.**

## Defining verses — the gift

- **Rom 6:23** — the antithesis is the verse's own structure: WAGES (opsonia, soldier's
  pay) vs GIFT (charisma). Death is earned; life never is. (Author's #2, #3, #5 all in
  this one verse + 1 John 3:4 + Rom 3:23.)
- **Eph 2:8-10** — "by grace… through faith… the GIFT of God: not of works, lest any man
  should boast" — AND v10: "created in Christ Jesus UNTO GOOD WORKS, which God hath
  before ordained that we should WALK IN THEM." The granted-then-worn pattern
  (= Rev 19:8a + Eph 2:10 in the Garments chapter). Gift ≠ wages; gift → walk.
- John 4:10 (living water); Jas 1:17.

## Honest counter-texts, weighed

- **Titus 3:5** ("not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his
  mercy he saved us") and **Rom 11:6** ("if by grace, then is it no more of works") —
  the tradition's strongest cards. They hold, and the recovery AGREES with them: the
  gap is unclosable by obedience (Luke 17:10 — "when ye shall have done ALL… unprofitable
  servants"; Ps 49:7-8 — none can ransom his brother). The recovered definitions never
  make mercy wages; they state mercy's covenant CONDITIONS (repentance, forgiving
  others) and grace's TEACHING office — which the same epistle states three verses
  earlier (Titus 2:11-12) and Eph 2:10 states one verse later.

## The author's 7-point framework, tested

1. God is perfect — Deut 32:4; Matt 5:48. HOLDS.
2. Wages of sin = death; sin = transgression of the law — Rom 6:23a; 1 John 3:4. HOLDS
   (already book equations).
3. All sinned, earned death — Rom 3:23; Ezek 18:4. HOLDS.
4. Repentance = confess + plead guilty + change heart — Prov 28:13 (confess AND
   forsake); Ps 51:3-4 (plead guilty "that thou mightest be justified when thou
   speakest"); Ezek 18:30-31 ("make you a new heart"); 1 John 1:9. HOLDS, with ONE
   REFINEMENT: Scripture adds RESTITUTION where possible (Ezek 33:15 restore the
   pledge, give again the robbed; Luke 19:8 Zacchaeus) — a fourth element.
5. Gift of God = eternal life — Rom 6:23b verbatim. HOLDS.
6. Grace = while we were yet sinners, Christ died — Rom 5:8 (verse says LOVE; grace
   named 5:15-21). HOLDS as grace's TIMING (favor that moves first); add grace's
   CONTENT: it teaches (Titus 2:11-12); counterfeit = license (Jude 4).
7. Mercy = canceling the debt — Matt 18:27; Col 2:14 (the nailed debt-note); Ezek
   33:16 ("none of his sins shall be mentioned"). HOLDS, with Scripture's own two
   boundaries: granted on asking/repentance (Matt 18:32 "because thou desiredst me";
   Prov 28:13), revoked from the unmerciful (Matt 18:34-35; Jas 2:13; Matt 6:15) —
   Ex 34:6-7 holds both poles in the Name.

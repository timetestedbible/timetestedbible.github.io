# Research: Lion (and the beasts of prey) — behind the sym-lion entry

Done 2026-07-02 while re-reading Isa 35:8-9 in ch. 7 (the highway: "no
lion… nor any ravenous beast"). The lion is a type of the sym-beast
(beast = kingdom/nation, Dan 7:17), and Scripture defines it in
dictionary form.

## Explicit definitions

- Prov 28:15 — "As a roaring LION, and a ranging BEAR; so is a WICKED
  RULER over the poor people." (Dictionary form: lion/bear = wicked
  ruler.)
- Zeph 3:3 — "Her PRINCES within her are roaring LIONS; her JUDGES are
  evening WOLVES."
- Ezek 22:25, 27 — prophets "like a roaring LION ravening the prey…
  they have made her many WIDOWS"; "her PRINCES… like WOLVES ravening
  the prey."
- Ezek 19 — the lament "for the PRINCES of Israel" told entirely as
  lions: the lioness-mother, the whelp taken in chains to Egypt
  (Jehoahaz), the second brought to the king of Babylon.
- Jer 50:17 — "Israel is a scattered SHEEP; the LIONS have driven him
  away: FIRST THE KING OF ASSYRIA… and LAST THIS NEBUCHADREZZAR."
- Dan 7:4 — Babylon "like a LION" (first beast); Jer 4:7 "the LION is
  come up from his thicket, and the DESTROYER OF THE GENTILES is on his
  way."
- Joel 1:6 — "a NATION is come up upon my land… whose teeth are the
  teeth of a LION."
- Nah 2:11-12 — Nineveh = "the dwelling of the LIONS… filled his holes
  with prey."
- Isa 5:29 — the invading nation "shall roar like young lions… lay hold
  of the prey… and NONE SHALL DELIVER" — the exact counter-image to the
  highway where "the redeemed shall walk" (Isa 35:9).
- NT: the devil "as a roaring LION… seeking whom he may DEVOUR"
  (1 Pet 5:8); Paul "delivered out of the mouth of the lion"
  (2 Tim 4:17, of Nero's Rome).

## The rightful Lion

The figure is royal, not merely evil: "Judah is a LION's whelp… who
shall rouse him up?" (Gen 49:9) → "the LION of the tribe of Juda… hath
prevailed" (Rev 5:5). The lion = the predator-king; the question, as
with worship and shadow, is WHOSE.

## The healing of the symbol

Isa 11:6-9 / 65:25 — "the LION shall eat straw like the ox… They shall
not hurt nor destroy in all my holy MOUNTAIN" — the predator-kingdoms
tamed inside the kingdom of God; same promise as the highway's "no lion
shall be there" (Isa 35:9) and Ps 91:13 ("tread upon the lion…").

## Meaning

Lion = the devouring ruler or empire — the beast-kingdom at its most
royal and most predatory; Assyria, Babylon, wicked princes, the devil
himself. Its rightful holder is the King of Judah's line, and in His
kingdom the lion is tamed: nothing hurts or destroys on the holy
mountain, and no lion walks the highway of holiness.

# Disciple — the one in whom the law is sealed (2026-07-09)

Gap: 274 KJV occurrences, no glossary entry. Verified against kjv_strongs.

## Scripture's own entry conditions

Jesus does not leave "disciple" to the dictionary; He states conditions,
three times with "cannot":
- "If ye *continue in my word*, then are ye my disciples INDEED" (John
  8:31) — indeed: real against nominal.
- "By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have *love*
  one to another" (John 13:35) — and love is the keeping (1 John 5:3).
- "Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much *fruit*; so shall ye
  be my disciples" (John 15:8) — fruit: works (glossary).
- "If any man come to me, and *hate not* his father, and mother… CANNOT be my disciple" (Luke 14:26).
- "whosoever doth not *bear his cross*… CANNOT be my disciple" (Luke 14:27).
- "whosoever… *forsaketh not all* that he hath, he CANNOT be my disciple"
  (Luke 14:33).

## The Hebrew definition

The OT's disciple-word appears in the law's own custody clause: "Bind up
the testimony, **seal the law among my disciples** (limmudim)" (Isa 8:16)
— a disciple is one in whom the law is sealed (the Seal chapter's
vocabulary). Isa 54:13 "all thy children shall be *taught* (limmudei) of
the LORD"; 1 Chr 25:8 talmid, the scholar.

## The commission connection

Matt 28:19's verb is matheteusate — "DISCIPLE all nations": make
limmudim, ones in whom the law is sealed — Isa 8:16 at nation scale,
which is exactly what v20 says ("teaching them to observe all things…
forever," HG). Joined into the Gospel chapter's Commission closer.

## The bridge (author's ruling, 2026-07-09)

The divergence closes from the consensus's own side, through verses each
side already owns. The consensus says a disciple is baptized, follows,
and grows into Christlikeness. Scripture supplies each term's content:

- Baptized — "baptizing them **in the NAME**" (Matt 28:19): the entry
  rite places the disciple into the Name, and the Name is the covenant.
- Follows toward likeness — "every one that is *perfect* shall be **as
  his master**" (Luke 6:40): the disciple's stated telos.
- The Master — the One of whom the Father said "**my name is in him**"
  (Ex 23:21); who kept His own "in thy name" (John 17:11).
- The covenant's content — "I will put my **law** in their inward parts,
  and write it in their hearts" (Jer 31:33): the new covenant IS the law
  written within — Isa 8:16's sealing stated as promise.
- The arrival — "his Father's **name written in their foreheads**"
  (Rev 14:1); "I will write upon him the name of my God" (Rev 3:12).

Christlikeness, defined by Scripture instead of sentiment, IS the law
sealed within. The common teaching's words were right; their content was
left undefined — the entry supplies it. (Badge stays DIVERGENT: the
tradition does not say this.)

# Research: North, South, East, West (TODO item, drafted for review)

All key texts verified against the corpus 2026-07-02.

## North — the quarter of the invader; the usurped seat

- "Out of the NORTH an evil shall break forth upon all the inhabitants
  of the land" (Jer 1:14); the flood-armies come down from the north
  (Jer 47:2 — ch. 3); Babylon's own destroyer is "an assembly of great
  nations from the NORTH country" (Jer 50:9, 41).
- The seat: mount Zion is "on the SIDES OF THE NORTH, the city of the
  great King" (Ps 48:2) — and Lucifer's boast targets exactly that
  address: "I will sit also upon the mount of the congregation, in the
  SIDES OF THE NORTH" (Isa 14:13). The invader from the north is a
  claimant to the northern seat.

## East (qedem) — beginning, front, the glory's road

- *qedem* = east AND ancient/before: Eden "EASTWARD" (Gen 2:8); the
  glory of God returns "from the way of the EAST" (Ezek 43:2); "as the
  lightning cometh out of the EAST" (Matt 24:27); the kings OF THE EAST
  cross the dried Euphrates (Rev 16:12). East is where beginnings and
  returns come from — the face of the compass.
- The east WIND is the LORD's judgment-wind: Ex 14:21 (the sea split);
  Ex 10:13 (locusts); Jon 4:8; "an east wind… the wind of the LORD"
  (Hos 13:15). (Proposed for ch. 11 Four Winds.)

## West (yam) — the sea at the back; hindermost

- Hebrew for west IS "sea" (yam) — Israel faces east, the sea at its
  back; "the hinder sea" = the western (Zech 14:8; Joel 2:20 "his face
  toward the east sea, and his hinder part toward the utmost sea").
- "The HINDERMOST of the nations shall be a wilderness" (Jer 50:12 —
  said of Babylon's mother). Facing qedem, west is what lies behind.

## South — verify further before writing

- "Out of the SOUTH cometh the whirlwind" (Job 37:9); Dan 11 king of
  the south (Egypt); negev = the dry land. Thinnest of the four —
  needs its own corpus pass before a chapter asserts a meaning.

## The four together — the regathering compass

- "I will bring thy seed from the EAST, and gather thee from the WEST;
  I will say to the NORTH, Give up; and to the SOUTH, Keep not back"
  (Isa 43:5-6) — all four quarters surrender the dispersed.
- "As far as the EAST is from the WEST, so far hath he removed our
  transgressions" (Ps 103:12); "many shall come from the EAST and
  WEST, and shall sit down with Abraham" (Matt 8:11).
- Ties: the four winds (ch. 11) traverse the quarters; the four
  kanaph-corners of the earth (ch. 16) are where the dispersed wait.

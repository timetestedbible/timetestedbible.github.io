## The two anchor texts

- Ps 90:4 — "a THOUSAND YEARS in thy sight are but as YESTERDAY when
  it is past, and as a watch in the night" (Moses' psalm).
- 2 Pet 3:8 — "be not ignorant of THIS ONE THING… one day is with the
  Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day" — stated
  as a KEY ("be not ignorant"), in a chapter about the delay of the
  day of the LORD.

## The lock it may open (for the author to weigh)

- Hos 6:2 — "after TWO DAYS will he revive us: in the THIRD DAY he
  will raise us up, and we shall LIVE IN HIS SIGHT" — spoken of
  Israel's national restoration; read with the key: two millennia of
  putting-away, the third of raising (cf. ch. 9, God's divorce and
  remarriage; "in his sight" echoes Ps 90:4's "in thy sight").
- Jonah's "three days" ↔ "great city of three days' journey" ↔ 3000
  years (already flagged in ch. 5's material).
- The six days of creation → sabbath-millennium structure (common in
  early tradition — Barnabas 15, if the author wants a witness; VERIFY
  quote before use).

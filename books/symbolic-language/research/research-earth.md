# Research: Earth — erets, adamah, aphar (and the Greek gē)

Question (author, 2026-07-16): full word study of EARTH / adamah. Does the
ground carry a symbolic office? Does the earth/sea polarity hold at
structure grade? And can Gen 8:13's "face of the adamah" honestly be heard
as mankind's ground (the Noah Uncovered interlinear row)?

Method: four full-corpus sweeps against kjv_strongs.txt / wlc.txt /
data/hg-chapters/*.json — erets H776 (2,504 tags, 2,191 verses), adamah
H127 (225 tags, 211 verses), aphar H6083 (110 tags, 103 verses), Greek gē
G1093 (252 tags, 226 verses) + agros G68 (34 verses), with Aramaic ara'
H772, yabbashah H3004, sadeh H7704, avak H80, efer H665 as side-checks.
All quotes below tag-verified against the files.

## The finding in one paragraph

The KJV flattens three Hebrew words into "earth/land/ground" and dissolves
their boundary completely (erets: land 1,509 / earth 712 / ground 98;
adamah: land 124 / earth 53 / ground 43 — one word gets three renderings,
and each rendering serves two words). Unflattened, the three words hold
three distinct offices. **Erets** is the named, bounded, heritable
dominion — by Gen 1:10 a bestowed name for what stood up out from under
the gathered waters. **Adamah** is the ground in relationship — mankind's
ledger, the soil that answers for the man taken from it. **Aphar** is the
mortal material — "dust thou ART" (Gen 3:19). The sea-polarity belongs to
erets; the mankind-bond belongs to adamah; mortality belongs to aphar.
Greek gē covers all three at once, but the Hebrew Gospels un-flatten it
again — and where it matters most (Rev 13:11; 21:1) they choose adamah.

## 1. Erets H776 — the named, bounded, heritable dominion

### The founding definition

- **Gen 1:9-10** — "Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together
  unto one place, and let the dry land (yabbashah H3004) appear… And God
  called the dry land Earth (erets); and the gathering together of the
  waters called he Seas." Erets is a NAME bestowed on what emerged out
  from under the waters — separated, bounded, and only then called erets.
- **Ps 24:1-2** — "The earth is the LORD's… he hath founded it UPON the
  seas, and established it upon the floods." Order resting on top of what
  it was drawn from.
- **Jer 5:22 / Prov 8:29 / Ps 104:9** — the sea bounded by perpetual
  decree "that they turn not again to cover the earth"; the sea's decree
  and the earth's foundations are one act, two sides.

### Usage range (sample-classified, n=100 of 2,191)

Nation's territory ~35% ("land of Egypt" ×210 verses) · THE covenant land
~28% ("the land which I sware") · whole earth/creation ~15% · soil
underfoot ~12% · inhabitants-metonymy ~6% (Gen 6:11; 1 Kgs 10:24) ·
heaven-and-earth pair (180 verses with H8064). Idiom: "go the way of all
the earth" = die (Josh 23:14). KJV renders "land" over "earth" 2:1, and
the choice is pure translator scope-judgment — **Deut 4:26 renders one
H776 both ways in a single verse** ("I call heaven and EARTH to witness…
ye shall perish from off the LAND"), and Prov 2:21-22 does it in one
sentence ("the upright shall dwell in the LAND… the wicked shall be cut
off from the EARTH… rooted out of IT" — same referent).

### The inherit chain is one promise

Ps 37:9, 11, 22 ("earth") and 37:29, 34 ("land") are the same
yarash-erets promise; with Ps 25:13, Prov 2:21-22, Isa 57:13 ("possess
the land… inherit my holy MOUNTAIN" — the kingdom-word in parallel),
Isa 60:21. Matt 5:5 quotes Ps 37:11 verbatim (Shem Tov: yirshu ARETZ,
H776 vocabulary). The meek inherit the covenant inheritance — the erets —
not "the planet" as against "heaven"; the wicked's counterpart verb is
always "cut off" FROM it.

### Earth vs sea — relational polarity, not moral badge

The genuine structural set (after removing ~20 compass/boundary verses
where yam = "west"): the triads (Ex 20:11; Neh 9:6; Ps 146:6; 135:6;
96:11; Hag 2:6 — a FOURfold shake: heavens, earth, sea, dry land H2724);
dominion "from sea to sea, unto the ends of the earth" (Ps 72:8 = Zech
9:10); sea-commerce feeding "kings of the earth" (Ezek 27:33) and
dethroned "princes of the sea" coming down to sit "upon the ground —
erets" (Ezek 26:16); instability as reversal of the separation (Ps 46:2-3
— mountains carried into the midst of the sea = kingdoms collapsing back
into the peoples; Isa 5:30 — invaders roar "like the roaring of the sea"
and the land goes dark); and the redemptive inversion — "the earth shall
be full of the knowledge of the LORD, **as the waters cover the sea**"
(Isa 11:9 / Hab 2:14): the erets finally covered again, by knowledge
instead of water.

### The Daniel 7 seam — resolved by the text itself

Dan 7:2-3 (Aramaic yam H3221): the four winds strive on the great sea and
the beasts come up FROM the sea. Dan 7:17 (ara' H772): the same beasts
are kings that arise "out of the EARTH." Dan 7:23 supplies the key: "the
fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom UPON EARTH… and shall devour the
whole earth." The sea is where a kingdom comes FROM (the churning
peoples-mass); the earth is what a kingdom IS and RULES once constituted.
Vision-language and interpretation-language for two phases of one event —
confirmed by Dan 2:35, where the stone-kingdom becomes a mountain and
fills the whole ARA', not the sea. The rest of the H772 corpus is
uniformly dominion-arena ("bear rule over all the earth," 2:39; "all…
that dwell in all the earth," 4:1).

### Earth swallowing

- **Ex 15:12** — "the earth (erets) swallowed them" — sung of Egyptians
  who drowned (15:10 "the sea covered them"). The sea-floor grave is
  erets; erets receives the dead even when the sea kills them (cf. Jonah
  2:6, "the earth with her bars" spoken from the bottom of the sea).
- **Korah**: Moses' spoken sign uses **adamah** — Num 16:30 "if the
  adamah open her mouth" (patsah + adamah — the Gen 4:11 blood-receiving
  idiom verbatim), and 16:31 "the adamah clave asunder that was under
  them"; the narration and retrospectives shift to **erets** (16:32-34;
  Deut 11:6; Ps 106:17 — with kasah "covered," the same verb the sea does
  to Pharaoh). The ground-in-relationship receives them; the wide earth
  closes over them.

### Counter-texts (steelmanned) — what they kill

(1) Dan 7:17 blurs sea/earth in the one inspired interpretation — answer:
7:23's upon/devour keeps the two phases distinct. (2) Isa 34:1 — erets
itself does peoples-metonymy ("let the earth hear… the world, and all
things that come forth of it"). (3) Gen 6:11-13 — the erets is the
corrupt thing and the flood-sea is God's instrument: moral polarity
inverted. (4) Isa 24:19-20 — the erets reels like a drunkard; and 24:5
gives the covenant reason ("broken the everlasting covenant"):
settledness is CONDITIONAL, not constitutive. (5) "Kings of the earth"
is Scripture's standing title for the rebel imperial system (Ps 2:2).
Together these kill any claim that erets is intrinsically righteous or
covenant-keeping. What survives: erets is the OFFICE of settled, bounded,
heritable dominion — who holds it (the meek, or the beast that devours
it, Dan 7:23) is exactly the question the prophets keep open until the
new heavens and new erets, where seed and name remain (Isa 66:22).

## 2. Adamah H127 — mankind's ledger

### The bond, stated by Scripture in five steps

- **Gen 2:7** — "formed man (adam) of the dust of (min) the ground
  (adamah)" — the derivation text: adam is named FROM adamah.
- **Gen 3:17-19** — juridical: "cursed is the adamah FOR THY SAKE…
  till thou return unto the adamah; for out of it wast thou taken."
- **Gen 4:10-11** — forensic: the adamah "opened her mouth to receive thy
  brother's blood" and reports it ("crieth unto me from the adamah").
  Job 31:38 runs the same mechanism ("if my land cry against me").
- **Gen 8:21** — God's own accounting: "I will not again curse the adamah
  any more FOR MAN'S SAKE (ba'avur ha'adam)."
- **Gen 12:3 / 28:14 / Amos 3:2** — the sealed phrase, exactly three
  times in Scripture: **mishpechot ha'adamah, "all the families of the
  GROUND"** — mankind's clans named by the soil they were taken from.
  Abraham's blessing formula uses adam's own word (Gen 18:18 and 22:18
  switch to goyei ha'aretz — the alternation is the text's, and
  deliberate). Deut 32:43 caps it in bare apposition: "kipper admato
  ammo" — "he will atone his adamah, his people" (KJV supplies the
  "and"); blood avenged, ground atoned — Gen 4:10-11 run in reverse.

### The corpus (all 211 verses classified)

Covenant-land refrain **77** (sworn to the fathers, days prolonged upon
it, fruit of it, rooted out of it, regathered to it) · **penei
ha'adamah 34** — and the census of that phrase's business is man on it
or man cut off from it (multiply on it Gen 6:1; destroy man from it Gen
6:7, 7:4, 7:23, Zeph 1:2-3, Amos 9:8, Jer 28:16; distinguished on it Ex
33:16, Deut 7:6; corpses dung on it Jer 8:2, 16:4, 25:33; only ~6 of 34
are physical-surface uses) · plain soil/material **31** · tiller texts
**13** (Cain; **Gen 9:20 "Noah began to be an ish ha'adamah — man of the
adamah"**) · **Ezekiel's admat yisrael 17** — Ezekiel never writes erets
yisrael for the homeland promise; he coins "the SOIL of Israel," and in
the dry-bones oracle the word does both of its offices at once: "I will
open your GRAVES… and bring you into the ADAMAH of Israel" (37:12) — the
ground graves are dug in and the ground Israel is planted in, one word ·
man formed/returning **7** · other **32**.

### Adamah vs erets — the rule that survives all 45 shared verses

Erets can always substitute geographically; **adamah is chosen when the
ground is in relationship** — owned, worked, promised, cursed, mourning,
yielding, receiving blood, receiving bodies. Cleanest three: Gen 4:12
(the adamah that knows Cain withholds HER strength; he wanders in the
indifferent erets); Deut 29:28 (rooted out of their ADAMAH, cast into
another ERETS — exile vocabulary keeps the rule, cf. Jer 16:15, 23:8);
Deut 26:2 (fruit of the ADAMAH brought from thy ERETS). Against sadeh
(11 shared verses): sadeh is open country as a PLACE — sold, walked,
where beasts live and bodies are found; adamah is the soil as party.
**Joel 1:10 draws the line in one verse: "the field (sadeh) is WASTED,
the land (adamah) MOURNETH"** — a fact about a place versus an act of a
person. Sadeh never grieves, opens a mouth, or receives blood anywhere
in the corpus; adamah does all three.

### Counter-texts (steelmanned) — what they forbid

(1) **Gen 2:19** — beasts and fowl are ALSO formed min-ha'adamah, same
verb yatsar: origin alone does not make the symbol. What is unique to
adam is the dust-of + inbreathing + the NAMING — the man is called after
the source; the beast is not. (2) Ex 20:24 altar of earth; 2 Kgs 5:17
Naaman's two mules' burden; 1 Kgs 7:46 foundry clay — pure material.
(3) Gen 47:19-26 — Egypt's farmland bought and taxed, no covenant
valence. (4) remes ha'adamah — the fixed habitat term for creeping
things, used before man exists (Gen 1:25). (5) A fifth of the corpus is
plain agronomy. These forbid CIPHER strength (adamah = mankind). They do
not touch LEDGER strength.

### Verdict — adamah

Adamah never MEANS mankind (not identity grade; there is no Rev 17:15
analogue). The link runs at **structure grade anchored by an explicit
derivation text**: **adamah is mankind's ledger — the ground that
answers for the man taken from it. What befalls the adamah is the
published verdict on the adam.** Judgment on people is spoken as
judgment on the adamah's face; mercy on people as mercy on "his adamah,
his people"; resurrection as adamah-sleepers waking (Dan 12:2 admat
aphar) and graves opening into admat yisrael (Ezek 37:12).

### Gen 8:13 — the specific question

Yes — "the face of the adamah was dry/desolated" can honestly be heard
as mankind's ground desolated, **on cross-text evidence inside the flood
narrative itself**, not by import. The account is an inclusio in one
formula: Gen 6:1 "when MEN began to multiply on the face of the ADAMAH"
(defines the phrase as the surface mankind fills) → Gen 6:7 "I will
destroy MAN… from the face of the ADAMAH" (the decree) → Gen 7:23 "every
living substance was destroyed which was upon the face of the ADAMAH,
both man, and cattle" (the execution) → **Gen 8:13 "the face of the
ADAMAH was dry (charvu — the desolation root)"** (the verdict published)
→ Gen 8:21 "not again curse the ADAMAH for MAN's sake" (the accounting).
Zeph 1:2-3 replays the formula as end-time prophecy ("I will consume man
and beast… cut off man from off the face of the adamah") — the same
hearing Luke 17:26 licenses for the whole account. CAUTION for the
chapter and the interlinear table: state it at ledger strength
("mankind's ground"), never cipher strength ("ground = mankind").

## 3. Aphar H6083 — the mortal material

### The identity is declared, not inferred

Gen 2:7 (man = aphar taken from the adamah — the compound returns
inverted in Dan 12:2, admat-aphar, "ground-of-dust"); **Gen 3:19 "for
dust thou ART" (ki-aphar attah)** — identity statement, not simile; Ps
103:14; Ecc 3:20; 12:7. The corpus then uses "the dust" as metonym for
the dead and the abased: the dust cannot praise (Ps 30:9); the sleepers
sleep in it (Job 7:21; Dan 12:2); "the dust of death" (Ps 22:15).

### Resurrection is uniformly OUT of dust, never dust glorified

Dan 12:2 + Isa 26:19 share the awakening verb (qitz): "awake and sing,
ye that dwell in dust… the earth shall cast out the dead." **KJV-masked
find — Job 19:25: "my redeemer… shall stand at the latter day upon the
EARTH" is {H6083}: v'acharon AL-APHAR yaqum — "at the last he shall
arise upon the DUST"** — the Redeemer takes his stand on the very dust
where the sleepers lie. Ps 119:25 ("my soul cleaveth unto the dust;
QUICKEN thou me"). The reversal texts run dust-to-throne: 1 Sam 2:8 =
Ps 113:7 ("raiseth up the poor out of the dust… to make them inherit
the throne of glory"); 1 Kgs 16:2; and the matched throne-swap — Isa
52:1-2 "Shake thyself from the dust; ARISE… O Jerusalem" against Isa
47:1 "Come down, and sit in the dust, O virgin daughter of Babylon."

### The three multitude figures — a trajectory, not a taxonomy

Dust-seed (Gen 13:16; 28:14; Num 23:10; 2 Chr 1:9) is uniformly
land/adamah-anchored — Gen 28:14 glues dust-seed to four-directions
spreading AND to mishpechot ha'adamah in one verse. Sand clusters with
sea, gates of enemies, remnant-out-of-mass (Isa 10:22; Hos 1:10). Stars
carry the righteousness adjacency (Gen 15:5→6) — but Deut 1:10, 10:22,
28:62 apply star-multitude to the mortal wilderness nation, and Gen
22:17 + Jer 33:22 use two figures for one seed: **a strict three-caste
split is falsified.** What stands instead is Dan 12:2-3, the one text
that ORDERS dust and stars: sleepers in the dust awake → the wise shine
as the stars. The seed begins as dust (mortal, of the ground) and the
righteous of it end as stars. Trajectory, not census.

### The serpent's food (flagged as a raised question, not a conclusion)

Gen 3:14 assigns the serpent dust as diet five verses before "dust thou
art" (3:19); Isa 65:25 keeps that one diet in force in the restored
creation while all other predation ends; Mic 7:17 puts the humbled
nations in the serpent's posture ("lick the dust like a serpent"). Does
the serpent feed on man-as-dust — the mortal Adam-material? The texts
put the pieces adjacent; no verse states the equation.

### Counter-texts

Ex 8:16-17 (dust of the land → lice: the seed-promise phrase aphar
ha'aretz meaning plain soil); Isa 40:12 (aphar ha'aretz measured with
waters and mountains as raw cosmic material — the PHRASE is not
self-symbolizing); Lev 14:41-45 (scraped plaster); Job 39:14 (warm
ground). ~35% of the corpus is irreducibly physical. Answer: the office
is not "aphar always means X" — Scripture states the identity in Gen
2-3 and the non-literal uses trade on it; exactly the book's symbol
test. **Dust = the mortal man / the multitude in its mortal aspect
holds at structure grade.** Side-check: efer H665 (ashes) is the
mourning/residue word; avak H80 the fine airborne dust (enemy multitude,
Isa 29:5); neither carries the creation-mortality identity — that office
belongs to aphar alone.

## 4. The Greek gē and the Hebrew Gospels' second witness

### The parable split — zero crossover on the tags

In Matt 13, gē G1093 carries the sower's soil (13:5, 8, 23) and agros
G68 carries the tares/mustard/treasure field (13:24, 27, 31, 36, 38,
44); the two never trade places. Both keys are given inside the text:
**Luke 8:15 puts the same adjective (kalos G2570) on the gē and the
kardia — "that on the GOOD ground are they, which in an honest and GOOD
heart… keep it"** (ground = heart is Luke's own equation); **Matt 13:38
"the FIELD is the WORLD (kosmos)"** — and 13:39 uses the other
world-word for the harvest ("end of the AIŌN"). Mark 4:26-28: the gē
brings forth fruit OF HERSELF while the sower sleeps. John 12:24; Matt
25:18 (talent hidden IN the gē — the word buried in the heart that
returns no fruit is the same picture).

### Shem Tov's renderings sharpen it

Matt 13:38 sadeh = **ha-olam ha-zeh** ("this world," the rabbinic term);
the good ground = **eretz tovah** — Deut 8:7's exact phrase for the
promised Land, silently welding the sower's fruiting ground to "the meek
shall inherit the ERETS" (Matt 5:5 = Ps 37:11 verbatim in Shem Tov:
ha-anavim yirshu aretz). The SHALLOW soil is **afar on the rock** (dust,
H6083) — the manuscripts reserve eretz for ground that fruits.

### Revelation's earth/sea structure

19 of Revelation's 68 gē-verses pair earth with sea, structurally: two
beasts from two domains (13:1 sea / 13:11 earth); the angel's feet
claiming both (10:2); both held from harm until the sealing (7:1-3); woe
to the inhabiters of both (12:12); the earth helps the woman and
swallows the dragon's flood (12:16 — Num 16 inverted: the mouth that
swallowed rebels swallows the surge against the faithful); the earth
reaped (14:15-16 — the sower's gē at end-state); kings and priests
"shall reign ON the earth" (5:10 — Matt 6:10's jurisdiction realized);
and the polarity abolished together — "a new heaven and a new earth…
and no more sea" (21:1).

### The highest-value find: Hebrew Revelation un-flattens gē

Whole-book sweep of the Hebrew Revelation (Cochin, data/hg-chapters/):
eretz H776 ×61, olam ×30, yam ×26, **adamah H127 ×13**, sadeh ×5, aphar
×2 — three Hebrew words under one Greek gē:

- **Rev 13:11 (Heb v12): the second beast rises "from the ADAMAH"** —
  not the eretz. Not "the Land" (which a reader could take as Israel)
  but the sown soil itself — a homegrown power out of the field's own
  dirt, lamb-horned like what good ground should grow, dragon-voiced.
- **Rev 12:16 (Heb v19): "the woman had deliverance from the ADAMAH,
  for it opened its mouth and swallowed the waters."**
- **Rev 14:3: the 144,000 "redeemed from the ADAMAH."**
- **Rev 21:1 (Hebrew): "new heavens and a new ADAMAH, for the first
  heavens and the ERETS had expired, and the sea was no more"** — the
  old order passes as erets (dominion), the new arrives as adamah
  (fresh soil, the relationship word).
- **Rev 10:2 (Hebrew): the angel's feet on the sea and on the
  YABBASHAH** — the Gen 1:9 / Ex 14:16 creation-and-exodus dry-land
  word, a third distinction Greek cannot show.

### Honest counter-data (reported as found)

- **Shem Tov Matt 12:40 reads "in the BELLY (beten) of the earth… in
  the GRAVE (ba-qever)"** — this witness runs against the heart-of-the-
  earth = Jerusalem reading. Before weighing it, the systematic-
  rendering check is owed (the tower protocol): does Shem Tov render
  kardia/"heart" as belly/grave elsewhere, or is this verse-specific?
  UNRESOLVED — do the check before citing either way.
- John 8:6 (writing on the ground), Matt 27:51 (the earth quaked), 2 Pet
  3:5-10 (earth burned up — though 3:6 says it was the KOSMOS "that then
  was" which perished) — the NT freely uses gē physically; the office is
  genre-activated (parable, vision, promise), per the book's
  structural-not-lexical rule.

## 5. Candidate definitions (pending author approval — not glossary yet)

- **Earth (erets):** the named, bounded, heritable dominion that stands
  out from under the sea of peoples — the arena where kingdoms are
  constituted (Dan 7:17, 23) and the inheritance the meek receive
  (Ps 37; Matt 5:5). Not intrinsically righteous: who holds the erets is
  the question every prophecy keeps open. Opposite-pole: sym-sea.
- **Ground (adamah):** mankind's ledger — the soil bound to the man
  taken from it and named from it. It receives his blood, is cursed for
  his sake, publishes his judgment on its face, and holds his sleepers
  for the waking. Ledger strength, never cipher strength.
- **Dust (aphar):** man in his mortal, ground-taken state — "dust thou
  art" — and the multitude counted in its mortal aspect. Resurrection is
  awakening OUT of the dust; the arc of the seed runs dust → stars
  (Dan 12:2-3).

## 6. Implications for the books (author's rulings needed)

1. **Noah Uncovered interlinear row (the live question):** "of mankind's
   ground" earns a symbol-grade gloss at LEDGER strength — but only if
   the chapter adds one sentence citing the inclusio (Gen 6:1 → 6:7 →
   7:23 → 8:13 → 8:21, the same penei-ha'adamah formula five times).
   As the chapter stands (a passing letters-note), the table row should
   stay "of the earth." Author's call which way.
2. The **Gen 8:13 first clause** distinction is real and currently
   invisible: "the waters were dried from off the ERETS… the face of
   the ADAMAH was dry" — two words, one KJV blur, and the chapter's
   rehearing gains precision from it (dominion drained; mankind's
   ledger published).
3. **Rev 13:11 beast from the adamah** (HG) — homegrown-power reading;
   candidate material for Fall of Babylon or a future study.
4. **Dan 7 sea/earth seam** — resolved (from-the-sea / upon-the-earth);
   useful wherever the four beasts appear.
5. **Shem Tov Matt 12:40 counter-data** — flagged for the Sign of Jonah
   defense; systematic-rendering check owed before it is weighed.
6. Possible glossary entries Earth and Ground (and Dust) per §5 —
   blind-test protocol before badging.

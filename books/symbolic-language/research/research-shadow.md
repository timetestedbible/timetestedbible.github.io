# Research: Shadow — the full corpus for a future chapter

Author's thesis (2026-07-02): **it is GOOD to be in the shadow** — shadow is
the covering of a greater presence, not the gloom it is typically read as.
Corpus: 82 KJV verses (shadow/shade/overshadow); Hebrew *tsel* H6738 47×,
*tselem* H6754 (image!) 15×, *tsalmavet* H6757 (shadow-of-death) 17×; Greek
*skia* G4639 7×, *episkiazō* G1982 (overshadow) 5×.

## The root discovery: shadow / image / defence are one family

- **H6738 *tsel***: "shade, whether literal or figurative; **KJV: defence,
  shade(-ow)**." The KJV itself renders *tsel* as DEFENCE: "their **defence**
  is departed from them" (Num 14:9 — the nations' shadow is their
  protection); "wisdom is a **defence**, and money is a **defence**" (Eccl
  7:12 — Hebrew: *in the shadow of wisdom… the shadow of money*). Two
  KJV-flattened gems — the symbol hidden by translation.
- **H6754 *tselem*** — the "IMAGE" of Gen 1:26 — is "from an unused root
  meaning **to shade**; a representative figure." Man is God's
  shadow-figure. And Heb 10:1 sets the pair in Greek dress: the law has "a
  **shadow** (skia) of good things to come, and not the very **image**
  (eikōn) of the things" — shadow and image, tsel and tselem, outline and
  likeness.
- **H6757 *tsalmavet*** = *tsel* + *maveth* (Strong's): the shadow OF DEATH
  is a compound — death's shadow, not death; a shadow cannot kill, and a
  shadow proves a light is shining somewhere.

## Tier 1 — Refuge under the Almighty (the defining uses)

- Ps 91:1 "abide under the **shadow of the Almighty**" — THE definition.
- The shadow of thy wings: Ps 17:8; 36:7 ("the children of men put their
  trust under the shadow of thy wings"); 57:1; 63:7 → ties directly to the
  WINGS chapter (kanaph, the covenant covering); Heb 9:5 the cherubim
  "shadowing the mercyseat."
- Ps 121:5 "the LORD is thy **shade** upon thy right hand" (tsel — the LORD
  Himself named the shadow).
- Isa 25:4 parallel columns: "a refuge from the storm, a **shadow** from the
  heat" ∥ strength to the poor; Isa 4:6 tabernacle "for a shadow… a place of
  refuge"; Isa 32:2 "the shadow of a great ROCK in a weary land" (rock =
  covenant); Isa 16:3 "make thy shadow as the night… hide the outcasts."
- Isa 49:2; 51:16 "in the **shadow of his hand** hath he hid me… I have
  covered thee in the shadow of mine hand… Thou art my people."
- Song 2:3 "I sat down under his shadow **with great delight**."
- Hos 14:7 "They that dwell under his shadow **shall return**; they shall
  revive."
- Lam 4:20 the anointed of the LORD: "Under his shadow we shall live among
  the heathen."
- Gen 19:8 "under the shadow of my roof" (the protection of a host).
- Jonah 4:5-6 — the gourd "a shadow over his head, **to deliver him**" (and
  Jonah was "exceeding glad" — ties to ch. 5).
- The OVERSHADOWING presence: Luke 1:35 "the power of the Highest shall
  **overshadow** thee" — the Son conceived under the shadow; Matt 17:5 /
  Mark 9:7 / Luke 9:34 the bright cloud of the transfiguration; Acts 5:15
  Peter's shadow heals. Episkiazō — the Shekinah verb.

## The equation (author, 2026-07-02): wings = shade = commands = protection from the consuming fire

What the shadow shields from, at full strength, is God's OWN holiness:
"our God is a consuming fire" (Deut 4:24; Heb 12:29); "Who among us shall
dwell with the devouring fire?… He that walketh righteously" (Isa 33:14-15);
Moses covered by God's hand-shadow while the glory passed (Ex 33:22); "the
sun shall not smite thee" under the LORD's shade (Ps 121:5-6). And the
covering is the covenant: Ps 91:1+4 sets "shadow of the Almighty" beside
"under his wings shalt thou trust"; the wings are the kanaph where the
commandments hang (tzitzit, Num 15:38-39 — Wings ch. 16); the cherubim's
WINGS shadow the mercy seat (Heb 9:5). The commandments are not the fire —
they are the shade that lets a man dwell beside it (law-positive); the same
Sun rises "with healing in his wings" on them that fear His name (Mal 4:2).
AND (author): the covering hand itself is YESHUAH — Ps 98:1 "his right hand
… hath gotten him the victory" = H3467 yasha (his right hand works
salvation); the hand that covered Moses (Ex 33:22) is God's salvation —
Yeshua — standing in the gap (Isa 49:2, the Servant hidden in the
hand-shadow); and Ps 84:11 holds both halves in one breath: "the LORD God
is a SUN and SHIELD" — the fire and the covering in one Person. He shades
us from Himself, and the shade is His Messiah. Now stated in ch. 28.

## Tier 2 — A kingdom's shadow: protection over peoples (ties to Trees)

- Judg 9:15 the bramble: "put your **trust in my shadow**" — kingship
  offered AS shadow (Jotham's parable of the trees).
- Ezek 31:3, 6, 12, 17 — Assyria the cedar: "under his shadow dwelt **all
  great nations**"; when he falls, "the people… are gone down from his
  shadow."
- Dan 4:12 — Nebuchadnezzar's tree: "the beasts of the field had shadow
  under it."
- Ezek 17:23 — God's own planted cedar: "in the shadow of the branches
  thereof shall they dwell"; Mark 4:32 — the mustard tree of the kingdom:
  "fowls of the air may lodge under the shadow of it."
- Ps 80:10 — the vine Israel: "hills were covered with the shadow of it."
- FALSE refuge: Isa 30:2-3 "the trust in the **shadow of Egypt** your
  confusion"; Jer 48:45; Hos 4:13 (idols chosen "because the shadow thereof
  is good" — even idolatry is shadow-seeking, at the wrong tree).
- Num 14:9 — "their defence (tsel) is departed" — a nation stripped of its
  shadow is naked to judgment.

## Tier 3 — The typological shadow (outline of a coming body)

- Col 2:17 "a shadow of things to come; but the **body** is of Christ."
- Heb 8:5 "example and shadow of heavenly things"; Heb 10:1 "a shadow of
  good things to come, and not the very image."
- The insight: a shadow is cast when light strikes a body. The law's shadow
  proves the Body exists and the Light is behind it — to stand in the law's
  shadow is to stand in the outline of Christ, in His protection. GOOD to be
  in — not "mere" shadow (ties to Signs & Similitudes ch. 4; law-positive:
  the shadow does not abolish the body, nor the body the shadow).

## Tier 4 — The shadow of death, disarmed

- Ps 23:4 "though I walk THROUGH the valley of the shadow of death, I will
  fear no evil: for **thou art with me**" — a shadow cannot kill.
- Amos 5:8 He "turneth the shadow of death **into the morning**."
- Ps 107:10, 14 "he brought them OUT of darkness and the shadow of death";
  Job 12:22 "bringeth out to light the shadow of death."
- Isa 9:2 / Matt 4:16 / Luke 1:79 — on them that sit in the shadow of death,
  the LIGHT springs up.
- The rest of the Job cluster (3:5; 10:21-22; 16:16; 24:17; 28:3; 34:22;
  38:17); Jer 2:6; 13:16; Ps 44:19.

## Tier 5 — Days as a shadow (transience) and the rest

- 1 Chr 29:15; Job 8:9; 14:2; 17:7; Ps 102:11; 109:23; 144:4; Eccl 6:12;
  8:13; Song 2:17; 4:6; Jer 6:4. Note Job 7:2 — "a servant earnestly
  **desireth** the shadow": even the transience texts long FOR the shadow
  (evening rest).
- James 1:17 "no variableness, neither shadow of turning" — the Father of
  lights casts no moving shadow.
- The sundial: 2 Kgs 20:9-11; Isa 38:8 (Hezekiah's shadow drawn back —
  see the jubilee-sign TODO).

## Prevalence angle (for the future table — research before numbers)

Commonly taught: "shadow" read as gloom/threat (valley of the shadow of
death as the emblematic use) or as "MERE shadow" (the law dismissively
contrasted with substance in Col 2:17/Heb 10:1 — often taught as
law-negative). The corpus shows the opposite center of gravity: refuge,
covering, presence. Chapter thesis writes itself against both misreadings,
and the law-positive reading of "shadow of good things" will need the
prevalence table treatment (quote sources per the rule).

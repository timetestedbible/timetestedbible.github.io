# Research: Butter (chemah) — author-commissioned symbol (2026-07-05)

Author: "Very few people, if any, would connect butter to sound doctrine
and meat… definitely one for the symbol table." Witnesses supplied by the
author (Job 29:6; 2 Sam 17:29; Judg 5:25; Isa 7:15, 22) plus study finds.
All verified against kjv.json.

## The derivation

- **Prov 30:33** — "the CHURNING of milk bringeth forth butter": butter is
  MILK WORKED — the word received, labored into solid. The bridge food
  between milk and meat (established in the Introduction).
- **Isa 7:15** — "Butter and honey shall he eat, THAT HE MAY KNOW to
  refuse the evil, and choose the good": the discernment diet — Heb 5:14's
  senses-exercised outcome assigned to butter, seven centuries early.
- **Isa 7:22** — "for the abundance of milk… butter and honey shall every
  one eat THAT IS LEFT IN THE LAND": the remnant's diet.
- **Job 29:6** — "When I washed my STEPS with BUTTER, and the ROCK poured
  me out rivers of OIL": four symbols in one verse — the steps (the walk,
  the way) washed in churned word; the rock (the covenant) pouring out oil
  (proven works). The walk purified by worked doctrine.
- **2 Sam 17:28-29** — in the wilderness, faithful hands bring David and
  his people "honey, and butter, and sheep, and cheese of kine… for they
  said, The people is HUNGRY, and WEARY, and THIRSTY" — the anointed fed
  butter in the wilderness while hungering and thirsting.
- **Ps 55:21 (counterfeit)** — "The words of his mouth were SMOOTHER THAN
  BUTTER, but WAR was in his heart: his words were softer than OIL, yet
  were they drawn swords." Counterfeit butter AND counterfeit oil in one
  verse — smooth speech that mimics worked doctrine. And the pairing is
  the SAME STORY: Psalm 55 is David on the betraying counselor
  (Ahithophel), while 2 Sam 17:29's true butter reaches David from
  faithful hands in the same Absalom crisis. True butter feeds the
  fugitive king; smooth-butter counsel hunts him.
- **Judg 5:25** — "He asked water, and she gave him milk; she brought
  forth butter in a lordly dish" — Jael's escalation: asked water, given
  the worked richness. (Author's question — is the asked-for water the
  living-water register? CANDIDATE, marked, pending the living-water
  study.)
- Supporting: Gen 18:8 (butter and milk set before the LORD, eaten);
  Deut 32:14 (butter of kine in God's own provision-list).

## Meaning (glossary)

Butter: the word churned solid — milk worked by labor into sound
doctrine: the diet that teaches to refuse the evil and choose the good,
the remnant's food, the wash of the walking feet. Its counterfeit is
speech smoother than butter with war beneath.

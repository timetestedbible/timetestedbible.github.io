# Research: Shavuot — the plural Sevens as the Jubilee count

Question (author, 2026-07-16): is Shavuot — WEEKS plural, the Feast of
Weeks — a symbol of the Jubilee count? Not the singular week (the
glossary's existing scale-flexible unit) but the plural: the Sevens.
All claims tag-verified against kjv_strongs.txt / wlc.txt.

## The whole corpus (H7620 shabua — 17 verses, exhaustive)

**Singular — one seven:**
- Gen 29:27, 28 — "fulfil her WEEK" — the BRIDAL week, the
  marriage-covenant seven (Jacob completes Leah's shevua to receive
  Rachel).
- Dan 9:27 — "confirm the covenant with many for ONE WEEK… in the
  midst of the WEEK" — the covenant shavua, which Daniel Unsealed
  (ch. 36) reads as the marriage covenant: THE SAME WORD, SINGULAR, AS
  THE BRIDAL WEEK OF Gen 29:27. The two singulars answer each other —
  the word's own flagship use is a marriage seven.

**Dual:** Lev 12:5 — shevu'ayim, "two weeks" (a distinct form; not the
plural).

**Plural — the feast and its count (every free plural outside Daniel):**
- Ex 34:22 — "the feast of WEEKS, of the FIRSTFRUITS of wheat harvest."
- Num 28:26 — "the day of the firstfruits… after your WEEKS be out."
- Deut 16:9 (×2) — "Seven WEEKS shalt thou number… begin to number the
  seven WEEKS."
- Deut 16:10, 16 — "the feast of WEEKS."
- 2 Chr 8:13 — "the feast of WEEKS" (listed with sabbaths and new
  moons — the appointed cycles).
- Jer 5:24 — "he reserveth unto us the appointed WEEKS (shavuot
  chukkot — statute-weeks) of the harvest."
- Ezek 45:21 — anomaly, see counter-texts.

**Plural — Daniel:**
- Dan 9:24, 25 (×2), 26 — shavuim, BARE (no scale named).
- Dan 10:2, 3 — shavuim YAMIM — "sevens OF DAYS" (KJV hides yamim
  under "three FULL weeks / three WHOLE weeks"). When Daniel means
  day-sevens, he marks the scale — one chapter after leaving 9:24's
  sevens bare.

## The pattern

The plural Sevens is Scripture's name for the COUNTING-FEAST: seven
sevens numbered to a fiftieth. The count formula is written twice, at
two scales, in nearly the same words:
- **Lev 23:15-16** — "seven sabbaths shall be complete: even unto the
  morrow after the seventh sabbath shall ye number FIFTY DAYS" — the
  feast (days).
- **Lev 25:8, 10** — "seven sabbaths of YEARS… and ye shall hallow the
  FIFTIETH YEAR" — the jubilee (years).
Shavuot is the jubilee run in days — the Torah's own day-for-a-year
model, rehearsed every spring. The bare plural in prophecy therefore
carries the jubilee-shaped count, and Dan 10:2-3 proves the grammar
from Daniel's own mouth: bare sevens are NOT day-sevens by default;
day-sevens get yamim.

## The fiftieth carries the same cargo at both scales: liberty

- Lev 25:10 — "proclaim LIBERTY (deror) throughout all the land… it
  shall be a jubile."
- Acts 2 — the Spirit poured out ON the fiftieth day (Pentecost =
  Shavuot); 2 Cor 3:17 — "where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is
  LIBERTY."
- Luke 4:18-19 — "to preach deliverance to the captives… to preach the
  acceptable YEAR of the LORD" — Isa 61's jubilee proclamation in
  Messiah's mouth.
- Ex 34:22 adds the firstfruits thread: Shavuot is "the feast of
  weeks, of the FIRSTFRUITS" — the count runs FROM firstfruits (the
  resurrection day in this book's calendar) TO the liberty-day.

## Counter-texts weighed

- **Ezek 45:21** — "ye shall have the passover, a feast of seven days"
  where the Hebrew reads chag SHAVUOT (plural) of unleavened bread —
  the one plural not obviously the counting-feast. Either the plural
  here names "a feast of sevens" (the paired seven-day feasts), or the
  form is textually disputed; flagged honestly as the anomaly. It does
  not break the pattern: it attaches the plural to an appointed
  feast-cycle, never to ordinary calendar weeks.
- **Lev 12:5** — dual, not plural; a different form for "a pair of
  weeks."
- **Dan 10:2-3** — plural without the feast — but scale-marked with
  yamim, which CONFIRMS the rule rather than breaking it.

## Verdict

The plural Sevens (shavuim/shavuot) is not "weeks" in the calendar
sense anywhere in Scripture apart from an explicit scale-marker; it is
the name of the counted cycle — seven sevens crowned by a fiftieth of
liberty — the jubilee's own shape, which the Feast of Weeks rehearses
in days every year. Daniel's seventy Sevens inherit that office, and
his one singular shavua (9:27) inherits the singular's own flagship:
the bridal week of Gen 29:27. Structure grade, with the formula-pair
(Lev 23:15-16 / 25:8-10) as the derivation texts and Dan 10:2-3 as the
in-book grammar proof.

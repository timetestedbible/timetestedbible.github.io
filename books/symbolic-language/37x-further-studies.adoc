The digital edition — free at https://TimeTested.Bible[TimeTested.Bible] — carries the chapters the binding could not:

*Behold the Hand, Behold the Nail.*
Before Israel wrote in Babylon's square letters, every letter was a picture: _yod_ the hand, _he_ the word _behold_, _vav_ the tabernacle's nail.
What the oldest pictures spell in the Name of God.

*Clouds.*
A cloud in Scripture is scarcely ever weather; it is a vehicle — __“who maketh the clouds his chariot”__ — and its cargo is defined: __“my doctrine shall drop as the rain.”__
Whom the clouds carry, from the pillar to the Son of man coming with them.

*The Moment.*
Babylon falls __“in a moment, in one day”__ — and the Hebrew behind that word is the wink of an eye, the same twinkling in which the dead are raised: judgment does not creep; it blinks.

*The Sabbath.*
A sabbath can be __“complete”__ (Leviticus 23:15) — and only a span can be complete or broken.
The law counts __“seven sabbaths”__ as __“seven weeks,”__ and the unit scales to the great week whose seventh millennium is the rest that remains.

*The Disciple.*
Scripture defines the word once — __“seal the law among my disciples”__ — and Christlikeness gets its content: to walk as He walked, who kept His Father's commandments.

*The Weeping and the Gnashing.*
Jesus says *the* weeping and *the* gnashing — the scene Psalm 112:10 wrote: the wicked sees, gnashes, and melts away.

*The Remnant, in full.*
The count printed in this book compresses a fifteen-line study — the gleanings, the field's geometry, the gold assay, the lion's census — published complete as “Last Call Before the Fall.”

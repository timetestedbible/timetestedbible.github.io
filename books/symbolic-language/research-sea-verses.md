# Every "sea" verse in Scripture, ranked by connection to people

Working reference for the Sea & Waters chapter. All **376 KJV verses** containing
*sea / seas / sea shore*, grouped from the most explicit people-connection down to plain
geography. Within each tier, canonical order (with a few thematic clusters).
**✦ = already quoted or cited in the current chapter draft.**

Vocabulary note: Hebrew *yam* (sea), *mayim* (waters); "flood" renders several words
(*nahar* river, *sheteph* overflowing, *mabbul* the Deluge, *ye'or* the Nile). The symbol
matches by structure, not spelling — verify the word before claiming "same word."
A selected list of *waters/waves/flood* verses that lack the word "sea" but carry the
people-connection explicitly is at the end.

---

## Tier 1 — Scripture states the equation itself

The verse (or its immediate neighbor) sets sea/waves beside peoples/nations/the wicked as
the same thing. These are the definition verses; everything else hangs on them.

- **Isaiah 17:12** ✦ — "the multitude of many people, which make a noise like the noise of the seas... the rushing of nations... like the rushing of mighty waters." The double parallel: people‖seas, nations‖waters (v13 continues).
- **Psalm 65:7** ✦ — "the noise of the seas, the noise of their waves, and the tumult of the people" — one breath, three names for one thing.
- **Psalm 98:7** — "Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein." The sea's fulness ‖ the world's inhabitants. GEM — same-breath equation nobody notices in a praise psalm.
- **Luke 21:25** ✦ — "distress of nations, with perplexity; the sea and the waves roaring."
- **Isaiah 57:20** ✦ — "the wicked are like the troubled sea, when it cannot rest."
- **Jude 1:13** ✦ — ungodly men are "raging waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame."
- **James 1:6** — the wavering man "is like a wave of the sea driven with the wind and tossed." A single person = a single wave; wind = what drives him. GEM.
- **Isaiah 5:30** — the invading nation (vv26–29) "shall roar against them like the roaring of the sea."
- **Jeremiah 6:23** — the army from the north: "their voice roareth like the sea... set in array as men for war against thee."
- **Jeremiah 50:42** — the identical sentence turned back on Babylon: "their voice shall roar like the sea... against thee, O daughter of Babylon." GEM — the same wave that broke on Zion returns on Babylon, word for word.
- **Jeremiah 49:23** — Hamath and Arpad "are fainthearted; there is sorrow on the sea; it cannot be quiet." The cities' anxiety *is* the unquiet sea.
- **Jeremiah 51:42** ✦ — "The sea is come up upon Babylon: she is covered with the multitude of the waves."
- **Isaiah 60:5** — "the abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee, the forces of the Gentiles shall come unto thee." The verse interprets itself: abundance of the sea = the Gentiles' wealth/host. GEM — pairs with Deuteronomy 33:19 below.
- **Isaiah 23:4** — the sea speaks as a mother: "I travail not, nor bring forth children, neither do I nourish up young men, nor bring up virgins." The sea bears and rears *people*. GEM.
- **Isaiah 23:11** — "He stretched out his hand over the sea, he shook the kingdoms." Hand over sea = kingdoms shaken.
- **Ezekiel 26:3** — "many nations... as the sea causeth his waves to come up." Nations = waves, stated as the simile of the whole Tyre oracle.
- **Ezekiel 32:2** — Pharaoh: "a young lion of the nations... a whale in the seas... troubledst the waters." A king among nations = a monster in the seas; and v3 answers him "with a company of many people."
- **Job 26:12** — "He divideth the sea with his power, and by his understanding he smiteth through the proud." Sea-power ‖ smiting the proud (Hebrew: Rahab).
- **Psalm 89:9** ✦ — "Thou rulest the raging of the sea" — and the very next verse: "Thou hast broken Rahab in pieces" (v10, Egypt). Rule of sea = rule of the proud nation.
- **Zechariah 10:11** — "shall smite the waves in the sea... and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the sceptre of Egypt shall depart." Smitten waves = humbled empires, in one verse.
- **Haggai 2:6** — "I will shake the heavens, and the earth, and the sea, and the dry land" — interpreted by the next verse: "and I will shake **all nations**" (v7). GEM.
- **Habakkuk 1:14** ✦ — "makest men as the fishes of the sea" (v15, taken with nets).
- **Daniel 7:2, 7:3** ✦ — four winds strive on the great sea; four beasts rise from it; interpreted in v17: "these great beasts... are four kings."
- **Revelation 13:1** ✦ — the beast rises "out of the sea" — the empire out of the mass of peoples (waters defined at 17:15).

## Tier 2 — The symbol at work: people-events told as sea-events

Prophecy and vision where the sea is doing what only peoples can do — enthroning kings,
swallowing kingdoms, giving up its dead. The interpretation is nearby or supplied by Tier 1.

### Kings enthroned on or amid the sea
- **Ezekiel 27:3** — Tyre "situate at the entry of the sea, which art **a merchant of the people** for many isles." Her trade is *people*.
- **Ezekiel 27:4** — "Thy borders are in the midst of the seas."
- **Ezekiel 27:9, 27:25** — all the ships of the sea serve her; she is "made very glorious in the midst of the seas." Ezekiel 27 is one sustained allegory: Tyre is a ship of state, her crew the peoples.
- **Ezekiel 27:26** — "the east wind hath broken thee in the midst of the seas" — a wind breaks the ship (winds = spirits stirring the sea, as in Daniel 7:2). GEM.
- **Ezekiel 27:27, 27:29, 27:32, 27:34** — her whole company falls "into the midst of the seas in the day of thy ruin"; "What city is like Tyrus, like the destroyed in the midst of the sea?"
- **Ezekiel 27:33** — "When thy wares went forth out of the seas, thou filledst many people."
- **Ezekiel 28:2** — the prince of Tyre: "I sit in the seat of God, **in the midst of the seas**" — a throne amid the peoples, claiming to be God's.
- **Ezekiel 28:8** — "thou shalt die the deaths of them that are slain in the midst of the seas."
- **Ezekiel 26:5** — Tyre becomes "a place for the spreading of nets in the midst of the sea" — the fallen kingdom becomes fishing-ground (fish = men). GEM.
- **Ezekiel 26:16** — "all the **princes of the sea** shall come down from their thrones."
- **Ezekiel 26:17** — the city "strong in the sea, she and her inhabitants."
- **Ezekiel 26:18** — "the isles that are in the sea shall be troubled at thy departure" (isles = the set-apart peoples; see the chapter's Islands section).
- **Isaiah 23:2** — "the merchants of Zidon, that pass over the sea, have replenished" the isle.
- **Zechariah 9:4** — Tyre again: "he will smite her power **in the sea**."
- **Nahum 3:8** — "populous No... whose rampart was the sea" — a *populous* city walled by waters; her defense was her multitudes.
- **Daniel 11:45** — the willful king plants "his palace between the seas in the glorious holy mountain" — enthroned between the peoples, on God's mountain.

### Kingdoms cast into, dried out of, or judged as the sea
- **Psalm 46:2** — "though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea" — read with v3 ("though the waters thereof roar") and v6 ("the heathen raged, the kingdoms were moved"): the psalm runs the symbol and its meaning in parallel columns. GEM.
- **Matthew 21:21; Mark 11:23** — "say unto this mountain, Be thou removed, and be thou cast into the sea" — faith topples a kingdom back into the peoples (spoken on the road from the fig tree, facing Jerusalem).
- **Luke 17:6** — the sycamine tree "planted in the sea" — a tree (ruler/nation, see Trees chapter) transplanted into the mass of peoples by a word of faith.
- **Revelation 8:8** ✦ — the burning mountain cast into the sea —
- **Revelation 8:9** — "the third part of the **creatures which were in the sea**... died; and the third part of the ships were destroyed." The casualties of a kingdom's fall are the sea's living things.
- **Revelation 16:3** — the vial on the sea: "it became as the blood of a dead man: and **every living soul died in the sea**." Souls live in this sea. GEM.
- **Revelation 18:17, 18:19** — "as many as trade by sea" wail over Babylon — the deliberate re-run of Ezekiel 27's Tyre lament; those who grew rich trafficking on the sea of peoples.
- **Revelation 18:21** — the millstone "cast into the sea, saying, Thus with violence shall that great city Babylon be thrown down" — the verse interprets the act; cf. Jeremiah 51:63–64 (the scroll sunk in Euphrates) and the millstone sayings in Tier 3.
- **Revelation 20:13** — "the sea gave up the dead which were in it" — the nations surrender their dead at judgment.
- **Revelation 21:1** ✦ — "there was no more sea." The capstone.
- **Jeremiah 51:36** — "I will dry up her sea, and make her springs dry" — Babylon's sea = the multitude she draws on (cf. 51:13 "many waters"; 50:38 "a drought upon her waters"). GEM.
- **Isaiah 21:1** — Babylon is "the desert of the sea" — the sea that will be dried to desert. GEM of a title.
- **Isaiah 19:5** — Egypt judged: "the waters shall fail from the sea" — the nation's supporting multitude/wealth fails.
- **Isaiah 27:1** — "he shall slay the dragon that is in the sea" — the tyrant power that lives in the mass of nations.
- **Job 41:31** — leviathan "maketh the sea like a pot of ointment" — the monster boils the peoples; pairs with Isaiah 27:1.
- **Amos 9:3** — "though they be hid from my sight in the bottom of the sea, thence will I command the serpent" — hiding in the depths of the nations; the serpent finds them there.
- **Joel 2:20** — the northern army driven "with his face toward the east sea, and his hinder part toward the utmost sea" — the locust-army drowned in the seas, exactly as Exodus 10:19 cast the locusts into the Red Sea. GEM pair.

### God's people drawn out of, through, and above the sea
- **Psalm 68:22** — "I will bring my people again from the depths of the sea" — regathering from among the nations, ‖ "from Bashan."
- **Isaiah 11:11** ✦ — the remnant recovered "from the islands of the sea."
- **Isaiah 11:15** — "the LORD shall utterly destroy the tongue of the Egyptian sea... and make men go over dryshod" — a second exodus out of the nations.
- **Micah 7:12** — "from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain" — the return from all peoples and all kingdoms.
- **Amos 8:12** — "they shall wander from sea to sea... to seek the word of the LORD" — adrift among the nations in a famine of hearing.
- **Micah 7:19** — "thou wilt cast all their sins into the depths of the sea" — sins drowned the way Pharaoh's host was drowned; v15 has already invoked the Exodus. GEM.
- **Zechariah 14:8** — "living waters shall go out from Jerusalem; half of them toward the former sea, and half of them toward the hinder sea" — the healing water sent into the peoples east and west.
- **Ezekiel 47:8** ✦ — the temple river goes into the sea, "and the waters shall be healed" — the nations healed.
- **Ezekiel 47:10** — fishers stand on the healed sea "from Engedi even unto Eneglaim... their fish... exceeding many" — the healed nations teem, and the fishers (of men) work them.
- **Isaiah 24:14** — "they shall cry aloud **from the sea**" — worshippers singing out of the midst of the nations.
- **Isaiah 24:15** ✦ — "glorify ye... the name of the LORD God of Israel in the isles of the sea."
- **Isaiah 42:10** — a new song from "ye that go down to the sea... the isles, and the inhabitants thereof."
- **Isaiah 11:9; Habakkuk 2:14** ✦ — "as the waters cover the sea" — knowledge of the LORD as the flood that finally covers the mass of humanity.
- **Revelation 4:6** ✦ — the sea of glass before the throne — the upper sea, stilled.
- **Revelation 15:2** ✦ — the victors stand on the sea of glass with harps.
- **Revelation 7:1, 7:2, 7:3** — the four winds held; "Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God" — the realms of men held back from harm for the elect's sake (same winds+sea machinery as Daniel 7:2).
- **Revelation 10:2, 10:5, 10:8** — the mighty angel plants his right foot on the sea, left on the earth — dominion astride peoples and land while the little book goes out.
- **Revelation 10:6** — his oath is by "him... who created... the sea, and the things which are therein."
- **Revelation 12:12** — "Woe to the **inhabiters** of the earth and of the sea!" — the sea has inhabitants; the devil comes down to *them*. GEM.

### Dominion from sea to sea; the Messiah's hand on the sea
- **Psalm 72:8** — "He shall have dominion also from sea to sea" — over all the peoples.
- **Zechariah 9:10** — "he shall speak peace unto the heathen: and his dominion shall be from sea even to sea" — heathen and sea in one verse.
- **Psalm 89:25** — of David's seed: "I will set his hand also **in the sea**, and his right hand in the rivers" — the Messiah's hand laid on the peoples (v27: "higher than the kings of the earth"). GEM — hidden in a covenant psalm.
- **Psalm 65:5** — God the confidence "of them that are afar off upon the sea" — the distant peoples, before v7 names them.

### The vine over the sea
- **Psalm 80:11** — Israel the vine "sent out her boughs unto the sea, and her branches unto the river" — the people spreading into the peoples.
- **Isaiah 16:8** — Moab's vine: "her branches... are gone over the sea."
- **Jeremiah 48:32** — "thy plants are gone over the sea, they reach even to the sea of Jazer."

### More of the symbol at work
- **Deuteronomy 33:19** — "They shall call the people unto the mountain... they shall suck of the abundance of the seas" — people, mountain, and seas in one blessing; Isaiah 60:5 supplies the interpretation (abundance of the sea = the Gentiles). GEM pair.
- **Job 7:12** — "Am I a sea, or a whale, that thou settest a watch over me?" — a man asks if he is the restless, guarded deep.
- **Psalm 104:25** — "this great and wide sea, wherein are things creeping innumerable, both small and great beasts" — the sea is full of beasts small and great (v26: ships, and leviathan). Daniel 7 in a nature psalm. GEM.
- **Psalm 96:11; 1 Chronicles 16:32** — "let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof" — read with Psalm 98:7 (Tier 1), the fulness is the world's dwellers.
- **Matthew 23:15** — "ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte" — sweeping the whole world of peoples for one convert.
- **Jeremiah 5:22** — sand set "for the bound of the sea by a perpetual decree... though they roar, yet can they not pass over it" — and the very next verse: "But this people hath a revolting and a rebellious heart" (v23). The sea keeps its bound; the people will not. GEM.
- **Jeremiah 31:35** — He "divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar" — cited as the guarantee that "Israel shall not cease from being a nation before me for ever" (v36). The ordinance over the sea underwrites the people's existence.

## Tier 3 — The people in the sea: fish, sand, and things cast in

### Fish = men (Habakkuk 1:14 in Tier 1 is the key)
- **Matthew 4:18** — Peter and Andrew "casting a net into the sea" — interpreted by the very next verse: "I will make you **fishers of men**" (4:19).
- **Mark 1:16** — the parallel call.
- **Matthew 13:47** ✦ — the kingdom-net "cast into the sea, and gathered of every kind."
- **Genesis 1:26, 1:28** — man given dominion "over the fish of the sea" — the first dominion named is over the sea's living things.
- **Genesis 1:22** — "fill the waters in the seas" — the same be-fruitful-and-multiply spoken over the fish and over man.
- **Genesis 9:2** — "upon all the fishes of the sea; into your hand are they delivered."
- **Numbers 11:22** — "shall all the fish of the sea be gathered together for them, to suffice them?"
- **Job 12:8** — "the fishes of the sea shall declare unto thee."
- **Psalm 8:8** — "the fish of the sea, and whatsoever passeth through the paths of the seas" — under *his* feet (Hebrews 2 gives this psalm to the Son of Man).
- **Ezekiel 38:20** — "the fishes of the sea... and **all the men that are upon the face of the earth**, shall shake at my presence" — fish and men in the same shaken list.
- **Hosea 4:3** — the land mourns, every dweller languishes, "the fishes of the sea also shall be taken away" — the people's judgment mirrored in the fish.
- **Zephaniah 1:3** — "I will consume man and beast... the fishes of the sea... and I will cut off man."
- **Isaiah 50:2** — "at my rebuke I dry up the sea... their fish stinketh... and dieth for thirst" — dry up the nation and its people perish.
- **Leviticus 11:9, 11:10** — fins and scales in the seas: the clean and unclean among the sea's creatures. (The dietary law stands as written; the symbol only deepens it: there is discerning to be done among what the sea yields.)
- **James 3:7** — "things in the sea... tamed of mankind."
- **Matthew 17:27** — "go thou to the sea, and cast an hook, and take up the fish that first cometh up" — the first fish drawn from the sea carries the temple ransom in its mouth. GEM.

### Sand of the sea = the innumerable multitude
- **Genesis 22:17** ✦ — seed "as the sand which is upon the sea shore."
- **Genesis 32:12** — "thy seed as the sand of the sea, which cannot be numbered."
- **Joshua 11:4** — the enemy hosts, "much people, even as the sand that is upon the sea shore."
- **Judges 7:12** — Midian and Amalek "as the sand by the sea side for multitude."
- **1 Samuel 13:5** — the Philistines, "people as the sand which is on the sea shore."
- **2 Samuel 17:11** — "all Israel... as the sand that is by the sea for multitude."
- **1 Kings 4:20** — "Judah and Israel were many, as the sand which is by the sea."
- **Isaiah 10:22** — "though thy people Israel be as the sand of the sea, yet a remnant... shall return."
- **Jeremiah 15:8** — "their widows are increased to me above the sand of the seas."
- **Jeremiah 33:22** — "as... the sand of the sea [cannot be] measured: so will I multiply the seed of David."
- **Hosea 1:10** ✦ — "the number of the children of Israel shall be as the sand of the sea."
- **Isaiah 48:18** — "thy righteousness as the waves of the sea" — and the next verse: "thy seed also had been **as the sand**" (v19). Waves and sand, both the people Israel might have been. GEM.
- **Romans 9:27** — "as the sand of the sea, a remnant shall be saved."
- **Hebrews 11:12** — "as the sand which is by the sea shore innumerable."
- **Revelation 20:8** ✦ — Gog and Magog, "the number of whom is as the sand of the sea."

### Cast into the sea
- **Matthew 18:6; Mark 9:42; Luke 17:2** — the millstone: better "drowned in the depth of the sea" than offend a little one — the same millstone-into-the-sea that ends Babylon (Revelation 18:21). GEM cross-link.

## Tier 4 — Sea narratives that enact the symbol

Real events on real water — and each one is the symbol acted out: an army the sea swallows,
a prophet given to the deep for the Gentiles' sake, the Lord of the sea treading it underfoot.

### The Red Sea: a nation drowned, a people brought through
(The blood arc, first plague to last battle: Exod 1:22 sons drowned in the river → Exod 7:20-21 that river turned to blood, its fish die → Exod 14-15 the drowners drowned. Revelation replays it: Rev 8:8 third of sea to blood; Rev 16:3-6 sea/rivers to blood **with the interpretation spoken** — "for they have shed the blood of saints... thou hast given them blood to drink"; Rev 14:20 the horse-bridle river of blood; and Ezek 32:6 gives Pharaoh, the sea-dragon, that same ending — "the rivers shall be full of thee." Also: yam suph ים־סוף shares its consonants with soph, "the end" — the Sea of the End. GEM arc.)
- **Habakkuk 3:8** — "Was the LORD displeased against the rivers?... was thy wrath against the sea?" — the prophet himself asks whether the war was ever really with water. Best door into this whole tier. GEM.
- **Habakkuk 3:15** — "Thou didst walk through the sea with thine horses."
- **1 Corinthians 10:1, 10:2** — "all passed through the sea; and were all **baptized**... in the cloud and in the sea" — the apostle reading the crossing as symbol. GEM.
- **Exodus 10:19** — the locust-army "cast... into the Red sea; there remained not one" — the dress rehearsal for Pharaoh's host (and Joel 2:20's echo).
- **Exodus 13:18; 14:2, 14:9, 14:16, 14:21, 14:22, 14:23, 14:26, 14:27, 14:28, 14:29, 14:30** — the crossing itself: the sea a wall for the people, a grave for the army; "Israel saw the Egyptians dead upon the sea shore."
- **Exodus 15:1, 15:4, 15:8, 15:10, 15:19, 15:21, 15:22** — the Song of the Sea: "the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea."
- **Numbers 33:8** — "passed through the midst of the sea" in the itinerary of the journeys.
- **Deuteronomy 11:4** — "he made the water of the Red sea to overflow them" — the overflow-verb of invading armies (Isaiah 8:8) turned on the invader.
- **Joshua 2:10** — Rahab: "we have heard how the LORD dried up the water of the Red sea... and your terror is fallen upon us" — the nations melt when the sea is dried.
- **Joshua 3:16** — Jordan's waters cut off toward the salt sea; "the people passed over."
- **Joshua 4:23; 5:1** — Jordan dried "as... the Red sea"; when the kings heard, "their heart melted."
- **Joshua 24:6, 24:7** — "he... brought the sea upon them, and covered them."
- **Nehemiah 9:9, 9:11** — "thou didst divide the sea... their persecutors thou threwest into the deeps."
- **Psalm 66:6** — "He turned the sea into dry land."
- **Psalm 74:13** — "Thou didst divide the sea... thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters" — Egypt the dragon broken in its own element (v14: leviathan's head, "meat to the people"). GEM.
- **Psalm 77:19** — "Thy way is in the sea, and thy path in the great waters, and thy footsteps are not known" — v20: "Thou leddest thy people." The unseen walker on the sea. GEM with the Gospels.
- **Psalm 78:13, 78:53** — "the sea overwhelmed their enemies."
- **Psalm 106:7, 106:9, 106:22** — "He rebuked the Red sea also, and it was dried up" — the sea *rebuked*, the same word the Lord speaks on Galilee.
- **Psalm 114:3, 114:5** — "The sea saw it, and fled... What ailed thee, O thou sea?" — the sea flees before the redeemed people. GEM.
- **Psalm 136:13, 136:15** — divided the Red sea; "overthrew Pharaoh and his host" in it.
- **Isaiah 10:26** — "as his rod was upon the sea, so shall he lift it up after the manner of Egypt" — Assyria promised the Red-Sea treatment.
- **Isaiah 43:16** — "which maketh a way in the sea, and a path in the mighty waters."
- **Isaiah 51:10, 51:15** — "made the depths of the sea a way for the ransomed to pass over."
- **Isaiah 63:11** — "Where is he that brought them up out of the sea with the shepherd of his flock?"
- **Acts 7:36** — wonders "in the Red sea."
- **Hebrews 11:29** — "By faith they passed through the Red sea... which the Egyptians assaying to do were drowned."

### Jonah: the prophet given to the sea (see the Sign of Jonah chapter)
- **Jonah 1:4, 1:5, 1:11, 1:12, 1:13, 1:15** — the sea rages until the fleeing prophet is cast into it, "and the sea ceased from her raging" — one man given to the deep stills the wrath; Gentile mariners fear the LORD and sacrifice.
- **Jonah 1:9** — "the God of heaven, which hath made the sea and the dry land" — the creator-confession spoken *in* the storm.
- **Jonah 2:3** — "thou hadst cast me into the deep, in the midst of the seas."

### Galilee: the Lord of the sea among the multitudes
- **Matthew 8:24, 8:26, 8:27; Mark 4:39 ✦, 4:41 ✦** — the storm rebuked: "even the wind and the sea obey him" — Psalm 89:9 in a boat.
- **Matthew 14:24, 14:25, 14:26; Mark 6:47, 6:48, 6:49; John 6:16, 6:17, 6:18, 6:19, 6:22, 6:25** — walking on the sea — the thing Job 9:8 says God alone does, and Psalm 77:19's unseen path made visible.
- **John 6:1** — "the sea of Galilee, which is the sea of Tiberias" — the stage for the loaves, the walking, and the bread-of-life discourse.
- **Matthew 8:32; Mark 5:1, 5:13** — Legion — "for we are many" (Mark 5:9) — driven into the swine, and the herd "ran violently down a steep place into the sea" — the *many* drowned in the sea like Pharaoh's host, in Gentile Decapolis. GEM.
- **Matthew 13:1; Mark 2:13, 3:7, 4:1, 5:21** — the staging nobody notices: the multitude stands on the land, and Jesus teaches *from the sea*, sitting in a ship — "he... sat in the sea; and the whole multitude was by the sea on the land" (Mark 4:1). He addresses the crowd from upon the very element that names them. GEM.
- **Matthew 15:29** — from the sea of Galilee up into a mountain, where great multitudes come to him — sea to mountain, peoples to the kingdom-seat.
- **John 21:1, 21:7** — the risen Lord at the sea of Tiberias: the net full of great fishes, 153, "and for all there were so many, yet was not the net broken."

### Elijah: the cloud out of the sea
- **1 Kings 18:43, 18:44** — "there ariseth a little cloud out of the sea, like a man's hand" — water drawn up from the sea to become rain on Carmel: the vapour-traffic of Jeremiah 10:13 acted out, shaped like a man's hand. GEM.

### Paul: brought through the sea onto the island ✦ (already told in the chapter)
- **Acts 27:5, 27:30, 27:38, 27:40, 27:41, 27:43** — the voyage, the tempest, "a place where two seas met," every soul saved through the water.
- **Acts 28:4** — the barbarians: "though he hath escaped the sea, yet vengeance suffereth not to live" — even they read the sea as the executioner of the guilty.

### The sailors' psalm
- **Psalm 107:23** — "They that go down to the sea in ships" — read through v30: he makes the storm a calm and brings them to their desired haven; the whole Galilee pattern, a millennium early. GEM.

## Tier 5 — Maker, ruler, and bounder of the sea

The creation and dominion texts. Flat readings stop at cosmology; once the symbol is known,
every one carries a second sense: the One who made and bounds the sea made and bounds the peoples.

- **Genesis 1:10** — "the gathering together of the waters called he Seas" — a sea is, by definition, a *gathering* of waters. The first hint. GEM.
- **Exodus 20:11** — "the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is" — inside the sabbath command.
- **Nehemiah 9:6** — "the seas, and all that is therein."
- **Psalm 95:5** — "The sea is his, and he made it."
- **Psalm 135:6** — his pleasure done "in the seas, and all deep places."
- **Psalm 146:6** — "which made heaven, and earth, the sea, and all that therein is."
- **Acts 4:24; 14:15** — the apostles' creator-formula, sea included.
- **Revelation 14:7** — "worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea."
- **Revelation 5:13** — "every creature... such as are in the sea... heard I saying, Blessing, and honour" — the sea's creatures join the doxology; the peoples will praise.
- **Psalm 69:34** — "the seas, and every thing that moveth therein" praise him.
- **Psalm 24:2** — "he hath founded it upon the seas" — the ordered world established over the waters.
- **Job 38:8** — "who shut up the sea with doors, when it brake forth, **as if it had issued out of the womb**?" — the sea is *born*, and bounded from birth. GEM.
- **Job 38:16** — "Hast thou entered into the springs of the sea?"
- **Proverbs 8:29** — "he gave to the sea his decree, that the waters should not pass his commandment" — the peoples under decree (Jeremiah 5:22 turns this on Israel).
- **Job 9:8** — God "treadeth upon the waves of the sea" — He alone; then the Son of Man does it (Tier 4). GEM.
- **Psalm 33:7** — "He gathereth the waters of the sea together as an heap."
- **Psalm 93:4** ✦ (epigraph) — "mightier than the noise of many waters, yea, than the mighty waves of the sea."
- **2 Samuel 22:16** — "the channels of the sea appeared... at the rebuking of the LORD" — and the next verses: "he drew me out of many waters; he delivered me from my strong enemy" (vv17–18). The rescue interprets the rebuke. GEM.
- **Nahum 1:4** — "He rebuketh the sea, and maketh it dry" — the rebuke-word again.
- **Amos 5:8; 9:6** — he "calleth for the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth" — the vapour-cycle doubled (twice, like Jeremiah 10:13 ‖ 51:16): the sea drawn up and poured back on the earth.
- **Job 36:30** — his light "covereth the bottom of the sea."

## Tier 6 — The sea in the temple

Solomon names a temple vessel "the sea" — a sea of cleansing standing on twelve oxen before
God's house, where the priests wash. A made, stilled, bounded sea in the sanctuary: the sea of
glass before the throne, in brass. Its fate tracks the kingdom's: Ahaz takes it down off the
oxen; Babylon breaks it and carries the sea away captive.

- **1 Kings 7:23, 7:24, 7:25, 7:39, 7:44** — the molten sea on twelve oxen facing the four quarters.
- **2 Chronicles 4:2, 4:3, 4:4, 4:10, 4:15** — the parallel account.
- **2 Chronicles 4:6** — "the sea was for the priests to wash in" — the purpose stated. GEM.
- **1 Chronicles 18:8** — David's captured brass becomes the sea — spoil of the nations, recast as the stilled sea in God's house. GEM.
- **2 Kings 16:17** — Ahaz "took down the sea from off the brasen oxen."
- **2 Kings 25:13, 25:16; Jeremiah 52:17, 52:20** — the Chaldeans break the sea in pieces and carry the brass to Babylon.
- **Jeremiah 27:19** — the word of the LORD "concerning the sea" among the vessels destined for Babylon.

## Tier 7 — Comparisons of measure and manner

The sea as a byword for what is vast, deep, unquiet, or far. Weak people-connection on the
surface; a few still glint once the symbol is known.

- **Genesis 41:49** — corn "as the sand of the sea."
- **1 Kings 4:29** — Solomon's largeness of heart "as the sand that is on the sea shore."
- **Job 6:3** — grief "heavier than the sand of the sea."
- **Psalm 78:27** — feathered fowls "like as the sand of the sea."
- **Job 11:9** — "broader than the sea."
- **Job 14:11** — "as the waters fail from the sea."
- **Job 28:14** — "the sea saith, It is not with me" — wisdom not found in the deep; not obtainable from the mass of men.
- **Deuteronomy 30:13** — "Neither is it beyond the sea" — the word is near thee (Paul reapplies it, Romans 10).
- **Psalm 139:9** — "the uttermost parts of the sea" — no distance among the peoples is out of reach.
- **Proverbs 23:34** — the drunkard "as he that lieth down in the midst of the sea" — asleep, adrift on the deep.
- **Proverbs 30:19** — "the way of a ship in the midst of the sea" — trackless.
- **Ecclesiastes 1:7** — "all the rivers run into the sea; yet the sea is not full" — everything flows into the mass, and it is never satisfied.
- **Lamentations 2:13** — "thy breach is great like the sea: who can heal thee?" — answered at last by Ezekiel 47:8: the waters *shall* be healed. GEM pair.
- **Lamentations 4:3** — "even the sea monsters draw out the breast" — kinder than the daughter of my people become.
- **Jeremiah 49:21** — Edom's fall "heard in the Red sea."
- **2 Corinthians 11:26** — "in perils in the sea" — listed among perils of waters, robbers, heathen, false brethren: every peril but one is people.

## Tier 8 — Geography: borders, routes, coasts, ships

The literal sea as boundary and highway. Least connected to people — though the borders
themselves are the LORD parting land from sea for a people, and a few carry a wink.

- **Genesis 14:3** — the vale of Siddim, "the salt sea."
- **Genesis 49:13** — "Zebulun shall dwell at the haven of the sea" — the tribal seed of "way of the sea, Galilee of the nations" (see Isaiah 9:1 below).
- **Exodus 23:31** — "from the Red sea even unto the sea of the Philistines" — the covenant bounds.

- **1 Kings 5:9; 9:26, 9:27; 10:22** — cedar floats and the navy of Tharshish; "shipmen that had knowledge of the sea."
- **2 Kings 14:25** — the coast restored "according to the word of... his servant **Jonah**" — the sea-prophet's only other appearance. GEM footnote.
- **2 Chronicles 2:16; 8:17, 8:18** — floats to Joppa; ships and servants "that had knowledge of the sea."

- **Isaiah 9:1** — "the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, **Galilee of the nations**" — the sea-road district named for the nations; Matthew 4:15 quotes it as "Galilee of the Gentiles" to open Jesus' ministry. GEM in plain sight.

## Appendix — key "waters / waves / flood" verses without the word "sea"

Not exhaustive (the word *waters* alone is 600+ verses); these are the ones where the
people-connection is explicit or nearly so, for the chapter's waters/flood threads.

- **Revelation 17:1 ✦, 17:15 ✦** — the definition: the waters "are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues."
- **Revelation 19:6** — "the voice of a great multitude... as the voice of many waters" — the equation run in reverse: a multitude *sounds like* many waters. GEM.
- **Revelation 1:15; Ezekiel 43:2** — "his voice as the sound of many waters" — the one voice that carries the sound of the multitude.
- **Ezekiel 1:24** — the living creatures' wings "like the noise of great waters."
- **Psalm 144:7** — "deliver me out of great waters, **from the hand of strange children**" — one verse, both halves. GEM.
- **Psalm 18:16–17; 2 Samuel 22:17–18** — "he drew me out of many waters; he delivered me from my strong enemy."
- **Psalm 124:2–5** — "when **men** rose up against us... then the waters had overwhelmed us, the stream had gone over our soul." Explicit.
- **2 Samuel 5:20** — Baal-perazim: "the LORD hath broken forth upon mine **enemies** before me, as the breach of **waters**." GEM.
- **Isaiah 8:6–8** ✦ — the waters of the river = the king of Assyria; "he shall overflow... he shall reach even to the neck."
- **Isaiah 28:2, 28:17** — the Assyrian "as a flood of mighty waters overflowing"; "the waters shall overflow the hiding place."
- **Jeremiah 46:7–8** — "Egypt riseth up like a flood... I will destroy the city and the inhabitants thereof." Explicit.
- **Jeremiah 47:2** ✦ — waters out of the north, an overflowing flood = the invader.
- **Isaiah 59:19** — "the enemy shall come in like a flood."
- **Daniel 9:26** — "the end thereof shall be with a flood" — per the TimeTested.Bible verse patch (dan-9-24 study): the verb ישחית is singular, so **He** (Messiah) destroys the people of the coming ruler, and *their* end comes with a flood. The flood is still an army — the Messiah's own host of saints (Jude 1:14; Rev 19:14) sweeping the enemy away, like Pharaoh's host at the Red Sea. NOT "the people of the prince destroy the city."
- **Daniel 11:22** — "with the arms of a flood shall they be overflown" — armies as floodwater.
- **Nahum 1:8** — "with an overrunning flood he will make an utter end."
- **Psalm 29:3, 29:10** — "the voice of the LORD is upon many waters... the LORD sitteth upon the flood; yea, the LORD sitteth King for ever" — enthroned over the peoples.
- **Psalm 93:3–4** — "the floods have lifted up their voice" (the epigraph's neighbors).
- **Psalm 46:3 + 46:6** — waters roar and are troubled ‖ "the heathen raged, the kingdoms were moved." Explicit parallel columns.
- **Psalm 32:6** — "surely in the floods of great waters they shall not come nigh unto him."
- **Psalm 69:1–2, 14–15** — "the waters are come in unto my soul... let not the waterflood overflow me" — amid "they that hate me" (v4).
- **Isaiah 43:2** — "when thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee."
- **Song of Solomon 8:7** — "many waters cannot quench love, neither can the floods drown it" — no mass of men, no invasion, can put it out.
- **Numbers 24:7** — Balaam: "his seed shall be in many waters" — Israel's seed among the peoples. GEM.
- **Isaiah 48:1** — "come forth out of the waters of Judah" — a people issuing from waters.
- **Revelation 12:15–16** ✦ — the serpent's flood after the woman; the earth swallows it.
- **Jeremiah 51:13 ✦, 51:55** — Babylon dwelling on many waters; at her fall "her waves do roar like great waters" while her own "great voice" is destroyed — the peoples' roar drowns the city's voice. GEM.
- **Ezekiel 26:19** — Tyre: "when I shall bring up the deep upon thee, and **great waters** shall cover thee" — the many nations of 26:3 closing over her.
- **Ezekiel 31:4–5; 19:10** — the waters made the Assyrian cedar great; the vine "planted by the waters... by reason of many waters" — kingdoms and rulers *fed* by the peoples (Trees chapter).
- **Genesis 6–9** — the Flood itself: judgment on "all flesh" delivered as water covering the world — and Jesus makes it the pattern of the end (Matthew 24:37–39; see the Days of Noah study).
- **Revelation 16:4–6** — rivers and fountains become blood, "for they have shed the blood of saints... and thou hast given them blood to drink."

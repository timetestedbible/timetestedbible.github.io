Genesis places the covenant bow in the cloud, and it is almost universally understood as a rainbow.
Yet the Bible's symbolic language reveals a much deeper meaning that yields profound insight throughout Scripture.
This chapter lets Scripture interpret Scripture, showing that the clouds of heaven are God's chariot and are associated with the full moon, which serves as His throne and guides the steps of His people.

Noah states the connection directly:

[quote.scripture, Genesis 9:13, 16]
____
I do set my *bow in the cloud*… And the bow shall be *in the cloud*; and I will look upon it, that I may remember the everlasting covenant.
____

The covenant bow is assigned a location.

[.scripture-pair%unbreakable]
--
Scripture then names what the cloud is:

[quote.scripture, Psalm 104:3]
____
Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the *clouds his chariot*: who walketh upon the sym:sym-wings[*wings*] of the sym:sym-wind[*wind*].
____
--

Isaiah supplies the corresponding action:

[quote.scripture, Isaiah 19:1]
____
Behold, the LORD *rideth upon a swift cloud*.
____

Israel's cloud-pillar likewise governed the timing of the nation's walk through the wilderness: when the cloud lifted they journeyed, and while it remained they camped (Exodus 40:36-37; Numbers 9:17-23).

Ezekiel then opens the chariot-cloud:

[quote.scripture, Ezekiel 1:4-5]
____
Behold, a whirlwind came out of the north, a *great cloud*, and a fire infolding itself, and a brightness was about it…
Also out of the midst thereof came the likeness of four living creatures… and they had the likeness of a man.
____

The things moving within the cloud appear as fire and light:

[quote.scripture, Ezekiel 1:13-14]
____
As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, and like the appearance of *lamps*… and the fire was bright, and out of the fire went forth lightning.
And the living creatures ran and returned as the appearance of a *flash of lightning*.
____

Ezekiel later says, __“I knew that they were the *cherubims*”__ (Ezekiel 10:20).
Chronicles names their office:

[quote.scripture, 1 Chronicles 28:18]
____
The *chariot of the cherubims*, that spread out their wings, and covered the ark of the covenant of the LORD.
____

[.scripture-pair%unbreakable]
--
The vision now rises above the cherubic chariot:

[quote.scripture, Ezekiel 10:1]
____
Then I looked, and, behold, in the firmament that was above the head of the *cherubims* there appeared over them as it were a sym:sym-sapphire[*sapphire*] stone, as the appearance of the likeness of a *throne*.
____
--

The linked symbol supplies the identification: sym:sym-sapphire[Sapphire] is the full moon in its enthroned form.
Without the points beneath the throne-word, the final phrase may also be heard, __“as the appearance of the likeness of a full moon.”__
Ezekiel then reveals the One seated there:

[quote.scripture, Ezekiel 1:26]
____
And above the firmament that was over their heads was the likeness of a *throne*, as the appearance of a sym:sym-sapphire[sapphire] stone: and upon the likeness of the throne was the likeness as the appearance of *a man above upon it*.
____

The vision closes by returning to Noah's bow:

[quote.scripture, Ezekiel 1:27-28]
____
I saw as the colour of amber, as the appearance of fire round about within it… and it had brightness round about.
As the appearance of the *bow that is in the cloud* in the day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness round about.
This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD.
____

=== The Throne as the Moon

Scripture makes the throne–moon comparison directly:

[quote.scripture, Psalm 89:36-37]
____
His seed shall endure for ever, and his *throne as the* sym:sym-sun[sun] before me.
It shall be established for ever *as the* sym:sym-moon[moon], and as a sym:sym-faithful-witness[faithful witness] in heaven.
____

The word rendered __“before me”__ is _neged_ — before, in front of, facing, opposite.
The full moon alone occupies that relation to the sun: the two lights face one another from opposite sides of the earth.

.The throne before the sun — in front, facing, opposite
image::geometry-of-the-day.svg["Top: the full moon stands opposite the sun with the earth between, while the new moon sits dark beside the sun and the quarter at right angles. Bottom: at evening the full moon rises in the east as the sun sets in the west; at dawn the sun's light out of the east finds the moon setting in the west",pdfwidth=86%,align=center]

At the dark phase, the illuminated hemisphere faces away from the earth; at the full phase, the moon stands opposite the sun and the face visible from earth is covered with the sun's light.
The chariot psalm gives the same covering:

[quote.scripture, Psalm 104:2-3]
____
Who *coverest thyself with light* as with a garment… who maketh the *clouds his chariot*.
____

Daniel's throne is fire, and Ezekiel's cloud-borne throne shines with fire, brightness, and the bow round about it (Daniel 7:9; Ezekiel 1:26-28).
The full moon is the sym:sym-faithful-witness[faithful witness] covered with the greater light and bearing it toward the earth.

Job then lays the cloud directly over the face of the *throne (full moon)*:

[quote.scripture, Job 26:9, ASV]
____
He incloseth the face of his *throne*, and spreadeth his *cloud* upon it.
____

Above the cloud-borne chariot is the throne with its Rider: the full-moon seat with its Rider enthroned.

[discrete]
=== The Bow Around the Throne

John now sees the bow around the throne itself:

[quote.scripture, Revelation 4:2-3]
____
Behold, a *throne* was set in heaven, and *one sat on the throne*… and there was a *rainbow round about the throne*, in sight like unto an emerald.
____

Genesis places the bow in the cloud; Ezekiel reveals its brightness around the glory above the cloud-borne throne; Revelation places it around the throne itself.
Ezekiel's stated likeness is the bow __“in the day of rain,”__ and within that same vision it surrounds the throne Scripture compares to the moon.
Through most of its visible changing, the moon itself appears as a bow carried toward and away from fulness.
The covenant reminder is thereby rehearsed across most of the month.

[discrete]
=== The Rider of the Clouds

Daniel gives the cloud its royal passenger:

[quote.scripture, Daniel 7:13-14]
____
One like the *Son of man came with the clouds of heaven*, and came to the sym:sym-ancient-of-days[Ancient of days]… And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom.
____

Read as a title, He is the Venerable One whose throne is established of old. Read as calendar language, He is seated at the advancement of days—the beginning from which the month, year, and age proceed.

Jesus claims Daniel's scene before the high priest:

[quote.scripture, Matthew 26:64]
____
Hereafter shall ye see the *Son of man* sitting on the right hand of power, and *coming in the clouds of heaven*.
____

At His ascension the cloud receives Him:

[quote.scripture, Acts 1:9, 11]
____
While they beheld, he was taken up; and a *cloud received him* out of their sight… this same Jesus… shall so *come in like manner* as ye have seen him go into heaven.
____

The cloud receives the Rider and carries Him from sight; the angels make that manner of departure the manner of return.

[discrete]
=== White Cloud, White Horse

John next sees the Rider upon a white cloud:

[quote.scripture, Revelation 14:14]
____
And I looked, and behold a *white cloud*, and *upon the cloud one sat* like unto the Son of man, *having* on his head a golden *crown*, and in his hand a sharp sickle.
____

The same rider-formula returns upon a white horse:

[quote.scripture, Revelation 6:2]
____
And I saw, and behold a *white horse*: and *he that sat on him had a bow*; and a *crown* was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer.
____

Both visions place a seated, crowned figure upon something white; one is a cloud, the other a horse, and the horse-rider carries the bow Genesis placed in the cloud.
John elsewhere sees Faithful and True seated upon a white horse when heaven opens (Revelation 19:11).

Zechariah completes the bridge:

[quote.scripture, Zechariah 6:3, 5]
____
In the *third chariot white horses*… These are the four *spirits of the heavens*, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth.
____

In Revelation, the white cloud and white horse occupy the place beneath the seated figure; Psalms calls the cloud a chariot, and Zechariah joins white horses to a chariot.

[discrete]
=== The Definition

*Cloud* — the visible covering and mobile throne-chariot of heaven: it manifests and veils its Rider's presence, bears the throne, and carries those received into it.
In prophetic rider scenes, the white cloud occupies the same conveyance-position as the white horse.

# Research: The virgin → the woman in travail → the man child (TODO)

The Isa 7:14 hybrid sign (ch. 4 parenthetical) completed as typology.
All texts verified 2026-07-02.

## The chain

1. Isa 7:14 — "a VIRGIN shall conceive, and bear a SON" — the sign
   given to the house of David (ch. 4: hybrid — an oracle whose
   fulfillment is itself a miracle).
2. Rev 12:1-5 — "a great SIGN in heaven; a WOMAN clothed with the
   sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of
   TWELVE STARS… she brought forth a MAN CHILD, who was to RULE ALL
   NATIONS with a rod of iron." The sun/moon/twelve-stars household is
   Joseph's dream (ch. 22) — the woman is covenant Israel, not one
   maiden only; the virgin scales the way every symbol scales.
3. Isa 66:7-8 — "she was delivered of a MAN CHILD… shall a NATION be
   born at once?" — the exact phrase of Rev 12:5, and Isaiah's own
   gloss: the man-child birth is a nation born.
4. Mic 5:3 — "he will give them up, UNTIL the time that SHE WHICH
   TRAVAILETH hath brought forth: then the REMNANT of his brethren
   shall return" — the travailing woman marks the turning of the age.
5. Jer 31:22 — "the LORD hath created a NEW THING in the earth, A
   WOMAN SHALL COMPASS A MAN" — inside the renewed-covenant oracle.
6. Gen 3:15 — "enmity between thee and the WOMAN, and between thy seed
   and HER SEED" — the first promise is already the woman-and-child
   sign; Rev 12 (the dragon waiting to devour) is its last scene.

## Meaning proposed

The virgin (2 Cor 11:2, chaste bride — sym-virgin) carries the SEED;
the sign of Isa 7:14 is one scene of the master-similitude: the
covenant woman travails and brings forth the ruling Son — and, at
scale, a nation born at once (Isa 66:8). The harlot's opposite number
from first promise to last vision.

Recommend the latter short-term (the chain is only ~6 verses).

# Every "seal" verse in Scripture — what the sealing of Revelation 7 is (2026-07-08)

Working reference behind the Four Winds chapter's sealing and the red-horse study
(research-peace-taken.md). Coverage: the full seal word-families, gathered by
Strong's number and verified against kjv_strongs.txt — Hebrew *chatham* H2856 (to
seal, 24 verses), *chotham* H2368 (signet, 13), *tabbaath* H2885 (signet-ring:
Gen 41:42; Esth 3:10; 8:2 etc.), the Aramaic sealing of Dan 6:17, the *tav* (mark)
of Ezek 9:4; Greek *sphragizō* / *sphragis* G4972/G4973 (33 verses, 22 of them in
Revelation). Companion sign-texts (Ex 13:9; Deut 6:8; Ex 31:13-17) carried with the
study. Full lists in the appendix.

## The finding

A seal is a signet-impression, and a signet carries one thing: **the owner's name**.
Everything Scripture does with sealing follows from that. The impression (1) marks
ownership and identity, (2) ratifies irreversibly, (3) secures what is sealed
against tampering, and (4) closes a thing until its appointed opening. The sealing
of Rev 7 is all four at once: the Name impressed on the servants (ownership),
before the winds are released (ratified in advance of judgment), protecting them
through what follows (Rev 9:4), until the redemption they are sealed *unto*
(Eph 4:30).

And Scripture does not leave the seal's inscription to imagination — it quotes it,
twice over:

> "The foundation of God standeth sure, having this **seal**: *The Lord knoweth
> them that are his.* And, *Let every one that nameth the name of Christ depart
> from iniquity.*" (2 Tim 2:19)

Two engravings on one seal: **ownership** ("knoweth them that are his" — the very
words of Num 16:5, "the LORD will shew who are his, and who is holy") and
**obedience** ("depart from iniquity"). The seal of God names whose you are and how
his own behave — the same two faces as the whole book's doctrine: the Name owned,
the commandments kept.

## Tier 1 — the forehead seal exists in the law

- **Ex 28:36-38; 39:30** — the high priest's golden plate, engraved "like the
  engravings of a **signet**, HOLINESS TO THE LORD," worn "always upon his
  **forehead**." The forehead-seal is priestly equipment, and its text is the
  Name's holiness. Rev 14:1 gives the 144,000 exactly this: "his Father's **name**
  written in their **foreheads**" — a kingdom of priests wearing the plate.
- **Ex 13:9 with Deut 6:8** — the two placements, decoded in the verse: "a **sign**
  unto thee upon thine **hand**, and for a memorial between thine **eyes**, *that
  the LORD's law may be in thy mouth*." What is bound on hand and forehead is the
  law — the commandments as frontlets (Deut 6:8). The seal's location is where the
  law lives.
- **Isa 8:16** — "Bind up the testimony, **seal the law among my disciples**." The
  law sealed *in* the disciple: Rev 7's sealing in Isaiah's words. (And it stands
  two verses from the Shiloah/River oracle — the sealed disciples and the refused
  soft water share a page.)
- **Rom 4:11** — "the **sign** of circumcision, a **seal** of the righteousness of
  the faith": the covenant mark decoded as seal, in as many words (the Foreskin
  chapter's ground, ch. 42).
- **Ex 31:13, 17; Ezek 20:12, 20** — "my sabbaths... a **sign** between me and you
  throughout your generations, that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth
  **sanctify** you... for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth." A royal
  seal carries name, office, and domain; the sabbath command is the one place the
  law carries all three — the LORD (name), Maker (office), heaven and earth
  (domain) — stamped as a perpetual sign into time itself.

### The counterfeit takes the same two places

"He causeth all... to receive a **mark** in their **right hand**, or in their
**foreheads**" (Rev 13:16). The beast's mark sits precisely where the Shema binds
the commandments — hand and forehead. It is the anti-frontlet: the question the two
marks ask is one question — *whose law fills your hand and your head?* And note the
direction of the true seal's hand-image: God wears His chosen as "the **signet upon
my right hand**" (Jer 22:24; Hag 2:23, "I will make thee as a signet: for I have
chosen thee"; Song 8:6, "set me as a seal upon thine heart, as a seal upon thine
arm"). God's people are the seal on *His* hand; the beast's people carry *its* seal
on their own.

## Ezekiel 9 — the type-scene of Revelation 7

"Set a **mark** upon the foreheads of the men that **sigh and that cry for all the
abominations**... Slay utterly old and young... but **come not near any man upon
whom is the mark**; and begin at my sanctuary" (Ezek 9:4-6). Every element of Rev
7:1-3 is here first: the forehead mark, set *before* the destroyers pass, sparing
its bearers, with the criterion stated — grief over lawbreaking. The marked are the
commandment-keepers in mourning; judgment cannot begin until they are marked, and
it begins at the sanctuary. The mark is the letter **tav** — in the paleo-Hebrew
script the prophets wrote, a cross-shaped stroke (the Behold the Hand study's
alphabet); the last letter, set on the men judged worth keeping.

## The mechanics — what sealing does, verse by verse

- **Ratifies beyond reversal**: "sealed with the king's ring, **may no man
  reverse**" (Esth 8:8); the lions' den sealed "that the **purpose might not be
  changed**" (Dan 6:17); the covenant written and **sealed** by princes, Levites,
  priests (Neh 9:38-10:1).
- **Transfers authority**: Pharaoh's ring onto Joseph's hand (Gen 41:42); the
  king's ring writes law (Esth 3:12; 8:8, 10).
- **Secures and preserves**: the tomb "sealing the stone" (Matt 27:66); the bride
  "a **fountain sealed**" (Song 4:12); "sealed up among my treasures" (Deut
  32:34); "my transgression is sealed up in a bag" (Job 14:17); instruction sealed
  into opened ears (Job 33:16); and the deed: Jeremiah's purchase of the field,
  "this evidence of the purchase, both which is **sealed**... put them in an
  **earthen vessel**, that they may continue **many days**" (Jer 32:10-14) — the
  land-redemption deed sealed and stored against the day of return. Set that
  beside Rev 5: a book "sealed with seven seals" that only the Kinsman-Redeemer,
  the Lion-Lamb, is worthy to open — the stored deed brought out at last; and
  while its seals open, the heirs themselves are sealed (Rev 7). The deed unsealed
  and the inheritors stamped are one transaction.
- **Closes until the appointed time**: the vision "as the words of a book that is
  sealed" (Isa 29:11); "**seal up** the vision and prophecy" (Dan 9:24); "seal the
  book, even to the **time of the end**" (Dan 12:4, 9 — the Daniel Unsealed
  chapter's hinge, ch. 32); "seal up those things" (Rev 10:4); and the
  counter-command that dates Revelation's openness: "**Seal not** the sayings of
  the prophecy of this book: for the time is at hand" (Rev 22:10).
- **The Spirit is the impression**: "ye were **sealed** with that holy **Spirit**
  of promise" (Eph 1:13); "sealed unto the **day of redemption**" (Eph 4:30); "the
  **earnest** of the Spirit in our hearts" (2 Cor 1:22); "him hath God the Father
  **sealed**" (John 6:27). The signet is the Name; the wax is the man; the hand
  that presses is the Spirit. And in Rev 7 the two operations of one element meet:
  the four *ruchot* are held back while the *ruach* is pressed in — no wind blows
  until the Spirit has stamped the last servant.
- **Creation under the signet**: "It is turned as **clay to the seal**" (Job
  38:14) — the earth itself as wax under God's ring (see research-clay.md, "the
  hand and the seal"); Leviathan's scales "shut up together as with a close seal"
  (Job 41:15); "he **sealeth up** the hand of every man; that all men may know his
  work" (Job 37:7).

## Where this touches the book

- **The Four Winds, ch. 30** — the sealing the winds wait on; with the corners
  take (the commandment-rememberers at the kanphot), the sealed and the gathered
  are one company described twice: law in the forehead, tassel at the hem.
- **research-peace-taken.md** — the two companies at the sixth seal; Rev 9:4 shows
  the seal protecting the on-earth company *through* the trumpets.
- **Daniel Unsealed, ch. 32** — sealed books and their appointed opening; Rev
  22:10's "seal not" as the counter-date.
- **The Foreskin, ch. 42** — Rom 4:11, the covenant sign as seal.
- **Worship / the calendar chapters** — the sabbath as the sign of sanctification
  carrying name, office, and domain.
- **Behold the Hand (digital)** — the paleo tav of Ezek 9:4.

# Research: Wings — full kanaph audit behind the expanded ch. 15

Done 2026-07-02. *kanaph* H3671: 108 occurrences under 18 KJV
renderings — the scatter is the point: the reader must learn to lift
the English and find the wing, because kanaph is the SIGNAL that a
verse may involve remembering the commandments (Num 15:38-39).

## The renderings (from the tagged corpus)

wings 61, wing 13, skirt 12, winged 2, "sort" 2 (birds of every
sort/wing), borders 2 (Num 15:38 ×2 — the tzitzit command itself!),
ends 2 (Job 37:3; 38:13), feathered 2, corners 2 (Isa 11:12; Ezek 7:2),
skirts 2 (Jer 2:34; Ezek 5:3), quarters 1 (Deut 22:12 — the second
tzitzit command!), uttermost part 1 (Isa 24:16), overspreading 1
(Dan 9:27), + grammatical splits (1 Kgs 6:24-27), flying 1
(Ps 148:10), Prov 1:17 ("of any bird" = owner-of-a-wing).
NOTE: both tzitzit commands hide under different glosses — "borders"
and "quarters" — neither says "wing," so English readers never connect
the commandment-fringe to the wings of refuge.

## The covenant under the wings (new section material)

- 1 Kgs 8:6 — the ark of the covenant set "into the most holy place,
  even UNDER THE WINGS of the cherubims" (8:7 they "covered the ark").
  Scripture's own phrase: the covenant dwells under the wings.
- Ex 25:20-22 — wings cover the mercy seat; the TESTIMONY inside the
  ark beneath; "I will commune with thee from between the two
  cherubims… of all things which I will give thee IN COMMANDMENT."
  The commanding voice speaks from between the wings, above the law.
- Ps 91:4 — "under his wings shalt thou trust: his TRUTH shall be thy
  shield" (truth = law, sym-truth).
- Ex 19:4-5 — "I bare you on EAGLES' WINGS, and brought you UNTO
  MYSELF. Now therefore, IF YE WILL OBEY MY VOICE… and KEEP MY
  COVENANT" — the wings land at Sinai, at obedience. Deut 32:11-12.
- Rev 12:14 — the woman given "two wings of a great eagle" to the
  wilderness: the Exodus replayed.
- Isa 6:2 — even seraphim cover face and feet with wings before the
  fire (ties ch. 23 shadow/fire).

## The torn skirt (new section material)

- 1 Sam 15:27-28 — Saul grasps Samuel's SKIRT (kanaph), it TEARS:
  "The LORD hath RENT THE KINGDOM of Israel from thee this day, and
  hath given it to a neighbour… BETTER THAN THOU" — the torn kanaph
  enacts the torn kingdom; the deposed-verdict formula of ch. 9.
- 1 Sam 24:4-5, 11 — David cuts off Saul's kanaph; "David's HEART
  SMOTE him" — a knife into the covenant edge of the LORD's anointed;
  he later holds it up as proof of covenant-KEEPING ("see the skirt of
  thy robe in my hand… I have not sinned against thee").
- The record in the skirts: "in thy SKIRTS is found the blood of the
  souls of the poor innocents" (Jer 2:34); "her filthiness is in her
  SKIRTS" (Lam 1:9) — what a people carries in its kanaph is its
  covenant record. Hag 2:12-13 — holiness does not rub off through the
  skirt; uncleanness does. Ezek 5:3 — the remnant bound "in thy
  SKIRTS."
- Deut 22:30/27:20 — "discover his father's SKIRT": the kanaph as the
  covenant boundary violated (in the same chapter as the tzitzit
  command, Deut 22:12).
- Counterfeit wings: Assyria's "stretching out of his WINGS shall fill
  the breadth of thy land, O Immanuel" (Isa 8:8); the "great eagle
  with great wings" empire-kings of Ezek 17:3, 7. Whose wing covers
  you (ch. 23's question).
- Matt 23 is framed in kanaph: v. 5 the enlarged kraspeda (fringes
  made broad, "to be seen of men") → v. 37 the refused wings ("as a
  hen gathereth her chickens… ye would not").

## The corners of the earth (new section material)

The earth wears a garment: gather the dispersed "from the four
CORNERS (kanaph) of the earth" (Isa 11:12); God takes "the ENDS
(kanaph) of the earth, that the WICKED might be SHAKEN OUT of it"
(Job 38:13 — the earth shaken like a garment); songs "from the
UTTERMOST PART (kanaph) of the earth" (Isa 24:16); "the end is come
upon the four CORNERS (kanaph) of the land" (Ezek 7:2). The dispersed
wait at the world's kanaph — the edges where the gleanings are left.

## Kept as-is (verified earlier)

Woman with the issue (kraspedon = LXX's tzitzit word; Matt 9:20-21;

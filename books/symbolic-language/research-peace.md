# Research: PEACE (shalom H7965 / eirēnē G1515) — full symbol study

Author 2026-07-05: "seams that PEACE is a symbol we need research and a
study on, it keeps getting droped by quoted scriptures... the gospel of
PEACE..."

All verses verified against kjv.json, bibles/akjv_strongs.txt,
bibles/wlc.txt, strongs dictionaries, bibles/hg.txt (2026-07-05).
KJV verses containing "peace": **420**.

## Verdict: symbol (passes the test)

The dictionary meaning of _peace_ — absence of conflict, inner calm, a
truce — is not what Scripture spends the word on. Scripture's peace is
**the covenant made whole**: the completed state of a kept covenant
between God and a people. The lexicon itself confesses it: _shalom_
(H7965) derives from _shalam_ (H7999), "to be safe; figuratively, to be
(causatively, MAKE) COMPLETED; to be friendly; to restore, repay, make
whole." Peace is a **completeness word** — the same axis the book already
runs on: meat is the word obeyed to completeness; peace is the STATE that
obedience completes. The two symbols are lexical cousins in function:
_teleios_/meat = the process finished in the man; _shalom_/peace = the
covenant finished between the parties.

## The definitional core (Scripture says it out loud)

1. **Isa 32:17** — "And the work of righteousness SHALL BE peace; and the
   effect of righteousness quietness and assurance for ever." A flat
   equation: peace is not a mood but the WORK-PRODUCT of righteousness.
2. **Ps 119:165** — "Great peace have they which love thy LAW: and
   nothing shall offend them." Peace belongs to law-lovers by definition.
3. **Isa 48:18** — "O that thou hadst hearkened to my COMMANDMENTS! then
   had thy peace been as a river." Peace is conditional on commandments
   heard; its figure is a river (waters stilled into a channel).
4. **Prov 3:1-2** — "forget not my LAW... length of days, and long life,
   and PEACE, shall they add to thee"; **Prov 3:17** — wisdom's (= the
   law's, Deut 4:6) "paths are peace."
5. **Mal 2:5** — "My covenant was with him [Levi] of LIFE AND PEACE...
   the law of truth was in his mouth." The covenant's own content is
   named: life and peace, held by the man with the law in his mouth.
6. **Lev 26:6** — the blessing-for-obedience chapter: "[if ye walk in my
   statutes] I will give peace in the land."

## Two in-text decodes (Scripture translates the word itself)

- **1 Chr 22:9** — "his name shall be Solomon [Shlomo], and I will give
  PEACE [shalom] and quietness unto Israel in his days." God names a king
  by the word and glosses the name in the same breath.
- **Heb 7:2** — Melchizedek, "King of Salem, which is, King of PEACE."
  The New Testament stops mid-argument to decode a place-name into this
  symbol — the same explicit-decode class as Rev 17:15 (waters) and
  Rev 17:9 (mountains). Jerusalem — city of _shalem_ — sits inside the
  same name (Ps 76:2 "In Salem also is his tabernacle"; Ps 122:6-8 "pray
  for the peace of Jerusalem... peace be within thy walls").
- Related names: Jehovah-shalom (Judg 6:24, Gideon's altar); the
  **Shulamite** (Song 6:13) — the feminine of Solomon: the peace-d one.

## The covenant of peace (the phrase Scripture itself builds)

- **Num 25:12** — Phinehas: "Behold, I give unto him my COVENANT OF
  PEACE" — first occurrence; given for zeal against covenant-breaking.
- **Isa 54:10** — "the mountains shall depart... but... the COVENANT OF
  MY PEACE [shall not] be removed" — spoken to the widowed wife being
  taken back (Isa 54 = the Marriage/Remnant fabric).
- **Ezek 34:25** — "I will make with them a COVENANT OF PEACE" — in the
  shepherd chapter the book already mines for milk/fat (Ezek 34:3).
- **Ezek 37:26** — "I will make a COVENANT OF PEACE with them; it shall
  be an EVERLASTING COVENANT" — immediately after the two sticks are
  joined into one (37:15-22).
- **Mal 2:5** — Levi's "covenant of life and peace."
- **Isa 53:5** — "the chastisement of OUR PEACE was upon him" — the cost
  of restoring the broken covenant, carried by the Servant.
- **Gen 9 / the bow** — the bow chapter already calls Noah's sign "a
  universal covenant of peace"; Ezek 34:25's covenant of peace likewise
  makes "the evil beasts to cease" — the bow unstrung toward earth.

## The gospel of peace (the commissioned thread)

- **Isa 52:7** — "How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him
  that bringeth good tidings, that PUBLISHETH PEACE... that saith unto
  Zion, THY GOD REIGNETH!" What the _basar_-bringer publishes IS peace —
  the announcement that the covenant is made whole again under the
  returning King.
- **Nah 1:15** — the twin verse: "that publisheth PEACE! O Judah, KEEP
  THY SOLEMN FEASTS, perform thy vows" — the published peace lands as a
  command to covenant practice (already load-bearing in the Gospel ch.).
- **Rom 10:15** — Paul quotes it: "the feet of them that preach the
  GOSPEL OF PEACE"; **Eph 6:15** — "feet shod with the preparation of
  the GOSPEL OF PEACE" (the armor's feet = Isa 52:7's feet).
- **Eph 2:14-17** — the decode of what that peace is: "HE IS OUR PEACE,
  who hath made BOTH ONE... for to make in himself of twain ONE NEW MAN,
  so MAKING PEACE... and came and PREACHED PEACE to you which were afar
  off, and to them that were nigh" — quoting **Isa 57:19** ("Peace,
  peace to him that is far off, and to him that is near... and I will
  heal him"). Peace = the two made one — the two sticks (Ezek 37), the
  far-off and the near, joined whole. The gospel of peace announces the
  reunion the covenant of peace enacts (Ezek 37:26 follows Ezek
  37:15-22's joined sticks directly).
- **Rom 14:17** — "the kingdom of God is not meat and drink; but
  righteousness, and PEACE, and joy" — the kingdom's content named with
  righteousness → peace in Isa 32:17's exact order.
- Hebrew NT witness (Ele Ha-Sodot, bibles/hg.txt): John 14:27 reads
  "SHALOM I leave for you, my SHALOM I give to you" — the Hebrew NT
  hands eirēnē straight back to shalom. (Corpus covers
  Gospels/James/Jude/Revelation; no Ephesians/Romans witness available.)

## The doubled shalom — true and false (wordplay pairing)

- **Isa 26:3 (WLC)** — "Thou wilt keep him in PERFECT PEACE" is literally
  שָׁלוֹם שָׁלוֹם — _shalom shalom_, peace doubled, for "the mind stayed
  on thee." The KJV's "perfect" is the translators reaching for the
  doubling.
- **Jer 6:14 (WLC)** — the counterfeit uses the SAME doubling: "saying,
  PEACE, PEACE [shalom shalom]; when there is NO peace [v'ein shalom]"
  (= Jer 8:11). The false prophets pronounce the doubled blessing over an
  unkept covenant — "they have healed the hurt of the daughter of my
  people SLIGHTLY."
- **Isa 57:19-21** completes the frame in one breath: "Peace, peace to
  him that is far off, and to him that is near... But the wicked are like
  the TROUBLED SEA... There is NO PEACE, saith my God, to the wicked."

## The counterfeit (fully stocked)

- **Deut 29:19** — the self-blessed peace: "I shall have PEACE, though I
  walk in the imagination of mine heart" — peace claimed while the
  covenant is walked away from; the Torah's own definition of the
  counterfeit, inside the covenant-curses chapter.
- **Jer 6:14; 8:11; Ezek 13:10,16** — "Peace; and there was no peace" —
  the prophets who "seduced my people" build a wall and daub it "with
  untempered morter": a covenant-shaped structure with no covenant in it.
- **2 Kgs 9:22** — "Is it peace, Jehu? ... WHAT PEACE, so long as the
  whoredoms of thy mother Jezebel and her witchcrafts are so many?" — no
  peace while covenant-breaking stands; peace-talk cannot outrun it.
- **Dan 8:25** — the end-time horn "by PEACE shall destroy many."
- **1 Thess 5:3** — "when they shall say, PEACE AND SAFETY; then sudden
  destruction" — the last counterfeit before the day of the LORD.
- **Rev 6:4** — the red horse "to take peace from the earth... a great
  sword" (already carried by the sym-sword glossary entry).

## The opposite pole: the troubled sea

**Isa 57:20-21** — "the wicked are like the TROUBLED SEA, when it cannot
rest, whose waters cast up mire and dirt. There is no peace... to the
wicked." Peace is the anti-sea: the peoples stilled under covenant vs.
the restless nations. This locks onto the book's sea study (Isa 17:12-13;
Ps 65:7 "stilleth the noise of the seas... and the tumult of the
people"). Isa 48:18 gives the positive image: peace AS A RIVER — water
bounded and channeled, over against the sea's unrest; Isa 66:12 repeats
it ("I will extend peace to her like a river").

⚠ **Structural-not-lexical flag:** "Peace, be still" to the sea
(Mark 4:39) is G4623 _siōpaō_ ("be silent"), NOT _eirēnē_ — verified in
akjv_strongs. The stilled-sea scene echoes the peace/sea polarity
structurally (Ps 107:29 "he maketh the storm a calm"), but do NOT claim
the word peace is in it. If cited, cite as the enacted picture, not the
vocabulary.

## Counter-text weighed: "not peace, but a sword"

**Matt 10:34** — "Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came
not to send peace, but a sword." Harmonized by the Lord's own
distinction, **John 14:27** — "my peace I give unto you: NOT AS THE WORLD
GIVETH." The peace He refuses to send is the world's — the daubed-wall
"peace, peace" that leaves the hurt healed slightly; the sword divides
the false peace so the covenant of peace can be made. Isaiah holds both
poles in one book without strain: "There is no peace to the wicked"
(48:22; 57:21, the refrain closing two sections) AND "the chastisement of
our peace was upon him" (53:5). Same resolution as gospel≠good-news-only:
the word is covenantal, so it cuts before it binds.

## KJV-flattened shaloms (translation buried the symbol)

1. **Zech 8:12** — KJV "For the seed shall be PROSPEROUS" — the Hebrew is
   זֶרַע הַשָּׁלוֹם, "the SEED OF PEACE" (H7965 confirmed in the
   interlinear): "the seed of peace: the vine shall give her fruit... and
   I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these" — seed,
   vine, remnant, peace in one restoration verse.
2. **Song 8:10** — KJV "then was I in his eyes as one that found FAVOUR"
   — Hebrew כְּמוֹצְאֵת שָׁלוֹם, "as one that FINDETH PEACE" (H7965
   confirmed). The Shulamite (the peace-d one) finds shalom in the eyes
   of the king whose name is peace. The KJV breaks the name-chain the
   Hebrew built.
3. **Gen 37:4** — Joseph's brothers "could not speak PEACEABLY unto him"
   (לְשָׁלֹם); Jacob then sends Joseph to "see whether it be WELL with
   thy brethren" (Gen 37:14, shalom again) — the brother sent to seek the
   shalom of brothers who have none for him. Narrative-layer candidate;
   flagged, not load-bearing.

## Supporting constellation (verified, second tier)

- **Isa 9:6-7** — "PRINCE OF PEACE... of the increase of his government
  and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David" — peace as a
  government, not a feeling; ordered "with judgment and with justice."
- **Ps 85:10** — "righteousness and peace have kissed each other."
- **Ps 37:37** — "Mark the PERFECT man [tam]... the END of that man is
  peace" — the tam-man (Job's word, Noah's word) ends in shalom:
  completeness ends in completeness.
- **Ps 34:14** — "do good; seek peace, and pursue it" (quoted 1 Pet
  3:11); **James 3:18** — "the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of
  them that make peace"; **Ps 29:11** — "the LORD will bless his people
  with peace."
- **Isa 54:13** — "all thy children shall be taught of the LORD; and
  great shall be the PEACE of thy children" (three verses after the
  covenant of peace, v10).
- **Isa 59:8** — "The WAY of peace they know not; and there is no
  JUDGMENT in their goings: they have made them crooked PATHS" — peace
  has a way; it is walked, not felt (cf. Luke 1:79 "to guide our feet
  into the way of peace" — feet again).
- **Luke 10:5-6** — "first say, Peace be to this house. And if the SON OF
  PEACE be there, your peace shall REST UPON IT: if not, it shall TURN TO
  YOU AGAIN" — peace behaves like a covenant offer: extended, received or
  refused, returned. **Luke 19:42** — "if thou hadst known... the things
  which belong unto thy PEACE! but now they are hid from thine eyes" —
  Jeru-SALEM missing the visitation of her own name.
- **Isa 45:7** — "I make peace, and create evil" — shalom as something
  MADE by God, the covenant state He constructs (counter to peace as
  circumstance); **Ps 120:7** — "I am for peace: but when I speak, they
  are for war."
- **Hag 2:9** — "in this place will I give peace" (the latter house);
  **Gen 15:15** — "thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace" (covenant
  burial formula).
- **Rom 5:1** — "being justified by faith, we have PEACE WITH GOD";
  **Col 1:20** — "having MADE PEACE through the blood of his cross"
  (peace is made, at covenant cost — the chastisement of our peace).

## Proposed meaning (one line, glossary register)

**Peace: the covenant made whole** — _shalom_, from _shalam_, "to make
complete, restore, repay": the state of a kept covenant, the
work-product of righteousness — not the world's truce and not a feeling
(John 14:27 "not as the world giveth"). What the gospel publishes is
this: the covenant of peace restored under the reigning King. The
counterfeit says the word twice over an unkept covenant.

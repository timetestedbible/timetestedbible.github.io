# Research: Clouds (ch. 12) — the vehicle/chariot thesis

Author 2026-07-04: "I believe clouds are largely a vehicle of transport,
like a chariot." Keeps ch. 3's promise ("The clouds have their own
chapter"). All verses verified against kjv.json 2026-07-04.

## The chariot itself

- Ps 104:3-4 — "who maketh the CLOUDS HIS CHARIOT: who walketh upon the
  wings of the wind: who maketh his angels spirits" — the bridge verse to
  ch. 11 (Four Winds): cloud-chariot + wind-wings + spirits/angels in one
  breath (cf. Zech 6:1-5, the four chariots = the four spirits of the
  heavens — the last chapter's material).
- Isa 19:1 — "the LORD RIDETH UPON A SWIFT CLOUD, and shall come into
  Egypt" (also feeds the Egypt/Babylon arc, chs. 18-20).
- Deut 33:26 "rideth upon the heaven in thy help"; Ps 68:4 "him that
  rideth upon the heavens"; Ps 68:17 "the chariots of God are twenty
  thousand"; 2 Sam 22:11 / Ps 18:10 "he rode upon a cherub, and did fly…
  upon the wings of the wind"; Nah 1:3 "the clouds are the DUST OF HIS
  FEET" (He travels; clouds are the wake).
- Jer 4:13 — the text's own parallelism: "he shall come up AS CLOUDS, and
  his CHARIOTS shall be as a whirlwind" — even the invader is described
  cloud‖chariot.

## The pillar that drives

- Ex 13:21 — leads the way; Ex 14:19-20 — REPOSITIONS (front to rear,
  stands between camps; darkness to one side, light to the other); Num
  10:34 over them "when they went"; Ps 99:7 "he spake unto them in the
  cloudy pillar."
- Ex 40:36-38 — the cloud's lifting IS the departure signal: "when the
  cloud was taken up… the children of Israel went onward… but if the
  cloud were not taken up, then they journeyed not."
- Ex 19:9 "I come unto thee in a thick cloud"; Ex 16:10 glory appears in
  the cloud; 1 Ki 8:10-11 the cloud fills the house.

## Passengers

- Dan 7:13 Son of man "came WITH the clouds of heaven"; Matt 26:64; Luke
  21:27; Rev 1:7 "he cometh with clouds."
- Acts 1:9-11 — "a cloud RECEIVED him out of their sight… shall so come
  IN LIKE MANNER" (boarding, and the promised return trip).
- Rev 11:12 — the two witnesses "ascended up to heaven IN A CLOUD."
- Rev 14:14-16 — one like the Son of man SITS ON the cloud and reaps the
  harvest from it (a working platform).
- Luke 9:34-35 — the disciples "feared as they ENTERED INTO the cloud" —
  passengers, and the voice speaks from inside.
- 2 Ki 2:11 — Elijah's chariot of fire + whirlwind (the same motor pool;
  ch. 11's wind-vehicles).
- 1 Thess 4:17 — "caught up together with them IN THE CLOUDS, to meet the
  Lord in the air" — the gathering rides.

## Clouds made of people (ch. 3's vapour reading, kept)

- Heb 12:1 — "so great a CLOUD OF WITNESSES" — a cloud made of people.
- Isa 60:8 — "Who are these that FLY AS A CLOUD, and as the doves to
  their windows?" — the returning multitude in flight.
- Jer 10:13 — "he causeth the VAPOURS TO ASCEND from the ends of the
  earth"; 1 Ki 18:44 — the little cloud rises OUT OF THE SEA (sea =
  peoples, ch. 3): a cloud is what rises from the sea.
- Opposite/counterfeit: Ezek 38:9, 16 — Gog "like a CLOUD to cover the
  land" (a multitude in motion, the invading kind); Jude 12 "clouds
  without water, carried about of winds"; Prov 25:14 "clouds and wind
  without rain" — the vehicle that carries nothing.

## The covering en route

- Isa 4:5-6 — cloud over Zion = "a tabernacle for a SHADOW… a place of
  refuge" (feeds ch. 28 Shadow).
- Ex 14:20 — the same cloud is darkness to Egypt, light to Israel.

## Placement + hooks

Ch. 12, after Four Winds (Ps 104:3-4 bridge; Zech 6 chariots/spirits) and
before Trees (closing bridge: the cloud waters the earth — Isa 55:10 rain
→ seed — and what grows next is the trees). Glossary sym-cloud added;
count 120 → 121. Fulfils ch. 3:467's promise; 1 Thess 4:17 now proven
rather than asserted.

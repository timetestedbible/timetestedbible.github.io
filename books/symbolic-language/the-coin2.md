## The Coin 

The kingdom of heaven is like a WOMAN who had 10 SILVER COIN,
    if she loses one COIN, does not LIGHT a LAMP and
    sweep the house and seek diligiently until she finds it?

    And when she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, 
    Rejoice withme, for I have found the coin that I had lost.! 

    Just so, I tell you there is joy before the angels of God over one sinner who repents.


In this Parable Jesus makes an explicit connection defining a silver coin
as symbol of a repentent sinner, and it follows the pattern of the prodigal son with
the celebration at the return. 

Properly identifying this symbol unlocks a mystery woven through a half
dozen parables and ultimately reveals the wisdom needed to understand the
mark of the beast, the Image of the Beast, the NAME of the BEAST, 
and the NUMBER of its NAME and why no one can buy or sell without it.

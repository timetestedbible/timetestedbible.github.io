This is the whole vocabulary in one place: every symbol the book uses, each with its meaning, a defining verse, and the chapter where Scripture establishes it.

[[sym-babel]]Babel / Babylon:: The self-made kingdom that builds a sym:sym-name[name] against heaven — an empire raised over many kingdoms, the covenant counterfeit. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow], ch. 10__

[[sym-barley]]Barley:: The firstfruits — the first and humblest grain, reaped in spring; the sheaf waved before the LORD, which is Christ risen. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 23:10; 1 Cor 15:20) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/grass/[Grass], ch. 13__

[[sym-beast]]Beast, wild animal:: A nation or kingdom of the world — the wild, predatory figure of a people, as the sym:sym-tree[tree] is its rooted figure; different beasts are different kingdoms, and the unclean beasts are the Gentile nations God cleanses in Peter's vision. +
[.seeref]__(Dan 7:17; 8:20-21; Acts 10:12, 28) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees], ch. 12__

[[sym-belief]]Belief, to believe:: To obey — a trusting, obedient response, not bare assent; the opposite of “believe” is _apeitheō_, to disobey. +
[.seeref]__(John 3:36; Rom 10:16) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief], ch. 18__

[[sym-bow]]Bow:: The covenant that binds a people together. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 9:13) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow], ch. 10__

[[sym-branches]]Branches:: The members of a nation — broken off, or sym:sym-grafting[grafted] in, while the sym:sym-tree[tree] itself stands. +
[.seeref]__(Rom 11:17) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees], ch. 12__

[[sym-bread]]Bread:: The word of God. +
[.seeref]__(Deut 8:3; Matt 4:4) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless], ch. 16__

[[sym-cast-into-sea]]Cast into the sea (a stone, mountain, or millstone sinking):: The fall of sym:sym-babel[Babylon] — a ruling power irreversibly destroyed, sunk into the sym:sym-sea[sea] of peoples, never to rise. A phrase-symbol, not a single word. +
[.seeref]__(Jer 51:63-64; Rev 18:21; Ex 15:4-5) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters], ch. 3 — not yet proven__

[[sym-cedar]]Cedar:: A great Gentile empire. +
[.seeref]__(Ezek 31:3) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees], ch. 12__

[[sym-chaff]]Chaff:: The empty husk winnowed off the grain and driven away by the wind — what the wicked are likened to: weightless, rootless, blown off and burned. Not to be confused with the sym:sym-tares[tares]. +
[.seeref]__(Ps 1:4; Matt 3:12) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/grass/[Grass], ch. 13__

[[sym-darkness]]Darkness:: Concealed disobedience — deeds hidden from the sym:sym-light[light]. +
[.seeref]__(John 3:19-20) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness], ch. 20__

[[sym-day]]Day:: The time of sym:sym-light[light] — exposure and accountability, when deeds are seen and tested; the season to walk, work, and watch. The children of sym:sym-light[light] are the children of the day. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 1:5; John 9:4; 1 Thess 5:5) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness], ch. 20__

[[sym-divorce]]Divorce (bill of divorcement):: The covenant lawsuit executed — the wife sent out of the house: exile and scattering. Hated when it betrays the wife of the covenant; commanded when the union is itself sym:sym-fornication[fornication] — a marriage God never joined. God gave Israel the bill, and answered His own no-return rule at the cross. +
[.seeref]__(Jer 3:8; Isa 50:1; Deut 24:1-4; Ezra 10:3; Mal 2:11-16) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/marriage-and-divorce/[Marriage and Divorce], ch. 9__

[[sym-dog]]Dog:: The Gentile — an unclean animal outside the covenant house, fed on crumbs until brought in. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 15:26-27) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees], ch. 12__

[[sym-door]]Door, gate:: The destination of the sym:sym-way[way] — the entrance to the house of God, marked on the posts. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life], ch. 7__

[[sym-dragon]]Dragon:: A devouring kingdom. +
[.seeref]__(Pharaoh, Ezek 29:3; Babylon, Jer 51:34) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah], ch. 5__

[[sym-egypt]]Egypt:: Spiritually, the sym:sym-harlot[whore] of sym:sym-babel[Babylon] that rides the beast — the city that kills the prophets, “where also our Lord was crucified”: Jerusalem. +
[.seeref]__(Rev 11:8; 18:24; Matt 23:37) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah], ch. 5 — not yet proven__

[[sym-elam]]Elam:: sym:sym-babel[Babylon] under another name — the same self-bound kingdom God breaks. +
[.seeref]__(Jer 49:35) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow], ch. 10__

[[sym-ephraim]]Ephraim:: The scattered firstborn whose seed becomes the fulness of the nations. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 48:19) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim], ch. 21__

[[sym-faith]]Faith:: Faithfulness — trusting obedience that acts; “faith without works is dead”. +
[.seeref]__(Hab 2:4; Jas 2:17) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief], ch. 18__

[[sym-fatherless]]Fatherless, orphan:: God's covenant people left without their Father — the sym:sym-remnant[remnant]. +
[.seeref]__(Lam 5:3) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless], ch. 16__

[[sym-fear]]Fear of the LORD:: Reverent awe expressed as keeping the commandments and departing from evil. +
[.seeref]__(Eccl 12:13) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fear-of-the-lord/[The Fear of the Lord], ch. 27__

[[sym-feet]]Feet:: The walk — a man's conduct, his sym:sym-way[way]; washed clean while the body stays bathed. +
[.seeref]__(John 13:10) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments], ch. 14__

[[sym-fig]]Fig leaves:: Self-made righteousness — a covering of one's own cut, all show and no substance; the cursed fig tree bore leaves but no fruit. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 3:7; Matt 21:19) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments], ch. 14__

[[sym-field]]Field:: The world — Jesus' own decoding — sown with the sym:sym-seed[seed] of the word and reaped at the sym:sym-harvest[harvest]; its unreaped edge is where the sym:sym-poor[poor] and sym:sym-stranger[stranger] glean, and its hidden treasure is bought whole to be mined. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 13:38; Lev 19:9-10) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/grass/[Grass], ch. 13__

[[sym-firmament]]Firmament (Heaven):: The divider God set between the two sym:sym-sea[seas] of people — the restless nations below and the gathered sym:sym-sea[waters] above, the heavenly assembly — and the place where the lights are hung. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 1:6-8, 14-17) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters], ch. 3__

[[sym-fish]]Fish:: Men in the sea of nations — and the redeemed drawn out of it, the catch of the fishers of men gathered in the net. +
[.seeref]__(Hab 1:14; Matt 4:19; 13:47-48) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters], ch. 3__

[[sym-fish-2]]Fish (great fish, sea-monster):: A devouring kingdom that swallows and is forced to give back. +
[.seeref]__(Jer 51:34; Ezek 32:2) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah], ch. 5__

[[sym-flood]]Flood:: An army in motion — a nation invading, or the Lord's own host sweeping the enemy away. +
[.seeref]__(Isa 8:7; Jer 47:2; Dan 9:26) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters], ch. 3__

[[sym-fool]]Fool:: One who hears God's word and will not do it — the lawless, not the unintelligent. +
[.seeref]__(Ps 14:1; Matt 7:26) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise], ch. 19__

[[sym-fornication]]Fornication, whoredom:: Spiritual unfaithfulness — breaking covenant with God to go after other gods and kingdoms; not merely the physical sin. The out-of-covenant union is itself the whoredom. +
[.seeref]__(Ex 34:15-16; Hos 1:2; Jer 3:9; Rev 17:2) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/marriage-and-divorce/[Marriage and Divorce], ch. 9__

[[sym-four]]Four winds:: The four spirits of heaven — God's executing agents, shown as the four horsemen. +
[.seeref]__(Zech 6:5) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-four-winds/[The Four Winds], ch. 11__

[[sym-fruit]]Fruit:: A nation's works, by which it is judged — “every good tree bringeth forth good fruit”. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 7:17) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees], ch. 12__

[[sym-garden]]Garden:: A planted stand of sym:sym-tree[trees] — a grouping of nations. +
[.seeref]__(Ezek 31:9) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees], ch. 12__

[[sym-garment]]Garment:: Righteousness — the righteous acts of the saints. +
[.seeref]__(Rev 19:8, ASV) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments], ch. 14__

[[sym-gospel]]Gospel:: The gospel of the kingdom — the renewed covenant, the law going forth from Zion. +
[.seeref]__(Isa 52:7) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/gospel/[Gospel], ch. 6__

[[sym-grafting]]Grafting (grafted in):: The wild sym:sym-branches[branch] — the sym:sym-stranger[stranger], the Gentile — received into the covenant sym:sym-tree[tree]. +
[.seeref]__(Rom 11:17) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees], ch. 12__

[[sym-grass]]Grass:: The individual mortal — a man, flesh; and the field God farms unto harvest. +
[.seeref]__(Isa 40:6-8; Matt 13:38) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/grass/[Grass], ch. 13__

[[sym-harlot]]Harlot:: The covenant-breaker who wears the form of covenant without its substance. +
[.seeref]__(Rev 17) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah], ch. 5 — not yet proven__

[[sym-harvest]]Harvest:: The end of the age, when the field of mankind is reaped — the wheat gathered, the chaff burned. +
[.seeref]__(Joel 3:13; Matt 13:39) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/grass/[Grass], ch. 13__

[[sym-heart]]Heart of the earth:: Jerusalem — the navel and heart of the land. +
[.seeref]__(Ezek 38:12) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah], ch. 5__

[[sym-heaven]]Heaven:: The dwelling and government of God; the hope is heaven coming down, not man going up. +
[.seeref]__(Rev 21:2-3) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life], ch. 7 — not yet proven__

[[sym-highway]]Highway:: The sym:sym-way[way] of holiness — the prepared road home. +
[.seeref]__(Isa 35:8) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life], ch. 7__

[[sym-horse]]Horse, white horse:: The mount of an executing spirit; the conqueror who wins the nations by the sym:sym-bow[bow]. +
[.seeref]__(Rev 6:2) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-four-winds/[The Four Winds], ch. 11__

[[sym-image]]Image of metals (gold, silver, brass, clay):: The succession of gentile world-kingdoms, shattered by the stone-kingdom of God. +
[.seeref]__(Dan 2:32-45) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain], ch. 2__

[[sym-instruction]]Instruction:: The discipline of the law, received and kept — what only sym:sym-fool[fools] despise. +
[.seeref]__(Prov 1:7; 23:23) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-fool-and-the-wise/[The Fool and the Wise], ch. 19__

[[sym-iron]]Iron:: The fourth kingdom — Rome. +
[.seeref]__(Dan 2:40) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain], ch. 2__

[[sym-island]]Island, isle:: A set-apart, sanctified people — the covenant-rock risen out of the sym:sym-sea[sea] of nations (a sym:sym-mountain[mountain] breaking the surface), waiting for the law that sanctifies it; "the island of the innocent," delivered to safety while the kingdoms fall. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 10:5; Job 22:30; Isa 42:4; Rev 16:20) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters], ch. 3__

[[sym-israel]]Israel:: The sym:sym-new[new name] of the one who prevails with God — covenant and character, not blood. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 32:28; Rom 9:6) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim], ch. 21__

[[sym-jacob]]Jacob:: The crooked, supplanting human heart that God transforms. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 27:36; Jer 17:9) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim], ch. 21__

[[sym-judgment]]Judgment:: The law's ruling and the ruler's office — what God declares due, kept like a commandment and owed first to the sym:sym-widow[widow], the sym:sym-fatherless[fatherless], the sym:sym-stranger[stranger], and the sym:sym-poor[poor]. To judge is to rule and deliver by the law (the judges were saviours; “a king to judge us… and fight our battles”), so the righteous pray for judgment and creation rejoices at its coming; condemnation is one verdict of the office, not the office. +
[.seeref]__(Deut 10:18; Ps 119:160; Matt 23:23; 1 Sam 8:20; Isa 33:22; Dan 7:22) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/justice-and-judgment/[Justice and Judgment], ch. 24__

[[sym-justice]]Justice:: sym:sym-righteousness[Righteousness] under a second English name — one Hebrew word behind both renderings: the commandments done where a neighbor can be wronged or righted. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 18:19/Jer 22:3; Ps 89:14/97:2) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/justice-and-judgment/[Justice and Judgment], ch. 24__

[[sym-knowing]]Knowing, to know:: Covenant relationship proven by obedience — the intimate “knew” of sym:sym-marriage[marriage]; a sym:sym-stranger[stranger] is one not known. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 4:1; Matt 7:23; 1 John 2:3-4) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief], ch. 18__

[[sym-lamb]]Lamb:: Christ, the offering whose blood covers the guilty. +
[.seeref]__(John 1:29; Gen 3:21) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments], ch. 14 — not yet proven__

[[sym-lamp]]Lamp:: The commandment. +
[.seeref]__(Prov 6:23) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness], ch. 20__

[[sym-leaven]]Leaven:: sym:sym-bread[Bread] soured — the word made impure, embittered, and puffed up; the hidden corruption that ferments through the whole lump, purged before Passover and forbidden on God's altar. The NT names it the corrupt doctrine of the Pharisees. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 2:11; Matt 16:12; 1 Cor 5:7-8) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/grass/[Grass], ch. 13__

[[sym-liberty]]Liberty:: The King's jubilee release — out of the slaver's house (sin, sym:sym-egypt[Egypt], sym:sym-babel[Babylon]) and home to the household, inheritance, and service of God; a change of masters, never the absence of one. Its constitution is the perfect law of liberty; its counterfeit, liberty as license, is a return to bondage. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 25:10; Isa 61:1; Jas 1:25; 2 Pet 2:19) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/liberty/[Liberty], ch. 25__

[[sym-life]]Life (the life):: Covenant existence — the life the law produces. +
[.seeref]__(John 14:6; Matt 7:14) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life], ch. 7__

[[sym-light]]Light:: The law lived visibly — good works others can see. +
[.seeref]__(Prov 6:23; Matt 5:16) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/light-and-darkness/[Light and Darkness], ch. 20__

[[sym-lion]]Lion:: The devouring ruler or empire — the sym:sym-beast[beast]-kingdom at its most royal and most predatory: “as a roaring lion… so is a wicked ruler”; the princes are lions, and Assyria and Babylon “the lions” that scattered the sheep. The figure's rightful holder is the Lion of the tribe of Juda — and in His kingdom the lion is tamed. +
[.seeref]__(Prov 28:15; Zeph 3:3; Jer 50:17; Ezek 19; Rev 5:5) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-way-the-truth-and-the-life/[The Way, the Truth & the Life], ch. 7 — not yet proven__

[[sym-living]]Living creature:: A nation — as the sym:sym-beast[beasts] of Daniel's sym:sym-sea[sea] are kingdoms and the living creatures of Revelation peoples. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-bow/[The Bow], ch. 10__

[[sym-living-2]]Living water:: The Spirit and word of God going out to give life. +
[.seeref]__(Jer 2:13; John 7:38) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters], ch. 3 — not yet proven__

[[sym-lost]]Lost sheep:: The scattered house of sym:sym-israel[Israel], sought and regathered. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 15:24) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/jacob-israel-and-ephraim/[Jacob, Israel & Ephraim], ch. 21__

[[sym-love]]Love:: Keeping the commandments — “this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments”. +
[.seeref]__(John 14:15; 1 John 5:3) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/knowing-faith-love-and-belief/[Knowing, Faith, Love & Belief], ch. 18__

[[sym-marriage]]Marriage, bride:: The covenant union of God and His people — the one-flesh bond God joins: the wife called by the Husband's sym:sym-name[name], brought under His sym:sym-wings[wing]. +

[[sym-meat]]Meat:: The solid, full teaching — the doctrine only those willing to obey can understand. +

[[sym-milk]]Milk:: The simplified first message, for babes. +
[.seeref]__(Isa 28:9; Heb 5:12-13) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/introduction/[Introduction], ch. 1__

[[sym-moon]]Moon:: The faithful witness — the Son, who shines with no light of His own but the Father's reflected, the light that shineth in the world's night; the lesser light that rules the night and marks the appointed times. +

[[sym-mountain]]Mountain:: A kingdom — the seat of ruling authority. +
[.seeref]__(Jer 51:25; Dan 2:35) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/mountain/[Mountain], ch. 2__

[[sym-naked]]Naked, nakedness:: Without covenant covering — exposed in one's breach. +
[.seeref]__(Ezek 16:8; Gen 3:7) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/garments/[Garments], ch. 14__

[[sym-name]]Name:: The covenant itself — taken as a sym:sym-marriage[bride] takes her husband's: to be called by His name is to be brought under His sym:sym-wings[wing], whose corners carry the commandments. +
[.seeref]__(2 Sam 7:13; Ex 20:7; Isa 4:1; Ruth 3:9) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/the-name/[The Name], ch. 8__

[[sym-net]]Net:: The sym:sym-gospel[gospel]'s gathering of all kinds out of the sym:sym-sea[sea] of peoples. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 13:47-48) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sea-and-waters/[Sea & Waters], ch. 3__

[[sym-new]]New name:: The renewed covenant, known — kept — only by the one who receives it. +

[[sym-night]]Night:: The time of sym:sym-darkness[darkness] — hiddenness and sleep, when opportunity closes; the children of the night sleep and are drunken, doing their deeds in the dark. +

[[sym-nineveh]]Nineveh:: A great city of the sym:sym-sea[sea] — gentile peoples who repent and rise in judgment. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 12:41) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah], ch. 5__

[[sym-oil]]Oil:: The pressed, tested fruit of the sym:sym-olive[olive] — proven works that make sym:sym-light[light]; not the Spirit. +

[[sym-olive]]Olive, vine, fig:: sym:sym-israel[Israel], the covenant people. +
[.seeref]__(Jer 11:16; Hos 9:10) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/trees/[Trees], ch. 12__

[[sym-passover]]Passover:: The blood-marked deliverance, kept even by the sym:sym-stranger[stranger] who joins. +

[[sym-poor]]Poor, meek:: The bowed-humble before God — one Hebrew word family behind both renderings; empty-handed toward Him, and so able to receive: the sym:sym-gospel[gospel]'s hearers and heirs of the kingdom. +
[.seeref]__(Ps 37:11/Matt 5:5; Isa 61:1/Luke 4:18; Isa 66:2; Jas 2:5) · see link:/books/symbolic-language/orphans-widows-and-the-fatherless/[Orphans, Widows & the Fatherless], ch. 16__

[[sym-remnant]]Remnant:: The faithful few who glean the edge — sym:sym-widow[widow], sym:sym-fatherless[fatherless], sym:sym-stranger[stranger], as Ruth did; small by design, refined like gold out of dross, at a fixed ratio traceable from the gleaning law's own geometry to Elijah's seven thousand. +

[[sym-righteousness]]Righteousness:: The doing of God's commandments — the law kept, worn as the garment of righteous acts; not a vague goodness nor a verdict only received. +

[[sym-sackcloth]]Sackcloth:: The sym:sym-garment[garment] of mourning and repentance. +

[[sym-scarlet]]Scarlet:: Covenant blood made wearable — and counterfeited by those who wear the form without the substance. +

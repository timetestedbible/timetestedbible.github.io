Daniel Larimer spent twenty years building systems that encourage integrity by design.
He created three of the world's most-used blockchains — BitShares, Steem, and EOS, each entrusted with billions of dollars — engineering against the moral hazard and corruption of our financial and political lives, and his _More Equal Animals: The Subtle Art of True Democracy_ carried that work into governance.
Then came the realization that we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against powers in high places — and no protocol reforms those.

He now spends his days as Jeremiah spent his: in the Scriptures, watching for the fall of Babylon and the soon return of Jesus.
His earlier studies are published in _Time Tested Tradition: The Renewed Biblical Calendar_ and the _Biblical Tribulation Survival Guide_; his ongoing work is free at https://TimeTested.Bible[TimeTested.Bible], a study Bible that links every verse of Scripture to the studies, symbols, and sources behind this book.

The two callings are one discipline.
A blockchain survives because no one is asked to trust anyone: every claim can be checked, by anyone, against a record all can read.
This book is built the same way.
No argument in it rests on the author's say-so; the symbols are derived from Scripture's own definitions, the sources are dated and cited, and the reader is handed the tools to run every check himself.
What he asked of his networks, he asks of his readers: take no man's word — verify.

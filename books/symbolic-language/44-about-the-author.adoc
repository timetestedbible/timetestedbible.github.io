Daniel Larimer is the author of _Time Tested Tradition: The Renewed Biblical Calendar_ and the builder of https://TimeTested.Bible[TimeTested.Bible], a free study Bible that links every verse of Scripture to the studies, symbols, and sources behind this book.
He writes to test inherited tradition against the text — line upon line, precept upon precept — and to put the tools for that testing into the reader's own hands.

Daniel Larimer spent twenty years building systems that encourage integrity by design — three of the world's most-used blockchains, BitShares, Steem, and EOS, each securing billions of dollars against the moral hazard and corruption of our financial and political lives.
_More Equal Animals: The Subtle Art of True Democracy_ carried that work into governance.
Then came the realization that we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against powers in high places — and no protocol reforms those.
He now spends his days as Jeremiah spent his: in the Scriptures, watching for the fall of Babylon and the soon return of Yeshua.
His studies are published in _Time Tested Tradition: The Renewed Biblical Calendar_, the _Biblical Tribulation Survival Guide_, and at https://TimeTested.Bible[TimeTested.Bible] — a free study Bible linking every verse to the studies, symbols, and sources behind this book — tools for the reader to prove all things, and hold fast that which is good.

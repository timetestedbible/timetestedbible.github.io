# ESV (Crossway) — gratis-use policy

Source: https://www.crossway.org/permissions/ — fetched 2026-07-14.

- Up to **500 verses** may be quoted without written permission.
- Quoted verses may not exceed **25% of the total text** of the work.
- Verses may not amount to **more than one-half of any single book** of the Bible.
- The verses may not be part of **"a commentary or other biblical reference
  work"** — gratis use does not apply to such works (written permission
  required regardless of count).
- The ESV may not be quoted in any publication made available to the public
  under a Creative Commons license.

# NKJV (Thomas Nelson / HarperCollins Christian Publishing) — gratis-use policy

The policy applies to ALL Thomas Nelson translations: NKJV, NCV, ICB, NET
(commercial), Expanded Bible, The Voice.

Gratis Use Guidelines (all must hold, verbatim from the page):

- "No more than **500 verses** quoted in total."
- "Scripture does not make up more than **25% of the total text**."
- "Scripture does not account for more than **50% of an entire book** of
  the Bible."
- "Scripture is **not quoted in a commentary or any other Biblical
  Reference work**."
- "Scripture is properly cited (see Copyright Notices section below)."
- The product is not in the "Not Covered by Gratis Use" list (stand-alone
  verse products, study-Bible apparatus, lyrics, >50% of a Bible book or
  the whole translation).

Outside these guidelines, written permission via the HarperCollins
Christian Permission Request Form.

Required notice:

> Scripture taken from the New King James Version®. Copyright © 1982 by
> Thomas Nelson. Used by permission. All rights reserved.

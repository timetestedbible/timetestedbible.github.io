# NASB and Amplified (AMP) — The Lockman Foundation gratis-use policy

Source: https://www.lockman.org/permission-to-quote-copyright-trademark-information/ — fetched 2026-07-14.

Both NASB and AMP (same terms):

- "up to and inclusive of **one thousand (1,000) verses** without express
  written permission"
- the verses may not "amount to a complete book of the Bible"
- "nor do the verses quoted account for more than **50% of the total text**
  of the work in which they are quoted"
- "nor may more than 1,000 verses be stored in an electronic retrieval
  system" (applies to the web edition)
- For quotations exceeding 10 verses, a link to lockman.org is requested
  where the platform allows hyperlinks.

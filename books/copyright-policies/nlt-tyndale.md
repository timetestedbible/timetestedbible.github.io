# NLT (Tyndale House) — gratis-use policy

Source: https://www.tyndale.com/permissions and the NLT standard notice —
gathered 2026-07-14 via search excerpts (page fetch not attempted).

- The NLT "may be quoted in any print or eBook form, up to and inclusive of
  **500 verses** without express written permission of the publisher"
- "provided that the verses quoted do not account for more than **25
  percent** of the work in which they are quoted"
- "and provided that a complete book of the Bible is not quoted."
- Beyond 500 verses / 25%: written approval from Tyndale House Publishers.

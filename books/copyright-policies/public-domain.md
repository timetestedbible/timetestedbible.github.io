# Public-domain and freely-usable translations

No quotation limits, no required notices (customary attribution retained on
the copyright pages anyway).

- **KJV (1611/1769)** — public domain in the United States. (In the UK the
  Crown's letters patent govern printing; irrelevant to US publication via
  BookBaby, noted for foreign editions.)
- **AKJV** — the KJV with modernized orthography; public domain.
- **ASV (1901)** — public domain.
- **YLT (Young's Literal, 1862/98)** — public domain.
- **JPS (1917)** — public domain.
- **Darby (DBT)** — public domain.
- **Douay-Rheims (DRB)** — public domain.
- **Smith's Literal Translation (Julia Smith, 1876)** — public domain.
- **Brenton LXX (1851)** — public domain.
- **BSB / BLB (Berean Standard / Literal Bible)** — dedicated to the public
  domain (CC0) by Bible Hub; "courtesy of Bible Hub" credit is customary.
- The author's own renderings from the Hebrew/Greek and the Hebrew Gospels
  translations made for these books carry no third-party claim.

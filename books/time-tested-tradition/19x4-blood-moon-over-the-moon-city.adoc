=== The Moon and the Throne Share a Root

The Hebrew word for full moon is *keseh* (H3677), from the root H3680 _kasah_ = "to fill up."
The Hebrew word for throne is *kissé* (H3678) — from the same root.
When the moon reaches fullness, the throne is filled.
When the king takes his seat, the moon is full.

[quote.scripture, Psalm 81:3 (JPS)]

____

The shofar and the full moon are bound in the same command.

[quote.scripture, Proverbs 7:20 (JPS)]
____
He hath taken the bag of money with him; he will come home at the *full moon*.
____

The husband departs.
He returns at the keseh — the full moon.
The bride knows the season but not the exact day or hour.
The bridegroom returns when the throne is filled.

Established AS the moon.

=== The Same Word

It goes deeper than a shared root.
Remove the vowel points — the nikud added by the Masoretes roughly 600-900 AD — and look at the consonants alone:

* *כֶּסֶא* (keseh) = full moon — consonants: *כסא*
* *כִּסֵּא* (kissé) = throne — consonants: *כסא*

*Identical.*
כסא = כסא.
Kaf-samekh-aleph.
The only difference is the vowel points.
Without the pointing, the unpointed text כסא reads BOTH "full moon" AND "throne" simultaneously.
The Masoretic pointing forces a choice.
The consonantal text holds both.

There is a strong case that Revelation was originally written in Hebrew, not Greek (hebrewgospels.com).
The Hebrew text of Revelation 4:2 reads:

[quote.scripture, Revelation 4:2, Hebrew NT]
____
וְרָאִיתִי *כִּסֵּא* אֶחָד יֹשֵׁב בַּשָּׁמַיִם וְעַל הַ**כִּסֵּא** יֹשֵׁב אֶחָד
____

Without vowel points:

[quote.scripture]
____
וראיתי *כסא* אחד ישב בשמים ועל ה**כסא** ישב אחד
____

"I saw a *כסא* set in heaven, and one sat on the *כסא*."

Throne or full moon?
The consonantal text says _both_.
If Hebrew was the original language, John didn't use a word that _resembles_ the full moon — he used the same word.
Every כסא in Revelation — all 47 of them — carries both readings in the unpointed text:

* Revelation 12:5 — Man child caught up to his *כסא* = to his throne / to his full moon
* Revelation 14:3 — 144,000 sing before the *כסא* = before the throne / before the full moon
* Revelation 16:17 — "It is done" from the *כסא* = from the throne / from the full moon
* Revelation 20:11 — A great white *כסא* = a great white throne / a great white full moon

The great white throne IS the great white full moon.
The man child is caught up to the full moon.
"It is done" is declared from the full moon.
The consonantal text doesn't distinguish between them because they ARE the same concept: the place of fullness, the seat of authority, the appointed time of the king.

And it is not unusual for the כסא to speak.
Psalm 19 already established that the heavens have a voice:

[quote.scripture, Psalm 19:2-4]
____
*Night unto night showeth knowledge.*
There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard.
Their line is gone out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world.
____

Night unto night — that is the moon's domain.
The moon _shows knowledge_.
Its voice is heard in every language.
Its line reaches all the earth.
So when Revelation 16:17 says "a great voice from the כסא, saying, _It is done_" — the full moon has been speaking since Genesis.
It is a faithful witness (Psalm 89:37).
It shows knowledge.
And on the appointed day, it declares the kingdom.

A Greek reader sees _thronos_.
An English reader sees "throne."
A Hebrew reader sees כסא — and hears the full moon.

[quote.scripture, Deuteronomy 1:3]

=== The Blood Moon

Joel 2:31 says the moon will turn to blood "before" the great day.
The Hebrew word is *liphnei* (H6440) — literally "to the _face_ of."
Built on the word for face (paniym).
It can mean "prior to" in time.
Or it can mean "*in the presence of*" — facing, in front of.

If "before" means _temporal_, the blood moon is an advance warning you can watch for before the day arrives.
If "before" means _facing_, the blood moon IS the day arriving — the signs and the day are one.
There is no gap.

Peter settles which reading is correct.
At Pentecost, he quotes Joel and declares:

[quote.scripture, Acts 2:17-20]
____
And it shall come to pass in the last days, saith God, I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh...
And I will shew wonders in heaven above, and signs in the earth beneath; blood, and fire, and vapour of smoke: The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before that great and notable day of the Lord come.
____

And just before this quotation, Peter says:

[quote.scripture, Acts 2:16]
____
But this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel.
____

"*This IS that.*"
Peter declared that the Joel prophecy — Spirit outpouring, blood, fire, smoke, sun to darkness, moon to blood — was _already unfolding_ at Pentecost.
The liphnei window is not a narrow gap before a distant event.
It is the era that faces the great day.
But the moon was not blood for 2,000 years.
The specific signs — sun darkened, moon to blood — happen at specific moments _within_ that facing-era.
Peter proves we are inside the window.
The blood moon tells you where in the window you are standing.

"The last trump" — G2078 _eschatos_ (farthest, final) + G4536 _salpinx_ (trumpet).
Last of what sequence?

The word "Jubilee" itself is a trumpet.
Hebrew *yobel* (H3104) means "the blast of a horn; ram's horn; trumpet."
The Jubilee year is literally the _ram's-horn year_.
When Leviticus 25:9 says "cause the trumpet of the jubile to sound," the Hebrew is nearly redundant — "cause the horn of the horn to sound."
The Jubilee IS a trumpet blast.

Now consider: if the shofar is blown at every full moon (Psalm 81:3), then the twelfth month's full moon carries the _last_ full-moon trumpet of the year.
The "last trump" could be the trumpet of the last month — Adar's full moon.

And if yobel = ram's horn = trumpet, then the "last yobel" = the last jubilee trumpet.
If we are at the end of the 70th jubilee, the "last trump" is the final blast of the final jubilee cycle — the trumpet that closes the era and opens the kingdom.

The "last trump" may work at three scales simultaneously: the last of the seven trumpets (Revelation 11:15), the last full-moon trumpet of the year (Adar), and the last jubilee trumpet of the 70th cycle.
All three converge on one moment.

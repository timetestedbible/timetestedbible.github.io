=== Diet Laws

The church has wrongly taught that the dietary laws have been done away with since Jesus.
When reading the New Testament Scriptures, it is important to keep in mind that the definition of the word “food” is found in Leviticus 11 and Deuteronomy 14.
Food did not have the modern meaning of “anything we put in our mouth.”
When we eat “biblical food,” it goes into our mouth, is purified by our stomach, and is then expelled, thereby cleaning all “biblical food.”

On the day of judgment, those who willfully eat swine and other “unclean food” without repentance will be thrown into the fire.

[quote.scripture, Isaiah 66:16-17, BSB]
____
For by fire and His sword (nukes), the LORD will execute judgment on all flesh, and many will be slain by the LORD …those who eat the flesh of swine and vermin and rats—will perish together,” declares the LORD.
____

Biblical Food:

[%hardbreaks]
Land animals: Animals with split hoofs that chew their food are permitted, but camels, pigs, rock badgers, and hares are not.
Water animals: Fish with fins and scales are permitted, but shellfish and fish without fins and scales are not.
Fowl: Certain fowl are forbidden, including birds of prey, birds that eat carrion, and certain water birds. Poultry, pigeons, and doves are permitted, as are wild birds not on the forbidden list.
Blood: Blood from mammals and birds is forbidden.
Fat: Forbidden fat is the covering fat of mammals, not the fat marbled into the meat.

=== Judge Not

[quote.scripture, Matthew 7]
____
Judge not, that ye be not judged.
For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.
____

This particular verse and concept has been distorted by those who seek to nullify the law.
The idea is that only the LORD can judge a person’s heart and pronounce that person guilty or not.
It is not judging others to remind them of what the law of the LORD says or to declare certain behaviors to be breaking the LORD’s law.
The line is crossed when you condemn others to hell or ascribe salvation unto yourself by virtue of your keeping of the law in more points than them.
Keeping the law can be a source of pride that tempts many people to think too highly of themselves.
That pride is a deadly sin and the penalty of breaking the greatest or the least of the laws is the same: death.
Since all have sinned and continue to sin while in the flesh, it is foolish to condemn others simply because they disagree with you.
I do not wish to be judged by my ability to discern all doctrine correctly and follow it; therefore, I do not condemn others simply because they disagree with my conclusions.

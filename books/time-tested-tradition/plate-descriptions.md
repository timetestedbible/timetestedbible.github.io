# Chapter Plate Descriptions — TTT 2nd ed — scene concepts (DRAFT)

One-line scene concepts matching plate-captions.md; each expands into a full
generation paragraph (MEAT's Rembrandt-etching series style — see
books/symbolic-language/plate-descriptions.md for the template and the rules:
never show the face of Christ; no winged angels; no legible generated text;
counts stated must be countable) when the author approves the set.

- **01-searching-king** — a robed king alone at night in a library of scrolls, one unrolled under the lamp, his finger on a line; the window behind him open to the stars.
- **02-inherited-scrolls** — a father solemnly handing bound scrolls and a small idol down a line of sons, each generation's face more trusting; the last son looks away toward a distant sunrise.
- **03-map-and-terrain** — a surveyor on a hilltop holding a creased map against the actual valley below, where the river visibly runs a different course than the map shows.
- **04-before-the-council** — two plain fishermen standing upright before a curved bench of seated robed elders, the seat at the center empty.
- **05-day-lost-at-sea** — a battered carrack at anchor, sailors on deck counting on their fingers against a churchman's calendar held up from the dock; the sea horizon curving away behind.
- **06-bridegroom-dawn** — the sun breaking over hill country like a bridegroom from his chamber door, its light washing out the last constellations; below, a camp stirs and manna is gathered.
- **07-watchman-full-moon** — a watchman on the temple parapet, shofar half-raised, the full moon rising huge in the east exactly opposite the setting sun.
- **07x-lucifer-moon** — REUSE: copy MEAT's images/masters/30-lucifer-moon.jpg + print twin + caption (same chapter, same author).
- **08-abib-sheaf** — a barley field at first light, ears bent heavy and green-gold; a man kneels testing a head between his fingers, sickle waiting on his shoulder.
- **09-watchman-tower** — a mud-brick tower at dusk, one man atop it with a measuring rod at arm's length against the western sky where a thin crescent and the sunset stand together.
- **10-sabbath-quarters** — a family at rest under a fig tree outside their house, tools laid down; above, the moon drawn four times across the sky's arc — full, waning half, dark, waxing half.
- **11-star-of-saturn** — a Roman street shrine: a six-rayed star cut in stone over Saturn's hooded image, market crowds passing beneath without looking up.
- **12-darkness-at-noon** — Jerusalem from a distance at midday, the sun blackened above the temple mount, birds fleeing, lamps being lit in windows at noon.
- **13-herods-eclipse** — Herod's palace window at night, the old king slumped in shadow; through the window the moon half-swallowed by eclipse over the courtyard where guards look up.
- **14-three-nights** — the sealed tomb in a garden shown under one sky painted in three passages — dusk, deep night with stars wheeled round, and the grey before dawn.
- **15-backs-to-the-temple** — five and twenty men in the temple's inner court, their backs to the sanctuary door, arms lifted to the rising sun beyond the east gate.
- **15x1-two-reckonings** — a scribe at a table with two counting-boards and two open scrolls, one dated from a crowning, one from a conquest, a puzzled student watching.
- **15x2-crowned-in-rome** — young Herod on the Capitol steps between two Roman patrons, laurel and eagle standards above; in the sky-line distance behind, war galleys locked at Actium.
- **15x4-steadfast-heavens** — an ancient astronomer with a sighting-tube and a modern observer with a telescope back to back on the same hill under one unchanged wheeling sky.
- **16-thief-beside-him** — two crosses seen from behind against the darkened noon sky, the thief's face turned toward the center cross (face of Christ not shown), city walls below.
- **17-taught-at-the-door** — a father at his doorpost at evening, hand binding the words, children looking up; the doorframe catches the last light.
- **18-booths-full-moon** — a hillside of leafy booths at harvest, families beneath them, tables set; the full moon of the fifteenth flooding the whole scene.
- **19-fringes-remembered** — a man pausing mid-step in a market, his eye caught by the blue-corded fringe on his own garment corner; behind him a stall of forbidden wares.
- **23-repent-and-be-baptized** — a river baptism at first light: one man rising from the water, another waiting on the bank with folded garments, a crowd of witnesses along the shore.

# TTT ↔ MEAT consistency audit — 2026-07-17

Seven parallel sweeps: the three fork pairs (03x/29 sun-moon-stars, 07x/30 Lucifer,
16/24 path-to-salvation) plus four doctrinal cross-cuts (Daniel chronology, calendar
mechanics, passion-week/firstfruits, cross-references + Babylon/rapture). TTT .adoc
text is the verbatim 1st-edition conversion (2026-07-12); wording drift alone was not
flagged — only reader-visible contradictions, claims one book makes that the other
denies, and broken pointers. Nothing has been edited in either book.

**Correction note (2026-07-17, same day):** the first draft of this report miscalled
the rapture/return cluster. After the author's review ("both are pre-trib, both teach
signs"), TTT 19x3, MEAT 33x, and MEAT 38 were re-read in full from the primary texts
and Section I-A below was rewritten. The author also ruled the keseh item (I-B.14).

Severity: CONTRADICTION = the two books answer the same question differently.
DRIFT = same side of the question, but one formulation undercuts the other.
QUESTION = a coordination/harmonization decision only the author can make.
BROKEN-REF = a pointer or citation that fails.

## I-A. Babylon / rapture cluster — corrected readings

What the two books actually teach (verified against the full texts):

**TTT 19x3** builds a two-stage structure. Stage 1: the overcomers (the man child)
vanish during normalcy before the birth pains — malat (Isa 66:7, Lot's escape word),
harpazo (Rev 12:5), the Enoch ayin — and their removal releases the restraint; the
four winds ride. Stage 2, after the two 1,260-day halves: Babylon falls at the 7th
trumpet = 7th bowl = 7th seal, the kingdom is declared, and "last trump = kingdom
transfer = dead raised (1 Cor 15:52)" (19x3:1048); the remaining elect are gathered
with an apantesis escort toward Mount Zion.

**MEAT** carries the same architecture. Ch. 36's Daniel 9:26: Messiah cuts the
covenant, "he vanishes — and to him the watchful — and he sanctifies: and he shall
destroy the people of a ruler who is coming" — the watchful are removed BEFORE the
destroying (pre-trib in structure). Ch. 38:113 places the resurrection trumpet at the
7th, at Babylon's fall — identical to 19x3:1048. Ch. 33x supplies the sign the
watchful hold: every physical mark of the Day of the LORD is a full-moon mark; the
phase is knowable while the date is concealed.

**On signs:** 19x3:1065-68 ("No cosmic signs. No trumpet. No warning.") describes the
world's normalcy at the vanishing — in that chapter "cosmic signs" ARE Babylon's
smoke (19x3:664-682), which has not yet risen. The watchful are never said to be
signless: 19x3:610 gives the fig-tree preconditions for the return, 19x4 is an entire
sign chapter, and 33x gives the phase. Thief to the world, not to the bride
(1 Thess 5:4). The initial audit's "signless vs sign-marked" contradiction was a
misreading; withdrawn.

**On the thief:** 19x3:618 assigns the thief to the vanishing; MEAT 38:86-87 has the
thief-day end the unprepared in sudden destruction. 19x3's own Lot section (:996):
"on the *same day* the righteous are extracted, fire falls" (Luke 17:29-30) — one
arrival with two faces, and MEAT 38:104 says the moment is the unit in which God
acts "whether the act is a judgment or a rescue." Not a contradiction; withdrawn.

What genuinely remains in this cluster:

1. **Babylon's identity — RULED 2026-07-17.**
   The author's official stance: **Babylon is the G7 (the ridden empire/bloc); the
   whore that rides on her is Jerusalem.** Under the ruling the two books carry
   complementary halves, not rival identifications: TTT 19x2 names the ridden
   (:11 USA/G7/NATO the empire; :147-150 the seven mountains = the G7, "the heart of
   Babylon the Great"), and MEAT 35:74 states the stance verbatim — "The last Babylon
   sits at the far west of the nations: a bloc of seven mountains that even counts
   itself as seven, *ridden by the city that killed the prophets*." The initial
   audit's CONTRADICTION framing conflated the woman with Babylon; withdrawn.
   Naming spots that cross sides of the ruling (wording fixes, the author's call):
   - TTT 19x2:56 — inserted gloss "the harlot *(Babylon)* sits" identifies the RIDER
     as Babylon; under the ruling the parenthetical belongs to the ridden (or reads
     "(Jerusalem)").
   - TTT 19x2:69 — the ten horns burning the HARLOT (Rev 17:16) is quoted as the
     empire's destruction; under the ruling that verse judges the rider.
   - TTT 19x2 nowhere names Jerusalem as the rider — the whore's identity is simply
     absent from the chapter.
   - MEAT 35:57 ("Only a covenant partner can wear that name") and :217 ("Babylon
     for her boast — three spiritual names on one gate") assign the NAME Babylon to
     the woman; :198 reads the forehead "MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT" as her
     mystery-name. **RULED (same day): the name on her head is the name of the
     BEAST — the beast's mark** (Rev 13:17; 14:11), the counterfeit of the seal
     (the Father's name in the forehead, Rev 7:3; 14:1): the forehead names the
     owner, not the wearer. Re-aim :57/:198/:217 accordingly; :61's "counterfeit
     inscription in the counterfeit place" already carries the frame.
   - MEAT 38:134 — the Isa 47 / Rev 18:7 queen read as "the wife of the covenant"
     (rider-flavored); align its Babylon-language to the same convention.

2. **[QUESTION] 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17's scene assignment.**
   MEAT 33x:36-38 reads the shout/trumpet/rising/catching-up as one scene at the
   full-moon trumpet; TTT 19x3 attaches harpazo (shared with Rev 12:5) to the stage-1
   vanishing and apantesis to the stage-2 escort, while also quoting the whole passage
   at Jericho (trumpet → extraction → fire). Decide how each book presents the verse
   so a reader of both hears one doctrine.

3. **[QUESTION] Matthew 24:36's application — intra-TTT.**
   Four usages: TTT 08:325 (from that time on, no one knows — the from-THAT-day-
   forward reading); 19x3:608 (the verse applies to the vanishing); 19x4:38 (bride
   knows the season, not the day or hour); 07:549 (calls "the day and hour no one
   knows" a biblical idiom for Yom Teruah in the very chapter proving the feast
   predictable). MEAT never cites the verse. Rule which application(s) the 2nd
   edition teaches.

4. **[QUESTION] The cloud of the appearing.**
   19x3:682 — "The smoke of Babylon's judgment IS the cloud of Christ's appearing"
   (Matt 24:30 through the smoke). MEAT 37:69 / 33y:208 — the cloud is the
   throne-chariot, "the vehicle of the departure is the vehicle of the return"
   (Acts 1:11). Both-at-once is the house method; rule whether the smoke hearing
   stands alongside the chariot or yields.

5. **[QUESTION] Abomination scales.**
   19x3 reads Matt 24:15 at the future midpoint and 70 AD as an enacted scale (its
   own principle, :198 "history is enacted parable"); MEAT 36:313-321 reads Dan
   12:11's setting-up at AD 692 (Dome of the Rock). Multi-scale fulfillment can hold
   all three; the open question is only whether either book should name the other
   scales so readers don't collide.

6. **[DRIFT] Named agent of "he shall destroy" (Dan 9:26).**
   19x3:293-297 — "points to God as the agent… Messiah vanishes. God destroys."
   MEAT 36:251 + 40:210 + the translation patch — Messiah is the subject throughout.
   Both reject the people-as-destroyer; align the drafts to one name.

7. **[QUESTION] Daniel 9:27.**
   19x3:147 — "midst of the week" at the future midpoint (sacrifice ceases,
   counterfeit installed). MEAT 36:263-269 — verse 27 strengthens (gabar) the
   covenant verse 26 cut: cross → ascension → return. Rule how the readings sit.

8. **[ALIGNMENT] "When Babylon falls" wording.**
   19x2:12 "destroyed… at the start of the tribulation"; 19x3 has the collapse
   mechanism activate at the vanishing (:1015) with the declared fall at the 7th
   trumpet (:1038); MEAT 38 the moment / one hour. Process vs consummation — align
   the drafts' wording so "start" and "moment" don't read as two different falls.

9. **[DRIFT] First seal's package.**
   19x3:541 — "deception, war, famine, and death" under God's four executing spirits.
   MEAT 33y:193-194 (untracked draft) — the crowned rider carries the covenant bow,
   Rev 19:11 alignment. Both keep the horsemen God's agents; the first rider differs.

10. **[DRIFT] Jubilee arithmetic display.**
    19x3:859 — "70 jubilees … = ~2024 AD." MEAT 36:103 — the seventieth jubilee year
    closed spring 2026 (and TTT 18:615/643's own rule agrees). The no-year-zero sum
    from 1406 BC gives the jubilee year spring 2025–2026; update the draft's number.

11. **[KEEP] 19x4's hard dates and satellite items.**
    :10 "On the first day of the twelfth month — just weeks from now —" reads false
    once passed (MEAT 33x:197 refuses dates). Also: Ancient of Days rendered AS the
    moon (:244) vs MEAT 33y:122-131 (the full moon is the seat/witness, the Rider
    distinct); extensive Hebrew script in body prose (:59-227); a hedged "Not
    everything maps cleanly" section (:551).

12. **[QUESTION] TTT 18's Day-of-the-LORD identification.**
    18:14 — "the Day of the LORD, the Last Great Day, the Atzeret, as October 9th,
    2024" (the 22nd — not a full-moon day on TTT's own sabbath table, 18:147-148),
    with 18:540-541 arguing the gathering is not on Trumpets. MEAT 33x — full-moon
    marks, no date. The 2024 date has passed in any case; rule how the 2nd edition
    treats the identification.

## I-B. Other doctrinal items — the author's ruling required

13. **[CONTRADICTION] keseh etymology (Ps 81:3) — RULED 2026-07-17.**
    TTT 07:117-125 prefers "Aramaic loanword" and calls the Hebrew cover-derivation
    "forced… strained," aiming at the dark-moon-as-covered metaphor. MEAT 31:625 —
    "Both words grow from one root, _kasah_, to cover, to fill — the throne is the
    covered seat, the full moon the filled light" (so too TTT 00-preface:15, 19x4:17).
    **The author's ruling:** the cover root is NOT the forced reading — "covered with
    DARKNESS" is what is forced; the Bible defines the throne like the moon facing
    the sun (Psalm 89:36-37). Fix direction for the merge pass: TTT 07 keeps the
    kasah root (covered/filled with the sun's light, the whole face) and aims
    "strained" only at the covered-with-darkness application; drop the
    loanword-over-root preference.

14. **[DRIFT] Head of the year.**
    TTT 08:349 rules for the equinox method ("first renewed moon on or after the
    spring equinox"), showing the Rev 12/Virgo sign fragments locations by a month
    (08:231). MEAT 29:248 states the Virgo full moon as the head flatly, no equinox
    anywhere — and TTT's own insert 03x:272 imports MEAT's sentence. In years like
    2026 the two rules yield different first months.

### The Lucifer fork (07x vs MEAT 30) — either/or vs both-at-once

    TTT 07x:14/103-116 — the received reading displaced ("purely a translator

16. **[CONTRADICTION] Throne replaced, not doubled.**
    TTT 07x:35 — "isn't exalting his 'throne' but his 'full/covered moon'." MEAT

17. **[CONTRADICTION] North denied.**
    TTT 07x:79-83 — "'North' … makes no sense." MEAT 30:57-59 — "The north reading

18. **[CONTRADICTION] Mount of the congregation.**
    TTT 07x:67-74 — assembly sense "derived and stretched… obfuscates." MEAT
    30:47-48 — appointed time/place/meeting inseparable; the saints AND the times
    attacked in one sentence (Dan 7:25).

19. **[CONTRADICTION] Reflected light for the righteous.**
    TTT 07x:141-142 — "the Son and the righteous together, governing the darkness
    with reflected light." Violates stars-burn-from-within (MEAT 29:191-196). The
    03x/29 pair honors the ruling in both books; 07x is the outlier.

20. **[DRIFT] ben shachar.**
    TTT 07x:48-53 — dawn-noun itself redefined ("up to the translator"). MEAT

21. **[DRIFT] "Tradition of men" for the vowel points.**

### Passion-week presentation

22. **[PRESENTATION] Friday-to-Sunday narration — context confirmed 2026-07-17.**
    TTT 14:369-426 answers the Wednesday/Thursday two-sabbath theories inside the
    critics' own weekday frame — the planetary names are granted arguendo (the
    Summary at :472 scare-quotes "Friday") — but the section never announces the
    grant, and the first resolution installs the sunrise day-start INSIDE the
    traditional weekday assignment (a hybrid belonging to neither system). A reader
    holding ch. 12 ("not a single year with a Friday Passover") or MEAT 33:19
    ("Monday crucifixion") reads :420 as endorsement. Fix: one framing sentence at
    the section top + recast :420's conclusion to what it proves (preparation-day
    crucifixion, one sabbath, third-day resurrection — the 14th/15th/16th).

23. **[BROKEN-REF] The garbled Passover-Saturday sentence.**
    TTT 14:524 — "then 33 AD has Passover on Saturday in 30 and 33 AD" (doubled year;
    already comment-flagged). Contradicts ch. 12's own table (33 AD Passover =
    Thursday Apr 2). Reader-visible nonsense as written.

24. **[DRIFT] Lev 23:13 assigned to Shavuot.**
    TTT 19x1:70-74 labels the wave-sheaf-day offering (16th) "first fruits at
    Shavuot." Lev 23:12-13 belong to the sheaf day; Shavuot begins v. 15. Misdates
    the offering the firstfruits-16th case rests on (MEAT 32:100).

## IV. Checked and consistent (the bedrock holds)

- **Rapture architecture (corrected reading):** both books pre-trib in structure —
  extraction of the watchful before the destroying (MEAT 36 = TTT 19x3 stage 1);
  resurrection at the last/7th trumpet at Babylon's fall (MEAT 38:113 = TTT
  19x3:1048); fall of Babylon = Jubilee = 7th-trumpet kingdom transfer (19x3:874-940
  = MEAT 38:113-117); signs for the watchful, thief for the world; extraction-before-
  fire prototypes (Lot/Rahab/Noah) shared; Dan 9:26 flood = armies = Rev 12:15 in
  both; four winds = four spirits = horsemen as God's executing agents.
- **Chronology:** 458 BC decree; 490th year = spring 32-33; AD 32 cross everywhere;
  Suleiman 1535 / 60th jubilee; Jordan crossing 1406 BC (no-year-zero); covenant of
  the pieces ~1876 BC, 70×70 = 4,900 (19x3:832-854 = MEAT 36:106-108); Dan 12:11's
  1,290/1,335/692/2027 is MEAT-only, nothing in TTT collides.
- **Calendar:** month at the full/renewed moon in both; Ps 81:3 keseh=chodesh read
  identically; lunar sabbaths 8/15/22/29; firstfruits fixed 16th + Pentecost 16th of
  month 3 (omer checksum near-verbatim shared); day starts at first light; east-gate
  (Ezek 46:1) argument shared; cross-darkness supernatural in both.
- **Passion:** heart of the earth = Jerusalem; 14th/15th/16th; the 26-parallel Jonah
  table byte-identical; no Sunday-firstfruits anywhere in MEAT.
- **Sun-moon-stars fork:** stars burn / moon reflects honored on both sides (07x the
  lone violation); sun=Father, moon=Son/faithful witness; Rev 12 rendering
  word-identical; tribe-to-sign tables match sign-for-sign; angel=messenger.
- **Path to salvation:** the three-step path identical in content and order; the
  perfect-obedience Q&A verbatim in both; truth = the law; willful vs unintentional
  framework identical; both books law-positive throughout.
- **Cross-refs that resolve:** MEAT 05→TTT 12 (AD 29 release); MEAT 36→TTT 12; MEAT
  29:399→TTT 07 zodiac table; MEAT 32/31→TTT 10/07/11; MEAT 33's Second-Edition
  claims match TTT; both bibliographies carry each other's exact titles.
- **Masoretes:** never "hostile" in either book (07x's "tradition of men" is the one
  framing slip, item 21).

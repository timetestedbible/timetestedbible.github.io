This is the vocabulary the book works in.
Where this book carries the proof, the entry cites its chapter and verses; where the full derivation lives in the companion volume, the entry says so — _MEAT The Bible's Symbolic Language_.

[[sym-accession-counting]]Accession-year counting:: The convention in which a king's first partial year is his accession year and year 1 begins at the next new year — opposed to inclusive counting, which numbers the partial year as year 1. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/herod-the-great/[Herod the Great]__

[[sym-appointed-times]]Appointed times (moedim):: The LORD's fixed meeting days, proclaimed by the lights He set "for signs, and for seasons" — appointments kept on His calendar, not movable traditions. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 1:14; Lev 23:2; Ps 104:19) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/appointed-times/[Appointed Times]__

[[sym-atzeret]]Atzeret:: The eighth-day assembly closing Tabernacles, on the 22nd of the 7th month — "the last day, that great day of the feast." +
[.seeref]__(Lev 23:36; John 7:37) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/appointed-times/[Appointed Times]__

[[sym-azimuth]]Azimuth:: Compass bearing along the horizon — 0° north, 90° east — the measure by which the Gibeon and Aijalon stations of Joshua's long day are surveyed. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/stability-of-astronomy/[Stability of Astronomy]__

[[sym-consular-year]]Consular year:: Rome's year named by its two consuls — the frame Josephus uses to anchor the start of an event sequence, not necessarily its completion. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/herods-appointment-and-battle-of-actium/[Herod's Appointment & Battle of Actium]__

[[sym-crescent]]Crescent:: The first western sliver after the conjunction — the month-start of the received calendar, and in Scripture the hardware of crescent-moon worship: the saharonim stripped from Midian's kings and the daughters of Zion. +
[.seeref]__(Judg 8:21-26; Isa 3:18) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-month-start/[When Does the Month Start?]__

[[sym-dark-conjunction]]Dark conjunction:: The moon's hidden phase beside the sun, showing the earth its unlit side — the only phase that can cover the sun, and the reckoning by which the counterfeit calendar begins its months. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/lucifers-declared-plan/[Lucifer's Declared Plan]__

[[sym-day-of-the-lord]]Day of the LORD:: The appointed day of resurrection and judgment; every physical mark the prophets give it — the homecoming, the trumpet, the east-to-west shining, the whole moon turned to blood — is a mark of the full moon. +
[.seeref]__(Ps 81:3; Prov 7:20; Joel 2:31; 1 Cor 15:52) · developed in MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-ephemeris]]Ephemeris:: The computed table of where the lights stand on any date — free software (Stellarium, on the NASA JPL tables) by which every date in this book can be rerun. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/stability-of-astronomy/[Stability of Astronomy]__

[[sym-equinox-rule]]Equinox rule:: The year begins with the first renewed moon on or after the spring equinox — the sun's turning (tekufah, Ps 19:6) anchoring the first month, stable at every longitude. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-year-start/[When Does the Year Start?]__

[[sym-faithful-witness]]Faithful witness:: The full moon — the throne's witness in the sky, bearing the sun's whole light through the night; the office the counterfeit phase cannot fill. +
[.seeref]__(Ps 89:36-37; Rev 1:5) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/sun-moon-and-stars/[Sun, Moon, and Stars]__

[[sym-first-light]]First light:: The start of the biblical day — dawn, not sunset; the tomb already empty "while it was still dark" belongs to the first day of the week. +
[.seeref]__(John 20:1; Gen 1:5) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-day-start/[When Does the Day Start?]__

[[sym-firstfruits]]First Fruits / first fruits:: Capitalized *First Fruits* names the barley wave-sheaf observance, always on the 16th of the first month—the third day from Passover on the 14th and the fixed date of the resurrection. Lowercase *first fruits* is the broader harvest category: the first offerings of barley, wheat, wine, and oil. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 23:10-17; Num 18:12) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-is-the-sabbath/[When is the Sabbath?] and link:/books/time-tested-tradition/appointed-times/[Appointed Times]__

[[sym-full-moon]]Full moon (keseh):: The moon's whole face covered with the sun's light — the phase Scripture sets at the month's head, and a word written with the letters of the throne. +
[.seeref]__(Ps 81:3; Prov 7:20) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-month-start/[When Does the Month Start?]__

[[sym-gospel]]Gospel:: The good tidings of the coming kingdom, in which the law goes forth from Zion — proclaimed beforehand to Abraham, and announced with the command to keep the appointed times in the same breath. +
[.seeref]__(Nah 1:15; Isa 2:2-3; Gal 3:8) · derived in the Gospel chapter of MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-heart-of-earth]]Heart of the earth:: Jerusalem — the city at the navel of the land, in whose power the Son of man spent the 3 days and 3 nights; not the tomb. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 12:40; Ezek 5:5; 38:12) · derived in MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-high-sabbath]]High sabbath:: A feast rest fixed by date; on the biblical calendar the 15th is at once the weekly sabbath and the feast day, which is why John calls the sabbath of the crucifixion week "an high day." +
[.seeref]__(John 19:31; Lev 23:6-7) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-is-the-sabbath/[When is the Sabbath?]__

[[sym-inclusive-counting]]Inclusive counting:: Counting both endpoints of a span — "after three days" and "on the third day" describe the same interval; the 14th, 15th, and 16th are 3 days. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 27:63-64; Luke 24:21) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/passion-week/[Passion Week — 3 Days & 3 Nights]__

[[sym-intercalation]]Intercalation:: The 13th month that keeps lunar months aligned with the solar year — governed by the equinox rule, not by committee decree. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-year-start/[When Does the Year Start?]__

[[sym-jubilee]]Jubilee:: The fiftieth year of liberty crowning seven sabbaths of years; the clock runs from the Jordan crossing in 1406 BC. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 25:8-10) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/32-ad-resurrection/[32 AD Resurrection]__

[[sym-lunar-sabbath]]Lunar sabbath:: The seventh-day rests counted from the renewed moon — the 8th, 15th, 22nd, and 29th of every month, with the renewed-moon day standing outside the sevens. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 23:15-16; Isa 66:23) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-is-the-sabbath/[When is the Sabbath?]__

[[sym-mare-clausum]]Mare clausum:: "The closed sea" — the ancient halt of Mediterranean sailing from roughly November to March, which constrains every reconstructed voyage timeline, including Herod's journey to Rome. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/herods-appointment-and-battle-of-actium/[Herod's Appointment & Battle of Actium]__

[[sym-mazzaroth]]Mazzaroth:: The twelve-sign circuit brought forth in its season — the standards of the twelve tribes mapped to the signs, the sky's yearly clock face. +
[.seeref]__(Job 38:32) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-month-start/[When Does the Month Start?]__

[[sym-metonic]]Metonic cycle:: The 19-year cycle — 235 lunar months — after which the moon's phases return to the same solar dates; the reason a rare sun-moon alignment recurs about once in 19 years. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/stability-of-astronomy/[Stability of Astronomy]__

[[sym-molad]]Molad:: The rabbinic calculated mean conjunction from which the modern Jewish calendar reckons its months — a computed dark moon, standing in for an observed light. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-month-start/[When Does the Month Start?]__

[[sym-moon]]Moon:: The lesser light — the Son's sign: the faithful witness bearing the sun's whole light through the night, and the light appointed to govern the seasons. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 1:16; Ps 89:37; 104:19) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/sun-moon-and-stars/[Sun, Moon, and Stars]__

[[sym-mountain]]Mountain:: A kingdom — the stone cut without hands becomes "a great mountain," and Daniel interprets it as the kingdom set over every other kingdom. +
[.seeref]__(Dan 2:35, 44) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/lucifers-declared-plan/[Lucifer's Declared Plan]; the full study in MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-olympiad]]Olympiad:: The Greek four-year cycle counted from 776 BC — Josephus' second dating frame, naming the 184th olympiad at Herod's appointment. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/herods-appointment-and-battle-of-actium/[Herod's Appointment & Battle of Actium]__

[[sym-omer]]Omer count:: The 50-day count from the wave sheaf to Pentecost — "seven sabbaths shall be complete" — which completes only on the lunar month; the calendar's built-in checksum. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 23:15-16) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-is-the-sabbath/[When is the Sabbath?]__

[[sym-regal-defacto]]Regal vs de facto reign:: Josephus' dual count for Herod — 37 years from the Roman decree (regal, de jure), 34 from the death of Antigonus (de facto); his unqualified references default to the regal count. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/herod-regal-vs-defacto/[Herod: Regal vs De Facto]__

[[sym-regnal-year]]Regnal year:: A king's year of reign as ancient dating formulas count it — from accession, or from the calendar new year that follows it, by the local convention. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/herod-the-great/[Herod the Great]__

[[sym-renewed-moon]]Renewed Moon Day:: The first day of the biblical month — the first light after the full moon, the waning moon still standing 12-24° above the western horizon at dawn. +
[.seeref]__(Ps 81:3; Job 10:17; Ps 89:36-37) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-month-start/#atlas-renewed-witness[When Does the Month Start?]__

[[sym-retrocalculation]]Retrocalculation:: Running the ephemeris backward to recover an ancient sky — verified against independently dated observations to 2134 BC, deeper than any date this book computes. +
[.seeref]__see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/stability-of-astronomy/[Stability of Astronomy]__

[[sym-sabbath]]Sabbath (shabbat, sabbaton):: A complete seven crowned by rest — the week itself: Hebrew counts "seven sabbaths" (shabbatot) where Deuteronomy counts the same span as "seven weeks" (shavuot), Greek calls the week the sabbath ("twice in the sabbath," dis tou sabbatou, is twice a week), and the unit scales to the land's seventh year and the seven sevens of Jubilee. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 23:15 with Deut 16:9; Lev 25:8; Luke 18:12; Matt 28:1) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/the-sabbath/[The Sabbath]__

[[sym-sabbath-day]]Sabbath day (yom ha-shabbat):: The marked seventh of the sabbath-unit — Hebrew says "the day OF the sabbath" exactly as it says "the day OF the new moon," and Ezekiel 46:1 sets the two genitives side by side; when the law means the resting member itself it doubles the word: shabbat shabbaton, "the sabbath of rest." Greek writes the same construction: "the day of the sabbaths" (Luke 4:16). +
[.seeref]__(Ex 20:8; Ezek 46:1; Ex 31:15) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/the-sabbath/[The Sabbath]__

[[sym-sea]]Sea, waters:: Peoples and nations in an unrooted mass — the restless multitude through which rulers move, and into whose heart Jonah was cast. +
[.seeref]__(Rev 17:15; Isa 17:12; Jonah 2:3) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah]; derived in MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-shadow]]Shadow:: The appointed times as outlines cast by a coming substance — "a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ." A shadow is proof of the body that casts it, and keeping the shadow traces the substance. +
[.seeref]__(Col 2:16-17; Heb 10:1) · derived in MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-shining-one]]Shining one (helel):: Isaiah's fallen "Lucifer," heard twice from the letters: son of the dawn, and son and bearer of blackness — the claimant of the covered lunar seat. +
[.seeref]__(Isa 14:12-15) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/lucifers-declared-plan/[Lucifer's Declared Plan]__

[[sym-ship]]Ship:: An organized kingdom, city, or state riding upon the sym:sym-sea[sea] of peoples — its rulers steer it and its inhabitants crew it; one man is given to the storm so the ship of the nation may live. +
[.seeref]__(Ezek 27:2-32; Jonah 1:4-15) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/sign-of-jonah/[The Sign of Jonah]; derived in MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-sign-of-jonah]]Sign of Jonah:: The Passion traced scene for scene in Jonah's voyage — the one sign given to that generation, laid out in the 26-parallel table. +
[.seeref]__(Matt 12:39-40) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/passion-week/[Passion Week — 3 Days & 3 Nights]; derived in MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-stars]]Stars:: The righteous — "they that turn many to righteousness" shine "as the stars for ever and ever," the host governed with the lesser light. +
[.seeref]__(Dan 12:3; Gen 1:16) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/sun-moon-and-stars/[Sun, Moon, and Stars]__

[[sym-sun]]Sun:: The greater light — the Father's sign: "the LORD God is a sun"; the source whose light the moon bears and the stars answer. +
[.seeref]__(Gen 1:16; Ps 84:11) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/sun-moon-and-stars/[Sun, Moon, and Stars]__

[[sym-throne]]Throne (kisse):: Written with the same letters as keseh, the full moon — the covered seat, defined in the sky by sun and moon together: the moon at fulness, facing the sun, covered with its light. +
[.seeref]__(Ps 89:36-37; Job 26:9) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/when-does-the-month-start/[When Does the Month Start?]__

[[sym-shavuot]]Weeks, the Sevens (Shavuot, Feast of Weeks):: The jubilee-shaped count — seven sevens crowned by a fiftieth of liberty; the feast is a count, not a week, and Daniel's Sevens carry its shape into the 490-year prophecies. +
[.seeref]__(Lev 23:15-16; 25:8-10; Deut 16:9-10; Dan 9:24) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/32-ad-resurrection/[32 AD Resurrection]; derived in MEAT — The Bible's Symbolic Language__

[[sym-year-of-release]]Year of release:: Every seventh year — debts forgiven, slaves freed — proclaimed at Atonement; the "acceptable year of the LORD" Jesus read at Nazareth. +
[.seeref]__(Deut 15:1-2; Luke 4:19) · see link:/books/time-tested-tradition/32-ad-resurrection/[32 AD Resurrection]__

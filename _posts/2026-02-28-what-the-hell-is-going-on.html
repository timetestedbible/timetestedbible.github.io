What the Hell Is Going On?

Bombs are falling. Nations that have stood for generations are being shaken. People are dying — soldiers, civilians, children. And the question everyone is asking, whether they say it out loud or not, is the same four-letter word in the title. What is happening? Is this judgment? Is God doing this? And if fire is falling from the sky — what does Scripture actually say fire IS, who it falls on, and what happens to the people standing in it?

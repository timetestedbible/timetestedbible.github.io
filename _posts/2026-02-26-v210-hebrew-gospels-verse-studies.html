Time Tested Bible v2.1

11 translations. 151 symbol studies. 31 verse studies. 10 blog posts. All offline.

This is the biggest content update since launch. Here's what's new.

Hebrew Gospels Translation

A new Hebrew Gospels translation is now available — the 11th translation in the reader. Based on the Cochin Hebrew New Testament manuscript tradition (~1700 AD), it renders the New Testament from a Hebrew-origin perspective, showing where the Greek may have obscured the original Hebrew meaning.

The translation includes verse-level study notes that flag Hebrew primacy issues, Greek deviations, and translation contradictions. Revelation alone has over 870 study note markers. Chapters include collapsible note sections with detailed commentary.

Torah Eternal — 31 Verse Studies

Every major passage cited to argue "the law has been abolished" now has a detailed verse study — 31 in total. Each study examines the passage in its original language, reads the verses before and after, and shows what the text actually says in context.

Passages covered include Matthew 5:17, Romans 6:14, Romans 7:4, Romans 10:4, Galatians 3:25, Galatians 5:18, Ephesians 2:15, Colossians 2:14, Hebrews 8:13, 2 Corinthians 3:7, Acts 15:10, and more.

Cross-References Panel

The research panel now includes a cross-references tab. Select any verse and see the top related passages with verse text previews — no need to look them up manually. Each reference is clickable and navigates directly to the passage.

151 Symbol Studies

The symbol dictionary has grown to 151 studies — from Almond to Zion. Each study traces how Scripture uses a recurring image, word, or concept across both testaments. Tap any highlighted symbol word in a blog post or verse study to open its full study, or hover for a tooltip preview.

Improved Bible Text

Poetry sections (Psalms, Proverbs, prophets) now display with proper line breaks and indentation. Paragraph spacing, verse number styling, and line height have been refined across all 11 translations for a cleaner reading experience on both mobile and desktop.

Social Sharing

When you share a blog post link on social media, messaging apps, or group chats, it now displays a rich preview card with the title, description, and hero image. No more bare URLs.

Take It With You — Works Offline

Everything in this app — all 11 Bible translations, the Hebrew lexicon, 151 symbol studies, 31 verse studies, the historical timeline, Josephus, Philo, and every blog post — is stored on your device after first load. There is no server, no database, and no internet connection required after installation.

That means searches are instant — no waiting for server responses. It also means the app works when you have no cell signal, no Wi-Fi, or if the grid goes down entirely. If you're preparing for what's ahead, a complete Bible study library that works without infrastructure is not a convenience — it's a necessity.

276 commits since the last update. Here's what's new for readers and students.

Hebrew Gospels Translation

A new Hebrew Gospels (HG) translation is now available in the Bible reader. Based on the Cochin Hebrew New Testament manuscript tradition (~1700 AD), this translation renders the New Testament from a Hebrew-origin perspective — showing where the Greek may have obscured the original Hebrew meaning.

The HG translation includes verse-level study notes that flag Hebrew primacy issues, Greek deviations, and translation contradictions. Chapters include collapsible note sections with detailed commentary. Revelation alone has over 870 study note markers.

Torah Eternal — 31 Verse Studies

Every major passage cited to argue "the law has been abolished" now has a detailed verse study — 31 in total. Each study examines the passage in its original language, reads the verses before and after, and shows what the text actually says in context.

Passages covered include Matthew 5:17, Romans 6:14, Romans 7:4, Romans 10:4, Galatians 3:25, Galatians 5:18, Ephesians 2:15, Colossians 2:14, Hebrews 8:13, 2 Corinthians 3:7, Acts 15:10, and more. Start with the overview page that links them all together.

Cross-References Panel

The research panel now includes a cross-references tab powered by OpenBible.info data. Select any verse and see the top related passages with verse text previews — no need to look them up manually. Each reference is clickable and navigates directly to the passage.

151 Symbol Studies

The symbol dictionary has grown to 151 studies — from Almond to Zion. Each study traces how Scripture uses a recurring image, word, or concept across both testaments. New studies include Throne, Lamb, Lion, Trumpet, Wrath, Jericho, Ten Horns, Net, Scarlet, Snare, Bear, and dozens more.

Symbol references are now linked throughout blog posts and verse studies using the $[symbol] markup — tap any highlighted symbol word to open its full study.

Improved Bible Text Formatting

The Bible reader now renders text with better readability and structure. Poetry sections (Psalms, Proverbs, prophets) display with proper line breaks and indentation. Paragraph spacing, verse number styling, and line height have been refined across all translations for a cleaner reading experience on both mobile and desktop.

Social Sharing

When you share a blog post link on social media, messaging apps, or group chats, it now displays a rich preview card with the post's title, description, and hero image — powered by Open Graph meta tags. No more bare URLs.

Strong's Tooltips in Blog Posts

Blog posts that reference Strong's numbers (like H8199 or G646) now show hover tooltips with the Hebrew or Greek word, transliteration, and definition — the same tooltips available in the Bible reader, now working throughout all study content.

Symbol Hover Tooltips

Symbol-linked words throughout the site now show a preview tooltip on hover with a summary of the symbol's meaning. Tap to open the full study, or just hover to get the gist without leaving your reading position.
